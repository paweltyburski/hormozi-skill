# lead-getters

The canon already covers: Lead Getters (customers, employees, agencies, affiliates run the Core Four for you), the Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals, the Hinge Method, Referral Rate minus Churn, the Four R's, the Agency Learning Agreement and Selection Checklist, the Affiliate Army with its three integration levels, Three-Tier Affiliate Commissions and the 10-20 affiliate test group. The recordings add: growth maths and benchmark numbers, exact referral scripts and gift-card mechanics, affiliate activation details and launch results, influencer outreach, agency handling in practice, many answers to owners in specific sectors, and several places where he changed his mind.

## Rules and thresholds

- Flat when monthly referrers equal monthly leavers (10% vs 10%); doubling per interval at 20% referring vs 10% leaving. He gives a rough illustration for the vast majority of businesses ("let's call it") of ~2% referring against ~10% leaving. [YT-IFpHasZ7jN4@00:09:03] [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:03:05]
- "Golden ratio": share of customers referring each month above monthly churn (example 10% vs 5%) means growth without paid ads. [YT-XZEjp2mP1U0@00:09:48]
- Word of mouth is the only quadratic channel; every other channel is dollars in, fixed amount out. [POD-0442@00:04:03] [POD-0515@00:30:30]
- Targets: ~1.1 referred customers per buyer makes advertising unnecessary; 0.3 per customer cuts effective CAC ~30%; if one customer in three refers, CAC falls by a third. [POD-0678@00:13:39] [YT-Uki3IUkUu7Q@00:10:16] [YT-cy2k1GdA-9o@00:10:21]
- Benchmarks: 25-30% of customers by word of mouth is a strong sign (acquisition spend effectively a third cheaper); ~30% of new business from referrals is typical across his companies when asked consistently; Gym Launch ran ~0.4 referred clients per client and over 50% of new customers by referral (stated as 'now', no year given). [POD-0546@00:17:01] [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:02:18] [POD-0267@00:00:48] [POD-0028@00:04:59]

## Tactics and scripts

- Ask "who else do you know?" (not yes/no), wrap it in a compliment, push lightly on "no one". [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:01:31] [YT-cy2k1GdA-9o@00:08:14]
- Make the introduction on the spot: selfie on the customer's phone texted to the friend, or a live three-way text. [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:13:23] [POD-0518@00:06:10] [YT-CaiLcj6tzBQ@00:05:07]
- Grant a discount only for three friends' names given on the spot (book version: $400 instead of $500 for 3 friends). [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:08:00] [POD-230819-15@00:28:45]
- After the sale ask "want to know how to get this free?"; one test paid $100 cash per referred buyer on a $500 offer and ~30% of each promotion's sales came this way. [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:11:26]
- Gift cards: sell $200 cards for $20, max two per customer, only as gifts, 30-day expiry, text follow-up, credit toward a $2,000 package (book version: one-third value, 7-14 day expiry). It fails if clients redeem on themselves. [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:03:56] [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:06:24] [POD-230819-15@00:30:40] [YT-3Ju1I37jWUM@00:06:49]
- Small invitation-only events for 10-15 top clients and referrers, each bringing a plus-one, roughly every other month; make them visible with photos and a VIP name. [POD-0012@00:00:46] [POD-0012@00:05:12] [POD-0012@00:04:04]
- Quarterly appreciation events with a handwritten invitation whose P.S. is a forwardable plus-one pass (30-50% of the room were friends). Cards every ~6 weeks with a share-with-a-friend P.S. [YT-qpQvdBFW_yI@00:15:02] [YT-sPkMHh8zTMI@00:08:42]
- Reward options: pay the referrer the CAC or split it (example $100 + $100), spouse discount, unlockable bonuses, a 40% lifetime commission with a contest on top. [POD-230819-15@00:26:13] [YT-sPkMHh8zTMI@00:20:08] [POD-250819-03@00:23:01]
- Automations: a referral offer in an auto-DM added ~10%; a "how did you hear about us" dropdown with referrer name at check-in. [YT-TooAB8Ow6cQ@03:29:45] [YT--Koq14DPXC4@00:47:18]
- Cold messages: ask whether they know anyone who wants help, citing a past client's struggle and result. [POD-230819-06@00:13:40] [YT-w7g08dVTwaE@00:05:17]
- Find partners: ask where best customers buy and spend time (200-item list); name your ten biggest potential partners and spend real money to reach them, or do 100 reach-outs a day; widen to any non-competing business serving your avatar at the same buying moment. [POD-230819-17@00:06:33] [YT-XwZH-lOKG9c@00:55:13] [YT-SmiOK8Yun4s@00:17:13]
- Volume: ~20 plain emails a day; 40 emails yielded 6 partners worth a third of revenue. Reverse-engineering example: 100 outreach/day, 2 partners/week, ~10 appointments per partner. [YT-SmiOK8Yun4s@00:19:25] [YT-SmiOK8Yun4s@00:14:41] [POD-0485@00:28:37]
- Send a prospective partner about five customers first, then propose the deal; send referral partners business before gifts and aim for 10-20 of them. [YT-YZdE8U5eD_s@00:50:21] [YT-h6y0nYVZgwE@00:13:32]
- Build the affiliate offer like a customer offer, framed as extra money from existing customers with no build or support, and as a benefit to their audience; peel off a component and let them keep 100%. [POD-230819-17@00:07:28] [POD-0762@00:24:22] [YT-FMzKk73iUhw@00:30:29]

## Changes of mind and contradictions

- Word of mouth for everyone? Several recordings say slow growth with customers means a weak product; later he says virality depends on the product and retention is the universal target; B2B on both sides can have limited runway.
