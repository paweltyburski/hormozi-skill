# management-ops

The canon covers Document Demonstrate Duplicate, Game Tape Review, the RICE matrix, one priority at a time, firing with warnings, 4-6 protected hours, tools by headcount stage and culture. The recordings add what the canon lacks: the cost of changing a process (the 20% rule), delegation arithmetic and handoff tests, maker versus manager calendars, why a person is not doing a task (STAR, Management Diamond), deadline compression, leader pay, founder-operator splits, and how his own views moved. Hiring, sales pay and pricing have their own pages.

## Rules and thresholds

1. By Hormozi's rough estimate, changing a process people execute tends to cost about 20% of that function's output at first, so he adopts only changes he expects to gain clearly more. A 5% gain can take 4-5 months to make up; a 40% gain at 50-50 odds qualifies for him. [POD-0808@00:09:59] [POD-0862@00:01:53] [YT-hfwZwPGsbIo@00:09:53] [YT-hfwZwPGsbIo@00:10:52]

3. He estimates roughly 52 funnel tests a year (with sufficient traffic) but maybe only about four people-process changes per department. In a mature business the control usually beats the variant (14 of 16 split tests lost). [YT-hfwZwPGsbIo@00:13:08] [POD-0808@00:08:00]

## Tactics and scripts

1. Time study: log every 15-minute block for one to two weeks, rank by revenue value, eliminate what is not worth solving, delegate and train the rest, hire for the biggest slice. Colour tasks green, yellow, red and give each a project, process or person. [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@01:46:13] [YT-0EqJD2o-Mnk@00:16:00] [YT-mr4Pw66_490@00:05:39]
2. Hire for the cheapest-to-buy task that eats the biggest share of your time; jump two steps by hiring for your job and the role you would take next. [YT-aOUK4DG0-kM@00:00:00] [YT-EdUAAVKl_io@00:01:09]
3. Handoff in three stages: they watch you, you watch them, you stay reachable. For sales hand over a third, a half, three quarters. [YT-mr4Pw66_490@00:14:09] [POD-0074@00:06:17]
4. Give a decision box: a spending limit, an ordered list of fixes, the few cases that escalate (e.g. up to $1,000 per problem; up to 100% of an upset customer's last month). [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@01:47:38] [YT-unshZobTt6Q@00:43:09] [YT-unshZobTt6Q@00:44:04]
5. Staff bring decisions pre-digested ("a six-inch putt": context, options, recommendation); the Shopify guest describes giving one tie-breaker question for decisions you never see. [POD-0835@00:49:47] [YT-YZdE8U5eD_s@01:09:10]
6. "Nose in, hands out": quarterly advisory meeting per company, no homework, a private channel outside company tools. His delegated-business test: not in its Slack, one lead reaches him, one weekly meeting with its head. [YT-V7oUcAfof34@00:03:59] [YT-gpKz22P84iM@00:06:18]
7. Hire a real leader the team admires, not an "ops person"; define every activity a head-of-operations role owns or it becomes a junk drawer. [LS-0RjIw9M1j28@00:25:19] [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@01:38:13]
