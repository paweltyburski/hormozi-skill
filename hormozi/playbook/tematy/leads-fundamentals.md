# leads-fundamentals

The canon already covers Engaged Leads, the Core Four, Lead Magnet Steps with the Three Lead Magnet Types and Four Delivery Mechanisms, the Problem-Solution Cycle, More Better New, the Rule of 100, Open to Goal, the Advertising Checklist, nurture, and the second-channel rule at 50-99 staff. The recordings add: numeric benchmarks (opens, opt-ins, CTR, response time, show rate, test size), the "four hours a day" rule with its revenue thresholds, single-channel doctrine and its exceptions, the "market is not capped" argument, friction rules for lead quality, many lead-magnet naming tactics, a six-channel taxonomy, and live diagnoses of real businesses. Later recordings also show him revising earlier views on content, inbound and outbound.

## Rules and thresholds

Volume

1. When someone says a channel failed, ask how many per day: 100 DMs over six to eight weeks is about 1/42 to 1/56 of 100 a day. [POD-230819-18@00:09:04] [POD-0515@00:03:15]
2. A business owner tested flyers at 5,000 and then sent 5,000 a day; Hormozi's 300 was about 20x too small. In cold calling, the effort gap he described was about 50x, not 2x to 4x. [YT-WrCt0R3FBFs@00:09:00] [POD-0401@00:11:20] [YT-WrCt0R3FBFs@00:24:38]
3. Outbound volume floors: about 100 calls a day; 200 to 500 cold emails a day (500 gives roughly 5-10 meaningful responses). [POD-0249@00:05:42]
4. Felt volatility is a symptom of too little volume over too short a window; scale a year's activity into one day (12 a year to 12 a day is about 365x). [YT-HxEQCHpZzHk@00:28:34] [YT-A_tx40lNpf8@00:17:27] [POD-0840@00:46:40]
5. Size an ad jump from the sales gap: one sale a week to one a day is about 7x the advertising, about 14x allowing for falling efficiency. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:38:09]
6. Doubling takes effort; an order of magnitude forces a change of method, and conversion tweaks only add increments. [LS-noE1MQPMvAc@00:09:29] [POD-0794@00:00:00]
7. Volume needs a feedback loop: review each 100 pieces, take the top 10, make the next 100 resemble them. [LS-X39rITUMEPA@00:22:19] [POD-0852@01:25:29]

Time and revenue thresholds

8. Under about $1M revenue, spend the first four hours of each day on awareness with one Core Four method; elsewhere the cut is $1-3M, or until about $3M a year. [YT-HxEQCHpZzHk@00:00:00] [YT-reisEL_D7xc@00:38:26] [POD-0747@00:51:49]
9. Set goals on controllable actions (a daily promotion block) and score adherence, not the revenue number. [YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY@00:02:25] [YT-VBoRLJimVzc@00:34:37]
10. Zero to $1M: one channel, one product, one avatar, run consistently with predictable input-to-output numbers ($1M a year is about $20,000 a week). $1M to $10M: add a back end to lift LTV, then use it to afford broader channels. Is the market capped?

21. A city with about 400 gyms signs up roughly 4,000 members a month and an owner needs 20-40; he estimates the real market at 100x-1,000x what you see. [POD-0063@00:11:54] [POD-0238@00:00:00]
22. Before declaring saturation, do the "should do" list (faster lead calls, follow-up, content, cold calling, more platforms) and zoom out one level at a time; dozens of rivals on one platform signals an uncapped niche. [YT-9gVdCR7W8o8@00:07:40] [POD-0531@00:01:24] [POD-0531@00:03:01]

24. A service that cannot afford $100-$200 to get someone in the door has a business problem; "more leads" is often really a repeat/referral problem. [YT--UvmyixFszw@00:00:00] [YT-Uki3IUkUu7Q@00:04:41]

Speed to lead

25. Contact new leads within 30 minutes, ideally 5 (early episode, about 3x sales); later cited figures: within 60 seconds lifts close odds 391% (as cited), over 5 minutes cuts them 80%. [POD-0017@00:01:56] [YT-StVqS0jD7Ls@00:03:03] [YT-pLhQOYMGa88@00:15:54]
26. Show rate rests on volume of outreach, personalisation, speed and availability; self-worked leads showed about 3x better than one employee's at his first gym. [POD-200710-01@00:33:51]
27. Text opens 98%+ against 30-40% for a new opt-in email (depending on offer) and about 10% for a list. [POD-200710-01@00:37:44]
28. Poor scheduling on the thank-you page usually means inconvenient slots; if speed basics exist and show stays low, look at too little friction earlier. [POD-0155@00:02:27] [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@01:15:53]

Lead quality, friction, free

29. Too many bad leads: add friction; too few: remove it; bad leads at minimal friction: targeting is wrong. Good friction removes only bad prospects; bad friction (slow page) removes everyone. [YT-fSbqaTlWaYI@00:10:11] [YT-nrounb8NlFQ@00:32:21]
30. Add filters when volume is high and quality poor, accepting a 33-50% throughput loss. Five ways to add friction: qualification, information asked, steps, forced consumption, ad length. [YT-Kk6Vdfdwmxw@00:05:16] [POD-251114-05@00:03:23]
31. In four gym split tests of 10 markets, free and non-free leads closed at the same rate while going to free most times cut lead cost 5x or more. [POD-251114-05@00:07:36]
32. A small workshop price typically cuts the unqualified share from about 90% (free) to about 40%. [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:09:04]
33. Qualified-lead criteria: the fewest basic benchmarks that predict buying (example income above $100,000). Send qualified and unqualified to different thank-you pages and fire the pixel only on the qualified one. [POD-0811@00:09:53] [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:10:28]

Lead magnets

34. A magnet may lose money if it lowers total cost per paying customer ($25 magnet cut cost per customer from $3,000 to $1,000); he says to be willing to charge for what you give away, such as $100, $1,000 or $10,000. [YT-7NMH1oAkgLY@00:03:44] [POD-230819-04@00:29:31] [YT-reisEL_D7xc@01:06:11]
35. Test packaging harder than content: the headline alone moves uptake 2x-10x; test 10-30 names by click-through. Easier consumption lifts take rate 2x-4x. [YT-Mst4hreQYl0@00:04:39] [LS-lidFtb1Ga0o@00:27:19] [POD-230819-04@00:22:20]
36. Prospects prefer an immediate smaller magnet to a bigger slower one; make it risk-free, easy, fast. [YT-nrounb8NlFQ@00:21:48]
37. Advertise the core offer directly first; add a magnet when buyers need more information, common with pricier offers. [POD-230819-04@00:06:41]
38. A magnet mostly acts as a fresh hook for a smaller market; to warm a large audience, free content is the real magnet. [LS-DfXy_PzlC0o@00:19:18]

## Tactics and scripts

1. Warm list from email contacts, DM-able followers and phone contacts; start on the platform with most contacts (2,000 contacts at 100 a day is 20 days per pass). [POD-0571@00:00:59] [POD-0448@00:13:45]
2. Free help for the first 5-10 clients in exchange for feedback and a review; convert by saying you can no longer do it for nothing. [YT-w7g08dVTwaE@00:03:12] [YT-oRMG_HpOAN4@00:13:59] [POD-0474@00:13:46]
3. Rule of 300 with no audience: post, comment, then message the people who reacted; comments beat posts for reach. [YT-HsQeQM1jUeg@00:05:50] [POD-0764@00:01:47]
4. A beginner with a weak PDF should offer about five hours of personal help; ask what working together would look like at a given price. [POD-0766@00:05:38] [POD-0766@00:09:11]
5. Attach a phone call to the funnel rather than selling through it (10x-20x revenue reported). [POD-200609-01@01:09:26]
6. Name the magnet by result, not vehicle; patterns: number plus outcome plus time frame, "how to X without Y even if Z", adjective plus asset, "X mistakes". [YT-Mst4hreQYl0@00:22:50] [YT-Mst4hreQYl0@00:25:26]
7. Rotate up to 12 magnet versions to keep ads fresh; diagnosis magnets suit problems that worsen with waiting. [POD-230819-04@00:18:19] [POD-230819-04@00:11:30]
8. Turn something you already deliver into a time-boxed challenge; rerun quarterly to non-buyers under a new name. [YT-OpeN4O5myIg@00:04:12] [YT-OpeN4O5myIg@00:12:44]
9. Place the magnet about a third into the VSL; split a webinar into short modules so it does not read as a pitch. [YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U@00:23:28] [YT-TooAB8Ow6cQ@00:06:10]
10. Rebuilt funnel: headline promising the free assessment to stated qualifiers, short BANT questions, scheduler only for the qualified; thank-you page with sales video above the scheduler. [YT-nrounb8NlFQ@00:24:29] [YT-n6SHLmKcY0E@00:22:01]
11. Congruence audit: click your own ad and read every message in order; add a short filter page before the sales page if heavy traffic converts badly. [POD-0769b@00:08:06] [YT-HwPXctiw_rY@00:48:24]
12. Lift webinar show rate with a nine-email series, SMS, behaviour-triggered content and voicemail drops (18% to 24%). [YT-4GQLJjH9-oA@00:01:06]
13. Put non-buyers into long-term nurture and rerun them later; a basic follow-up email to opt-ins who did not join lifted one school's conversion 40%. [YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U@00:27:39] [POD-0765@00:02:26] [YT-pLhQOYMGa88@00:23:04]
14. Email craft: reward open, read and click; he keeps under about 200 words; he uses a P.S. (highest clicks); one or two CTAs is safe; plain text for complex or high-ticket offers. [YT-pLhQOYMGa88@00:09:28] [POD-0765@00:09:23] [YT-pLhQOYMGa88@00:17:58] [YT-HwPXctiw_rY@00:54:24]
15. Segment by self-identification at opt-in, accepting about 10% lower opt-in rate; B2C tends to do better Monday or Tuesday, B2B Wednesday. [POD-0765@00:11:41] [YT-pLhQOYMGa88@00:33:14]
16. One action per CTA, three seconds or less in video; on YouTube link in five places (about 30% into the video, description, profile, pinned comment, community post). [POD-230819-08@00:33:48] [LS-eW04CvHKvMo@00:43:17] [POD-0892@00:32:05]
17. Content that attracts everyone brings poor leads: add a triage step with 6-7 screening questions; about two direct-offer posts a week, rest centred on magnets. [LS-eW04CvHKvMo@01:43:00] [LS-eW04CvHKvMo@00:47:09]
18. Aim content at the problems your best buyers already have; early on treat content as nurture and ignore views. [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@06:51:29] [YT-UGEc9-7X3OQ@00:00:57]
19. Review content every 100 pieces and add two checklist points per cycle, since content cannot be split-tested like ads. [POD-0850@00:47:18]
20. Sample-test every list source (a few hundred leads) before buying volume. [POD-230819-10@00:08:42] [YT-aBPWCdaJJqA@00:00:14]
21. Fast deal with whoever already serves your customers just before they would buy from you. [LS-A8p2HCI_23E@00:04:19]
22. Affiliates: run two promotions at once (customers and affiliates), email registered affiliates twice a week, Hormozi personally asks his own audience only two, max three times. [YT-60_7PU9JDIw@00:18:43] [YT-60_7PU9JDIw@00:21:09] [YT-60_7PU9JDIw@00:19:30]
23. Bid on your own brand terms (about 10 minutes, 20-40:1 on spend); cheap attribution via a separate short URL per channel. [YT-XwZH-lOKG9c@00:46:50] [POD-0757@00:07:13]
