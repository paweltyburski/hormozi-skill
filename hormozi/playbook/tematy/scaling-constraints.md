# scaling-constraints

The canon covers the $100M Scaling Roadmap (ten headcount stages, Eight Business Functions, Three Elements of Scaling), Theory of Constraints on the funnel, and the founder role by stage. The recordings add a working kit: supply-versus-demand triage and the questions that expose it, ceiling arithmetic, the more/better/new choice with the "20% change penalty", revenue-band rules (1-1-1 to about $1M, lifetime gross profit at about $3M, the $1-3M "swamp", the $30M founder ceiling), gates for a second location, "under-talent" in place of overexpansion, owner-independence tests, and many coached cases.

## Rules and thresholds

Diagnosis
1. Triage first: supply constrained (too few people to deliver) or demand constrained (customers scarce); never both at once, and the constraint ping-pongs, so stalls come from still working the old one. [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:00:00] [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:30:17] [YT-cnbHgYYHzyE@00:06:46]
2. Supply test, four yes/no questions that likely show a supply problem: could you serve more at standard, are you turning people away, are top slots always sold out, do you run out of inventory or face long shipping delays. [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:31:50]
3. Demand diagnosis runs acquisition channel, lead capture, conversion, then people, process, offer, price; another order is awareness, schedule, show, close, price, churn. [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:33:44] [YT-h6y0nYVZgwE@00:25:19]
4. In 1,026+ workshop businesses, every one was limited by leads, sales, delivery (retention) or profit. [LS-6_CCutkM11g@01:05:28] [LS-B2QbLzHvKbg@01:35:46] A quarterly diagnostic question "why can't we do more of what works" is routed through metrics, market, model, money, manpower. [POD-0945@00:01:40]
5. A business grows to its constraint, not its potential; work that adds potential without throughput is wasted. [POD-0362@00:06:42] [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:27:12] [POD-0590@00:24:24]
6. Fixing the wrong constraint can make things worse; if removing the chosen one does not grow the business, it was picked wrong. [YT-sGv2BTUCcCM@00:34:33] [YT-U_s0ekwPK5g@00:25:29]
7. The constraint is rarely what the founder likes or is good at; if you cannot name it, you are it. [POD-0894b@00:02:32] [LS-QgmRlzh0Y7M@00:07:43] [POD-0747@00:48:39]
8. Once you get it right, the business-level constraint doesn't change that often; sub-constraints chunk up to "more customers" or "customers worth more", and those oscillate. Give the current one four hours a day. [YT-gD0X-PLax5I@00:30:12] [YT-h6y0nYVZgwE@00:18:06]

Ceilings and unit economics
9. Revenue levels off near new customers per month times LTV; subscribers level at monthly signups divided by monthly churn. In practice a business stalls at 80-90% of that figure. [POD-0239@00:03:12] [POD-0447@00:04:59] [YT-XZEjp2mP1U0@00:08:11]
10. If capped, only two levers exist: more units or more per unit. [POD-0463@00:05:54] To go from a $250K ceiling toward $1M a month, fix churn first. [YT-FXzDLLdxsCk@00:09:39]
11. A business that can sell is capped when exits exceed entries; do not scale a leaky business. [YT-CoPs-Bk8M9Y@00:25:36] [YT-unshZobTt6Q@00:45:46]
12. Required LTV:CAC rises with human steps: 3:1 only when attraction, conversion and delivery are automated, then 6:1, 9:1, 12:1+. [YT-i7bLRKwKSms@00:15:01] [YT-A_tx40lNpf8@00:06:42] (see section 5)

14. Feast-or-famine sales mean too little volume over too short a window. [YT-A_tx40lNpf8@00:17:27] Acquisition cost almost always rises, so today's CAC is probably your best. [YT-i7bLRKwKSms@00:17:22]

## Tactics and scripts

1. Ask early: "If we double your lead flow, can you handle it?" splits demand from supply. [YT-OQf2Ba-Lp_4@00:03:57] [POD-0894@00:27:00]
2. Ask "why can't we 10x what already works?" or "what stops you doing ten times more?" and sort answers into ads, hiring, delivery. [YT-KhFlD54nQrY@01:02:18] [YT-oRMG_HpOAN4@00:36:07] [LS-6_CCutkM11g@05:47:59]
3. "Why can't we have 100 salespeople when we have 10?" separates physics from skill gaps; "what breaks first if you double?" [POD-0747@00:23:33] [POD-0894b@00:16:26]
4. Apply the same fixed absolute gain to every funnel step; the one that multiplies output most is the constraint. [POD-0590@00:26:41] [POD-0519@00:02:32]
5. Break a revenue goal into a weekly target, because weeks are steadier than days: $1M a year is about $21K a week. [YT-BSwJQsogah0@00:01:47]
6. Quarterly, grade each department on what, how, who; rank ideas by impact, confidence, ease (he would probably take medium impact at high confidence over big at low); keep one to three moves. [YT-gpKz22P84iM@00:07:23] [YT-uYds0zcAFWM@00:02:59] [POD-0894@00:01:47]
7. Add lead filters only when volume is high and quality poor; accept a 33-50% throughput loss. [YT-Kk6Vdfdwmxw@00:05:16]
8. Seed a new office with an existing high performer; pre-fund a local leader for a new geography. [POD-0861@00:23:14] [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@02:24:25]
9. Beta each play on 20+ sites including bottom performers; about two in three fail. [POD-200710-01@00:21:28]
10. Replace a unique person by splitting the role, one hire per part (took about three). [YT-rc7cxL7ql7Y@00:24:10]
11. Headhunt the missing department head from a firm 2-3x larger. [POD-0609@00:11:36]
12. Customer-funded growth (2-3x acquisition cost back within 30 days) fixes capital, not people. [YT-oRMG_HpOAN4@00:48:29] [YT-k-3PoOT4vOM@00:15:58]
13. For a declining metric, find when growth first flattened and what changed then; often a person was added. [YT-cnbHgYYHzyE@02:24:52]
14. Set end-of-day deadlines in place of end-of-week (claimed about 7x speed). [YT-cnbHgYYHzyE@00:47:01]

## Changes of mind and contradictions

1. Demand versus supply first: one atom says that with both stuck he now says to raise capacity first, reversing his old demand-first advice; another says get demand and cash first, since cash buys capacity. [YT-YONIUpk-kAs@01:11:02] [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:56:06]
2. Required LTV:CAC: a 3:1 floor rising by 3 per human step (to 12:1) versus 20:1 or more for manual models (aim 30:1, minimum 15:1). [YT-A_tx40lNpf8@00:06:42] [YT-i7bLRKwKSms@00:15:01] [YT-uYds0zcAFWM@00:12:22]
3. How long to hold 1-1-1: $1M, about $3M, or below $10M; second channel at $1M a month (he began his own at $4M) versus "from $10M it becomes important" (his opinion). [YT-oRMG_HpOAN4@00:09:47] [YT-neTSqOAMgao@00:45:06] [POD-0439@00:20:25] [YT-okA9Yt2KZuk@00:07:24]
