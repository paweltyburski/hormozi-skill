# retention-ltv

The courses already give the canon frameworks here: Activation Points with the Five M's, Referral Rate minus Churn, GROWTH Onboarding and BAMFAM, the Four R's and Customer Journey, HEAR and the Angry Boat, NPS, Ascension Path, Cohort Analysis, Fee or Feedback, Win-Back, Revenue Churn. The recordings add benchmarks by business type, the churn curve by tenure, ceiling and LTV arithmetic, many onboarding, contact, billing and cancellation tactics, caller cases, and several changes of mind (price cuts, over-delivering, when to fix retention).

## Tactics and scripts

- Take a backup card right after the first; declines are half or more of churn for many and the backup recovers about two of three. Retry declines on paydays, several times a day (about a third recovered). [POD-0183@00:00:21] [POD-0018@00:06:21] [POD-250819-06@00:12:23]
- Tell customers at sign-up that cancelling needs an exit interview; it saved a third to a half of those who agreed. [YT-sPkMHh8zTMI@00:01:36] [POD-250819-07@00:23:12] [YT-h6y0nYVZgwE@00:23:41]
- Call customers the moment they ask to cancel or refund; one in four unhappy callers buying an offer 4x larger recovers all refund losses. [YT-oRMG_HpOAN4@00:29:58] [YT-afbP6sB_Atc@00:33:42]
- Offer freezes instead of cancellations; pre-empt busy, money, travel reasons at the sale. [POD-0192@00:10:11] [POD-0171@00:11:24]
- Let members earn a lifetime discount by passing the average drop-off month. [POD-250819-07@00:21:15]
- Add an annual plan about 16-17% off, announced now. [YT--j8_YCWZ05Q@00:15:14]
- Deliver a first win in 7 days (up to 14) by peeling off the fastest result. [POD-0415@00:24:12] [POD-0742@00:27:17]
