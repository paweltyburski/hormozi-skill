# How Hormozi diagnoses a business

Source: all consulting-case atoms in `ekstrakcje/` (Q&A livestreams, Hotline calls, "for a Stranger" builds, launch-stream calls; 611 atoms, 299 cases). Canon frameworks are named, not re-explained; see `CANON_OUTLINE.md`.

How to read this file. Every substantive line carries one label:

- **Stated**: Hormozi gives it as a general rule or habit in at least one recording. Citations follow as `[recording@timestamp]`.
- **Pattern (n cases)**: the note-taker sees it across n cases; Hormozi does not state it as a rule. Two to five of the cases are cited.

Points made by Leila Hormozi, Sharran Srinivasan, owners, viewers or unclear speakers are not used as his. Advice he gave a single caller appears only as evidence inside a Pattern line. His hedges ("usually", "probably", "his guess") are kept.

- Margin. Pattern (6 cases): a single-digit or low-teens net margin turns the conversation to pricing and the model, not to leads. [YT-BMF2fWHyDrg@00:00:00] [LS-B2QbLzHvKbg@03:35:30] [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@04:58:39] [LS-6_CCutkM11g@05:14:35] [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@00:44:58]

## 2. Finding the constraint

**The frame.**

- Stated: across 1,026+ workshop businesses each was limited by one of four constraints: leads, sales, delivery (retention) or profit (said in a promotional context). [LS-B2QbLzHvKbg@01:35:46] [LS-6_CCutkM11g@01:05:28]
- Stated: ask "what stops you from doing ten times more of what already works?"; the answer names the constraint, and someone must make a strong case before he looks at anything new. [LS-6_CCutkM11g@05:47:59] [YT-QwEb78e5a8Y@00:08:47]
- Stated: the end of "I would, but I can't because…" is the constraint, and it should get all the resources; effort aimed elsewhere raises potential, not throughput. [LS-32fVmwvLmTM@00:12:23]
- Stated: a test has a guaranteed cost and no guaranteed upside, so run one only when you can argue it hits the constraint. [POD-0762@00:20:07]
- Stated: changing a working process that involves people typically costs about 20% of output at first (his observation); do not take that for a possible 5% gain. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:16:44]
- Stated: a process left alone tends to improve by roughly 1-3% a month, so limit yourself to one or two material bets a year. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:20:38]
- Stated: owners often fix a problem in a way that worsens the current one, e.g. more leads into a business that cannot serve them. [YT-OQf2Ba-Lp_4@00:03:57]
- Stated: if you feel you are firefighting with several initiatives spinning, a big obvious decision is probably being avoided; he estimates about 90% of his audience is blocked by hard decisions, not tactics. [LS-ehmBlLNGgsA@00:20:47]

**Demand (leads) branch.**

- Stated: nine times in ten, especially below ~$1M, the answer is "more"; "better" and "more" alternate as you grow; "new" comes only when neither is possible. [POD-0782@00:03:15]
- Stated: if demand-constrained, choose between a new channel, lower CAC (conversion, creative, offer) or simply spending more. [YT-MH-IMJxbUY4@00:04:42]
- Stated: felt volatility (big months, small months) is a symptom of too little volume; below about $1M it usually is. [LS-I0nCuqu7Cac@03:20:49] [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@00:09:38]
- Stated: when lead quality falls as spend rises, he asks whether it is weak creative, too little fresh creative, or spend spilling into colder audiences. [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@01:20:39]
- Stated: when most of a funnel looks right but paid leads fail, he eliminates in order: offer, targeting, then creative and sales process. [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@00:20:36]
- Stated: if spend cannot grow because lifetime gross profit per customer is too low: better offer, cheaper channels, or (his preference) a longer money model. [LS-32fVmwvLmTM@00:10:01]
- Pattern (9 cases): a channel returning well but funded at a token level is read as underspending, not as a marketing failure; first move is to raise spend, often several-fold. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:47:16] [YT--Koq14DPXC4@00:00:00] [LS-32fVmwvLmTM@00:09:11] [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:12:58] [LS-TLgSkSQUql4@00:00:00]

**Sales (conversion) branch.**

- Stated: walk the funnel in daily numbers (leads, bookings, shows, closes, what counts as a lead); the step where volume collapses is the likely constraint. [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@04:56:07]
- Stated: he asks how leads are worked (speed, attempts, personalisation, reminders) because poor lead-working is among the largest revenue holes in small businesses. [YT-sBJppqCeFGI@00:09:19]
- Stated: if speed-to-lead and call attempts are in place and show rate stays low, booking is probably too easy: add friction. [LS-NcpyWPT90RE@01:15:53]
- Stated: "Are this month's leads the same as last month's?" If yes and close rates swing, he bets on weak script adherence. [POD-0933@00:22:48]
- Stated: he judges a workshop by the share of qualified attendees who buy, not overall conversion. [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:11:15]
- Stated: "let me think about it" from an unqualified prospect is time to release; from a qualified one it is an education gap to fix before the call. [LS-QgmRlzh0Y7M@00:16:00]

**Delivery (retention) branch.**

- Stated: when unsure, first collect data on what precedes the problem (low consumption before churn); nine in ten owners do not. [YT-QwEb78e5a8Y@00:06:27]
- Stated: split conversion, churn and LTV by buyer type, early actions, channel and avatar to find the customers worth targeting. [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:36:16]
- Stated: a supplier's churn mirrors the volatility of its customers; to make volatile small customers sticky, sell what they already do not cancel. [LS-I0nCuqu7Cac@02:43:53]
- Pattern (4 cases): with high churn he forbids scaling ads until activation or retention is fixed. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@01:04:53] [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:33:38] [LS-6_CCutkM11g@05:31:48] [YT-n6SHLmKcY0E@00:00:45]
- Pattern (2 cases): where churn is structural to the category (weight loss, digital memberships) he accepts it and pushes the front end instead. [LS-lidFtb1Ga0o@00:55:13] [LS-DfXy_PzlC0o@02:28:15]

**Supply (capacity, talent, owner time) branch.**

- Stated: if recruiting is the constraint, any work that is not recruiting is not working on the business. [POD-0892@00:26:07]
- Stated: when demand and capacity are roughly balanced, get the demand and cash first; the cash buys the capacity. [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:56:06]
- Stated: one-off roles go to recruiters; a repeatable role gets its own funnel and its own numbers (cost to acquire talent, gross profit per employee). [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:50:20]
- Stated: supply-constrained with a high close rate means double or triple the price, tested with the same pitch. [LS-I0nCuqu7Cac@04:05:03]
- Pattern (8 cases): supply-constrained service firms are told to run recruiting like customer acquisition, with referral or recruiter pay sized to the gross profit a hire brings; where close rates are high (3 of the 8) he adds a price rise. [YT-QwEb78e5a8Y@00:25:09] [LS--HbwUdqUcEs@00:29:21] [LS-I0nCuqu7Cac@04:01:57] [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@00:41:34] [LS-_ooj0V4hpgw@00:44:11]
- Pattern (3 cases): when the owner's own time is the bottleneck, he starts with a time study, then delegates and trains on work staff are already paid for. [LS-tnDC4ZJ0oy8@01:34:38] [LS-DfXy_PzlC0o@00:29:53] [LS-6_CCutkM11g@06:24:33]
- Stated: if the same quarterly objective is missed twice and the what and how still look right, it is usually a "who" problem. [POD-0782@00:05:01]
- Stated (his hunch): when one model is profitable at some locations and not others, the lever is probably the people. [YT-sBJppqCeFGI@00:14:36]

**Cash and profit branch.**

- Stated: he cites a study that 82% of businesses fail on cash flow; the money model should make 30-day gross profit exceed twice the cost of acquiring and serving a customer. [LS-B2QbLzHvKbg@00:39:06]
- Stated: when cash is needed fast, look to current customers (prepay), then past customers (credit toward a bigger package), then leads. [LS-0RjIw9M1j28@01:23:21]
- Stated: pay the working owner a market wage in the numbers, or margins and expansion returns are overstated. [YT--Koq14DPXC4@00:17:44]
- Pattern (5 cases): when the first transaction does not cover the cost of starting a customer, or customers pay slowly, he restructures the upfront offer (setup fee, prepay, move-in fee) or finances it (receivables line, leasing, equity) rather than changing the model. ### Made-to-order and manufacturing (3 cases)

- Pattern (2 cases): he checks margin and repeat purchase before marketing.
