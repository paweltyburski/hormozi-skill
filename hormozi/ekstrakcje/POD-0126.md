### POD-0126#01 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Ignorance tax is the income you forgo each year by lacking the skill to earn much more.
detail: He took it from a speaker who told an audience member earning $50,000 that he pays life $950,000 a year for not knowing how to make a million. Not learning when information is in front of you is, in effect, choosing the tax.
numbers: Example: $1M potential minus $50,000 earned = $950,000 yearly.

### POD-0126#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: product-delivery | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:52
claim: Per his words to his legacy gym owners, run the retention system you already have for 12 weeks before picking up another book on retention.
detail: He is annoyed when top owners read general books on retention without having run his retention system, which he says brought every gym that installed it to under 4% churn within 12 weeks. A general book needs translating, applying and testing over weeks, and may still fail, whereas a specific system is already tested.
numbers: Churn below 4% within 12 weeks (his claim); 12 weeks of consistent execution.
### POD-0126#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:41
claim: After a base level of security, put surplus money into the business and into adjacent skills, not into markets you do not understand.
detail: His business was growing about 400% a year, which no stock matches, and he describes market returns of roughly 10% a year, maybe over 20 years, as a game he does not want to play. He spends his money on courses that teach a skill, and says airport-type books are too general to give a return. He says to buy the skill set adjacent to what you have; this goes with his talent stacking video.
numbers: Business growth ~400% per year (his statement); about $700,000 spent on information over time (his estimate).
### POD-0126#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:07

detail: He realised he taught sales and sales management well but gave marketing as copy-and-paste steps, never the thinking behind choices such as image, headline, sub-hook, funnel structure and file compression. He decided to share the theory so owners can innovate without copying.
