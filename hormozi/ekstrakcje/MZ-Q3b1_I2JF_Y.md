### MZ-Q3b1_I2JF_Y#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: You cannot both fit in and be exceptional; pick which discomfort you prefer.
detail: Fitting in brings internal conflict; standing out brings external conflict. Most people have the potential but suppress it to be accepted, and innovation starts with dropping a rule that never made sense.

### MZ-Q3b1_I2JF_Y#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:52
claim: Accept your own peculiarities early rather than conforming to a social archetype.
detail: Older people report less drama because they have no time for it; he starts now. Wearing uncomfortable boots to fit an image is a micro-rebellion against yourself.

### MZ-Q3b1_I2JF_Y#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00

detail: People anchor on who you were and want to shove you back into the box, so expect awkward conversations when you change.
