### MZ-0QzS_rOwKHE#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:13
claim: He maps response to hardship on four dimensions: fortitude, tolerance, resilience and adaptability.
detail: Fortitude is how much bad stuff can happen before your behaviour changes. Tolerance is how low you sink once you cross the threshold (shorter with staff, or something worse). Resilience is how long it takes to return to baseline. Adaptability asks whether you end up better, the same or worse than before. He wrote it down to decide how to show up after a loss.

### MZ-0QzS_rOwKHE#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:21
claim: In a bad stretch, solve only the part you can do now and keep daily routines, chipping away in bites.
detail: With nine open lawsuits and a spouse in a painful injury about to have surgery, he more or less followed an approach from a Bill Ackman interview: you cannot solve it in one day, so take one bite at a time and look for progress every month or two. He kept working, making content, helping businesses and training because those things made him feel better, and changed as little of his life as possible.
numbers: Nine open lawsuits in Q1 2025; spouse's recovery about 18 months.
### MZ-0QzS_rOwKHE#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:34
claim: Suffering is not proof of love, and there is no single correct way to grieve.
detail: He rejected the assumption that how much one suffers measures how much one cares, and asked how a loss could make him better rather than permanently worse.

### MZ-0QzS_rOwKHE#04 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:18
claim: The $100M Offers book launch used a free-or-99-cent book plus a course to show the power of an offer plus heavy advertising, without monetising it directly.
detail: He sold the offers book at a token price with a course bundled, advertised it with the methods in the book, and had a money model behind it, which let the weekend gross about $106M. He did not monetise the launch itself, only wanted to demonstrate the combination. It was a multi-year project.
numbers: About $100M-$106M over the launch weekend; book priced at 99 cents.
