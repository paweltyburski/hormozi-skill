### MZ-IYjn0NhjLGg#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: You do not need to go through hell, only to learn many skills, and learning them without a guide hurts because mistakes are costly.
detail: A mentor or parent who already did it would cut the pain. Hard childhoods do not explain success since many with them fail.

### MZ-IYjn0NhjLGg#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:00
claim: Winners share a short chain: behaviours that produce activity, products people love and keep buying, a price with high gross profit, and reinvestment for disproportionate growth.
detail: Look for the few things common to both sides of a comparison, and ignore variables present in both successes and failures.
