### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#01 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:11
claim: Add 99 cents to every menu transaction and a 3% card fee in fine print at the bottom of the menu.
detail: Both are small increases customers rarely object to. The 99 cents is added to each ticket (example item at $16); the card fee is printed at the foot of the menu and applies across all transactions. Together he estimates roughly 4-5% extra revenue, which is material for a restaurant.
numbers: +$0.99 per transaction; +3% card fee; combined about 4-5% more revenue.
applies_to: Restaurant in a tourist town.

### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:11
claim: Order menu items inside each section by absolute gross profit in dollars, highest first, and box the top three as can't-miss dishes.
detail: Sort by dollars of gross profit, not by price or margin percentage. The boxed top three answer the visitor's question of what to eat on a single weekend and steer tourists toward the best earners.
applies_to: Restaurants with many one-time visitors.

### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Three-part alcohol strategy, since liquor carries the highest gross margin on the menu.
detail: 1) Print a wine or spirit pairing next to each dish. 2) Have the mixologist invent a drink of the week and let the whole team taste it, so staff can recommend it honestly. 3) Put the drink special on a table A-frame.

### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:21
claim: Script and drill four table conversations daily: first visit, drink special opener, pairing prompt, review request.
detail: Great chains give the same interaction every time. The drink opener asks whether they want to try the special so one or two drinks reach the whole table. The pairing prompt fires when an order has no drink, unless the guest says they don't drink. A napkin marker flags first-time guests so staff can set up a return visit.

### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#05 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:43
claim: Capture reviews when the bill arrives: manager asks, then leaves a QR code to the review page, and the server earns a spiff.
detail: After the guest says the meal was great, the manager mentions the staff worked hard and leaves a QR code straight to the review site. Frame the spiff as meaningful to the server. Done daily, thousands of reviews and a higher average score in about six months.
numbers: Thousands of reviews within roughly six months at daily cadence.

### MZ-_YQA9f1g5Jw#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:33
claim: Wendy's opening trick: the first 500 guests get a keychain that earns unlimited free Frosty cones, creating 500 regulars.
detail: Groups arrive with one keychain holder, and everyone else buys fries. Adapted for a sit-down restaurant: a small margherita pizza, cheap to make, free to the first 500 for a year.
numbers: 500 keychains, free item limited to one year.
