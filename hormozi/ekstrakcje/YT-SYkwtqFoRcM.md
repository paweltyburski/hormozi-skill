### YT-SYkwtqFoRcM#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Hormozi's personal approach before quitting a job: about six months of living costs saved, side income matching the job, and that income shown to be steady for months.
detail: Live well below the job's pay first. He says a step people may overlook is sustaining matched income for about six months; matching it once then dropping sharply would be stressful and would affect his work and selling. If the side business earns half your pay in 4 hours a week, ask honestly whether the other 36 hours would close the gap.
numbers: Savings about 6 months of expenses (his comfort level); matched income sustained about 6 months.
### YT-SYkwtqFoRcM#02 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:43
claim: Hormozi left his job by running a "charity" online fitness programme for friends, then asking them to pay him instead.
detail: About 20 friends paid $200 a month for remote fitness and nutrition programming, all of which he donated. After six months of steady payments he asked them to pay him; all agreed, replacing his $50,000 salary and funding his early gym.
numbers: 20 clients x $200/month = about $4,000/month; 6 months before asking.

### YT-SYkwtqFoRcM#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:51
claim: He saved $50,000 in two years on a $50,000 salary, living on about a third of income, including a $400 rented bedroom.
detail: The cushion came before going all in. The landlord, a gym acquaintance, gave the discount in exchange for shared Sunday meal prep.
numbers: $50,000 saved over 2 years; rent $400/month.

### YT-SYkwtqFoRcM#04 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:19
claim: Choose a side business you already have skill in, such as doing your job as a contractor; the first pick need only be directionally right.
detail: Such firms hold little enterprise value but teach hiring, firing, selling, invoicing, payments and setting up an LLC. First businesses are rarely last ones; they are stepping stones.

### YT-SYkwtqFoRcM#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:36
claim: Without a fallback career like Bezos or Zuckerberg had, respect the risk and copy a lower-risk path.
detail: Going to the big leagues before being ready is a common failure. Cut overhead (live at home or share housing) so part-time work covers costs and the remaining days go to the business.
