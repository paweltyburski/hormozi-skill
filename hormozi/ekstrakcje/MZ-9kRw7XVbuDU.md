### MZ-9kRw7XVbuDU#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team, verticals | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A luxury home builder selling about six $1.5M homes a year himself wanted a top salesperson to free his time.
detail: Revenue $10M, about $2M take-home, target $30M. Operations and building are not the problem; he can personally handle 18 sales a year but wants his time back. A formal letter to competitors' salespeople failed.
numbers: Revenue $10M; take-home about $2M; 6 homes at $1.5M; goal $30M.

### MZ-9kRw7XVbuDU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:03
claim: Every business is demand-constrained either for customers or for talent, and both are solved by advertising to the right people.
detail: Leaders and top players are found mainly by outreach, the same way customers are.

### MZ-9kRw7XVbuDU#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: Recruit top producers by expanding the region, offering relocation, and using LinkedIn outreach at much higher volume.
detail: Reach out to other luxury construction salespeople and look at top producers in nearby areas. Most owners fail from insufficient volume of outreach, not from the wrong message.
