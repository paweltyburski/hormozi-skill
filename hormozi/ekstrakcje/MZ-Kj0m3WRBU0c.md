### MZ-Kj0m3WRBU0c#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Motivation is the opposite of deprivation: drive comes from a felt lack you want to fill.
detail: Pinning down one cause of drive is not possible because many variables shaped it. Hunger and sleepiness are physical versions; a desire for achievement works the same way.

### MZ-Kj0m3WRBU0c#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Ask "what does my life or my work mean to me?" instead of "what is the meaning of my life?" to get a tangible answer on drive.
detail: The answer is rarely one thing; family, friends, team and company usually all appear. His own aim is to do things he likes with people he likes.

### MZ-Kj0m3WRBU0c#03 | EVOLUTION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:27
claim: After earning enough around age 31, he took a year off, found it did not suit him, and adopted "hard work is the goal" as his operating mode.
detail: He has run that mode for about five years and is now in a reflective post-launch period.
numbers: about 1 year off at 31; about 5 years of hard-work mode.

### MZ-Kj0m3WRBU0c#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: People crave novelty, so engineer contrast into the routine: a few weekly or monthly rhythms keep you from burning out.
detail: When working constantly you want a day off, and when resting you miss work. The middle path takes more skill than either extreme, and the right cadence differs per person; for him it is still more work than most do.
