### MZ-y1Mh5b0_OMI#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Planet Fitness removes the equipment that heavy users need, so the members who cost the most never join or stay.
detail: His reading: most sign-ups (he says 92%) never want to use the gym, and the costly minority are real users. Dropping heavy weights, squat racks and chalk repels roughly 80% of actual users. The gym can then be smaller and sign up far more people who will not come.
numbers: 92% of members do not want to use the gym (his claim); removing three items repels about 80% of real users.

### MZ-y1Mh5b0_OMI#02 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A small perk such as a monthly pizza gives non-users a reason to feel the $10 is covered, so they keep paying.
detail: The low price plus a freebie creates the story that one visit a month pays for the membership. Members stay for the idea of going someday, and most never even come for the pizza.
numbers: $10 per month membership.

### MZ-y1Mh5b0_OMI#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: The model only works at volume: an average location carries about 10,000 members at $10 a month.
detail: He says the economics need roughly 10,000 active members per location. In a city of about 30,000 people the brand keeps cycling through the same 10,000 as people sign up, lapse and return.
numbers: about 10,000 members per location at $10/month (about $100,000 per month); city of 30,000 people.

### MZ-y1Mh5b0_OMI#04 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Make leaving easy and friendly, because a volume model lives on word of mouth and returning customers.
detail: Most gyms enforce contracts and send leavers to collections, which breeds bad reviews. Planet Fitness lets people cancel by phone immediately and invites them back, so they are not afraid to rejoin when motivation returns. Staff would rather not argue even when a member rarely comes.
