### POD-0536#03 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:13

detail: Misses (unhappy), those who hit and wanted to continue (the vast majority; success just under 80%), and those who hit and asked for the money. The point was to teach that change is a lifelong process, not six weeks.
numbers: Price $300; target 20 pounds in 6 weeks; success rate just under 80%.

### POD-0536#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: retention-ltv | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:43
claim: At the halfway check-in, ask whether the person will still want to change after reaching the goal and who they want to do it with, then credit the $300 toward a year.
detail: Sequence: confirm progress (10 pounds down), ask how much more they want to lose, note nobody has a perfect body, contrast doing it alone (what failed before) with doing it with him, then credit the challenge money toward the year and celebrate the win. Some committed a year before finishing.
numbers: Halfway point about 10 pounds; credit equals $300.
