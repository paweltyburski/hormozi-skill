### POD-0113#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The average gym loses about 10% of its recurring members each month; transient members from paid marketing stay only three to four months.
detail: Benchmark reports by gym software vendors show the average lifetime of a member at about 10 months, skewed by owner-operated gyms with grandfathered, lower-paying original members. Owners who have moved to ownership see shorter lives from marketing-sourced members.
numbers: 10% monthly churn; average member lifetime about 10 months; marketing-sourced members 3-4 months.
applies_to: Small gyms, boot camps, studios with 50-100 members.

### POD-0113#02 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:34
claim: A three-part routine cuts churn from 10% to under 3%: reach anyone not yet in by Wednesday, check in every 14 days, and send a monthly handwritten card.
detail: Contact the absent (he guesses 20%) by Wednesday of the week. Check in with everyone twice a month. Write a monthly handwritten card inviting them to an event or offering a friend a free month. Cutting churn from 10% to 3% lifts lifetime value about 3.3 times.
numbers: Churn 10% to 3% = 3.3x lifetime value; reach-outs every 14 days; contact by Wednesday.

### POD-0113#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:13
claim: The routine costs about $10 per member per month, including labour.
detail: A card takes about a minute and a half, 20 cards an hour at $15 an hour, roughly $2 with stamp and paper. The remaining about $8 is 40-45 minutes of a trainer's time per month for reach-outs and texting.
numbers: About $2 per card; $10/month all in; labour $15/hour; 20 cards per hour.
### POD-0113#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:04
claim: Spending about $140 on retention across a member's life adds roughly $1,500 of revenue, about 10 to 1, on a $150-a-month membership.
detail: Without the routine a member stays about four months ($600). With it, about 13-14 months, ten more months at $150, for about $140 total labour. No dependence on ad platform or headlines. Treat $10 of each membership as the retention budget.
numbers: $150/month; 4 months to about 14 months; $140 cost vs $1,500 extra revenue.

### POD-0113#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:33
claim: Gym members mainly pay for accountability and a relationship, since the workouts alone are available cheaper elsewhere.
detail: Big chains offer the programme at about $29 a month with better facilities. People who keep using something almost never quit it, so the goal is to keep them attending.
numbers: Competing chain price about $29/month.
