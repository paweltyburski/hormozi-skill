### YT-SmiOK8Yun4s#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: BANT | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:39
claim: A service lead magnet can be restricted to prospects who pass BANT, because giving it to everyone is pure cost.
detail: It need not be an ebook or video or automated; there is no rule against choosing recipients. Offering a free jet ride only makes sense if the person could afford a jet. Qualify on budget, authority, need, timing before gifting it. He adds that publishing weak lead magnets is a mistake: most people never buy, so the market judges you on your free work.

### YT-SmiOK8Yun4s#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:05
claim: Move the repeated information from calls into pre-sell material to cut calls from several to one and from 60-90 minutes to 15-20.
detail: A decision takes a moment; the calls exist to deliver information. Moving the most important elements to the front halves the call count: cutting from two calls to one doubles sales for the same team. Companies with three to six-call processes can often get to one or two. This works for conversations or emails alike.
numbers: Two calls to one = 2x sales for same team; call length 60-90 minutes to 15-20.

### YT-SmiOK8Yun4s#19 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:50

### YT-SmiOK8Yun4s#20 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:16
claim: Make continuity the default next step using an opt-out frame, assuming the close, and offer more of or more help with what they just bought.
detail: He cites a country that got far more organ donors by making donation the default (about 70% against under 10% elsewhere). People follow what looks like the norm. So continuity is positioned as the natural next phase after onboarding ("make it stick"), not repitched. His two reliable upsells are more of the thing they bought, or more help with it.
numbers: Default effect: ~70% opt-in under opt-out versus under 10% under opt-in (donor example).

### YT-SmiOK8Yun4s#21 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:41
claim: The point of maximum profit lies beyond the point of maximum conversion, so keep raising price until profit, not conversion, falls.
detail: For luxury goods price signals value (a Veblen good). Example logic: if price triples and yeses halve, you earn more. Luxury buyers want the best, so without a premium path they default to the lowest tier. He had her keep raising price until gross profit fell.
numbers: Illustration: 3x price with half the buyers = 1.5x revenue.
