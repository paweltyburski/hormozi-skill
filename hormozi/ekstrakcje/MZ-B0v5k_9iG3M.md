### MZ-B0v5k_9iG3M#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: An 18-year-old salesperson was offered 5% of a friend's pest control firm if he brought in $2M revenue and asked whether to partner.
detail: The firm made about $200K per year, the friend owned 100%, and the teen had zero equity today. Hormozi asked what the partner has that he lacks (answer: aggression, with no clear track record) and what stops him doing the $2M under his own name. The teen valued avoiding fulfilment. Hormozi called 5% a stake, not a partnership.
numbers: $200K current revenue; 5% equity at $2M; goal asked about: $10M per year.
applies_to: Very early career, first venture

### MZ-B0v5k_9iG3M#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Partner only with someone who brings money, skill, time, experience or distribution that you lack.
detail: If two partners are alike, one is unnecessary. Two people may even rate the same effort differently (a "10 out of 10" for one is a 4 for the other), which is where conflict starts.
numbers: Five contributions: money, time, experience, skill, distribution or network.

### MZ-B0v5k_9iG3M#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Build a business first and become friends later, since starting from friendship is hard.
detail: He paraphrases Rockefeller: great businesses rarely come out of great friendships, but great friendships often come out of great businesses.

### MZ-B0v5k_9iG3M#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:21
claim: A beginner should spend about four years inside a company growing from roughly 15 to 100 people, aiming to learn rather than earn.
detail: The reason is that someone else takes the risk while you see what doing it right looks like. Continue selling if you wish, but make sure you learn the other functions. If the goal is $50M, owning 5% of a $200K firm will not get there; first define the goal, then pick the highest-odds path.
numbers: About 4 years; company size about 15 to 100 headcount.
