### POD-0235#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Brooke Castillo's model: circumstances (objective facts) lead to thoughts, which create feelings, then actions, then results.
detail: A circumstance must be court-provable (the exact words said, not "she yelled"). Castillo said business is not stressful and that thoughts about business create the stress; Hormozi notes the thoughts are our own and circumstances do not create them.
### POD-0235#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Two entrepreneurs faced the same circumstance (a spouse not doing something); one felt rejected, the other felt more loved.
detail: Different thoughts about identical facts produced different feelings and behaviour.

### POD-0235#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:10
claim: When stressed, ask what thought produced the feeling, whether it fits the facts, whether it serves you and whether it is true.
detail: Then test a replacement thought and see what feeling, action and result follow. Hormozi says that stepping out to observe the emotion makes it lose a little bit of control over you.
### POD-0235#04 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:58
claim: Stress about software onboarding led Hormozi through a chain of thoughts to a fear of losing self-respect if he failed.
detail: Circumstance: onboarding was not activating users fast enough. Thoughts: "I can't figure it out" then fear his father would stop respecting him. His father said effort mattered, not outcome, but no relief came until he saw he respected himself only conditionally.

### POD-0235#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:10
claim: He believes the company has stalled at about $30M because he runs it from fear of failure, and the owner is the limiter.
detail: Castillo told him fear and pain got him to $30M, but he cannot pain his way to $100M; decisions need to be fear-free. The mastermind had eight people with combined revenue above $500M a year, and his was the second smallest.
numbers: Stuck at roughly $30M for three years; mastermind of 8 companies over $500M combined.

### POD-0235#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:40
claim: Respect yourself for effort, not outcome, since only you can judge your effort.
detail: He respected others' daily hustle but judged himself only on results.
