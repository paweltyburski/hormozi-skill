### MZ-T3qqM4Lx9xM#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: An arm-health program for baseball players ($830K revenue) sells identical content to individuals and to teams, and teams are worth far more.
detail: Individuals pay $45 a month or $450 a year; LTV $360 against CAC about $120. Teams (packaged as 20-25 accounts) have an LTV of $6-8K (three years in, still early) with CAC unknown. Team revenue is 60%, individuals 40%.
numbers: Individual LTV $360, CAC about $120, price $450 per year; team LTV $6-8K; revenue split 60/40.

### MZ-T3qqM4Lx9xM#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:06
claim: Focus on the team customer: even if CAC is 3x higher, LTV 20x higher is a better game, and teams stay sticky while players graduate.
detail: No need to shut the individual line; keep it for brand. Many small firms land one enterprise account worth dozens of small ones and then should do more of that.
numbers: CAC maybe 3x higher; LTV about 20x higher (his estimate).
