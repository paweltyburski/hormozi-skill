### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:24
claim: Do not ask for the sale until the buyer states why they are here and owns the problem; "just want information" is not an answer.
detail: Ask what the information is meant to solve. In reviews he finds reps eight minutes in not knowing the prospect's clients, revenue, profit or main pain, only talking about their own firm. Train reps on the prospect, not the product.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Three Pillar Pitch | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:19
claim: In the pitch, show that the buyer is missing one piece of a three-part solution and that this is not their fault.
detail: Anyone who calls with a problem they have not solved must be missing a key, so the seller holds the upper hand unless they lose the frame. Fitness example: workouts alone fail without nutrition and accountability. Stories break beliefs, not jargon about modules.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#07 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:18:10
claim: Use short anecdotal analogies per pillar, such as childhood tooth-brushing for accountability or looking forward to a favourite TV show for nutrition.
detail: External accountability becomes internal habit; people do not need willpower for things they look forward to. The point of each is to agree on the goal, not to describe macros or coaching calls.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:20:32
claim: Sell the vacation, not the plane ride, and offer different routes to the same destination as service tiers.
detail: Buyers do not want modules, meal plans, funnels or ad targeting, which are the airport queues. Sell the destination, then price the route: swim, boat, regular flight or private jet with direct, easy arrival.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#09 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-team | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:48
claim: Memorize two things: the stories about your product and your replies to the main concerns; the rest of the call is milestones.
detail: Price objections mean value was not made clear; decision-maker objections usually point to an earlier mistake. By this point you are in the "red zone", so prepare these replies in advance.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#10 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:24:58
claim: For a decision-maker concern, lean on past agreements and use a delayed close with a down payment.
detail: The partner already dislikes the problem, so remind the buyer of that. Offer a deposit now with the rest later, promising to tear up the contract if the partner objects. Sometimes act first and ask forgiveness later, depending on ticket size.

### YT-q32-l3Yoqg4#11 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:25
claim: For a stall such as "I need to think about it", ask for the main concern and think it through together; ask what they fear most.
detail: Stalls come from fear of mistakes and a wish to let life decide. Ask what they are most afraid of (often being taken for their money) and address it.
