### YT-0mqqbuM9sAk#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: outreach | fw: List Building (Scrape, Buy, Elbow Grease) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Gym Launch's first customers came only after a scraped-list ad funnel failed and he messaged leads one by one.
detail: An assistant scraped a fitness franchise's member emails, he built a lookalike audience and ran a webinar nobody watched. None of ~80 opt-ins bought. He then found each person manually, messaged them on Facebook, and a few paid to have him fly to their gym for a turnaround.

### YT-0mqqbuM9sAk#02 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:35
claim: To improve a community's results, find the best performers on the weak metric, interview about 20, and roll out only the few practices they all share.
detail: Start from a current customer problem (churn, margin, leads, close rate), form a hypothesis, survey for the best numbers, hold a call with ~20 and take notes. Do not adopt everything said; pick the few common things and present them as worth the cost of change.

### YT-0mqqbuM9sAk#05 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:40
claim: Supplements sold in the gym covered acquisition cost without extra delivery work because they ship direct from a central operation.
detail: Pitch to owners: sell every member supplements and product margin pays for acquisition. Brand sold only by gyms, priced about 30% higher on the public site, with a selling system and a subscription. Rolled out to 10 gyms, then 20-30 a month, then everyone.
