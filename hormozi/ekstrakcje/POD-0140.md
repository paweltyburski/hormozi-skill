### POD-0140#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Consistency beats intensity: he replaced the company tenet "go the extra mile" with "do the boring work".
detail: Intense bursts followed by rests are unsustainable and unpredictable. Daily repetition compounds, makes the business predictable, lets you change one variable at a time, and raises margin through steady flows. Compare a trainer who always arrives on time and gives a solid session to one who is late some days but great.

### POD-0140#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:38
claim: Grit has two parts: handling failure as learning and refusing shiny objects; effort counts twice, once for skill and once for output.
detail: From a book on grit he cites an eight-question scale. He now reads a constant stream of new ventures as a sign of lack of focus. Hardest days are 17, 67 and 127, when boredom sets in, not day two.
numbers: Boredom spikes at days 17, 67, 127.

### POD-0140#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:26
claim: Break a vague wish to earn more into funnel steps, as in this $10,000 example.
detail: Ask what they earn now, set the extra, then work back: two extra sales a week at a 50% close rate means four appointments; seven appointments set to get four to show; at a 70% set rate that is ten leads a day; at $15 a lead that is $150 a day. His point: do the boring work of making the ads and spending that.
numbers: +$10,000/month; 2 sales/week; 50% close; 7 set for 4 shows; 70% set rate; $15/lead; $150/day.

### POD-0140#05 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:13:26
claim: A new sales manager raised each rep's productivity about 2.5x by changing nothing except one-on-one coaching.
detail: He kept the processes and pay, listened to each rep's recordings, wrote improvement notes, role-played and reviewed footage together on tonality, questions asked and talking too much. The company then had its best month ever for sales and lowest turnover (term unclear in audio).
numbers: 2.5x productivity per rep.
