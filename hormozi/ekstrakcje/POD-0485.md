### POD-0485#08 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:13:06
claim: In his opinion, there are three main skills in business: sell (get people to pay), build (the product or service), and lead (people who deliver for you).
detail: Mastering one lets you earn well; all three makes you very hard to beat. Opportunity is found by looking for problems people hate, inefficiencies and daily nuisances. You do not need an original idea: cleaning, lawn care or roofing companies can reach very large size by selling, delivering consistently and leading better than peers.

### POD-0485#12 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:18
claim: Reverse-engineer a revenue goal into daily appointments by dividing by customer lifetime value, then by close rate.
detail: Steps: pick the revenue target; divide by lifetime value (not price) to get unit sales; divide per year to get per day; divide by close rate to get appointments needed; then list the lead sources and choose the one you can do. Dog example: $1M at $1,000 lifetime value needs 1,000 sales, about 3 a day; closing 1 in 2 means 6 appointments a day.
numbers: $1M / $1,000 LTV = 1,000 sales/year ≈ 3/day; 50% close rate = 6 appointments/day.
