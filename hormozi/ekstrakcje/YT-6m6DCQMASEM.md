### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: pricing | fw: Sell to Goal | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:51
claim: A weight-loss coach with three packages was told to sell to the goal using a weekly price times weeks needed.
detail: Packages: 3, 6 and 12 months at $2K, $3K and $5K; the most common goal is losing 30 pounds, and the six-month package sells best. Advice: run a diagnostic sale; take the gap between current and goal weight, divide by a safe pace of about a pound a week plus a maintenance period, and multiply by a weekly rate. Selling to the goal converts more; pair it with a "keep working until you hit it" condition. Longer maintenance extends the contract.
numbers: Example: 40 pounds at ~1 lb/week = 40 weeks x $129 per week (about $5,160 total).
applies_to: Fitness and coaching programmes

### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:59

### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#04 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: pricing, unit-economics | fw: Implied Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:33
claim: A severance-package service with low-salary leads was told to keep Meta ads and add a performance-pricing component before changing platforms.
detail: Owner spends about $10K a month on Meta, qualifies leads by phone, and wants senior, higher-paid prospects, thinking of LinkedIn or Twitter. Hormozi: LinkedIn suits executives, but do not turn off what works; test on top. ### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#05 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Game Tape Review | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:35

### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:54
claim: People improve at sales by watching many recordings of good sellers, then doing immediate corrective role-play on one script section at a time.
detail: Correct the line, say try again, repeat until it becomes automatic; then move to the next section. Once memorised, the rep can listen on the call instead of planning the next sentence. He estimates about 95% of companies cannot train sales measurably.

### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:09
claim: To turn around a sales team, make everyone follow the script exactly, then edit the script, and cut it to the simplest form.
detail: Without a process each rep sells their own way, some over-promise, and customer service suffers; training is pointless if nobody follows the script. ### YT-6m6DCQMASEM#09 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent, finance-exit | fw: Eight Business Functions | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:15:44
claim: A used-car dealer with too many problems was told he was missing about three people, with a finance hire first.
detail: Eight years in, former pipe welder, stock grown from 15 to 30 to 45 to about 100 cars, plus a service shop. Commercial vans earn more per deal.
