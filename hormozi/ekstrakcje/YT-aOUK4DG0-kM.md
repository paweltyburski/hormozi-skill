### YT-aOUK4DG0-kM#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Choose the next hire by listing where your time goes and picking the cheapest-to-buy activity that eats the biggest share of it.
detail: Split your day into blocks (admin, sales, marketing, billing, customer-facing). Value each block by how much money it makes you, not by market wage. Outsource first what takes the most time yet earns the least, and keep the money-making work such as sales. Early on, play defense: hand off tasks that can hurt the business least if done badly.

### YT-aOUK4DG0-kM#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:27
claim: The goal of a hire is to buy back your time at the lowest price, so you can climb to higher-revenue work until you only make decisions.
detail: Ask how to get the most time back for the least money, and repeat that over and over. The top of the climb is making decisions, then setting a shared vision that others use as the lens for their own decisions.

### YT-aOUK4DG0-kM#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:27
claim: Hire against a real constraint; many owners solve problems that are not actually constraints in their business.
detail: Before hiring, confirm the problem you are paying to remove really exists and limits you.

### YT-aOUK4DG0-kM#04 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:03
claim: A bootstrapped founder's first two hires are usually a generalist admin helper (often a believer such as a spouse) and a fulfilment assistant.
detail: The first-follower type works harder than a normal employee because they are invested. Fulfilment help only works once the service is productized: narrow the customer and outcome so someone else can repeat the result. Expect to work overtime at first, running the business while building that foundation.
applies_to: Founder-owned, unfunded businesses (his estimate: 99% of them).
