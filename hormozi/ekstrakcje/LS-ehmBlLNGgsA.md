### LS-ehmBlLNGgsA#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: Grow through subtraction: cutting hurts now and adding hurts later, so when a business is stuck it is almost always doing too much.
detail: Hormozi: people default to solving by addition because it feels easier today. Running the business consumes most resources; growth comes only from the excess, and constant new initiatives eat that excess until even the core suffers. Businesses behave more like biology than machines: growth, bloat, cutting, efficiency, then growth again (restated at 00:25:38). The boss's job is to cull regularly; if you do not, the market will do it for you.
