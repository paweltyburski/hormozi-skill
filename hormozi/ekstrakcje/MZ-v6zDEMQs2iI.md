### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:56
claim: He built his selling skill from volume: about 20 in-person consults a day for four or five years, and about 4,000 sales before he knew sales training existed.
detail: Cheap Facebook leads from 2013 supplied appointments. Sales stacked up for him across his locations, so he would visit one gym and close about 25 sales that day, then the next location. He found out about the sales-training world only when he wanted to scale a sales team.
numbers: 20 consults a day for 4-5 years; about 25 sales per location day; about 4,000 sales before any training.

claim: In the turnarounds he sat at a gym's front door with a folding table and ran appointments every 30 minutes, doing the same pitch repeatedly.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#03 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:42
claim: The whole sales training for turnaround reps lasted about 60 minutes and was built to make them close, not to teach sales.
detail: Structure: open with a joke (the "world's shortest tour"), ask what brought them in and what they struggle with, and if they say they only want information, ask whether they are collecting information across many options or solving a problem, so the problem is admitted before moving on. About six or seven key questions each carry four or five prepared offshoots.
numbers: 60 minutes of training; 8 reps each taking about 20 appointments a day; 6-7 key questions with 4-5 offshoots each.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:28
claim: Prepare for the full list of objections, because with enough repetition nothing a prospect says should be new.
detail: His gym list: needs to think, unsure the workouts fit, body type or a health condition, talk to spouse, visit other gyms, wants time, has not got the card, too expensive. He notes there are not many of them and each can be walked through without sounding like hard selling.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:24
claim: Compress and delete sales material, because few things drive a purchase and salespeople more often talk themselves out of sales than close.
detail: He retells a shoe-store story: customers only want to know it is comfortable, looks good and is affordable. His teams analyse call data across portfolio companies and shorten scripts.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#06 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:03
claim: Every statement gives the prospect something to disagree with, so answer an objection with a question about it.
detail: To "are your trainers certified?" ask which certification they are looking for, then what it would accomplish for them. Often they asked only to seem informed, and the objection falls away. Goal: the prospect says the agreeing sentence themselves.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#07 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:26
claim: At a bought e-commerce company he built a sales team from zero to 40 in 90 days to call front-end buyers and sell them something else.
detail: The company had an automated front-end sales process; calling those buyers for an ascension offer earned more. The method was to compress the script as short as possible. Separately, at teeth-whitening studios he trains $10-an-hour staff to sell a $1,000 treatment many times a day by making the process colour-by-numbers.
numbers: 0 to 40 reps in 90 days; $10 per hour staff; $1,000 ticket.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#08 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:32
claim: After the yes, walk the buyer through exactly what happens next, because cold feet appear at that handshake and cause chargebacks and refunds.
detail: Link the sale to the first step of delivery so it is a smooth process.

### MZ-v6zDEMQs2iI#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:17
claim: There is no magic closing line; ask for the close only when you already know the answer is yes, and care more about the prospect than they do.
detail: He says the sale can be ruined in the first five seconds and the end is easy when everything before was right. If not sure, ask what else worries them. The frame "human first" came from his wife.
