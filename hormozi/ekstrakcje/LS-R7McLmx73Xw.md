### LS-R7McLmx73Xw#13 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:19:43

detail: Test: if 50% more customers arrived tomorrow, could the business serve them? ### LS-R7McLmx73Xw#25 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:33:06
