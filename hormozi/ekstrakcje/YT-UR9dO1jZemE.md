### YT-UR9dO1jZemE#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: operator-mindset | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:26
claim: Volume produces skill, and skill makes you do more volume, so low-volume attempts rarely reach the point of results.
detail: Even the best advertiser or cold caller needed volume to get there. Expect to be poor at first, hope to break even, and let skill improve with repetition rather than seeking big skill with little volume.

### YT-UR9dO1jZemE#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: outreach | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:28
claim: When someone wants more clients, first ask whether they do 100 reach-outs or spend $100 on ads every day.
detail: If neither, tell them not to pass go: pick one and do it daily for 90 days. Spending once, or reaching out only when motivated, does not count. For a solo founder outreach is cheapest since it only costs time.
numbers: 100 reach-outs or $100 of ads per day, for 90 days, per person working.

### YT-UR9dO1jZemE#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:20
claim: If outreach returns are strong, ten people performing at half your level beat you alone, so delegate even at a quality drop.
detail: Hires will likely do half or a third as well, but with solid returns the extra volume wins. Expect a short step back when someone else starts selling, then go from one to three to five sellers as you stop being the bottleneck.
numbers: Example returns of 10:1 to 30:1 on outreach effort; replacement at 33-50% effectiveness; ten people.

### YT-UR9dO1jZemE#04 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Paid ads are outbound too: you pay a platform for the attention instead of doing the manual reach-out.
detail: Both follow a small response pattern of about 1% (clicks or replies), after which the person comes inbound.
numbers: ~1% click rate on ads or reply rate on outbound (rough).
