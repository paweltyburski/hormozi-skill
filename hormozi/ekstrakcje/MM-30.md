### MM-30#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics, scaling-constraints | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A money model blends attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity offers to generate the most cash within 30 days, letting you acquire customers profitably and grow.
detail: Newcomers should choose one offer to spark change; experienced operators combine two or more. Only legality constrains you: an attraction offer may serve as an upsell, and a continuity offer can be the front end. The order in the course is merely the most logical one across Hormozi's businesses — feel free to innovate (00:07:11).

### MM-30#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Hormozi's gym money model used five offer types and let his gyms outcompete others in their markets.
detail: Front end: win-your-money-back challenge. Buyers: upsell buy-X-get-Y-free (prepaid time), then a continuity bonus as a rollover upsell into membership. Those refusing win-your-money-back: downsell a trial with penalty, then a menu upsell of supplements, then the rollover upsell on the back end.

### MM-30#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Gym Launch's money model used six structures: decoy offer, classic upsell, menu upsell, and payment-plan, feature and free-trial downsells.
detail: Front end: a decoy offer (the strategy call — arguably free-with-consumption). Then a classic upsell, then a menu upsell into the Gym Legacy back end, letting owners pick services (e.g., ads only, no agency management, but sales-team training). Those who declined went through payment plan → feature downsell → free trial.

### MM-30#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:02:17
claim: The newsletter model used a free trial as the attraction offer and a pay-less-now/lifetime-discount combination to make it cash-flow; Hormozi would add a bulk prepay only if retention were short.
detail: Free trial, then $399/month — or skip the trial, pay today, get two years of back issues and a lifetime lower rate (continuity discount plus pay less now/pay more later). Back end: consulting upsells. Hormozi's addition: buy-X-get-Y-free on continuity (e.g., buy six months, get six). If members average ~3 more months, buy-six-get-six doubles LTV and front-loads cash; if they average two years, it would lose money (00:03:27).
numbers: $399/month; buy 6 get 6 sensible when average remaining stay ~3 months.

### MM-30#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: - | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:04:14
claim: Build your own money model in four steps: attraction offer, then upsell, then downsell flow, then continuity.
detail: 1) Attraction offer to get leads to engage (win your money back, buy X get Y free, decoy…). 2) Upsell for those who come in: menu, anchor, rollover or classic. 3) Downsells for anyone who says no: payment plans, then feature downsell, then free trial — choose only what you can execute now. 4) Continuity offer. Result: cash back within 30 days, more customers, more money.

### MM-30#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: money-models | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:04:59
claim: Perfect one offer at a time — launching four or six new offers at once will break the business.
detail: Anything new already adds complexity; one is enough to start. Book and video each contain points the other lacks, but either is enough to act on.

### MM-30#07 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:04:59
claim: Launch a new offer at a lower price to collect yeses, then raise the price in stages.
detail: Presenting the payment-plan price with a prepay discount shows what prices the market will sustain before you raise them.

### MM-30#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Customer-Financed Acquisition | novelty: canon | ts: 00:04:59
claim: Use the fewest pieces that let you cover CAC and recycle cash within 30 days — once that works, acquisition stops being the bottleneck and you need no outside capital.
detail: Fewer parts means fewer things that can break. Start with the simplest structure that hits the objective, then stop adding.
quote: Simple scales, fancy fails.

### MM-30#09 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: lead-getters | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Fill gaps in your money model with affiliate products — e.g., a buy-X-get-Y-free on someone else's product — to earn cash with no operational burden.
detail: Keep selling your main product and send traffic to the partner's offer. Choose complementary products that don't compete with your core offer.

### MM-30#10 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Turn any attraction offer into continuity by adding a clause that it renews into continuity at a set price.
detail: Buy six get six rolls into continuity after the year; a win-your-money-back programme rolls into membership after its period; the premium side of a decoy offer rolls into continuity afterwards.
