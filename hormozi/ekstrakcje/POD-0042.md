### POD-0042#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:00
claim: His own approach: compare a one-off offer with your daily income, today's and projected, before accepting it.
detail: A $50,000 fee for one day of consulting looked too good to a viewer, but his own paid group business already brought in more per day. The Bill Gates joke (stopping for a $100 note loses money) illustrates undervaluing time that is worth far more. He also compares it with projected daily income, not only today's.
numbers: about $65,000 per day from his paid group business (his figure) against a $50,000 offer.
### POD-0042#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:40
claim: He turns down offers below his projected income level, because reaching that level means he can't accept less today.
detail: If he believed his income would cap at the offered amount, he might take it. He does not believe that, so he refuses anything below the projected rate today.
### POD-0042#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:26
claim: Convert a day-rate offer into a true rate by counting prep, recovery, aftermath, and the inefficiency of work outside your main business.
detail: A consulting day needs preparation, a recovery day, and follow-up questions afterwards. Because it is not his core stream, with no support team or template, everything is custom, so the total effort may be three to four days.
numbers: $50,000 for one day becomes about $10,000 to $12,000 per day of real time.

### POD-0042#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:11
claim: Opportunity cost matters more than the fee: the same days spent on the leveraged main business earn more.
detail: Three days put into the existing machine for gym owners would yield more than the offer. The test is whether you actually use the saved days with full focus on the business. Side gigs also distract; running another business means leaving the current one.

### POD-0042#05 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:54
claim: Stop doing tasks that could be replaced at $15 per hour, starting with personal training for gym owners.
detail: One of the first things he has gym owners do is quit running training sessions, over fears everyone will leave. His reply: people do not come for you, and someone can probably do the job better. Moving up means constantly getting out of the next role.
numbers: replacement cost reference $15 per hour.
