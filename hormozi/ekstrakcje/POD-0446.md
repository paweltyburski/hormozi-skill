### POD-0446#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: He reads in bursts aimed at the problem in front of him rather than on a book-a-week schedule.
detail: When he needed copywriting he read four books in a week, then read little for a long time. He thinks a reading quota is another form of media consumption: someone on book 36 can be where they were four years ago. Better to read four books and execute them.

### POD-0446#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:44
claim: The skill that speeds learning most is knowing what to learn next, judged against a pipeline of have something to sell, get people to find it, and learn to sell.
detail: If you cannot do one of those three, that is likely why you are not making money. He uses a bridge image: strengthening a brick you already have (copywriting) is wasted, fill the missing one that blocks the first dollar crossing.

### POD-0446#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: Masterminds mostly sell perspective, helping you see where you are deficient, while the fixing remains your own work.
detail: Nobody does the work for you. Many people spend years reinforcing one strength and never earn the first dollar because they cannot see where they are lacking.

### POD-0446#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:42
claim: The expensive coaching he bought, as he lists it, was not a lot: a fitness/gym mastermind, a weekend Facebook ads workshop, a one-on-one ads tutor and Russell Brunson's mastermind (besides targeted books, articles, videos, and mostly learning by doing).
detail: The weekend Facebook ads workshop earned him nothing that weekend but gave a conceptual base, after which he ran ads himself. Later he paid a tutor for pixels, retargeting and audiences. A mastermind with Russell Brunson taught him the online and digital product world even though he had no online business. He says he never logged into the materials; the value was thinking in bigger increments.
numbers: Weekend Facebook ads workshop $3,000. One-on-one ads tutor $750 per hour.
### POD-0446#05 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:28
claim: To learn a dreaded skill, clone an existing example first and accept that the next step becomes clear once you start.
detail: After about three years paying others to build pages, he cleared a Sunday to copy a page on the builder, expecting a full day or more, and finished in about four hours. Most people try to plan the first thousand steps before the first; he compares it to a flashlight lighting one step ahead.
numbers: Expected a full day; took about 4 hours; delayed about 3 years.

### POD-0446#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:05
claim: Offering to work for free is expensive for the mentor unless you bring something of value they need.
detail: He worked three months for his mentor at his gym because he already had fitness and nutrition knowledge to give. Requests to fetch coffee, he says, take two hours a day of attention while offering little. He cites Graham telling Buffett his free work was overpriced.
numbers: Worked from 4 a.m. to 4 p.m. for about 3 months for free.
