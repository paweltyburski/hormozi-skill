### YT-zzleYxkf39k#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:51
claim: Pricing in the same measured unit as every competitor (e.g. per hour) turns you into a commodity, because buyers just compare that one number.
detail: Callers ask only for the hourly rate, so $120 versus $125 decides the job. For this caller he first suggested quoting by job instead: a crew could take eight hours or four, and the customer wants it done fast and well. When the owner objected that volume is hard to estimate for a flat price, Hormozi moved to tiers and added elements to differentiate within hourly pricing.

### YT-zzleYxkf39k#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: offers, objections-closing | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:32
claim: When selling a commodity, you sow doubt about the competition along risk, speed and ease, because buyers fall back on price whenever they are given nothing else to compare.
detail: Risk here: vetted movers, insurance, a price cap, materials included where rivals add surprise fees. Speed: same-day service at a premium. He states at 00:16:56 that customers always go to price if you offer no other variables; the aim is to turn apples-to-apples into apples-to-oranges. He closes the episode (00:40:41) on the same idea: unless your strategy is to be cheapest, be the value leader by listing what customers had not considered.

### YT-zzleYxkf39k#06 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:56
claim: "Local to global" call structure: after the logistics questions, ask about preferences the buyer never considered, then quote the top tier with those items included.
detail: Steps for the mover: (1) date (availability); (2) size, stairs, parking; (3) preference questions: do you care who the movers are? do you have packing materials? anything worth over $500?; (4) quote VIP with master movers, materials and insurance (plus date premium if same day); (5) on budget, offer standard; (6) on price objection, list what cheaper rivals omit; offer to match only if all are matched. He names it at 00:20:30.

### YT-zzleYxkf39k#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:24:29
claim: Aim partner deals at the organisation that controls many referrers and get built into its sales process; once integrated and performing, they stop looking elsewhere.
detail: Applied here: offer the head of a brokerage free VIP upgrades for all its agents' clients in exchange for being the first call and the only mover in the packet given to buyers. He would rather the owner win one brokerage worth ~60 moves a year than chase single agents, adding one a week so they stack; the next bottleneck is managing the relationships, needing a full-time person. After any first realtor deal, visit that office in person (donuts), cite the agent's experience, and make the offer.
