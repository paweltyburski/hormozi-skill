### POD-0276#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: lead-getters | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Growth has two sides, more customers and more value per customer, and customers come from six sources: paid, earned, owned, partners, client referrals, manual outbound.
detail: Paid is ads. Earned is platforms distributing content (like this podcast). Owned is lists you control (email, phone numbers). Partners are firms serving the same clients in a different way, paid per deal or for continuity. Referrals are your own clients sending others. Outbound is cold calls, emails and messages. Tactics sit beneath each source.
applies_to: Companies growing from about $1M a year toward $1M a month.

### POD-0276#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:51
claim: Rate each lever, pick the one with the simplest, highest-likelihood payoff, and repeat on a consistent cycle; the exercise should calm you, not overwhelm.
detail: His one-day consulting session spot-checks the whole business, finds the largest opportunity, and goes all in, often on a single lever. Repeating over longer horizons pushes the chance of success toward certainty. Earlier-stage owners should partner more and build less.
numbers: The consulting day costs about $50,000.

### POD-0276#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:01
claim: Meal-prep example: revenue-based lifetime value of $1,000 against $200 CAC hides a loss, because lifetime gross profit is only $100.
detail: A meal costs $9 to make and ship and sells for $10, so $1 gross profit per meal. A customer buying 10 meals a week for 10 weeks brings $1,000 revenue but $100 gross profit. Spending $200 to acquire loses $100 per customer. Software writings treat revenue and gross margin as interchangeable because margins near 100%; goods businesses must use gross profit over lifespan.
numbers: $10 price, $9 cost, 10 meals/week, 10 weeks: $1,000 revenue, $100 gross profit, CAC $200.

### POD-0276#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: Crazy Eight | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:34
claim: Five ways to raise lifetime gross profit: raise price, cut cost, resell (retention), cross-sell, and upsell.
detail: He earlier named two (buy more, cross-sell). Cutting delivery cost, such as lighter meal shipping, raises margin and spending room. Resell means a recurring membership resold each month, tied to churn and activation. Cross-sell is another need, via your own second business or as an affiliate partner (e.g. a landing-page tool commission). Upsell is a better, premium version.
