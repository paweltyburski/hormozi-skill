### MZ-0c21D8j5aqI#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: offers | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: The bigger the promise, the higher the conversion but also the churn; a smaller promise attracts stayers.
detail: A "90 days to lose 10 kilos" community faces what happens on day 91. Selling with no promise to rare buyers gives lower churn.

### MZ-0c21D8j5aqI#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Problem-Solution Cycle | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Ask whether members stay for other members or for you, and what new problem the solved one creates.
detail: If they stay for you, you must keep producing. The next problem becomes the continuity.

### MZ-0c21D8j5aqI#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:30
claim: Sell a one-time system up front, then a consumable input needed to run it.
detail: A 3D-printing community taught the method once, then supplied weekly trending products; monthly churn was about 2-3%. Wholesaling lists work the same.
numbers: ~2-3% monthly churn.
