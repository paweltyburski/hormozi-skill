### YT-qxQIcDrre1E#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:13:40
claim: Four ways to apply leverage to grow: scale the entrepreneur, the market, the deliverable and the business.
detail: Results come from the entrepreneur multiplied by the opportunity, which is market times how scalable the deliverable is, plus the business acumen to scale the enterprise itself. A top-level entrepreneur on a weak opportunity does fine but not great. Hormozi applies the four when investing and when scaling his own companies.

### YT-qxQIcDrre1E#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:15:32
claim: Every business is capped by the entrepreneur in one of three ways: a missing skill, character trait or belief.
detail: Picture a ladder with skills, traits and beliefs as its sides: if any one is missing, you are limited to the lowest rung however strong the others. Treat each as a continuum (how well, not whether). Rephrase a complaint into a solvable question, such as how do I create sales consistency.
