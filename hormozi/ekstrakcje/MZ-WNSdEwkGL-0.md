claim: An agency selling SEO and paid ads to home-service providers has reps who both sell and manage accounts, and asks whether to split roles.
detail: Low churn comes from client relationships and low price. The top rep has not tapped out. An optional business app is not the reason for retention.

### MZ-WNSdEwkGL-0#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: SMB churn behaviour shifts at price steps of about $300, $1,000-$1,200 and $2,500-$3,500 a month.
detail: Around $300 a month is where small businesses typically stay; at $1,000-$1,200 there is another tier of churn, and $2,500-$3,500 is the next point.
numbers: ~$300, $1,000-$1,200, $2,500-$3,500 per month.
