### POD-230819-10#07 | TACTIC | topic: outreach | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: List Building (Scrape, Buy, Elbow Grease) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:42
claim: Sample-test every list source before taking volume from it, and prefer hand-built lists for freshness.
detail: Pull a few hundred records per tool or broker and check that contact data is current, people respond and they are the type claimed. ### POD-230819-10#08 | SCRIPT | topic: outreach | also: sales-process | fw: Three Problems of Strangers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:48
claim: A cold call to a dog trainer shows the sequence of moves that keeps a stranger on the line.
detail: Say their name; cite content of theirs and a result it gave you; apologise for jumping ahead and say who you work for; name a nearby business you helped with its result; ask if they offer similar services; propose reusing the campaign; light humorous close; book a walkthrough later that day.
numbers: Reference result: 100 appointments in 30 days.

### POD-230819-10#13 | DEFINITION | topic: outreach | also: - | fw: More Times More Ways | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:33
claim: Volume rises on two separate axes: automating delivery of the message and automating its distribution.
detail: Delivery is conveying the content: manual means a live call, personal voice memo or handwritten letter; automated means pre-recorded voice memo, voicemail or video, or a templated email or text. Distribution is the sending: manual means dialing or clicking send individually; automated means a robot dialer or a blast of a thousand messages.
