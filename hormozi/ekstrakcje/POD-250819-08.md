### POD-250819-08#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Decoy Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Gym Launch's first licensing money model was a decoy offer on a free call, which reached $476,000 a month within three months.
detail: Free books, courses and trainings each came with a free call. On the call the choice was: do it yourself from the free plan, or pay for help implementing it. With only one product, revenue was expected to plateau, which prompted the upsell.
numbers: Premium option $16,000 over 16 weeks; $476,000 per month in three months.

### POD-250819-08#02 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Downsell Flow | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:11
claim: The advanced upsell had a four-rung downsell ladder ending in a free start financed by continuity discount.
detail: Upsell for advanced gym owners: playbooks, services and a community. Prepay discounted; if no, payment plan; if no again, a big down payment and the rest over time; if no again, weekly payments over a year; last, start free, with the free time placed up front until the first offer was finished, then roll into continuity. Revenue reached $1.5M a month.
numbers: $42,000 per year, $6,000 off if prepaid; $10,000 down; $800 per week for 52 weeks; $1.5M per month.

### POD-250819-08#03 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:01
claim: A menu upsell with feature downsells lifted Gym Launch to $2.3M a month in 14 months, then $4.4M a month by month 20 with Prestige Labs.
detail: Owners who kept saying no were given a menu: done-for-you advertising, done-for-you sales training, turnkey campaigns, down to a minimum package of continued access and tech support. Nearly everyone bought something.
numbers: $2.3M per month within 14 months; $4.4M per month at month 20 after adding Prestige Labs.

### POD-250819-08#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:17

### POD-250819-08#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Pay Less Now or Pay More Later | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:02
claim: One newsletter offer can act as attraction, upsell and continuity at once.
detail: Free trial, then the standard monthly price after 30 days, combined with pay-less-now (lower price now) and a lifetime rate if the customer commits at once. Hormozi calls it a six-headed combination.
numbers: $0 for 30 days then $3.99/month; or $2.97/month locked for life.

### POD-250819-08#07 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Buy X Get Y Free | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:52
claim: Dog-food money model: bulk free-months attraction, classic upsells, premium-only downsell, automatic renewal.
detail: Buy four months, get two free. Upsell monthly delivery, toys, vitamins. If no, just the premium food. After the six-month bulk purchase it auto-continues month to month, cancel any time.
numbers: Buy 4 months, get 2 free.
