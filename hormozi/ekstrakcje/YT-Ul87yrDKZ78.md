### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#01 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Pre-empt the "forgot my wallet" stall by asking for ID first, then trading it back for the payment card.
detail: After the close, ask whether they have ID; asking for ID is easier than asking for a card. Take it (photograph it on Zoom), write something from it, build rapport about their hometown, then hold it and offer to swap it for the card they want to use, gesturing toward their wallet. Returning the ID builds trust. Seeing their cards makes a stall harder.

### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#02 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:44
claim: If they have no card, take bank details from their phone: open the banking app, go to statements, and copy the account number for an ACH payment.
detail: Ask which bank they use and have them open the app, choose any month's statement and read the account number at the top right. Nearly everyone has a phone with a banking app. Your processor must support ACH; if not, he says to set that up because it opens another way to close.
applies_to: Mainly in-person sales

### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:08
claim: As a second fallback, use Apple Wallet or take a card they would rather not use and postpone the charge by 24 hours.
detail: Call it policy and take the card they want least; then set the charge for tomorrow so they can swap cards at home. Agree a time, set an alarm on both phones and call or text them then. The contract is signed either way, so the swap is only housekeeping.

### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:08
claim: The ID-for-card trade alone settles about 80% of wallet stalls.
detail: Most prospects hand over a card once the ID swap is requested; the later fallbacks are for the remainder.
numbers: About 80% of wallet-stall cases resolved by the ID-for-card swap (his estimate).

### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#05 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:04
claim: If the stall is "not enough money", ask when they next get paid and set the charge for that day.
detail: Check their commitment first ("do you still want to do this?"). If yes, schedule the payment for the payday, such as the coming Friday, and sign them in now. This separates a real cash timing issue from a lack of desire.

### YT-Ul87yrDKZ78#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: A forgotten wallet probably means something was messed up earlier in the sale; depending on rapport, he backs up and asks what would make this not a fit.
detail: He says no one really leaves home without a way to pay. Depending on rapport, he may pivot and ask why they came in and what they have done before, which re-reminds them of the sale.
