### POD-0249#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: outreach | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Outbound is scalable by adding people, so Hormozi dropped his old belief that only paid advertising scales.
detail: He now thinks outbound may be more scalable, consistent and reliable than paid. A founder who says her outreach is not scalable can pay someone else to do it, then pay more people. Different channels (phone, email, direct messages) are like different ad platforms.

### POD-0249#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: outreach | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Gym Launch's cold calling funnel per week: 500 calls give about 10 interested people, 7 show up, 1 sale.
detail: One part-time caller makes 100 calls a day, 5 days a week. "Interested" means agreeing to another conversation. That works out to about 2 interested per 100 calls.
numbers: 500 calls a week -> ~10 interested -> 7 show -> 1 sale.
### POD-0249#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: outreach | fw: CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:30
claim: Outbound CAC was about $400 per sale against $2,000 to $2,500 in paid ads.
detail: Cost assumed about $1,600 a month for the part-time caller, giving about four sales a month. That is two to six times cheaper than paid, which also needs a creative and funnel team on top of ad spend. Twenty such callers would give 80 sales a month for about $32,000.
numbers: $1,600 a month / 4 sales = $400 CAC; 20 callers = 80 sales for $32,000.

### POD-0249#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: outreach | also: unit-economics | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:01
claim: Outbound output equals lists x activity x people; it is worth scaling when gross profit per customer is above 3x CAC.
detail: With CAC of $400, scale if gross profit after fulfilment exceeds $1,200. The operational work is training people on a script and sourcing lists.
numbers: 3x CAC threshold = $1,200 on $400 CAC.

### POD-0249#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: outreach | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:42
claim: Volume floors: he says you probably need about 500 cold emails a day (200 minimum) and about 100 calls a day for the volume to be meaningful.
detail: From 500 emails a day you might get about 5 to 10 meaningful replies. Phone lists can be scraped and fed to a dialer; many cold-email tools exist. For messaging, start with followers and friends, then members of groups where the avatar gathers.
numbers: Email: probably 500 a day (200 minimum); from 500 you might get 5-10 meaningful responses; calls: 100 a day is probably the minimum.
### POD-0249#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: outreach | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:42
claim: People who respond to outbound seem warmer than cold-ad traffic, perhaps because they are in a moment of pain.
detail: Hormozi gives this as a theory: ad traffic is interruption-based, while someone replying to outreach is already more interested.

### POD-0249#07 | CASE | topic: outreach | also: unit-economics | fw: CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:20
claim: A virtual assistant at $500 a month booking 10 appointments a week gives CAC around $60.
detail: With two sales a week (eight a month) the cost per customer is about $60; selling a $2,000+ offer returns about 30 to 1. He says to target offers of $2,000 or more for cold outreach. Failure is usually not doing enough of the activity.
numbers: $500 / 8 sales a month = ~$60 CAC; $2,000 offer = ~32:1; 3:1 is described as "exciting".

### POD-0249#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: outreach | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:36
claim: Each added outbound person adds a steady four to eight sales a month, which avoids the uneven cash flow of paid campaigns.
detail: Treat it as a slow-growth base while paid provides the bursts. Failed or stopped ad campaigns create troughs in cash flow.
numbers: +4 to +8 sales a month per added person.
