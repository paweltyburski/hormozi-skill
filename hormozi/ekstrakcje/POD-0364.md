### POD-0364#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:48
claim: People underperform for three reasons: they do not know why (motivation, solved by vision), do not know how (training and process), or do not know what is wanted (communication); he also adds offhandedly that if they have weird character traits you can let them go.
detail: Miscommunication includes using the wrong medium for that person.
### POD-0364#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:44
claim: A smart-sounding outcome goal such as "cut churn 20% this quarter" is a lagging indicator; people can finish actions, not metrics.
detail: Even a specific, measurable, timed goal fails if the team cannot tell which action produces it.

### POD-0364#03 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent, management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:52
claim: An outbound sales goal sat unmet for two quarters; naming the true first action got the team staffed and trained in 21 days.
detail: The manager said they needed five more outbound reps; that is a leading indicator but not the first. The first step was how many job ads and outreach requests to run and how many interviews to hold. Normal attrition cancelled out interviews, so headcount stayed flat for two quarters. After the true primary action was found, hiring took about a week and training about two weeks.
numbers: 5 reps; 2 quarters of no change; 21 days to staff and train.

### POD-0364#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:28
claim: Pick as the leading indicator the action that happens first in the chain of dominoes, not merely one that happens before the result; keep pulling the thread to the weak link.
detail: Naming it is the job of the owner or CEO.

### POD-0364#05 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:51
claim: If you wrote the same goal quarter after quarter with no progress, it is likely because the goal is outcome-based and the actions you planned are only preceding actions, not the primary action that creates the outcome.
detail: Use it on both team goals and personal goals.
