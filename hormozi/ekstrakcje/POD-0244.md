### POD-0244#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: offers | fw: MAGIC Naming Formula | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:52
claim: Separate the monetization structure (what is sold behind the scenes) from the naming wrapper shown to the market.
detail: Over four years the structure may never change while the wrapper is changed around 20 times. Refreshing the headline keeps ads converting without rebuilding the business model.
numbers: Wrapper changed about 20 times in 4 years.

### POD-0244#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: paid-ads | fw: Promotion Refresh Order | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:05
claim: When refreshing a MAGIC headline, the things you will especially switch are the duration, and possibly the goal and the magnet; the avatar is unlikely to change.
detail: Be specific about who you call out. Splitting an audience (men over 40 versus 20-year-old men, or men versus women) means quite different creative, funnels and copy, and sub-segmenting the clientele can give you higher converting offers.
### POD-0244#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: Promotion Refresh Order | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:24
claim: Local B2C offers need new headlines far more often than national or B2B offers, to beat banner blindness.
detail: Local consumer marketing may need a new wrapper about every 12 weeks, sometimes monthly. National or B2B marketing varies the offer much less.
numbers: Local B2C refresh: about every 12 weeks, sometimes monthly.
applies_to: Local consumer businesses

### POD-0244#04 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: - | fw: MAGIC Naming Formula | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:04
claim: Not all five MAGIC parts are needed; keep a headline as short as possible, using words that do double duty.
detail: A word can carry two roles. "Free six week summer slim down" has two magnet words (free, summer), a duration and a goal-container in one phrase, so adding "blueprint" would be redundant. Rhyming across the parts earns extra credit. Container words listed: incubator, intensive, challenge, blueprint, system, sprint, kickoff, jumpstart.
