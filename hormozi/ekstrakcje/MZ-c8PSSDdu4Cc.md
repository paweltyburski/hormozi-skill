### MZ-c8PSSDdu4Cc#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A poker coaching firm for already-winning players sells two overlapping front ends and is demand-constrained on the expensive one.
detail: At about $2M, goal $10M. Offers: $7.5K for 28 days (about 12 sales a month, roughly $90-100K) and $2.8K for 7 days (16-32 a month), which works as a downsell for part-timers rather than an upsell step. Hormozi suspects an ops issue and asks which tier they prefer to serve. Cutting either now would just lose money because leads come only from warm inbound content; paid ads are what unlock growth.
numbers: $7.5K offer about 12 per month; $2.8K offer 16-32 per month; revenue about $2M to $10M target.

### MZ-c8PSSDdu4Cc#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:59
claim: To find the constraint, ask: if you had unlimited capacity, could you sell more?
detail: The owner answered yes for the 7-day product, which needs less of their time. Hormozi told this owner that cutting either offer right now would lose money, because they have no on-demand way to get customers.
