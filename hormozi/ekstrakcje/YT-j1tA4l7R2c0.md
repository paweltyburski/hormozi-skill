### YT-j1tA4l7R2c0#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: objections-closing | also: money-models | fw: Classic Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Typical bolt-on upsell take rates are 20-30%, while the "you don't want anything else, do you?" assume close can reach 90% or more.
detail: Works for simple, low-complexity add-ons (supplements, meals, scarves, orthotics, serums), not core complex offers. People feel they are being sold, so agreeing to "no" completes the sale.
numbers: 20-30% normal; 90%+ with the close.

### YT-j1tA4l7R2c0#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Classic Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:19
claim: A furrier gave free fur earmuffs for storing a coat, then said they too needed storing and conditioning, and added small extras like a loose button.
detail: Conditioning added a couple of hundred dollars to every coat across thousands of coats; the earmuffs were wrapped with the coat so both went to the back. Each "anything else?" brought another small service.
numbers: ~$200 added per coat; thousands of coats.

### YT-j1tA4l7R2c0#03 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:11
claim: Phrase upsells as a negative the customer will reject: "you don't want to pay full retail, do you?" then "so I'll send it at the discounted price to the address on file."
detail: Same logic for recommended supplements: after prescribing, ask whether they want anything else; their "no" confirms the sale.
