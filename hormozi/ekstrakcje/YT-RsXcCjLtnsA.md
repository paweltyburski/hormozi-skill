### YT-RsXcCjLtnsA#01 | TACTIC | topic: other | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:58
claim: Remember names by repeating the person's name at the start of your sentences, aiming for about ten times in a conversation.
detail: Hearing their own name signals respect. Hormozi says it makes him good at recalling names.
numbers: About 10 repetitions per conversation.

### YT-RsXcCjLtnsA#02 | TACTIC | topic: other | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:43
claim: Right after getting contact details, text them a light, funny line that includes both names and a detail about each of you.
detail: Putting both names and a memorable descriptor in the thread (for example your own trademark look, and their trade) builds rapport, saves the contact properly, and makes the first message easy to remember later.

### YT-RsXcCjLtnsA#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:28
claim: Aim each conversation at how you can help the other person, and give unsolicited value first.
detail: Example: after hearing a need, send a list of suppliers you have used. The more effort in the unsolicited give, the more people respect you and keep you around.

### YT-RsXcCjLtnsA#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: other | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:14
claim: Always say yes to the first ask, and he sometimes moves existing plans to do it; if you are the asker, ask many times.
detail: People usually ask only once, and the person who becomes a huge player later may simply be busy at first. Hormozi calls it his biggest networking secret.
### YT-RsXcCjLtnsA#05 | TACTIC | topic: other | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:52
claim: Never ask for favours; describe a problem you are facing and let the other person offer help.
detail: Direct asks make people assume you are using them. With rapport and earlier giving, reciprocity usually leads them to propose a reasonable ask. If they do not, you do not need that friendship.
