### MZ-oq5gpeZdcD4#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: He roots what he teaches in statements that cannot be disproven, such as that more people knowing about you raises sales, because nobody can buy before finding you.
detail: He calls these fight-me statements and expects to hold them in ten years. That guards against teaching something he later disowns.

### MZ-oq5gpeZdcD4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:58
claim: His mentor is feedback: he spends most time consuming data on what the company does and how it works.
detail: He agrees that what a person consumes most is effectively what they worship.

### MZ-oq5gpeZdcD4#03 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:40
