### POD-0228#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: finance-exit | fw: Virtuous Cycle of Price | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:00
claim: Profit comes before growth: raise price to fund marketing and talent rather than lowering it to attract more customers.
detail: Math: a 20% price increase in a business with 20% margin doubles profit. "Reinvesting everything in growth with no profit" is backwards; high-growth companies fund growth from real profit. He says this holds in many, not all, cases.
numbers: +20% price at 20% margin = profit doubles.

### POD-0228#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:47

detail: Owners limit themselves with two salespeople when five or ten may be right. More reps force higher ad spend, which is a backwards but easy way to start.

### POD-0228#06 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Peter Principle | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:18
claim: Outsource in three layers as you grow: doing, then management of the doing, then leadership, each needing different people.
detail: Usually the first five core team of doers gets you to about a million; delegating management gets you to roughly three-ish million; from three to ten million you outsource leadership, and a lot of times you must pull leaders from outside. The thresholds flex depending on the business. Promoted in-house people usually managed one jump (doer to manager) but not two, then floundered at their level of incompetence.
numbers: Doers usually to about $1M; managers to roughly $3M (three-ish); leadership for $3M to $10M; thresholds flex by business.
### POD-0228#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:43
claim: The missing role is someone who reads data proactively and spots problems before they escalate, a thinking function the founder had not delegated.
detail: Staff take initiative on problems already visible. An executive takes data in and extrapolates where a problem will occur. His reporting was pull-based (asking staff to count manually); it should be pushed to him daily or weekly on a dashboard. Test: if I were away, would anyone check these numbers?

### POD-0228#08 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-process | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:13:55
claim: If sales fall short of the modelled number, check in order: execution, then system, then marketing, and the offer last.
detail: Execution splits into unmotivated, untrained or unclear communication. System asks whether the process is too complex, too simple or missing steps. Marketing checks message, creative, funnel and post-funnel. Changing the offer changes the whole business, so it comes last. If revenue is short despite sales, look at back-end pricing, churn and lifetime value with the same order, then at the product, tested with betas.

### POD-0228#09 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:19:54
