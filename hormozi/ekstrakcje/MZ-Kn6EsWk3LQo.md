### MZ-Kn6EsWk3LQo#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Vet vendors by interviewing around ten, telling each you are bidding widely, until pitches repeat.
detail: By the seventh pitch you have heard about 90% of what everyone says, so the better ones stand out. It costs perhaps two extra weeks but avoids months with a bad vendor and a redo costing double. Tends to choose a higher-priced provider: buy nice or buy twice.
numbers: ~10 pitches; ~90% overlap by pitch seven.

### MZ-Kn6EsWk3LQo#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Judge a vendor's communication during the sales process as a preview of delivery, and favour proof over a wonderful offer.
detail: Clear expectations, quick replies and realistic timelines are good signs. An unproven vendor with a great offer loses to one with 2,000 five-star reviews.
numbers: 2,000 five-star reviews example.
