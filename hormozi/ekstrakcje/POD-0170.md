### POD-0170#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Gym owners reported a 51% close rate but their software, which forces marking each appointment, showed 34%.
detail: Allen software requires each appointment to be marked showed, no-show, closed or not closed and the amount. Gyms closed about one in three walk-ins, so self-reports overestimate ability.
numbers: reported 51% vs measured 34% (about 1 in 3).
applies_to: Gyms

### POD-0170#02 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:51
claim: To get a card on a free trial or any required step, say "that's just how we've always done it."
detail: Use it when the prospect asks why they must fill in the card on a free trial; people follow the pack and just need to know others do it this way. It also answers "do I have to take these products?" with "this is what goes with the programme".

### POD-0170#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:26
claim: His first supplement sale came when he stopped explaining ingredients and asked "chocolate or vanilla?"
detail: A friend's supplement shop did about $75,000 a month, which annoyed him. After a day of science talk failed, he ended an orientation with the either-or question, then asked strawberry or kiwi for the intra-workout, and charged the card on the contract. He asked for only two items so as not to scare her.
numbers: friend's store about $75,000 a month.

### POD-0170#04 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:24
claim: To the question of whether they need the products, answer "if you change the variables, you cannot expect the same outcome."
detail: Said after "what you did before has not worked"; it works for needing supplements at all, these ones, or using ones they have.

### POD-0170#05 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:14
claim: Say "most people just get this" to move buyers to a bundle, then assume the close.
detail: Many buyers want to follow what most people do and avoid risk. It sells a four-pack or eight-pack before the end of the pitch.

### POD-0170#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:00
claim: Answer "I need to think about it" with "what's your main concern?"
detail: It is an overcome because it forces them to name the issue instead of hiding behind the stall.

### POD-0170#07 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:33
claim: Use the consider-the-options close: list the alternatives they have tried or might try and show each fails.
detail: Steps: ask what else they could do; each time recall why it failed (Planet Fitness, Weight Watchers, regaining more); exhaust the options until only accountability and a guided plan remain. Use it when your service depends on their effort, so no result is guaranteed.

### POD-0170#08 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:13:18
claim: His sales team's close rate fell because they did not know what the coaching side now delivered; once Layla listed the touchpoints it recovered within a week.
detail: Layla described day one, two, three, five and seven touchpoints; the reps' belief and closing returned to normal.
numbers: touchpoints on days 1, 2, 3, 5, 7.

### POD-0170#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:01
claim: Some gyms sell better in their first weeks than six months in, because early conviction is high and fades as problems accumulate.
detail: The most important sale is between you and your service. A leader should stay in the trenches to keep conviction up.
