### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#01 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: Crazy Eight | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A coaching founder said there was no way to make customers more valuable; Hormozi answered with six levers.
detail: Business grows by getting more customers or making each worth more. The six: raise price, cut cost of goods, increase purchase frequency (lower churn), cross-sell, upsell, downsell. Applies to any advisory service, such as weight-loss coaching.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#02 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: product-delivery | fw: Crazy Eight | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:40
claim: In a coaching business, lower delivery cost by using cheaper (e.g. offshore) staff or by raising the coach-to-client ratio.
detail: Moving a coach from 10 to 20 clients halves cost of goods per client and lifts margin.
numbers: Ratio 10 to 20 clients per coach = cost of goods cut by half.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:57
claim: Keeping a client one more month on a three-month average stay raises LTV by 25%.
detail: Purchase frequency in recurring businesses means lower churn and longer stay.
numbers: 3 months to 4 months = +25% LTV.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:50
claim: Cross-sell add-ons that remove execution bottlenecks the core service does not cover, such as ad creation or drilling the client's sales team.
detail: Not everyone buys them, but those who do get large value. Example: 20% of clients take a $1,000 monthly add-on on top of a $1,000 core fee, lifting average monthly revenue per client by 20%.
numbers: 20% take +$1,000/month on a $1,000/month base, so average rises from $1,000 to $1,200.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#05 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: A cross-sell is a different item sold alongside the core; an upsell is more of what they just bought.
detail: Using a burger-chain illustration: fries with the burger is a cross-sell, a bigger burger is an upsell. In coaching, an upsell is more frequent meetings with the coach or more access.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:21
claim: Customer willingness to pay is fractal, following repeated 80-20 splits, so a much dearer tier always has takers.
detail: Roughly one in five customers will pay twice the core price, and 20 of 100 pay up to five times the base. Within that top 20, about four will pay another five times more. A semi-private small-group tier should cost about four to five times a large-group one.
numbers: 20 of 100 buyers at 5x price doubles LTV; small group priced 4-5x large group.

### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:32
claim: In his opinion and by his preference, offer a front-end downsell only to callers who fail your qualification criteria, and never to those who qualify for the core offer.
detail: He prefers to keep sales rules black and white. Track the core-offer rate before adding the downsell so it does not reduce core sales. For unqualified callers (marketing already paid for) use a low-touch or no-touch do-it-yourself or done-for-you-at-home programme; those who succeed can ascend later and the downsell offsets acquisition cost.
applies_to: Services where applicants are screened against predefined requirements.
### YT-MwhsM7qijy8#08 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:37
claim: As information loses value after being learned, offer departing customers a back-end downsell of what they still consume, such as community, troubleshooting or accountability.
detail: Ask what they need or want to keep once training is done. Consumable support persists in value month to month while knowledge does not.
applies_to: Education, coaching and info-service businesses.
