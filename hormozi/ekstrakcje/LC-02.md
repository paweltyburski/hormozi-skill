### LC-02#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: - | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Most businesses get fewer leads and sales than they want for one simple reason: they are not advertising enough.
detail: Too few sales usually means too few opportunities — you need more "at-bats". The constraint is advertising volume.

### LC-02#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: - | fw: More Better New | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Fix a lead shortage by advertising in three directions: more, better, and new — producing a reliable flow of cheaper, higher-quality leads from numerous channels.
detail: He states the aim as more leads, cheaper leads and better leads, reliably, from lots of different places so you never need to worry about one of them going away. (The transcript does not spell out separate definitions of more, better and new in this lesson.)

### LC-02#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00

### LC-02#08 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: - | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:03:36
claim: A lead is simply a person you can contact.
detail: Hormozi could not define a lead when his editor pressed him: an ad click, a bought list, a follower, a subscriber, a phone number, a booked call, a walk-in? The test is contactability; interest is not part of it.

### LC-02#09 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: - | fw: Engaged Leads | novelty: canon | ts: 00:04:22
claim: An engaged lead is a person you can contact who has shown interest in what you sell — engaged leads are the true output of advertising.
detail: Contactability alone is not what a business wants; it wants interest. You give an indifferent contact something that makes them interested, and they act. Signals: giving contact information on a site, replying to an email campaign, following you where you can then message them. The next question: how to get leads to engage naturally.
quote: Engaged leads are the true output of advertising.
