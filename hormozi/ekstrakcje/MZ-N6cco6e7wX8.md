### MZ-N6cco6e7wX8#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:47
claim: Skills are chains of reinforced adaptive behaviours, so AI that learns by reinforcement can in principle learn what humans do.
detail: He sees human drive as the product of reinforcement we may not recall, and a skill as a behaviour chain that produces an outcome. If conditions are controlled, outcomes are teachable; "unteachable mastery" simply means nobody has structured the teaching.

### MZ-N6cco6e7wX8#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:13
claim: His company trained an AI sales development rep for outreach and follow-up, and it stopped repeating a mistake once corrected, unlike human hires.
detail: Hallucinations remain a problem, but instruction-following improved immediately after feedback. He expects data quality and physical-world experimentation to become the learning constraint and expects AI to be embodied in robots.
