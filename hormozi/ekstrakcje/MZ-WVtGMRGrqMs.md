### MZ-WVtGMRGrqMs#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A skill is an adaptive chain of behaviours; soft skills only look vague because more behaviours predict success, so define each as observable steps.
detail: Empathy example: recap what they said, nod, say you're sorry it felt that way, acknowledge the feeling at least twice, ask about the feeling. Hard skills are easier to define because success has fewer criteria.

### MZ-WVtGMRGrqMs#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:06
claim: Replace "build rapport" and "empathetic tone" with concrete moves: ask questions about them until you find something similar, link it, then ask if they are ready to start; vary speed, volume and pauses.
detail: Voice has few dials (louder, softer, faster, slower, pauses). A robotic sound comes from reading a script; memorising the script produces natural pauses.

### MZ-WVtGMRGrqMs#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:15
claim: To learn from a practitioner who cannot explain how they succeed, ask enough questions to tease out what behaviours produced the outcome.
detail: Great salespeople often cannot teach it; separate first-party data from repeated phrases.
