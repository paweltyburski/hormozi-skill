### MZ-fFtoFqezKw0#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Bet on lasting human wants: looking beautiful and young, managing wealth, and fractionalising risk through insurance.
detail: His examples of durable demand: longevity, med spa, aesthetics and peptides (a large supply-demand gap, already booming), wealth advisors (often earning more than their skill level would suggest), and insurance. The logic is choosing demand that will not change.

### MZ-fFtoFqezKw0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Classic Meta ad agencies are now harder to enter than they were 15 years ago; newer service niches are easier.
detail: In his view the agency opportunity is saturated and its nature has changed. He points to Reddit reputation management and clipping agencies as emerging offers, and still endorses automating work for small businesses with AI.

### MZ-fFtoFqezKw0#03 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:35
claim: A service-based gym owner at $10M a year could earn more applying the same skills to an easier-to-scale business.
detail: The gym used personal or semi-private training with about 100 staff, so quality depends on low-skilled labour and constant culture upkeep. Hormozi calls this type easy to start and hard to scale, unlike equipment-access gyms. His advice: the same management skill applied to a superior vehicle would pay more, though the owner may be content.
numbers: $10M annual revenue, about 100 employees.

### MZ-fFtoFqezKw0#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Look at unglamorous industries that people avoid mentioning at parties; the discount in status can hide big profits.
detail: He cites trash and waste management as examples, noting the owner can be a billionaire even if the work lacks social prestige. Also notes that a restaurateur doing $20M a year probably could do far more in a different business.
