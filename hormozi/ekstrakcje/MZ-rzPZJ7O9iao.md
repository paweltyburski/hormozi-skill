### MZ-rzPZJ7O9iao#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals, content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:37
claim: A coach for mothers running beauty businesses wrote a Profit First book for salons but lost clients and onboarded fewer.
detail: She moved to internal structure last year. Hormozi says the model is scalable but she has no acquisition, her earlier call funnel being the hardest type without a brand.
numbers: New clients 20 to 10, each ~$30K, with 10 lost; 40 active; book sold 3,000-4,000 copies in under two years; LTV ~$90K over 3 years.

### MZ-rzPZJ7O9iao#02 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads, sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:12
claim: Run ads to a book (free plus shipping), then a thank-you video offering a low-pressure call.
detail: Pitch: seven principles; we pull the chapters that fit you; first call, no credit card, nothing sold. A book funnel works through brand halo and star reviews.
numbers: Book free plus $4.95 shipping.

### MZ-rzPZJ7O9iao#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: Epiphany Bridge | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:03
claim: Interview clients about before and after and use their worst moment as the ad hook.
detail: Ask what the worst moment before working together was (for example, not making payroll). The ad shows that pain, with the book as the bridge to the good outcome.
