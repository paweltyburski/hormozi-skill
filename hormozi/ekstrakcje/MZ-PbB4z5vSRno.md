### MZ-PbB4z5vSRno#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Info and education are easy to make some money in and hard to make a lot: a handful reach $30M a year, many reach $1-3M, and most cap near $3-5M.
detail: Without real systems a business tops out near 120-150 people to manage (Dunbar's number), which at typical info-plus-service price points equals the cap. Higher ticket spaces (real-estate, investment) reach $10-20M, then ROAS falls and margins compress.
numbers: ~$30M handful; $1-3M common; $3-5M typical cap; Dunbar 120-150 people; $10-20M for high ticket.

### MZ-PbB4z5vSRno#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:36
claim: Milk analogy: coaching does not scale because buyers prefer a shot of the best product over a full glass diluted by water.
detail: Teaching others to coach in a weekend dilutes it. Scalable versions: (1) long-time alumni running their own business under your umbrella with half or more of the revenue; (2) a large event business like Tony Robbins, where five days with the master beat five days with his coaching staff on NPS (his guess).
numbers: rev share of 50% or more, not 10%.

### MZ-PbB4z5vSRno#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:17
claim: You cannot have fast growth, high profit and high enterprise value together: pick two.
detail: Info businesses depend on the key person so rarely sell. A friend lived on $70,000 for 11 or 12 years, then sold for $250M; others prefer a few million a year with no exit.
numbers: $70,000/year for 11-12 years; $250M exit.
