claim: Insure a specific worry, and get a second opinion on any deal that looks like a grand slam.

### YT-INm4U2S7Vu8#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:30
claim: Pressure, rush or a single available deal means do not proceed.
detail: With nothing to compare against, one deal looks better than it is; keep backups and treat pressure as a stop signal.

### YT-INm4U2S7Vu8#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:19
claim: In a partnership move only on agreement, and a partner who always yields adds no value.
detail: Buying with the intention of selling is trading, not investing. Investing has its own rules, and mixing business thinking into investing (or the reverse) causes trouble because they are different skills.
### YT-INm4U2S7Vu8#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:05
claim: Pass on what is complicated or above your head, and slow down when you feel fear of missing out.
detail: Barely understanding it is gambling; full understanding lowers risk.

claim: Require diligence and a model: see the math and what must be true for it to work.
detail: Examine assumptions behind projections instead of listening to a story. Unresponsive counterparties, or those not meeting their stated reply times, are a reason to decline.

claim: Repeat what has worked and always under-borrow.
detail: Compounding comes from more of the same; debt multiplies by zero eventually, so lower returns that survive beat juiced ones.
