### MZ-isoBXgDHp5w#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Put all focus on the team segment if its LTV gap far exceeds its CAC gap; no need to abandon individuals.
detail: CAC may be three times higher for teams but unlikely twenty times; teams also retain better, which fits a compounding business.
numbers: CAC maybe 3x higher, not 20x (his guess).
