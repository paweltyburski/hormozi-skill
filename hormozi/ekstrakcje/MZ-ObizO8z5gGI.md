### MZ-ObizO8z5gGI#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His first business, the Free Training Project, turned charity donations into testimonials that seeded a for-profit online business.
detail: Donors gave $500 to $1,000 to a charity of their choice and he trained them. Goodwill plus testimonials followed. He wanted fitness full-time, the charity paid him nothing, so he switched to charging and signed about 20 clients using those testimonials.
numbers: donation $500 to $1,000 per client to charity; about 20 paying clients at the switch.

### MZ-ObizO8z5gGI#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:27
claim: When something is not working, change the delivery slightly instead of abandoning the goal.
detail: The interviewer notes he kept the aim (fitness full-time) and only changed the model from free charity to paid.

### MZ-ObizO8z5gGI#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:27
claim: Before opening his first gym he apprenticed by following a California gym owner's daily routine for about three months.
detail: He kept online clients on weekends, travelled from the East Coast, and mirrored the owner's schedule (arrive and leave with him). He then opened his first gym in Huntington Beach.
numbers: about 3 months of shadowing.
