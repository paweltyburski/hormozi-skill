### MZ-dC2G9YQj15g#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A new opportunity looks easier mainly because it is pitched like a sales pitch; every business is hard, only the kind of difficulty differs.
detail: Switching puts you back at zero: you pass early levels fast, then meet the same obstacle again, while usually keeping the old business too. You can own many businesses but run only one well.

### MZ-dC2G9YQj15g#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Each business type has its hard part at a different stage.
detail: Information: easiest to start, hardest to scale. Software: hardest to start, easiest to scale. E-commerce sits between, needing capital then facing logistics. Services scale slowly because people must be recruited and trained.

### MZ-dC2G9YQj15g#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:40
claim: Ask "why is my business not already a $100M business?" and treat the honest answer as the problem to solve.
detail: He holds any business can reach $100M a year. Most entrepreneurs who do not know how to solve it blame the business, industry, market or vehicle. Local services get there by opening more locations, which he counts as one business.
### MZ-dC2G9YQj15g#04 | EVOLUTION | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:02:28
claim: He sometimes regrets having talked about switching opportunity vehicles; had he stayed with gyms, he says he would probably have 200-300 gyms now and still be just as rich.
detail: The vehicle limits you only when the model itself caps you, which is rarer than it feels. Doing one thing for decades and improving usually pays.
numbers: 200-300 gyms as his hypothetical path.
### MZ-dC2G9YQj15g#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:57
claim: Pick a business around a pain you overcame, a passion or a professional skill; persisting, every business converges on a team of five to seven functional heads.
detail: Caring about the customer sustains growth, and the owner's day then looks similar whatever the product.
numbers: 5-7 senior people in the end-state team.
