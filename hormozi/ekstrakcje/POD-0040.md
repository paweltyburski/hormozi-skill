### POD-0040#01 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Answer a stall by asking "what is your main concern?" and repeat until concerns are exhausted.
detail: It pulls them back from leaving. If the concern is schedule, circle the class times with them and point out that any plan needs a time slot anyway.

### POD-0040#02 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:06
claim: For "I do not decide on the spot", empathise, then show that more time brings no new information.
detail: Say you used to believe more time meant better decisions. The only information source about the programme is the seller across the table, so ask what else they want to know. Then name the real fear: choosing wrong.

### POD-0040#03 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:42
claim: Spell out both feared outcomes and neutralise each: wasting money by not showing up, and not getting value.
detail: First, if they fear paying and not attending, note that they said the amount means a lot to them, so they are unlikely to waste it. Second, if they fear no result, point to the service guarantee, a refund if they do not feel they got value. Ask "sound fair?" after each.
