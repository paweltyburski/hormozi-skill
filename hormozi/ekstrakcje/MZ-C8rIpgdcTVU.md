claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: shift from role-based to workflow-based thinking and map what every person does at each step of each process.
detail: Hierarchies descend from span limits of about 3-8 reports, so information compresses at every level. He guessed only about 10 of this agency's 85 people really run it. He said they would probably need, he didn't know, around 3-5 engineering hires to help map the workflow, and he would lean toward staff who lean into AI.
numbers: Span of control 3-8; a team of 10 could run it with more tech.
