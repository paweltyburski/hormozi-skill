### YT-Wr6n_zNKvMk#03 | TACTIC | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:41
claim: Plan on the fewest "need-to-believes": find the single assumption that, if true, reaches the goal, then list only the conditions attached to it.
detail: Steps: (1) take the plan to its extreme and ask what one thing would have to be true (here: ten commission-only reps each doing five sales a day); (2) check that it clears the goal (it overshot by ~8x); (3) list the dependent assumptions (can you find houses to knock, can you cover rep pay and first-quarter costs up front); (4) solve only those and ignore everything else. Reason he gives: each extra assumption sharply lowers the chance the plan happens.

### YT-Wr6n_zNKvMk#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics, pricing | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition, Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:50:02
claim: To grow debt-free, sum every cost of starting a customer before their next payment and design offers so the blended upfront cash per sale exceeds it.
detail: His recap: add delivery cost until the next bill, equipment you front (here bins) and the sales commission to get the break-even upfront figure ($208). Then build an A/B offer: a core option that already clears it, plus a larger prepaid option with a sweetener (free equipment) taken by a minority. Judge by blended average cash per sale, not per option: even if one in five takes the bigger option, it lifts the average for everyone. He prefers growing debt-free, believing a self-funding structure can usually be found.

### YT-Wr6n_zNKvMk#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:53:27
claim: Train door-to-door reps by modelling plus rapid feedback: drill the opener on a real door until automatic, then shadow, then let them knock while you watch.
detail: People learn by watching and by doing with feedback; role-play speeds the loop. The first seconds after the door opens decide the sale, so rehearse knock and opener repeatedly at a physical door. A two-minute sale needs a short script (about four questions, close, standard concerns such as spouse, thinking it over, card). Practise while walking between houses. The owner should never knock alone: a trainee always watches until someone else becomes the best rep and lead trainer (pay the trainer slightly more per sale).

### YT-Wr6n_zNKvMk#06 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 01:02:43
claim: For commission-only roles keep the hiring bar low: screen out only obvious misfits in group calls, then make offers in person on the spot.
detail: Reasoning: let many people prove work ethic rather than interviewing skill. Write the job ad to the salesperson's pains and the promise (high income, choose four or eight hours a day, grades irrelevant, honest about heat and cold). Process: group call with about two minutes per applicant; invite the normal-seeming ones in person; offer on the spot with a start date; one week shadowing, then work in pairs. Expect several hires per keeper. He looks for hunger, often driven by circumstances such as a child on the way.
