### MZ-aw14G--LXJQ#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:00
claim: Always hire for the smallest skill deficiency, whichever kind of skill it is.
detail: Resolves "attitude versus aptitude". Choose whichever gap costs least to train, soft or hard, so as to get the highest return per hire.

### MZ-aw14G--LXJQ#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:51
claim: Kindness is harder to train than call setting because it hides many sub-skills.
detail: He can teach appointment setting in about 48 hours, but "kind" contains around 30 sub-skills. For a rare specialist, such as a healthcare M&A CFO, take the experienced person and train the manners.
numbers: Call setting about 48 hours; about 30 sub-skills inside "kind".

numbers: Eight-week bootcamp (what elite firms do, per his example).
