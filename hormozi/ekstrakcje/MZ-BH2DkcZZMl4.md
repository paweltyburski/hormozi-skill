### MZ-BH2DkcZZMl4#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:13
claim: Your own results are the evidence that contains your message; advice from someone who visibly lacks the result will not be heard, whatever the algorithm tricks.
detail: Example: a friend wanted to grow a fitness business but was not in shape; Hormozi said he did not look like anyone's goal. Ask what single big domino would make everything else follow.

### MZ-BH2DkcZZMl4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:17
claim: Not knowing what you are doing is the sign of growth; wanting growth and certainty together is contradictory.
detail: Strong character and an easy life, or growth without hardship, are paired wants; the price has to be paid, and a beginner should call themselves a white belt, not pretend to be a black belt.
