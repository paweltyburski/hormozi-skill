### POD-0120#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:12
claim: The traits that take you from zero to one (rebellion, novelty) are the opposite of those that take you from one to many (consistency, process).
detail: Entrepreneurs like variety and dislike process; after starting, the work is unlearning what started you.

### POD-0120#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:07
claim: Chick-fil-A's edge is repeating the same fundamentals (smile, greeting, kindness) on every interaction.
detail: He cites it as the most profitable fast-food chain with no debt and over a billion dollars in value (his statement). The results are reviews and word of mouth.
numbers: Zero debt; "billion dollar plus" value (as stated).

### POD-0120#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:49
claim: The difference between five-star and lower tiers of service is skipping nothing, not doing different things.
detail: A hotel or sandwich shop remembers names and orders, serves fresh, and never forgets the steps. A gym equivalent: always post new ads, work leads, greet by name, give the pre-qualifying questionnaire, tour the facility, and show before-and-after photos.
