### MZ-_XD4nqvx9KM#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:53
claim: Leaders hold power over livelihoods, so people act differently around them and the leader misreads intent.
detail: A friend told him he is no longer punishing but is threatening by position. He admits early in his career his team avoided him because he only pointed out faults. His wife is never fooled because people relax with her.

### MZ-_XD4nqvx9KM#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:56
claim: Thank the bearer of bad news for accurate facts, and give negative feedback by asking people to review their own work first.
detail: Ask how they think it went or have them watch the call and say what happened. Most are hard on themselves and find the problem, then you praise the observation and praise again when they fix it. It takes more patience and skill than punishment, which is faster.

claim: A slow, rewarded reader outlasts a fast reader who burns out, so reward can win over punishment on a long horizon.
detail: Someone reading 600 words a minute who stops after two books versus one reading 100 words a minute for a year; with enough reward the second can pass the first.
