### MZ-e4xdEQIQIZw#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals, hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A medspa with $3M revenue is limited by the owner-physician's time, so hire a doctor and free her bandwidth.
detail: Wellness and weight-loss clinic, word-of-mouth, one location, owner is 80% physician and 20% owner. Most profitable lines are weight loss and concierge. Some labor sits outside cost of goods. A part-time physician is easy to find; she is hunting a unicorn.
numbers: Revenue $3M, goal $20M in 2-5 years; net about $700,000, owner earnings about $900k-$1M (medical director draw about $200,000); cash up $20-50k per month; replacement physician about $200,000 a year.

### MZ-e4xdEQIQIZw#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: unit-economics | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Ask how much the bank account grows from start to end of a month to check cash against reported EBITDA.
detail: Differences arise from equipment purchases and labor left out of cost of goods.

### MZ-e4xdEQIQIZw#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:31
claim: Ask which of the many offerings is most profitable per unit of the owner's time, to choose what to stop doing.
detail: Aim is to find out whether she needs more headcount or just needs to choose to do fewer things; she herself said she has to do fewer things.
### MZ-e4xdEQIQIZw#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:23
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: sales probably isn't his constraint, because an extra $300k of profit wouldn't change his life; his time is, so remove work before building more.
detail: Hormozi bet this scattered owner had many half-built bridges. Roughly 30% margin on seller discretionary earnings made the business okay; at $400k including owner pay it would be too thin to pay for hires.
numbers: Roughly 30% seller discretionary margin is okay; $400k profit including owner comp would be too thin to hire.
