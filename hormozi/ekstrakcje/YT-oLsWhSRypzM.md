### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#01 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Hormozi left a consulting job at about 22-24 with roughly $50,000 saved and burned through it and several years of income.
detail: He was a defence-sector management consultant with a condo and nearly $50,000 saved. Quitting took six months of deliberation even with no spouse, children or dependants. He sold the condo, drove to California and started a gym; family disapproved. The savings went within three months on mentors, coaching programmes and a badly structured gym. For about four years he roughly broke even, earning less than as a consultant. He calls it partly luck.
numbers: About $50,000 saved, spent in the first 3 months; about 4 years at break-even or below consultant pay.

### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:14
claim: According to the Small Business Administration, median small-business owner income is about $49,000 a year and the average about $73,000.
detail: The median is the most likely outcome and roughly equals US median household income; the average is pulled up by top earners. He does not think many of his ex-employees who went out on their own make more than before, and they probably have more stress.
numbers: Median $49,000 per year; average $73,000 per year (US small business owners, SBA, as quoted).
### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:07:48
claim: Before quitting, work down a ladder of lower-risk fixes and quit only if dissatisfaction survives all of them.
detail: Steps: 1) change company if the employer is the problem; 2) change role to one that pays you to learn business skills; 3) if you like the work but the pay is low, ask the boss how you can provide more value, rather than asking for a raise; 4) build the business as a side venture with spend and outreach until it exceeds your salary; then jump.

### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:48
claim: Ask why you are dissatisfied: the company, the work itself, or the pay.
detail: Each answer has a fix that does not require starting a business. Added in the opening: ask whether you are dissatisfied enough with your life to uproot it, since entrepreneurship is lonely and little supported at the start.

### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:36
claim: Expect a three-to-five-year drop in income after leaving employment, and live cheaply so the drop does not cost you money.
detail: The payoff is long term: you reinvest in skills, beliefs and character and become a more valuable asset. Most people lack the risk tolerance and should not be entrepreneurs.
numbers: Income decrease of 3 to 5 years expected.

### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:11
claim: The largest risk of starting alone is not knowing what you are doing, so cut downside by learning first on someone else's payroll.
detail: Hormozi calls his own path stupid and lucky, because he paid for expert guidance early. Getting paid to learn is underrated, since school teaches nothing useful for business, but that pay is not meant to be lifetime income.

### YT-oLsWhSRypzM#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:45
claim: In his view, if the earlier steps fail, he would say go ahead and start a business when you have savings, a vehicle you know is tested with others doing it, and the ability to do it.
detail: These conditions come after the earlier steps have failed to remove your dissatisfaction. Side-hustle first to test fit before betting everything.
