### POD-251114-24#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:07
claim: Lead stages map one-to-one onto hiring: uncontacted, contacted, engaged, qualified, sold become uncontacted, contacted, engaged, interviewed, hired.
detail: Customer stages: advertise, incentivise to engage, qualify by problem and money, make an offer, get paid. Employee version: a job offer replaces the sales offer and acceptance replaces the close. Equivalents: warm outreach is asking your network, referral equals employee referral, affiliate equals guild or listserv, agency equals staffing firm.

claim: During training, pause rather than take over, praise correct execution of the checklist even when results are wrong, and fix the checklist immediately.
detail: Give no penalties; learning is punishing enough. If several steps fail, correct one step at a time until right.

### POD-251114-24#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:43
claim: In his own practice, depending on the business, he or his managers meet each lead-getting employee 6 to 11 times a week: one weekly coaching meeting plus short huddles at shift start and end.
detail: The weekly meeting is 30 to 45 minutes of coaching feedback and pay or success review. Huddles set expectations, then report back and allow daily praise. When problems or questions keep recurring, he adds them to the training checklist for the next hire; over time a team leader or manager can take over the huddles and eventually the one-on-ones.
numbers: 6-11 contacts per week (depending on the business); one 30-45 minute meeting.
