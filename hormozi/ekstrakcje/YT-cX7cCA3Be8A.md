### YT-cX7cCA3Be8A#01 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Keep the same bedtime and wake time on weekends as on weekdays.
detail: He used to wake early on weekdays and sleep in on weekends and found it did not work. To make 4 a.m. possible he books restaurant dinners around 5:45 so he is home early; friends know it. He argues entrepreneurs are more reactive and scattered, so basic regularity matters.
numbers: Wake about 4 a.m.; dinners seated around 5:45, home by about 7:30.

### YT-cX7cCA3Be8A#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Use the early hours for work on the business (big projects), not in it; after about nine o'clock expect interruptions.
detail: From about 4 to 9 a.m. he does self-work and then two to five dedicated hours on large projects. After 9 or 9:30 he still takes legal or personnel calls, so no protected CEO block exists mid-day. He calls this an investment of time versus an expense of time.
numbers: 4:00 to about 9:00 is protected; about 2 to 5 focused hours on projects.

### YT-cX7cCA3Be8A#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:14
claim: Give help first with no expectation, going further than asked, and the favours return later.
detail: Example: when asked about a topic, he connects the person with four contacts, pre-screens them, shares his view of two and his experience. This builds a stronger relationship and a stock of favours he can call on. He describes it as rooted partly in a wish to be liked.

### YT-cX7cCA3Be8A#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:21
claim: Expect zero return from the first 20 hours of a new skill, yet that is where about 70% of the gains come from.
detail: The fear of being bad blocks starting. After about 20 hours returns diminish sharply. His example: 20 hours of study on running Facebook ads would put you ahead of 99% of people who try. Pride then stops people from learning more.
numbers: About 70% of gains in the first 20 hours; 20 hours of study beats about 99% of marketers (his claim).

### YT-cX7cCA3Be8A#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:25
claim: Take absolute ownership of every situation; blaming anyone hands them your power.
detail: He applies it to his marriage, employees and the market, and tells clients to blame nothing on his company while he takes extreme ownership of their results. Anecdote from a nutrition PhD partner: customers get results from consistent sleep and consistent eating, whatever the diet.
