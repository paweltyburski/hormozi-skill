### YT-PxpPynt1mis#01 | DEFINITION | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Five S's of Advanced Compensation | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:40
claim: Phantom equity is a right to a share of a sale that vests only on a trigger such as a change of control, giving owner-like behaviour without transferring real shares.
detail: The employee pays nothing for it and is not taxed when it is granted, nor exposed to business liabilities, but payouts are generally taxed as ordinary income at the sale. Terms are negotiable: participation can be capped, proportional, or limited to sales of a majority stake so that a buyer keeps key staff.

### YT-PxpPynt1mis#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:56
claim: Use a vesting schedule with a cliff and forfeiture on leaving to protect the owner.
detail: Example: 5% over five years is 1% a year; with a one-year cliff, nothing vests for the first 12 months, then 1% at month 13, and later portions such as 0.25% a quarter. Leaving before vesting loses the unvested portion, which keeps people longer. Equity and profit distributions are separate levers: someone can have either one without the other.
numbers: 5% granted over 5 years; 1-year cliff; 0.25% per quarter after the cliff (example).

### YT-PxpPynt1mis#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:03
claim: Senior leaders on the executive track typically get 0.5-2% phantom equity; a true executive running about half the company may get 3-5%.
detail: Depends on business value, seniority and experience, per mentors he consulted.
numbers: 0.5-2% for senior leadership; 3-5% for an executive running around half the company.

### YT-PxpPynt1mis#04 | EVOLUTION | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:03:29
claim: He moved from loose equity giving, to refusing to give any, to a middle path: phantom equity sized to contribution, for key players only.
detail: Early on he gave away disproportionate chunks and paid for it. His lesson now is that making more money for others makes him more money, and that the best people want a stake. Sam Walton said the employee stock option program was one of the biggest influences on Walmart's growth.
### YT-PxpPynt1mis#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: For a role whose output can be measured (such as a marketer), pay for performance, not equity; give equity-like pay to positions that are hard to measure.
detail: A new owner wanted to give a marketer a share of the whole business; the conclusion was that a performance relationship fits better.
