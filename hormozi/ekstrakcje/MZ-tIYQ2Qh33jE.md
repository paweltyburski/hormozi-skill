### MZ-tIYQ2Qh33jE#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Chinese-language programme for Chinese Americans asks how to raise LTV once it has promised fluency.
detail: Hormozi: keep the destination fixed and sell the route (difficulty, speed, likelihood) as service levels. Education also graduates customers, which limits enterprise value unless new learners keep arriving.

### MZ-tIYQ2Qh33jE#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Ascension Path | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:40
claim: Upsell fluency in new silos, such as business or negotiation language, like 101 to 401 courses.
detail: Fluency is relative to a domain; customers fluent in one silo may want another.
