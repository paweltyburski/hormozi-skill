### YT-UGEc9-7X3OQ#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:38
claim: The advanced form of the give:ask ratio is content that only gives, with paid ads carrying every ask.
detail: Organic never asks, so the brand is protected and no ratio has to be balanced inside each piece. Ads do the calling to action. In a person's feed the 3:1 mix still holds: several free pieces from you, then one ad.

### YT-UGEc9-7X3OQ#09 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:10:02
claim: Brand is an association, with three elements: strength of influence, direction, and reach.
detail: A brand is a link between something people know and something they do not. Influence is how much it changes behaviour on sight; direction is toward or away; reach is how many people hold the association. The ideal is maximum people moving toward you.

### YT-UGEc9-7X3OQ#15 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: Five Topic Sources | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:08
claim: Find topics in four buckets: real-time notes, last week's calendar, far past belief changes, and manufactured stunts.
detail: Review every meeting from the week for problems solved. Far past means lessons that shifted your beliefs. Manufactured means challenges such as living on $100 for a month, closer to entertainment, which he rarely uses.

### YT-UGEc9-7X3OQ#17 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:18:46
claim: Gym Launch built its retention framework by interviewing its best-retaining gyms.
