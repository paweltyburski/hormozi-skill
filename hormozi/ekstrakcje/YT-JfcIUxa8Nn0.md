### YT-JfcIUxa8Nn0#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:56
claim: A mark of a newer operator is not repeating successful actions, either their own wins or the system they paid for.
detail: Two forms: doing something that worked then moving on to something else rather than doing more of it, and being told to do it a certain way and not repeating the winning action. The advice came from a mercenary CEO who had exited four companies: fundamentals (put the strong players in the middle and run), not fancy plays. He says people need reminders more than teaching.

### YT-JfcIUxa8Nn0#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:04
claim: Until you earn more than the person who taught you the system, do it exactly as outlined; change it only after matching their results.
detail: Their method probably already balances the same goals and drawbacks you have. Following the steps gives you the understanding to improve later; a first-try home run does not tell you why it worked. Not following paid advice is like burning money.
### YT-JfcIUxa8Nn0#03 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:04
claim: Two agency owners doing about $3 million a year saw better results after dropping their experiments and running the outlined play exactly.
detail: Asked for last month's wins, both said they tried many things and finally followed the plan as outlined. He gave the same advice to an 18-year-old neighbor who had just bought a real-estate flipping system and wanted to alter it.
numbers: ~$3M a year each (revenue not specified).

### YT-JfcIUxa8Nn0#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:54
claim: His team tested over 100 elements of a funnel in about three weeks, so he thinks the chance that someone's changed version beats it is probably low.
detail: Headlines, buttons, page flow and destinations were each changed multiple times. Once the conversion process is optimised he probably won't tweak it much and will put his attention on filling the top with traffic.
numbers: 100+ tested pieces in ~3 weeks.
### YT-JfcIUxa8Nn0#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:07
claim: Put about 90% of optimisation effort above the fold: the headline and first image can double to quadruple a page's conversion.
detail: The first one or two things a visitor reads swing results most; he has repeatedly wasted days on bullets and images below the fold, and deleting everything below the fold sometimes beat having it. Applies to desktop and mobile.
numbers: 2x-4x throughput from top-line changes; ~90% of attention above the fold.

claim: A new headline tripled an agency-partner landing page; removing a "you finished this step" headline on a scheduling page multiplied conversion by five.
detail: The removed line made people think they were done and not take the next step. Both changes were on the software partnership funnel.
numbers: 3x on the landing page; 5x on the scheduling page (step three).
