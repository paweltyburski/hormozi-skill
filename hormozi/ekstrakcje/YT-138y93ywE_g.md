### YT-138y93ywE_g#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Entrepreneurs, including legit businesses of roughly $1M to $50M, are limited not by tactics but by three behaviours: focus, patience and frustration tolerance.
detail: He observes owners visiting his headquarters weekly (businesses of roughly $1M, $10M and $50M) who seek tactics but are limited by character. Humans want new, now and easy, which is the opposite of what business needs: keep doing the old thing, delay reward and keep going after failure. Focus is doing one thing, patience is doing it for a long time, frustration tolerance is absorbing repeated hits. A focused fool beats a distracted genius.
numbers: Visiting owners range roughly $1M to $50M revenue.
### YT-138y93ywE_g#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:22
claim: To get detached judgment, ask what the most dangerous competitor would do in your exact situation.
detail: People give others better advice than they follow themselves (the Solomon paradox / third-party bias), because they lack emotional attachment when advising. Taking the competitor's view imports that distance, making you behave more logically.

### YT-138y93ywE_g#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:48
claim: Before acting on an urge to change the business, ask whether the action is to make the emotion go away or what the business needs; more often than not, keep doing what you were doing.
detail: Taking action often relieves anxiety, but that doesn't mean the action was correct. The result is whiplash for staff and confused marketing for customers, so the company stands for nothing. Emotions pass like weather; acknowledge them and continue.
### YT-138y93ywE_g#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:58
claim: Before starting a second business or revenue line, ask whether anything logically stops the existing business from doing twice, five times or ten times as much besides it being hard.
detail: The new thing seems easier only because it is unknown; it will be hard too. Choose known hard over unknown hard.
### YT-138y93ywE_g#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:01
claim: Great results come from "surface area of thinking": many repeated passes at one narrow problem in different moods and with different perspectives.
detail: Like coats of paint with time between them, repetition from different lenses gives a more complete and elegant solution. Patience means giving a problem that many passes; it requires not starting new projects.
