### MZ-PV7mSLeiEu4#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Choose "better" over "more" only when the incremental return on better exceeds that of more, per effort.
detail: With one salesperson, adding a second doubles sales and cuts single-person risk, beating a lift from 25% to 40% close. With 20 reps, lifting close from 25% to 30% (+20%) can be easier than adding four reps (+20%).
numbers: 25% to 40% close rate = +60%; 20 to 24 reps = +20%; 25% to 30% = +20%.
