### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:30
claim: The pain-cycle questions are a loop of what they tried, how long, how long ago, how it went, and what else.
detail: Repeat the loop until they say they have tried everything. Then empathise, tell them it is not their fault and that they are close, and only then move to the pitch. A script missing any of the clarify/label/overview milestones loses prospects without anyone seeing why.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-process | also: verticals | fw: Three Pillar Pitch | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:15
claim: In any niche the pitch sells three things; for a mortgage-lead seller they were exclusive, timely and qualified, set against Zillow leads.
detail: Each pillar is a short bullet, then a commitment question, then a memorised 30-second story. Fitness example: motivation is the past failure, so compare the programme to a favourite TV show you look forward to watching.
numbers: Pitch under 3 minutes (180 seconds).

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#05 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:46
claim: The decision-maker close runs in four moves: ask what if they say no, ask the partner's likely objection, cite past agreements, then ask forgiveness not permission.
detail: First ask what happens if the partner says no (some go ahead). Then ask what the partner's biggest objection would be and answer it as if present. Next use past agreements: the partner already knows the problem, so why oppose solving it. Last, take the card, delay the charge to Friday, and promise to tear up the contract if the partner objects.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:31
claim: Handle a stall by teaching them how to decide, with yes-no criteria.
detail: Can it meet your needs, are you more likely to succeed with us, do you want to work with us, and do you have access to funds or know someone who does. People stall from fear of a wrong decision.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:15:35
claim: Conviction framework: words persuade the logical brain, tone persuades the emotional brain that actually decides.
detail: Reps who followed the script still failed because of tone, drawn from a book on hidden dialogue. A rising pitch turns a statement into a question; lowering the voice signals importance. Training tone takes years, so the shortcut is conviction: if the rep believes, the tone corrects itself.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#08 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:17:57

detail: He consulted a nine-person sales floor. Asked a rep how good the leads were; the vague answer showed lack of belief, since a believer would rave. Reps who start strong often drop after seeing negative reviews or support complaints.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#10 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: product-delivery | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:19:46
claim: Rebuild conviction daily: read fresh testimonials aloud, own product flaws, and bring support staff to sales meetings when sales dip.
detail: Reps get hung up on daily, so affirm their impact. Do not blame customers for poor results; fix the product. When sales slip, have the head of customer support describe the onboarding care customers get, which restores reps' belief.

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#12 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:11
claim: Daily 60-minute training splits into script out loud, obstacle drills and call review.
detail: Read the question script aloud with correct tone for 25 minutes, restarting after any mistake; drill objection overcomes for 5 minutes; listen to a 30-minute recording asking what went right, what went wrong and what to do next. Memorise only the 30-second stories and the objection answers, which also speeds onboarding.
numbers: 60 minutes a day, five days a week (25 + 5 + 30).

### YT-oi7bnS8uyJM#13 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:38
claim: Give reps one fix at a time, and replay wins as highlight footage in a daily wrap-up.
detail: Golf-swing analogy: a list of corrections paralyses, one thumb adjustment can be applied. Sales directors ask what went right, what went wrong and what to change. Weekly one-on-ones for new reps, every other week for experienced ones. Gong is recommended for Zoom calls.
