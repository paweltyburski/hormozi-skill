### MZ-TNS8E02GR0U#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A wellness dentist with a coaching platform wants to bring client practices into a dental support group.
detail: He believes combined groups gain buying power and a higher valuation. Hormozi had bid on a similar business and lost to private equity.
numbers: Practice revenue $4M; coaching about $1M; goal: $100M valuation in about three years.

### MZ-TNS8E02GR0U#02 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Offer practice owners a share of the multiple uplift from selling together and lock the rights up front.
detail: Alone a practice sells around 4x; combined perhaps 10-12x. Offer 4x, then 7x with you, you keep everything above 7x. Start with four or five practices for proof, then 25. Take an option on 100% plus drag-along rights agreed in advance, since the greediest holdout hurts everyone.
numbers: Standalone about 4x EBITDA; combined 10-12x; owners get up to 7x; start with 4-5 practices.

### MZ-TNS8E02GR0U#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: Keep the coaching business as the goose and sell the eggs, licensing its IP to the acquirer.
detail: Coaching consolidates operations, which is about three quarters of a roll-up's work. Keep it in a separate entity and license it for a limited period or territory.
