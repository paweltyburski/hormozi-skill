### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#01 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:19
claim: A managed-IT services firm for 30-100 person companies ($2M, $7,500 per month, three-year contracts) has no sales or marketing function and the owner closes every deal.
detail: A new SEO agency produced about 10 first appointments, six unqualified; long cycles come from many stakeholders. Goal is $10M by 2030.
numbers: $2M revenue; $7,500 per month ticket; 3-year contracts; 10 appointments, 4 qualified (40%).

### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:24
claim: Have prospects fill out a short pre-call form (such as employee count) and cancel calls from non-qualifying shops.
detail: Forty percent qualified is a good rate for this segment, so SEO is working; keep going.

### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:55
claim: PPC channels existing demand with warmer leads but higher cost; social ads create demand; choose one channel and stick to it.
detail: PPC needs a more dialed sales motion. Other channels: affiliates (businesses who already own your customers), LinkedIn content, outbound to centers of influence.

### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:44
claim: Contact PPC leads within 60 seconds by double dial and text; Meta leads tolerate about five minutes.
numbers: 60 seconds for PPC leads; about 5 minutes for Meta leads.
detail: PPC is highly competitive and the first responder wins.

### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:24
claim: Cold traffic needs added steps: a warm "buy our product" offer fails with strangers, so add a try-first offer to turn cold into warm.
detail: Order of fixes is offer, sales motion, then traffic. Content-driven businesses that scale into cold traffic fail because their warm motion fits warm audiences only. He calls the added friction a "microwave".

### MZ-wQYg_7ooaUs#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:05
claim: The founder should learn to sell and never outsource traffic, conversion or delivery; new process with old person, then duplicate.
detail: Legal, accounting and insurance can be outsourced. A founder converts better than a hired seller because of conviction and knowledge, and selling improves marketing.
