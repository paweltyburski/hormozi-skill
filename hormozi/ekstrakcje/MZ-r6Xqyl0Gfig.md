### MZ-r6Xqyl0Gfig#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A firm with 50 to 60 joint-venture partners had only a 10,000-person list because every partner trade was reciprocal.
detail: New leads get a short sequence and then promote other partners, burning the list. Partners with bigger lists send two or three times more leads than he can return.
numbers: JV partners grew from 20 to 50 in a year; list 5,000 four years ago, now 10,000; his top affiliate sent 10,000 opt-ins.

### MZ-r6Xqyl0Gfig#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:16
claim: Pay affiliates with money rather than reciprocal promotion, and segment the list instead of promoting everything to all.
detail: Reciprocity limits you to peers with lists as large as yours.
