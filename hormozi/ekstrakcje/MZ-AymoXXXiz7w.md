### MZ-AymoXXXiz7w#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: money-models | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:17
claim: The owner of a diet brand with a shut-down meal-prep company and 120,000 YouTube followers should licence the method through a B2B certification.
detail: Hormozi would start from the highest value he could give and work back. A certification licensing the IP at no extra cost per licensee turns graduates into the affiliate base that coaches clients; the recurring meals sit behind it. Content has a certification stage on coaching, then a business stage on selling.
numbers: 120,000 YouTube followers; goal of $1M a month.

### MZ-AymoXXXiz7w#02 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: content-brand | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:50

detail: One click changes old videos that still get daily views. The webinar qualifies prospects before they book a call.
numbers: Certification price suggested about $10,000; webinar clip 30 minutes.
