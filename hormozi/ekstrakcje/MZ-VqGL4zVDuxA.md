### MZ-VqGL4zVDuxA#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: Hormozi's personal practice: he expects the business to still scale with a normal employee producing one half or a third of his own output; if so, it can scale without him. Where he is strong, he builds in padding.
detail: His example: if the plan needs an 80% close rate, a normal employee is not going to hit that when the price is set appropriately, so the numbers must make sense against what else you could do with your time.
numbers: For him: normal employee at one half to one third of his own production.
