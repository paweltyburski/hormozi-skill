### POD-0444#01 | EVOLUTION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:06
claim: He once thought outearning his mentors proved he was better; he now credits their head start and honours teachers rather than gloating.
detail: He says mentees and mentors should both see it as a head start passed on, so mentees stop disparaging mentors and mentors stop holding back secrets.
### POD-0444#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:40
claim: If you run an education business and none of your students surpass you, you are not really teaching or not sharing the real drivers.
detail: The usual explanation (that you are better than everyone) is ego. Teaching works when students move through the path faster than it took you, then the two work as peers on the new landscape.
applies_to: Course, coaching and education businesses.

### POD-0444#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:31
claim: He spends about half his working day writing books and outlining frameworks, accepting less business growth in order to document.
detail: Reasons: written material outlasts courses, and he forgets nuance and details if he does not document while still operating. His firm earns by buying minority stakes (implementation), while the know-how is free.
numbers: About 50% of his day on documenting and writing.
