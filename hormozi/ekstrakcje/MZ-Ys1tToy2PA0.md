### MZ-Ys1tToy2PA0#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A residential solar company buying third-party leads wants one cheaper channel to master.
detail: Hormozi calls the question too vague and lists options: door knocking, search ads, interruption ads on Meta, YouTube or TikTok.

### MZ-Ys1tToy2PA0#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Search ads need little creative but become a bidding contest on LTV; interruption ads have less competition but need constant new creative.
detail: Search: one winning ad can run for months because new people search monthly; rivals bid on the same terms, so the biggest LTV wins. Interruption: people were not looking, so you sell in a vacuum and pay less per lead. He prefers interruption; at scale use both.

### MZ-Ys1tToy2PA0#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: paid-ads | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Interruption-based ad creative needs to be refreshed about every 3 weeks in a local market; nationally he says you can sometimes get away with every six weeks, sometimes every eight.
detail: Depending on market size, a larger market lets creative last longer.
numbers: Local about every 3 weeks; national sometimes every 6 weeks, sometimes every 8.
### MZ-Ys1tToy2PA0#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Hormozi typically picks the first channel by asking where your skill deficiency is smallest.
