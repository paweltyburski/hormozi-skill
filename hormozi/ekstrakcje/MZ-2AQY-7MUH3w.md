### MZ-2AQY-7MUH3w#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Two physical therapy clinics ($1.2M, 10% margin) are fully booked, short of providers, and have no sales process.
detail: All insurance-paid; new patients wait one to two months. Hormozi: sell packages to a goal, not single sessions, and move to cash.
numbers: $1.2M revenue; ~10% margin; $3,000-$5,000 packages possible.

### MZ-2AQY-7MUH3w#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:59
claim: Send a video sales letter after booking (promise, proof, pain), take an ID for a card at arrival, show a second VSL after assessment, then close.
detail: First VSL: pain list, cases by avatar, four-step plan, old way versus new way, and each belief answered (what they think, why wrong, what is right, proof). At the desk, trade the ID for a card. Intake form asks the same pain question many ways. Second VSL uses new examples; a patient coordinator closes.

### MZ-2AQY-7MUH3w#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:40
claim: When a payer fixes your price, margin comes only from cost control, like a discount retailer.
detail: Insurers price near break-even. Remove every cost except the scarce resource (providers) and pay them more to scale. At $5M he expects maybe $500K-$750K profit with five locations.
numbers: $5M revenue could net $500K-$750K.

### MZ-2AQY-7MUH3w#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:06
claim: Bridge to cash: ask each patient whether they want the best care or only what insurance covers.
detail: Then take them through the process, explaining insurance covers part but this path gets them better.
