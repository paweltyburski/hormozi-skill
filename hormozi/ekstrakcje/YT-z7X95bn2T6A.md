### YT-z7X95bn2T6A#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His deal team used AI-personalised videos to lift meeting show-up rates and an AI writing tool to cut follow-up emails.
detail: One recorded video was re-synced so he appears to say each prospect's name. Follow-up emails took 10-20 minutes each, over two hours a day, which the tool removed, worth about 25% more output over a 40-hour week at the same cost.
numbers: 10-20 minutes per follow-up email; 2+ hours/day saved; about 25% more output.

### YT-z7X95bn2T6A#02 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: His content team used three tools (voice clone, image/audio editing, transcript-based video editing) to reach 250 pieces a week.
detail: The voice clone, trained on about 10 minutes of audio, makes audio versions of emails and tweets without him recording, saving him interruptions. Transcript editing lets editors jump to a spoken line instead of scrubbing, leaving time for packaging and music choices, which he says moves a video from 100,000 to a million views.
numbers: 250 pieces of content per week; voice model trained on about 10 minutes.

### YT-z7X95bn2T6A#03 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops, retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:11
claim: Onboarding videos made by an AI avatar can be updated by editing text, removing re-shoots and hundreds of hours of training labour.
detail: Technology plus education raise worker productivity. Recruiters also used AI-personalised outreach to approach employed top talent, noting job boards mainly reach dissatisfied people, plus an AI note-taker for interviews.
numbers: note-taking saved 10-15 minutes per call per day.

### YT-z7X95bn2T6A#04 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:48
claim: Operations used AI meeting notes and an AI calendar tool that fills the gaps between meetings, claimed to raise company output about 40%.
detail: An AI notetaker attends internal meetings and a consolidated summary reaches executives in roughly a tenth of the time. The scheduler reshuffles calendars to avoid 15-45 minute dead gaps and replaces thousands of coordination emails.
numbers: notes read in about 1/10 of the time; claimed 40% output gain from calendar optimisation.
