### MZ-wQA6vpgMtlw#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Present your premium as the retailer's marketing budget: you bring traffic and extra purchases.
detail: Show how many people walk in daily for your product and how many other items they buy, so price stops mattering. Retailers pay rent for passers-by and bear acquisition cost; be their lead generation.
numbers: Example claim: buyers buy two other items on average.
