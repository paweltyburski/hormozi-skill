### YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY#01 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand, outreach | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:52
claim: Self-publishing author afraid of not selling 1,000 copies: Hormozi says the fear comes from wanting a guarantee, so stop waiting for certainty and test the marketplace, which always answers.
detail: The author had missed goals on earlier books. Advice: pick one path, commit to it, and judge success by actions executed, not sales.

### YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Set large goals around actions you control: a daily time commitment that would make missing the sales number unreasonable, then score yourself on adherence.
detail: Thought experiment: if you had to sell 10,000 books in 12 months or lose your life, you would use every method (one-to-one, content, ads, affiliates, referrals, agencies, employees). If you do the whole plan for a year and sell 999, it is a win. If you sell 90, you learn what works and do 100 times more of it on this book or the next. The skill gained is the outcome.
numbers: Target 1,000 books; commitment 4 hours of promotion every day.

### YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: For a book, contact people who already own an audience on the topic; even if one sells only 20 copies, reach a thousand of them.
detail: Treat volume of outreach as the lever. Daily four hours of promotion gets recognised by the market.
numbers: One partner selling 20 books, times 1,000 partners (illustrative).

### YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: Many-Sided Die | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:30

### YT-0_Gf5v8DEMY#05 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals, sales-process | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:13
claim: Service owner with a 100,000-follower Instagram audience, two years of daily posts, and cash-flow pressure asks how to balance "give until they ask" with paying bills.
detail: Answer: give in public, sell in private. When people reply to stories, DM or ask questions, define a scope of free help, then offer a call for more. Add light calls to action in comments or stories ("if you need help with that, I can help") without damaging goodwill.
