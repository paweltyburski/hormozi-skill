### POD-0782#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:30
claim: Hormozi's quarterly-planning filter: What (more customers, higher LTGP, or lower risk), How (more, better or new), Who (one owner and a deadline).
detail: What: every initiative must ladder to one of the three; otherwise park it (e.g. staff asking to redesign a site that converts). If it does, ask how many, by how much, at what time and effort, how likely, and whether another option gives more for the same investment — usually one does. How: see #02. Who: see #03.

### POD-0782#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:15
claim: Hormozi: nine times in ten, especially below ~$1M revenue, the answer is "more"; as you grow, "better" and "more" alternate; "new" comes only when neither more nor better is possible.
detail: Going from one salesperson to two can double sales; with 20 reps a 20% improvement may be easier than hiring about five more. After a "better" gain you may return to "more". Owners love new things, usually the last thing needed; do 10 times more of a working channel.
