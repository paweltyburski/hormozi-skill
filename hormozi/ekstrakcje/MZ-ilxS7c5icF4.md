### MZ-ilxS7c5icF4#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:39
claim: A helicopter tour operator turned profitable only after becoming a broker of other firms' unused capacity.
detail: He first owned machines and flew himself, doing marketing and service, and lost money for two years. He then sold the aircraft, dropped the pilots, hired capacity from others, and uses contractors for marketing and phone sales. He works about an hour a day on escalations.
numbers: Revenue $1.5M, profit $250K-$300K last year; losses of $50K in year one and $30K in year two; less than 1% recurring customers; two sites (Melbourne, Sydney).
applies_to: Niche location-bound service company.

### MZ-ilxS7c5icF4#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:54
claim: If the ultimate version of the business is not one you want to own, plan your exit now.
detail: Not wanting a $500M helicopter company is a non-negotiable mismatch, like a partner who does or does not want children, not a temptation to resist. Build the business you want to own instead. Hormozi thinks the demand is durable and hard to replace with AI.
numbers: Owner's personal goal $500M revenue; Hormozi says a global version with a better acquisition model could reach it.

### MZ-ilxS7c5icF4#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:09
claim: A business where the owner is effectively the only salesperson has limited sale value.
detail: It becomes a real business only when someone else runs sales. If they do, the natural buyer is that person, so the practical exit is to hand it to them.

### MZ-ilxS7c5icF4#04 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:55
claim: Exit to contractors or staff by selling them the profit stream, or by giving half the profit for no further involvement.
detail: Option one: they pay you the profit for two years, then own it, and you teach them. Option two, favoured by Hormozi: give them half the profit and they never call you, so your cash flow stays.
numbers: Two years of profit as payment; or 50% of profit.

### MZ-ilxS7c5icF4#05 | SCRIPT | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:43
claim: Test a successor by role-playing crises and checking that every answer is "do not call me".
detail: Offer half the business in exchange for the phone never ringing. Run scenarios one after another (a customer injured, an aircraft breaking down mid-flight). Each correct answer avoids escalating to you. Once they pass, sell or finance the half.
