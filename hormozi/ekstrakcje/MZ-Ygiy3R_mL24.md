### MZ-Ygiy3R_mL24#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:49
claim: Keep trading 40 hours of doing for 2 hours of managing, then repeat one level up.
detail: Training a salesperson dips revenue short term but buys time back; at 40 hours of management, hand that to someone for 2.
numbers: 40 hours selling for 2 hours managing.

### MZ-Ygiy3R_mL24#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:34
claim: The swamp ($1M to $3M revenue) is financially the hardest stage.
detail: Owner income is about half of the take and profit the rest; the choice is working more or betting almost all profit on one hire.
numbers: Example 25% margin: half of profit is pay, half is the business's bet.
