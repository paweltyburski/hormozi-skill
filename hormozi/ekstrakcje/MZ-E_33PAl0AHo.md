### MZ-E_33PAl0AHo#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A caller reports a $22 monthly membership at a $90 CPA with about 0.25 ROAS; Hormozi says that even a ~$110 LTV against $90 spend is a bad business.
detail: Assuming 20% churn, LTV is about $22 x 5 = $110. He wants about 2.5x conversion improvement to have half of buyers pay a second time within 30 days, and ideally 4x.
numbers: Caller's CPA $90; price $22/month; 52 purchases; LTV ~$110; need 2.5x-4x conversion.
### MZ-E_33PAl0AHo#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models, sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:11
claim: Four conversion points for a community: onboarding call, DM with a higher-tier offer, weekly Q&A as a soft pitch, and a scripted video lesson.
detail: The DM can sell a pricier second group. The weekly call gives value and a soft CTA to personalise. The video or webinar with slides is the first thing consumed.

### MZ-E_33PAl0AHo#03 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:24
claim: Five-module sequence: origin story and opportunity, then three concerns (circumstances, other people, themselves), then the call to buy.
detail: Each concern module answers one belief barrier, ending with the offer.
numbers: Five modules.

### MZ-E_33PAl0AHo#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: paid-ads | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:11
