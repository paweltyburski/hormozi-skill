### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A single-location music school (about 20 years old, group, ensemble and one-on-one) wants a national online and in-person presence; Hormozi called it an entirely new business.
detail: Going from service business to media business changes everything except the music topic. A gym for music, same mechanics. Caution: ask whether the owner has paid-traffic experience, since that would change the answer.

### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:24
claim: Judge an expansion by the skill deficiency between what the owner can do today and what the new model needs; usually repeat the proven model.
detail: The owner already makes an impact, so the real goal is more money; open a second and third location where risks are controllable. Advice: reinvest in a second location and expand corporate-owned that way.

### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:08
claim: A mass-market online education business is a low-ticket media arbitrage game requiring skills a service owner lacks.
detail: Needed skills: organic content, branding, direct response, membership community tech. High ticket will not fit selling massive volume. It becomes finding music influencers to post about you, which only works with standout credibility such as a Grammy.

### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#04 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:14
claim: A CEO of an 18-year-old fast-growing company asked how Hormozi protects long maker blocks and whether he creates key-man risk.

Hormozi: yes, he is key-man risk at Acquisition.com, which is acceptable if you do not plan to sell (like Musk at Tesla); sellable businesses need the opposite. His maker time exists because Layla runs the rest.

### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:06

### MZ-G0HQtTYZ25M#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: management-ops | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:16
claim: To place a founder-CEO he asks who holds the visionary role and where the person's functional expertise lies (product, marketing, sales).
