### MZ-oNuUNal2_Es#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Small-business owners who get excited about AI often start an AI company, a mistake that puts them against the biggest firms in a field they know less well.
detail: Instead, make parts of the existing business even 10% more efficient and compound. A dry cleaner should win at dry cleaning, not build an operating system for dry cleaners; rivals are probably misusing AI, so competing locally is easier.

### MZ-oNuUNal2_Es#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:59
claim: Using AI to do the wrong things faster just adds token costs, as with meeting summaries when the real need is deciding the next most important task.
detail: He says he has never needed notes to reach his goals.

### MZ-oNuUNal2_Es#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:48
claim: Shift each function from org-chart thinking to workflow thinking, and let AI take over most of the workflows a person no longer needs to do.
detail: Example: an editor in six workflows now handles three, with AI doing most of the other three, giving 3x to 5x output. Revenue per head in service firms should fall and margins rise.
numbers: Output gains of 3x-5x (his example).

### MZ-oNuUNal2_Es#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: Keep AI invisible: run a service business that looks human-staffed, charge human prices, and bank the margin gap while prices have not yet adjusted.
detail: He calls this the good game; the window exists because market prices will not adjust for a while.

### MZ-oNuUNal2_Es#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:18
claim: Rebuilding existing software yourself is a trap: spending 200 hours to replace a $9-a-month scheduling tool creates fragile code.
detail: A single error can cost thousands; many tools already include AI features, such as a CRM with smarter targeting, for free.
numbers: $9 per month tool versus 200 hours of building; error cost about $9,000 (his example).
