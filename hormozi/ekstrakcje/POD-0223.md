### POD-0223#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Every touchpoint of the sales process must match the image you want to project, or the illusion collapses.
detail: Lesson from Dan Kennedy's sales book written with a brothel operator: staff voices, first email, wording and follow-up all fit a top-tier service. If one part clashes, the premium image breaks.

### POD-0223#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:12
claim: Facing a gym that was really five rooms in two rented apartments, Hormozi reframed it as a private, discreet transformation centre and sold on that story.
detail: The owner had promised something upscale; the site was in DC, with barred windows and dirty stairs. He told prospects privacy suits beginners who dislike being watched and suits clients who wish to stay unseen. Close rates went up.
numbers: 191 people sold at that site (from his video title; unclear in audio).
applies_to: Gym Launch in-person sales trips.

### POD-0223#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:00
claim: For anything that looks like a negative, craft a story that flips it and positions you better.
detail: The story behind the story must be sincerely believed first; the rest of the sales process stays as usual.

### POD-0223#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Conviction, not slogans, separates strong salespeople; build it in yourself first by deciding what type of company you are.
detail: Many founders say they want to change lives but are not convinced they are the best. Prospects sense conviction between the words.

### POD-0223#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:00
claim: Sell new salespeople on the company's story first, so they speak with the weight of the brand, as luxury-brand staff do.
detail: Show why you are positioned as world class, then choreograph the whole process to reinforce it. If you do not define the story, one gets made for you.
