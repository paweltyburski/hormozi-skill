### MZ-CybFcg9feiA#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A disability-support provider with $1.5M revenue gets leads without ads but faces government rate caps and thin margins.
detail: No advertising for 18 months; referrals bring more than ops can deliver. Gross margin 25-45%, EBITDA about 15% (about $200K+). An average client pays about $1,000 a week, or about $50K a year, giving about $7,500 profit.
numbers: Gross margin 25-45%; EBITDA 15%; $1,000 per week per client; about $7,500 yearly profit per client.

### MZ-CybFcg9feiA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:45
claim: Under rate caps, turn down low-margin demand for a season while automating operations so margin can reach about 30%.
detail: Reframe it as filling the tank, not slowing. Say you are busy and offer a wait list starting in three months (a 90-day sprint), with an optional one-time fee to join to prove seriousness.
numbers: Margin target from 15% to about 30%; wait list 90 days.
