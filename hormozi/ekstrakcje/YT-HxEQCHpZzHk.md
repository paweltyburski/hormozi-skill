### YT-HxEQCHpZzHk#03 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A "boom" is an order-of-magnitude change in the business, as opposed to an optimisation, which is capped at 100%.
detail: Raising close rate or opt-in rate by 10% is an optimisation. Advertising can multiply the number of people who learn about you by 100x. Small owners get stuck in an "optimisation mousetrap" (moving 10.1:1 to 10.2:1) when more leads would help far more.
