### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Influence, meaning the likelihood that an audience complies with a request, comes from four stackable elements: status, power, credibility and likeness (SPCL).
detail: Each element works alone, but the most influence comes from stacking all four. They are continuums, not yes/no traits. Influence is also specific to a domain: a person strong on all four for business has little influence on fashion. Content should be designed to show all four so viewers are more likely to watch the next piece and respond to any ask.

### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#02 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Status means controlling a resource other people want in a given setting.
detail: Illustrated with a bartender in a busy bar (status inside the bar, none on the street) and an heir whose money gives some status even though unearned. Defined in observable terms: what you can see someone control, not an emotional label.

### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#03 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:32
claim: Power is built from say-do correspondence: you tell people to do something, they do it, and something good follows.
detail: Each such cycle makes the next request more likely to be followed. Hormozi would pick power over the other three if he had to keep one. His example is someone who gives stock picks that all rise, which beats a rich heir who only has money. In content, power means giving directions that visibly work for those who follow them.

### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#04 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Credibility is third-party, observable evidence, and one event can supply status, credibility and power at once.
detail: A sold company or a record launch gives money (status), outside confirmation (credibility), and later, when followers copy the method and succeed, power. He paid Guinness adjudicators to attend his book launch so the record would be verified by an organisation whose business rests on trust.

### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:24
claim: His forward media strategy is live and interactive, because real-time, unscripted formats give the most reinforcement time with prospects.
detail: Reasoning: the internet drifts toward what is true, so raw formats beat polished ones. Shorts are a way for strangers to sample you, longs convert them, and live is stronger still. The aim is time with prospects, not views.

### YT-dMZ-n2KSlxE#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:18:33
