### POD-0179#01 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Trial with Penalty | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:07
claim: At the close, ask for ID first so the prospect takes out their wallet before you request the card.
detail: Say you need their ID; once the wallet is out they cannot claim it is missing. Check details, chat briefly, then ask which card they want and gesture toward the wallet, like a trade.

### POD-0179#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Believe that almost nobody really leaves home without a card; the "forgot it" line is usually an objection.
detail: People forget their wallet perhaps twice a year, so the rep's conviction should drive the delivery.

### POD-0179#03 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: Trial with Penalty | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:57
claim: When a trial prospect asks why a card is needed for something free, say it is how it has always been done.
detail: Shrug lightly, then move straight to asking which card they prefer. Avoid the word "policy"; the phrase implies precedent and social proof, and that the matter is out of the rep's hands.

### POD-0179#04 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: If they lack the card they want to use, take any card now and let them swap it at the next visit.
detail: Start them immediately and close with "fair enough?".

### POD-0179#05 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:16
claim: If the wallet is in the car, joke that the workout starts now and send them to fetch it.
detail: With strong rapport, offer to walk out with them; Hormozi calls that slightly sketchy. Some people leave the wallet behind on purpose to avoid signing up.
