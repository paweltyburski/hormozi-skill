### POD-230819-11#04 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Bonus Checklist | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:28
claim: In one-to-one selling, ask for the sale before mentioning bonuses, and reveal them only after a yes.
detail: Revealing them after the signature gives a wow moment and reinforces the decision. If the prospect says no, pick the bonus that matches their stated obstacle, agree with them, add it and ask whether that concession is fair. Repeat with further bonuses; reciprocity makes refusing harder.
applies_to: One-on-one selling (group selling works differently).

### POD-230819-11#09 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Unconditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:41
claim: Broad unconditional guarantees suit low-ticket consumer offers; higher-ticket and business offers should lean towards specific guarantees.
detail: At low prices many people will not bother claiming a refund. As ticket and fulfilment cost rise, move to guarantees with conditions or without refunds. He adds that an unconditional guarantee needs you to be good at fulfilment, and it is the highest form because you could do everything right and still be refunded.

### POD-230819-11#11 | CASE | topic: offers | also: paid-ads, unit-economics | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:23:49
claim: A $25,000 buy-out guarantee on a roughly $2,997 ecommerce ad course lifted sales by about $3M, at a cost of ten payouts.
