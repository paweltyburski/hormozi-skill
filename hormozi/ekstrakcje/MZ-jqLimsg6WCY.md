### MZ-jqLimsg6WCY#02 | SCRIPT | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:45
claim: A roll-up offer is an arbitrage: owners sell alone at one multiple and with you at a higher one, and you keep a share of the difference.
detail: Example: alone at 4x, with you at 9x; you give them 7 and keep 2. Negotiate decision authority and tag-along rights up front; reject sellers who refuse for spite.
numbers: 4x alone vs 9x aggregate; owner gets 7x, roll-up keeps 2x.
