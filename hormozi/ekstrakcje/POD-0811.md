### POD-0811#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A personal wardrobe-and-styling service for people in their 40s to mid-60s sells a high-priced onboarding package followed by retainer styling.
detail: Four years in business, 1,000+ clients over the founder's career, about 50/50 men and women. A 60-day onboarding, then a monthly or quarterly plan with lookbooks and on-demand text help. She had dropped to one luxury tier because higher tiers earned more than one-off work. Goal: $5M, possibly via stylists in other cities. Main problem: not enough leads.
numbers: Revenue ~$309,000/year, profit ~$130,000 (42% net margin); onboarding $8,500 over 60 days; then $2,000/month or $4,500/quarter; ~10% of revenue from vendor commissions.
applies_to: Premium service business, solo founder, ~$300K revenue.

### POD-0811#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:12
claim: Strong unit economics despite a leaky funnel told him the problem was lead volume and conversion, not the model.
detail: Continuity had almost no churn. A ratio this good despite inefficiency means the leak is in the lead process.
numbers: Blended CAC $600; LTV $10,000/year; LTV:CAC 16:1; close rate 50%; show rate 100%.

### POD-0811#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:12
claim: Before judging a paid channel, ask what the click lands on (prices shown? plain opt-in?) and how many clicks became leads.
detail: Google spend landed on an opt-in page without prices, and the owner did not state how many leads it produced. Because searches like "personal stylist near me" are intent-based, he guessed at least 250 to 500 opt-ins from the clicks, unless the offer on the page was a big mismatch.
numbers: $3,400 Google spend for the year; $1.89 per click; ~1,800 clicks; 8 sales; guessed at least 250-500 opt-ins (roughly 14% to 28% of clicks, ~20+/month).

### POD-0811#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:12:58
claim: Systems break whenever volume triples (the "rule of three and ten"), so plan growth in triples: $300K to $1M first.
detail: $300K to $5M is about 17x. Several available fixes were each roughly a triple. Unless an order-of-magnitude change exists, expect the business to change at each tripling.
numbers: $300K to $5M = ~17x; $300K to $1M = 3x.

### POD-0811#08 | TACTIC | topic: outreach | also: lead-getters | fw: List Building (Scrape, Buy, Elbow Grease) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:29:24
claim: Build outreach lists by segment of professionals who already serve the same customers: high-end trainers, executive coaches, matchmakers, divorce attorneys.
detail: Find them via Google, Instagram search and list brokers (search "list broker" plus city, take a national list, narrow to locality). They can also be affiliates since their audiences already buy related services. About half of the service can be remote, widening geography.
