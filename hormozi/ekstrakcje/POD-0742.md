### POD-0742#08 | DEFINITION | topic: offers | also: - | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:20:21
claim: Effort means things the buyer must start doing; sacrifice means things they must stop doing that they enjoy.
detail: In personal training, effort is waking early and being sore; sacrifice is giving up sleeping in or a favourite meal. List both for each customer step, plus anything adding risk or delay, and build a solution for each. Also, B2B offers cost more because they tie to return on investment.
