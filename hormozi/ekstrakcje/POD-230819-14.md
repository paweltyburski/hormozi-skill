### POD-230819-14#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:51
claim: Worked constraint example: improving the weakest funnel step by 5 points gives a 2x lead gain against 16% and 10% elsewhere.
detail: Funnel: 30% opt-in, 5% apply, 50% schedule. Opt-in to 35% is a 16% relative gain; apply to 10% is a 100% gain; scheduling to 55% is a 10% gain. If unsure where the constraint is, find the step where the most leads drop off.
numbers: Opt-in 30% to 35% (+16%); apply 5% to 10% (+100%); schedule 50% to 55% (+10%).

### POD-230819-14#07 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: management-ops | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:56
claim: Run one split test per platform each Monday, log every result, and move on to the next constraint if four tries or one month fail to beat the control.
detail: Why one at a time: otherwise you cannot tell what worked; steps affect each other; it forces prioritising meaningful tests; and a week is long enough to see a change without wasting time on the next constraint. On the next Monday: choose winners, write results in a master log so later work starts far ahead, and set the next challenger.
numbers: 1 test per platform per week; 4 tries or 1 month before moving on.
