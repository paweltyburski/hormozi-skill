### MZ-AF0-UjuKGjs#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A custom screen-print and embroidery e-commerce firm ($17.5M) uses 80% of its capacity on one contract customer worth about 45% of revenue, yet its own business is about three times as profitable.
detail: Goal is $50M then $100M. The contract customer is also a competitor. Production is in-house in Kentucky and about 25% of capacity is free. Constraint is more ad creative, not spend or LTV:CAC.
numbers: Revenue $17.5M; contract customer 45% of revenue, 80% of capacity; own business 3x margin; 25% free capacity; margin about 20%.

### MZ-AF0-UjuKGjs#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: Hormozi says his firm never stops a flow until it creates another one, and he advised this caller not to kill her big contract customer; he suggested using her spare capacity to double the more profitable side.
detail: He ideally prefers not to stop flows at all. The caller, not Hormozi, said the whale keeps her workflow steady.
### MZ-AF0-UjuKGjs#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: paid-ads | also: hiring-talent | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:42
claim: Aim for about 50 ads a week, which three editors costing around $200K total can produce.
detail: Spend now is about $25,000 a month with roughly 15 ads. Hire editors with organic backgrounds; the only change you'd probably make is maybe adding a lower-third CTA to an existing winning organic piece, or you can slice a CTA with the lead magnet onto the end.
numbers: 50 ads per week; $25K ad spend per month; 3 editors for $200K total; profit about $3-4M.
