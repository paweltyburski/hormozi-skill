### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Choose a physical product that is cheap to make, consumed regularly and needs no willpower.
detail: Nose strips cost pennies and sell for about 100 times cost; repeat use gives auto-ship potential; sleep is a daily activity, unlike supplements that must be remembered. To him, beauty products are by and large more interesting than fitness ones.
numbers: Example cost 2 cents, price $1 (about 50x; he says ~100x).
applies_to: Physical product launches
### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:10
claim: At $20 a pack with a $4 shipping cost, he expects about $16 gross profit per sale, an 80% margin.
detail: Retail $19.99-$24.99 for 30, 60 or 90 packs, split-tested; competitors sell at about 30 cents a strip. Cost of goods is mostly shipping ($2.50-$4 depending on the third-party logistics firm). Terms of 1, 3, 6 and 12 months.
numbers: Price $19.99-$24.99; shipping $2.50-$4; gross margin ~$16 on $20; 12-month discount up to 15%.
applies_to: Subscription physical products

### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: unit-economics | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:57
claim: A subscriber base stops growing at monthly signups divided by monthly churn.
detail: With 500 signups and 10% monthly churn the base plateaus at 5,000 subscribers. At $20 that is $100,000 a month in revenue and roughly $80,000 gross profit, about $1M a year. He calls 10% churn not very low for a physical product.
numbers: 500 / 10% = 5,000 subscribers; $100,000 revenue and ~$80,000 gross profit per month.

### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#05 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:52
claim: Pair a functional wedge product with a broader identity promise: the strip helps people breathe, the brand helps them be better.
detail: Print one word on the strip as a daily reminder (patience, forgiveness). Brand it on the prospect and who they want to become, not the founder.

### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#06 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:52
claim: On-demand custom printing is a moat that allows 2-3x the price.
detail: A custom word costs $30 against a standard $20 pack. Without it, anyone can white-label the glue strip. A monthly text asks which word to work on and the answer feeds straight into production and shipping.
numbers: $30 custom vs ~$20 standard; price 2x-3x.

### YT-LvHDT0ZxSmw#07 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: finance-exit | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:41
claim: Offer influencers 40% of revenue on custom-word strips; a hundred of them could multiply volume tenfold.
detail: Each might do a tenth of his volume, so together ten times. After 40% to partners and about 20% cost, he keeps roughly 20% on that volume (after halving again, unclear in audio). He estimates $3-4 million EBITDA, a recurring business worth about $40-60 million.
numbers: 40% share; 100 influencers; EBITDA $3-4M; value $40-60M (10-15x).
