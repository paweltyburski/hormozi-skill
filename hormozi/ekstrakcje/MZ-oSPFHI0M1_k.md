claim: A couple runs a VSL funnel for a relationship subscription at about $600K a month with erratic Facebook results and rising CPA when spend rises.
detail: Only Facebook ads. Back end is two low-priced upsells. Goal $1M a month.
numbers: $600K a month; 3:1 ratio; goal $1M a month.

### MZ-oSPFHI0M1_k#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:03
claim: Write hooks by awareness level: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware and most-aware, and widen creative as you scale.
detail: From Eugene Schwartz. Unaware needs entertainment or curiosity; problem-aware starts with pain; solution-aware compares options; most-aware just needs an offer (reactivation). He says most people underestimate how much more and better creative is needed to keep scaling, and that for advertisers who hit an artificial limit the gap is the quality of creative.
### MZ-oSPFHI0M1_k#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: CRO | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:31
claim: Test the first five minutes of a VSL: cut it, retell it tighter with proof up front, and run several variants.
detail: This tends to be among the largest conversion lifts.
numbers: 20%-30% lower CAC.

### MZ-oSPFHI0M1_k#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:18
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: since he had only low-ticket upsells on his VSL, Hormozi said he needed a high-ticket upsell of roughly $5,000-$10,000 to increase how much he can spend on ads.
detail: Adding phones typically doubles or triples LTV. Together the three fixes might take $600K a month toward $2M.
