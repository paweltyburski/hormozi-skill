### MZ-o-TQtwQfK9o#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An online men's sexual-health group-therapy business at $3.5M and about $800-900K profit asked for a new model to reach $100M.
detail: Hormozi: no new model; offers and pricing give easy wins, but the lever is a big brand. AI will mostly be the same model delivered at more scale.
numbers: Revenue $3.5M; profit about $800-900K; organic 60-70% of customers.

### MZ-o-TQtwQfK9o#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:49
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: keep about a 70/30 organic-to-paid customer split and let organic dictate how much to spend.
detail: He predicted that for this caller, otherwise ad spend rises, organic share falls, margins compress, and at $5-6M revenue profit stays flat with more headache.
numbers: 70/30 organic to paid; $5-6M revenue same profit.
### MZ-o-TQtwQfK9o#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:11
claim: Aspirational brand associations raise perceived value and reach; celebrities are a weak pool, so use micro-influencers, live streamers and long-form creators.
detail: Hypothetical celebrity endorsement shows the effect, but a stigmatized niche may refuse.

### MZ-o-TQtwQfK9o#04 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:55
claim: For an embarrassing problem referrals are weak; recruit businesses that already serve these customers and have a different money model.
detail: Use centers of influence who acquired them for free and send over services they do not want to deliver.
