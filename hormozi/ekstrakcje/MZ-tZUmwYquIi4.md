### MZ-tZUmwYquIi4#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A cybersecurity compliance consultancy fell from almost $5M to $3.6M revenue and from 35% to 18% net margin after the founder shifted from selling to people management.
detail: Margin went into hiring and "people debt"; leads dropped because the founder stopped hunting while learning to be a CEO. Constraint turned out to be recruiting; next hire is a technical practice leader. Customers are mid-market moving to enterprise.
numbers: Revenue almost $5M (2023) to $3.6M; net margin 35% to 18%.

### MZ-tZUmwYquIi4#02 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:33
claim: A solo seller of big-ticket B2B services alternates events and outbound, so one fills the pipeline while the other is being worked.
detail: Run conferences first because they fill the pipeline fast and cash exists. Pipeline then thins as deals close and outbound rises until the next event.

### MZ-tZUmwYquIi4#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:33
claim: Once cash flow allows, hire an SDR to set and qualify outbound; it is the biggest time constraint and not expensive.
detail: The founder closes in a couple of calls; large companies just take longer. Recruiters who do outbound for candidates are the closest comparable background for the SDR role. Post the ad concurrently with the events.
