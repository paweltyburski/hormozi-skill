### YT-BHMeYaHEMpc#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: To decide between franchising and owning all locations, weigh four things: return on capital, who does the work, the math to the exit goal, and the entrepreneur's own type.
detail: 1. Cost versus return on each dollar put into a new location. 2. Whether opening is centralised (franchisor carries drag) or decentralised (franchisee does the work). 3. Reverse the target exit value (often $50M-$100M) into the number of locations needed. 4. Whether the founder is promotion-driven (loves selling franchises) or product and operations-driven. Even if never selling, build it as a sellable asset.
applies_to: Local multi-location chains

### YT-BHMeYaHEMpc#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:43
claim: If return on capital per location is under 100% a year, franchise; if it is several hundred percent, open more yourself.
detail: His case: location sales about $500,000, profit about $250,000, cost to open about $50,000 gives 5x a year, so reinvesting compounds fast (1M to 5M to 25M to 125M). A McDonald's costs about $1.1-$1.2M to open and earns about $150,000 a year, roughly 13-15%. When you generate real money you must switch from the business hat to the investor hat.
numbers: Own location: $50K in, $500K top line, $250K profit (5x). McDonald's: ~$1.1-1.2M in, ~$150K a year.

### YT-BHMeYaHEMpc#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:38
claim: At 7.5% royalty and a 15x multiple, a franchised location adds about $300,000 of company value, versus about $2 million for an owned one at 8x.
detail: The franchisor earns about $35,000 gross (unclear in audio; 7.5% of roughly $500K) and about $20,000 net per franchisee. Franchisor businesses get roughly double the multiple because investors expect contracted openings. Owned location: 8 x $250,000 = $2M; franchised: 15 x $20,000 = $300,000.
numbers: Royalty 7.5% of top line; net ~$20,000 per franchise; multiple 15x vs 8x; value added per location $300K vs $2M.

### YT-BHMeYaHEMpc#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:46
claim: Convert the exit target into location counts and check the opening pace: a decentralised model may open ten franchises for the effort of one, a centralised model favours owning.
detail: A $100M exit needs about 50 owned locations or 333 franchised. At one or two openings a month, 50 owned sites take two to three years. Franchisors often find their own internal limits, such as only three or four openings, stop them, and franchise sales are not always easy.
numbers: $100M = 50 owned vs 333 franchised locations; pace 1-2 openings a month.

### YT-BHMeYaHEMpc#05 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:25
claim: When the numbers and the founder's temperament disagree, treat the disliked part as a gap in the business and hire a specialist for it.
detail: A promotion-driven founder with an owned-locations model hires someone who has built staffing from 50 to 250 sites; an operations-minded founder facing a franchise model brings in a hungry franchise seller.
