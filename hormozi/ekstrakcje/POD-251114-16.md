### POD-251114-16#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: management-ops | fw: Offer Stack | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Add offers only where they bring revenue with little added cost, time or complexity.
detail: More offers and services fast-track operational complexity, so any addition must carry a high profit-to-complexity ratio. The aim is to match ticket price and value to each customer's buying ability and desire.

detail: Most businesses refer revenue out; affiliate commissions fall to the bottom line. If a referred service earns more, consider buying it.

### POD-251114-16#05 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: money-models | fw: Downsell Flow | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:49
claim: Sale one in the weight-loss flow is a service ladder that steps down from the full offer to a free orientation.
detail: Full high-ticket offer; then half down with a payment plan; then quarter down with a longer plan; then quarter down, shorter programme, no plan; then a free trial with card and commitments; finally a complimentary nutrition orientation 24 to 72 hours later. If all fail, he says trust is the problem.
applies_to: One-to-one or phone sales.
