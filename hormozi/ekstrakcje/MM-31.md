### MM-31#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: offers, leads-fundamentals | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Hormozi's three books answer three questions in order: what to sell ($100M Offers), how to find buyers ($100M Leads), and how to get them to buy ($100M Money Models).
detail: Offers: an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Leads: you advertise. Money Models: a deliberate series of offers.

### MM-31#02 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics, scaling-constraints | fw: Customer-Financed Acquisition | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A money model is a purposeful sequence of offers that increases the number of customers, the amount each pays and the speed of payment; a good one earns more profit within 30 days than it costs to acquire and serve the customer.
detail: That is the minimum bar. A "$100M money model" earns enough 30-day profit from a single customer to cover acquiring and serving several customers, so cash stops limiting growth. Hormozi credits it for his growth pace: Allen from zero to $1.7M/month in six months, Gym Launch from zero to $4.4M/month in 20 months, Skool faster still (figures not disclosed), and other portfolio companies likewise.
numbers: Allen $0 → $1.7M/month in 6 months; Gym Launch $0 → $4.4M/month in 20 months.

### MM-31#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: offers, objections-closing | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:55
claim: The four offer types and the offers recapped under them in the course, each in a line.
detail: Attraction (free or discounted entry, often with a pricier better deal): win your money back; giveaways (prize for contact info, runners-up offered a discount); decoy offer (free/cheap offer beside a premium one); buy X get Y free; pay less now or pay more later; free with consumption. Upsell = whatever you offer next: classic ("you can't have X without Y"), menu (say what they don't need, prescribe what they do), anchor (most expensive first, then a cheaper fit), rollover (credit past purchases toward the next). Downsell = whatever you offer after a no: payment plan, trial with penalty, feature downsell. Continuity: bonus, discount (applied front, end, spread or in between), waived fee. Pick Your Price and Freemium (software only) were cut.

### MM-31#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Money Models | novelty: canon | ts: 00:05:12
claim: Build a money model one stage at a time: reliable customers, then customers who pay for themselves, then customers who pay for others, then maximised lifetime value.
detail: Sequence: 1) get customers reliably; 2) make them pay for themselves reliably; 3) make them pay for acquiring other customers reliably; 4) maximise each customer's long-term value; 5) then "print as much money as possible." Executed, this makes cash no longer a constraint on the business.
