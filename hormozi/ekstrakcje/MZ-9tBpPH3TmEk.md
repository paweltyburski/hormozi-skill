### MZ-9tBpPH3TmEk#01 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Defuse a negative thought by running it through several reframes until it stops mattering.
detail: First ask why the thought is judged negative at all; that dissolves most of them. Then look for an alternative thought, or a reality where it would be good. Then the veteran frame (would it still feel bad if it happened a thousand times, i.e. habituation). Last, cosmic irrelevance, the one he uses most: in a few generations nobody who knows him is alive.

### MZ-9tBpPH3TmEk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:23
claim: Decide in advance what "enough" is for each goal, because goals set far above most people's but not at the top keep the game enjoyable.
detail: He stopped adding muscle at about 250 lb and says a billion or two in net worth would be enough, not a hundred billion. Recognising enough lets you unplug and set new objectives (he expects longevity at around 45).
numbers: Body weight peak about 250 lb; wealth target of one to two billion dollars.

### MZ-9tBpPH3TmEk#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:45
claim: Design days around three ingredients and work until nothing is left to give, since those days feel best.
detail: His great day is a workout with someone he likes, meals with people he likes, and building something. Ending the day with nothing left on the field is what makes the evening satisfying, so he pushes hard. Others may define their day differently.
