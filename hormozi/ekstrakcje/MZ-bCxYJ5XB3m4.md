### MZ-bCxYJ5XB3m4#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Dave Ramsey's company shows four monetization lines around a free media brand, and savings claims are easy to prove.
detail: Savings claims need no proof of math because spending less leaves more money. His streams are media (largest), an app, coaching, and courses and events. A new finance community can give content away if it has a plan for what to sell next.
numbers: Media about $120M of about $300M top line (as stated by Ramsey publicly).

### MZ-bCxYJ5XB3m4#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: offers, money-models | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Give away information and sell implementation, ideally in a semi-private group of about six.
detail: One-to-six remote groups can earn about as much as one-to-one, keep accountability and multiply income about six times.
numbers: Group of 1:6 versus 1:1; about 6x income.

### MZ-bCxYJ5XB3m4#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: The same information sells at higher prices in richer media because live delivery raises the perceived odds the buyer will use it.
detail: Ladder: PDF (cheapest or free), then eight-week live stream coaching, then in-person classroom. Real-time answers reduce the fear of not getting the result.
numbers: Example ladder: PDF, 8-week live online, in-person classroom.
