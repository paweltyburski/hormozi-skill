### MM-10#01 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: offers, pricing | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An attraction offer generates leads and converts them into customers by giving something free or at a discount.
detail: Everyone wants a great deal. Strangers can only take your word on value but fully understand price, so a discount makes almost anything a great deal — the bigger the better, and free is the biggest.

### MM-10#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Free-Discount Continuum | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Demand sits on a continuum between full price (weakest demand) and free (strongest demand): the bigger the discount, the stronger the pull.
detail: Six attraction plays follow: Win Your Money Back, Giveaways, the Decoy Offer, and Buy X Get Y Free, the pay-less-now-versus-pay-more-later play, and Free with Consumption (the last one is absent from the book).
