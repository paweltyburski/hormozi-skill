### LS-TLgSkSQUql4#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:47
claim: When one metric is the constraint, make it the headline everywhere and talk about it constantly; frequency of communication tells staff what matters.
detail: Open the daily huddle with the number, ask each person about their result, review recordings. Mentioned monthly, it seems unimportant; mentioned twice a day, it is clearly the priority. Example: a portfolio company with a churn problem put client exits and saves into the daily huddle and created an emergency channel for at-risk customers; churn fell by more than half "just by trying", and their red-yellow-green system grew from it.

### LS-TLgSkSQUql4#08 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:59
claim: Prevent "you're more expensive" rather than answer it: in a commoditised market, frame the sale from local cost to global cost before price comes up, ideally with a pre-call video.
detail: If several providers cost the same, other factors decide value: speed, rework, risk. Locally, another vendor saves money on this job; globally, across the home or project, delays, rework and opportunity cost make them dearer, so your return is better over a longer horizon. He bets most such sellers lack a VSL that sets this frame before the call. Overcoming it after the price is shown wins less often.

### LS-TLgSkSQUql4#09 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:45
claim: Before firing a toxic top seller, isolate and teach the actions that make her successful, run a 30-60 day push with the rest of the team, and recruit sellers who follow process.
detail: Shield the team from her behaviour. Tell trusted reps to lift their game because changes they will like are coming, without announcing a firing. His experience: each time he let such a "cancer star" go, nobody matched her, but the team average rose and total production did not drop, and process became easier to enforce. One rep far above all others usually signals missing process and training.

claim: When selling new technology to a change-averse market, sell the outcome, not the method, and if needed take the risk yourself by working free or at hard cost.

### LS-TLgSkSQUql4#12 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:33:42
claim: To sell without being pushy, sell better, not harder: agree with an objection and extend the prospect's logic with a question rather than disproving it.
detail: Example for "I can't afford it": agree it is expensive, then ask whether past investments of this kind explain where they are now. Nobody buys from someone who proved them wrong. Most sales close at the first ask when the prospect is qualified, pre-framed and taken through intro and discovery properly; objection handling is a last resort, what happens when the sale went wrong.
