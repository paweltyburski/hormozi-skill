### YT-bxs5pN0ZnRw#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:51
claim: Hormozi's thesis is that demand for high-value skills is unmet and education will fragment into niche vocational training.
detail: People want to be good at something and trade it for money, but formal education does not deliver. Quoting Naval Ravikant, technology democratises consumption and consolidates production, so the best in the world serves everyone. Expect niches like hairdressing, plumbing, running gyms, online training, life coaching.

### YT-bxs5pN0ZnRw#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: product-delivery | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:03
claim: Hormozi's own litmus test for releasing a product is that it is good enough to be better than what came before; he says the gap between great and remarkable is wider and takes several coats of work.
detail: He lets material sit, simplifies and distills it so the transfer is fast and not boring, and refuses to give release dates.
### YT-bxs5pN0ZnRw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:25
claim: Make a sale to get a customer rather than getting customers to make sales; the back end decides what you can spend on acquisition.
detail: Anyone can sell something once; builders create a repeat relationship built on value beyond the price paid. The more gross profit per customer, the more can go to acquisition and the faster the company scales. The product, not marketing, is the number one success factor.

### YT-bxs5pN0ZnRw#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: other | also: money-models | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:21
claim: He ordered his book series to mirror how a business is fixed: offers, leads, money models, sales, scaling sales teams, then pricing and lifetime gross profit.
detail: Money models are described as growing without outside capital by using customer cash to pay for acquisition, with 30-day cash exceeding acquisition cost so acquisition stops being the bottleneck and sales becomes the next constraint.
