### MZ-A1-Wg2CH9MM#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A rural Wisconsin cleaning company ($1.3-1.4M, 25 techs) struggles with turnover, and its best techs come from healthcare.
detail: Hormozi solves churn before attraction, and says to widen the candidate pool beyond healthcare backgrounds. The business is really a training business where the people problem is a feature; promise to take anyone to competence in 14 days and give a career path.
numbers: Revenue $1.3-$1.4M; 25 techs; target 14 days to speed.

### MZ-A1-Wg2CH9MM#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:14
claim: Train groups by role play: open with kudos, the trainer models, pairs practise with real-time thumbs-up feedback, and "lock in" by three clean repeats.
detail: Training one costs nearly as much as training five. Break great behavior into observable actions (smile, nod, "my pleasure") rather than vague terms like "upbeat". Stop errors in the moment instead of listing faults at the end.
numbers: Lock-in = 3 repeats in a row.

### MZ-A1-Wg2CH9MM#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:40
claim: Screen physical-job candidates with a training day on their feet; it models the work and removes poor fits early.
detail: Also give tools that make the job easier; recruit from on-your-feet industries, not only healthcare.
