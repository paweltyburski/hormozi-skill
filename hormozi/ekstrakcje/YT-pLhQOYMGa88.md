### YT-pLhQOYMGa88#06 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Content Unit (Hook-Retain-Reward) | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:09:28
claim: Design the email as a chain of rewards: reward the open, reward the read, reward the click.
detail: Behaviour repeats because it was rewarded last time, so look at what happens after the action. Layout he uses: (1) subject, (2) an instantly rewarding opener such as a quote or tweet, (3) body of one tactic, (4) contextual CTA, (5) sign-off with a P.S. that rewards the reader again. He says this is not a rule, just what he does. Even when linking out, show a real nugget from the blog and click for the rest.
numbers: Body kept under about 200 words (roughly one minute).

### YT-pLhQOYMGa88#13 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics, retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:19:08
claim: Annual renewal fee: a one-time yearly charge on top of the monthly price lifts revenue 8-24% at no extra cost without hurting sign-ups.
detail: Charged a year after joining, so it does not reduce front-end conversion, and people who stay a year rarely leave over it. Size it at about one to three months of the monthly price. numbers: Fee about 1-3x monthly price; +8-24% revenue; $468 to $567 per customer per year.
applies_to: Recurring or membership businesses.
