### YT-kULFeI3LRYk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:29
claim: A course fails to pay off when the skill it teaches is not the broken link; learn the skill that is missing, not the one you enjoy.
detail: Picture the path from where you are to the first dollar as a bridge of bricks (skills) or a pipe from eyeballs to money (headline, ad, copy, click, conversion, sale, upsell). Taking six copywriting courses does nothing if the narrow tube is elsewhere. The last course in a success story is often just the final brick others helped build. Find the gap and attack it.

### YT-kULFeI3LRYk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:39
claim: A small business needs every function covered; even if you will not specialise in one, learn enough to judge who is good at it.
detail: Solopreneurs and teams of 5-10 may lean toward acquisition, product or fulfilment, but the business still needs all roles. Knowing enough to be dangerous lets you assess whether a hire fits.
applies_to: Solo owners and teams of about 5-10

### YT-kULFeI3LRYk#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:43
claim: Customer results follow a bell curve: a better teacher shifts it but outliers remain, and half of students are below average.
detail: People are angry at courses yet not at four years of Spanish they never learned, because of expectations: they assume buying means instantly succeeding. Some enrol and never show up. Selling and marketing add to inflated expectations, but students carry responsibility, and the stronger the teacher, the less skilled the student needs to be.

### YT-kULFeI3LRYk#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:00
claim: Sellers should aim for 100% of customers getting value and never blame them, while customers should own their side of the result.
detail: Hormozi says blaming customers does not improve the business at all; winners take responsibility on both sides. Tactic for learners: extract something from every course, including bad ones, where what you learn is what you will not do in your own.

### YT-kULFeI3LRYk#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:51
claim: Convert recurring choices into if-then rules to save decision effort; for example "no diligence, no deal" and "do not judge people you do not know firsthand".
detail: He and his wife use these algorithms; both must agree or they do not proceed. Reasons for withholding judgment of people: his own past judgment looks foolish in hindsight, the source passing on the opinion is also changing and may be telling their own story, and judging in binary (good or bad) loses what you could learn. He once avoided a useful person for five years because of a post.
