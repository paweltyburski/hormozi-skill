### MZ-3GIXMxy3428#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A $50M pest control company is constrained by retention of average delivery technicians, not sales or A players.
detail: Third-generation owner wants $1B in sales. Sales are mostly organic (paid search, SEO, upsell, referrals) plus acquisitions; delivery backs up each year. A players are promoted fast, leaving no average talent. Recruiting is treated like sales, mostly job boards. Career path has only team lead, branch manager, regional manager.
numbers: Revenue $50M; EBITDA 20% ($8M last year); organic growth 20-21% a year; door-to-door about $1M a year (2% of sales); acquisitions $0.5-1M a year; average branch $2M, so about 475 more locations for $1B.

### MZ-3GIXMxy3428#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: retention-ltv, management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:52
claim: Split each role into junior, normal and senior steps, alternating title changes with perks and pay bumps, for 9 or 18 rungs.
detail: Applies to repeated roles. Perks include no Saturday nights, a parking spot, choosing your calendar, a better truck. Staff are always three to six months from a promotion, and lack of advancement is probably one of the reasons leavers cite. Show the map at orientation.
numbers: Rungs: 3 expanded to 9 or 18; next promotion always 3-6 months away.
### MZ-3GIXMxy3428#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:09
claim: Tie promotions to cumulative volume and make the first step an easy win inside the early churn window.
detail: First milestone about 25 completed jobs (unit unclear in audio), then 100, each reinforcement cycle slightly longer. The biggest turnover is in the first 30 to 45 days, so the first rung should be a six-inch putt.
numbers: First milestone about 25; second 100; churn peak in first 30-45 days (his guess).
