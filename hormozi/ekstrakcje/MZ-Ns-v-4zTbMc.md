### MZ-Ns-v-4zTbMc#01 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Put the negatives first and the positive after "but": the last clause is what the listener keeps.
detail: Name the downsides yourself (hard, long, complicated), then pivot to the benefit (it works better than anything tried before). Most people say the good first then the bad, which signals a bad ending. Idea borrowed from Eminem's habit of owning every negative about himself.

### MZ-Ns-v-4zTbMc#02 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: Answer a money objection by agreeing, then asking if it matters to them, and say people who invest the most relative to their means succeed most.
detail: The structure: agree it is a lot, ask if it is a meaningful amount that would change their life, then label the objection as the mark of someone who will succeed.
