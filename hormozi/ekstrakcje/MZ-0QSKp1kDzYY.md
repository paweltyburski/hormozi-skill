### MZ-0QSKp1kDzYY#01 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: One-sentence posts are made memorable with contrast, groups of three, repetition and alliteration applied to a plain idea.
detail: Ask which permutation of "do hard things" would be easy to remember. Opposites (low and high, give and get) create rhythm. He writes the lines himself without AI, queues them in an automation tool, and reposts audience members quoting him.
