### MZ-fvjyOngt5ic#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:19
claim: Doing four things conditions you as a reinforcer, which is something that gets someone to do something again.
detail: 1) Show visibly that you control what the audience wants. 2) Give behavioural instructions they can follow for a good result. 3) Have third-party credibility that your claims are true. 4) Be like them. Content should give non-obvious instructions that remove friction; people credit part of the outcome to you.
