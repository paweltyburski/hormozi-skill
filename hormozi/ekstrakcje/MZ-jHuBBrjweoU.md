### MZ-jHuBBrjweoU#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: People will not make trades because they want everything, and once they have it they stop wanting it.
detail: Desire is compelling and unreachable, having is unsatisfying, so people oscillate rather than pick.

### MZ-jHuBBrjweoU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:46
claim: Loss aversion makes people flip between two paths and realise neither.
detail: When one path starts to fade, the emotional reaction pushes a change of course. Losing five pounds hurts more than finding five feels good, so people avoid loss rather than speed gain.

### MZ-jHuBBrjweoU#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:23
claim: The operator's habit of seeing flaws clashes with life satisfaction; treat it as a problem to manage, not a paradox to solve.
detail: Being dissatisfied in one area makes you good at business, but satisfaction needs accepting things as they are. No clean answer exists; do your best.
