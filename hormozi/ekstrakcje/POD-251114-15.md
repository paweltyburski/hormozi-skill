### POD-251114-15#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A $3M-a-year consulting client had fine marketing but one offer with no upsell or continuity, so earned far less per customer.
detail: Hormozi sold about twice as many people, yet made far more profit; his customer paid him about ten times as much, while the client's CAC was about 50% higher. The fix was more per customer, not more leads.
numbers: 2x customers, 56x profit, about 10x revenue per customer, CAC ~50% higher for the client; $50,000 consulting day.

### POD-251114-15#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:24
claim: The value grid lists every prospect against every offer, instead of a stair-step ladder that assumes everyone buys in order.
detail: Buyers skip offers (one then four; three and five), and several same-priced offers can serve different needs. Total revenue across the grid divided by prospects gives lifetime value, and from it CAC affordability and 30-day cash.

### POD-251114-15#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:02
claim: A single-trial business generates $75 per trial taker, which leaves nothing once sales labour is counted.
detail: Of ten prospects, eight take a trial and three buy the paid back end. Working the leads costs $600 to $1,000 in labour, consuming all revenue.
numbers: 10 prospects: $600 total; $60 per prospect; $75 per trial taker (30-day cash); at $12 a lead, 50 leads break even before labour.

### POD-251114-15#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: Offer Stack | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:04
claim: The same business, redesigned with a high-ticket front end, downsells and 30-day upsells, earned $1,763 per client.
detail: Revenue per client rose sharply (he states 14 to 15x; $1,763 against $75 is nearer 23x, unclear in audio). A competitor able to pay $100 per lead is outbid by one paying $1,500 and still profitable.
