### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:19

detail: In big-box gyms people join in January, fail within three or four weeks, realise they need help and then buy personal training. Even so, January should still be dominated.
applies_to: Gyms

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: - | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:51
claim: In his opinion, heavy marketing starting more than a month out kind of backfires because it crescendos too early; he feels 10-14 days is where you build to max hype.
detail: He suggests a whisper post about a month out, then maybe one email and one post a week later, then the final roughly 14-day cycle. He usually uses a 10-day countdown, maybe because he dislikes writing many emails, and because he thinks people's attention spans are short.
numbers: Whisper about a month out; heavy marketing 10-14 days (his own: usually 10 days).
### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#04 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:03
claim: The whisper stage is a countdown graphic with the challenge logo, posted before the product is even ready.
detail: Post it in the warmest audience (free group or client group) with no copy, only that it is coming; repeat a week later with fewer days. Excitement builds from the hint alone.

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#05 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: outreach | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:57
claim: Run five or six emails in the last 10 days, every other day, each addressing a belief or pain.
detail: Send on days 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1 and launch day (day 0); day 1 is a short "24 hours left, get ready". Rotate through the program's facets and recycle them, since not everyone reads every email and being reminded often beats being taught.
numbers: Emails on days 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1, 0.

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#06 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:38
claim: Make each countdown email aggravate one customer pain tied to one part of the program.
detail: For a gym program, split the pains by facet: nutrition (no time to cook or count macros), training (wandering aimlessly, feeling judged, bored on the treadmill and leaving ashamed), and accountability (no motivation, nobody helping). Speak in the customer's terms.
applies_to: Gyms with multi-part programs

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#07 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: outreach | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:17
claim: On launch day send about six very short emails, and text alongside them.
detail: He copied this from big internet marketers. The aim is only to stay at the top of the inbox; a subject like "four hours left" with almost no body is enough. Texts must be one-bullet distillations. On off-days alternate: email day 10, text day 9, email day 8 and so on.
numbers: ~6 emails on launch day.

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#08 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:03
claim: For an in-person business, skip the webinar and have warm contacts book a time slot directly.
detail: In the last 48 hours the link only asks them to sign up or book, not to pay. Warm clients rarely no-show, so run orientation consultations in 30-minute blocks (one coach, two people), and invite each to bring a friend, who can then be closed on the spot.
numbers: 30-minute consult slots, two people each.
applies_to: Gyms and in-person businesses with a warm list

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#09 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: promotions-launches | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:26
claim: Post in a Facebook group once a week, with a 24-hour reminder on the last week.
detail: A post takes about two days to be seen by everyone, and more frequent posting feels aggressive. Schedule posts at four, three, two and one weeks out plus a reminder the day before. The first two are whispers; the last two give a high-level overview of the program.
numbers: 1 post per week; 4 weekly posts plus 24-hour reminder.

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#10 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:00
claim: A gym owner who bought a $20,000 EMS suit machine wanted a special pitch; Hormozi told him to sell it like everything else.
detail: Clients want to lose weight, so the equipment type (cycle, spin, kickboxing, EMS) is irrelevant. The only real selling point was that two 20-minute sessions a week need less commitment, which raises value by lowering effort. His rule: clarify why people are there, then sell that.
numbers: Equipment $20,000; 2 sessions of 20 minutes per week.

### YT-4PX-hE6LTSU#11 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: - | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:12
claim: Hormozi would recommend that, if a launch starts in three days and the hype was done wrong, you take the 10 days from now as your hype period and follow the whisper-tease-shout process for at least those last 10 days.

numbers: He would recommend using 10 days from now as the hype period.
