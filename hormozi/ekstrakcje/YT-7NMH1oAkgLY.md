### YT-7NMH1oAkgLY#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:57
claim: Massage example: a $10 trial yields about six customers from 100 people, versus about one at a $100 price.
detail: At $100 about one in 100 says yes. At $10 about 20 say yes and a third of them buy the package or subscription. Even if each trial costs $20 to deliver, $200 buys six customers worth about $2,000.
numbers: 100 people: $100 price ~1 buyer; $10 trial ~20 takers; 1/3 convert = ~6 customers; $200 spent, customers worth ~$2,000.
applies_to: Local service businesses with a package core offer.

### YT-7NMH1oAkgLY#07 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: offers | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:57
claim: Seven-step lead magnet build order.
detail: 1. Pick problem and exact person. 2. Choose how to solve it (assessment that reveals a problem, trial, or mix). 3. Choose delivery form by perceived value. 4. Name and package it. 5. Make it easy to consume in several formats. 6. Make it genuinely good. 7. Make the next step easy.
