### MZ-cU3vhFF5cjc#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A book publishing services firm used every acquisition channel and wanted to know what to do more of.
detail: Referrals are first, then organic (speaking, social, guest coaching), then cold email and Facebook ads. He did 300 sales calls himself and hired six salespeople.
numbers: Revenue $1M, goal $3.2M; back end $195 a month (semi-private) or $595 for four months; churn 50% on both, about 30% weighted.

### MZ-cU3vhFF5cjc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:26
claim: When service fails, refunding is not enough; make it more than right.
detail: Six attendees flew in for a workshop that was not ready; the team spent the day with them and took them to dinner.
numbers: 6 attendees.

### MZ-cU3vhFF5cjc#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:07
claim: If a prospect declines the full package, give one or two sessions free as a gift while still pitching the back end at the end of each group call.
detail: Illustration: six sessions at about $100 each (made-up numbers).

### MZ-cU3vhFF5cjc#04 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:12
claim: Churn follows a drop in consumption, so escalate outreach as attendance falls, the reverse of working a lead.
detail: Track usage in the CRM (three visits a week, then two, then one means cancel). Call once after a miss, twice after the next, three or four times after that.
numbers: Pattern: 3, 3, 2 visits per week triggers the escalation; 1 per week means cancellation.
