### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: In a market where offers are easy to compare, win on at least one of three vectors: faster, less risky, or easier.
detail: Home services such as roofing and remodeling always have some way to do each. If you cannot afford it, raise the price to fund it, since you stop being a commodity. If the market will not pay, go up to wealthy buyers who value speed, risk and ease.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: offers | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Ask what it would take to deliver the outcome in one third of the time.
detail: Work the answer with the team instead of saying it cannot be done. Example: pay contractors 50% more for same-day or next-day response, then test whether customers will pay for it.
numbers: one-third of the time; contractors paid 50% more.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#03 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Reverse risk by buying an insurance policy on the customer's biggest fear and saying damages up to a set amount are covered.
detail: He has implemented it in several businesses; the cost built into the price is usually lower than expected.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#04 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Increase ease by doing preparation for the customer in intake and onboarding before they ask.
detail: Few businesses do it, so customers notice. Example: knowing the customer's name and business when they arrive.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: money-models | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:54
claim: A kitchen-remodel giveaway turns losers into leads by offering second place a $2,000 discount.
detail: For Buy X Get Y Free, give away more free than paid and adjust the price: $100 a month for 12 months ($1,200) becomes buy four, get eight, at $300 each. Check local advertising laws.
numbers: $1,200 for 12 months = 4 paid months at $300 each + 8 free.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#06 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:16
claim: Ads to his workshop gave higher up-front ROAS, but the Scaling Roadmap lead magnet had higher ROAS over a longer horizon and scaled better.
detail: A direct workshop ask reaches only the warmest segments; a softer ask reaches many more. Small businesses chasing a smaller revenue number do get higher ROAS from direct asks.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#07 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: offers | fw: Three Lead Magnet Types | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:47
claim: Examples of the three lead-magnet types: a problem reveal, a free trial of the solution, and one step of a multi-step process.
detail: Reveal: seven mistakes homeowners make fixing HVAC. Trial: get a local business to top three in maps free, call in seven days. Splinter: a fashion consultant finds your colour palette, then automates it. "Request a quote" is not a magnet.

### MZ-7qY7gBMWOB4#08 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: pricing | fw: Penny Gap | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:28
claim: Pull one component out of a $2,000 offer and give it away at 80-90% off, so buyers then want the rest.
detail: The discount must be large enough to change behaviour, not 10-20%; pick a component that costs you little, then run the upsell into the main offer, which lowers lead cost.
numbers: 80-90% off; 10-20% too small.
