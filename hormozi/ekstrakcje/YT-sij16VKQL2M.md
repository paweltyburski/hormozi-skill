### YT-sij16VKQL2M#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:40
claim: Reputation cannot be curated; change who you are and let others' perceptions catch up.
detail: After a bad first semester at university (about a 1.9 GPA, a gossip thread about him), his father told him to stop managing perception. He stopped going out and drinking and studied, reaching a 3.2 for the rest of the term, yet reversing the damage still took about two years. First impressions are easy to get wrong and hard to repair.
numbers: GPA about 1.9 rising to 3.2; about 2 years to repair reputation.
