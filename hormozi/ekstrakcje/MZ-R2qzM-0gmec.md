### MZ-R2qzM-0gmec#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals, sales-team | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A company sells a kit of mental-health, nutrition and coordination calls around surgery, but its margins are low and coordinators resist a higher price.
detail: Coordinators work for the surgeon and get a kickback per kit; hospitals refer patients but cannot collect payment. The kit also covers bariatric and rapid-weight-loss patients. It takes burdens off the office, so it frees staff for revenue.

### MZ-R2qzM-0gmec#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:43
claim: His rule of thumb for services is 80% or higher gross margin; he prefers 90-95% if he can get it.
detail: With a 50% cost basis on a service, little remains after other costs.
numbers: Target 80%+ gross margin, ideally 90-95%.
### MZ-R2qzM-0gmec#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-team | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:43
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: Hormozi thinks it is probably a compensation issue, so she can double the price and 4x what she pays the sellers to make the uncomfortable moment worth it.
detail: Gross margins are on delivery, so commission is included in acquisition cost. If hospitals refer directly, he doesn't think she has to worry about the higher price.
numbers: Price can be doubled; seller pay can be 4x (his suggestion).
### MZ-R2qzM-0gmec#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:27
claim: Start with an opt-in at an artificially high price to teach the behaviour; later it becomes part of the surgeon's package.
detail: Making it mandatory typically takes 90 to 180 days once staff see their workload shrink.
numbers: 90-180 days to inclusion.
