### POD-0786#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Acquisition.com flywheel: media draws business owners, some become deals, outperformers get a liquidity event, and the documented stories raise credibility for more media.
detail: Sequence: media for owners (books, content, emails, podcast), owners find the firm, a few select deals on fair terms, some companies outperform, they sell or list after three to five years, the stories reinforce the brand. He admits it is not yet fully self-propelling. He thinks all businesses start with getting attention.
### POD-0786#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Any company can reach $100M+ of enterprise value, meaning roughly $10-15M of profit, if you think of its more scaled version.
detail: A single brick-and-mortar site cannot, but a multi-site version can; online businesses may need to go international. A billion depends on the opportunity vehicle.
numbers: $100M enterprise value corresponds to about $10M-$15M profit.

### POD-0786#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:08
claim: A second content stream aimed at executives (his partner's) is a deliberate way to attract operators for portfolio companies.
detail: Her LinkedIn followers skew to executives running large teams, which are the people needed to grow low-mid and mid-market firms to larger ones. Hence her content differs from his.

### POD-0786#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:01
claim: Acquisition.com holds minority stakes in all but two portfolio companies, averaging about 38-40%.
detail: The average moves because deals sometimes include options and performance triggers. Flaw in the plan: it takes time to attract owners, do deals and see growth, and an investor without a ten-year record is hard to judge.
numbers: Average stake about 38% to 40%; minority position in all but 2 companies.
### POD-0786#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:53
claim: Keep portfolio companies under NDA and separate his personal brand from the work, so he does not become key-man risk for everything he owns.
detail: Reasons: public association makes it look as if he alone grew the company, which is unfair to the founder, and it would worsen sale terms for every company. Exception: School. He brings brand and traffic, cash and expertise to the table, and tells the story vaguely (consumer, B2B, local chain) with the founders' numbers withheld.

### POD-0786#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:43
claim: Do not signal to the market that you are winning, because it invites competitors; expand quietly and let the scale surprise them.
detail: He will wait for large final outcomes before sharing results, acting in the portfolio companies' interest even at his own public cost. Short term the internet is a voting system, long term a weighing one (Buffett's stock framing); aim at accomplishments, not popularity.

### POD-0786#07 | FRAMEWORK | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:08:45
claim: ACQ Ventures is a lighter-touch vehicle for founders who want capital and some access to the team but not an exit or heavy implementation.
detail: Funded only with his and Layla's money (from the gym company sale and distributions), no outside limited partners. Venture checks are usually smaller by ownership share, hands-off, a volume game with higher risk per deal but lower aggregate risk. Private equity side is the opposite: concentrated, work-heavy. He thinks the first three deals will be done by December.
numbers: He thinks 3 ACQ Ventures deals will be done by December (year not stated).
### POD-0786#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:17
