### MZ-KyKUym0Ezhw#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals, unit-economics | fw: Virtuous Cycle of Price | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:41
claim: A Canadian performance marketing agency moved up-market but kept SMB prices, leaving a 25% margin and fear of investing.
detail: Healthcare and medtech clients, leads from events and LinkedIn videos. Churn fell from high early turnover to a lower rate. Hormozi: simply forgot to raise price; a fair average is about $6,000 a month, giving 50%+ margin and room to hire.
numbers: Revenue $2M, goal $10M; 40 clients; retainer $3,500-$10,000 a month; churn 10-20% a year; margin 25% (about $400,000; owner earnings $600-700k); close rate 60-70%; one client signed at $15,000 a month without hesitation.

### MZ-KyKUym0Ezhw#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:45
claim: Use close rate as a pricing gauge: about 35% is right; higher means room to raise price.
detail: Close rate bands tell roughly how much to raise price.
numbers: 40-50% close: probably raise 25-50%; 50-60%: closer to 1.5 to 1.75 times; 60-70%: at least double; 70-80%: probably double or triple.
### MZ-KyKUym0Ezhw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: operator-mindset | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:13
claim: Owners sell from their own wallet, and too-cheap mid-market pricing can look not legitimate.
detail: Beliefs about price and ad spend come from upbringing and peers; a mechanic never pays for an oil change, but a buyer who needs leads will. Cheap quotes cost legitimate buyers.

### MZ-KyKUym0Ezhw#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:33
claim: Charge a flat base plus a decreasing percentage of ad spend by tier, never retroactive, with a minimum spend requirement.
detail: Example: $6,000 a month regardless, up to $20,000 monthly ad spend; an extra roughly 5% on spend from $20k to $100k; about 2.5% from $100k to $200k. The top tier is made tiny, so buyers aim for it. Extra spend is almost pure margin. Require at least about $20,000 monthly ad spend.
numbers: Base $6,000 a month; extra 5% then 2.5% in bands; minimum ad spend about $20,000 a month.
