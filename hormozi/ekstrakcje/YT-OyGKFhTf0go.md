### YT-OyGKFhTf0go#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:49
claim: The Rule of One: build a message on a single big idea that is easy to understand, easy to believe, and interesting or unique.
detail: From Michael Masterson's book on leads. Stacking every good feature so one will land produces "toss salad" copy. Lead with one concept and make all bullets, stories and facts support it; the same goes for a unique selling proposition. Choosing the idea is the hard part; the rest is easy to write.
