### MZ-1-ck3eTu5HE#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:57
claim: The strongest marketing message tells the whole truth, even the uncomfortable part.
detail: Example: a day-trading educator with a large verified track record admitted trading ruined his health. Hormozi told him to say that: the course shows how he made money enough to stop, with no promise. That is more believable than a generic motive.
numbers: Claimed $100M verified trading earnings (as stated).

### MZ-1-ck3eTu5HE#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:29
claim: Define vague words such as politeness or laziness as observable behaviours before acting on them.
detail: Asked what kinder meant, the employee wanted statements phrased as questions; once done, the problem was solved. Likewise "lazy" becomes "slow to reply on Slack", which can be fixed by a request.

### MZ-1-ck3eTu5HE#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Money is just a unit of exchange, not something mystical.
detail: Mystical talk about frequency or attraction confuses people into actions that earn nothing.
