### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#01 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: Classic Upsell | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:50
claim: Use "sawdust" as bait: give away a by-product that costs you nothing but customers still value, to bring them in for the upsell.
detail: The dealer made small fur purses and earmuffs from scraps and gave one to every customer who turned in a coat; customers then also bought storage, which was not free. His other examples: furniture from lumber-mill waste, whey protein once a dairy waste product.

### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Sell an "insurance product" without insurance by charging for the assurance that what was bought keeps delivering value for longer.
detail: The dealer compared coat storage and repair to a $50,000 car carrying about $100 a month of insurance, which made an unfamiliar product understandable. Summer had no demand, so this filled the gap at very low delivery cost.
numbers: Analogy: $50,000 car, about $100 a month insurance.

### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-process | also: retention-ltv | fw: HEAR | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: When a customer arrived angry over a missing button on a $25,000-range coat, the owner out-raged her about his own staff, and she calmed down and asked only for a fix.
detail: He said only one person can be in the angry boat. Downplaying ("not a big deal") makes angry people escalate and shows no ownership; validate the anger even if the complaint is petty. The repair took five minutes.

### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: pricing | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:32
claim: The store doubled coats sold per customer using scarcity cues: no dealers allowed, a limit of two coats per customer, "final price, all sales final" signs, and customers let in single file with a fixed number inside.
detail: The cues created perceived demand and a feeling of needing to claim a share.

### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:33
claim: A recurring low-ticket service between big purchases makes you the customer's dealer for life; it pays even unprofitably if customers buy about two more products.
detail: The annual coat storage and repair meant customers stopped price shopping and came to the dealer first.
numbers: Customers buy about two more products on average.

### YT-vZfatNSouDQ#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:33
claim: The dealer survived five generations by never stopping the boring basics; big owners differ from small ones by repeating the same things for a long time.
detail: Hormozi says the portfolio owners do all the basics they know they should (blog, email follow-up, content, sales team) consistently; big outcomes take big time.
