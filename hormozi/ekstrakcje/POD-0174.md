### POD-0174#01 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Keep a running log of your failures and review it periodically to extract principles.
detail: Hormozi keeps an ongoing email thread to himself where he writes down each mistake as it happens, then periodically reviews the list for the lesson behind each. This recap was grouped into business, people, relationships and self.

### POD-0174#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:08
claim: Every product must solve a pain, not offer a gain, because people act far harder to avoid loss than to earn.
detail: Lesson from the failed "Bolt-On" (supplement sales packaged as a cold front-end offer). People treat $500 saved and $500 earned as unequal.
numbers: People are about 10x more willing to save $500 than to make an extra $500, per his claim.

### POD-0174#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: offers | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:08
claim: A sale can break only one belief, not two.
detail: Bolt-On needed prospects to believe in selling supplements and also in buying from Prestige Labs. His own community trusted him, so only one belief needed breaking; cold traffic needed two and the offer failed.

### POD-0174#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:59
claim: Protect the core and explain things in terms everyone understands, because everyone hates change.
detail: He relabelled one-to-one nutrition and accountability as "Hybrid", and the audience reacted badly. He had shaken things up for no reason and was partly reacting to competitors while being roughly 10 times larger than them.
numbers: Roughly 10x bigger than competitors (his estimate).

### POD-0174#05 | CASE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:59
claim: Hormozi spent about $1M on a client-retention app that stayed "eight weeks from done" for six months, because small owners do not truly care about retention.
detail: His conclusion is that this explains why there are marketing agencies but no retention agencies.
numbers: About $1,000,000 spent; roughly 8 weeks from finish, repeated over 6 months.

### POD-0174#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:28
claim: Give small owners what they want, which is acquisition, not what they need.
detail: When he spoke about retention, his own attrition rose: he lost clients because they did not care. For a small business owner nothing outranks getting customers.

### POD-0174#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:37
claim: He over-focused on top-line revenue in 2019 while profit stayed flat.
detail: Revenue grew, but the bottom line was unchanged versus 2018 because he reinvested heavily in new ventures. He still ended the year with growth despite the mistakes.
numbers: Top line grew about 50% in 2019 versus 2018 (he first said 2020, then corrected); bottom line roughly flat.

### POD-0174#08 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:37
claim: Build data collection into every new system at launch, or problems surface too late.
detail: In 2019 he failed to input data properly in new systems and learned of problems much later than he should have.

### POD-0174#09 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:28
claim: Never split focus: running three businesses at once didn't work for him; get better at one thing and stop adding.
detail: He says this was probably his biggest error of all, in his view, and the opposite of what he tells others. He adds that releasing more products does little; making the same thing better gets more ("more is less, less is more, better is best").
numbers: 3 businesses run at once.
### POD-0174#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:30
claim: Give each business entity one singular driver, with no cross-department or cross-entity roles.
detail: People cannot serve two masters. The founder can stay as chairman, but each entity needs one person who thinks only about it.

### POD-0174#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:30
claim: Define success as effort and judge people, including family, by intention and effort, not outcomes.
detail: He later refused to see launch numbers for a new meals product because no number would satisfy his very high expectations, and judged himself on effort instead. He says this was freeing and changed how he viewed his father.

### POD-0174#12 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:48
claim: Hire people only for work they have already done, because senior mistakes are costly and they learn on your dollar.
detail: In 2019 the company promoted frontline staff into director roles they had never held, made mistakes, and replaced most of the directors.
numbers: 10 of 11 directors replaced in one year.

### POD-0174#13 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:24
claim: If a department looks like it is crushing it, you are probably too far from it, since there are always hidden problems.
detail: Stay close enough that you can always see problems.

### POD-0174#14 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:24
claim: Do not shelter a star: building processes around one great person's weaknesses reshapes the organisation and backfires.
detail: It happened to him several times in one year.
numbers: Two to three times in 2019.

### POD-0174#15 | DEFINITION | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:24
claim: A boss's job is communication, motivation and training.
detail: Communicate clearly what you want done; after that, only motivate and train. If your time goes to something else, you are not doing the boss's job.

### POD-0174#16 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:59
claim: Fire for ego: it produces entitlement, credit-taking and putting colleagues down, which hurts the team.
detail: Try not to hire it in the first place; once it flares up, remove it to save the headache.

### POD-0174#17 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:49
claim: Expect clients, vendors and employees to act in their own self-interest, and align their interests with yours so helping you feels good to them.
detail: Whenever money separates two parties, the dynamic changes. Leaning into self-interest means you are never surprised.
