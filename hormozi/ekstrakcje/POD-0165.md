### POD-0165#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: Fire immediately for character problems (disrespect, undermining, resisting change, badmouthing); diagnose performance problems first.
detail: Character does not change soon and sows doubt among staff and clients. Examples: telling clients a launch will be weak, rolling eyes in meetings. Estimated to be about two thirds of the cases owners agonise over.
numbers: About two thirds of doubtful cases are character or culture.

### POD-0165#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:32
claim: A performance problem has three possible causes: they do not know what you want, they do not know how, or they are not motivated.
detail: He lists them as the communication piece, the skill piece and the motivation piece, and says the next step is to work out which one is the missing link. Entrepreneurs often fail at the first, because the biggest myth of communication is believing it happened.
### POD-0165#06 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:09
claim: Put every expectation in writing; if it is not written, it did not happen.
detail: Follow a spoken conversation with an email summary, tasks in a task manager, and a one-page role summary for a major role change.

### POD-0165#07 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:50
claim: Train in stages: presentation with a demonstrate-back, recordings, ride-along, reverse ride-along with live correction, then tapering check-ins.
detail: The learner first sees correct examples (recordings cost no time), rides with you, then drives while you correct in real time. Then daily or twice-daily check-ins for new people, then 2 to 3 times a week, then weekly, never less.
numbers: Check-ins: 1-2 times a day, then 2-3 a week, then weekly minimum.
