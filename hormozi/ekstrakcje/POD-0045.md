### POD-0045#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The seller's biggest advantage is that the prospect showed up, which proves they do not already have the solution.
detail: The main reason a sale fails is that no gap was built between goal and history. The key line: if you had everything needed, you would already have the result and would not be here. It works for gym owners (if you had a cash-printing marketing method you would not have applied) and for weight loss alike.

### POD-0045#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Build the gap in three questions: where they want to be, where they are, and what they have tried.
detail: Example: lose 30 pounds, now 200, target 170. Asking about past attempts does two jobs: it brings back the emotional cost of previous failures, and it anchors the gap. Keep drilling until the prospect admits they are not at the result.
numbers: example 200 lb to 170 lb target.

### POD-0045#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:48
claim: Explain why earlier attempts failed so the prospect is not to blame and sees what was missing.
detail: Example: a gym membership used alone, with no diet help and no check-in calls. This breaks the belief that they failed personally and shows what a new method must add. Then answer "what will you do instead?" to block the exit.

### POD-0045#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:40
claim: Roughly 30% of prospects buy because they decided before walking in, and about 50% must be fought for.
detail: The gap technique is for the second group, who resist out of pride and do not want to admit failure. Figures are his rough estimate; the remaining 20% is not discussed.
numbers: about 30% pre-decided; about 50% need persuasion (his estimate).

### POD-0045#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:35
claim: Inbound applications give the upper hand that cold selling lacks, because the prospect raised a hand and admitted the problem.
detail: When they hesitate, ask what they are unsure about, remind them nothing else has worked, and back the pitch with proof such as before-and-after walls and reviews.
