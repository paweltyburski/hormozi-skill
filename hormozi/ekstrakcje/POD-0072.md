### POD-0072#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Success creates deficits: an acquisition system does not solve problems, it exposes the ones already in the business.
detail: Before volume, hidden weaknesses are not under pressure. A machine built for a "category one earthquake" (such as word-of-mouth volume) breaks at a category five. Gym Launch intentionally forces growth by fixing lead generation, nurture and sales first.

### POD-0072#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:28
claim: As a gym grows, the bottleneck moves in order: acquisition (lead generation, nurture, sales), then service delivery, then churn, then leadership and incentives.
detail: Acquisition first; once classes fill, service seems to drop because trainers lack processes to serve two or three times as many; scale fulfilment; then churn shows up; then leadership, inspiring the team, and commissions tied to KPIs.
numbers: Service load assumed at 2x to 3x the previous member count.
