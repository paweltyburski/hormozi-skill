### MZ-T7cD63IlWzA#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A TikTok-shop agency founder hit $200K profit in month seven of his second business, then stalled for a year because sales stop when he steps away.
detail: His first business reached $150K+ monthly profit within a year and floated four years. Customers come from organic social and word of mouth; ops cannot take more volume. Diagnosis: sales velocity must rise, but operations break when selling picks up.
numbers: $150K+ monthly profit (first), $200K month seven (second), 6 weeks away from the business.

### MZ-T7cD63IlWzA#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:20
claim: Do not look for a "unicorn" who has lived all your roles; break your job into component parts and hire the right person for each.
detail: Founders learned marketing, sales, product and hiring, then expect one person to do all for a fraction of their pay. Analogy: need the horn, so find a rhinoceros for the horn part.
