### MZ-JVkTOLTJP-E#01 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Add an annual renewal fee equal to one to two months of dues, about 16% extra revenue.
detail: Combined with four-week billing (+8%) and a processing fee, it takes a 20% margin business to much higher; the changes seem small.
numbers: annual fee $99-$199 (one to two months); +16% revenue; 4-week billing +8%; sum +24% doubles profit at 20% margin.

### MZ-JVkTOLTJP-E#02 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Use the processing fee as a bargaining chip for a second payment method, which can cut churn about 30%; ACH adds a cheaper rail.
detail: Offer to waive the fee if they give a second card or ACH; the fee exists because chasing failed payments costs resources. ACH saves about 2% on fees. Several 2-5% moves together lift margin from 20% to 50%.
numbers: churn reduced 30%; ACH saves ~2%; fee 3-4%.

### MZ-JVkTOLTJP-E#03 | SCRIPT | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: Four Ways to Display a Discount | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Price-rise letter in three sections: what you already do for them, what the extra money will fund, then grandfathering for six months.
detail: Put several price changes in one letter. Softer variant: raise price but add a credit to the account so that the discount disappears instead of the price rising; if the rise is large, do it in two phases.
numbers: grandfather for 6 months; example $200 credit across the period.
