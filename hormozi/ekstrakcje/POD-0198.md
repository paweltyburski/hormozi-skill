### POD-0198#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: unit-economics | also: paid-ads | fw: Payback Period | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:11
claim: A day-one break-even rule caps customer count at new customers per month divided by monthly churn; relaxing it to day 30 or 60 lifts that cap.
detail: 10 new customers a month at 10% churn stops at 100 customers. Day 30 break-even gives about 30 new a month and a 300 cap; day 60 may give 5x, about 150 new a month.
numbers: 10 new/month at 10% churn = 100 max; day 30 = 30 new/300 max; day 60 = about 150 new (5x).

### POD-0198#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: unit-economics | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:44
claim: A longer break-even window lets you reach warmer then colder buyers, a market 10 to 50 times larger.
detail: Red-hot buyers are few; reaching wider audiences costs more per customer but opens a far larger pool.
numbers: Reachable market 10x, 20x or 50x larger.

### POD-0198#03 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: paid-ads | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:12
claim: Fund acquisition with a credit card or other people's money, repay after 30 days, and keep the customers and excess cash.
detail: Example: $10,000 borrowed for 30 days brings 100 customers at about $100 profit each after all costs; next month the budget can be $20,000.
numbers: $10,000 for 30 days; 100 customers; $100 profit each.
