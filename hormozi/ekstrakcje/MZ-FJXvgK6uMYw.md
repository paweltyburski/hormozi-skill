claim: A sports social media agency can't staff bigger sponsor accounts, but a director with a pod yields $1.25M gross profit a year.
detail: Team balked at winning four State Farm-sized clients. The director costs about $200k and the pod about $350k, already deducted from gross profit.
numbers: Revenue $12M, goal $25-30M; director gross profit $1.25M a year; cost $200k plus $350k pod.

### MZ-FJXvgK6uMYw#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:44
claim: Value a hire by lifetime gross profit and enterprise value, then spend accordingly on acquiring them.
detail: Four years of tenure brings $5M. At an 8x valuation, adding $1.25M of annual gross profit adds about $10M enterprise value. He would spend $125k one time.
numbers: $5M lifetime over 4 years; 8x multiple; $10M enterprise value; $125k spend.

### MZ-FJXvgK6uMYw#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:31
claim: A blue chip recruiter typically charges about 30% of first-year pay, e.g. $60,000 on a $200,000 role.
detail: Pay is small against $1.25M a year. For the dentist, three hires at $100k each cost $300k, taking profit from $4M to $3.7M to hit a three-year goal in six months.
numbers: Fee about 30% of salary; dentist: $300k total fees.
