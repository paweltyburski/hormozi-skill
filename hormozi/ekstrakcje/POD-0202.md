### POD-0202#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Labeling means giving the prospect a positive attribute and tying it to the action you want, so they act consistently with it.
detail: Hormozi learned it in a college psychology class and again from a Frank Kern training, which carried an ethical warning because it can help or manipulate. It works within a conversation, not as lasting identity change. Example he gives: praise someone's work ethic, then link it to why they do their hundred daily reach outs.
### POD-0202#02 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:37
claim: Ask early whether they must check with anyone before deciding, then label whoever says no as a confident decision maker.
detail: Steps: (1) near the start, when pressure is low, ask if they need to check with a spouse or anyone else to act for their own health; (2) on "no", repeat it as a trait (strong, confident woman who makes her own decisions); (3) link rapport by saying your own wife is the same; (4) move on. Having owned the label, they are less likely to raise the spouse at the end.

### POD-0202#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:26
claim: Use tone to ask the decision-maker question: stress "your own health" and let the voice rise at the end so it invites a yes.
detail: Tonality sets how often people agree, especially on phone sales. A flat or doubtful delivery weakens the technique.

### POD-0202#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:40
claim: If the obstacle comes up at the end anyway, never cite what they said earlier; stay neutral and curious.
detail: Proving them wrong wins the point and loses the sale, and calling them a liar costs them status so they hang up. Treat the obstacle stage as a dance: light tone, childlike curiosity ("why do you think that is?"), and draw out from them the reasons the spouse would support them, based on past support.

### POD-0202#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:26
claim: Place the label as the second or third line after the introduction, as part of the pre-frame.
detail: Ask whether they make their own decisions, get the yes, label, and continue. It carries more prospects to the end without the spouse issue returning.
