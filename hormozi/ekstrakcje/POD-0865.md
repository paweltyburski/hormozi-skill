### POD-0865#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A lawyer with three units (services, education, software) lost money and had months of cash left.
detail: Reported figures (as told to Hormozi): about $300,000 revenue, minus $100,000 profit, six months of cash. The lawyer added a DIY education product for people who could not afford the service, then software for a higher enterprise value. Only $300,000 a year was spread over three businesses; the software alone lost money. Fix: cut it down to the core service, with the education as a lead front end.
numbers: Revenue $300,000/year; profit minus $100,000/year; cash runway 6 months.

### POD-0865#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:14
claim: Until $1M a year, run one product, one avatar and one channel.
detail: Past $1M, the usual next step is more of the same; sometimes a back end is added and paths diverge. Shiny-object additions (courses, agency, side businesses) before the core is mastered are the typical service-business failure.
numbers: One product, one avatar, one channel until $1M/year.

### POD-0865#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:12
claim: Underpricing to dodge tough sales talks and "making it affordable for everyone" starves the mission.
detail: Without cash a business cannot grow or help more people, so the owner must accept making money. Hormozi tells of his wife, then a trainer with the same bleeding-heart attitude, who agreed she could help more people if the company earned.

### POD-0865#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:15
claim: Fix leaking YouTube traffic with CTAs at several places inviting viewers to DM on Instagram.
detail: Viewers were already DMing him, so the team eased the path rather than building a funnel. Steps: a CTA about 30% into each video; the CTA in the description; the same CTA in a pinned comment (it survives description changes); the same in the YouTube profile; and proactive DMs to new Instagram followers, most of whom came from YouTube.
numbers: Estimated $20,000 to $50,000 a month left on the table; CTA at the 30% mark of the video.

### POD-0865#06 | TACTIC | topic: outreach | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:25
claim: Filter DM conversations with a one-line question to cut wasted time.
detail: Message each new follower asking whether they came for free material or for legal help. Anyone wanting help becomes a lead. This qualifies early and clears time-wasters out of the inbox.

### POD-0865#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:16
claim: Use one front door: many lines in the water, one boat.
detail: Point all traffic to a single place to convert rather than six separate offers.

### POD-0865#08 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:12:18
claim: Hourly billing capped the lawyer at about $1,500 per client; an outcome price with a premium anchor fixed it.
detail: He charged $250/hour, so average client value was $1,500 (about six hours), though clients came for a result. For serious musicians he moved to a $5,800 outcome fee plus a commission, shown beside a $25,000 offer (anchor). Hormozi's expected effect: customers worth three to five times more before commission.
numbers: $250/hour x 6 hours = $1,500; new offer $5,800; anchor $25,000; 3x to 5x value per customer.

### POD-0865#09 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:26
claim: Outcome pricing aligns incentives, moves talk from cost of time to value of outcome and qualifies buyers.
detail: Five effects listed: the lawyer gains when the client succeeds; the high anchor makes the middle price look cheap; the talk shifts from time to outcome; only serious buyers proceed; premium price signals premium service. Pitch line: some bill hourly and gain from taking longer, we bill outcome and a commission, which motivates both sides to finish fast.

### POD-0865#10 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:16
claim: If conversion is hit or miss, check whether a repeatable sales flow exists.
detail: Here leads arrived via Instagram and random Calendly bookings, with no clarity on what was pitched to whom, when or how. Without a repeatable sales process, there will be volatility in the business.
