### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: Impact vs Investment Matrix (RICE) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: When a client is stuck, look first for cash opportunities lying on the ground and for threats; only then address strengths and weaknesses.
detail: He cites the MBA reversal of SWOT ("OTSU") that weights opportunities and threats more. A business bleeding cash should not start with culture fixes.

### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: retention-ltv | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: If payroll is due within 30 days, implementing a retention system will not solve that problem, because it takes about 12 weeks to pan out.
detail: Retention is still essential afterwards; churn of 30% a month counts as a threat, not a mere weakness. Cash opportunities and threats come first.
numbers: Retention system payoff about 12 weeks; churn example 30% a month.
### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:10
claim: A coach's value is choosing which of many plays to run first by opportunity cost, which needs a large arsenal of plays seen executed.
detail: Beginners fall back on more ads, harder lead work and better selling. Example opportunity: a gym without a one-on-one nutrition and accountability programme beside group training.

### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: promotions-launches | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:20
claim: Fastest cash: text existing customers a reason-why offer with very few spots, for example three people for a one-year transformation at $3,000.
detail: All three quick plays sell only to the existing base, because there is no time for a new campaign. With about 100 customers, around three take it. Limit the number you sell and use the cash to buy time.
numbers: 3 spots x $3,000 = $9,000; about 3 takers per 100 customers.
applies_to: Gym or local business with an existing member list.

### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: money-models | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:52
claim: An internal paid challenge priced at $100-$200 normally gets about 20-25% of the customer base to join.
detail: With 150 members that is about 35-40 sign-ups at around $150, roughly $5,000, though with fulfilment cost. He suggests the three-person play might be better, and if that one was done recently, this would probably be the play.
numbers: 20-25% take rate at $100-$200; 150 members x 25% at $150 = about $5,000.
### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#06 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: - | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:41
claim: For more money, give the challenge away free to members and upsell food and supplements, which reaches about 85% take rate and 80-90% closing on the upsell.
detail: There is little fulfilment. In his supplement company the order is passed on without touching inventory; he buys at about $5 and sells at $20 and keeps the difference. Yields $10,000-$20,000 in a month.
numbers: 85% take, 80-90% upsell close; $10,000-$20,000 per month; item bought at about $5, sold at $20.

### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#07 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:35
claim: Give all 150 members 14-28 days of free one-on-one nutrition and accountability, then charge about half the new-customer price to continue; half staying yields about $10,000 a month profit.
detail: Daily contact (weight, check-in, text to show up) raises retention. Assume 85% start (about 120), half stay (60), at $200 a month extra. A coach takes 20% ($2,400), leaving about $9,600 a month. One person can handle 60 clients, and can be the owner.
numbers: $49/week continuation (half the price for new buyers); 120 start; 60 stay x $200 = $12,000/month; coach 20% = $2,400; net about $9,600.

### YT-SvIcS-Q1Hl4#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: money-models | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:04
claim: Match the play to how fast you need cash: tomorrow means a paid-in-full text blast, two weeks means an internal apply/promotion, and if you can wait a month you should probably do the free-then-upsell hybrid.
