### POD-0064#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Before Gym Launch, he filled other people's gyms in 28 markets, selling about 100 people per 21-day campaign at a $500 ticket.
detail: He and his wife set up a tent outside gyms in the middle of nowhere and ran ads, in markets where $100 memberships struggled. Local owners doubted it. The goal was to prove it worked in every market before investing further.
numbers: 28 markets; about 100 sales per 21 days; $500 ticket vs $100 memberships.

### POD-0064#02 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:36
claim: He then hired six reps and flew each to a client gym for 21 days, covering travel, hotel, ads and commission while still profiting.
detail: In the first two weeks reps ran about a 50% show rate on appointments, compared with the roughly 70% he reached himself. The travelling model was later dropped because it was hard on reps.
numbers: 6 reps; 21 days on site; show rate about 50% vs his 70%.

### POD-0064#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: promotions-launches | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:59
claim: In the last five days of every on-site campaign the reps' sales doubled.
detail: A rep typically sold about 50 in the first two weeks and about 50 in the final week, about $25,000 of upfront sales in the last five days.
numbers: about 50 sales in the last week = about $25,000 upfront ($500 each).

### POD-0064#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: sales-process | fw: Urgency | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:51
claim: The doubling came from real scarcity: with no script change, the reps' tone and phrasing shifted because they truly would leave.
detail: Lost appointments meant lost commission, so they offered early or late slots. Real scarcity beats faked; if you must fake it, believe it. He asks why the same belief cannot be applied when you could in fact reschedule.

### POD-0064#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:42
claim: Only book same-day or next-day appointments; if the lead will not, do not schedule and handle it as an obstacle on the call.
detail: Show-up on day three drops sharply. Ask when they planned to work out, and if they say e.g. after work, offer a slot right after; if they resist, say you will stay late.
### POD-0064#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:43
claim: To get a same-day slot, ask when they planned to work out, then offer a time just after work.
detail: Steps: 1. ask when they planned to train; 2. if they say after work, ask when work ends and offer a slot just after; 3. if they cannot do today or tomorrow, ask how many times a week they plan to train (at least three) and offer a choice of two days; 4. shift tone to "I'll stay late for you".
numbers: at least 3 workouts a week (every other day).

### POD-0064#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:26
claim: A lead booked four days out shows up 25% of the time or less, so only about 12.5% become customers if half of shows close.
detail: Even with reminders, one in four shows; closing half yields 12.5% of booked leads. The lead feels they accomplished something by signing up; the story starts there for you but often ends there for them.
numbers: 25% show rate at 4 days out; 50% close (his hypothetical); 12.5% end to end.
### POD-0064#08 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:33
claim: Adopt a firm "no reschedules, please confirm your appointment" policy, framed as being for serious people, to double the show rate.
detail: Say it with conviction; he argues it helps the prospect, cuts your work, and lets you help twice as many people.

### POD-0064#09 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: sales-process | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:22
claim: A Boise, Idaho gym launch by Chris and Robin Barnett got 171 signups in 19 days, and its show rate rose from 45% to 70% in the last week.
detail: They told leads there was no rescheduling and they had to come in that day. Ad spend was $9,000.
numbers: 171 signups in 19 days; $9,000 ad spend; show rate 45% to 70%.
