### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: AI does not cancel other forms of leverage (capital, media, teams); they multiply with it.
detail: Leverage is the gap between input and output. Owners who maximise AI use often do lower-priority tasks faster and see token costs rise without more profit. Even frontier AI labs still employ thousands of people.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:54
claim: His company's latest step-up in revenue was not caused by AI, and several wealthy peers admit they personally barely use it.
detail: He said the increase would have come anyway. Rich peers publicly promote AI but, asked privately, do not use it daily; their teams do. Taken as informal evidence only.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:18
claim: Lacking AI forces prioritisation; most AI use cases he sees do not hit the business constraint.
detail: Exceptions he finds useful: more ad creative and an AI sales rep, which is more efficient and scalable but not the limiter, since he already knows how to build sales teams. Many constraints are not solvable by AI; otherwise everyone would be richer.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#04 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:07
claim: Raise delivery leverage by moving from one-on-one to one-to-many, or from scheduled to asynchronous appointments.
detail: Going from one-on-one to one-on-ten gives roughly ten times the leverage from one decision. Asynchronous delivery cuts the people needed. No technology expertise required.
numbers: One-on-one to one-on-ten: about 10x leverage.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:07
claim: Educate prospects through the sales process so fewer, shorter, better-qualified conversations let two reps replace ten.
detail: When prospects arrive with more information they need less from reps, who then close the same volume. His illustration is ten salespeople reduced to two without AI.
numbers: 10 reps to 2 for the same closes (illustrative).

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:55
claim: The highest-leverage act is deciding something is not a priority; that beats automating it.
detail: Cancelling a block of work frees the whole team, while automating an irrelevant task yields irrelevant output.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: offers, scaling-constraints | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:24
claim: AI is like access to cheap virtual assistants; growth still comes from demand, conversion and delivery quality, and a bad offer is not rescued by AI.
detail: Virtual assistants have long existed and the marketplace stayed the same: brands, good and bad offers. He is unsure AI can improve an offer.

### YT-WttZ6b-KUP4#08 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:05
claim: Ask whether implementing AI made you more money; if not, you are likely using it in the wrong place.
detail: Return to identifying the true constraint and focus the small amount of resources on the one thing that moves the needle; that skill outranks AI.
