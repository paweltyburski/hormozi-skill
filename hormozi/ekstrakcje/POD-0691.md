### POD-0691#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:11
claim: Take large risks with small money to build a fortune, and small risks with large money to keep it.
detail: After you already have wealth, the goal is preservation with slow growth, not tenfold bets. He compares to professional gamblers who enter a casino with $10,000 hoping to leave with $12,000, a narrow spread.
numbers: Gambler example: $10k stake targeting $12k.

### POD-0691#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:37

### POD-0691#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:45
