### MZ-4Jo8bWSpLgA#01 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A men's salon and hair-loss clinic owner cannot get staff to take before-and-after photos consistently.
detail: He pays a monthly bonus tied to a goal sheet (pre-books, photos, etc.) and is a rewarder by temperament. Staff chose bonuses over a front-desk hire.
numbers: $100 monthly bonus in total.

### MZ-4Jo8bWSpLgA#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Rewards work through low latency: pay $5 to $10 right after the session, then gradually thin out.
detail: A small immediate reward beats a larger month-end one. Later, reward every other time, extending the interval as in addiction.
numbers: $5-$10 per photo set.

### MZ-4Jo8bWSpLgA#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Reduce the social cost and build the task into the sign-out process.
detail: A customer cannot be checked out until the step is done. Photos at arrival as a "fashion shoot", afterwards at exit, handled by the front desk.

### MZ-4Jo8bWSpLgA#04 | SCRIPT | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:29
claim: Condition the bonus on the task, and say you will hire someone with that money if it is not done.
detail: Structure: the money is for this work; if not done, it funds a hire; give a 30-day window to fix it.
numbers: 30 days to fix.
