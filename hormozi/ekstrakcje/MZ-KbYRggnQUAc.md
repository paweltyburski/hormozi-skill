### MZ-KbYRggnQUAc#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Scattered attention comes from fear of being left behind; trust that you will change when something truly affects your business.
detail: Until then keep running the loop: gather attention, point it somewhere, make a compelling offer, deliver. Below roughly $1M, only letting people know and delivering make money; a converting sales script or webinar need not be remade.

### MZ-KbYRggnQUAc#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:05
claim: Split a long workday into thirds: promote, build, deliver, on a timer.
detail: Of 12 hours, the first four go to promotion, the next four to building, the last to delivering what was built; scale it to your hours. Know your day so it holds no surprises.
numbers: roughly 4 hours promoting, 4 building, 1 delivering (his example of a 12-hour day).

detail: Every added activity makes you less focused, so choose how much non-focus you allow.
