### POD-0747#01 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: A personal brand is the set of things you choose to be associated with, and you can only add positive pairings, never remove them.
detail: The mind cannot process negatives, so a brand is built by pruning: decide what you refuse to be linked with, such as podcasts hosted by people you dislike, and what is left becomes purer and stronger.

### POD-0747#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Impostor syndrome comes from pretending; state facts, and if they are not compelling, do more and then talk about it.
detail: Describe exactly what happened (for example dials made and mortgages sold) and nobody can fault you. Effort alone is a credential: "35,000 pieces of content" is interesting even with zero followers. Formula: do the thing, talk about it, do something bigger, talk about it.
