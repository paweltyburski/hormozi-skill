### YT-ZLGqnXkjfsQ#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:26
claim: Decision method: name the outcome you want, name the best and worst expected results, ask which path gets closer, then ask why you are not taking it, which moves the question from logic to emotion.
detail: Used with an 18-year-old deciding whether to return to college after trouble: he could not state a positive outcome, and when Hormozi suggested college might be a socially acceptable shield against not yet succeeding in business, he answered "well, maybe." Comparing four years and $200,000 in college versus on starting a business made the logic point to the business; the emotional driver preventing that choice was still to be examined.
numbers: 4 years; $200,000.
### YT-ZLGqnXkjfsQ#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:17
claim: People often choose a socially acceptable option to avoid facing an emotional reason such as fear of failing; naming the fear removes its power.
detail: Failing does not make you a failure. Choose a "maybe" over a "never".

### YT-ZLGqnXkjfsQ#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:40
claim: Hormozi's early mindset was to out-sacrifice, not outwork, rivals, and he lists what he gave up (football, all TV, going out, some fitness and sleep) while in the hard stretch.
detail: He argues great results come from unbalanced effort for a season, not a whole life, and writes down trade-offs one is willing to make.

### YT-ZLGqnXkjfsQ#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:29
claim: Successful marketers and sellers typically spend three to five years of hard repetition before breakthrough; Hormozi did 4,000 one-on-one consultations over four years at 15 to 20 appointments a day.
detail: He also cites Russell Brunson pitching on stage for five years, a peer cold calling for two to three years and Dan Kennedy selling door to door for three.
