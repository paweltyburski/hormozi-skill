### MZ-sTrQApreIqw#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An agency selling for founders' high-ticket offers ($5M revenue, six clients) wanted 50, but its clients' product was weak.
detail: About 80% of revenue is done-for-you business-opportunity clients. Hormozi: 50 small clients means four make 70% of revenue, equity worthless, regulated gray area.
numbers: $5M revenue; 6 clients; goal 50+; 4 clients could hold 70%.

### MZ-sTrQApreIqw#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:58
claim: Apply marketing and sales skill to legitimate franchisors, becoming their sales arm for fee and equity.
detail: A 10-year franchise can bring $150K up front, kept by the sales org; 10-30% stakes in the parent have value. Replace six clients with six legitimate ones.
numbers: $150K upfront; 10-30% stake.

### MZ-sTrQApreIqw#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:32
claim: Only about 5% of franchisors reach 100 locations; in the typical story he describes, a franchisor sells to friends and family, opens 10-15 locations, then stalls because they don't know how to attract customers.
detail: Legal setup cost spoken as "$250, $500" (unit unclear in audio). Founders aren't willing to pay franchise salespeople well, which is the opening.
numbers: About 5% reach 100 locations; typical stall story at 10-15 locations.
### MZ-sTrQApreIqw#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: retention-ltv | fw: Cohort Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:54
claim: Sales churn is cohort churn, and mercenary reps leave for 1% more; passion for the business helps.
detail: Example: hire three, keep one who stays three years. Also, direct response is declining, with CAC up and trust down.
numbers: Hire 3, keep 1.
