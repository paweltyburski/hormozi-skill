### YT-cGup9yYrOoc#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: In five years 22 clients or employees tried to repurpose his licensing material, and his early overreactions hurt only his own business.
detail: Copiers typically also disparaged him and solicited his staff and clients. Once he dramatically changed his business model in response to someone targeting his best clients, and it only damaged his own company; most customers never knew the conflict existed. By his count he beat all 22.
numbers: 22 copycat attempts in 5 years; 22 of 22 defeated.

### YT-cGup9yYrOoc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:54
claim: You predict people well by assuming they act in their own best interest, so align their interest with yours.
detail: Take yourself out of the equation and ask what serves them. To get anyone to do what you want, make their best interest and yours coincide.

### YT-cGup9yYrOoc#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:08
claim: When a copycat gets loud, get louder about your own message and never name or acknowledge them.
detail: Speaking their name raises them to your level. Use the irritation to market and sell harder, and judge only by results ("beat them in the field").

### YT-cGup9yYrOoc#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:19
claim: Copiers depend on your customer base and model, so they stay a subset of you; they signal that you are being watched, not that you are losing.
detail: Their imitation shows they are followers acting from scarcity, and their focus on you directs attention to you. Being copied means what you have is desirable.

### YT-cGup9yYrOoc#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:53
claim: A customer is only proven once a cheaper copy has been offered and refused, so worry is answered by raising value, not by attacking the rival.
detail: Attributed to the HubSpot founder. Channel nervous energy into what you give customers, since whoever gives the most to them wins.
