### MZ-GwKqMVnXeCk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:40
claim: Customers are fractal: one percent of customers can bring as much revenue as the other 99, and each price tier adds another doubling.
detail: Example ladder: $100 a month, then $500, then $2,500 and higher, each tier roughly doubling revenue.
numbers: 1% of customers can equal the other 99% in revenue; about 2x revenue per tier at roughly 5x price.

### MZ-GwKqMVnXeCk#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:40
claim: Make premium offers available and state the whole truth: this is very expensive and only for those it suits, with cheaper options for others.
detail: Owners feel awkward and sell from their own wallet. A marketing plaque: state the facts and tell the truth.
