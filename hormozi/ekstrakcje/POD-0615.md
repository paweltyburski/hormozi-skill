### POD-0615#16 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:31:04

detail: He asked for a contract a fifth-grader could read, because lawyers relaying messages create misunderstandings and cost. In one deal the lawyers argued two weeks over a 24-month, 50-mile non-compete; he asked the founder if he planned to compete with his own business, got no, and settled it in two minutes.
