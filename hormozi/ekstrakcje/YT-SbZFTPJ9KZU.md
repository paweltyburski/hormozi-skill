### YT-SbZFTPJ9KZU#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: The four scarcest skills are: raise and multiply capital, capture and multiply attention, return time to people at no extra cost, and influence behavior at mass scale at no extra cost.
detail: Examples: allocating raised money at higher returns than anything else; Rogan or Zuckerberg capturing then multiplying attention; Google saving search time, or software that gives back 10 minutes a day to everyone; changing beliefs in a mass audience, which changes behavior. Any one can build a billion-dollar fortune. They are judged at maximal scale and as degrees, not yes or no.

### YT-SbZFTPJ9KZU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:15
claim: Each skill matches a leverage type (capital, media, technology that returns time, labor/influence) and each compounds rather than adds.
detail: Money multiplied gets more money; attention multiplied is passed on by recipients; saved time attracts more users; influenced people tell others. A technologist friend: all technology automates luxury, meaning less convenience cost, effort and sacrifice.
