### MZ-LbzLYThyd-4#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: operator-mindset | also: other | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: He cites research that monks with 40,000+ meditation hours score three to four standard deviations above average on well-being, and weighs devoting about four hours a day.
detail: By his estimate, a year of practice gives the first chunk of the effect, an hour a day for a decade perhaps one to one and a half deviations, and replicating the monks needs about four hours a day. His reasoning is maximising: if the answer to spending all available time on it is yes, take it seriously. Evidence is observational (selection effects acknowledged).
numbers: 40,000+ hours; +3 to +4 SD; ~4 hours/day to replicate; 1 hour/day for 10 years = ~+1 to 1.5 SD (his estimate).
