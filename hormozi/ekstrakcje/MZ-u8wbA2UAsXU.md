claim: A founder of a real-estate TV and media company, already a week into a sale process, asked how to choose among a strategic buyer, a portal and private equity.
detail: B2B model first (real estate professionals), content repurposed to social. He owns 100%, wants to stay on, calls himself the David and fears joining a Goliath.
numbers: $16M gross revenue; $4M EBITDA; target to double EBITDA; founder age 46.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Screen buyers in order: the people, then the product vision, then the economics.
detail: If you dislike the people nothing else matters. Next ask whether the shared vision feels heavy or light. Only then judge the economics.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:18
claim: Very few businesses sell to institutional buyers, and many processes start but fewer finish, often underwhelming after diligence.
detail: Behave as if the deal will not happen, because diligence tends to bring renegotiation on terms.
numbers: About 1 in 10,000 businesses sell to an institutional buyer (his recollection, math unsure).

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Ask a would-be seller why they want to sell and whether they want to leave or stay.
detail: The honest answer decides which buyer fits; reasons like loneliness or the "dream" are weaker than cash and risk.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:54
claim: After a majority sale you become functionally an employee, and many entrepreneurs are more depressed afterward.
detail: A big buyer brings bureaucracy and politics; your say shrinks. Expect to run out the non-compete or earn-out and then want to start something new. Be sure before giving up the business.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: A financial buyer gives more autonomy than a strategic one only while numbers keep rising; a missed projection gets the company taken over.
detail: Outcomes are deal specific. Choose the trade you can live with.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:11
claim: Selling to de-risk your family is a fully acceptable reason; if the reasons are softer, think harder first.
detail: Check yourself honestly in the mirror about the real motive, not the one that sounds good.
numbers: Example math: about $40M proceeds, $30M+ after taxes, invested to live on about $1.5M a year.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#08 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:03
claim: A solar sales company losing its tax credit wanted to build an HVAC business to exit later.
detail: Solar is outsourced installation; roofing and HVAC are done in-house. His constraint is his own time. Hormozi expected technician supply, not demand, to bind first.
numbers: $6M revenue, about $5M from solar; 35-person solar sales team.

### MZ-u8wbA2UAsXU#09 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:40
claim: Build the same recruiting, onboarding, training and manage-up-or-out funnel on the technician supply side as on the sales side.
detail: Technician supply will be the next big constraint; if the team can sell door to door, getting business is easy.
