### YT-21flGkcZO3A#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: If you sell your own product, he says to focus on content that brings customers rather than views. Chasing views makes sense mainly if your business is selling media to advertisers.
detail: Advertisers mostly price media by audience and views, so media businesses are incentivized toward volume. Most creators are usually building businesses and use content to get customers, so buyers matter more than reach.
numbers: Last year: 3 billion impressions, 4.5 million new subscribers, 35,000 pieces of content, over $106M of sales in one launch weekend.
### YT-21flGkcZO3A#02 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: verticals | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:56
claim: A dietitian with under 6,000 followers made over $1M a year teaching only how to bill insurance.
detail: Her posts got 9 to 20 likes, but her followers were almost all other dietitians. The ultra-narrow topic matched a paying audience, which broke his belief that small reach cannot make money.
numbers: Under 6,000 followers; over $1M a year; about 9-20 likes per post.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#03 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:43
claim: A quarter of broader top-of-funnel content broke view records while book sales, leads and applications fell.
detail: Test about 18 months earlier on the theory that a bigger net finds more big customers. The metrics he cares about dropped while vanity metrics rose.
numbers: One quarter; test about 18 months before recording.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#04 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:14
claim: A later quarter repeated the lesson: the six most-viewed videos (about 1.2M down to 350K views) produced zero sales.
detail: They were beginner-oriented. The top revenue video earned about $270,000 and covered where the money is and how to segment customers, which speaks mainly to existing owners; the next top earners were deep interviews with multimillion-dollar businesses, at roughly 100,000 and 250,000 views.
numbers: Top six videos 1.2M, 1M, 800K, 500K, 500K, 350K views, $0 sales; best revenue video $270,000; revenue videos 100K and 250K views.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:44
claim: The algorithm shows what most people like, not what the most valuable people like, so views are the wrong signal for a business.
detail: He cites a video showing wealth distribution against population: half the audience holds a tiny share of money, the other half most of it. The ideal is a video that has both quality and volume, but if forced to choose, pick revenue.
numbers: Illustration: 50% of the audience holds $2, the other 50% holds $98.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#06 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:31
claim: Make "vertical value" content that helps beginners and large operators alike, rather than content useful only above one revenue level.
detail: A video on how the top 1% think about money serves rich and new viewers and gets views and revenue. A video on going from 1 to 10 million will get few views but attract buyers with more purchasing power. Do not worry that low views mean you are underserving people.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#07 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:20
claim: To aim content at buyers, study your top 20% of customers by spend and make videos about their common traits, messages and problems.
detail: Expect views and subscribers to fall while sales rise. The alternative is a low-cost, serve-everyone strategy, which is hard to run.
numbers: Top 20% of customers by spend as the reference group.

### YT-21flGkcZO3A#08 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:20
claim: Attribute revenue to videos by putting tracking links (UTMs) below each video and a call to action for a lead magnet or next step inside.
detail: This lets the back end show which videos produced revenue.
