### POD-0817#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: A painting teacher runs a free community, a $29/month school with a weekly live class, and a roughly $5,000-per-year mentorship, and wants to reach $100K a month.
detail: Delivery ladder: free gets long tutorials; $29 adds a weekly live class run as 8-10 week courses; $5,000 adds two classes a week plus on-demand one-on-ones. Traffic comes from YouTube to a free blueprint opt-in, then free community, a setter in DMs, then a call. About half the $29 members are old lifetime-access buyers.
numbers: ~20 high-ticket clients (~$8K/month averaged); ~150 monthly payers (~$4.5-5K/month); total ~$13-14K/month; 9,000+ email subscribers; 56K YouTube subscribers at ~10K views per video; 100-email welcome sequence.
applies_to: Creator with community, around $13K/month.

### POD-0817#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:58
claim: Plan growth as doubles and triples rather than one miracle fix: here $14K to $30K is a double, and a triple on top reaches $100K.
detail: Rarely does one thing change the numbers; big results come from stacking several 2x and 3x steps. Sum-up: a 5x on free-to-call conversion times a 3x on the $29 buyers is about 15x.
numbers: ~$14K to ~$30K (2x) then 3x to ~$100K; 5x * 3x = 15x.

### POD-0817#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:58
claim: Ask for conversion at each step: how many join, how many respond, how many are offered a call, how many book, show and close.

### POD-0817#06 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: Ascension Path | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:38
claim: Seven people a week were buying the $29 plan on their own and nobody spoke to them; include a free onboarding call to convert them upward.
detail: The owner pitched $5,000 first, then annual, then monthly, but only on calls he took (about 2 a week). Giving every $29 buyer a call adds up to seven more weekly sales conversations; this was the first-priority fix and could triple the business. Use the calls as trust-building, offer the annual plan with a bonus call, and optionally a one-month trial of the high tier; unwilling buyers get the annual.
numbers: ~7 self-serve $29 sign-ups/week (2.69% page conversion last week); calls taken ~2/week.

### POD-0817#10 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: Content Unit (Hook-Retain-Reward) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:23
claim: Open YouTube videos with promise, pain hook and pulled-forward proof, then a numbered roadmap, all in about 10 to 15 seconds.
detail: The video had no roadmap, so the viewer did not know where it was going. Proof should be as relevant as possible (helped thousands of people paint gouache; sold paintings in that medium), and the roadmap should be stated fast ("six things I will cover"). Show the face, not just the painting.
numbers: Intro time target: 10-15 seconds.

### POD-0817#11 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:33

detail: Use YouTube's built-in A/B test. Text the thumbnail to your phone to see how few words are legible at real size, since most designers work on large screens.

### POD-0817#12 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:29:20
claim: Place the lead-magnet call to action about a third of the way into a video, tied to what was just taught, and print the freebie and flip to a page on camera.
