### POD-0437#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:46
claim: Admitting something against your interest, such as pointing to a cheaper source, built enough trust to lift his close rate to nearly everyone.
detail: Out of a key supplement during a challenge launch, he told a customer she could buy it cheaper at a big-box store. Selling improved sharply and he taught newcomers the same move. He calls it the damaging admission.
numbers: He reports ~100% himself; trainees closed 80-90% of retail walk-ins.

### POD-0437#02 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:06
claim: Tape a number on each product and attach its use to an existing routine (next to the toothbrush, with makeup removal).
detail: People struggle to form or break habits, so piggyback on current ones. It also shows you coach, not just sell.

### POD-0437#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:06
claim: Offer the card on file from the signed contract and have them sign as their ticket out, making the add-on a one-click yes.
detail: He confirms the card's last digits from the previous day's paperwork; no wallet or new decision is needed.

### POD-0437#04 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:12
claim: For price concerns ask if they are on a budget, then drop the least important item and offer a free substitute, never implying it was unnecessary.
detail: Rank items by necessity versus faster benefit, remove the lowest, ask if that works, and if not remove another with advice on what to do instead. Calling the removed item useless makes you look like an upseller.

### POD-0437#05 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:55
claim: When they hesitate, reintroduce the problem the product solves so the cost of skipping it is felt.
detail: Since you prescribed it, they trust it and often decide it is worth it.

### POD-0437#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:55
claim: Ask which old-identity habits they will stop (going out, drinking, smoking) and what those cost, to find the money.
detail: He then says they just found some money and ties spending to the new identity.
