### MZ-DIUP4-VAPT8#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: paid-ads, strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A handmade skincare brand, mostly paid-ad driven with fading celebrity endorsements, wants to double sales in a year.
detail: Founder left TV production, started in 2017, friends gave free video endorsements. Operation: handmade, shipped by a small team. Ads no longer perform and there are too few of them.
numbers: Revenue $1.3M; profit $275,000; 70% of customers from Meta and Google ads.

### MZ-DIUP4-VAPT8#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Ignore key-man risk until you plan to sell: it lowers value for an acquirer, and nobody buys a business this small.
detail: At around $1M the founder is expected to be key. Focus on growth levers.

### MZ-DIUP4-VAPT8#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: Crazy Eight | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: For this skincare founder's e-commerce business, Hormozi names three levers: sell to more customers, raise average order value, get customers to buy more times.
detail: Any one of them would double the business; doing all three would 8x it.
numbers: 2x on three levers is 8x revenue.
### MZ-DIUP4-VAPT8#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:01
claim: Without a brand an e-commerce firm is a click arbitrage business whose margins erode as audiences get colder.
detail: Buying clicks for a dollar and selling for two ends at $10M revenue with no profit. Brand gives cheaper clicks, higher conversion at premium prices and higher repeat rates. Commoditized category (skincare is mostly water).

### MZ-DIUP4-VAPT8#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:47
claim: Run UGC campaigns with many avatars and layered email flows, and judge on blended ROAS.
detail: Ad platform favors many micro niches over single hero ads. Choose influencers who represent the brand's values, vary hooks by awareness level, add flows to bring people back. Some ad sets fall while blended ROAS rises.
