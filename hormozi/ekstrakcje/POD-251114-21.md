### POD-251114-21#02 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:57
claim: A continuity product delivering a new money-making play every 14 days took Gym Launch from $300,000 to $4 million a month.
detail: Sold to a gym that had no supplements, semi-private training or churn systems, as a priced upsell with a pre-start bonus; each play targeted that gym's current bottleneck. It exhausted him for almost two years but funded customer acquisition.
numbers: $42,000 a year for three years (about $3,200 a month quoted); revenue up more than 13x.

### POD-251114-21#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:59
claim: A good offer gets customers to start; recurring bonuses give reasons to stay.
detail: Revenue scaled by giving existing customers reasons to keep paying, not by selling more new ones.

### POD-251114-21#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:52
claim: When Hormozi makes a retention bonus, he thinks of a when (delay or milestone) and a what (one-time, variable or lifetime upgrade) and pairs them.
detail: Delay: after a time or number of payments. Milestone: after a customer action or result. One-time bonuses extend stay once; variable ones arrive on a schedule but change; a lifetime upgrade is a permanent change in status. Ideas: faster, easier, risk-free.
### POD-251114-21#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:31
claim: Sample bonuses for local service, digital, consumable and lease businesses.
detail: Four months in a row unlocks advanced status and an annual event; month five unlocks first-in-line slots; each referral adds a module; monthly treat or toy; lifetime free item while subscribed (lost on cancelling); free car service at set mileage.
numbers: Four months; every 3,000 miles or six months.

### POD-251114-21#06 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:17
claim: Tell customers what type of bonus is coming but keep its content a surprise, and preview the next one right after giving one.
detail: Customers cannot stay for a bonus they do not know exists; surprise adds flexibility and value.

### POD-251114-21#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:02
claim: Prefer variable bonuses unless an upgrade is large and permanent, since customers adapt to anything.
detail: Frequent new or better items, even less valuable, hold interest longer; streaming services do all three.

### POD-251114-21#08 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:02
claim: Set milestones that make customers more successful or advertise for you, ideally both.
detail: For example, publicly announcing a weight-loss challenge raises adherence and advertises you.

### POD-251114-21#09 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:14
