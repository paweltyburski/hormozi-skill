### MZ-Y5P2lMu4LQY#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:26
claim: In his view, discerning good feedback comes down to two things: aligned incentives and real domain competence.
detail: Does the person benefit from your doing better, and do they have enough domain expertise? Friends who have eaten yogurt could very well judge taste; a mother who has never run a business is in a different position on how the business works. Ignoring all feedback is very difficult in practice: his podcast, emails and sales presentations changed in response to feedback.
