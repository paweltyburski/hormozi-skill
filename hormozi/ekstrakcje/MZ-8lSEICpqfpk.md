### MZ-8lSEICpqfpk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: money-models | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Move an information business toward services built on consumables (community, accountability, feedback), because information value falls fast.
detail: A course video is used once, while consumables are wanted again each month. For enterprise value, build a sellable asset; repeated new courses are fine for cash but not sale value. Use the information as lead magnet.

### MZ-8lSEICpqfpk#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A sales-coaching business should give away all information and sell implementation, such as listening to calls and live coaching.
detail: Demonstrated expertise and trust can later flip to recruiting or done-for-you. A model that depends on what a competitor can give away better for free is in trouble.
