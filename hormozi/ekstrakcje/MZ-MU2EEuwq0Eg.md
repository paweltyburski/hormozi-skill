### MZ-MU2EEuwq0Eg#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: If you do not get what you want, either your model of the world is wrong or it has the wrong or too few variables.
detail: He says thinking purely in inputs and outputs has made his predictions more accurate, which has reinforced the habit.

### MZ-MU2EEuwq0Eg#02 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A trait label describes a behaviour pattern but does not explain it; explanation means the reinforcement history behind the behaviour.
detail: "Johnny stole because he is dishonest" is circular, since dishonest only names the habit of stealing. The real cause is that he was rewarded for stealing, or copied someone who was. People who confuse description with explanation are repeatedly surprised by reality.

### MZ-MU2EEuwq0Eg#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Make any goal actionable by unbundling it into concrete behaviours, practising them, then re-bundling under the original word.
detail: A child told to get better at basketball cannot act, so split into dribbling, passing, shooting, then into exact motions such as a left-foot step and elbows out, with a clear success test (the pass reaches the runner). Repeat until the behaviour chain is the skill.

### MZ-MU2EEuwq0Eg#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:27
claim: Conflicts persist because both sides define a word like love or kindness as different behaviours, so ask what it means.
detail: A couple fight over whether paying bills or being told you are pretty shows love. Two employees at Acquisition.com: one asked for kindness and politeness; asked what that meant, the answer became a concrete request to ask more questions instead of making statements, and the conflict ended.
