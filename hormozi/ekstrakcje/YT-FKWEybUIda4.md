### YT-FKWEybUIda4#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His first business went from a failed Groupon and a $5,000-a-month lease to 29 clients through Facebook ads learned at a conference.
detail: He had $5,000 in savings and no local contacts. The $3,000 workshop promised $10,000 by the weekend and did not deliver that, but taught him Facebook ads.
numbers: Rent $5,000/month; savings $5,000; workshop $3,000; 29 first clients.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#02 | EVOLUTION | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: Core Four | novelty: updates | ts: 00:01:53
claim: He started as "the paid ads guy" but moved all or most effort toward channels two to six (earned, owned, outbound, affiliates, referrals) because they carry less risk and make the company more sellable.
detail: With experience, he diversified. The people-based channels do not depend on ad accounts or platform rules, so he sleeps better. He did not drop paid, earned or owned media, but adds the others. Most of his customers no longer come from paid ads.
numbers: He estimates about $4-5M a year of ad spend.
### YT-FKWEybUIda4#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:53
claim: Outside consumer products, the largest companies are not built on direct-response ads, and ads can exhaust a niche quickly.
detail: Consumer goods firms behave like media companies. B2B examples such as Salesforce grew mainly through word of mouth and outbound. At scale in B2B, paid ads can burn through a niche market fast, so you must learn other channels.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:31
claim: Group the six channels into platform-based (paid, earned, owned media) and people-based (manual outbound, affiliates, referrals); referrals compound while the rest are linear.
detail: He runs this first when taking on a $3-10M company: is the current channel best long term, and are there quick wins to double or quadruple customers? Linear means a fixed input-to-output ratio, such as 1 to 3 at any volume.
numbers: Portfolio entry size $3-10M revenue; example linear ratio 1:3.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:02
claim: Owned media includes every friend and follower you can message directly, such as 2,000 Facebook friends plus 2,000 followers, which is 4,000 free leads.
detail: Entrepreneurs without customers often have not contacted them. He knows someone who makes $1M a month from one platform's contacts.
numbers: 4,000 contacts in the example; $1M a month for one contact.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: pricing | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:41
claim: Integrate affiliates into their customer journey and give them a better value proposition than they have alone, rather than leaving business cards.
detail: Walk partners through what their customer experiences, such as a note stapled to the bag with a few agreed words, and pay per redemption. Affiliates cost only the cost of acquiring the affiliate.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#07 | EXAMPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: offers | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:46
claim: A dry cleaner offers a clothing store's buyers a free cleaning of the item just bought, which improves the store's offer and costs the store nothing.
detail: The cleaner can also pay the store per redeemed customer. Doing this with 200 such stores is the aim; a tailor partnership works the same way.
numbers: Scale target in the example: 200 partner stores.

### YT-FKWEybUIda4#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:46
