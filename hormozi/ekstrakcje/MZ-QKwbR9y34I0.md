### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#01 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Drop emojis from ad copy because they no longer look native.
detail: Emojis began as a native-looking trick around 2014. Now every marketer uses them, so the trend has swung back. The ad should still follow ad structure but look like a normal feed post.

### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#02 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: Call-Outs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:01
claim: Do not call out a label people will not self-identify with.
detail: A "manifestors" label is too narrow. Move it up or down a level to a belief group ("people who believe in...").

### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: offers | fw: Call-Outs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:41
claim: Open with the pain in their current life, phrased so the reader nods.
detail: Example hooks: "ever keep trying things and nothing sticks?" or "ever look in the mirror and wonder why you are not further along?". Also reformat testimonials so they punch in like video.

### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:54
claim: Pair every feature with its benefit, then with the benefit of the benefit.
detail: Meal plan, benefit: never wonder what is for dinner; benefit of the benefit: no guesswork, hit your goals. If the phrase is banner-blind ("no guesswork"), reword it: the easiest thing to eat is also the right thing.

### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#05 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:09
claim: Fix most copy with simple sentences, few commas and audience-matched analogies.
detail: Use ellipses to chain points. Pick analogies from what the audience knows: cars for auto-shop owners, children for mothers.

### MZ-QKwbR9y34I0#06 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:24
claim: Teach inside the ad and show process steps, not abstract images.
detail: Value before purchase proves value after it. Replace colourful AI-style images with the implementation steps and why they work.
