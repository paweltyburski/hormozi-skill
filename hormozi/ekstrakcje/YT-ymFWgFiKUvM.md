### YT-ymFWgFiKUvM#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: other | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:23
claim: Pay off high-interest debt before chasing investment returns, because it is a guaranteed negative return.
detail: Example comparison: stock returns of about 9-10% versus credit cards charging 24%. Compounding works against you on debt, so remove it before pursuing market gains. Mortgage and car payments count as debt too.
numbers: Cards ~24% interest versus ~9-10% market return.

### YT-ymFWgFiKUvM#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: other | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:11
claim: A money ladder modelled on Dave Ramsey's baby steps: starter emergency fund, debt payoff, bigger emergency fund, cut risky spending, invest 15% pre-tax, pay off the mortgage.
detail: Order: (1) $1,000-$5,000 starter fund before debt, to learn to save and absorb the unexpected expense that arrives about monthly. (2) Pay off consumer debt. (3) Build 3-6 months of living expenses in an interest-bearing account, as safety, not investment. (4) Remove risky spending (leases, expensive cars). (5) Invest 15% of pre-tax income automatically. (6) Downsize or pay off the house. Presented as what he did himself to get where he is, which viewers can model if they find it useful.
numbers: Starter fund $1,000-$5,000; full fund 3-6 months of expenses; invest 15% of pre-tax income.
