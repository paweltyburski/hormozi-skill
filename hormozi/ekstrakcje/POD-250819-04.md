### POD-250819-04#01 | SCRIPT | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Decoy Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:50
claim: The tanning salon turned a $5 pass into a membership with a "turkey talk" about why more sessions are needed.
detail: Staff ask how tan the customer wants to get. When they name a few shades darker, explain that a turkey cooked at double heat burns, so colour needs at least five to ten spaced sessions, longer than five days. Then offer to credit the pass toward the first month, and contrast many $25 day passes with unlimited use at $19.99 a month.
numbers: 5-day pass $5; day pass $25; membership $19.99/month; 5 to 10 sessions needed.

### POD-250819-04#08 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Decoy Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:08
claim: Present the premium first; if they want the decoy, ask whether they are here for free stuff or lasting results.
detail: Keep the decoy in your back pocket, but if the lead asks for it you must present it. Ask the "free or results" question. If results, go straight to premium. If free, present the decoy, immediately contrast it with the premium, then ask which will get them to their goal faster. They will pick premium and you proceed.
