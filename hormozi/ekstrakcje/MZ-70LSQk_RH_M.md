### MZ-70LSQk_RH_M#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Identity is the sum of behaviors, so swap a behavior when the relationship matters more than the habit.
detail: Treat yourself as a list of a thousand behaviors; ask whether changing one is worth more than the relationship, especially when it costs little and helps the other person. This reduces emotional rigidity.
