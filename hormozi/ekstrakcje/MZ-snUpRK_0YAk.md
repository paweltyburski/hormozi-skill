### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints, retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An Austin med spa with three locations and 15% profit loses focus; fix margins before a fourth site.
detail: Botox, filler, hormones, peptides, weight management. Pivoted from a 10,000 sq ft center to 1,200-1,500 sq ft sites. Leads mostly organic and SEO after an Instagram shadow ban. The star injector's share of revenue dropped as others were added.
numbers: Revenue $6.7M, goal $20M; profit about 15%, target 35-40%; star injector 60% of revenue to 23%; potential 5-7 Austin sites.

### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:05
claim: Each new location tends to dilute margin because founder attention is split; over-expansion means under-talent.
detail: First site has the best margins, second and third come in lower, so liability doubles while profit stays flat. Backfill talent and tweak pricing and sales motion first.

### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery, pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: Build a membership bundle so every visitor leaves with a membership, raising retention and scheduling utilization.
detail: Memberships make sessions per hour per technician and revenue per square foot rise. Walk through sales motions before adding sites.

### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#04 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: retention-ltv, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:55
claim: Give providers a career path and phantom stock vesting so clients stay loyal to the brand, not the injector.
detail: One-on-one providers take clients when they leave. Equity goes to providers, not managers, on a vesting schedule. Pay more even when margins are thin.

### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:38
claim: Organic and SEO work in a hot market because supply-demand is lopsided; learning paid ads gives an edge.
detail: Most med spa owners get customers by just being there.

### MZ-snUpRK_0YAk#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:31
claim: The business must serve customers, not the owners' entrepreneurial itches; shiny object syndrome shows.
detail: Customers notice when you serve yourself and go elsewhere. Do more of the model that works.
