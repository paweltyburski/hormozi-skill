### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A healthcare data-staffing firm on a fixed-fee retainer asked how to price larger clients with more systems.
detail: Advice: keep the fixed fee up to a threshold, then add tiers above it at a lower rate.

### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Use a waterfall: each increment of the value metric is charged at a lower rate, applied to that slice only and not retroactively.
detail: Example: 10% on the first 100K, 7% to 200K, then 5%, then 3%. Optimistic buyers assume they will reach top tiers, but the blended rate ends near 7%, still more money than a flat rate.
numbers: Slices 10% / 7% / 5% / 3% per 100K; blended near 7% (his estimate).

### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:18
claim: Tie tiers to the core value metric that rises with the value the client gets.
detail: For e-commerce it might just be revenue; for healthcare practices the number of providers (or seats) is a pretty good proxy. If they say yes to the first amount, the rest should be a shoo-in.
### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:06
claim: Add a very high top tier few will reach as a reverse anchor.
detail: Buyers fixate on the first price. His software companies all used this.

### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#05 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:44
claim: A motocross coach (camps in California, travelling clinics, online) thought he could not pass $1M a month; Hormozi said any business can scale.
detail: Do more events; when travel binds, train coaches against a checklist of the founder's intangibles, using a branded master coach with 1,000+ hours under him on a profit share. The new constraint is advertising.
numbers: Master coach 1,000+ training hours; target above $1M a month.

### MZ-k_CVWlRD-xk#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:20
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: if he had to pick, he guessed most of the motocross coach's revenue comes from the in-person offers (flying out and people flying to him), so do more of that and forget the online product.
detail: Here that means in-person offers, not the online product.
