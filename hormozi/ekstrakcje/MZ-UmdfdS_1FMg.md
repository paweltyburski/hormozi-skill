### MZ-UmdfdS_1FMg#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Open each training with manager kudos on the behaviour you want more of, then peer shout-outs, then role play.
detail: If you want more reviews, the first kudos goes to reviews. The manager sets the culture, because in any team the leader is the one at fault when results lag.

### MZ-UmdfdS_1FMg#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Role-play a four-part script by day: intro on Monday, discovery Tuesday, pitch Wednesday, close Thursday, and a "hot topic" on Friday.
detail: The hot topic is whatever the team struggles with that week, run all the way through the script. Practising only objection handling creates too many objections because the intro was poor; the whole script is sharpened each week. The manager models it, locks it in with one rep, then pairs reps.
numbers: Five days: intro, discovery, pitch, close, hot topic.

### MZ-UmdfdS_1FMg#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:09
claim: Structure the weekly one-on-one in three parts: personal, short term, long term.
detail: Personal comes first (a crisis takes the whole slot). Short term: reps send three calls they struggled with beforehand, and the manager spots the issue (intro, frame, letting the prospect lead) and role-plays it. Long term, for those hitting quota: career goals and skills to invest in; a day sitting in a senior meeting is a prized reward.
numbers: Three calls submitted before each one-on-one.

### MZ-UmdfdS_1FMg#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:58
claim: For repeated roles use a two-prong ladder (junior and senior setter, junior and senior closer), giving six steps, with early and frequent wins.
detail: Promote in the first 30 days for doing the basics (a raise), then titles after about 10 sales and a pay bump after about 50. Senior setter earns no weekend work and qualification for the next closer opening. Junior closers start and step up in pay (figures illustrative, units unclear in audio). Beyond closer: manage, advise or run a portfolio company. Space steps one to two months apart at first, later 3-12.
numbers: Six career steps; first promotion within 30 days; steps tied to 10 and 50 sales (his illustration).

### MZ-UmdfdS_1FMg#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:25
claim: Promote on gross output, not tenure, and pair each volume metric with a quality metric.
detail: Example thresholds are 100 or 200 sales: as he recounts it, a fast mover gets through in 18 months, a medium one in three, a slow one in four and a half (the units are unclear in the transcript). Pairs: sales volume with close rate above a floor so leads are not burned; for clinicians, number of sessions with five-star reviews or zero refunds. Add small perks to titles (parking, no weekends) so ranks are not read as favouritism.
numbers: 100 or 200 sales as steps; 200 sales in about 2 months for a strong rep.
