### YT-HKbFUWJwEG0#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:16
claim: Business value rises with more customers and higher lifetime gross profit per customer and falls with risk; he says ten things each unlock another level of enterprise value.
detail: Enterprise value = customers x lifetime gross profit / risk. The ten features: leadership team running day to day; marketing without the founder; delivery without the founder; several reliable acquisition channels; reliable recurring revenue; diverse customers; automated metric tracking; high cash flow that is profitable and growing with a story; audit-ready financials; $5M+ EBITDA. Missing pieces are filled in like a painting with gaps.

### YT-HKbFUWJwEG0#19 | DEFINITION | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:29:40
claim: Net negative churn means that, with no new customers, revenue still rises each month because remaining customers grow in value.
detail: A subscription that all cancel after four months only steps up and plateaus. Salesforce-style models tie price to seat count, contacts or revenue passing through, so customers' growth is theirs; if each customer may be worth a million, the allowable acquisition spend is huge. Consumer firms hope for no cancellation; B2B has few customers and needs expansion revenue.

### YT-HKbFUWJwEG0#20 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: other | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:34:17
claim: Nine Cs raise stickiness of recurring revenue: consumption, collateral, cost of switching, choice, control of money flow, cause, community, contracts, communication.
detail: Consumption: almost everyone is willing to pay for a gym they use, and almost no one for one they don't. Collateral: holding their data or goods. Switching cost: lose benefits like friends if you leave. Choice: patent or trade secret. Money flow: processing their payments. Cause: align with values. Community around content. Contracts as commitment. Communication: gym events cut churn before them but churn rises after, so always have the next one.
