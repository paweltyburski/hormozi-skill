### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: Make content when the idea and emotion are fresh, not in a calendar slot with a quota.
detail: Best material comes right after a client call or insight, so he records at once, sometimes several pieces. Blocking time and demanding five pieces creates self-inflicted pressure, and creativity does not work under constraint.

### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:00
claim: Before every piece, be certain who exactly you are talking to; topics then come from that avatar's real problems.
detail: These topics often differ from what the whole niche discusses. A fitness coach for people wanting to become a better version of themselves should skip generic subjects like carb timing.

### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:41
claim: Talk only about what you have done; if your content is sourced from other people's content, do not make it.
detail: Own anecdotes that add value make content interesting. A coach with seven clients has authority on those seven. Step one: do things; step two: talk about them.
numbers: His authority base: six companies built and exited in about ten years, across software, brick and mortar and services.

### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:15
claim: If a piece is not worth saying, do not say it; in his opinion, he would rather someone not consume it than consume it and decide never to listen to him again because it wasted their time.
detail: Content made for its own sake, to add to the subject's knowledge, hits; content made because it is easy or with a side agenda does not.
### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#08 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:20
claim: To address an audience belief, tell about a real named client who struggled with it and how it was handled, rather than lecturing.
detail: A real case from your roster gives instant domain authority.

### YT-eVY4Zo-hpqU#09 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:20
claim: Phrase opinions as "how I see it and what my clients experienced", not as declarations of fact.
detail: A declaration is easy to contradict; experience is not. It keeps intent honest and lets you later say how your view changed.
