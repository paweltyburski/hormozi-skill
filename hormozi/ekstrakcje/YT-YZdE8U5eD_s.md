### YT-YZdE8U5eD_s#14 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand, lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:34:57

### YT-YZdE8U5eD_s#15 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:41:43

### YT-YZdE8U5eD_s#18 | EXAMPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:48:29
claim: A chiropractor can build a partnership network with the businesses its customers already use, such as smoothie bars, gyms and yoga studios, for free leads.
detail: Put a free-adjustment coupon with every smoothie; map where customers work out, shop and eat (a Venn diagram); make the loop reciprocal so partners also recommend you. Doctors do a closed referral circle. Hormozi says partnerships and affiliates produced about as much revenue as direct sales in his companies.
applies_to: Local service businesses
