### MZ-jLnZH4RDXsA#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:23
claim: Any skill is trainable once broken into small observable behaviours; "charisma" or "attention to detail" are bundles of them.
detail: "Be charismatic" is useless, but "smile, say their name, nod, raise your tone at sentence start" can be practised.

### MZ-jLnZH4RDXsA#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Record yourself doing the task, write the steps or decision tree, watch the recording for missed steps, then watch the trainee use the checklist.
detail: If the trainee errs while following it, the checklist is wrong; fix the checklist and hand it back.

### MZ-jLnZH4RDXsA#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:18
claim: Train adherence to the process first; if people follow the script and results are weak, fix the script.
detail: If people win off-script, the script is wrong or should be updated. Following the process absolves the trainee; a win off-process is still wrong until the process changes.
