### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#01 | DEFINITION | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:10
claim: Respect means letting someone's word change what you do even when they cannot make you.
detail: This separates respect from fear or compliance: behaviour persists when the person loses any power to punish. He built the framework for the company's leaders.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:10
claim: POWERS lists six behaviours that earn respect: Pay the cost, Outcomes, Word, Enforce, Restraint, Steady.
detail: Pay the cost: sacrifice visibly for the group. Outcomes: things repeatedly improve through your involvement (competence, traceable to you). Word: what you say happens. Enforce: do not let people cross you repeatedly. Restraint: hold back from punishing more than needed and give more credit than needed. Steady: act the same in high-stakes moments.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:08
claim: In his fraternity pledge days, volunteering to go with a pledge sure to be hazed earned respect from the whole group.
detail: One tiny sacrifice that spares everyone else counts, and it earns more when seen only eventually rather than advertised.
numbers: Group of about 18 other pledges.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:56
claim: Enforcement without competence makes a tyrant, and competence without enforcement makes an admired doormat.
detail: Enforcement is the compliance floor; the other behaviours turn compliance into respect.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:50
claim: A transgression exists only when the standard was known, the person could follow it, and they chose not to.
detail: Unspoken standards are premeditated disrespect, and punishing someone who never knew the rule makes you look tyrannical. State preferences explicitly.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:18
claim: Consequences for crossing a standard must be consistent, immediate and escalating, aimed at the behaviour and not the person.
detail: Irregular punishment teaches people to gamble on being let off. Escalate: first a warning to return and do better, then sent home, then not back. When the pattern repeats, it is about the person, and you owe the standard to everyone, so release even a perceived high performer.

### MZ-J---CYzg7a4#07 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:21
claim: A demanding head chef who is nonetheless loved dumps a bad plate, comps the table, tells the new sous chef he is still with the team, and demands better at 6 a.m.
detail: The correction targets the mistake, not the person; labeling someone lazy is the opposite.
