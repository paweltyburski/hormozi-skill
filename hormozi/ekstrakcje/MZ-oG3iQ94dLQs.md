claim: A dental practice owner who tripled revenue felt guilty about time with young kids and wondered whether to grow further.

### MZ-oG3iQ94dLQs#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:55
claim: Hire the associate if the time bought back can earn more than the hire costs.
detail: Associate costs a third of income but frees chair time; ask whether freed time can recoup it, then whether the family time is enough. Owner profit about $1M on $3M.
numbers: Associate $300,000 a year; buys about 30 hours a week; income falls to about $700,000 before recouping.

### MZ-oG3iQ94dLQs#06 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand, verticals | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:04
claim: The owner of a trademark on a popular shade tree, collecting royalties with no marketing, wanted to lift demand and income.
detail: Royalty is paid by the final customer to the grower, per baby tree sold. He took it over from his mother and wants to free time for a charitable foundation.
numbers: Royalty $325,000 a year; $1 per tree; peak 750,000 to 800,000 units; sales may cap at 500,000 to 600,000 units; royalty unchanged since 2001; one rival tree charges $2.80.

### MZ-oG3iQ94dLQs#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters, paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:14
claim: Reinvest royalty cash into marketing with two prongs: seed influencers for organic reach and push growers and distributors at B2B events.
detail: Think in neighbourhoods and centres of influence, not trees; the media shifted from print to social and influencers. Competitors push distributors while this brand does not.
### MZ-oG3iQ94dLQs#08 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:11
claim: Raise the royalty by a quarter as a price test and revert if sales fall.
detail: The final price per tree is about $2.80 plus the royalty, bought in lots of thousands, so a small change matters at scale.
numbers: Test from $1.00 to $1.25 per tree; buyers order about 10,000 at a time.

### MZ-oG3iQ94dLQs#09 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:26
claim: If a growth bet is very hard, collect the royalty and spend effort on businesses with more upside.
detail: He called this the hardest case that day, since national awareness of a tree is tough.
