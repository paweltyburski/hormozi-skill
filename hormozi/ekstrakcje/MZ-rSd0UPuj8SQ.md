### MZ-rSd0UPuj8SQ#04 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:43
claim: Batch content: two days planning ideas for the week, one day recording, then edit, instead of making and posting one by one.
detail: Working end to end per piece is the least efficient. Separate idea mode (hooks, trends, formats), filming and editing; likely triples output.
numbers: Plan 2 days, record 1 day per week; about 3x output.

### MZ-rSd0UPuj8SQ#05 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: content-brand | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Use a diagnostic-style lead magnet as one hero piece so every video can hook into it.
detail: A guide that helps people find where they are gives endless front-end hooks without a new magnet per video. Chunk in, chunk out; link to the shop.

claim: Systematize shorts for three to six months before adding long form or live, laying one brick at a time.
