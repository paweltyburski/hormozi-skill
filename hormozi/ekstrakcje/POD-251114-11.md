### POD-251114-11#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:50
claim: A service with one account rep per ten clients has 80% gross margin.
detail: Ten clients at the monthly fee give revenue per rep; subtract the rep's cost, with no other delivery cost assumed, to get gross profit. Per-customer gross profit is the fee times the margin.
numbers: 10 x $3,000 = $30,000; minus $6,000 rep = $24,000 (80%); per client $2,400.

### POD-251114-11#02 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: Estimate lifetime transactions by exporting customers, sorting by purchase count and averaging.
detail: CRM figures are often wrong, so use this napkin check; for recurring revenue use churn instead. The number rises as the business ages and customers keep buying.
numbers: Example average of four transactions.

### POD-251114-11#03 | DEFINITION | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: Revenue Churn | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:23
claim: Churn counts only how many of the original customers left, so new signups do not change it.
detail: Take customers at the start of the period minus those still present, divided by the start number.
numbers: 100 start, 95 remain: 5 / 100 = 5% churn.

### POD-251114-11#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Product LTGP is gross profit times transactions; service LTGP is gross profit divided by churn.
detail: The widget example sells at $100 with $20 delivery, so $80 gross profit; times four orders is $320 (the audio says $360, an arithmetic slip, unclear in audio).
numbers: Widget $80 x 4 = $320. Service $2,400 / 5% churn = $48,000.

### POD-251114-11#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: strategy-positioning | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: CAC can only fall to zero while LTGP has no ceiling, and making ads efficient is easier than making people stay longer.
detail: Whoever makes customers most valuable can outbid rivals; CAC is about getting customers, LTGP about keeping them.
