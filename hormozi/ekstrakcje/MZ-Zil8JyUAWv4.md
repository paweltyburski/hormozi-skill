### MZ-Zil8JyUAWv4#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: A fund like his prefers businesses with roughly $2M-$3M or more in profit as a comfortable starting point, because smaller profit leaves not a lot of cushion for hiring or volatility.
detail: At about $80K a month of profit, hiring two good people can cut profit in half, which stresses founders who live off the business. The $1M profit figure is a minimum, not a target.
numbers: Minimum $1M profit; preferred $2M-$3M+; example $80K per month profit.
### MZ-Zil8JyUAWv4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:01
claim: Owning a business and running it are separate roles; first-time founders tie their self-worth to profit swings.
detail: The runner need not be the owner. Tying worth to controllable effort rather than volatile revenue makes bad months easier.
