### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:45
claim: Pull appointments forward: move a later-booked call into an open slot today, and push unconfirmed same-day calls to tomorrow.
detail: Same-day appointments show up more and the prospect is more interested. Filling gaps with responsive leads gives back-to-back calls with the hottest people. Unconfirmed prospects due in a few hours get a short message and a move to the next day.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#04 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:34
claim: A solar rep sold about four times the units of the second-best by calling every new booking immediately.
detail: The call qualified the lead, then split in two: if the prospect had time now, he went straight into the sales call; if not, the reason for the call was to fill an opening later that day. Both raised time on call, and freed a later slot for another buyer. Adopted company-wide, then across the portfolio.
numbers: ~4x the units of the next best rep.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#06 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:10
claim: Give reps two checklists: an on-the-call SOP for what to say and an off-the-call SOP for what to do between calls.
detail: Most companies only say "work the leads", losing output. The off-call list covers following up, dragging prospects forward and cancelling dead appointments; the rep switches hats the moment a call ends.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#07 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: - | fw: BAMFAM | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:40

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#09 | SCRIPT | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:14
claim: Ask "who do you know?" rather than "do you know anyone?", wrap it in a compliment, and push back lightly on "no one".
detail: Steps: 1. Compliment ("who do you know as awesome as you who would benefit?"). 2. Ask for a name, not yes/no. 3. If "no one", joke that they must dislike their friends, then ask whether the doubt is about you.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#21 | DEFINITION | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:26:21
claim: An obstacle arises before the price is mentioned; the same issue after the price is an objection; "kill zombies" means resolving obstacles up front.
detail: Time, money, fit, other decision makers, self-doubt are always the same. Raising them early is easier than after money is on the table. Once price is named the clock on the bomb is ticking. Outside blame (time, money) comes first, then other people, then self; "I need to think" means you are already deep.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#22 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:29:31
claim: Break a belief with a past story and a future play-out, then isolate permission versus support.
detail: Decision maker: tell of a similar woman who left and later resented her husband; reframe as needing support, not permission. Ask "what if they say no?" One in three would still buy; the other two get "you want permission, not support". Money: "resourcefulness, not resources". Pre-sale consumer deals under $1,000-2,000 usually must close on the spot; multi-call B2B may need stakeholders.
numbers: ~1 in 3 buy anyway; consumer deal threshold ~$1,000-2,000 for same-call decision.

### YT-cy2k1GdA-9o#25 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:35:18
claim: Best case, worst case: ask what they are afraid of, play it out, and compare the worst case with the upside.
