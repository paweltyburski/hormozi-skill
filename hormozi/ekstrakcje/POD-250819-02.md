### POD-250819-02#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: The car-rental counter ran five offers in order, each solving a problem the customer had not raised.
detail: Roomier vehicle (the customer was a big man), late return, premium insurance, a minimum-insurance downsell once premium was declined, and prepaid fuel at $3.75 a gallon when the pump price was about $3.50. Each offer is tied to a worry: space, flight timing, damage, rushed refuelling. Result: a $19-a-day rental became about $100 a day, a 5x difference.
numbers: $19/day to ~$100/day; prepaid fuel $3.75 versus ~$3.50 a gallon at the pump.

### POD-250819-02#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:12
claim: Frame each add-on as solving a larger future cost or hassle for a smaller fee paid now.
detail: The customer pays more today, faster, but is spared bigger fees and problems later, which is why they gladly accept.

### POD-250819-02#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:12
claim: A business that loses money on every new customer follows a predictable spiral to collapse.
detail: Spend on acquisition, discover at month end that spend exceeded profit, cut advertising, then get fewer customers than can be handled, stop advertising, prop the business with personal cash, loans or credit, sell equity to survive, wait months or years for payback and finally fall behind until the business is lost.

### POD-250819-02#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: Making each customer twice as valuable grows the business about eight times faster; tripling gives about 27 times.
detail: Double customer value and you can afford twice as many customers, and the faster payment lets you acquire them twice as quickly, so growth compounds as 2 x 2 x 2. At triple value the same logic gives 3 x 3 x 3.
numbers: 2x value = 8x growth; 3x value = 27x growth.
