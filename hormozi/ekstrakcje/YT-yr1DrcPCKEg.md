### YT-yr1DrcPCKEg#01 | DEFINITION | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:57
claim: A margin loan lets you borrow against a stock portfolio at very low interest with no closing costs, and the borrowing is not taxable income.
detail: Established banks typically lend a fraction of portfolio value as a revolving line; you pay interest only while the money is out. Wealthy holders borrow against appreciating stock instead of selling it, so no tax is triggered while the stock gains more than the loan costs. Broker platforms offer it by upgrading the account.
numbers: Lending ratio about 50-65% at larger banks, 30-60% generally (some as low as 35%); interest 1-2% at the time of recording (2022), variable.

### YT-yr1DrcPCKEg#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:24
claim: Margin-call arithmetic: a 60% loan on $100,000 with the portfolio falling to $50,000 forces repayment of half the loan.
detail: Allowed borrowing becomes 60% of the new value, i.e. $30,000, so $30,000 of the $60,000 must be repaid. If you lack the cash, they sell your holdings, and the moment they force a sale is when the holdings are worth the least.
numbers: $100,000 portfolio, $60,000 borrowed, portfolio at $50,000, so $30,000 repayment.
### YT-yr1DrcPCKEg#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:20
claim: Use margin only for short, secured transactions where the return is far above the rate, and borrow only 10-20% of the limit.
detail: He used it for 6-12 month hard-money loans paying 12-20% against secured collateral, repaying quickly. His advice is to stay far below the cap so a margin call cannot happen. Personally he held no debt at the time, reasoning that debt is a form of risk that over a long horizon can expose you to zero.
numbers: Hard-money return 12%, 15% or 20% versus 1-2% borrowing cost; recommended draw 10-20% of capacity.
