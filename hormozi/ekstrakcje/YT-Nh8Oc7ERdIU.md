### YT-Nh8Oc7ERdIU#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:06
claim: To unchain an entrepreneur, figure out the reasons they give for why they can't do something (known limits), then look for the unknown unknowns, which he calls the dangerous ones and, in his opinion, the most powerful to overcome.
detail: Stated blockers ("I'm not number one", "the app doesn't load well enough") are known limits because the person can explain them. The invisible ones are beliefs never questioned because they never occurred to the person, such as not knowing another opportunity vehicle existed.

### YT-Nh8Oc7ERdIU#08 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:13:09
claim: Four types of leverage, the four Cs, are collaboration (people), capital (other people's money), code (software built once) and content (made once, seen by many).
detail: Adapted from Naval Ravikant. Leverage is what you get out relative to what you put in. Examples: managing a salesperson two hours instead of selling 40; recruiting a top recruiter in two weeks who then feeds 100 salespeople over time; a coder or podcaster reaching millions with one piece of work. Skill itself is leverage: in his illustrative example (numbers chosen for the sake of math), 100 cold calls give about 10 appointments for a skilled caller against about 1 for a novice.
numbers: Hypothetical cold call example: 10 versus 1 appointment per 100 calls.

### YT-Nh8Oc7ERdIU#25 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:50:05
claim: Business is an infinite game, and entrepreneurs lose by playing it with a finite frame (end goal, fixed rules, known players).
detail: Adapted from Simon Sinek. Quarters and goals are finite games inside it; health and marriage similarly have no finish. The day you start playing is the day you win, so find games you do not want to quit; he found he liked business more than fitness when he opened his gym.

### YT-Nh8Oc7ERdIU#26 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:52:57

detail: Marketing, sales, product, support, finance and legal exist in every business. A micro business lets you try all roles, then backfill the rest by documenting, demonstrating and having others duplicate.
