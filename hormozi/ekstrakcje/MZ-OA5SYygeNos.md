### MZ-OA5SYygeNos#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Go new only when (1) the marketplace is maximised, usually local, (2) the platform is shrinking, or (3) risk-adjusted returns on new beat more or better, which is rare.
detail: Maximised means large market share or your spend covers the market many times over. A platform losing about 25% per period, like newspapers, compounds against you, so getting a head start on another platform is probably a good idea. Doubling spend on your main machine carries less risk.
numbers: example shrinkage 25% per period.
### MZ-OA5SYygeNos#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: operator-mindset | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: After doing more, ask whether you can do more again; the answer is often yes, and these hard but solvable problems beat shiny new ones.
detail: Keep asking how to do even more before leaving the main channel.
