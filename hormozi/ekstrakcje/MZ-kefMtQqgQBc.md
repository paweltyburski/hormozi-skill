### MZ-kefMtQqgQBc#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A top financial-services producer (number two of about 3,000) is selling his book, faces a non-compete forcing a 60-mile relocation, and holds two side businesses; Hormozi says rebuild the main business.
detail: His network is local multifamily; the first build took six years but he estimates a third of that now, about 24 months. Side businesses are a real-estate portfolio and a junk-removal company feeding leads to it. Hormozi: if you believe you can win, do the main thing and drop the rest.
numbers: ~1,000,000 expected proceeds (USD, unclear in audio); 6 years first build versus about 2 years rebuild.
applies_to: Owners with several half-run businesses.

### MZ-kefMtQqgQBc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Do not make a side hustle your main game: business returns exceed real-estate returns, and a low-risk skill-based business holds the upside.
detail: He states real estate makes more millionaires and private equity makes more billionaires; own the property as a safety net, not as the main game.

### MZ-kefMtQqgQBc#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: Ask "if a gun were at my head and I had to make money, what would I do?" and do that.
detail: On some level, side businesses are almost like a hedge against risk, but he says they add risk because they lower the odds that the one sure thing stays sure. His own answer would be launching a gym; his wife Layla suggested repeating what they are best at and skipping the rest.
