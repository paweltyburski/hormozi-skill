### YT-neTSqOAMgao#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: lead-getters | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:37
claim: There are six acquisition channels: paid, owned and earned media, plus manual outbound, affiliates, and referrals or word of mouth.
detail: The first three are media; the last three are people-based. Five behave linearly (a fixed ratio back), and with most of them the return ratio diminishes as scale rises. Word of mouth is quadratic, the hardest to earn but with disproportionate payoff. Between a good and a great product, the gap in this return is large.
### YT-neTSqOAMgao#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:02
claim: Build the superior product once for compounding returns instead of repeating campaigns forever.
detail: Wealthy people he knows invest once in something that pays continually; others must keep launching campaigns, outreach and calls, and the business stops when they stop. A superior product also needs no acquisition cost and lets the owner endure unhappy high-ticket clients.

### YT-neTSqOAMgao#09 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:15

### YT-neTSqOAMgao#19 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: objections-closing | fw: Guarantees | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:34:34

detail: Such an offer makes acquiring customers easy and moves the bottleneck to operations, where you can fix errors.
