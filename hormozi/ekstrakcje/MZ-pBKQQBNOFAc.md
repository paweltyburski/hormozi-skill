### MZ-pBKQQBNOFAc#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Puddles-Ponds-Lakes-Oceans | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A tiny niche audience can support a very profitable business even with weak engagement numbers.
detail: A nurse taught registered dietitians how to bill insurance. Her posts got only about 28 likes each, yet the audience was exactly her buyers and she ran a seven-figure business with very high margin.
numbers: About 28 likes per post; over $1M per year; margins in the 90s percent.

### MZ-pBKQQBNOFAc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Interest-based feeds and newer ad systems identify your customers on their own, so content that drifts from them is shown less.
detail: He believes the platform already knows who your customers are, and ads got more specific after Meta's Andromeda update; the more off-topic your content, the less likely your buyers see it. His stated guess is that the content engine is at least as good.

### MZ-pBKQQBNOFAc#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Decide on day one whether you are building a personal brand to keep forever or media that feeds one specific business.
detail: A personal brand should be a faithful reflection of you, so nobody is surprised meeting you (his mix: fitness, dark philosophy, business, humour), and it may or may not feed the business. Business-only media pigeonholes the content but surely drives more revenue.

### MZ-pBKQQBNOFAc#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:33
claim: His best-selling video was a narrow how-to with about half his usual views.
detail: The video that drove the most sales on his channel was a very specific one about how to train a business, not a broad hit.
numbers: About 180,000 views, roughly half his channel average.
