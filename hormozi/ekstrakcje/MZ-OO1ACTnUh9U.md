### MZ-OO1ACTnUh9U#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A community owner's ads stopped working after a price rise; reverting did not help, so price likely was not the cause.
detail: Facebook starts with people who know you; a small pool is used up quickly and the next ring converts worse. Ads working then stopping then working is common.

### MZ-OO1ACTnUh9U#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:16
claim: With a free community, a good normal benchmark is 20% or more of visitors joining, and if you manually work the leads you can get about 10% of them to buy the next thing.
detail: Cost per new member is about five times cost per click. In his observation, the people consistently outperforming have both a free and a paid community.
numbers: 20% or more opt-in (1 in 5); cost per member = CPC x 5; can get ~10% free-to-paid as a baseline when manually working leads.
### MZ-OO1ACTnUh9U#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:02
claim: Use four mechanisms to convert free members: an onboarding call, a five-video course, a weekly call with a soft CTA, and DMs.
detail: Onboarding is functionally a sales call. The weekly call adds value and mentions the advanced paid community. DM only after authentic dialogue.

### MZ-OO1ACTnUh9U#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: content-brand | fw: Epiphany Bridge | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:02
claim: The free course is five videos structured like a VSL: origin story, three case stories, then the call to action.
detail: The three stories cover obstacles from self, other people and circumstances; the fifth explains the paid community.

### MZ-OO1ACTnUh9U#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:41
claim: The model is viable if cost per free member multiplied by ten is covered by the paid price's first payment.
detail: After that, each recurring payment is profit.
numbers: x10 (10% conversion).
