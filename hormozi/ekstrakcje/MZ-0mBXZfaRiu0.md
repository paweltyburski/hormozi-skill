### MZ-0mBXZfaRiu0#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A soccer tournament operator is limited by sales capacity because the president is also the only salesperson.
detail: Business: 5v5 tournaments nationwide, revenue from team registrations, needing a partner club first for fields and marketing. Leads exceed what one person can sell at five calls a day. Advice: hire salespeople via a recruiting channel with career path, compensation, onboarding and management.
numbers: Revenue $1.5M, goal $15M in 4 years; about 50% yearly growth for five years; profit $500,000 (30%); five sales calls a day.

### MZ-0mBXZfaRiu0#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:16
claim: When metrics, market, model, money and leads exist, the bottleneck is conversion capacity, i.e. manpower.
