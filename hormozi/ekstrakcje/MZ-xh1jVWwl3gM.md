claim: Pick one of three avatars: enterprise brand clients, personal brands, or professionals seeking career growth, and match the business model.
detail: Enterprise branding is an enterprise sale, so a community is probably not the solution. Personal brand and career development both fit a community. Prove expertise through your own brand or a company you owned.
