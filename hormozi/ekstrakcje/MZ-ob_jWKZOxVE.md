### MZ-ob_jWKZOxVE#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:14
claim: Success lives in the boring middle, and motivation vanishes, so design conditions where the right action is the likeliest one.
detail: Do not rely on perfect willpower; set things up so success is the default outcome. Be anti-fragile: arrange so that things can go wrong and you still win.
