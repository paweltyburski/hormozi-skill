### MZ-vuIudYOBxkU#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: When a sales script or ad underperforms, cut and shorten it; nine times out of ten that fixes it.
detail: Marketers overthink details (seconds, colours) while the answer is to state what you have and show it is for them. If you cannot state the point of the script or ad, that is the problem.
numbers: about 9 out of 10 portfolio scripts improved by removal (as stated).

### MZ-vuIudYOBxkU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: operator-mindset | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Doing 10 outreaches or five pieces of content a week and expecting growth is a mismatch of expectation versus reality.
detail: Quit too soon or too low and results do not appear.
numbers: 10 outreaches/week; 5 content pieces/week named as insufficient.
