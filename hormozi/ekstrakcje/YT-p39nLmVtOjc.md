### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: HEAR | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: A refund is only an apology; to get back to neutral you must also compensate the time and expectations lost.
detail: Customers lose the evening, travel or preparation as well as the money, so returning the payment leaves them below zero. Expect recovery to cost more than the sale, so "ROI is out the window". Aim first for neutral, then for a magic moment that turns the person into a champion, then prevent a repeat. Because it costs real money, it also pushes you to avoid mistakes.

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:48
claim: Disney reportedly says it takes 37 magic moments to offset one tragic moment.
detail: Avoid the tragic moment because compensating takes so much; once it has happened, do a lot. Hormozi also says never become a customer's project, i.e. someone who stews about how to punish you.
numbers: 37 magic moments per tragic one (cited, as attributed to Disney).

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:18
claim: A one-star review is the customer's attempt to punish a company that apologised but did not do enough, and minimising a complaint escalates it.
detail: Telling the customer they are overreacting invalidates them, so they escalate. The only person who can lower the pain is the customer, and that happens when you are visibly more upset than they are. Research on malpractice suits: patients sue doctors they dislike, not those who make the most errors.

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#04 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: management-ops | fw: HEAR | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:04
claim: After a scheduling error left seven visitors with nothing to do on arrival, Hormozi refunded travel and added dinner and personal consulting.
detail: Five had come from abroad, some after applying for visas. No excuses (such as the email they should have read). They refunded all travel expenses, took everyone to dinner for three hours and he planned about three hours of consulting for each business. He owned it as the owner because "the buck stops" with him.
numbers: 7 visitors, 5 international; about 3 hours of consulting each; 3-hour dinner.

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:39
claim: A gym equipment seller shipped a damaged machine and refused responsibility, while a hotel manager comped and upgraded after a slow dinner; the hotel won a repeat customer.
detail: The seller said delivery acceptance made the damage Hormozi's liability and charged extra for parts, so he threw the machine away and warns others. At the hotel, a three-and-a-half-hour dinner prompted the manager to comp it, upgrade rooms, send fruit and drinks to eight guests, book two restaurants and seat them in person. He now books that hotel and names the manager.
numbers: dinner lasted 3.5 hours; drinks after 45 minutes, starters after 90 minutes; 8 guests in 4-5 rooms.

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:23
claim: An employee who caused a mistake should skip the silent guilt and do two things: make it right and make sure it cannot happen again.
detail: Nobody gives credit for beating yourself up privately. Do not minimise it to the boss either; show real upset, since the boss will otherwise be the one to produce it. Whoever has the power to fix an issue owns it, even outside their division (the hotel manager).

### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: retention-ltv | also: management-ops | fw: Employee Empowerment Wheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:40
claim: Give front-line staff a box of decision-making power: a few set fixes matched to the severity of the mistake, and fix the process if they use more than five a month.
detail: Hotel example: for a 20-minute bellhop wait, offer two drinks; for smoke in a room, you probably refund the previous night and give a free upgrade for the next. Costs are baked into the profit margins. If you're using up more than five of these a month, fix the process.
numbers: more than 5 of these gestures a month signals a need to fix the process.
### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:18:09
claim: If a large share of customers ask for refunds, fix the product or the expectations you set, or stop selling it.
detail: Refunds signal that you oversell or deliver badly; a company that says it cannot afford refunds maybe shouldn't be in business.
### YT-p39nLmVtOjc#09 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:20:44
claim: A hot-sauce seller whose kit lacked caps mailed extra caps with a handwritten note, and the customer raised a one-star review to five stars.
detail: He then added a handwritten note and an extra cap to every kit. Customers who have been made right often update reviews saying the founder reached out.
