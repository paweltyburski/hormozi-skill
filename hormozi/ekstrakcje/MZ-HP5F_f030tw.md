claim: As a rough placeholder, Hormozi estimates that becoming famous costs about $50M of advertising, about 5 billion impressions, based on one film (The Hunger Games) that took its lead actress from near obscurity to fame.
detail: At $500,000 a year that would take 100 years, but the math can be scaled down to being famous in your niche or local market, where impressions are concentrated.
numbers: $50M budget = 5 billion impressions; $500K/year = 100 years.
