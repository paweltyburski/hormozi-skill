### POD-0852#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:59
claim: Expect to need several teachers, since each supplies some bricks of the bridge (say 80, then 10, then 10); navigate by direction, not by a perfect pick.
detail: Ask whether a person gets you closer to the goal, walk that way and correct as you learn. Later teachers look better only because earlier ones laid the foundation. The fallacy of the perfect pick keeps people stuck for years.

### POD-0852#08 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:00

### POD-0852#09 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:50

detail: With 20 reps at 30%, lifting all to 40% is a 33% gain, versus hiring seven more reps; compare the risk-adjusted return. More and better alternate like an accordion.
