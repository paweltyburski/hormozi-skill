### MZ-_I9_hDwH1Lc#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A buyer of his parents' paper-based promotional products shop (embroidery, screen print) wants to grow it from $1.1M to $10M in five years.
detail: EBITDA is roughly $230,000-$250,000 (units unclear in audio). After announcing it, inquiries surged and quotes pile up.
numbers: $1.1M revenue; EBITDA $230K to $250K; goal $10M in 5 years.

### MZ-_I9_hDwH1Lc#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:56
claim: To separate manpower from cash flow, ask whether prepaid orders would let you hire the people to deliver.
detail: The owner said yes, so the issue is cash flow. Prepayment is already required before ordering inventory, except net-30 schools and large firms.

### MZ-_I9_hDwH1Lc#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:52
claim: Scaling on broken systems will make the pain worse; the choice is to skip the opportunity and build systems, or take it and set the expectation that the next 18 months will probably be very hard.
detail: You'll still have to fix the plane while flying; if you do a good job, customers will keep ordering. His mental frame, borrowed from Ackman, is just make progress one bite at a time.
numbers: probably about 18 hard months.
### MZ-_I9_hDwH1Lc#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:35
claim: Organize the company by workflow throughput, not an org chart, since people can manage only five to eight reports.
detail: The org chart reflects human limits. A business is attention plus labor and materials turning into money.
numbers: human span of control 5 to 8.

### MZ-_I9_hDwH1Lc#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:51
claim: List every step from attention to money on a whiteboard, with subtasks under each, then hand pieces to AI.
detail: His example count is about 167 steps. Content alone breaks down into idea, headline, script, recording and thumbnails. At the tactical-task level you can ask AI to take over one at a time.
numbers: about 167 steps from eyeballs to money.
