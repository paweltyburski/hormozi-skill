### MZ-hpOoEsd4lYM#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Education has many monetization paths but is hard to grow; entertainment grows easily but is thin to monetize.
detail: Entertainment creators usually monetize via a commoditised consumable that most of the audience buys (drinks, snacks), where fans choose their creator's brand over equal options.

### MZ-hpOoEsd4lYM#02 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Set up a C-corp with a roughly 10% share pool and give other creators shares plus commission, with a non-compete on customers.
detail: Creators do not want to be mere affiliates; they want co-ownership, and the shares keep them from marketing competitors. Short term sell direct and via these affiliates, then use distribution.
numbers: ~10% share pool.

### MZ-hpOoEsd4lYM#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:56
claim: Decide whether you love creating or the business; if business is your art, go media mogul; if creating, stay artist.
detail: He says that to replace his creator income he could go 80-20: keep 80% of revenue with 20% of the work and use the other 80% of the work to build. A person has only about four 7-10 year cycles, so spend them on the best bet.
numbers: ~4 cycles of 7-10 years in a lifetime.
