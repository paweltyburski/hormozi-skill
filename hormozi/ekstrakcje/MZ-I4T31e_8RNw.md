claim: A content agency for real estate agents ($800K, goal $3M) is limited by Instagram restrictions on cold outbound and is moving to paid-boosted organic inbound.
detail: Its top outbound setter was moved to inbound, so outbound sales fell while inbound was not yet working. Hormozi: keep both, since content also feeds outbound.

detail: If qualified people book but do not buy, add friction instead of removing it.
