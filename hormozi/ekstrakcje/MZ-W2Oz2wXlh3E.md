### MZ-W2Oz2wXlh3E#01 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:10
claim: Resilience is how quickly you return to baseline after a bad event; toughness is how long your fuse is.
detail: The two are separate. The size of the blow does not matter: a resilient person can have a terrible day and be back the next. He keeps a toolset of reframes to make the recovery curve V-shaped.

### MZ-W2Oz2wXlh3E#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Imagine your own funeral in concrete detail to see how little the small stuff will matter.
detail: At a funeral there are caterers, comments that the food was cheap, a checklist, absences, then dinner; people move on. A small mistake in a book printing therefore does not matter.

### MZ-W2Oz2wXlh3E#03 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:31
claim: Veteran frame: feeling calm on the thousandth repeat shows calm is available now, since the circumstance is the same and only the choice differs.
detail: Ask how you would react if this inconvenience happened a thousand times in a row, then adopt that reaction on the first time.
