### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A partner should bring knowledge, money or time that you lack; if all three overlap, one of you is unnecessary.
detail: Matching partners on all three just splits equity for no added benefit. A need alone is not a reason to give equity: pay an accountant for the service instead. Equity is the most expensive thing you give up, so give it to people who will drive growth over a ten-year view.
applies_to: Founders considering an equity partner.

### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Use inverted thinking: list what would guarantee a terrible partnership and avoid each item.
detail: He applies Charlie Munger's inversion to partnerships and lists ten failure keys, which reduce to complementary resources, documented expectations, a generous start, and shared mission, values and lifestyle.
numbers: Ten failure keys.

### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:57
claim: Write down each partner's expectations and the agreement before starting; walk away from anyone who refuses written agreements.
detail: Handshake deals avoid hard conversations, and a partner you cannot have them with before money is at stake will not manage them later. The contract's purpose is to surface and agree expectations (what each does, what happens if it goes wrong), written in the hope it is never needed, not to involve lawyers.

### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:48
claim: Give the other partner more than equitable terms at the start; squeezing them creates resentment and a later renegotiation.
detail: Doing a disproportionate amount of work for little breeds resentment in you, and extracting maximum from a partner who does the work makes them try to renegotiate once reality sets in. Think ahead and give more, which builds a relationship instead of a negotiated contract.

### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:10
claim: Partners need aligned mission (where to go), values (how to get there) and lifestyle or interests.
detail: Many partner pairs he speaks to disagree about direction; settle it or one should leave. Different values cause endless conflict even with the same goal. A partner's messy personal life or money habits (saver versus heavy borrower) will spill into the business, and an unreliable partner makes an unreliable business. You should be proud to introduce them.

### YT-JShQ8BX08rs#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:14
claim: Test fit by asking: if both of us saw the same data, would we make the same decision?
detail: A yes predicts far less long-run conflict; the target is complementary skills with aligned mission, values and interests.
