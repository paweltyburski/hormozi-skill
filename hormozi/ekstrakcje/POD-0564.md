### POD-0564#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:47
claim: The line between helping and manipulating is intention, and if the prospect knew all you know and still would not buy, you are deceiving them.
detail: Reps who learn more about a weak product slow down just when they must lean in, because they stop believing. Sales is education that helps prospects make decisions in their own interest.

### POD-0564#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: Lean on logical closing: emotions fade within days, so the logic must still make sense two weeks later.
detail: Present both scenarios and let the numbers carry the decision. Buyers sit on an emotional to logical continuum; higher-level business buyers tend more logical, and exposure to marketing shifts you toward logic. Do not rely on your own story: a trainer's story only closed people like them.

### POD-0564#03 | DEFINITION | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:53
claim: Selling is everything before the offer is presented; closing starts when the offer is presented.
detail: Obstacles are handled before the offer, while you are on offence and can attack limiting beliefs. Objections arrive after it. Beliefs: people want to believe and need help justifying; expect a no, because those who knew they needed it would not need you.

### POD-0564#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:22:27
claim: Treat the stated reason for the call as an obstacle and dig for the real problem before the offer.
detail: A prospect who says "just finding out about the programme" is usually there because they are overweight and unhappy. Address that underlying problem or it blows up at the close.

### POD-0564#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:25
claim: If the price did not draw a gasp, you did not quote high enough.
detail: This prepares reps to aim at what they used to fear; when the prospect says it is a lot, the reply is "I know".
### POD-0564#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:59
claim: Selling is transferring belief over a bridge of trust, and both are continuums, so ask how much, not whether.
detail: New reps often start with belief and trust, then learn about the company, lose the belief and cannot transfer it. If you want to fix this, fix the product, onboarding or belief first, then sell. People sense intention ("commission breath"). Keep the human first before each meeting.

### POD-0564#07 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:54
claim: At a mortgage-leads seller, a struggling rep was asked how good the leads were and his answer showed he did not believe, so Hormozi tore up the script and the team then closed.
detail: A believer would be recruiting relatives into using the leads. Belief matters more than the script; the script is only sets and reps.

### POD-0564#11 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:40
claim: Onion of blame: obstacles come in layers (circumstances, then other people, then self), so a surface answer may leave two or three layers to go.
detail: Ellis's three causes of upset map to five obstacles: time, value (money), fit, authority (spouse or partner), and avoidance (self, fear of a mistake). Money and time are often the shallow layer; the real talk starts at fear of failing. Prospects must also believe the product reaches the goal their way, that you will support them, and that it will work for them.

### POD-0564#12 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Five concern types | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:29:43
claim: Overcome "now is not a good time" by pointing out that busy is when they most need support.
detail: Steps: accept the premise, note they will be busy for the rest of their life, and ask if they want results then. If they only wait until they are not busy, they will never begin; learning when it is hard makes the rest easy.

### POD-0564#16 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: What Would It Take | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:38:09

### POD-0564#19 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:42:14
