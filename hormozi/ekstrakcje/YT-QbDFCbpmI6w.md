### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: He read a few books many times and executed everything in each before starting a new one.
detail: After six to twelve months of reading self-help with no change in his life, and noticing that many books contradicted each other, he made it a rule not to read a new book until he had applied the previous one. He names one book on dealing with people as his first repeated read.
numbers: Read about 30 books before reaching ten-million-dollar scale (his claim).

### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:59
claim: He learned selling by doing it, with no sales training or sales book until he was already managing teams doing a few hundred thousand dollars a month.
detail: His first purchased sales product added useful ideas but did not change how good he was. He considers study without practice productive procrastination.
numbers: First sales product bought at a few hundred thousand dollars a month in sales.

### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:38
claim: Selling rests mostly on conviction in the product and care for the prospect; reps who sell what they do not believe in burn out.
detail: With real care you will not push someone into a bad decision. Many reps feel a conflict between being a good person and needing income, then start strong and crash once they learn the product is oversold, which raises sales-force churn.

### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:02
claim: To learn a skill, buy a course on that specific task, follow it while doing the task, then read only to solve a current bottleneck.
detail: Example: to build a webinar he bought a webinar course and made the webinar as he went. Reading six sales books with different methods does not tell you which one works until tested. He paid one-to-one only for a lesson on how to run ads.

### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#05 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: Track Lose Print | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:05
claim: Set a monthly learning budget as money you accept to lose on trial and error, to remove emotional attachment to early results.
detail: Advice from Ezra Firestone. The percentage may be 5%, 10% or 25% of monthly income depending on stability; for example $500 a month. Treat it as an investment in education, not a failed purchase.
numbers: 5%-25% of monthly income; example $500 a month.

### YT-QbDFCbpmI6w#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:13
claim: His biggest gains came from paying people above him to break his beliefs about what was possible, not to hold his hand on skills.
detail: Those above you rarely teach copywriting step by step; skills come from doing, and you will be bad at first. Even highly skilled people fail, so waiting to be perfect is pointless.
