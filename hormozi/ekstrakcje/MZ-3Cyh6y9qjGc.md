### MZ-3Cyh6y9qjGc#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: strategy-positioning, verticals | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:14
claim: A 12-year-old payroll firm with 4 staff and over 300 clients, fed by CPA referrals and no advertising, considers niching into cannabis.
detail: It competes with large payroll providers and holds government contracts that clash with a cannabis site. Hormozi guesses average spend near $500 a month and good margins.
numbers: 300+ clients incl. three large; about 3-4 new clients a month; ~30 CPA referrers; ~$500/month average (his guess).

### MZ-3Cyh6y9qjGc#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: paid-ads | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:11
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: with 300 customers and no advertising, he feels they should advertise more, since niching mainly solves an efficiency problem.
detail: Niching raises lifetime gross profit per customer and, with well-targeted ads, lowers CAC; it would make sense if ads do not convert or delivery differs enough to productise.
### MZ-3Cyh6y9qjGc#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:58
claim: A compounding business has either customers who never stop buying or partners who never stop selling for you.
detail: This firm has both, so the task is volume through outbound to CPA firms using existing clients as references.

### MZ-3Cyh6y9qjGc#04 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: offers | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:50
claim: Replace "20% for referrals" with an irresistible offer: include your service in theirs at no extra cost.
detail: Everyone offers 20% and gets no referrals. Bundle your service, or a part of it, inside the partner's service and then advertise that to the partners.

### MZ-3Cyh6y9qjGc#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:42
claim: Activate existing referral partners by building yourself into their customer journey.
detail: If partners must remember you, roughly one in ten does each month. Make every customer they sell come with something from you, so you can upsell all of them. Thirty partners giving four referrals is far below potential.
numbers: 30 referrers to ~4 clients; goal 300 referrers; growth 5, 10, 15 per month.

detail: Partners bundling the freebie send hundreds of appointments.
