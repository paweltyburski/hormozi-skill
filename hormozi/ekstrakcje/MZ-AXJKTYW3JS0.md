### MZ-AXJKTYW3JS0#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Vietnamese coffee brand with one flagship café, two hybrid franchisees and a planned subscription wants to scale and hesitates between self-owned and franchised growth.
detail: The hybrid model plugs the coffee brand into an existing food-service business, with the franchisor taking a share of the host's revenue. Hormozi says the hybrid offer is already good and that the owner is distracted by the direct-to-consumer side idea.
numbers: Flagship $2.1M revenue, about 22% margin; each hybrid franchise about $700K revenue, brand takes 7-8%, about $60-70K net per franchise; flagship costs $300-400K to open and takes about two years to reach $2M.

### MZ-AXJKTYW3JS0#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:18
claim: A hybrid franchisee pays roughly $100K all in and earns roughly $200K a year, which Hormozi calls a pretty good deal.
detail: The franchise fee is $40-50K, the build-out under $50K because the host already trades, and the host nets about 20% after royalty (Hormozi's estimate).
numbers: Cost to open about $100K; franchisee profit about $200K per year; host revenue about $700K.
### MZ-AXJKTYW3JS0#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:57
claim: Work backwards from the exit price to compare owned stores with royalty franchises.
detail: A $100M exit at an 8x multiple needs about $12M profit, which at $400K per store means about 30 stores, or 40 if some underperform; at $400K each to open that is about $16M of capital. Franchise royalty income of $60K at roughly 40% margin and a 12x multiple is worth about $288K per unit, so about 330 units. The caller, not Hormozi, said 40 owned stores sounds easier than 330 partner stores; Hormozi then recommended that route if it sounds easier to the caller.
numbers: 8x multiple; about $12M profit target; 40 stores; about $16M capital; roughly 333 franchises at 12x and 40% margin.
### MZ-AXJKTYW3JS0#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:39
claim: Commit to one growth model (franchised or corporate-owned), and write the reasons in a letter to yourself.
detail: He says mixed models usually mean the franchisor could not decide. After buying out franchisees (18 or 27, he does not recall) he made the brand corporate-owned. He writes a letter to himself with his exact reasoning so he does not have to retrace the path on bad days.
### MZ-AXJKTYW3JS0#05 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:34
claim: Fund new locations from the flagship's profit, ideally through separate location entities, and hire managers before opening.
detail: He suggests a corporate structure for tax reasons (check with an accountant) and fronting capital as a franchisee would, earning return on invested capital.
