### POD-0210#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:35
claim: When a tactic is not producing the result, audit whether it is being executed as designed before changing it.
detail: He says most tactics work and the strategy is usually not wrong; failures usually come from execution (wrong tonality, untimely delivery, missing questions, irregular outreach cadence). He estimates the large majority of errors in his business are execution errors.
numbers: He puts execution at 90-99% of the errors in his business.
### POD-0210#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:49
claim: Past the stage of marketing and selling yourself, scaling is the ability to get other people to repeat the result.
detail: The thing that takes you from zero to one is different from what takes you to ten: ten people doing the same thing consistently. After hiring more people, results can drop and staff blame the market; the usual fix is to go back to the process, not to change strategy.

### POD-0210#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:56
claim: Keep quality assurance through a fixed communication cadence with direct reports and regular drills.
detail: If you stop talking about something, staff conclude it no longer matters. Reps' reports are not necessarily lies, just uninformed, so owners need to know the skill themselves to spot errors. Build a training programme for sales with timed blocks of the day and a daily report back of what was done.

### POD-0210#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:21
claim: Verify execution by listening to call recordings and onboardings and watching facility footage against the checklist.
detail: Check energy of workouts, daily announcements and checklist adherence. Often no script change is needed: re-drilling the team and re-reading testimonials to re-convince them restored sales overnight.

### POD-0210#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:14
claim: A manager's job is motivation and training (from Andy Grove), with communication added first, since people may think they know the plan but do not.
detail: If an outcome is missing and the person knows what is wanted, either they do not know how or they are not motivated; both are fixed by training or motivation, which is repetitive work.
