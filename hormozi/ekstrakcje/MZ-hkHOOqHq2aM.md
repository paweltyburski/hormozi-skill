### MZ-hkHOOqHq2aM#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Serbian small-business mastermind ($2.4M revenue, $1.2M profit, aiming at $5M profit) has nothing to sell after the year-long programme and a 20%-close conference funnel.
detail: Guarantee: 30% net profit increase or money back. Four groups by prior profit band (0-5K, 5-50K, 50-150K, 150-500K). Funnel: content, webinar, conference every six months; about 500 people in the room, 20% close, roughly $1M per event. Options: price rise, second product, profit share.
numbers: Revenue $2.4M; profit $1.2M; 200 clients at $1,000/month; 500 in room, 20% close, ~$1M per two-hour pitch.

### MZ-hkHOOqHq2aM#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:50
claim: Billing monthly for a one-time deliverable is just a payment plan called a membership, and churn will look high when payments end.
detail: Separate consumables (calls, community, in-person meetups) from what they learn once. A car loan is not a five-year LTV.

### MZ-hkHOOqHq2aM#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:46
claim: Offer an alumni network with two or three meetups a year, optional Q&A, at about one third of the programme price.
detail: Owners like being around peers and it is less lonely. It is light touch with high gross margin and brings referrals.
numbers: ~1/3 of the normal price; 2-3 meetups/year.

### MZ-hkHOOqHq2aM#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:38
claim: At a 20% close rate, he would probably increase event frequency (one a quarter) rather than raise price; raising price is fine if you want to serve fewer people.
detail: Twenty percent is roughly at KPI, so a price rise would probably just break even. If there is a supply issue (satisfaction may fall at 500 clients), he says you can raise price.
numbers: 20% close from room; events twice a year to quarterly.
### MZ-hkHOOqHq2aM#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:16
claim: Prefer high gross margin and revenue retention to building a pricier back-end you cannot deliver.
detail: A $10,000 product costing $100 sold six or seven more times beats building a $100,000 product costing $30,000. He would "downsell the upsell". Charging 4x needs a robust team or people will hate you.
numbers: $10K product at $100 cost; $100K at $30K cost ($70K margin); extra ~$60K gross margin from 6-7 more $10K sales.
