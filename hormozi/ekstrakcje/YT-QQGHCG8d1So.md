### YT-QQGHCG8d1So#07 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: offers, sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:14:54
claim: Solve problems by inversion: list ways to destroy the outcome, then reverse each into a priority.
detail: From Munger. Examples: how would I wreck my marriage or business; what would guarantee nobody buys this offer; how would I make sure this person never buys. Brains list threats more easily than solutions, and the reversed list becomes the strategic anchors. He needed deliberate practice before using it quickly.
