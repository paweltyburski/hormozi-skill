### MZ-FFZ1E_RxAW0#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:41
claim: Great products come from hundreds of small improvements, not one silver bullet.
detail: His examples are the details of a canned product: shipping, weight, colour scheme, shelf appearance, how the name looks on hats and shirts. A missing hyphen in copy printed on a million cans still bothers him. He now counts refusing to stop being picky as a main reason for his success.

### MZ-FFZ1E_RxAW0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00
claim: Make the product for yourself, since a minority of people share your problems and fears; products made for an imagined audience become recycled and solve nobody's problem.
detail: On large projects many people contribute, but one vision must hold, otherwise the result looks like a patchwork of copied past work.

### MZ-FFZ1E_RxAW0#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:01
claim: He prepared his book-launch presentation with about 100 full run-throughs, three a day for 30 days.
detail: Method: first out loud and recorded, then watch the recording and edit in real time, then repeat. The live audience was around half a million. His point: the first five reps give visible gains, the next 95 take it from great to a masterpiece, and that is where most people stop.
numbers: About 100 run-throughs, 3 per day, 30 days; roughly 500,000 live viewers.
