### LS-A8p2HCI_23E#08 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: management-ops, scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:12
claim: Before scaling a service, list every daily action, group them into the workflows that produce the customer's result, document each, and automate them one at a time.
detail: He cites an investor's line that the biggest future winners will look like service firms but run on technology underneath. A founder may find dozens of workflows (he guesses 67). Doing it early, before running the business overwhelms you, is a newcomer's advantage over incumbents carrying large headcount. Advice: do not grow faster for an arbitrary reason; fix operations first ("go slow to go fast").

### LS-A8p2HCI_23E#09 | DEFINITION | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:16
claim: "Scale zero": get the owner's or a leader's time in a unit down to zero before scaling it.
detail: Phrase he and Leila use with their team. If a leader spends 20% of time on something and it grows 5x, they hit 100% and growth caps. Brick-and-mortar example: an owner still spending half their time at location one cannot open location two. Pressure-test by stepping away; time freed can build the next unit only if the first keeps growing and earning.
