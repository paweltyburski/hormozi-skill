### LS-TLpLmhWqKEU#09 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: unit-economics | fw: Lead Scoring | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:53:19
claim: A real-estate wholesaling coaching company with CAC about $5,000 and LTV about $35,000 within 90 days (7:1) had a low close rate; Hormozi said to add qualification at the front.
detail: Its sales team suffered from hearing no all day. Qualifying removes disqualified people, raises close rate and morale.
