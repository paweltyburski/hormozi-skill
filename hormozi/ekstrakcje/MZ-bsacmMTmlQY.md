claim: Pay a team bonus from a shared pot that unlocks only if everyone clears a threshold, so reps coach the weakest member.
detail: Because one low performer blocks everyone's payout, the team drills the laggard. He wants the enemy outside the ranks (competitors who rip off customers) and the team side by side. He says his earlier style was cutthroat, and he changed as teams grew.
