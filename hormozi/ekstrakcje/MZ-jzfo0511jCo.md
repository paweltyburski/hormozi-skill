### MZ-jzfo0511jCo#01 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: operator-mindset | fw: Agency Learning Agreement | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Learn a skill by paying an expert hourly to sit with you while you do it and explain each decision, without letting them touch the keyboard.
detail: He did this for paid ads, expecting to try harder than any agency. The expert must explain bids and targeting as you work.
numbers: $750 per hour; about 6 hours to learn the whole thing.

### MZ-jzfo0511jCo#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: For a salon-owner market, choose ads over content, affiliates or direct outreach; do content slowly in parallel for presence.
detail: Buy a list of salon owners, make a lookalike audience, offer something free and valuable for an opt-in.
