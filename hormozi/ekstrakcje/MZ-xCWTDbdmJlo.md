### MZ-xCWTDbdmJlo#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals, unit-economics | fw: Payback Period | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:25
claim: A health-and-fitness newsletter with 110,000 subscribers has a five-month payback on each new subscriber and needs a self-liquidating offer.
detail: Subscribers come from Facebook at about $1.45 each and earn about $0.30 a month from ads; revenue ~$40,000 a month. Eight sends a month, max three ads each. Past offers (meal plans, home exercises) sold to barely any: 2-3 buyers on about $10,000 a month of ad spend.
numbers: CPA $1.45; ~$0.30/subscriber/month; payback ~5 months.

### MZ-xCWTDbdmJlo#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:27
claim: He says you can expect roughly 2% of cold free sign-ups to upgrade to a paid offer, and with acquisition cost around $1.4 you'd probably need about a $100 offer.
detail: $97 at 2% returns about $2 per subscriber, which pays for acquisition. Even 0.5% would cut payback roughly in half. A brand is not required at $100.
numbers: 2% upgrade; $97 offer; 0.5% still halves payback.
### MZ-xCWTDbdmJlo#03 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Build the offer from proven content plus a title that answers the avatar's daily irritation.
detail: Bundle the 30 most read newsletters. For US women 40+, an idea is a book of 55 no-prep meals under five minutes, titled around the question "what's for dinner".

### MZ-xCWTDbdmJlo#04 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:01
claim: Use a quiz funnel to qualify cold traffic and route people to one of five or six offers.
detail: Expect about 50% drop-off from question 1 to 2 and again from 2 to 3, then almost none through question 20. Put belief-breaking, pain and testimonials between questions; the quiz acts as the VSL.
numbers: ~50% lost at each of the first two steps.

### MZ-xCWTDbdmJlo#05 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: Promote your own product hard in the first four days after sign-up, the peak conversion window.
detail: Two emails a day plus one text each day, 12 contacts in four days, in the welcome sequence.
numbers: 12 messages over 4 days.
