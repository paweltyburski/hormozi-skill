### YT-gZvW1Hghv6U#02 | EVOLUTION | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:04:38
claim: He made passive income the goal for years, then found it boring and empty, and decided to re-engage in 2022.
detail: He had been taught that being needed means you do not have a real business. Having done almost nothing for 9-12 months, he regrets optimising for it, since he loves the game. He sees some peers in their 40s-60s in his building pursuing a pleasure-seeking life and finds it one of the saddest things to watch; "no off switch on a tiger". Freedom means the freedom to choose, not to do nothing.
numbers: About 9-12 months of near-zero involvement.
### YT-gZvW1Hghv6U#03 | DEFINITION | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:30
claim: A business that maintains cash flow without you is not the test; a real business grows without you because enough talent is in place.
detail: Hormozi says he has proven he can build that, so he now chooses to play rather than watch. Stepping away to let others rise has a cost: he cannot jump back in without displacing them.

### YT-gZvW1Hghv6U#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:23
