### MZ-mkx1mzw1Lc8#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Scale personal risk to the size of the goal, working back from the target; the worst combination is a small goal with massive risk.
detail: Goals up to roughly $1M a year have well-known paths with high odds, so risking much is unnecessary; for a $20M or $100M goal, few companies can get you there and you will probably have to take on more personal risk. Example of the bad combination: putting life savings into a speculative coin for a double.
numbers: $100,000 to $1M a year reachable by employment or known routes; $20M a year has few companies that offer it and probably takes a long time.
### MZ-mkx1mzw1Lc8#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Be either earning or learning; a job is good when you learn skills from someone you admire, since you get paid to learn.
detail: Elsewhere you pay with money or time. The question is not job or no job but what the daily activities are and how you are paid for them.
