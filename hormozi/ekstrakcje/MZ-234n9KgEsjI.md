### MZ-234n9KgEsjI#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals, money-models | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A coaching company for Christian authors and speakers sees LTV:CAC fall from 10x to about 2x and is torn between coaching and a partner-delivered degree program.
detail: The degree is delivered by an accredited partner under a master services agreement: the company markets and sells and keeps about 80% of revenue. It is the same funnel, repackaged. Coaching $8,000 a year.
numbers: Revenue $4M, goal $12M; degree CAC $3,000-$4,000, LTV $10,000-$12,000 (~3:1); coaching ~2:1, previously 10:1.

### MZ-234n9KgEsjI#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:45
claim: If CAC rises faster than ad prices, the company is outpacing negative or neutral word of mouth.
detail: Bad word of mouth can go viral too and is more sensitive.
numbers: Bad-to-good word-of-mouth ratio about 15:1 (his claim).

### MZ-234n9KgEsjI#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:39
claim: Many coaching businesses keep launching new offers because the first one was never good enough.
detail: Each relaunch promises "this time is different".

### MZ-234n9KgEsjI#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: unit-economics | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:25
claim: A CAC that was one-fifth of today's means a temporary media arbitrage; the lasting arms race is LTV, not CAC.
detail: CAC across industries tends to be similar. Their $800-$900 CAC in 2020-2022 became about $4,000 as the market caught up. If rivals still survive, they have better advertising or higher LTV.
numbers: CAC $800-$900 then; $3,000-$4,000 now, which he calls benchmark.

### MZ-234n9KgEsjI#05 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:10
claim: Raise LTV to $20,000-$30,000 by selling a back end that credits the first purchase.
detail: Get half of buyers of the $12,000 product to a $30,000 one, credit $15,000 against it, and the total reaches ~$27,000. He fixes business back to front so ad-cost swings do not matter.
numbers: Target LTV $20K-$30K; 50% take-up; $15,000 credit.
