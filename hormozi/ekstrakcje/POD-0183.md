### POD-0183#01 | SCRIPT | topic: retention-ltv | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:21
claim: At sign-up, right after taking the card, ask for a second backup card.
detail: Frame it as protection against theft or loss. If they hesitate, say you simply need a second one, as always. Put both on the contract authorising billing; an ACH account also works. On a decline, swap and rerun.
numbers: declines are half or more of churn for many; the backup recovers about 2 of every 3.
applies_to: Any recurring-billing business
