### MZ-0_Yyxwtrt_Q#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A women's resort-wear designer at $4.5M, half wholesale, asks whether to keep premium prices or go broader.
detail: Hormozi: keep the premium. Swimsuits cost $30-40 to make and sell for $200-400, so gross margin is about 90%; cost sits in marketing.
numbers: $4.5M; target $100M in ~10 years; net margin 20%; ~90% gross margin; average order ~$350.

### MZ-0_Yyxwtrt_Q#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:45
claim: To reach $100M focus on lifting repeat purchase from 30% to about 65%.
detail: Cold markets need a brand loop; repeat buyers refer, lowering blended CAC and aiding wholesale.
numbers: Repeat rate 30% to 65%.

### MZ-0_Yyxwtrt_Q#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:42
claim: Without a founder face, build the brand through 5-10 influencers whose audiences match the price tier.
detail: Pick people with personality and true following, whitelist them for ads, and run testimonial contests via their links.
numbers: 5-10 influencers.
