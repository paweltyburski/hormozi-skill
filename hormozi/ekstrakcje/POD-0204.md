### POD-0204#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: "I do not have the card on me" probably means the sale was lost earlier.
detail: Likely causes: missed disengagement, an obstacle raised and ignored, or too little pain brought out. Hormozi compares it to trying to board a plane while the prospect still carries unresolved worries in an emotional backpack.
### POD-0204#02 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:03
claim: On a phone sale, step one is to wait while they fetch the card, because they are at home with cards.
detail: Say no problem and stay on the line. If the card they want belongs to a spouse, move to step two.

### POD-0204#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: objections-closing | fw: Payment Plan Downsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Step two: start on the card they have with a small amount, and swap in the preferred card for the rest.
detail: Suggest starting now and swapping the spouse's card tomorrow.
numbers: Example: instead of a $300 sale, start with $49 on the card at hand.

### POD-0204#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: objections-closing | fw: Downsell Flow | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:23
claim: Step three: start them with the card they have, delay the charge about two days, and swap the card at orientation.
detail: Set them up with the group and orientation as normal; at orientation they swap the card. The point is a card on file so the process begins.
numbers: Delay: 2 days.
