### YT-k-3PoOT4vOM#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:51
claim: Repositioning a commodity table from $100 to a $1,000 white-glove product lifts net profit per unit from $20 to $620, about 31x.
detail: Before: price $100, product cost $50, sales and marketing $30, net $20 (gross $50). After: price $1,000; product $50 plus $200 for delivery by truck and staff; sales and marketing rises to about $130; total cost about $380; net $620. The higher price pays for delivery, assembly and doorway-fitting service the buyer wants anyway. Half-doing the white glove at a plastic-table price kills the margin.
numbers: Net profit per unit $20 to $620 (31x); price 10x.

### YT-k-3PoOT4vOM#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:20:54
claim: The 30-day window is a free-credit window, so faster cash collection multiplies how many times you can reinvest per month.
detail: Anyone can get a credit card, even with a low limit, and pay it off next day. If the cash returns in 7 days you can repeat the cycle about four times a month; if daily, about 30 times. Growth speed therefore depends on the cash conversion cycle. Most businesses stay slow because they believe their industry standard cannot change.
numbers: 7-day cycle: ~4 repeats a month; daily: ~30 repeats a month.

### YT-k-3PoOT4vOM#16 | FRAMEWORK | topic: unit-economics | also: finance-exit | fw: Cost to Acquire a Permanent Customer | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:27:34
claim: "Wealth alchemy": the gap between what a permanent customer costs and the enterprise value that customer's recurring revenue adds.
detail: Worked case: free-trial start costs $100, 1 in 3 trials convert (CAC $300), 1 in 3 customers never leave (permanent CAC $900), price $100 a month ($1,200 a year). A SaaS firm valued at 10x revenue with good retention adds $12,000 of value per permanent customer. Scale: 10,000 such customers cost $9M, add $12M annual revenue and about $120M enterprise value. He says the enterprise value increase is tax-free.
numbers: Permanent CAC $900; $1,200 ARR; 10x revenue multiple gives $12,000; 10,000 customers cost $9M for $120M.
applies_to: Subscription businesses with strong retention

### YT-k-3PoOT4vOM#21 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Cost to Acquire a Permanent Customer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:37:05
claim: Find which customers become permanent, then advertise only to that type, even if the cost per customer rises.
detail: Define permanent customer (for an agency, someone staying 2+ years). If 1 in 10 become permanent, permanent CAC is 10x CAC. Study how those customers differ and target them: if CAC per customer doubles but the hit rate becomes 1 in 3, the permanent customer costs less.
numbers: Example: $100 CAC and 1 in 10 gives $1,000; $200 CAC and 1 in 3 gives about $600.
