### MZ-iW_kL2DfrVY#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process, verticals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A two-year-old US employee-benefits nonprofit ($3M revenue, paid about 20% of client savings) has 170 referral partners yet closes roughly one account a month at about 10% close rate.
detail: Partners are CPAs and financial advisers, found through the founder's network and LinkedIn automation; many are inactive and know five to ten companies each. Clients (50+ employees) do not want to be public testimonials because of tax disclosure. Hormozi: sales process comes first (could triple), then affiliate strategy.
numbers: $3M revenue; 170 partners; ~1 account/month; ~10% close; fee ~20% of savings; 2 years old.

### MZ-iW_kL2DfrVY#02 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: A too-good claim ($200K saved for 100 employees, $2,000 per head) sounded like a scam, so give a believable reason, such as zeroing the industry's profit margin.
detail: Suggested pitch: save about 15% because as a nonprofit we take the ~20% industry profit margin down to zero and pass it to you.
numbers: 15% savings; 20% industry margin; 5-10% broker commissions.

### MZ-iW_kL2DfrVY#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:11
claim: When all customers arrive through referral partners, treat the partners as the customers and compute acquisition metrics and messaging for them.
detail: Moving up one level of thinking also gives more leverage.

### MZ-iW_kL2DfrVY#04 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: - | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:59
claim: Launch then integrate: first have each partner refer ten people to create an internal case study, then plug your offer into their onboarding so you reach 100% of their new customers.
detail: Also offer to do the work free in exchange for the right to use the story; partners include the savings percentage in their own pitch. Goal: each of 170 partners sends one account a month, which would make a $100M business.
numbers: 10 referred accounts for the launch; target 170 accounts/month.
