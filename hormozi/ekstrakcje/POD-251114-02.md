claim: A private-equity firm known for software buyouts scored customers by tenure and spend, then cut low-value channels and doubled down on the best ones.
detail: A seam of underserved valuable customers made a company worth buying. Hormozi saw this as Pareto on steroids and made it a pillar of his portfolio method.

### POD-251114-02#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Survey customers on four areas and give them a benefit for answering.
detail: Demographics; business numbers before and now; goal on purchase; buying process (best reason, trigger event, content and testimonials consumed, how many pieces, time from first contact to purchase, where they first saw you, referral).

### POD-251114-02#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Sort by liked, highest spend and longest tenure, keep the top 20%, and extract the fewest shared qualifiers.
detail: Usually three to five qualifiers. Then advertise to them directly, stop selling to non-fits and put more effort into channels they come through; competitors rarely do this analysis.
numbers: Top 20%; 3 to 5 qualifiers.

### POD-251114-02#06 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:17

### POD-251114-02#08 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: sales-process | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:43
