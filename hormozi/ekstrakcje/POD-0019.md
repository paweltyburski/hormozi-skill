### POD-0019#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Use an automated dialer so each hour on the phone produces more calls and more appointments.
detail: More calls per hour means more appointments per hour and more money per hour spent calling.

### POD-0019#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Open a lead call like a friend: say their name as a greeting, then who you are and that they just signed up.
detail: Steps: when they answer, say "what's up, John" instead of asking "is this John?"; pause; give your first name; then your business and that they just opted in; they usually react with surprise at the speed. This avoids the subservient "who is this?" position and avoids sounding like a telemarketer. Rapport comes within ten seconds.
numbers: about 3 seconds to make an impression (his estimate).

### POD-0019#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:18
claim: When a lead deflects why they signed up, restate it with mild sarcasm until they admit the need.
detail: Ask why they signed up. If they say it just looked interesting, reply "so you just opt into things for fun, and you're in perfect shape?" They answer with the real need (for example to lose weight), and you assign it to them before moving into the sale.
