### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: What you are paid, as employee or business, depends on value created, the share you ask for, how replaceable you are, and who you serve.
detail: Found by inverting: how would you earn as little as possible? Provide little value, ask for little of it, be easy to replace, and serve people who gain little. To flip it, raise value (the value equation), price or negotiate higher, differentiate into a category of one, and pick customers for whom the problem is worth more.

### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:47
claim: The same asset is worth very different amounts depending on the buyer.
detail: A teenager's old car is offered $5,000 by a dealer, $1,000 by a scrapyard, and about $200,000 by an antique dealer. The point: price depends on who you are talking to, so serve the buyer for whom the problem has most value (a billionaire versus someone unemployed for weight loss).
numbers: $5,000; $1,000; $200,000.

### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:26
claim: Do not set prices from your own wallet; what is easy for you is hard for others, so let the market set the price.
detail: Illustration: a mechanic finds an oil change trivial and overlooks that a non-car person happily pays $100 or $300 without noticing. Asking for more often simply gets more.

### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: hiring-talent | fw: Implied Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:18
claim: Ask for variable compensation tied to the results you drive, as an employee or as a B2B service provider.
detail: Employee version: show how your work ties to revenue and ask whether a percentage of it is unreasonable. Service version: state the expected result and ask for a share. Owners and enterprise value are themselves variable compensation. The more directly you can point to the revenue, the less the value gets discounted.

### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:14
claim: In a negotiation, the party with more options wins, so build alternatives before negotiating.
detail: More customers waiting, or more job offers, gives leverage. Top executive pay reflects how few people can do the job (supply and demand for that role).

### YT--Pz0NpcILOY#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:45
claim: Your earnings ceiling is a subset of the opportunity vehicle you work in, so pick a business whose vision is big enough for others to get rich.
detail: A dry-cleaning employee is unlikely to earn $1M if the business does not. He cites Schwarzman's idea that a company's vision should contain others' visions, and Buffett's rule that work should either teach you or pay you, ideally both.
numbers: Said about 8 million frontline workers quit in the prior two months (his figure, unverified).
