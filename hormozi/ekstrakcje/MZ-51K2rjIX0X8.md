### MZ-51K2rjIX0X8#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A founder-coach selling a $42K, 12-month group programme plus one-on-one at an 85% margin does everything herself.
detail: She sells, closes in DMs, delivers and creates content. Advice: remove one-on-one, run another group at the same price capped at 10-15, add in-person components so proximity is kept, and give DM setting to someone else.
numbers: Programme $42K for 12 months; margin 85%; group of 15-30; revenue around $1.1M-$1.2M last year (as stated).

### MZ-51K2rjIX0X8#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:14
claim: Dilute access, not quality: make the offer so good that even a fractional share of you is worth buying.
detail: He compares a few droplets of milk in glasses of water with small shot glasses of the best milk. Change the medium (in person) rather than the ratio to keep perceived proximity.

### MZ-51K2rjIX0X8#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: since she keeps an 85% margin by doing everything herself, she should pay roughly $100K-$200K for help; he says that might buy back about half her time and let her double the top line.
detail: He says the margin will press down a little, but the extra time could make up for it.
numbers: Help cost about $100K-$200K (his example).
