### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#01 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Phrase a request as a light presumption ("I know you are not leaving without putting your weights back") rather than a plea.
detail: Learned from a gym owner; it is non-accusatory and playful, and the client complies.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: After a no-show, joke about being heartbroken, say something surely came up, and rebook.
detail: Then go straight back into the offer. Prospects are busy and forgot, not bad.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Set the frame early so prospects treat you well.
detail: On a cold call, answer "how is your day?" with enthusiasm about how kind everyone has been; they then cannot be rude.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Internet-lead call opener: name, pause, identify, humour, then ask how they found you.
detail: Say you are a real person near a local landmark. Ask whether they saw an ad or a local best-boot-camp award, planting any award as legitimacy, then ask why they signed up.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#05 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:58
claim: Handle "I must ask my husband" by asking what happens if he says no, then projecting five years ahead.
detail: Childlike curiosity; talk to the decision maker, as overcoming obstacles is a dance, not a fight.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: What Would It Take | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:44
claim: Make the prospect decide in front of you and name the criteria that would make them certain.
detail: Say they may suspect you are foolish, ask how you can prove otherwise, and fix whatever they name on the spot.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#07 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:30
claim: Use a mental cue, "huh", with head tilted, when tempted to attack.
detail: Curiosity disarms the prospect and gives you a second; avoid "commission breath".

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#08 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:15
claim: On a money objection, play it out: what happens if you spend it, have you bought big before.
detail: Stay in the uncomfortable spot while keeping the prospect comfortable.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#09 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:02
claim: Agree with doubts and own the negatives first.
detail: Say it is expensive and hard and they should still do it; doubts show realistic expectations.

### MZ-NfY4wazzvYM#10 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:50
claim: Do the opposite of what the prospect expects from a salesperson.
detail: After an objection, smile, wave it off and drop your shoulders, which breaks their expected pattern.
