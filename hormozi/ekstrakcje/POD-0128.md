### POD-0128#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:39
claim: People already have what they most want; the gap between that and what they say they want shows their real priorities.
detail: Look at the trade-offs of someone achieving a lot and ask if you would make them. Accepting you do not want it is fine, but do not expect the same return while investing less.

### POD-0128#04 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:52

### POD-0128#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:09:51
claim: Optimizers seek the minimum effective dose (80/20, lifestyle business); maximizers accept diminishing returns because top ranks pay disproportionately.
detail: Gains from sale 1 to 100 exceeded those from 100 to 1,000, yet he is much better at 1,000. Like Olympians, small increments near the top carry large rewards, so maximizers beat optimizers. Both are acceptable lives; choose knowingly.
