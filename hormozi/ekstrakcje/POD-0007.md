### POD-0007#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: objections-closing | fw: Unconditional Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:59
claim: A satisfaction guarantee sounds easy to use but is rarely claimed, because the client must tell the owner to their face that the service was poor.
detail: Hormozi used a plain satisfaction promise (service quality matches the price; otherwise a refund) at most of his gyms and calls it his favourite. It is comparable to the extended warranty sold at an electronics store: sold to everyone, seldom used, and high-margin. He admits two of the claimants were people he should not have signed.
numbers: Across all his gym locations, only two or three people claimed it.

### POD-0007#02 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: - | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:42
claim: A results guarantee should carry qualifiers the client controls, such as attending every workout and logging food.
detail: Wording: lose a stated amount of weight by a date, provided they attend all sessions and log meals. At some point you have to assume the client is trying to succeed; self-sabotage exists but this type still works well.

### POD-0007#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Guarantees | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:42
claim: Use the results-based guarantee on short front-end offers to cold traffic, not on long back-end contracts.
detail: Suited to an entry offer of about four to six weeks, not guarantees stretching 24 weeks or a year. Cold buyers trust you least. When upselling into a 12-month or 24-week high-ticket contract, buyers already trust you and assume you will do your best, so heavy guarantees are not needed. Hormozi adds that he does not believe in low-ticket offers.
numbers: Entry offer length where it fits: 4 to 6 weeks.

### POD-0007#04 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: promotions-launches | fw: Guarantees | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:41
claim: A double-your-money-back guarantee sounds extreme, so it signals confidence while almost nobody claims it.
detail: A longer window, such as 60 days instead of 30, is a related variation. Put the guarantee at the end of the stacked benefits and bonuses ("and besides all that...") as the last push for people who say they need to think or ask a spouse.

### POD-0007#05 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:27
claim: Show the guarantee everywhere in the funnel and arm the sales team with it.
detail: Places named: thank-you pages, opt-in pages, and sales scripts.
