### MZ-zcI6Uz6eWkQ#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: pricing, verticals | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A trainer of advertisers sells a $2,000-$3,000 programme with no back end via organic and paid traffic to a webinar to a booked call.
detail: Because he sells by phone he can charge more. The avatar is agency or freelance employees wanting to improve at their job.
numbers: now $2,000-$3,000; suggested $7,000-$9,000 (e.g. $8,400 for eight weeks).

### MZ-zcI6Uz6eWkQ#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:12
claim: Two routes to recurring revenue from a short course: roll a third to a half of buyers into a year, or make the front end a one-year membership at the same price.
detail: The second turns a one-time sale into annual recurring revenue.
numbers: 8 weeks versus 12 months; a third to a half rolling into a year.
### MZ-zcI6Uz6eWkQ#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:29
claim: Make the paid community a professional association with a network and two in-person meetups a year, where deals get done.
detail: The network supplies job security, so it sticks. The avatar earns too little for a $25,000 back end.
numbers: 2 meetups per year.
