### MZ-kewx3UQAdAg#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A women's apparel brand grew from $1M to $2M, heading to $3M, by switching from printed t-shirts to its own cut-and-sew line made overseas.
detail: Goal is $10M; about 80% of sales come from ads; the brand has existed since 2015 but had weak product for seven years.
numbers: $1M to $2M last year; $3M this year; 80% of sales from ads.

### MZ-kewx3UQAdAg#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:21
claim: In e-commerce a fast arbitrage path can reach $1M a month in 60 days, but CAC only rises over time unless repeat business and word of mouth push back.
detail: So you make sales to get customers, not customers to make sales. The low ticket forces e-commerce operators to be good at brand.
numbers: $0 to $1M per month in about 60 days; arbitrage turning $1 into $1.50 (example).

### MZ-kewx3UQAdAg#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: lead-getters | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:09
claim: Meta's creative-hungry delivery needs a creator army: sign two or three on-brand figureheads, then recruit micro and nano creators to go live and make content.
detail: Three ways to get more creative: build it in-house (hits a wall), a mega-influencer, or a horizontal army. Target 10-50K followers on TikTok.
numbers: Creators with 10-50K followers.

### MZ-kewx3UQAdAg#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:40
claim: Email should supply about 35% of revenue for an e-commerce brand; at 20% it is low and all extra is incremental margin.
detail: Order of operations: fix email, then repeat purchase (share buying second and third items), then know LTGP per customer, then set incentives to bring more people.
numbers: Email at 20% of revenue vs about 35% target.
