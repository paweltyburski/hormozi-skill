### MZ-plV_nyqluOE#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints, unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A coffee company with three cafes and a roastery wants 20 locations but has thin retail margins.
detail: The roaster is a separate company that sells to the cafes at retail price, so retail looks worse; fully integrated it would be 20-25%. Roasting needs wholesale volume to justify. Hormozi says not over-expanded but under-talented.
numbers: Revenue $3-4M across three cafes; roasting $800k; 15 wholesale customers; margins 15% retail, 30% wholesale; goal $10M retail, 20 sites.

### MZ-plV_nyqluOE#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:18
claim: Don't open more stores to earn more; make the stores so profitable that opening more is the obvious use of cash.
detail: Get retail margin to 40%+ and triple profit across three stores before opening six more.
numbers: Target retail margin 40%+.

### MZ-plV_nyqluOE#03 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:32
claim: Raise store margin through roughly twenty small fixes across staffing mix, comp, menu order, register scripts and incentives.
detail: Optimize hours and staffing mix and pay structure; reorder the menu toward high-profit items; script attach rates with an incentive; use grassroots foot traffic marketing. Likely half the menu rarely sells and ties up inventory cash, so improve cash conversion.
numbers: Possibly 50%+ of menu items rarely ordered.

### MZ-plV_nyqluOE#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: Hormozi is fine with the wholesale business as long as it takes zero attention from the owner, and he would add no new customers, since its point is just to keep the vertical integration.
detail: The caller said he needs wholesale to keep the roasting operation operational; Hormozi's view is that wholesale is not there to make more money.
