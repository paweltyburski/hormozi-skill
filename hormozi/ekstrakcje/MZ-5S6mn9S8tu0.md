### MZ-5S6mn9S8tu0#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An affiliate-marketing educator stuck at $1-3M a year (about 60% via affiliates, nearly 100K subscribers, automated challenge/webinar to $2,000) is shown three levers to $10M.
detail: Options: add phone sales to convert more of the known audience at a higher price; make better content to widen the audience; run ads to reach those who consumed his content but do not follow.
numbers: $1-3M revenue; ~60% revenue from affiliates; ~100,000 subscribers; target $10M.

### MZ-5S6mn9S8tu0#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:36
claim: Only a sliver of followers see a post and fewer see its call to action; ads reach also the wider "interest graph" of people who consumed but never followed.
detail: Retarget the warm audience first; later decide whether to go to colder traffic or grow the warm one.

### MZ-5S6mn9S8tu0#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: pricing | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:11
claim: Phone sales team, higher price and paid ads on the warm audience are one-time levers; once they are pulled, the next question is whether to scale into colder traffic or grow the warm audience, or both.
detail: You might be able to go from about $3M to $10M with them, but at $10M you run into the same issue.
