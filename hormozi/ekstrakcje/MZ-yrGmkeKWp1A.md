### MZ-yrGmkeKWp1A#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: What separates those who scale from those who do not is rarely metrics; it is hard conversations, consistency, communication and changing things too often.
detail: He has not met a business that cannot reach $100M a year (a dry cleaner can open more locations), but not every entrepreneur has the skill to get there. Discipline means not overextending and patiently fixing the current model before adding.

### MZ-yrGmkeKWp1A#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: content-brand | fw: A Players | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:36
claim: "No good talent" usually means no good talent for you; intelligent people must want to work for you, and your brand and opportunity attract them.
detail: Acquisition.com attracts bigger thinkers than Gym Launch did, since gym consulting is a smaller opportunity.

### MZ-yrGmkeKWp1A#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: unit-economics | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Judge an offer or price change by downstream metrics: a better offer lifts conversion and retention, a wrong one lowers conversion and sees buyers leave quickly.
detail: Offer and price are inseparable. If conversion falls and those who buy leave fast, the offer or some of its components are wrong.
