### MZ-rMMOHeBzA50#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An agency serving restaurants and chauffeur companies wants to go from $1M a year to $1M a month.
detail: After losing its one big client, it has about 35 small restaurant clients from referrals. Two weeks into a chauffeur niche, trial customers cost $30-50 each against about $600 in restaurants. The funnel: $1 first month, then $197 a month, then agency services.
numbers: $1M revenue a year; ~35 restaurant clients; CAC $30-50 chauffeur vs ~$600 restaurant; $197/month tier.

### MZ-rMMOHeBzA50#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: make no change for about a month; cheap acquisition does not prove a good customer.
detail: Wait until LTV:CAC is known. Cheap leads in a new niche may hide poor customers, like personal trainers who are easy to acquire but low-paying and difficult.
### MZ-rMMOHeBzA50#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:24
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: he thinks it's unwise to have two entirely different avatars; if LTV matches the other business, it might make sense to sunset the old one.
detail: Reaching $1M a month costs giving up roughly half of the current small business.
