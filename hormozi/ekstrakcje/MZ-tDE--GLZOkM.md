### MZ-tDE--GLZOkM#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: hiring-talent, verticals | fw: Virtuous Cycle of Price | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An agency running social media and financial influencers for ETF issuers has strong top talent, a weak second tier, and prices far below its clients' scale.
detail: Compliance limits channels per client. He wants one top-tier client but worries about being 27; Hormozi says age is only in his head (he sold to 40-year-old gym owners at 26). Prices were raised by $5,000 each meeting.
numbers: Revenue pace $2.7M, profit about $700,000, goal $20M; average client $20,000 a month; earlier $8,500, then $15,000, then $20-25k.

### MZ-tDE--GLZOkM#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:17
claim: For this caller, whose clients are worth $60B+, he said $20,000 a month is not even a rounding error, and that if you will get to a higher price eventually you might as well get there now.
detail: He said the caller could skip to $100,000 a month if he wants, about $1M a year.
numbers: Clients worth $60B+; possible price $100,000 a month.
### MZ-tDE--GLZOkM#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:03
claim: Sell audience access priced against Meta ad spend, not services measured by AUM movement.
detail: Metrics cannot prove attribution, so measure reach and price as cheaper impressions to the right audience. Third-party endorsement is worth about five times a brand's own claim.
numbers: Retail investor CPM about $30-40; example reach 3 million people; endorsement about 5x.

### MZ-tDE--GLZOkM#04 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:35
claim: Recruit by tier: ads for mid and lower roles, outreach for director and above, industry sites or headhunters for specialists.
detail: Referrals are ideal. Managers and one level down respond to ordinary ads. High-level people can be reached through industry group postings, such as two general-counsel sites. Poaching from Fortune 100 firms is one of the better acquisition strategies for top talent.
