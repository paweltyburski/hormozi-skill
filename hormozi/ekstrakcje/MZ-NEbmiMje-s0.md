### MZ-NEbmiMje-s0#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:28
claim: Equity bundles cash flow, sale proceeds, risk and control; offer only the first two.
detail: Employees usually refuse risk and control, so replace equity with a profit pool and exit share.

### MZ-NEbmiMje-s0#05 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Five S's of Advanced Compensation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Give leaders a profit pool of 10-20% of profit, shared among them, with the percentage fixed as the pie grows.
detail: Pay depends on the leverage the hire has; a veteran who scaled before may justify more.
numbers: Pool 10-20%; he likes 10-15%.

### MZ-NEbmiMje-s0#06 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:14
claim: A phantom profits interest accrues about 1% a year up to a cap such as 4%, returns if the person leaves, and pays only on a sale.
detail: Requires 12-24 months tenure; payout taxed as ordinary income. Percentages are illustrative.
numbers: 4% cap at 1% per year (illustrative); 12-24 months tenure.

### MZ-NEbmiMje-s0#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:14

detail: If the shops' P&Ls are already separate, profit share can be calculated per shop.
### MZ-NEbmiMje-s0#08 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:01
claim: For fast-growth firms share a percentage of growth over last year; for local businesses use profit share and reverse-engineer target pay.
detail: Local growth is capped, so growth shares discourage. Example: $200K cumulative monthly profit over four stores is about $50K each, so 10% gives $5K per month ($60K per year).
numbers: Growth share example 30% of growth; 10% of $50K monthly profit = $5K.
