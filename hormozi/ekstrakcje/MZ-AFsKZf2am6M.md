### MZ-AFsKZf2am6M#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Content quantity and quality work as an accordion: make many, find the few winners, cut back to fewer better pieces, then expand again.
detail: Make several pieces and see which one drives most of the output. Then make only a couple at that quality, which often performs as well, then raise the count again at that quality and repeat. Extreme volume suits news, which has almost no long tail; education depends more on quality.

### MZ-AFsKZf2am6M#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: He tends to prescribe volume first (said to an educator), because you don't know what quality is until you have enough repetitions to compare.
detail: With a data set such as a hundred videos you can ask why some tended to do better than the other 90; before that it is very hard to learn anything.
numbers: A hundred videos as an example of a first data set.
