### MZ-X5zgFLgFwXE#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing, sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: To win US clients from abroad, first speak American English without accent and keep profile and videos in English; then you can charge US prices.
detail: If your profile and videos are in American English, buyers online would have little idea where you live; when buyers object that you have lower expenses, he calls that a sales issue, not a market one.
### MZ-X5zgFLgFwXE#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:57
claim: What you can charge equals the gain the buyer believes you deliver, discounted by perceived risk, times the share of that gain they are willing to give.
detail: If a buyer loses the frame, that is on the seller: they lost trust earlier in the sale, and the objection (for example about age or location) is just a reason the buyer uses to get out of the sale.
