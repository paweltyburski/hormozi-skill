### MZ-7KkZwJ56XxE#01 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A high-end residential cleaning firm at $1.5M plans 10 locations a year after finding outsourced lead gen poor and rebuilding it in-house.
detail: About 80% of business comes from Google PPC. Hormozi warns that ten openings in a year will be stressful and says the gap is a sales motion that adds the right friction between lead and sale.
numbers: Revenue $1.5M, goal $15M; PPC about 80% of business.

### MZ-7KkZwJ56XxE#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:11
claim: Never outsource the three core legs, attract, convert and deliver; vendors may help with tasks but must not own how the business works.
detail: If a vendor leaves you should still know how to do it.

### MZ-7KkZwJ56XxE#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:56
claim: Lead quality ranks referrals first, then content, then PPC, with display-network leads worst.
detail: Marketing comes down to finding the sweet spot of friction: a bare price and phone number is too little, a hundred form fields too much.

### MZ-7KkZwJ56XxE#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:59
claim: If you serve a good 20% and a poor 80%, you probably price mid-way and are underpriced by about 2x for the good avatar; change price and sales motion.
detail: Expect cost per lead and CAC to rise 2-3x. Doubling the price can lift gross margin about 5x. The poor segment will churn out once prices are right.
numbers: Underpriced about 2x; CPL up 2-3x; doubled price gives about 5x gross margin.
