### MZ-BOYphPIi4JY#02 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:59
claim: Local ad recipe: pin a radius, show before-and-afters, use an opt-in, have the front desk call, and take a card for a no-show fee.
detail: Targeting is the hardest part of ads and a local radius removes it. Scheduler can sit on the thank-you page. Cold traffic means no-shows, so the confirmation call asks for a card; if asked why, say it is how it has always been done.
numbers: 10-mile radius.

### MZ-BOYphPIi4JY#03 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: paid-ads | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:44
claim: For a five-fold jump, advertise a free Invisalign giveaway in the radius and count down daily.
detail: Message and text entrants until the drawing, then call every non-winner with a second prize of $2,000 off and ask when they want to book. The milder option is $1,000-$2,000 off.
numbers: Seven-day countdown; second prize $2,000 off.
