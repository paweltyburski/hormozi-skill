### MZ-PMO0ilv1HBQ#01 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: verticals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A betting and fantasy-football content creator earning per-signup affiliate fees wants to grow from $1M to $5M.
detail: Ten months after restarting alone: mostly short-form video driving incentivised sign-ups, with no ownership of customers or LTV. Content lasts about a day, so the best format is a listicle of picks for a given game. Aim: 7-10 strong pieces daily.
numbers: $1M revenue, $850K profit in 10 months; target $5M.

claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: post more on the same account; falling views are usually a quality issue, not a penalty for posting.
detail: Instagram has publicly said it does not penalise posting, and he describes each post as another dot that gets shown. He doesn't think topical versus evergreen matters; he looks at impressions per month.

claim: A pure affiliate with no product is hard to sell; the likely buyer is a larger platform.

numbers: Comparable firm ~$4M a year.

### MZ-PMO0ilv1HBQ#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:48
claim: A whale affiliate gains leverage when its volume is large enough that the platform fears losing it.
detail: A platform paying $10M a year earns $30-50M from you. At $50M brought in, 40% net margin and 12x gives about $240M in enterprise value to them.
numbers: $10M paid; $50M brought; 40% margin; $20M EBITDA; 12x multiple; ~$240M value.
