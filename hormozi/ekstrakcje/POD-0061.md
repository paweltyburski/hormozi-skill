### POD-0061#01 | DEFINITION | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:25
claim: A repeat buyer sticks because switching costs thought; a loyal customer skips a better or cheaper alternative to stay.
detail: Repeat buyers just meet a quality and price threshold. The difference between the two is inspiration versus manipulation with carrots and sticks such as discounts and penalties, the lever most firms use.

### POD-0061#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:12
claim: People buy and work for what a company represents; its products are tangible proof of beliefs.
detail: Buying says something about the buyer's identity. Transactional businesses get no loyalty from staff, many of whom feel unfulfilled. Decisions come from the limbic part of the brain, so cast an inspiring vision rather than listing benefits. Sinek's golden circle: start with why, not what and how.
numbers: about 90% of workers unfulfilled by work (his figure).

### POD-0061#03 | EVOLUTION | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:12
claim: By mid-2018 he saw parallels between a why-based cause and branding, which he called "sort of" a poo-poo term.
detail: A step between his 2017 rejection of branding and later views. He ties it to an infinite game: pursue an ideal you will not fully reach, and ignore competitors.
### POD-0061#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:07
claim: Authenticity means zero gap between private and public; most leaders cannot manage it because they lack beliefs that inspire.
detail: Real beliefs show in service quality and in how bought-in staff and customers are. Their company states beliefs: absolute truth, admit mistakes and flops, and treat employees as well as or better than customers.

### POD-0061#05 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:57
claim: Would your employees leave if offered the same role with better pay elsewhere?
detail: Loyal staff often would not. A lot of times people come for things like loving the job and feeling fulfilled, but stay for the belief and vision you cast.
### POD-0061#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:29
claim: A belief-based following yields lower turnover, higher productivity per employee, premium prices and longer lifetime value.
detail: Customers stop comparing, as with Apple buyers despite less powerful computers. Staff work unrecorded overtime. Trust lets people take initiative, whereas carrot-and-stick cultures give no innovation.

### POD-0061#07 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:07
claim: Write down your beliefs, say them publicly, and let go of staff who do not share them even if skilled.
detail: The more polarising, the more it attracts aligned people. A shared belief system also draws customers who match it.

### POD-0061#08 | EXAMPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:14:39
claim: He negotiated a discount for Gym Lord members from a rubber manufacturer and declined a 10-15% kickback, acting on a give-first value.
detail: Level one: give without reward. Level two: for a high-quality supplier serving thousands of gyms, give first and treat return as irrelevant, since the value is independent of payback.
numbers: refused commission of 10-15%.
