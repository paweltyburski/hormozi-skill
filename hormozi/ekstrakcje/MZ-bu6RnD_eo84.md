### MZ-bu6RnD_eo84#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:56
claim: Reverse pyramid of awareness (from Eugene Schwartz): unaware is widest, then problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, and most-aware, usually existing customers.
detail: Creators who speak only about their product address a thin slice of the market, which is why ads stall at $1,000 or $10,000 a day. Broad reach is won with curiosity ("how do people on the beach make more than me?"), not product terms.
numbers: ad spend plateaus mentioned: $1,000/day and $10,000/day.

### MZ-bu6RnD_eo84#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: Call-Outs (Labels, Yes Questions, If-Then, Ridiculous Results) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:51
claim: Pitch an unaware person with a curiosity opener that explains the idea in plain language, as to a relative's friend at dinner, before credentials.
detail: Example for affiliate marketing: some links pay people who did not make the product; ask if they ever recommended something unpaid, then offer to get paid. Credentials like "$250M" are generic proof only after curiosity.

### MZ-bu6RnD_eo84#03 | SCRIPT | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:40
claim: For problem-aware viewers, use a pain-based hook then reframe failure: perhaps the earlier attempts failed because the opportunity did not fit their psychographics.
detail: Sequence: name current pain, recall past failed tries, offer the reframe and an analogy (people with similar psychographics enjoy different exercise types), then match their problems to your solution and move down the funnel. Tactical videos get less reach.
