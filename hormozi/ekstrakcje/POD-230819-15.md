### POD-230819-15#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: lead-getters | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:24
claim: If every customer brings two more, a 4:1 LTGP to CAC becomes 12:1 and you get three customers for the price of one.
detail: At 4:1, a new customer costs 25% of a customer's lifetime gross profit; with two referrals each, the cost per customer falls to just over 8.3%. Referred customers also tend to buy more expensive items, buy more often and pay more up front.
numbers: 4:1 (25% of LTGP) to 12:1 (about 8.3%).

### POD-230819-15#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: retention-ltv | fw: Six Ways to Increase Value | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:16
claim: Keep lowering the promises in your offer until close rates start to fall, then stop.
detail: Early on he promised everything to get sales and the delivery became a nightmare. Inching promises down while keeping quality left room to over-deliver, which brought referrals. He says stopping at that point maximizes how many customers you get and the goodwill you build.
### POD-230819-15#06 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:50

### POD-230819-15#13 | EXAMPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: pricing | fw: Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:26:13
claim: Referral reward examples: pay the CAC to the referrer, or split it, and offer a household discount to spouses.
detail: One-sided: with a $200 CAC, get a real three-way introduction at purchase and pay a check or give the friend $200 off. Ads can effectively run for free because referral cash often covers their cost when LTGP:CAC is above 3:1.
numbers: $200 CAC; $500 program; $100 + $100 split; up to 3 friends; extra 25 to 30% signups.

### POD-230819-15#15 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing, lead-getters | fw: Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:28:45
claim: Use referrals as a negotiation chip, and answer full-price customers who discover a discount by offering them the same deal.
detail: A buyer at $400 on a $500 price can get the discount for a three-way text introduction to a few friends. If another customer complains, say that a named customer got $100 off for referring three friends, and offer the same if they refer three. They either back off or bring three friends.
numbers: $400 offered vs $500 price; $100 off for 3 friends.
