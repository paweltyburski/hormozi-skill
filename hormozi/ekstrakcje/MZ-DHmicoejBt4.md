### MZ-DHmicoejBt4#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A UAE tax advisory firm is limited by manpower, since the founder produces most revenue and hiring needs an office first.
detail: Seven agents are average for the industry; the market has enough prospects. A visa rule means he must lease an office for about 40 people on a four-year contract before hiring. Four desks are open now, about 50% growth.
numbers: Revenue $6M, goal $24M; founder about $4M (68%); seven agents about $2M together; profit about $4M; office about $350,000 a year.

### MZ-DHmicoejBt4#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:52
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: don't worry about spending extra time negotiating a small office-lease saving, since he earns the difference in a couple of days.
detail: Negotiating the rent down is worth little when the founder generates the difference in a few days of work.
numbers: Founder earns about $500,000 a month, so a rent saving is about two and a half days of work.
### MZ-DHmicoejBt4#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:51
claim: Shift identity from top closer to sales trainer and hard-block about 25% of time for training the team.
detail: Cost is roughly a million dollars a year of the founder's own output, but one extra deal a year per agent repays more. Content is organic, so recruiting and training is the business now.
numbers: 25% of founder time, about $1M a year cost.

### MZ-DHmicoejBt4#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:45
claim: A brokerage president spent his time only recruiting agents, because the system gave a known output per agent.
detail: Once input and output are known, capacity is how many people can go through the system.
