### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Interview salespeople for response speed, intelligence, listening and coachability, testing coachability with a role play, feedback and a retry.
detail: Quick replies show work ethic. Prefer an ambivert who listens. Role play quality does not matter: give feedback, ask for a second attempt. Taking feedback without ego and improving marks a coachable hire.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Train prospect knowledge more than product knowledge.
detail: A rep who describes the prospect's problem exactly is believed to understand them, so they buy whatever is offered.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: A 14-day onboarding: listen to about 40 good recorded calls, work on the script in between, role play every morning until a pass test.
detail: Recording software confirms they listened. If no good calls exist, sell well yourself and record them.
numbers: 14 days; ~40 calls.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: A script is a question framework built around one stated decision; a rep who keeps asking "any questions?" cannot close.
detail: Repeated question prompts invite extra reasons not to buy. Boil the call to one yes-or-no decision, for example whether the buyer earns more with the service than alone, beyond its fee.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:05
claim: After passing the role-play test, a rep works a half schedule so they burn through leads while still reviewing their own calls.
detail: Manager meets them twice daily. Full schedule once they hit the minimum close rate or other chosen metric.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:54
claim: Coaching loop per rep: 30 minutes reviewing calls at double speed with notes, 5 minutes naming the one or two top fixes, 25 minutes role playing them.
detail: Run it right after the review. Next day check whether the fix appeared; if not, role play again. Drill stall lines (think about it, ask my spouse, too much money) in the role-play slot. Keep a dated note per rep and follow up on the same two items; send them in writing.
numbers: 60 minutes per rep per week.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:27
claim: Culture rests on a live leaderboard, KPIs with a contest every 4 to 6 weeks, and cutting the bottom 10% quarterly.
detail: Contests optimised for cash collected up front worked best across his portfolio. Not cutting tells the team weak results are fine.
numbers: Contest every 30-42 days; bottom 10% cut quarterly.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:27
claim: Sales management is a skill of tolerance; the manager who tolerates most has the weakest team.
detail: Winning and losing teams differ mostly by the coach. The best salesperson is rarely the best manager; managers are steadier, giving, and lead by example.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#09 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:13
claim: Hormozi cut more than half of a bloated sales team, then reset standards and let the rest sleep on staying.
detail: New rules: 5am hour-long group drilling, on time to every call, daily pipeline cleaning, CRM notes. Breaking them means firing, even the top closer.
numbers: More than half the team cut.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:04
claim: A rep below KPI for two weeks goes on a performance plan and is treated as a trainee again; two more weeks without improvement ends employment.
detail: Say so at hiring. Cadence: once or twice a day for the first 14 days of onboarding, weekly meetings after graduation, and maybe every other week after a year if they are killing it.
numbers: 2 weeks below KPI, then 2 weeks on plan.
### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#11 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:24
claim: Meeting rhythm: weekly and monthly team meetings, weekly one-on-ones, a morning training huddle (mandatory for trainees and those on plans) and a short end-of-day huddle.
detail: End of day is a check-in and pipeline cleanup reminder.

### YT-2lA_A8BGRRs#12 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: retention-ltv | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:10
claim: Sustain conviction with contests and client results: best is a client on a sales call, then videos in the huddle, then testimonials read aloud by the rep who sold them.
detail: The aim is a rep pushing through "no" out of belief in the transformation, not ego.
