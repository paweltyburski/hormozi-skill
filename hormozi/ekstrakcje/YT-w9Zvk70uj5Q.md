### YT-w9Zvk70uj5Q#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Volume negates luck, because luck is what people call probability they do not understand.
detail: If a known share of contacts reply and a known share of replies close, closing deals at scale is arithmetic. He uses an illustration of 100,000 emails yielding 1,000 replies, and says his closing figure in the example is not realistic. Volume also produces skill, which further reduces reliance on luck.
numbers: Illustration: 100,000 emails to 1,000 replies (closing share in the example not realistic).

### YT-w9Zvk70uj5Q#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: outreach | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:53
claim: For a beginner at cold calling he expects about 1,000 calls per deal, and says that after 5,000 calls without one the answer is another 5,000.
detail: Progress is not binary; many skills (opening, getting past gatekeepers, holding a conversation) improve before the first sale. After one month his neighbour was already far better at openings.
numbers: About 1,000 cold calls per deal (beginner estimate).

### YT-w9Zvk70uj5Q#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:05
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: he told his young neighbor he could quit only if the whole wholesaling industry had disappeared; otherwise the missing piece was skill, gained through volume.
detail: Wholesaling real estate persists because buyer-seller inefficiencies persist. Half-built bridges come from swapping vehicles after learning two, three or four of the ten required skills.
### YT-w9Zvk70uj5Q#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:57
claim: An early lucky win can mislead you, since you still owe the usual number of no's afterwards.
detail: If the first call lands the one deal in many, you may think your method is special and then face a long run of no's. Increase the number of attempts rather than hunting for what you did differently.

### YT-w9Zvk70uj5Q#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:45
claim: Check expectations against time invested: a top 1% income after one month of a new skill is unreasonable, but after four years of steady practice one deal a month is reasonable.
detail: Wasted weeks from using a purchased system wrongly do not count toward the clock.
numbers: Example horizon: 4 years of full-time practice; first 3 weeks of wrong method wasted.
