### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Typical applicant: about $3M revenue, about $1M profit, Facebook ad spend of $50,000 that cannot rise without losing profitability, who plans new product lines instead of finding more customers in the current niche.
detail: Hormozi calls this a mistake: the first plateau means more ways to get customers have not been tried, and new lines only add complexity.
numbers: Revenue about $3M, profit about $1M, ad spend $50,000 (period not stated).

### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads, scaling-constraints | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Four lead levers, from broad to narrow: method (ads, content, outreach, referrals, affiliates), platform, media within the platform, and volume within that media.
detail: Check each before calling a market saturated. Instagram, for example, offers DMs, stories, posts and reels, each at some daily volume. To get an idea of market size, you could probably add up the ad spend of all agencies selling to the same niche on one platform.
applies_to: Owners who believe lead flow is capped.
### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:50
claim: Gym Launch had spoken to about 20,000 of roughly 50,000 US microgyms, through every channel, before widening to a new market.
detail: About 40% of the niche was reached, above the canon's 20-30% saturation rule, yet they expanded to health clubs.
numbers: 20,000 of 50,000 microgyms (about 40%).

### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#04 | EVOLUTION | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:50
claim: At a meetup of eight owners doing about $500M a year combined (he was at about $30M), the gap came from years in business and bigger markets, not intelligence or effort.
detail: Lessons: big things take time; and a niche eventually saturates, so market size matters.
numbers: About $500M combined revenue per year; his firm about $30M.

### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:46
claim: Pyramid of markets: five expansion directions: up market, down market, adjacent, broader and narrower.
detail: Up: fewer, larger buyers (franchisors, chains). Down: individuals, about 10 times more of them (stylists renting chairs rather than salon owners). Adjacent: similar owner and business (lash and nails). Broader: merge adjacent pyramids (all beauty). Narrower: a specialty (extensions); only this one shrinks the market.

### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:50
claim: To choose an expansion direction, ask which avatar we provide the most value to in dollars made.
detail: Gym Launch compared trainers, physical therapists, chiropractors and health clubs; health clubs won, gaining tens of thousands of dollars in month one and 2-3x bottom line via onboarding, sales, retention and upsell fixes.
numbers: Tens of thousands of dollars in month one; profit 2x to 3x.

### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:39
claim: Niching down has doubled, tripled or sometimes 5x'd revenue while cutting the target market by about 85%.
detail: Do it if the narrow avatar can make about 10x more money than the broad one; often, when you niche down, you can still make more money because you are not close to tapping all the methods, platforms and volume in the niche.
numbers: Revenue 2x to sometimes 5x with about 85% less market.
### YT-9gVdCR7W8o8#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: operator-mindset | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:40
claim: Before declaring saturation, work through the "should do" list (call leads faster, follow up, more content, cold calling, more platforms); he says you probably haven't done even one of the levers.
detail: Do one lever, then another, then all; building big takes time.
