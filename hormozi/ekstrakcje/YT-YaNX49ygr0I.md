### YT-YaNX49ygr0I#01 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:49
claim: At every decision point ask "what would this type of person do?" and use that question as a repeated refrain in place of a long checklist.
detail: Pick the person you want to become (a billionaire, a champion, a wise man) and ask the question when you don't know what to start. Each action is a vote for that identity, and the behaviour becomes effortless over time. He uses Solomon, Buffett and Munger depending on the situation, and puts the question on a note near the desk.

### YT-YaNX49ygr0I#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:54
claim: Winners and losers have the same goals; the difference is the activities they commit to, so committing to the activities is the goal.
detail: Writing goals alone means little. A strong month of revenue achieves nothing in itself, because the goal is the repeated activity. Three layers: the external result, the activities that produce it, and being the kind of person who does them. Fix the "being" and the habits follow.
numbers: Portfolio about $85M a year (context).

### YT-YaNX49ygr0I#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:24
claim: Affirmations fail without evidence; evidence comes from doing, and doing is what builds identity.
detail: Saying "I am a lion" does not make it true for a sane person. Identity is repeated beingness, so changing actions changes who you are, and the loop reinforces itself. If you do not know what the ideal person would do, lower the target (a millionaire, or someone trying to become one) and talk to such people.

### YT-YaNX49ygr0I#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:37
claim: Most people already know what to do, so the useful question is why they don't do it; people are reminded more than taught.
detail: Advertising means making your offer known, through private one-to-one outreach or public broadcast, and people know to do more of it yet don't. Extraordinary results come from doing the uncommonly obvious for an inordinately long time, because the doer does it for who they are, not for a finite outcome, and so does not ease off after one good month.

### YT-YaNX49ygr0I#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:21
claim: As you level up you change whom you compare yourself to, so you can always feel behind; the fix is to commit to activities, not to revenue milestones.
detail: He told a friend who was down after being overtaken financially to write down what a billionaire who lost it all would do rather than list goals.
