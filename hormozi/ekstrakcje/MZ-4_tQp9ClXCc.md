### MZ-4_tQp9ClXCc#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:47
claim: Start a channel when it has the highest risk-adjusted return, from expected upside, confidence and resources required.
detail: This applies to outbound, ads, content or affiliates at any revenue level.

### MZ-4_tQp9ClXCc#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:32
