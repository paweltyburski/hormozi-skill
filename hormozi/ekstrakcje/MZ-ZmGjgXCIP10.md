### MZ-ZmGjgXCIP10#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:59
claim: In a flood of AI content, the moat is reality: a real track record and reputation that cannot be copied.
detail: Even a teacher repeating word for word the advice of a famous investor would not get the same attention, because the original has the real business and decades of results. He says brand and reputation are the moat, and fiction is hit harder by AI than education.

### MZ-ZmGjgXCIP10#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:15
claim: The higher the risk of acting on content, the more credibility of the source matters.
detail: Plot content on a risk continuum: beauty tips are low risk, personal finance higher, business advice higher still because a wrong call can cost the business. Entertainment exists only to be consumed and will be hit harder than education, which aims to change behaviour. Authority also lowers the effort of consuming advice.

### MZ-ZmGjgXCIP10#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: Make only content that only you can make, such as live events with real owners and real stakes.
detail: He brings hundreds of owners with real problems (businesses from $500K to $10M a year) to in-person events, which AI avatars cannot replicate soon. Hard and scarce (many cameras, hard-to-get guests, real entrepreneurs) is the pillar of the host's own strategy too.
numbers: Hundreds of business owners per month fly in; businesses of $500K-$10M annual revenue.

### MZ-ZmGjgXCIP10#04 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:49
claim: Without proof of outcome, document proof of effort and clip the real stakes moments.
detail: A beginner in sales can record the whole day of selling and publish the best moments (for example, 22 calls compressed into minutes). Avoid commoditised tips and tricks with no proof, since viewers will ask why they should listen and go elsewhere within seconds.
numbers: Example: 22 calls in a 12-hour day compressed to 3 minutes; 14 years of advice compressed into 37 minutes.
