### POD-0171#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: When closing falls for an experienced seller, check conviction before drilling obstacle overcomes.
detail: A new Gym Launch client's close rate often rises, then drops as they see signups who fail. Skill is unchanged; belief erodes. The CLOSER framework is only a way to have the conversation; conviction drives it.

### POD-0171#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:11
claim: Keep conviction by selling against the alternatives: for this person, you are their best shot.
detail: No outcome is guaranteed because success depends on the person: stage of life, commitment, a supportive spouse or partner. Ask what her odds are with no sign-up, a plain gym or an online course; they are lower. Testimonials each morning also help; his trainers' sales soared after a "way-out day" watching people succeed.

### POD-0171#03 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:05
claim: A leads broker's rep called the leads "short"; Hormozi asked how his behaviour would change if every lead bought a house.
detail: The consulting job was scripting and motivating a team selling leads to mortgage brokers and realtors. The rep's belief had unsold the product; imagining the leads were hot, he said he would buy leads himself and send them to his realtor uncle. The script matters only through the delivering person's conviction.

### POD-0171#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:03
claim: Hormozi looks for conviction in salespeople; some of the best salespeople for you may be ex-clients with amazing results, since conviction can stand in for in-the-trenches experience.
detail: Counterweight it with not selling hot or overpromising.
### POD-0171#05 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:49
claim: Reclose after the sale: have the buyer ask "what if I want to cancel?" and answer that they cannot.
detail: Heard from a salesperson at "Loud the Room" (name unclear in audio). Steps: 1. have them ask the question; 2. say a year plan at a discounted rate cannot be cancelled; 3. only valid reason is moving 20 miles or more, and an online programme exists; 4. ask what situation they think would make them cancel; 5. say unpaid would go to collections, "you would not hold up your end?" Hormozi has never sent anyone to collections.
numbers: move of 20 miles or more is the only exception.

### POD-0171#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:24
claim: Pre-empt future cancel reasons (busy, money, travel) by comparing the commitment to other things they pay for.
detail: Busy and vacations will always recur; online covers moves. For money, ask what they spend on (clothes, eating out, nails, hair) and note they would not break those commitments either.

### POD-0171#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:09
claim: Coaching starts at the sale, not after it.
detail: Expectations and commitment are set in the sale. Do not think "I have to close this person"; think about her life and her best shot.

### POD-0171#08 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:19
claim: Sell by explaining why you personally believe, like recommending knee sleeves you use.
detail: You would not change how you talk or worry about price; you explain the same reason you believe.

### POD-0171#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:04
