claim: When a problem is too large to finish in a day, aim only to make some progress daily; it looks negligible at one day but material at 30 and 90.
detail: He cites an investor who lost about $4 billion in a fund while divorcing and coped by moving something forward every day. Maintaining counts as winning in some seasons.
numbers: Visible change at 30 days, material change at 90 days; about $4 billion loss (as recalled).

### MZ-9LSd0QE1w7Y#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:48
claim: Recalibrate the scale of what makes a day good so small events count, and let good moments expand into good weeks.
detail: He worries about running out of steam before his long-term goals, so he puts discretionary effort into making the ride enjoyable. He says a bad year is usually a few bad days replayed.
