### YT-NA61omfYgvI#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:20
claim: A brand can raise a shopper's confidence by demonstrating how to use the product, as Levi's did with a jean jacket.
detail: A video of about 90 seconds showed three ways to wear the jacket with dark, light and matching denim, so a buyer unsure about style saw how to reach the look. With physical products the outcome is shown by demonstration, whereas with skills you show someone using the skill and its results. Customer reviews do the same job.
numbers: ~90-second video, 3 outfit pairings.
applies_to: E-commerce and physical products

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#02 | TACTIC | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:01
claim: Unboxing and what-happens-next content lowers the risk of buying a physical product.
detail: Showing what arrives and what using it looks like sets expectations that the product then meets, which reduces fear at the moment of paying.
applies_to: E-commerce

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#03 | TACTIC | topic: verticals | also: product-delivery | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:01
claim: For physical products, time delay has two layers: shipping speed and speed of using the product.
detail: Amazon keeps pushing delivery faster (two-day, one-day, one-hour). A jacket has little delay in use, but for custom-tailored goods, faster production is real value and worth operationalising.
applies_to: E-commerce, made-to-order products

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#04 | TACTIC | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:58
claim: Cut purchase effort with one-click checkout using saved details.
detail: Express-checkout tools (such as Bolt and Shop Pay) make deciding and paying take a split second, which he credits with more sales for stores that use them.
applies_to: E-commerce

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:43
claim: E-commerce scarcity is stock counts per item and size; urgency is a short price window.
detail: Amazon shows "only two left" under items and by size. A limited drop (say 100 shirts, first come first served on Friday) is scarcity, and adding a one-hour or 60-minute deal makes it urgency, as in lightning deals.
numbers: 100-unit drop; 60-minute lightning deal.
applies_to: E-commerce

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: Implied Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:32
claim: Amazon's try-before-you-buy delays charging the card for seven days, acting as a second guarantee on top of easy returns.
detail: Money-back returns are now table stakes for physical goods; trying the item on before being billed lowers risk further, so the shopper is sure of the outcome before paying.
numbers: 7-day delayed billing.
applies_to: E-commerce

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#07 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: Bonus Checklist | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:23
claim: Bonuses for physical products are usage training: videos, tips and combinations.
detail: Examples are Levi's styling tips, kitchen-gadget how-to videos, and for supplements how to take them, what to combine them with, what time of day and how not to forget.
applies_to: Physical product brands

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#08 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: MAGIC Naming Formula | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:11
claim: Put the buyer's desired outcome into the product name, and name it with words people search for.
detail: A buyer wanting warmth responds to warm or fire words; someone wanting fashion to "fashion jacket" rather than plain "jacket". Amazon titles follow search terms.
applies_to: E-commerce

### YT-NA61omfYgvI#09 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:31
claim: Ask what job the product is being hired to do; he bets you may have different avatars attracted to different things, or the same avatar solving different needs.
detail: Naming, messaging and bonuses follow from that job. The same product can be framed for different avatars solving different problems.
