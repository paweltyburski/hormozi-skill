### YT-AH_R5cZNFFc#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Five S's of Advanced Compensation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:56
claim: For leaders in non-commission roles, Hormozi says his company usually splits variable pay about 50-50 between company results and personal objectives the person controls.
detail: Management by objectives, set by the company, with the employee accepting or negotiating a few terms. Personal half is checkbox items such as adding a channel, automating a process or hiring a manager, so it pays even if the business misses growth targets.
numbers: Example: $200,000 target earnings = $100,000 base + $100,000 variable; of the variable, $50,000 on company growth, profit or sales metrics, $50,000 on personal objectives.
### YT-AH_R5cZNFFc#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:20
claim: Let each employee set their own rolling 30-day goal, shown publicly, to unlock a variable slice of pay.
detail: A friend's system: every role has a variable share (10-50% by role). Employees pick a quantifiable monthly goal that must plainly support the department's goal. Public sharing creates social pressure that corrects sandbagging, because teammates challenge weak goals. Adds autonomy, variable reward and competition; useful for roles hard to tie to the bottom line.
numbers: Example: $50,000 salary with 15% variable = $7,500 a year, about $600 a month at stake. Hormozi had not implemented it yet.
applies_to: Support and non-sales roles.
