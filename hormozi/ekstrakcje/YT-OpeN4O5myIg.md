claim: A seven-day challenge emailed to a dormant list in a paid-ads consumer business produced $2.4M in a few days.
detail: Context: a nine-figure business with a 3.3M-email list that only got post-purchase follow-up, hit by weaker ads and a quarterly revenue gap.
