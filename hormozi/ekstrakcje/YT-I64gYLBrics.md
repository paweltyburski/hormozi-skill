### YT-I64gYLBrics#06 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:11:12
claim: Filter every initiative by three objectives: more customers, higher LTGP per customer, or lower risk that those two stop (raising enterprise value); drop anything that maps to none.
detail: If you can do only some, pick the highest likelihood for the least effort. Hiring a replacement for yourself probably adds neither customers nor LTGP but raises value.

### YT-I64gYLBrics#18 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:46:18
