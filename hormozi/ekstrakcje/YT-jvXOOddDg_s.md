detail: In 1992 he self-published 1,000 copies and promoted them on air. ### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:21
claim: An education business gains legitimacy from behaviour change, since money is about 80% behaviour and 20% knowledge.
detail: Teaching maths treats symptoms. The differentiator is systems that hold people accountable and encourage them when scared or prideful. When customers do not change, even through their own fault, the provider is judged illegitimate, as with a gym that produces no results. Critics are answered with: it works only if you work it.
numbers: Money split 80% behaviour, 20% head knowledge.

### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:35
claim: A trust brand must vet every endorsement, because one bad partner's damage exceeds any fee.
detail: Early on, advertisers that mistreated listeners forced him to cancel them. His acid test: would you send your sister, mother or friend there. He turned down two advertisers the same morning despite the money. If an endorsement is untrustworthy, the rest of the advice loses trust too. A mentor's line: never risk the empire for a pot of gold.

### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:28:57
claim: Live events have a physical capacity ceiling and cannot hockey-stick without pricing out the average customer.
detail: Limits are bodies on stage, setup crews and cities with crowds. If events grow 5% while another line grows 50%, events shrink as a share of the whole. Streaming could extend reach but monetises poorly.
numbers: Example: 5% growth vs 50% for another line.

### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#12 | DECISION_RULE | topic: product-delivery | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:30:13
claim: Dave Ramsey (not Hormozi) says the answer is probably more digital delivery because it is easiest to scale, lowest cost of goods and iterates more easily; betting everything on one delivery mechanism is dumb in his view.
detail: Ramsey says change is so fast that social media platforms become irrelevant quickly. He says he has only so many physical coaches for small-business teaching, so he wants a way that is less manpower hungry. He says Ramsey Solutions is platform and delivery agnostic: the aim is to get the principles to people with the least friction.
### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#13 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:31:52
claim: A budgeting app launched with major effort grew steadily for years, then took off when consumers started responding to the word "budget" again.
detail: Policy: when a product suddenly grows out of proportion to effort, pour gas on it ("join the parade"). He attributes it to the macro environment and to smartphone-native adults reaching budgeting age; no one hand-writes a budget now.

### YT-jvXOOddDg_s#20 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:52:06
claim: Leverage maths in business and real estate ignores risk; adjust returns for risk the way investing adjusts for beta.
detail: "Borrow at 2%, earn 8%, make 6%" omits inflation, taxes and risk. Debt equals risk. Funds are compared after volatility adjustment, but private businesses and real estate are not. Without risk adjustment unlimited leverage looks rational. Hormozi says his deals so far were all cash.
numbers: Example: $12M debt on $10M revenue vs $2M debt; beta 0.8 vs 2.0 vs market 1.0.
