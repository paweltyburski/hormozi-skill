### MZ-xBFrYHCk8-w#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Building a following that does not raise cash flow is fake work; an amazing product gets the personal brand by default.
detail: If you care about attention you can do it later. A personal brand can itself be a media company with cash flow, but media chasing justified as marketing, without money results, is ineffective.

### MZ-xBFrYHCk8-w#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: If your media earns more than your original business, the media may be your real business; keep reassessing what the market wants versus what you offer.
detail: He frames it as firm on the goal, flexible on the path. He says he hated being on camera and would not do it unpaid.

### MZ-xBFrYHCk8-w#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:52
claim: The proof an influencer needs depends on the risk the audience takes in acting; a business influencer needs evidence of business results.
detail: Lipstick advice carries low risk, personal finance more. A teacher repeating Buffett's advice will not become Buffett because she did not build Berkshire.

### MZ-xBFrYHCk8-w#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: content-brand | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:52
claim: His podcast (started July 2017 as Gym Secrets, later The Game) grew from about 2,000 downloads a month to millions only after he sold his company for $46.2 million.
detail: Audiences then believed he knew what he was talking about; the brand took off because he had evidence.
numbers: 2,000 downloads a month to millions; exit $46.2M.
