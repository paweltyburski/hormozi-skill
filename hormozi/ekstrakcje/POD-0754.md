### POD-0754#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:37

### POD-0754#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:09
claim: Unclear speaker: culture is the spoken and unspoken rules about what is rewarded and what is punished; values are umbrella words for many small behaviours.
detail: Newcomers learn the real rules quickly (lateness unpunished, cursing frowned on). To change culture, observe behaviour against the stated values. Feedback works when it names behaviours, not a label: not "stop being a jerk" but "stop interrupting and dismissing ideas"; when those stop, the label goes.
