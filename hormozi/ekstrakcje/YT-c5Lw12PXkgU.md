claim: He lost about $200,000 by selling a gaining holding within a year, then watching it return to the sale price.
detail: The loss came from short-term gains taxed as ordinary income, while simply holding would have left more. The amount sat inside the 3-5% of his assets reserved for play; about 97% sits in stable holdings.
numbers: roughly $200,000 lost (title says $217,000); 3-5% of assets for speculation, about 97% stable.

### YT-c5Lw12PXkgU#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:49
claim: To match what a long-term hold keeps after tax, a short-term trade must gain about 45% more.
detail: Worked US example: $100 doubled to $200. Taxed as ordinary income at about 43-45%, that leaves about $155; taxed as a long-term capital gain it leaves $180. To net $180 through ordinary income tax the $100 must grow to about $245. He adds that most traders lose money anyway (he guesses about 75%). US tax rules; check your own jurisdiction.
numbers: $155 vs $180 after tax on a $100 gain; $245 gross needed to net $180; tax rate about 43-45%.
applies_to: US investors.
