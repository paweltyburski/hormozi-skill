### POD-251114-04#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Offering the same 190 buyers a $10,000 offer instead of a $100 one would make advertising about 9.5 times more profitable.
detail: The $100 sale earns $50 profit, so 190 sales give $9,500. The $10,000 sale earns $9,500 profit; if about 5% of the 190 buy, that is 9.5 sales and $90,250, with about 10 clients to serve.
numbers: 190 x $50 = $9,500 versus 9.5 x $9,500 = $90,250. The 5% close rate is his assumption.

### POD-251114-04#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: pricing | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:43
claim: If he had to start over, Hormozi would not start with a premium offer; he would add a free or discount front end, which can be removed as reputation improves (essential for most at the beginning).
detail: Premium needs something very valuable plus a sales process that demonstrates the value. It also works as the second offer after a free one, if the first showed value.

### POD-251114-04#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:24
claim: A premium-only model has simple maths and better customers: fewer moving parts, less operational drag, higher lead quality.
detail: With no incentive, leads arrive expecting a sale, so the decision rests on belief and perceived value. You may sell a tenth of the volume.

### POD-251114-04#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:26
claim: Premium has the highest cost per lead and slowest break-even, leaving little room for error.
detail: Start only with a proven sales process or cash set aside to burn while learning.

### POD-251114-04#07 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:41
claim: Premium copy needs avatar-level specificity, while free or discount forgives weaker copy.
detail: Example: write "cleaning the bathrooms yet again" instead of "working hard in your business". Without knowing the avatar's fears and daily struggles, a premium offer gets little response.

### POD-251114-04#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:57
claim: A straight premium offer is the least efficient way to capture a market: ads reach many people for few buyers.
detail: Each result is worth more, but you will not lead on volume; treat that as a strategic choice.
