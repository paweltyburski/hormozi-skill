### MZ-blwsNBKPvmw#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Every position carries a built-in advantage, so limits come from resourcefulness, not resources.
detail: Younger means cutting edge, older means experience, small means personal, big means track record, rich means resources, broke means nothing to lose. Simplicity beats complexity in all of them.

### MZ-blwsNBKPvmw#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Use inversion: list everything that would guarantee failure, then do the opposite.
detail: People find threats more easily than gratitude, so the failure list is long and easy to write. Flip each item into an action.

### MZ-blwsNBKPvmw#03 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:41
claim: Imagine a version of yourself, with a mustache, who knows all your weaknesses and is trying to beat you; do what that person would do.
detail: Equivalent to asking what you would do with ten times the agency, since that person would be decisive and not doubt themselves.
