### MZ-DMVBwhLG30U#01 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Small businesses lack a data layer; centralising copy, testimonials, videos and collateral lets AI assemble ads and matching landing pages.
detail: Store all sales copy, testimonials, video and collateral in one repository, then have it pieced into ads in real time and paired with dynamic landing pages. Anything unique, including useful AI, starts with owned data.
numbers: Several hundred landing pages are pushed against several hundred ads at Acquisition.com, generated programmatically.

### MZ-DMVBwhLG30U#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:56
claim: One young editor can run a clip operation: AI finds highlights from long talks, a human checks quality, CTAs are inserted automatically.
detail: The editor feeds long recordings through a highlight tool, approves the output and publishes. Clips are split into mid-length cuts and shorts.
numbers: About 5 mid clips (3 to 10 minutes) and roughly 15 shorts per day, by one person.

### MZ-DMVBwhLG30U#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:30
claim: Hormozi's own philosophy for clipping and media: he wants three agencies working for him plus an in-house team rather than picking one model.
detail: His reasoning is that all the impressions are out there and he wants to catch them all; he agreed this was his philosophy when the questioner asked about in-house, agency or a CPM-paid Discord.
### MZ-DMVBwhLG30U#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:15
claim: E-commerce is the most useful place to watch paid-ad trends; winners share one decentralised playbook.
detail: The playbook is many creators' UGC, an AI back end that screens it for control, then heavy volume.
numbers: Companies on this playbook go from zero to $100M+ in roughly 12 to 24 months.
