### MZ-KIs-egpg1bg#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: outreach | also: content-brand, paid-ads | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The bigger the ticket and the narrower the target, the more cold outreach fits; broad low-LTV markets suit content and ads.
detail: A rule of thumb for best customers is about $10,000 or more. Low-priced self-serve software needs huge volume and is inefficient to hunt. M&A or private-equity targets are all outbound.
numbers: $10,000+ per customer as outreach threshold.

### MZ-KIs-egpg1bg#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:29
claim: Keep a minimum viable organic channel (three relevant posts a week on one platform) so prospects who check your profile after cold outreach see life.
detail: Organic has two levels: MVP nurture (proof you are alive) and organic as the main acquisition, which is a different investment. Link only the one platform you are active on.
numbers: 3 posts per week.
