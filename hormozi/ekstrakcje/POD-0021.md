### POD-0021#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:24
claim: Agreement that the result is worth the price is easy in B2B but must be actively built in emotional sales.
detail: He corrects his earlier statement that prospects usually say yes. In B2B, value ties to bottom-line return, so the worth question is clear-cut while conviction is the hard part. In emotional sales such as weight loss, the value must be built before asking.

### POD-0021#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:16
claim: Ask "if we were talking six months from now, what would have had to happen for you to be really happy?"
detail: Ask what they want first, then enlarge the picture into an emotional scene. The answer shows what they need to see to find it valuable.

### POD-0021#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:02
claim: Dig for the real reason with repeated "why", softened to "tell me more about that".
detail: Start from the stated goal (lose 30 pounds), then ask what it affects: being a good mother or wife, work. He avoids saying "why" every time because it annoys people, and keeps going until the main emotional driver appears.
numbers: "seven layers of why".

### POD-0021#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:29
claim: Value-paint the long horizon: permanent results across decades, not a quick 10 pounds.
detail: Ask what it is worth to be at the weight at 80 with mobility, energy, better sleep and kinder to a spouse, never regaining. Contrast with 10 pounds that water weight can erase. He warns you must be able to deliver permanence.

### POD-0021#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:34
claim: Anchor to their high-school weight, then ask for improvement rather than maintenance.
detail: Ask what they weighed in high school; if they say they would be happy at 150, ask what 130 and toned would be worth. Then "not maintain, but improve" and habits that carry into other life areas ("how you do one thing is how you do everything").
numbers: example: 150 versus 130 pounds.

### POD-0021#06 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:06
claim: "Throwing stones" challenges past dieting, then offers a changed life story.
detail: Steps: grant that crash diets work briefly; ask if they have dieted before (yes); ask why they are here (it came back); ask if they want their story to be in shape a few times for months; then paint the story "that was the day everything changed" for marriage, work, motherhood; ask what that is worth.

### POD-0021#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:00
claim: In weight loss you are selling a new identity, built from long-term results, permanence, habits and spillover.
detail: Stack value by talking long term, permanence, habits and other areas of life, framed as building their story.
