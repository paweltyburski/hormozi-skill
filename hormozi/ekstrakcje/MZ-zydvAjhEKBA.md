claim: A New Zealand coaching company has outgrown leader bonuses set at fixed targets that everyone now hits.
detail: Pay is monthly, by department, based on total sales, churn and booked first sales calls (triages). They had been in place six to 12 months and the owners do not want to renegotiate and outgrow them again.

### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Five S's of Advanced Compensation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:39
claim: Top leaders get a share of a profit pool, middle tiers get salary plus bonus bands scaled to experience.
detail: Pool around 10% of profits, e.g. 1% each to CMO, COO and CFO, with the unfilled remainder unallocated until seats are filled. Mid-to-high leaders: bonus up to 20%-100% of salary (lower for less experienced). Next level: 10%-35% of salary.
numbers: Pool 10% of profit; bonus 20%-100% and 10%-35% of salary.

### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:17
claim: Split leader bonuses roughly 60% company performance and 40% individual performance (range 40-60 either way).
detail: Example: $100,000 base, 20% bonus = $20,000, of which $12,000 is company and $8,000 individual. This rewards a strong individual in a weak year and vice versa.
numbers: 60/40 split; $12,000 + $8,000.

### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:07
claim: Company metrics for leaders are probably two to three; revenue and profit work, but his favorite is revenue, profit and growth.
detail: Without growth, a flat year would still pay bonuses.
### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:53
claim: In fast growth, tie pay to quarterly objectives instead of KPIs until metrics have a reliable baseline.
detail: A portfolio company going from $45M to $75M to about $115M kept changing its KPIs.
numbers: $45M to $75M to ~$115M.

### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:40
claim: Avoid purely individual metrics, and pair each with a counter-metric of quality.
detail: Individual-only targets kill teamwork (a marketer maximised booked calls). Examples: sales net of cancellations in four weeks, cleans versus re-cleans, tickets closed versus average rating. No metric is perfect.
numbers: Cancellations within 4 weeks deducted from sales.

### MZ-zydvAjhEKBA#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:39
claim: Review compensation annually and expect to redo a plan within three or four quarters in a fast-growing firm.
detail: Separate leader tracks from individual contributor tracks; both can have bonuses.
numbers: Review annually; outgrown in 3-4 quarters.
