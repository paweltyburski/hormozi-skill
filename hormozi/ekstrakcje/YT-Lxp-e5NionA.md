### YT-Lxp-e5NionA#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:46
claim: The customer pipeline has an internal mirror with five stages: application generation, application nurture, interview-as-sale, training, and retention plus ascension of talent.
detail: Borrowed from an external five-function model (lead generation, nurture to show, sales, fulfilment, retention and ascension). Internally: ads and outbound to get applicants; a structured follow-up so they book and attend; an interview run as a sale with a script that also indoctrinates values and disqualifies misfits; training as fulfilment; KPIs, pay and titles as retention and ascension. Applying the same six traffic sources (paid, earned, owned, outbound, affiliates, word of mouth) to applicants is what let the company scale hiring.

### YT-Lxp-e5NionA#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: outreach | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:37
claim: High-quality candidates find a job within about eight days, so slow first contact with applicants loses them.
detail: A six-day delay before the first contact is common, while the same firms chase sales leads hard. Treat applications with the same structured nurture as leads, aiming at a scheduled and attended interview.
numbers: 10 of 10 high-quality candidates employed within 8 days; 6 days to first contact is too slow.
