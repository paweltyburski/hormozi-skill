### POD-0267#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: "What got you here won't get you there" is mostly false for tactics: do more of the winning actions at larger scale; what must change is identity, beliefs and structure.
detail: Switching to something new because the old action "worked okay" just stops repeating success. Example: if sales works with one salesperson, do it with ten. Real shifts apply at inflection points such as changing business model or opportunity.

### POD-0267#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: lead-getters | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Each Gym Launch client refers about 0.4 new clients.
detail: Results marketing plus a product that delivers results produces word of mouth and easier sales, and returns more per marketing dollar.
numbers: 0.4 referred clients per client.
### POD-0267#03 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:10
claim: Across his gyms, turnaround trips and Gym Launch, the same actions drove growth: market results, sell well, deliver a product that works.
detail: He flew out to turn around gyms, promising to fill each gym to capacity within three weeks, and grew nearly 100% through word of mouth. Then he moved to a consultative model to reach more gyms: different vehicle and different beliefs, same actions.
numbers: 33 gyms turned around over almost 2 years; promise of 100 people in 21 days.

### POD-0267#04 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:21
claim: A $4M-a-year weight-loss business felt the market was capped, but only its single acquisition channel was; the fix was more of the same or another channel.
detail: The owner considered starting a second business for the same customers instead.
numbers: About $4M a year revenue.
applies_to: Single-channel businesses.

### POD-0267#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:30
claim: There are two content strategies: minimum content to look legitimate to people reaching you via ads, or content as your main acquisition, which demands far more volume.
detail: As main acquisition he says you need roughly 10 platforms, daily posting, often several times a day: a daily YouTube video, 3 to 5 blog articles a week, and podcasts at least 2 to 3 times a week and realistically daily. For the content-as-main-channel case, the rule is "more, not different".
numbers: About 10 platforms; 3-5 articles/week; podcasts 2-3 times/week minimum.
### POD-0267#06 | EVOLUTION | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:20
claim: In 2020 he thinks one of his mistakes was focusing a little more on content, although about 95% of sales came from advertising or word of mouth.
detail: He deleted Facebook and Instagram from his phone and returned to focusing on world-class marketing, sales and product.
