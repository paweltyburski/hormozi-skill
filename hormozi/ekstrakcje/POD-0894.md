### POD-0894#24 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: Blended ROAS | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:45:35
claim: A DTC skincare brand ($10M, 15% margin, aiming at $100M) is trapped in a direct-response loop and must build a brand.
detail: White-label gadgets and consumables sold by performance marketing; scaling raises CPA and compresses margin as ads move to weaker audiences. Hormozi was stuck there 3.5 years. Without differentiation the company has no sellable asset; buyers do not want arbitrage businesses.

applies_to: E-commerce and physical products.
