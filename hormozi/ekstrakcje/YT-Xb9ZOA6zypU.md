### YT-Xb9ZOA6zypU#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Sales to Fulfillment Continuum | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:43
claim: The 2016 done-for-you gym launch offer was to fill a gym in 30 days for free: Hormozi paid ads and travel and kept the upfront cash, the owner kept the contract value.
detail: He and his wife flew in and sat at the front desk. The owner paid a refundable $500 deposit to reserve the date. He named it "gym launch" because owners did not want to be "rescued". Each visit sold about 200 memberships in 21 days.
numbers: About 200 sales in 21 days, roughly 10 a day; $500 refundable deposit; sold at around $500 a sale.

### YT-Xb9ZOA6zypU#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: Sales to Fulfillment Continuum | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:16:59
claim: Selling a service that someone else fulfils produced a 35% refund rate; control the customer relationship and fulfilment end to end.
detail: After he left each site, two owners told members to cancel and sign up directly for less; one month a $100,000 refund hit wiped out savings. Margin was only about 20% since flights, hotels, commissions and ads were already paid, so he had to sell more every month to cover earlier refunds, a death trap. Cause: no relationship with the customer and no control of delivery.
numbers: 35% of sales refunded; margin about 20%; one $100,000 refund hit; sold 280,000 in one month to cover refunds.
