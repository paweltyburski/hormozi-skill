### POD-0824#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:32
claim: Content volume is an accordion: raise quantity, then tighten to fewer, better pieces to see if views hold or grow, then expand again.
detail: Example: from 2 shorts a day to 5, then 8; views grow, but about one in 50 shorts gets roughly 100 times the views, so he asks whether fewer, better pieces would do as well, then lifts volume again. It is a dichotomy to manage, not a problem to solve.

### POD-0824#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:23
claim: Maximise quantity times quality: halve volume only if quality more than doubles, and double volume if quality holds.
detail: Resources (time, money, team) are limited, so treat it as Q1 x Q2 and pick whichever lever lifts the product more. The ideal is as much top-quality output as possible.

### POD-0824#07 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:22
claim: Batch short clips: record about 100 in one day every 90 days and publish one new plus one repurposed daily.
detail: A vendor first cut clips from existing episodes at no extra work for him; later a dedicated day was added. Clips yielded roughly two posts a day. A later editor who actually edited and sequenced videos made them perform significantly better than simply trimming.
numbers: 100 clips per session; one session every 90 days; about 2 posts per day.

### POD-0824#13 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:19:53

detail: Adapted from Google's planning rule. Core is narrow-and-wide business content. A moonshot is about one video in ten, such as a tour of his office gym, to show expertise in another domain.
