### POD-251114-22#01 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: retention-ltv | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:55
claim: A gym franchise's opener would not launch a location unless 400 recurring members signed before opening, using a founding-member lifetime discount.
detail: Ads offered a 14-day trial leading to a lifetime membership discount, available only before opening. Trial leavers were few and members stayed to keep the rate.
numbers: 80% stay after trial; 500 trials give 400 billed on day 15; profitable in month one.

### POD-251114-22#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: promotions-launches | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:47
claim: A lifetime discount only works if you actually charge more when the offer ends, and Hormozi says adding urgency, scarcity and believable reasons makes it more attractive.
detail: Otherwise you just list a price and pretend it's a discount. Make sure you still make a profit after the discount.
### POD-251114-22#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:40
claim: Hormozi prefers lifetime discounts as upsells: a rollover discount is kept only if the credited payments are completed.
detail: It works like price protection while they keep paying.

### POD-251114-22#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:25
claim: Sample lifetime-discount offers with their stated reason.
detail: Local service for a new location; software early access because it will have bugs; supplement flavour tester for feedback; or a loyalty discount after five months with no urgency or scarcity.
numbers: $400 to $200 (50% off); $39 to $19; $19.99 to $14.99; $5 off per month after five months.

### POD-251114-22#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:23
claim: Percentage-off or dollar-off lifetime discounts are far more flexible than a fixed price, and Hormozi personally limits price protection to a fixed period rather than forever.
detail: Costs change; with percentage or dollar-off discounts you can adjust retail and customers still keep their discount. If offering a fixed price for life, know your numbers.
numbers: Example: $50 normal, $20 for the next 36 months.
### POD-251114-22#06 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: - | fw: Urgency | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:10
claim: Use real good or bad events as reasons for the discount: surprise cost, birthday, damaged goods, beta testers, new flavours.
detail: Never offer the same one-time deal twice; vary contents instead, and never quote two prices for the same thing at once.

### POD-251114-22#07 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:03
claim: Give a steep forever founder discount on one service provided they pay retail for a complementary one.
detail: For example, meal planning cheaply with meal prep at full price. This beats two mid-priced offers or a flat 25% off both.
numbers: Compared with a generic 25% off.

### POD-251114-22#08 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:03
claim: A cancelled customer returning does not get the lost discount; offer a down version at the same price instead, with a buy-down option.
detail: Reminding leavers they lose the discount saves some. A buy-down lets them pay the difference of an upfront payment to lock the lower monthly rate for life.

### POD-251114-22#09 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:36
claim: A tanning-salon mentor found the membership with the biggest upfront payment had the longest retention.
detail: A $100 sign-up that cut the rate from $18 to $10 kept customers, who even phoned before changing cars. A waived $100 initiation fee for a year commitment worked similarly.
numbers: $100 initiation fee; $18 to $10 per month.

### POD-251114-22#10 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: Waived Fee Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:23
claim: A large upfront payment with smaller scheduled ones is likelier to be completed than the reverse, because of sunk cost.
detail: $1,000 now then five payments of $100 beats $100 monthly with $1,000 at the end. Remind leavers of the cash and rate they forfeit close to cancellation.
