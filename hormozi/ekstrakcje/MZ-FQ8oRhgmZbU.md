### MZ-FQ8oRhgmZbU#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:25
claim: A PR agency with three- and six-month recurring plans wants quarterly prepayment and asks for bonuses rather than discounts.
detail: The plans each guarantee about 20 pitches a month and one placed article; the owner wants to raise LTV to CAC.
numbers: Three months at $1,200 per month; six months at $997 per month; guarantee 20 pitches per month plus one article.

### MZ-FQ8oRhgmZbU#02 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:25
claim: Guarantee only the pitches normally, and add the article guarantee for people who prepay.
detail: The reason offered to the client is that you are committing resources and relational capital, so you need to know they are serious.

### MZ-FQ8oRhgmZbU#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:09
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: for her PR agency, offer an extra article as the prepay bonus rather than a free month, since he said a month has no value and clients want articles.
detail: He said they'll probably want the articles, so give an extra article for prepaying.
### MZ-FQ8oRhgmZbU#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:07
claim: Do not retro-change terms for existing clients; offer them an extra higher-level deliverable for prepaying.
detail: Past sales stay as sold. Roughly 20% may take the prepay deal; new customers over time take a larger share.
numbers: About 20% of existing clients might upgrade.
