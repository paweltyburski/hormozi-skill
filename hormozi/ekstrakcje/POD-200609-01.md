claim: In Hormozi's own view ('at least how I saw it'), entrepreneurial growth repeats a three-step loop at each level: acquire skills, then character traits, then challenge beliefs about the opportunity vehicle.

### POD-200609-01#07 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals, offers | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:24:19
claim: Quoting the highest price he could think of to discourage owners backfired: they said yes, and the training became Gym Launch.
detail: He told eight booked gym owners he would stop launching gyms for them and teach them instead, hoping the price would put them off. First quote $6,000, then $8,000, and every one agreed, for about $60,000 in a day. He built the training over a weekend and all eight made about $40,000 in month one. He read it as the same skills in a better vehicle, and the sale was easy because they were already in debt and needed clients.
numbers: Prices quoted $6,000 and $8,000; $60,000 collected in one day; each gym about $40,000 in first month (March 2017).

### POD-200609-01#10 | CASE | topic: product-delivery | also: verticals, pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:29:56
claim: During the COVID shutdown he told gym owners to sell remote one-on-one accountability, keeping group-class prices but changing what is delivered.
detail: He had warned months before of a recession and an event that would pop brick-and-mortar group training, and urged adding remote services. His reasoning: you cannot out-compete Beachbody on online workouts or free diet plans, so the product is making sure the client does the work. The same $150 a month buys one-on-one instead of group. Owners converted existing clients, usually keeping about 90%.
numbers: Retention on conversion about 90%; $150/month group becomes $150/month one-on-one.

### POD-200609-01#17 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 01:09:26
claim: Faster route to first revenue: attach a phone call to the funnel instead of selling through it.
detail: Run a lead ad that captures name, phone and email, then call each lead promptly and ask what made them click. Getting contact details is far easier than a card payment through a page, and phone sales can be learned to competence in weeks. He says phone attachment yields 10 to 20 times the cash of funnel-only selling, works as a safety net for weak marketing, and lets you charge about four to five times the price of a page-only sale. Page-only selling requires a low price.
numbers: Phone vs funnel-only revenue 10x-20x; price multiple 4-5x; skill proficiency in weeks.
