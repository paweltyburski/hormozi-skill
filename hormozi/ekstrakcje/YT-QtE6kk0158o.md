### YT-QtE6kk0158o#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent, sales-team | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A goal of six more outbound reps was set and missed two quarters running; each rep produces about $2M a year, so the miss cost $10M-$12M in annual revenue.
detail: Hormozi had been pressuring the sales manager, who was not the constraint. His lesson: speed comes not from frenzy but from knowing which problems to solve in which order. Instead the company gained roughly one quarter of the benefit, about $2M-$2.5M.
numbers: ~$2M a year per outbound rep; 6 reps ~$12M; ~$2-2.5M gained.

### YT-QtE6kk0158o#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:41
claim: The hiring funnel math: 1 qualified candidate a week, 5 interviews per qualified candidate and 4 final interviews per hire meant about 20 candidates per hire, against 20% quarterly rep churn.
detail: With 10 outbound reps, 20% churn loses 2 a quarter; a process yielding about 1 new rep a month nets only one extra per quarter, far short of 6. Front-line roles churn more than managers.
numbers: 5 interviews per qualified candidate; 4 final interviews per hire; ~20 per hire; outbound rep churn ~20% per quarter.

### YT-QtE6kk0158o#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:14
claim: Fix: the sales manager runs about five group interviews of one hour each per week and picks one or two to onboard, instead of one-on-one interviews that were only used through a miscommunication.
detail: HR still runs the ads and schedules the interviews. The earlier focus was on the manager's training and candidate choice, not on the talent acquisition process.
numbers: 5 group interviews, 1 hour each, per week.

### YT-QtE6kk0158o#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: content-brand, retention-ltv | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:05
claim: The quad marketing calendar has four audiences: employees (vision and mission, internal), customers (rebuy, internal), prospects (acquire customers, external) and talent (acquire employees, external).
detail: Most owners run only the prospect calendar; the one missed most is acquiring talent. Hormozi says his HR director's lack of this framework cost the $10-12M.

### YT-QtE6kk0158o#05 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:42
claim: Run hiring as a parallel of the customer funnel: ads, leads, working leads, interviews as sales, job offers like offers, onboarding like customer onboarding, ascension like resale.
detail: The employee path mirrors the customer path stage by stage.
