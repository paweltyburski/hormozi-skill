### MZ-pO3vMSyYOhY#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals, unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A jazz online-course seller had founder bottleneck, low retention and only low-ticket offers.
detail: He runs paid ads, creative and email himself. Courses sell via $29 Meta ads and email promotions; discounts bring membership average to $24.
numbers: Revenue $1.36M, goal $5M; ads $30,000 a month; courses $29 to $197; membership $39/month or $390/year; yearly revenue retention 38%; about 300 transactions a month.

### MZ-pO3vMSyYOhY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:44
claim: Front ends sold as loss leaders cannot carry the business; the missing piece is a back end.
detail: Like a grocery store selling diapers at no profit: sell the other items too. Back end could be $3,000 to $8,000.
numbers: Back end $3,000 to $8,000.

### MZ-pO3vMSyYOhY#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: money-models | also: sales-team | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:11
claim: If 20 to 30% buy a $4,000 back end, average sale rises by about $800 conservatively, and two closers could add over $300,000 a month.
detail: Ten transactions a day with a 30% close at $4,000 gives about $12,000 daily; he said this takes the business "straight to five" without finishing the unit. Needs bridging language between product one and two.
numbers: +$800 per average sale; $12,000/day; $300,000+/month; 10 buyers/day; 2 closers.
