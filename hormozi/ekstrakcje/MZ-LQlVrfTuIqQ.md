### MZ-LQlVrfTuIqQ#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Almost nothing in a business is urgent; the one real emergency is losing the ability to transact.
detail: Ask a caller "is this an emergency?" (if yes, call emergency services; if not, why are you calling me). A trainer phoning at 5am about a leak should call a plumber. Even a payment outage costs only a half-day of sales.

### MZ-LQlVrfTuIqQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:14
claim: Train every new hire on how you respond: a thumbs-up or "OK" only means "heard, understood".
detail: Setting this expectation lets him ignore most messages without offending anyone.

### MZ-LQlVrfTuIqQ#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:03
claim: Ask of any report or data: what will this change about what I do?
detail: If a figure moving would not change behaviour, there is no point reporting it. He says this cuts about 95% of communication.
numbers: ~95% of communication removed.

### MZ-LQlVrfTuIqQ#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:30
claim: Focus is measured by the quantity and quality of what you say no to.
detail: Steve Jobs asked executives what they had said no to and wanted a really attractive idea, not a token.

### MZ-LQlVrfTuIqQ#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:17
claim: Find the big domino: the one thing which, if true, makes everything else irrelevant.
detail: His example four years ago was to build the biggest business brand, so he needn't be the best deal picker or negotiator because deals would come to him. He thinks most businesses don't know theirs and do many small things.
