### MZ-724tsFdUeiQ#01 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: verticals, paid-ads | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:56
claim: A golf-cart seller doing $4M with mostly referrals and about 3,000 followers needs an offer and local advertising to fill growing inventory.
detail: He closes everything himself, about two conversations a day, with a few sales staff and several stores (store count unclear in audio). About 20% of leads come from content. Hormozi says local ads are easiest but harder to scale; the product is high ticket and fun to show.
numbers: Revenue $4M, target $8M; inventory grew from 3-4 carts to about 100; content produces about 20% of leads.

### MZ-724tsFdUeiQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: paid-ads | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:15
claim: Giveaway the top cart with the price shown, so every opt-in knows the cost and wants the top item, then qualify heavily at opt-in.
detail: Run the best organic cart content as an ad in a 50-mile radius. Second prize about $2,000 off, "more than one winner may be selected", and a thank-you page video offering to skip the line by talking to a rep. Collect income and address, run a seven-day countdown, give 72 hours to claim then call everyone. He expects over 100 carts sold.
numbers: 30-day drawing; 72-hour claim window; second prize $1,000-$2,000 off; radius 50 miles; more than 100 carts expected.
