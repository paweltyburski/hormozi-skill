### POD-0618#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:33:24
claim: Quality has an asymptote: going from 5 to 7 takes about twice the work, from 7 to 9 about 100 times, and the top gap pays disproportionately.
detail: In relative terms the returns are exponential: first versus second place can be Uber against Lyft, ten times the size. A 9.5 rated book versus a 9.9 can be the difference between a decent income and a book you can live off. Working 100x harder can give a million-fold payoff at the top end.
numbers: 5 to 7: ~2x work; 7 to 9: ~100x work.
