### MZ-bz_oMbt9O6c#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A window tint, paint protection and wrap shop at $2.2M with 50% margin, two 50-50 owners, wants to copy-paste locations toward $100M.
detail: The 50% margin is likely inflated because both owners work as general managers. Hormozi's warning: owners split across two or three sites each underperform, new site peaks lower, old site slips, and profit stays flat while liabilities double.
numbers: Revenue $2.2M; margin 50%, target 70% (from Ed's method); one store; manager in place.

### MZ-bz_oMbt9O6c#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:16
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: before opening a second location, his litmus test is that the first can grow without the owners for six months.
detail: A named general manager is treated as an owner, with the shop identified as theirs. Nailing one location gives options: more shops, buy and flip failing ones, license or franchise.
numbers: Six months without owners.
### MZ-bz_oMbt9O6c#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:37
claim: Offer the GM a profit share on one condition: the owner's phone does not ring.
detail: Role-play a ceiling leak; any answer but "call me" is right. If the phone rings, the owner wants the money back.

### MZ-bz_oMbt9O6c#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:37
claim: List the hundred small things the owners do as observable behaviors, then assign them to daily tasks of the manager and team.
detail: A bad first location is expensive: cash flow shrinks and a wrong market can trap you for years, so stack cash rather than rush.
