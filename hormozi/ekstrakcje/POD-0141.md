### POD-0141#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: In gym software reports, fulfilment cost per member was about the same across small, medium, large and leader facilities, while price differed.
detail: Cost was about $80-90 a month in all tiers. Rough prices: small about $109, medium about $116, large about $124 a month. Profit per member was about $24, $35, $45, a roughly 50% rise from the smallest to the larger tiers.
numbers: Cost $80-90/member/month; price $109 / $116 / $124; profit about $24 / $35 / $45.

### POD-0141#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:10
claim: Leader gyms charged about 50% more than others, $150-170 a month against about $110, and took home three to four times as much, with little discounting.
detail: Gyms that struggled most discounted constantly. Gym Launch advises $167-$212 a month ($39-$49 a week). An average of $150 plus means some are much higher.
numbers: Leaders $150-170/month vs about $110; 3-4x take-home; Gym Launch range $167-$212/month.
### POD-0141#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:18

detail: Owners complain customers pay too little though they set the price.
numbers: $120 to $150 with $90 cost: profit from $30 to $60 per member per month.

### POD-0141#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:04
