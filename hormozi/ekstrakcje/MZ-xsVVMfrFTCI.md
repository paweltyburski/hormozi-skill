### MZ-xsVVMfrFTCI#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:27
claim: Show what a skill can do, not how to do it, and make each demonstration a lead magnet for a different avatar.
detail: Show a finished build and its result; viewers ask how, and the call to action points to the community. Build a matrix of functions by business use cases and keep any how-to to about 60 seconds.
numbers: Build walkthrough capped near 60 seconds.
