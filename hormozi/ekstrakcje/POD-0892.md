numbers: 5 schools; 150-200 students each; ~$300/month average; instructor earnings $40K to almost $200K; second-degree black belt ~5 years training; ~1,000-1,500 students in total (unclear in audio); signing bonus idea $5,000
speakers: owner (Jonathan): business facts; Hormozi: questions, diagnosis, advice
applies_to: multi-location service business whose growth is capped by finding skilled staff who must also sell
