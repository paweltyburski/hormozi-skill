### MZ-nPoP0d6M1kc#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An auto repair shop owner ($1.5M last year, over $2M this year, building capacity $3-3.5M, goal multi-shop $10M+) gave two employees 30% equity (20% and 10%) too early, and they do not perform at owner level.
detail: S-corp, true equity; each has a guaranteed $100,000 base salary and resisted a move to commission. He wants to keep the 10% partner but off equity. A manager hired in December crushed the job and proved the systems; the partners now do employee work worth about $80,000 against $100,000 paid.
numbers: 30% equity split 20/10; salary $100K each, work worth about $80K; capacity $3-3.5M.

### MZ-nPoP0d6M1kc#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:48
claim: Frame the mistake as paying down ignorance debt, then list the real levers: keep as is, confront, or change pay.
detail: Without them there is $200,000 in salaries saved; the owner could also change his own salary. Options: (1) leave this shop unoptimised and make the next location 100% yours; (2) hold the hard conversation, which either leads to a buyout or to replacing them; (3) raise the bar or cut pay, as for employees, and possibly shift expenses. Ethical honouring of the agreement is the owner's choice.
numbers: $200K of combined salaries in play.

### MZ-nPoP0d6M1kc#03 | TACTIC | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:26
claim: Treat location two as a clean slate with the owner holding all equity, and trigger it when location one passes the revenue level where the data say it works.
detail: The owner's trigger is shop one above $2.5M; his leadership team is blocking that, so he must bulldog through and make the call. Many in the room have done the same, so do not feel alone.
numbers: Trigger: shop one above $2.5M.
