### MZ-oiUNB6q8_GI#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals, hiring-talent | fw: A Players | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A German online bike parts reseller competing on price should hire a $100k manager to free the founder for buying and channels.
detail: Sells others' brands via Google Shopping only. The founder handles support and forklifts. Mid-level managers cost $60-80k; he recommends the $100k person.
numbers: Revenue $1.8M, goal $5M; margin 25% (about $450k profit); manager costs $60-80k mid, about $100k good.

### MZ-oiUNB6q8_GI#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:48
claim: For this caller, a $100k hire means he needs to make $400k back to break even (he does not give a general formula).
detail: Profit falls from about $450k to $350k, so he must add about $400k of revenue at 25% margin. Founder time on buying and channels must deliver that.
numbers: $100k salary / 25% margin = $400k extra revenue to break even.
