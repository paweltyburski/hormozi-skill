### MZ-JD6jSiY-4sM#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: Downside of trying hard is small, while the missed upside is huge, so people misprice risk.
detail: Brains are wired for survival, not potential. Bets that fail still build skills and network; treat attempts as scratch-off tickets.

### MZ-JD6jSiY-4sM#03 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:01

detail: She grew a family business from $4M to $44M in three years and hesitated over an editor. At $12M profit a year, a $2-4M bet for $100-200M more is sensible.
numbers: $4M to $44M in 3 years; ~$1M profit a month.
