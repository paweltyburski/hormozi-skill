### MZ-wrvt2Zjicxk#01 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A founder of corporate ranch events ($3.8-4.5M, goal $8M) sees each department unwind once she moves on to the next.
detail: Cause is leaders who lack skills: hired with too low a bar, or not trained well enough. She says things work when she is present. Hormozi calls it an ops issue and says she is the stimulus people look to for "good job".
numbers: Revenue $3.8M-$4.5M per year; goal about $8M.

### MZ-wrvt2Zjicxk#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Transfer reinforcement from yourself to the organisation: define good, demonstrate, have them try, watch, give feedback, and repeat until it sticks.
detail: Then kudos shift from you to peers and later peers correct each other privately. Culture is rules of reinforcement; the signal you are done is staff praising each other for doing it right. She was missing the repeat step.

### MZ-wrvt2Zjicxk#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:11
claim: Check that leaders restate your standards as theirs and live them; behavior dilutes at each level, so you must be the most extreme version.
detail: Typical miss: a manager says the boss is aggressive and relaxes when she is away. Start with the leader (for example, arrival time). If they lack the skills, you cycle them through the same loop.
