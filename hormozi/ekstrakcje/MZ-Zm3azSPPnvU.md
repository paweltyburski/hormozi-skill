### MZ-Zm3azSPPnvU#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team, verticals | fw: A Players | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:34
claim: A student-housing software seller entering the US ($1.6M, target $2.7M) weighs a 25-year industry veteran "farmer" against a hunter.
detail: Veteran has US market knowledge and a book of contacts but is a teller, not a seller, and works for a competitor. Owner already hired one hunter, has two budgeted slots and a team of A players to protect. Hormozi: he does not desperately need him, so trust the gut.
numbers: $1.6M revenue; target $2.7M; about 20 candidates seen.

### MZ-Zm3azSPPnvU#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:35
claim: A weak-fit hire may make sales yet still be a no if he costs team culture.
detail: Sales team culture is the spoken and unspoken rules that govern behaviour; letting a senior hire break them resets the standard. "Will you lose money? No. Cost in culture? Maybe." means no.

### MZ-Zm3azSPPnvU#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:44
claim: To fill one solid role his team works through 500 to 1,000 resumes to get three qualified candidates and one hire.
detail: Under pain, owners talk to three people and marry one; interview more. The recruiting team's brief is to hand over a very hard decision, since an easy one means too little talent.
numbers: 500-1,000 resumes per hire; 3 finalists.
