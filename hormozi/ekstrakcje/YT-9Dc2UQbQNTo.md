### YT-9Dc2UQbQNTo#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: CRO | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Ad auction prices keep rising, so the businesses that convert the same traffic better will win.
detail: He attributes the idea to Neil Patel: with auction platforms, advertisers compete for the same eyeballs, so conversion rate becomes the edge alongside price and model.

### YT-9Dc2UQbQNTo#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: paid-ads | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:10
claim: Run one split test per weekly marketing meeting, reviewing last week's winner against the control before choosing the next test.
detail: One test forces focus on the change expected to matter most; 52 weekly tests a year compound. Refuse the "I said we should have done that" attitude, and pick the few actions with the biggest return for the resources available.
numbers: One test per week, every week for 52 weeks.

### YT-9Dc2UQbQNTo#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: CRO | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: About 90% of tests should be headline or image on the landing page, because the biggest conversion jumps come above the fold.
detail: Above the fold is whatever shows before scrolling on desktop or phone. If a new headline makes the old image fit badly, test the headline-and-image pair against the old pair.
numbers: ~90% of his tests are headline or image tests.
