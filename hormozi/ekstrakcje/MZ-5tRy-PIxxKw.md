### MZ-5tRy-PIxxKw#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Founders who say nobody can do the job as well as they do are usually protecting ego and fear, not describing the market.
detail: The business exists to serve customers, not the founder's need to be needed. Teaching the role now, with all mistakes known, takes far less time than learning it did. Hiring also means taking responsibility for paying people, so "I can't trust anyone" can justify fear.

### MZ-5tRy-PIxxKw#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Do not hunt a unicorn who replicates you; find several people who each have one piece of what you do.
detail: Founders with two employees try to hire someone who will do everything. Instead of one person with all the skills, assemble three who have one each.

### MZ-5tRy-PIxxKw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: The core of any leader's job is holding the standard, and the highest person in a department should hold the highest standard.
detail: His early hiring mistake was a bar that was too low. If someone gets a higher standard for a department than he has, that person should run it.
