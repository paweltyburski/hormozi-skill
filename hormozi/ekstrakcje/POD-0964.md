### POD-0964#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Continuity Bonus Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: A craft-stickers membership (about $1M a year, goal $3M) cannot scale on ads because 30-day cash is below acquisition cost.
detail: Sells through a paid $10 five-day event, then $27 per month or $270 per year. Daily bonuses were open to everyone, so only about 10% chose annual. Advice: one or two big bonuses reserved for annual buyers; a 16% discount alone is weak.
numbers: CAC about $90 on Meta ads vs about $60 collected in the first 30 days per new member; monthly churn 7%; LTV about $385 ($27 / 0.07), with launches varying around $300.

### POD-0964#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:16
claim: After a paid event ends, run a mop-up campaign that retargets everyone who saw the ads to the plain monthly purchase page.
detail: Runs after the five days, drops all bonuses, so annual-exclusive bonuses remain the only reason to prepay. Also: make the option you do not want chosen less convenient, without lying.
applies_to: Membership or continuity businesses selling through time-boxed events

### POD-0964#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: Cohort Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: In this call he noted the owner's LTV was blended across all members, not specific to the ads funnel, and pointed to cohort tracking to measure it.
detail: The owner only had blended LTV. Skool shows cohorts by month, so each launch month can be followed.
### POD-0964#04 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:12:06
claim: A service firm selling to ultra-wealthy families and family offices adds two premium tiers above its 20-year core tier, priced per square foot plus an annual retainer.
detail: Revenue $1.6M, goal $10M. Tier one is the established service; tiers two and three are new, and the owner could not build the ladder. The top tier carries a stewardship retainer, giving recurring revenue. Hormozi says wealthy buyers will simply do the math on a per-foot formula, so sharing the formula before the call is fine.
numbers: $100 per square foot on the new tiers; $200,000 per year stewardship retainer on the top tier.
