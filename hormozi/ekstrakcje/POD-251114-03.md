### POD-251114-03#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:56
claim: Openly explaining the exact process and numbers of his six-week challenge on stage in 2016 created inbound demand.
detail: At 26, an unknown speaker with bare slides, he described the free challenge. Afterwards people crowded him asking for a course, coaching or help for a friend's gym. He first declined, but left with 100+ contacts, many in adjacent industries such as chiropractors and dentists.
numbers: Age 26; 100+ contacts logged.

### POD-251114-03#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: offers | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:16
claim: His first coaching-type offer was free: he ran marketing and sales for gym owners and was paid only from what he sold.
detail: He flew out, paid for marketing, closed the leads and kept the cash collected, while the gym got the customers free. He also taught supplement sales, shared his nutrition programme and showed how to convert customers to memberships. This became his first million outside his gyms and the seed of Gym Launch.
numbers: 33 gyms over 18 months; first $1M outside his own gyms.

### POD-251114-03#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: promotions-launches | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:02
claim: In a new market he starts free or deeply discounted for four reasons: forgiveness, testimonials, conviction, referrals.
detail: Roughness is forgiven; free work is the quickest route to testimonials; with no conviction in a new service, free makes the first sales easier to close; and free starts referrals and demand.

### POD-251114-03#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: offers | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:44
claim: A promotion is wrapping paper on the same gift: it makes the offer more attractive without changing it.
detail: Recycled paper or luxury bag, same contents. It matters most in cold markets, where people need an answer to what is in it for them.
