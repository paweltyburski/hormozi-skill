### MZ-sH-d8nXPH8U#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Run team calls on metrics with the dashboard as the first slide, and use one-on-ones to invest in individual skill gaps.
detail: Team calls start with the numbers (trailing seven days: calls, scheduled, shown, offer rate, take rate, cash collected, by rep), praise who did well and ask why others did not. One-on-ones ask where the person wants to be and how to upskill them, which aids retention.
applies_to: Teams of 30-40 people.

### MZ-sH-d8nXPH8U#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:23
claim: Reward the desired behaviour the moment it happens; a weekly check-in is too slow to change behaviour.
detail: The agency's issue was that people did not connect actions to results. Quick chat kudos or a nod does more than a weekly review, whether remote or in person.

### MZ-sH-d8nXPH8U#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:24
claim: Break a distant goal into daily targets and share daily stats so contributors can be rewarded along the way.
detail: Webinar example: aiming for 1,000 registrants in 30 days needs about 33 per day on average, with the pace ramping up, and daily numbers reported with praise.
numbers: 1,000 registrations / 30 days = about 33 per day.
