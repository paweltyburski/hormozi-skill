### POD-0433#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:14:42
claim: He prefers a diversified investing portfolio over building a software company because he sees the software billionaires as only the visible winners and judges the risk of missing his goal as much lower with diversification.
detail: Billionaires from software are visible, the many failures are not, so a single founder's odds are far lower than a diversified investor's. His thesis is to find the best operators in specific niches and wrap his delivery model around them. He also says he gets bored in about 24 months, so participating in many companies keeps his attention.
numbers: 4-8 businesses a year and 3-5x growth in 3 years (not in this excerpt); $100M EBITDA in 10 years = about $1B portfolio value; attention window about 24 months.
### POD-0433#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:29
claim: Thesis: education is fragmenting into niche skills while demand for high-value skills exceeds supply, so find the best operator in a narrow trade and wrap a proven delivery model around them.
detail: He argues technology democratises consumption and consolidates production, so the best in a niche serves everyone. He gives examples such as Amazon sellers outnumbering most professions, and targets the best salon owner or mechanic.

### POD-0433#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: promotions-launches | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:09
claim: Fix the offer, the first transaction, before copy and ads; and after the first traction fix the product rather than adding promotion.
detail: Most beginners work out of sequence, fixing ads or copy while the initial exchange is weak. Rule of thumb for scaling: zero to $1M is promotion, $1M to $10M is product, $10M to $100M is people. After the first dollars, improve the product until it drives virality and reviews, then ad dollars return more.
numbers: Stages: $0-$1M promotion, $1M-$10M product, $10M-$100M people.
