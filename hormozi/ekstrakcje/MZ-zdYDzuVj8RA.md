### MZ-zdYDzuVj8RA#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A $997 online piano course seller ($1.4M, target $3.6M) is stuck because videos rank for most beginner keywords, so more videos do not add leads.
detail: He sells via a YouTube channel (over a million subscribers), email, a sales page and webinars. Options debated: deeper content, another platform, ads, conversion work. Hormozi: funnel fixes can roughly double (lowest risk) but not 10x; if betting on what doubles the business, run ads.
numbers: $1.4M to $3.6M; course price $997; ~1M subscribers.

### MZ-zdYDzuVj8RA#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:30
claim: Content reaches only a small slice of followers and fewer see the call to action, whereas ads also reach the larger group of lurkers.
detail: Algorithms favour interest, not follower count. Warm people (lurkers who watched one to three videos, perhaps 10x the subscribers) convert better than true-cold ad audiences. Promotional videos tend to retain worse.
numbers: Lurkers about 10x the subscriber count (his estimate).

### MZ-zdYDzuVj8RA#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: lead-getters | fw: Agency Learning Agreement | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:58
claim: When you know you must do something, buy everything: hire an agency but require that you learn what they do.
detail: He pays more for the learning requirement, and tells owners to read the agency section of his leads book. Result: a money-printing machine you understand.
