### POD-0005#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:29
claim: When your current business is not working, starting a second one doubles your problems instead of fixing the first.
detail: He says the struggling owner who opens a side business ends up with two weak ones. Remedy: pick one and focus on it. A story he relays: a well-known mentor ran eight businesses at once (twelve at the peak) and never passed $3 million a year; only after shutting everything but one did it grow from about $3 million to $30 million a year. Food-prep side companies started by failing gyms also never made money.
numbers: Mentor: up to 12 businesses, stuck under $3M per year; one business, $3M to $30M per year.
applies_to: Owners tempted by a new venture while the main one underperforms.

### POD-0005#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Many applicants to the gym-turnaround service were gym owners who had turned a half-working marketing method into an agency selling to other gyms.
detail: Their own gym's ads stopped working because they could not refresh copy, offer or creative, had no reliable conversion process and could not scale it with a team. They then sold the same method to other gyms. He would not work with them: knowing the method works makes it hard to keep it exclusive, and he thought a gym making $50,000 a month would never have started an agency.
numbers: Gym at $50,000/month cited as the level at which the owner would not have started an agency.

### POD-0005#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: Epiphany Bridge | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:08
claim: The bikini-coach analogy: selling a method that no longer works for you to clients who cannot see that is an ethical and practical trap.
detail: A competitor who got fit for a show, regained weight afterwards, and still coaches others who then also regain it. Same for an owner whose ads stopped working but who sells ad services: the method got them to one point and has nothing further to give the client. Used as a diagnostic: ask whether the person selling the service has a working version of it.

### POD-0005#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:30
claim: In his opinion a gym owner approached by a marketing agency should not work with one but learn marketing himself, and should proceed with caution, especially if the agency runs only one gym and has few clients.
detail: He says that is usually where they split their attention and do neither well. He says learning it is not that hard and that he will probably cover it in another episode.
