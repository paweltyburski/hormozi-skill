### POD-0424#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit, retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:20:40
claim: The Value Acceleration Method works backwards from a 36-month target in 12-week blocks and fixes the back end before adding channels.
detail: Targets are internet businesses at about $3M-$10M revenue growing to $30M-$100M. Steps: define the enterprise, leadership, team, strategy and monetisation needed for the target multiple; fix customer success metrics and a CRM tracking click to close; raise customer lifetime value two to five times (a big profit boost); then use the freed cash to hire talent and open more acquisition channels (cold email, ads, TikTok, organic, partners).
numbers: Entry revenue about $3M-$10M; target $30M-$100M; 36-month goal; 12-week cycles; customer lifetime value up 2x to 5x.
applies_to: Internet businesses at $3M-$10M

### POD-0424#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:50
claim: Portfolio companies averaged about +80% revenue and 3x profit within eight months of partnering.
detail: As a minority partner with no legal power to remove the CEO, he relies on trust and influence to push price increases, continuity changes and dropping product lines, which a typical private-equity relationship would resist.
numbers: Average +80% revenue and 3x profit in first 8 months (his statement for the portfolio).

### POD-0424#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters, finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:11
claim: Give away the content of a would-be paid course to build inbound deal flow and let strong executors self-select.
detail: His company sells nothing and spends nothing on ads; the pitch is to help as many as possible free, then those who execute and get 3x sales seek him out. Inbound trust shortens diligence compared with the paid ads, affiliates and cold calling of his earlier companies. A friend convinced him fame was worth the cost of threats, which tipped him into publishing; first video was about 18 months before the interview.
numbers: Ad spend $0; about 18 months from first video to the interview.

### POD-0424#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:26
claim: Finance hires follow revenue stage: a staff accountant around $3M, a controller around $10M, a CFO around $30M.
detail: He warns against copying a role from two stages ahead, since it is the right move in the wrong sequence. Their model is an on-demand C-suite: copy the expertise a company of that size needs and swap or move people who cannot grow into it.
numbers: Staff accountant at ~$3M, controller at ~$10M, CFO at ~$30M revenue.

### POD-0424#05 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:17:17
claim: After selling majority stakes in two companies for cash he felt the loss of cash flow, not the lump sum.
detail: Gym Launch and Prestige Labs sold together for $46.2M, all cash with no seller financing, earn-out or consulting, and they kept 33%. A third company (a $12M top-line software firm) was sold the same year. They had already taken about $45M in dividends over the previous five years. The big-cash-flow hose dropped to zero and the lump did not register; he felt poorer than before. Sale proceeds went into safe holdings while Acquisition.com is the growth vehicle.
numbers: $46.2M all cash; 33% retained; ~$45M dividends in the five years prior; $12M top-line software company.

### POD-0424#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:24:12
claim: Entrepreneurship is a continuous relinquishing of control, so you keep raising the quality of the people you hand control to.
detail: Freedom and control cannot both be maximised. He recalls the progression of salary levels he was impressed by: first $50,000 hire, then $70,000, six figures, $250,000, and later a million-dollar employee. A mentor told him the best talent has not been hired yet.
numbers: Employee pay milestones: $50,000, $70,000, six figures, $250,000, $1,000,000 per year.
