### MZ-QQw8DJPewLM#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints, pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:51
claim: A flip-and-coaching owner in Fayetteville NC (about 300-400K people) with ~$4M revenue and seven integrated businesses wants to go national, but has barely worked his local market.
detail: He coaches new flippers every three months (about 10 students), giving them his crews and volume pricing, and owns electrical, HVAC, roofing and dumpster businesses as a platform. Hormozi: demand constrained in the local market; revenue of $4M equals 400 students at $10K or 100 at $40K; Raleigh is 1.5 hours away; $100M a year is realistic.
numbers: revenue ~$4M; coaching price $10K first class, now $15K; ~10 students per cohort every 3 months; HVAC and roofing ~$200K each.

### MZ-QQw8DJPewLM#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:44
claim: He asks for the student LTV before deciding whether to raise the coaching price.
detail: Owner's figures per student: $15K tuition, plus about $5-10K contracting profit per flip, ~$1K dumpsters and ~$3K HVAC; a good student may flip around ten houses in a year. Hormozi's suggestion: charge about $100K and deliver the services free or at cost.
numbers: $15K tuition; $5-10K contracting per flip; ~$1K dumpsters; ~$3K HVAC; up to ~10 flips a year.

### MZ-QQw8DJPewLM#03 | TACTIC | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:44
claim: When a COO takes over operations, use the freed time for content (the founder's impact itch) and learn paid ads and sales.
detail: The hired COO exists so the founder can do the work only he does. He can sell 20 to 100 students a week in his local market at the current offer, instead of relying on word of mouth and seminars.
numbers: 20-100 students a week (Hormozi's estimate for a local market).
