### MZ-VLBVhTNF5Ks#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:11
claim: Skills for hard things are domain specific; a marathon or combat does not make a hard conversation easy.
detail: Physical feats are publicly admired but do not predict being a good partner or owner. Elective hard things differ from decisive ones that need emotion.

### MZ-VLBVhTNF5Ks#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:43
claim: To generalise toughness, attach it to an identity label (such as "honest") that acts as a global reinforcer of behaviour.
detail: Personality is behaviour across conditions; the label drives choices in new situations and creates guilt when broken.

### MZ-VLBVhTNF5Ks#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:11
claim: One of the hardest things is to stop letting others' reactions control your behaviour, such as fear of what they say about starting a business.
detail: Say it plainly: "I am not doing this because of them" means they control you; the hard thing is ending that.
