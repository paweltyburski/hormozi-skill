### YT-Z2tgZC_XkT4#06 | TACTIC | topic: outreach | also: lead-getters | fw: Warm Outreach Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:18
claim: Find influencers in layers: first those you know, then your network's contacts (offer a cut for a successful intro), then people you follow, contacted on many channels.
detail: Define the ideal customer and the influencers they follow first. Almost everyone knows someone with a bigger following. For cold targets, use multiple platforms: LinkedIn inboxes are far less crowded than Instagram, and direct mail cannot be unsubscribed from. Also contact via their website's store or support email, since few people use it.

### YT-Z2tgZC_XkT4#10 | SCRIPT | topic: outreach | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:20:27
claim: Magic message structure: long-time fan, who you are, an estimate of their potential income, proof from others, generous split, ask for a 15-minute call.
detail: Order of moves: (1) familiarity opener; (2) state you run a company that partners with influencers on a specific product; (3) based on their following, estimate a monthly recurring-income range; (4) screenshots of other people's results; (5) they keep most of the profit and you do most of the work; (6) ask for a short call. Personalise it; start with people you already follow or who engaged with you; failures are free practice.
numbers: Call length 15 minutes; they keep the majority of profits.

### YT-Z2tgZC_XkT4#15 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:30:43
claim: Structure the 15-minute pitch as promise, proof, demo, plan, then offer, and make the offer so good that refusing feels foolish.
detail: Promise: what they get. Proof: past results or stats from similar people. Demo: bring the product if it exists. Plan: next steps and who does what, making their side look better. Then the offer; if they are bigger, give more. A mid-size influencer may value growth over money, so offer investment in their growth too.
