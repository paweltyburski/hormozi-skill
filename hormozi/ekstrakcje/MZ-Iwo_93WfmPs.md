### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#01 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: Five Topic Sources | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Decide who you want to attract, list the biggest problems they face now, and make all content solve those problems so each piece reinforces "listen to me and the problem is solved".
detail: For business owners his list is marketing, sales, cash flow, fixing the product, churn and recruiting. Content works because the host is also a real operator.

### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#02 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:45
claim: Goodwill is a history of positive reinforcement, which raises the odds that someone will act for you.
detail: He argues creators fear that asking spends goodwill only because they assume the thing sold is not good; a great product deposits goodwill itself, as at a company whose launches draw queues. Creators often have media skills, not product skills.

### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:17
claim: The more specific your instruction and the smaller the gap between advice and action, the more credit people give you for the result.
detail: People tend to attribute good outcomes to themselves, especially when they had to derive the steps. Example: if he supplies the headline, thumbnail and opening tweet, the lift is clearly his advice. For this reason he gives very specific directions.

### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Treat a podcast as an email list, not a growth engine; channel growth comes mostly from video platforms, and audio sustains it.
detail: Audio-only is hard to grow because it has little recommendation. A podcast subscriber is worth more than other subscriptions because most people follow only 5-7 shows against about 1,000 social accounts.
numbers: 5-7 podcast subscriptions per person versus about 1,000 followed Instagram accounts.

### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:21
claim: One hour of exposure in a 3-hour podcast is better than the same time in short clips.
detail: His arithmetic: a short a day for a year is about 365 x 30 seconds, roughly 3 hours at best, probably nearer an hour at real short lengths.
numbers: 365 shorts of 30 seconds is about 3 hours; real shorts about a third of that.

### MZ-Iwo_93WfmPs#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:58
