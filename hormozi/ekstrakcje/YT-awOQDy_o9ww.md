### YT-awOQDy_o9ww#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent, operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Entrepreneurship is a sequence of buying back time by relinquishing control; each step is uncomfortable, largely because we identify with the task we are holding.
detail: Order, as he describes it for most people: low-skill support and delivery tasks, then higher admin (billing, collections, contracts), then an assistant or right-hand operator who also tracks the money, then selling, then managing a sales team, then leadership of departments and culture.
### YT-awOQDy_o9ww#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:04
claim: Rough markers for founder control: up to about $1M a year you can sell yourself; past that it is usually difficult, so selling must be handed off; sales-team management comes in at maybe $3-10M; going from $10M to $30M means giving up leading everyone directly.
detail: At that size there are too many departments, so lead a small group who lead the others.
numbers: ~$1M, ~$3-10M, $10M to $30M per year.
### YT-awOQDy_o9ww#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:58
claim: Hand off any task the market staffs at about $15 an hour (such as teaching a class) if you have work worth $100 an hour.
detail: Owners resist because they like feeling unique, though others may do it better.
numbers: $15/hour vs $100/hour.

### YT-awOQDy_o9ww#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:27
claim: Know where the bodies are buried: if you cannot name a department's problems, or hear only good news, you are too far away from it.
detail: You hand over control but keep full accountability. The fix is more transparency or attention, not taking control back.

### YT-awOQDy_o9ww#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:29
claim: If the business model only works at your own sales level, the model is broken; it should work with reps closing half as many as you.
detail: He cannot outsell a team of 12; reps who only sell can eventually beat him because he is also running the business.
numbers: Reps at ~50% of founder close level should still work.
