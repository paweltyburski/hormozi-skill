### YT-bkPwFqa89gE#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Decide the next day's one to three tasks the night before and start on them on waking.
detail: He cannot sleep until he has cleared his mind by naming the few things to focus on; he gives himself at most three, sometimes just one (for example editing his book). No fixed number rule. Morning routine is only coffee, then work.
numbers: 1 to 3 tasks per day.

### YT-bkPwFqa89gE#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: For his first eight years in business he woke around 4 a.m. to get about six uninterrupted hours before talking to anyone.
detail: The early start suited a fitness-industry schedule and gave roughly four to six hours of focused work each day. The point for beginners: protected, undisturbed hours move projects forward.
numbers: Wake about 4:00; about 6 uninterrupted hours until about 10:00; first eight years.
applies_to: Early-stage owners

### YT-bkPwFqa89gE#03 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:35
claim: Remove repeated low-value decisions and physical clutter to save attention for higher-quality decisions.
detail: He eats the same breakfast, lunch and dinner daily. He says clearing belongings that nag at you frees mental bandwidth, like cleaning a garage.

### YT-bkPwFqa89gE#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:38
claim: Set an alarm for bedtime instead of wake-up time.
detail: He values a fixed bedtime over a fixed rising time (waking 3:10 to 5:30 depending on sleep). He can work on four hours of sleep for long stretches, but decides better when rested. Wealthy people he knows value sleep quality; one billionaire friend works nights and cannot work before 10 a.m., so find the schedule that suits you.

### YT-bkPwFqa89gE#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:12
claim: Elaborate routines become superstitions and crutches, and many productivity hacks are procrastination.
detail: Needing a ritual before you can function makes you less resilient. Of gratitude journals and affirmations he kept only simple things. As an owner rises, decisions matter more than actions, so protect thinking time.
