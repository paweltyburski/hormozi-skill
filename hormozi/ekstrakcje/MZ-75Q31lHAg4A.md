### MZ-75Q31lHAg4A#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A California real-estate brokerage (about $6.65M revenue, 80 agents, 12 in a new adjacent market) plans a second office with a first-time leader.
detail: Targets: 50 agents in the new area, about 40 by end of next year to make sense; home-office could max at about 200 agents. His risks are culture and managing a leader, not the sales process, which already runs one team model.
numbers: ~$6.65M revenue; 80 agents; 12 locally; target 40-50 agents; current office cap ~200.

### MZ-75Q31lHAg4A#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:55
claim: Define culture as the rules of engagement for every interaction type, condense them to about three statements and use them as the test for a new site; move the biggest culture champion there.
detail: List the conditions in which people interact and the rules for each, then chunk them up. Move the champion only if the culture can sustain itself without him.
numbers: 3 culture statements.

### MZ-75Q31lHAg4A#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Choose leaders you admire in part and who admire part of you; "you get to hire your mentors", because if you are always the best in the organisation it stops at you.
detail: Asked how to get someone better than you to work for you, he jokes that they just don't know they're better, and calls it his secret.
### MZ-75Q31lHAg4A#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:07:03
claim: Hormozi's preference, most of the time, is to open the second site when the first runs and grows without you and you are bored; "scale zero" means using the economics at zero founder time.
detail: Most of the time, failures come from the founder leaving the first site to run the next, then needing to return. The caller (not Hormozi) said his time managing was about 20% max (mostly meetings and trainings) and about 60% on proprietary deal flow.
numbers: Caller: ~20% of his time managing; ~60% proprietary deal flow; target 0.
### MZ-75Q31lHAg4A#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:47
claim: Give a new leader faster reinforcement loops: check-ins, monitoring and role-plays of leadership conversations, which are sales conversations that are more varied.
detail: Rely on question-based frameworks and put training wheels on the leader early by integrating them into the current office.
