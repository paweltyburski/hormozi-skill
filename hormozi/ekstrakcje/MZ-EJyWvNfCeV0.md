### MZ-EJyWvNfCeV0#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A supplement brand doing about $1.1M a month (just under $10M last year), all through Amazon ads, wants a second channel.
detail: Main product is a testosterone booster for ages 25-45. Hormozi suggests a TikTok Shop play: outbound to creators, front them cash and product, and put them in a community. 200 creators posting 2-3 videos daily gives about 600 a day; most flop, the top 20-30 go on retainer and their videos are whitelisted on Meta.
numbers: $1.1M per month; about $10M last year; 200 creators x 2-3 videos = about 600 per day.

### MZ-EJyWvNfCeV0#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:46
claim: Guard against brand dilution on creator programs with four controls.
detail: A brand guideline creators must read before activation; give them the best hooks and ban negative hooks; check creators match the avatar in look and setting; manual review once a creative passes about 50-100 per day before it is boosted.
numbers: Review trigger at 50-100 units per day per creative.
