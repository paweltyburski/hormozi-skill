### POD-0278#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Prestige Labs' support department hired 25 reps for work four people now do, and 20 had to be cut.
detail: About two and a half years in, after a $28M year, the team launched a supplement brand with first months near $1.5-1.7M. A director promoted up from the B2B consulting side, who had never built a department, got the physical-product support build-out. She hired ahead of volume. Reps said they were overworked, but remote work gave no visibility; one finally admitted doing almost nothing. Hormozi took the blame.
numbers: $28M prior year; $1.7M, $1.5M, $1.6M first three months; 25 reps vs 4 needed; about $600,000 beta in December; 20 cut.

### POD-0278#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:14
claim: Hire a department head who has built a team in the same kind of business, not only someone with related experience.
detail: Lessons: the person should have grown a department before; the experience should match the exact industry; and sometimes you have to investigate rather than take people's word. High-touch B2B service where clients pay thousands per month needs a different service level than a roughly $40 supplement buyer, so one model cannot be copied.
numbers: $40 supplement order versus thousands per month B2B.
### POD-0278#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:41
claim: Track support utilization: measure average minutes per task type, multiply by daily volume, compare with paid labour hours.
detail: The new director ran time studies, listing tasks such as replies, tickets, card changes, address or flavor changes and cancellations. This gives the reasonable workload for a given inflow. A weekly report shows utilization, and the team aims to stay in the high 80s to avoid burnout; above 90 signals another hire.
numbers: reports of 72% and 88% utilization; target high 80s; above 90% means hire.

### POD-0278#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:30
claim: Validate reported workload with data; quantified tasks keep culture healthy and expose idle staff.
detail: He calls this QA: do not take people's word, especially remotely. Idle employees who say nothing and just collect pay are, in his words, the biggest cancer. Transparent metrics also reassure honest staff that jobs are not at risk.
