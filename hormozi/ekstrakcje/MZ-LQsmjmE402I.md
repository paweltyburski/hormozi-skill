### MZ-LQsmjmE402I#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A custom car build and repair shop lost money because the front office manager would not chase payments.
detail: Three years in business; the new owner bought out two partners; a 50-50 partner is the mechanic. Parts are bought and work done on unfinished projects while customers delay payment. A 1% commission for the manager did not help. Cash was the problem.
numbers: Revenue about $1M; profit minus $150,000; commission 1% of gross revenue.

### MZ-LQsmjmE402I#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: unit-economics | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Ask whether it is a cash flow issue or a model issue: if you had collected all the cash due, would you have made money?
detail: Answer was yes, by $150,000, a $300,000 swing from collection.
numbers: $300,000 swing; break-even was missed by $150,000.

### MZ-LQsmjmE402I#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:29
claim: Do not fire a knowledgeable key employee; hire a dedicated collections person and demand payment up front.
detail: Take collections off her plate so she can use her strengths; the hire pays for itself against the $300,000 swing.
numbers: Collections hire about $60,000 a year.

### MZ-LQsmjmE402I#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:19
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: as a performance shop in this niche, demand is probably more inelastic than he thinks, so he would probably start with a 15 to 20% price rise across the board.
detail: A performance shop should be expensive, not apologise for it; he says this alone would take the shop to profitable.
numbers: 15 to 20% price rise.
### MZ-LQsmjmE402I#05 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:09
claim: Do not launch a second brand for generic maintenance; cross-sell maintenance to performance customers at a premium.
detail: Marketing generic repairs to the public brings low-margin customers.
