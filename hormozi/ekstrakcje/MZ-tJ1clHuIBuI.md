### MZ-tJ1clHuIBuI#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:16
claim: Hormozi doesn't think regional upheaval is a good reason to change industry; he suspects you may be attributing a business difficulty to an external factor you can't control.
detail: He uses the analogy of a one-man dry cleaner or lawn care service asking whether moving away from the Middle East makes the business not worth it, to show geography alone doesn't translate into a reason. He says there has always been strife there, so he wouldn't make it the reason.
