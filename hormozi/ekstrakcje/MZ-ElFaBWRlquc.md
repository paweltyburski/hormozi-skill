### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An intermodal trucking company with 18 locations cannot grow because it cannot recruit enough owner-operator drivers.
detail: Contractors lease exclusively to the firm, which handles insurance and sells their loads. Candidate flow only replaces turnover. Customers do not leave and the firm says no to freight more than yes; it does no marketing. Contractors come from job boards (Craigslist, Indeed) and referrals.
numbers: Revenue $36M in a bad year, $50M in a good one; goal $70M-$100M; ~$185,000 revenue and ~$70,000 gross profit per truck per year.
applies_to: Asset-light contractor-based logistics.

### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Supply-constrained firms have no use for demand-generation offers, so the problem is on the supply side.
detail: Selling marketing to a business that already turns work away gets "I'm fine". The fix is a supply-side acquisition channel.

### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Ask what business you are really in: a cleaning company is in the business of recruiting cleaners, a trucking firm in recruiting contractors.
detail: A friend moved from fitness into cleaning, found customers easy and reliable staff hard, and reorganised around recruiting.

### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: unit-economics | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Set the talent acquisition budget from the gross profit one hire generates, as with customers.
detail: An insurance owner earned $250,000 a year per productive agent, raised referral bonuses from $2,000 to $25,000 for an agent productive within 90 days, and growth changed. The trucker earns ~$70,000 per driver per year, so he may pay far more than his current bonus.
numbers: $2,000 to $25,000 bonus per $250,000 gross profit; current trucking bonus $5,000.

### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:56
claim: Split a large referral bonus into a small payment now and the rest after six months.
detail: Hormozi suggests about $35,000 (half of annual gross profit), $5,000 today and $30,000 at six months, making the referrer a champion for retention. Calm worries about more competition for loads by giving referrers first pick of routes.
numbers: $35,000 total = $5,000 + $30,000 at six months.

### MZ-ElFaBWRlquc#06 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:33
claim: Run cold-traffic ads to an application funnel with a short video pitch, adding friction to filter applicants.
detail: A portfolio company with the same problem built this and could open locations on demand. Drivers can be targeted at ports and rail yards. Mini video of three to five minutes: opportunity, what rivals cannot give, insurance covered, more demand than capacity.
numbers: Mini video 3-5 minutes.
