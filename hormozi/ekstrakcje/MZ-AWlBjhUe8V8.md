### MZ-AWlBjhUe8V8#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Do not try to untrain "hijacked" brains; train better behaviours through reinforcement.
detail: Drop the dopamine-addiction framing. Culture is the many small rules about what is rewarded or not (for example, punctuality), and the leader should become the source of reinforcement, rewarding right actions immediately.

### MZ-AWlBjhUe8V8#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Use mild negative feedback only after a large store of positive reinforcement.
detail: With many positives first, a correction is accepted as earned. Without them the boss is simply disliked.
numbers: Example ratio: about five kind acts before a correction.

### MZ-AWlBjhUe8V8#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:22
claim: A request to sift 500 DMs sat unanswered in a team chat until a senior leader volunteered, and the founder praised it publicly.
detail: Nobody in the 18-person channel answered for about 40 minutes. A public display like this reinforces values and shows why someone was promoted.
numbers: ~500 DMs; 18 people; ~40 minutes.

### MZ-AWlBjhUe8V8#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:02
claim: Praise publicly, criticise privately, and keep doing it forever.
detail: New hires bring their own rules, so culture dilutes as a company scales unless leaders stay more forceful about their beliefs.
