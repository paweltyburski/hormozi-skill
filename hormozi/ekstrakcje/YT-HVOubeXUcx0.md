### YT-HVOubeXUcx0#14 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:01
claim: Choose metrics with little delay between action and feedback, so behaviour can change quickly.
detail: Views were the default, but deals took 6-12 months to feed back. Daily or hourly metrics give about a hundred feedback loops instead of one a year.
numbers: Deal cycle 6-12 months.

### YT-HVOubeXUcx0#15 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:47
claim: He uses ad revenue (views times revenue per thousand views) as a quality metric paired with views, because for his business-owner audience it tracked audience quality.
detail: He had ignored it as cost offset, then found it the fastest measure that tracked book sales, opt-ins and applications. His audience's high spending power raises RPM, so for him rising RPM means more right people. The best RPM month was also best for sales, opt-ins and applications.

detail: Example: tickets resolved paired with a customer rating of the resolution; views alone give views for their own sake.
