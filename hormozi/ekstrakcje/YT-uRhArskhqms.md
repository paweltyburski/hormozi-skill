### YT-uRhArskhqms#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:19
claim: He describes his own case: when his attention was split, he made a rapid series of exit decisions over roughly 48 hours (a day or two) and consolidated onto one thing, Gym Launch.
detail: He first dropped both agency businesses, handing everything to the partner, then negotiated out of the gym partnerships and the newer gym. He sold two gyms at a better number and gave the rest away at fire-sale terms. The conversations were not super well received.

### YT-uRhArskhqms#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:14
claim: Each unmade decision or avoided conversation permanently occupies part of your thinking capacity, so attention, not time, is the scarce resource.
detail: Accumulated unmade decisions leave you reactive. Example: a problem that seemed unsolvable at night looks obvious after sleep, because focus returns. His coach had told him to aggregate his attention. Conversations to confront can be with partners, employees, family.

### YT-uRhArskhqms#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:01
claim: Hormozi says that when he feels simple decisions take an inordinate amount of effort, it is because he is distracted by too many unmade decisions and unconfronted conversations.
detail: This is his own experience: unmade decisions and avoided conversations use up thinking power. After he made a batch of tough calls over a day or two, he felt he had more attention and bandwidth.
### YT-uRhArskhqms#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:25
claim: Having many businesses can be a status shield while earning little; sequence them rather than run them in parallel.
detail: He admits he ran many ventures to feel successful while barely making money. Gym Launch started in 2016 and the stretch to mid-2017 was his worst 18 months, until he went all in on it.
