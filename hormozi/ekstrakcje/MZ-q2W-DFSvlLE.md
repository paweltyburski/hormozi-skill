### MZ-q2W-DFSvlLE#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Customer Journey | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A business teaching Brazilians abroad e-commerce bought a video-consultation health company, but its customers did not need it to reach their goal.
detail: The caller spends about $300K a month on ads and sees three pains in his audience: money, language, health. Hormozi asks whether customers need the new product to get the outcome they bought; they do not, so he compares it to buying newspapers because people read. Sell to them anyway via outreach or a monthly webinar, tied indirectly to their outcome.
numbers: About $300K per month on ads.

### MZ-q2W-DFSvlLE#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Buy businesses that sit on the customer's roadmap to success, so you own every tool they must use.
detail: Map everything the customer must do to succeed, then acquire tools on that path. Beginners tend to adopt whatever tools you recommend, which supports affiliate relationships and multiple businesses serving the same avatar.
