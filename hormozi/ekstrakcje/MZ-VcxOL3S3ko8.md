claim: A vacation-rental manager with no local competition asked when to drop low-end properties that do not fit a lakefront brand.
detail: He feared losing cash that pays the bills. Hormozi said no one will notice the brand change.

### MZ-VcxOL3S3ko8#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Drop a customer segment only when the return from reallocating the freed resources exceeds the income lost.
detail: Review underperforming locations yearly: if the operator is good and the market tough, trim the location and move the operator to a better market.

### MZ-VcxOL3S3ko8#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Keep low-end properties and add one capable manager; it is a "who" problem, not a customer problem.
detail: Property management cash flow is easier than agency work, and owners with small assets use property managers.
