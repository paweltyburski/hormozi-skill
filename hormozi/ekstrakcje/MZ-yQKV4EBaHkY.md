### MZ-yQKV4EBaHkY#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: What counts as a respectable business activity depends on which peer group you compare yourself to and want approval from.
detail: Tech founders seek tech approval; an HVAC owner seeks HVAC peers' approval, if that is the group they actually compare to. Status pressure, not fit, often chooses the business.

### MZ-yQKV4EBaHkY#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Many tech founders who sold for nine figures then look for cash-flowing lifestyle businesses such as gas stations, forgetting the operational pain.
detail: They want stable monthly cash flow but not the 2 a.m. robbery call. Hormozi knows many such people; cash-flow owners in turn complain about hard-to-sell businesses, low multiples, low-skilled labor and churn.

### MZ-yQKV4EBaHkY#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: People want the benefits of a trade-off without its costs, so whichever side you hold looks greener from the other.
detail: Employees envy entrepreneurs and the reverse; parents recall life without kids and the childless wish for them.
