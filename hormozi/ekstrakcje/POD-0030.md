### POD-0030#01 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Answer "how much is it?" by agreeing, comparing to a mechanic who cannot quote before seeing the car, then booking the visit.
detail: Steps: 1. praise the question; 2. say you cannot quote until you have assessed their needs; 3. use the mechanic analogy; 4. if pressed for a range, say a price without knowing them is arbitrary and unfair, and a gym quoting over the phone should be avoided; 5. offer a specific time to come in. He says the analogy handles the question about 85% of the time.
numbers: about 85% resolved by the analogy.

### POD-0030#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00
claim: Frame refusing to quote as ethical: it is a service to the caller, not evasion.
detail: Saying nobody should quote by phone puts you on the side of fairness, so they feel looked after.
