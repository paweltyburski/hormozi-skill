### POD-0902#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: paid-ads | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:20
claim: A $1.2M pool service and repair company, mostly inbound, under-spends on ads out of fear although each customer pays back many times over.
detail: Owner profit is about $50K after $145K owner salaries. Customers are recurring at ~$4,500 a year. Only ~$500 a month goes to Google local services ads. Hormozi's chain: staff leave early, prices are too low, higher prices are blocked by a weak sales process, so fix sales first. He tells the owner to spend more; the owner admits fear.
numbers: Google ads about $500/month give 2-3 leads/week (call it 10/month), so about $50 per lead; 30% of leads convert (Hormozi says 50% is reachable on inbound), so CAC about $150; gross margin about 50% on $4,500 gives ~$2,250 gross profit a year.

### POD-0902#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: Payback Period | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:58
claim: Even after cost of goods on repair work, on an assumed ~30% margin the pool firm would make about $500 gross profit in the first 30 days against a $150 CAC.
detail: First-30-day revenue per client averages ~$1,500, much of it cost of goods for repair work. At an assumed ~30% margin that is about $500 gross profit, so acquisition pays back inside 30 days.
numbers: ~$1,500 revenue per client in first 30 days; ~30% assumed margin, ~$500 gross profit; CAC ~$150.
### POD-0902#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:43
claim: Pay the trainer a bonus for getting a new technician field-ready faster, but make the trainer certify the trainee's work afterwards.
detail: Capacity is blocked by a six-week training ramp. Offer a $500 bonus if the trainer gets the person on the road in two weeks, on condition that the trainer signs off and covers the callbacks: any rushed job by the trainee is fixed by the trainer, unpaid, for the following month.
numbers: Ramp target six weeks down to two; bonus $500.

### POD-0902#04 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:30
claim: A dental clinic marketing to foreign patients should use content, with before-and-after photos collected as a built-in step of the patient journey.
detail: Hormozi cites Turkish hair-transplant doctors who post before and after images all day and get inbound demand. Schedule photo collection at fixed journey points (immediately, day 30, day 60) so every patient becomes a content item; rebates for photos are usually unnecessary.

### POD-0902#05 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:16:03
claim: A fence-visualisation app (about $500K a year) churns at 8% a month because users who do not sell a fence in the first 10 days leave.
detail: Pricing is $199/month for two users and $299/month for five, with a 10-day card-on-file trial. Users who sell a fence love the product and stay. Leads come from Facebook instant forms; the founder sells without commission. Hormozi: do not scale ads at all until churn is fixed; put all effort and incentive into activation.
numbers: Revenue ~$500K/year; churn 8%/month including trial cancels; CAC about $130 per trial (cost per lead as low as $1.50 on a best ad, which he says will not last).

### POD-0902#06 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: Trial with Penalty | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:20:13
claim: Charge the first month up front and make the second month free if the user uses the tool two or three times, instead of refunding.
detail: No refund: skip billing month two and bill month three. This rewards activation.

### POD-0902#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: Revenue Churn | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:11
claim: Measure churn only after a trial converts, and track trial-to-paid conversion separately; blending them distorts small companies' numbers.
detail: Also compute CAC per paying customer, not per trial: if one in three trials converts, a $130 trial cost means roughly $400-$500 per customer (Hormozi says closer to $500; the owner reported about 8 trials per 100 leads, 3-4 converting).
