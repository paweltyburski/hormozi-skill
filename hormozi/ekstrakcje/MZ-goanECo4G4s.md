### MZ-goanECo4G4s#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: To get a team to adopt AI, raise output expectations, because without higher standards there is no reason to change.
detail: When staff say tasks take long, show a faster way. Remove the repetitive, boring parts of a role, not the challenging ones.

### MZ-goanECo4G4s#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:54
claim: Break each role into its tasks and write SOP prompts for them; even 50-80% automation can give 2x to 8x leverage.
detail: Most jobs reduce to a list of tasks and behaviours; each becomes a prompt SOP.
numbers: 50% to 80% of task work; 2x, 4x, 8x leverage.

### MZ-goanECo4G4s#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Hire people who embrace change; those who will not adopt AI get replaced by those who do.
detail: Compared to refusing to use the internet. Some people switching is enough to start.

### MZ-goanECo4G4s#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: other | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:32
claim: Use a corporate AI account, not personal ones, and never enter anything you would not want public.
detail: Personal chat logs can be subpoenaed; a business instance is protected. Hormozi is not an alarmist and says how much information to expose is a personal risk decision (he publishes revenue openly).
