### POD-0036#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:35
claim: A partnership where both partners do the same job and merely split the work is a red flag.
detail: Eventually you could pay someone hourly to do the other partner's work, and you will resent that partner for taking half the income. Hormozi has walked several gym owners out of such partnerships.
### POD-0036#02 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:26
claim: A roofing contractor with roofs as the cash cow, plus general contracting, remodeling and real estate ideas, was told to drop the rest and just do more roofing.
detail: One business, one focus: a huge business can do one thing, and extra lines divert attention so both decline. Russell Brunson reportedly had about eight businesses and could not pass a revenue level until he cut to one. A gym owner should not start an online business alongside the gym.
numbers: Russell had about eight businesses at one point.
applies_to: Owners under about $1-3M with several side projects.

### POD-0036#03 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:47
claim: Of the five pipeline functions (lead generation, nurture, sales, fulfilment, referral), the contractor had only fulfilment and a little referral, so build the first three.
detail: He had no consistent lead source, no nurture, no sales process. Advice: go all in on generating leads, getting them to book appointments, and winning the comparison against other roofers.

### POD-0036#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:13
claim: A business never fully runs without the owner; treat it as a garden you tend, not a machine.
detail: Even the most successful founders think about their companies daily. The owner on autopilot gets bored within days. The "beach lifestyle" is a marketing image.

### POD-0036#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:59
claim: Hormozi's early gym years show hard work first, then smarter work: he now works about 70% as hard but about five times smarter.
detail: For the first nine months he slept on the gym floor, trained eight sessions a day, did marketing, appointment setting, evening sales consultations and billing, and slept about four hours a night. He says hard work was how he earned the skill to work smarter later.
numbers: About 4 hours of sleep for about 9 months; 8 training sessions a day; now about 70% of the effort, about 5x the effectiveness.

### POD-0036#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:59
claim: Price your own time: he does not drink because one unproductive day costs more than the night is worth.
detail: He calls it a business decision, not a religious one. The market ignores your idea of work-life balance and only rewards skill, so decide how fast you want to get good and what you will give up.
