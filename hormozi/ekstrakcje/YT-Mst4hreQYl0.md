### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:58
claim: Offering a free helpful step first, instead of asking to buy or request a quote, can bring three to five times as many buyers from the same traffic.
detail: Of 100 visitors, "buy now" gets maybe one. Helping first, then offering, reaches a multiple of that. It raises conversion, not traffic, so the gain falls to the bottom line. A direct "book a call" works only for warm, informed audiences.
numbers: 3-5x more buyers from the same 100 visitors (his illustration).

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:39
claim: Test the packaging of a lead magnet harder than its contents, since the headline alone can change demand 2-10x.
detail: A failed magnet means that magnet failed, not that magnets fail; same as one weak ad. Provided the content really solves a problem, the wrapper decides uptake. More advanced, business-owner audiences will probably see a webinar as a sales pitch; in his case, offering a consume-it-yourself case study got a strong response from them.
numbers: headline change moves magnet uptake 2x to 10x.
### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:22
claim: Answer "freebie seekers" with qualification: show the magnet only to prospects who pass a dropdown and send others to a different offer.
detail: Do not solve the core problem in the magnet; solve one that leads into it. Sell where deprivation is high, as with a dessert after an entree: the free item is the entree at cost, the paid item the pricier dessert.

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#04 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: objections-closing, offers | fw: Three Lead Magnet Types (reveal a problem, sample, one step) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:27
claim: A reveal-a-problem magnet should also show the gap between today and the solved state, in numbers, ideally for problems that worsen with waiting.
detail: Website-speed-test example: each second of load time costs 3% conversion; cutting load from 9 to 3 seconds means an 18% increase, small against the price of the fix. Fits posture checks, termite inspections, financial audits, because urgency builds. Be specific about negatives and vague about positives; this is more compliant and sets realistic expectations.
numbers: 3% conversion lost per second of load; 9s to 3s = +18%.

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#08 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: unit-economics | fw: Four Delivery Mechanisms | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:57
claim: Free services cost little if limited to qualified prospects: budget, authority, need and willingness to act now.
detail: Math: an hour of labour at $25 hard cost delivers something worth $250+; if one in four recipients buys, CAC is about $100, acceptable when a customer earns much more. Forms include free audits, same-day service, partial done-for-you. His first business offered a year of free training to collect testimonials; when demand outgrew capacity he asked them to pay and almost all agreed.
numbers: $25 cost, $250 value, 1-in-4 conversion = $100 CAC.

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#10 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: MAGIC Naming Formula | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:50
claim: Name a magnet by advertising the result, not the vehicle, and test candidate names by small ad tests or by polling your audience.
detail: His first gym ran a six-week squat and deadlift programme named for the glute result, since it sold better than a lifting seminar. Test with ad headlines to a clear avatar, or poll followers (screen out non-avatars), text people, or ask for A or B in comments. He split-tested six titles of his book, plus cover image and subtitle: two days of testing against two years of writing.
numbers: 6 title variants tested; 2 days testing vs 2 years writing.

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#12 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:27:36
claim: CTA formula: clear not clever, the exact next action, and a reason to act now.
detail: Salespeople who ask most often close most, so ask often and keep the right to ask by supplying value between asks (maximise value per second times number of asks). Place CTAs inside, before and after the magnet. Chapter-end gift links in his books lead to a site, then to a workshop or investment.

### YT-Mst4hreQYl0#13 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:30:18
claim: A service business always has a real capacity limit; state how many spots remain, or run rolling cohorts for a large business.
detail: Customers cannot see your size, so a small business can use its actual capacity ("four spots left"). A large one caps weekly starts or sets a Friday deadline. About 78% of businesses are services, so this applies widely.
