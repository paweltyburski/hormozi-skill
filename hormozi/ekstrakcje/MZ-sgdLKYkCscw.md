### MZ-sgdLKYkCscw#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: pricing, paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A three-person AI commercial studio burns most of its revenue and sells $25,000 ads when TV costs $500,000.
detail: Founder has 20 years in advertising. Two SMB clients came from cold inbound via Google search. Target price $50,000, one tenth of broadcast. Pitching ex-colleagues is hard because of AI negativity.
numbers: Revenue $250k; burn $32k a month; price $25,000 per ad, target $50,000; cost about $18k per 30-second ad.

### MZ-sgdLKYkCscw#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:22
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: he'd rather have the AI be a secret, since people just want customers, and sell at the traditional price.
detail: He would rather sell at around the traditional price and pocket the rest. A company he looked at that used a DIY ad method found no one cared and became a marketing agency.
### MZ-sgdLKYkCscw#03 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: paid-ads, verticals | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:38
claim: Sell creative volume to mid-market and up: take over the whole creative team for advertisers who need dozens of pieces.
detail: Qualify by a year of profitable advertising and large spend; aim at about $50 per decent ad. Media buying is the easy part, one buyer. Meta rewards volume and variety, not a few polished ads.
numbers: Qualify: $100,000+ a month ad spend, $1M+ a year, 50-100 creatives needed; target cost about $50 per ad.
