### MZ-uJo2TDJOyzw#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, pricing | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A plastic surgery consultant fixing every client problem should make the HR and hiring service his core.
detail: Offers: startups, assessments, fractional COO, HR. The staffing side is recurring and repeatable but he finds it boring. Practices want time, not profit. Leads come from referrals and conferences.
numbers: Revenue $1.2M, goal $5M; on-site assessment 2 days plus 60 days implementation for $15,000; 1-2 new practices a month; 8 conferences a year; travels about three weeks a month.

### MZ-uJo2TDJOyzw#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:14
claim: Your business serves customers, not your intellectual curiosity; do the boring repeated work.
detail: Between $1M and $3M revenue is typically when owners narrow the avatar and the product set.
numbers: Narrowing typically happens at $1-3M revenue.

### MZ-uJo2TDJOyzw#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: offers, verticals | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:29
claim: Raise the core service price to $35-50k, scaled to headcount, and speak in the buyer's units, such as surgeries.
detail: Cut the on-site days and use freed travel time for more conferences. Frame: roughly four surgeries, one day of work, returns twenty back.
numbers: Price $15,000 to $35,000-$50,000; conferences 8 to 24 a year.

### MZ-uJo2TDJOyzw#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:20
claim: Narrow the avatar for operational efficiency only if delivery differs; if delivery is the same, vary the messaging.
detail: Med spas can be served by the same back end with different hooks and sales messages.
