### YT-Q2VUuUGfpNY#01 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Crazy Eight | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:08
claim: Upsell means a better or bigger version of the same thing; cross-sell means an adjacent need; a downsell can mean referring a non-buyer to a partner.
detail: Examples: a small soda to a large one is an upsell, a burger with the soda is a cross-sell, a Honda to a Lexus is an upsell. Be precise about the type of sale you are discussing.

### YT-Q2VUuUGfpNY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:52
claim: The cross-sell is often where the profit sits: the first purchase can be a loss leader for the next natural need.
detail: Everyone needs the first item, but the next one carries the margin. If you know the numbers you can build the model on this.

### YT-Q2VUuUGfpNY#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: lead-getters | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:40
claim: Monetize people who cannot afford the main offer by referring them to a partner for a commission, or by selling a cheaper delivery of the same outcome.
detail: Paid traffic and sales calls produce many who say no. Refer them to a partner who specialises in that customer and take a referral commission, or create a done-with-you or do-it-yourself version of your done-for-you service, or an at-home version of a gym service.

### YT-Q2VUuUGfpNY#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Virtuous Cycle of Price | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:43
claim: Set prices by looking at mature large businesses, not at small ones, since the small ones copy each other's averages and are broke.
detail: Caution: some large firms use funded penetration pricing, so study firms that have grown and then sustained, whose pricing already proved it produces margin.

### YT-Q2VUuUGfpNY#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: unit-economics | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:42
claim: Raising a $20 price to $30 on a $10 cost doubles gross profit per sale, which few other levers do.
detail: Few other levers can double, triple or quadruple lifetime gross profit per client the way price can, and price is one that people have big limiting beliefs about and struggle to increase.
numbers: $10 cost; price $20 to $30; gross profit $10 to $20.
