### MM-16#02 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: content-brand, leads-fundamentals | fw: Free with Consumption | novelty: canon | ts: 00:01:39
claim: Free with Consumption gives away valuable education — 20 minutes to 5 days long — that breaks the prospect's beliefs, then offers what solves their next concern.
detail: Formats: video, PDF, audio, live event, workshop, book, webinar, VSL, video training — anything with undivided attention. You give away a selling tool: good ones deliver real value, bad ones are just pitches. Systematically break the beliefs prospects get stuck on — "FAQs on steroids" — then make an offer or use embedded calls to action for the next step.
numbers: 20 minutes to 5 days of consumption.

### MM-16#08 | FRAMEWORK | topic: promotions-launches | also: money-models | fw: Challenge Structure | novelty: canon | ts: 00:08:25
claim: Five-day content sequence: story and perspective shift, internal fear, external obstacle, offer teaser, pitch, then proof-heavy repitch.
detail: Day 1: our story, your story, why we can help, what we'll cover, and how our approach differs. Day 2: their ability/insecurity ("I can't do it"). Day 3: the external excuse ("something will block me"). Day 4: value plus "the offer comes tomorrow". Day 5: pitch, then repitch with Q&A, success stories, mini case studies and a restacked offer. Compressible to one day or a 90-minute video.

### MM-16#11 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: sales-process, strategy-positioning | fw: Free with Consumption | novelty: canon | ts: 00:09:11
claim: Beginners can run it one-to-one: five one-hour sessions toward a goal, permission on day one to make an offer at the end, then the pitch.
detail: Complex at scale, simplest one-to-one. Day 1: ask "Is it OK if at the end I make you an offer to work together long term?" — if no, stop. Learn goals, obstacles and past friction, CLOSER-style (clarify, label, past pain, sell the vacation, explain concerns, reinforce). Days 2–4: break their main limiting beliefs. End: recap, ask for referrals, then "Mind if I tell you about that offer now?" Unsure what to offer? Ask "What would working with me for $3,000 a year look like?" — after about 20 conversations you'll know. Selling after five hours of value is easy; just text people to schedule (00:13:03).
numbers: 5 × 1-hour sessions; ~$3,000/year probe; ~20 conversations.
applies_to: Beginners without sales skill or audience.

### MM-16#15 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: money-models | fw: Free with Consumption | novelty: canon | ts: 00:14:13
claim: A real strategy call — promising not to take a card — turns qualification into free value, then hands qualified prospects to a salesperson.
detail: Say you won't ask for a card, and mean it. Use the call to diagnose what constrains their business or goals; having helped, you can ethically book them with a salesperson. Edify the rep as someone who recently helped people like them with this system — no absurd hype ("John cured cancer").

### MM-16#16 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: canon | ts: 00:14:58
claim: Every sale must break three beliefs: circumstances stop me, other people stop me, and I can't do it.
detail: (1) Circumstances: "no money" means not resourceful, "no time" means not prioritising — move blame to themselves. (2) Other people (partner, spouse, employees, kids): they own their life; ask for support, not permission; reframe ("this will help your kids / your marriage"). (3) Self — fear of failing: have them confront the decision with the variables for a logical choice, framed as unrushed — decided long ago, today is just the first action.

### MM-16#17 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: content-brand | fw: Belief-Breaking Sequence | novelty: canon | ts: 00:16:13
claim: Break a belief in five steps — what you believed, why it's wrong, what's right, why it's right, proof — teaching concepts, not tactics.
detail: Everyone gets value from concepts; only executors get value from tactics. Teach generously the concepts that underpin what you sell.
