### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:36
claim: Brand growth compounds with years of volume: Hormozi's own subscriber and download figures rise steeply from year to year.
detail: Do not compare your first year to someone's tenth. A weekly post for ten years means 520 pieces against 52, all still bringing people in. First podcast was July 2017.
numbers: YouTube subscribers: year 1 about 88,000; year 2 over 600,000; third year so far over 1.1M. Podcast: about 2,000 downloads a month after two years; about 2M a month after six.

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:34
claim: Count your experience from when you got serious, not from when you first dabbled; shorten the gap between starting and committing.
detail: A person who "trained five years" but went to the gym on and off had really about six months of serious training. Comparing yourself on the wrong clock produces needless discouragement.

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:24
claim: Scale the unscalable: run occasional live Q&As that give someone access they would never otherwise get, and let the audience watch.
detail: A star cannot attend every fan's wedding but a surprise appearance simulates it for millions. His version is a sporadic live Q&A with audience members, plus free books, audiobooks and courses that spread one piece of energy to everyone.

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#04 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:13

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Hormozi chose to own his books for life rather than take a publisher's upfront check of a few million dollars he says he could have taken, so he can give them away as he sees fit.
detail: He says that, at least for him, conditions change, and deciding now for something to live with for life feels steep. Many creators sign away rights too early, and he notes people who have seen it before know what happens. If you have the stomach to be patient and believe you will go all the way, he says it never makes sense to sell.
numbers: A few million dollar upfront check from a publisher that he says he could have taken (hypothetical).

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#16 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:28:27
claim: For a personal brand "you are the niche": the unique mix of topics and their proportions forms your thumbprint.
detail: A business brand centres on products and customers; a personal brand centres on the person. Start with what you know deeply, which you probably underrate. Teach what you know rather than preach what others should do. Audience strength grows with the number of your interests a person shares.

### YT-5JLqmQlGG0U#24 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:52:05
claim: Inverted thinking on brand: to guarantee failure, talk about what you do not know, start-stop, sell early, sell the wrong things, associate with the wrong people, wait for perfect conditions and never change.
detail: Flip each to build: talk about what you know, stay consistent, delay selling and give far more than you ask, sell only what needs no change in who you are, partner with audiences like yours, start today with what you have, and embrace change in platforms, packaging and yourself.
