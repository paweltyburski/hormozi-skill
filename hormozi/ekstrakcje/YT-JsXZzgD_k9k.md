### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The entrepreneur, not the opportunity, limits a business, and the limit sits in one of three areas: skills, character traits or beliefs.
detail: He pictures a ladder with skills on one side, beliefs on the other and character traits as the rungs. A person can have strong skills and character and still be capped by a wrong belief about the world. Apparent overnight successes usually had two of the three high and one missing piece.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#02 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: His jump from about $2M to $7M to just under $30M in roughly three years came from a changed belief about which model was possible, not from new skills.
detail: He did not know licensing existed as a model. Learning it let him move into a better vehicle. He calls the vehicle a non-limiter; his belief about it was the constraint.
numbers: Revenue $2M to $7M to just under $30M in about 3 years.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:36
claim: Avoiding the feeling of being bad at something stretches the time to learn it; confronting it early collapses that time.
detail: Hormozi put off building a landing page for about three years, then did it in four hours the day he sat down. Looking things up (a new site widget in two hours) shortens as you advance. His point is to accept sucking quickly.
numbers: Landing page: 3 years of avoidance, then 4 hours of work. Widget: 2 hours.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:36
claim: One-on-one mentorship from people far ahead gave him the highest return, partly because they can spot skill, character and belief gaps.
detail: He paid many people for their time. Under that came masterminds, then other formats; Google by hours spent was the largest learning source.
numbers: Paid $20,000 to $30,000 per hour to various mentors.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:32
claim: When you pay for information you are really paying for the time someone spent sorting it, so money is exchanged for saved time.
detail: Information is free but finding the right piece costs time. Communities also pre-filter information. Seeing peers who are no smarter succeed also raised his belief that he could do it.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#06 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:23
claim: His order of buying education went from free interviews, to a mastermind, to a weekend workshop, to events, to coaching programmes bought for teammates.
detail: Free conversations with older guys (if someone is too far ahead, go down a rung or pay), then a $10,000 mastermind that became a three-month apprenticeship, a two-day ads workshop, a marketing conference, and later coaching programmes for staff.
numbers: Mastermind $10,000; ads workshop of 10 people over 2 days; apprenticeship about 12 hours a day for 3 months.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:09
claim: Hormozi thinks coaching programmes suit a specific problem or method, and that masterminds and one-on-one mentorship are more valuable if you have been stuck a long time.
detail: Coaching programmes buy speed and accountability on one method (for example YouTube ads or cold calling). If you have been stuck a long time, he thinks you probably have a strategic issue more than a tactical one, which is where masterminds and mentors are more valuable.
### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#08 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:56
claim: The best belief breaker is watching a peer do what you thought was impossible.
detail: In an early mastermind a member making about $350,000 a month spoke poorly, and he and his wife concluded that if he could do it so could they, when they earned only $20,000 to $30,000 a month.
numbers: Peer at about $350,000 per month; Hormozi at about $20,000-$30,000 per month then.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#09 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:56
claim: In a mastermind he offered every member a free rewrite of their sales script, then redeemed those favours as tutoring in the skills he lacked.
detail: He rewrote scripts in his structure for everyone, then cashed the IOUs for landing page and ads tutoring. Become valuable to each person first.

### YT-JsXZzgD_k9k#10 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:52
claim: A character trait is shown by actions, not by how it feels, so feeling impatient while acting patiently still means you are patient.
detail: His wife pointed out that his actions were long-term, so the discomfort did not make him impatient. A trait is a matter of degree built by repeatedly acting in line with it despite discomfort.
