### MZ-X4iOxTSMrJE#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A service guiding seniors through sorting belongings before assisted living had burned money on marketing firms; Hormozi called it one degree from a gold mine.
detail: It follows families from home to assisted living, memory care and after death, so LTV is probably huge. Sales split 50/50 between seniors and adult children.
applies_to: Niche B2C service with long family journeys.

### MZ-X4iOxTSMrJE#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Split the offer in two: a small paid preparation service now and the actual move later when the family is ready.
detail: Pitch: cognitive decline comes suddenly, so decide while the parent can take part. The move then follows within about 30 days, with referral fees from recommended facilities.
numbers: Move within about 30 days of the trigger.

### MZ-X4iOxTSMrJE#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Run it on Meta with a lead magnet, capture form and phone call; the build is small.
detail: Meta is where the oldest audience sits. Test lead magnets and call out the right people; since half the buyers are the seniors, use an angle about not burdening children.
numbers: Build about $5,000 in a weekend, not $100,000 (his estimate).

### MZ-X4iOxTSMrJE#06 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:22
claim: Two co-founders with identical skills (business under $1M, aiming for $1-3M) should specialise quickly around the constraint.
detail: Hormozi and his partner, both from sales, split marketing/sales versus delivery/team. Here sales is the constraint, so that founder works the pipeline and the other absorbs all customer issues.

### MZ-X4iOxTSMrJE#08 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:40
claim: A South Carolina personal injury firm buying leads from vendors should hire in-house media buyers to capture the vendors' margin.
detail: In law the top three firms own billboards and others compete on PPC, while social ads look underused. Headhunt the best media buyers with more pay, title and autonomy, or acquihire a small shop, with a small share of upside.
applies_to: Local PI firm, constraint is client volume.
