### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Eight C's make recurring revenue stickier: consumption, collateral, cost of switching, choice, control of money flow, cause, community and contracts.
detail: Each is a continuum, not a yes-or-no box. You do not need all eight; ask for each one how far you can push your business along it. Consumption means making the product worth using again through onboarding, value and habit.
applies_to: Any business with repeat purchase or subscription

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Recurring revenue is a spectrum, so ask how recurring your business is, not whether it is.
detail: Even a real estate agent can seek lifetime value from the same customer's later sales; the bank and escrow firm already earn recurring revenue from one house purchase. He claims adding recurring revenue to a physical product business multiplied lifetime value by 2.5.
numbers: Lifetime value 2.5x after adding recurring revenue to a physical products business.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Collateral: hold something of the customer's that makes leaving costly.
detail: Storage units hold goods, Dropbox and Google Drive hold files, a CRM holds contacts. Look for physical or digital items customers keep with you.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#04 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:54
claim: Raise switching cost with a large upfront fee that creates a sunk-cost bias.
detail: If a service costs $5,000 once and then $100 a month, customers cancel less than if it were $100 a month alone. Cost of switching also includes time and effort.
numbers: $5,000 upfront, $100 a month recurring.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:52
claim: Fewer alternatives make revenue stickier: patents, trade secrets, a unique combination of skills, or niching.
detail: Differentiation means reducing the buyer's choices. Being the only provider on an island is sticky but may be short-lived.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:11
claim: Recurring charges that are taken before the customer sees them are tolerated more than visible bills.
detail: Uber takes its cut before paying out, and employees feel taxes less than owners who see the cheques. He says he is describing efficacy, not ethics, and the lever is to get paid first or off the top.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:05
claim: Cause and community retain because people stay consistent with their identity.
detail: Churches (tithing), charities and brands like Tom's keep payers whose identity is tied to the cause. A community leaves people feeling they are quitting a group, not a business.

### YT-jOM0m34dxz4#08 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:38
claim: Selling a fixed term as one total is stickier than pay-as-you-go at the same monthly price.
detail: $2,000 a month month-to-month might stay four months; sold as a $12,000 six-month package paid in six instalments, it likely lasts longer, then rolls to monthly. He estimates this can lift lifetime gross profit two to three times, though it may be harder to sell. People who commit verbally to six months stay longer even if some still break it.
numbers: $2,000/month vs $12,000 six-month; assumed 4-month average stay; 2x to 3x lifetime gross profit (his estimate).
