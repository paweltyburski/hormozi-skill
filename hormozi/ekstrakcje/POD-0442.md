### POD-0442#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:54
claim: Five checks for a business: unique, expensive, sticky, "air" (almost no marginal cost) and run by a person of integrity with a long-term view.
detail: Unique can be a story rather than a different product, such as nobody having owned as many gyms or done two years of turnarounds. Expensive means gross profit per sale, not just price. Sticky means recurring through model or habit. The manager must not trade long-term for a quick pop.

### POD-0442#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: He wants about 97.5% gross margin rather than 80-90%, as a business with almost no marginal cost.
detail: A 95% margin business is twice as profitable as a 90% one because cost per unit is half, so you can sell twice as many for each increment of cost. Most people read it as 5 points.
numbers: Target 97.5% gross margin; 90% to 95% = 2x profit per cost unit.
### POD-0442#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:14
claim: Prefer one-time inputs with compounding returns, such as a book good enough that each reader passes it to others, over ad spend that must be repeated.
detail: Observed among people worth $250M+. His book was a test: a single Instagram post, no ads, selling about 1,000 copies a day. The aim is each of 10 readers giving it to two more.
numbers: About 1,000 copies a day with no ads.

### POD-0442#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:03
claim: There are six ways to get customers, and only word of mouth and referrals is quadratic; the others return linearly.
detail: The six: paid ads, earned media (followers), owned media (permission lists, DMs, CRM), manual outbound, affiliates, and word of mouth. Ads give a fixed return per dollar; calls keep a steady response rate; referrals multiply.

### POD-0442#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:42
claim: Product-market fit means customers stay, keep paying and buy more, not that they buy once; keep fixing the product until it sells itself, then turn on advertising.
detail: Promote only enough to fund improvement. When each customer brings one or two more, profitable ads follow.
