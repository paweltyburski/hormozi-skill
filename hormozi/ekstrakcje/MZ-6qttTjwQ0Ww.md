### MZ-6qttTjwQ0Ww#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:16
claim: Pick the top 10 to 16 of 80 clients by liking, spend and tenure, then rewrite messaging and offer for people like them.
detail: Three filters: which you like most, which pay the most, which stay longest. Then advertise only to that type. He calls it one of the most profitable ways to grow, not the easiest.
numbers: Top 10 or 16 of 80 clients.

### MZ-6qttTjwQ0Ww#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:16
claim: A PE pricing head's playbook: know the customer base better than the seller, find the most profitable customers and their channels, stop low-ROI channels, multiply the best.
detail: Steps: analyse customers, identify channels of the best ones, cut channels with weak return, then expand by about 5x the channel with the highest LTV to CAC. Because the business can already serve its existing customer count, little new infrastructure is needed. He says it is one of the first things his team does with every company.
numbers: 5x on the best-LTV:CAC channel; profit typically doubles or triples within five years.
