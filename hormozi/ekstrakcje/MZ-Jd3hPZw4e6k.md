### MZ-Jd3hPZw4e6k#01 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A real estate services firm needs the founder and COO to close every external deal, capping growth.
detail: Setters keep asking the founder to join. Leads are not the issue; the firm trains agents well but not sales staff.
numbers: Revenue $17-18M, goal $100M; two closers bring in about $5M a year externally; about 20 hours a week in closing meetings.

### MZ-Jd3hPZw4e6k#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:56
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: he and his COO should shift identity from sales god to sales training god, seeing each sale they close as a failure, and Hormozi would consider not paying commissions on sales they help close.
detail: He said the commission change would probably get the setters to stop asking the founder to close.
### MZ-Jd3hPZw4e6k#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:04
claim: A scalable sale rests on the prospect's own answers, not the salesperson's authority.
detail: Founder-led consultative selling relies on expertise and is hard to staff. A young rep asks questions in the right order and gives no answers: intro frame, discovery of pain, match pains to solutions, pitch on those matches.

### MZ-Jd3hPZw4e6k#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:26
claim: Cut the script by half or more to the minimum needed for a buying decision and answer questions about the sale, not the product.
detail: The rep's role is to establish whether the prospect has the problem, like a receptionist checking for a heart needing surgery. Detailed product answers belong to specialists.

### MZ-Jd3hPZw4e6k#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Most companies hire and see who sticks; training daily on a proven script makes almost anyone closable.
detail: Getting someone to say the right thing at the right moment is trainable, but most firms train too little.
numbers: His estimate: about 97% of businesses do not know how to train sales.
