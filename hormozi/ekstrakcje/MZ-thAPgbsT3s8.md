claim: A new managing partner at a life-insurance financial planning firm inherited aged clients not contacted for years and wanted to cross-sell.
detail: Some are unhappy from neglect; he wants to lift revenue quickly alongside new-client channels.
numbers: 14,000 existing clients; one of 78 managing partners.

### MZ-thAPgbsT3s8#02 | SCRIPT | topic: retention-ltv | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Open with the truth: we have not been in touch, that is on us, new management, and here is something new that could help you.
detail: Sequence: admit the gap, name the change, offer a specific new reason to talk. A direct call by the agent.

### MZ-thAPgbsT3s8#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:53

detail: Aim for "glad he called" so later calls get picked up; avoid a courtesy call without a reason.
