### POD-0108#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Generating should-do items is easy; the skill that decides income is judging which actually contribute to the bottom line.
detail: At any size, from a four-person gym to 85,000 employees, the list of things worth doing exceeds resources. His company grew fast not by doing more but by directing limited people, money and attention to the highest-value item, and by finding each new bottleneck.

### POD-0108#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:30
claim: Hormozi refused his sales director's request for a 150-day email nurture sequence because other work was worth more.
detail: He accepts the sequence would bring back some leads and might pay, but argued it would not beat the items he was working on.
numbers: 150-day email sequence requested.

### POD-0108#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:18
claim: People often start lesser tasks to avoid the uncomfortable one that would matter most.
detail: The key item may demand discomfort or a new skill, so owners procrastinate with busy work.

### POD-0108#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:39
claim: Ignore sunk cost: finishing a half-done project can earn less than spending the same weeks on the real bottleneck.
detail: Owners say they will finish an email sequence first after five weeks of work. Hormozi's reply: three more weeks on it still earns less than three weeks on the bottleneck. His coaching listens only for the weak link.
numbers: Example: 5 weeks already spent, 3 more to finish.

### POD-0108#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:08
claim: Fix the business's weakest link, not your personal weakness; staff the missing trait with someone else.
detail: Strengths drive the business early, then another function holds it back (his deadlift lockout analogy: more strength off the floor does not help). If you lack empathy and retention is weak, hire an empathetic person, give decision rights, and coach culture. A balanced business is built from unbalanced people.
