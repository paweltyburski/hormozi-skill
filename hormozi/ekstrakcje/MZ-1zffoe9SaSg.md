### MZ-1zffoe9SaSg#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:19
claim: Turn a talking-head page video into an "unboxing" that shows each component while explaining it.
detail: Demonstrations leave nothing to the imagination, and visuals plus voice give two kinds of stimulation.

### MZ-1zffoe9SaSg#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: content-brand | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:19
claim: Describe each component as moving the buyer from bad toward good, using the risk, speed and ease matrix.
detail: Ask of each bullet whether it makes the result more guaranteed, faster or easier. Write them as avoid-pain or gain-faster statements.
### MZ-1zffoe9SaSg#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:41
claim: Shoppers read a page like Amazon: click every tile image, skim bullets, check the price and options, then decide.
detail: So each tile should carry one feature, and the words underneath restate it for readers. Reviews will help if good.

### MZ-1zffoe9SaSg#04 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: retention-ltv | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:42
claim: Match each feature tile to one avatar problem and fill it with one testimonial created by running a training and asking for feedback.
detail: A member usually joins with one relationship in mind (partner, boss, phone), so one tile sells them. With only 15 members, run a live session on the topic and screenshot the feedback.
numbers: 15 members in the example.

### MZ-1zffoe9SaSg#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:20
claim: Test every sentence on a page by asking if it will make a higher share of viewers buy or bring in a new group who would not have.
detail: In almost every sales letter, the most-read parts are the headline, first line and the PS. He likes using the PS as an away statement: say who the offer is not for so the right people self-select.
