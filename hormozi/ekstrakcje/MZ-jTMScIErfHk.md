### MZ-jTMScIErfHk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: You are solving tomorrow's problems with today's resources, so early solutions will look unscalable.
detail: The essence of value creation is solving problems that did not look scalable. The advice is not to quit, take feedback and improve; the future you will have the money, skill and track record you lack now.

### MZ-jTMScIErfHk#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: Quit the idea when customers reject it after a real test, but not the business.
detail: In his dog-skateboard example, if a hundred prospects are pitched and none want it, even when shown it, you probably shouldn't sell that product. Treat that as a local failure rather than a global one, and keep iterating.
numbers: Test example: pitch 100 target customers.
