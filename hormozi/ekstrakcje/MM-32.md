### MM-32#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: Dalio Learning Loop | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Learning follows Ray Dalio's loop from Principles: set big goals, fail, extract principles from the failure, improve, then set more audacious goals.
detail: Hormozi shares what he has learned so others can skip some of the failure part of the loop. He claims no single right way, only what has worked: several businesses taken past $100M enterprise value and one near $100M a year in revenue. He can't yet say how to reach $1B.

### MM-32#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:01:08
claim: You cannot lose if you do not quit — hopelessness and feeling like a disappointment are normal parts of entrepreneurship, not signs you're on the wrong path.
detail: From a note Hormozi wrote in his early Gym Launch years: feeling hopeless means welcome to entrepreneurship; feeling you'll never make it means you're on the right path. Expect to hold total confidence and huge doubt at once. Keep moving, fighting and improving — one more step, call, sale. He adds that dying without sharing your knowledge wastes a life; making business education accessible is his mission (00:02:26).
quote: You cannot lose if you do not quit.
