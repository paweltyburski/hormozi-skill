### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:19
claim: A managed-IT firm at $2M, aiming for $10M by 2030, has no sales team and the owner closes every deal.
detail: Sells to companies of 30-100 staff on three-year contracts. Constraint is lead generation, not conversion. SEO via a new agency produced about 10 first calls, six unqualified. Hormozi says keep SEO, then choose one more channel.
numbers: $7,500/month average contract; 3-year terms; $2M revenue; ~10 calls, 4 qualified.
applies_to: B2B services with long sales cycles

### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:15
claim: To find the constraint, ask whether the shortfall underneath demand is lead generation or conversion.
detail: Then ask how leads currently arrive and who runs it, before recommending a channel.

### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:26
claim: Cut unqualified calls by requiring a pre-call form (such as employee count) and cancelling disqualified ones.
detail: About 40% of callers qualifying at this price level counts as good.
numbers: ~40% qualified calls is good for mid-size B2B.

### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:39
claim: Hormozi thinks PPC is probably easier to start than social for this caller (less creative, warmer leads), but lead costs are higher so the sales motion must be more dialed.
detail: PPC captures existing demand with warmer leads and less creative; social creates demand. The first Core Four options named here are affiliates or PPC.
### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-process | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:27
claim: Respond to PPC leads within 60 seconds, by double dial and text; Meta leads tolerate about five minutes.
detail: PPC is competitive, so a slower responder loses the lead to a rival.
numbers: PPC response under 60 seconds; Meta about 5 minutes.

### MZ-0d7JlGtlsVs#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:37
claim: Never outsource acquisition (traffic), conversion or delivery; outsource legal, accounting, insurance.
detail: Own what touches the customer so you control the message, the conversion events and the product. Learning to sell improves marketing and helps recruiting.
