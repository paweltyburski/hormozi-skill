### POD-0055#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Hormozi spent $250,000, roughly all he had made, on a second gym in month 15, and it earned no more than the first.
detail: The first gym went from zero to 250 active recurring members in about nine months, with sales and training management outsourced. The second was fitted with big equipment, forgetting that a micro gym cannot outspend big chains on facilities; its edge is service, which supports prices several times others.
numbers: First gym: 250 recurring members in 9 months. Second gym: opened month 15, cost $250,000.

### POD-0055#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:20
claim: Do not open a second location until the first has run boringly for six to nine months at strong take-home.
detail: After a quick jump in revenue, hold at least six months of solid margin, stack cash, pay down mortgage, car and debt first. Adding a site multiplies problems and hours (40 hours at one gym becomes about 80 at two); ego and status push owners to expand early.
numbers: Thresholds: 6 to 9 months at $20,000-$25,000 a month take-home before location two.

### POD-0055#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:19
claim: Reinvesting all profit with no pay is a repeated all-in bet; keep money in the bank.
detail: Owners who reinvest every month for three or four years often close after a few bad months because they hold nothing in reserve. Do not buy equipment as soon as cash appears; owning the business is for making money and building freedom (financial, location, time), so outsource it fully.

### POD-0055#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:30
claim: Typical case: a gym lifts revenue from about $15,000 to $45,000-$50,000 a month in about three months, then wrongly rushes to a second site.
detail: In one example recurring revenue rose from $25,000 to $45,000 a month in three months. The advice is to keep making money and keep stacking the extra cash.
numbers: $15,000 to $45,000-$50,000 monthly revenue in ~3 months; recurring $25,000 to $45,000 in 3 months.
