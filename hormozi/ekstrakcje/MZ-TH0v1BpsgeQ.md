### MZ-TH0v1BpsgeQ#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An Atlanta roofing and storm-restoration owner with 12 friendly peer firms worries about missing the PE exit window and about owners refusing to give up control.
detail: All use the same software and are interested in a merger. Hormozi finds the combined size interesting.
numbers: Combined EBITDA a little over $10M across 12 firms.
applies_to: Regional service firms preparing a consolidated exit.

### MZ-TH0v1BpsgeQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Take an option to buy 100% of each firm at a multiple above what it would get alone, then sell the combined company higher and keep the spread.
detail: Like wholesaling a contract. Solo, a firm is unsellable or fetches about 5x; offer 6x to 7x. The organiser can contribute goodwill. Each owner's slice reflects their contribution, and an M&A attorney papers it.
numbers: ~5x alone; 6x-7x option price; ~9x target sale.

### MZ-TH0v1BpsgeQ#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:51
claim: To close a multi-owner sale you need one person talking and in control.
detail: Aligned incentives (each wants a bigger slice) keep everyone pushing the multiple up.
