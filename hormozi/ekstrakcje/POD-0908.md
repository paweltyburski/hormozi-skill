### POD-0908#02 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:04
claim: Seller of a ~$35K program whose prospects agree on price but cannot pay is told to add real qualification questions up front and route only qualified leads to sales.
business: coaching-education — program around real-estate wholesaling and liquidity (description partly cut, unclear in audio); value collected within ~90 days, later only a CRM retained
detail: Problem: low close rate; buyers cannot afford the first payment or get financing. Diagnosis: unqualified leads, not a sales problem. Advice: add qualifiers to the funnel and the lead magnet (all company SOPs); thank-you page sends best leads to sales, the rest to an automated offer recovering ad cost. "Are you willing to invest" is not a qualifier; define the avatar first.
questions: why this model? / what do you want to happen? / what share stays 12 months? / LTV to CAC? / what stops you doing more?
numbers: LTV ~$35K within ~90 days; CAC ~$5K; 7:1
speakers: owner: facts and questions; Hormozi: diagnosis, advice, book-launch example
applies_to: high-ticket offers with many unqualified prospects

### POD-0908#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: unit-economics | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:17
claim: Add qualification friction to a funnel; he can hardly recall it ever making him less money.
detail: Expect cost per call or click to rise while CAC may fall and LTV rises. Reasons: higher close rates, a sales team not worn down by refusals, protected reputation, and accepting everyone repels buyers who can afford you. Example: book-launch opt-ins were asked whether they owned a business and its revenue, so only owners above a size got a call.
