### MZ-X_EDIO-6iO0#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Open a second location only if the first runs six months without you.
detail: Owner stays owner but makes no decisions and gets no escalations. A manager gets about 10% (later 10-20% of profit) and the owner's phone does not ring.
numbers: 6 months; manager 10% (10-20% of profit).

### MZ-X_EDIO-6iO0#04 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:34
claim: Have a grand opening plan that reaches at least 50% capacity before opening.
detail: Large chains fix numbers and spend by days out and do not open until hitting them.
numbers: at least 50% of facility capacity pre-opened.

### MZ-X_EDIO-6iO0#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:08
claim: Run the ad in three or four sub-areas before signing a lease.
detail: Use the best current ad in each; lead cost often differs 5x within one city. Open across town to avoid cannibalising.
numbers: ~$1,000 per market test; ~5x lead-cost spread; a CAC cut of 80% possible.

numbers: 400 franchised vs 67 corporate locations.
