### YT-lEIqyLE4iOY#13 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:14:03
claim: Proof ladder for educators: helped others like you, did what you want, or did work you will not and show it.
detail: Best is results for people like the viewer. Next is doing the thing the viewer also wants. Next is showing the result of work the viewer is not willing to do. Separately, if you lack proof, you can observe how someone else did the hard thing, ideally a big, successful person with easily provable metrics, and connect the dots for the audience.
