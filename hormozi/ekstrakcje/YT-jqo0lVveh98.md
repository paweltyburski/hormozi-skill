### YT-jqo0lVveh98#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:57
claim: SMB stick time depends on price: about 4-6 months at $1,500-3,000 a month, 30-40 months at $300-400.
detail: Napkin math for the $2K service: 10 sales a month x 5 months average stick x $2K ~ $100K a month run rate, at which the business stalls and units or LTV can't rise. Cheap "nuisance" services (maps ranking, reviews, local SEO for $300-500) stick for years and sell more easily, so LTV is similar but CAC stays low.
numbers: Stick 4-6 months at $1,500-3,000/month; 30-40 months at $300-400/month; steady state ~$100K/month for the example.

### YT-jqo0lVveh98#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:14
claim: Selling to small businesses works only at the cheap, automated end or at the expensive end for operators with proven metrics.
detail: Low: serve today's customers but make delivery almost nothing, using a setup fee then a small, high-margin recurring fee (big head, long tail), and offset CAC. High: raise price and serve a higher-level avatar that knows its metrics and has a sales process, instead of clients expecting you to solve what they haven't. The middle is a dead zone where agencies die.
applies_to: Marketing and service providers selling to small businesses.
