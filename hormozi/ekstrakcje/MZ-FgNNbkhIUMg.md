### MZ-FgNNbkhIUMg#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Brand influences only one part of the value equation, the perceived likelihood of achievement, by lowering risk.
detail: The other parts (speed, ease, size of the outcome) are not moved by brand. People buy a brand because it has rewarded them before, and the next product either reinforces or damages that cycle.

### MZ-FgNNbkhIUMg#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:28
claim: Give-give-ask matters less when the products themselves are consistently great, because the product makes the goodwill deposit.
detail: Customers consistently rewarded at every interaction face less friction, buy faster and accept a premium price. A flop is a withdrawal.

### MZ-FgNNbkhIUMg#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:36
claim: A restaurant chain illustrates goodwill: after about 20 good meals, one bad one is forgiven and a 21st visit still happens.
detail: A first-ever bad meal, with no history, may lose the customer for good.
numbers: 20 good visits cover one bad one (illustrative).
