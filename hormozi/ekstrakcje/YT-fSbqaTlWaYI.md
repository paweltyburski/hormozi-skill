### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#02 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:35
claim: A $600K acupuncture clinic should run lead ads for a $19-$99 offer (start at $19-$29), close it by phone to get a card on file.
detail: Local trust is high, so the offer matters more than the brand. Call every lead, take the card on the small offer, confirm the appointment, ideally sell the long plan before treating while the client is in pain, then give short-term relief and book the next visit. ### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Good friction raises lead quality; bad friction only raises cost.
detail: Cold traffic is low quality by nature; the sales process must filter. More filters make leads costlier but only useful filters make them better. A slow page adds bad friction; a video sales letter on the page adds good friction.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:11

detail: Example added friction: an application step after the opt-in.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#07 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:11
claim: A $2M beauty device brand at 4.6 ROAS should model the first three seconds of its best ads.
detail: Of about 1,000 ads in a year, take the top 5-10%, study their first three seconds and copy them onto the rest, or cut the opening off winners and paste it on 100-plus new ads, giving 10 to 50 times more creative per winner. Creative as a discipline is the lever; aiming for $20M means 10-50 times the output, supported by capturing and repurposing user content.
numbers: ROAS 4.6; top 5-10% of ads; 10-50x more creative per winner.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#08 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:17
claim: Bundle a year of consumables with the one-time device sale to lift average cart value.
detail: A buyer who has committed to using the product will accept a year's supply at a saving. Example: an $80 device plus a $40 annual pack of creams; if 30% take it, ACV rises $12, about 15%. Bundles beat one-off small add-ons, and he thinks the cart could double.
numbers: 30% take rate x $40 = $12 added to an $80 ACV (about 15%).

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#10 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:33
claim: A free Facebook or School group can feed calls four ways: onboarding call, five-video series, weekly webinar, and DM setters on active members.
detail: 1) A free onboarding call for every joiner that clarifies and labels the past before offering the next step. 2) A five-part video series that works as a split sales letter: big idea and story, three belief-breaking videos (universe, other people, self), then a call to action. 3) A weekly webinar, mostly value with a soft pitch, where setters DM people who chat. 4) Setters DM anyone who likes, comments or posts. If traffic works but calls are few, train more setters.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#11 | CASE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:18:11
claim: A $1.2M virtual-assistant firm for roofers priced its offer as a labour saving: about $25,000 a year in office labour replaced by $500 a month.
detail: Ad explains the three main tasks (estimates, card checks, similar), the saving of about $19,000 per person, with a guarantee that by day 90 clients earn at least five times the cost or get the rest of the year free. Visual proof names client size ($10M business, 20 people). Call every lead, telling them the call is not a sales call because a second call closes. Lookalike off an email list over 1,000.
numbers: $25,000/year labour vs $6,000/year ($500/month); saving about $19,000; guarantee 5x by day 90.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#12 | DECISION_RULE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:22:02
claim: Split creative effort 70-20-10: the proven ad, adjacent versions, wild ideas.
detail: Taken from Google. In an eight-hour day: about 45 minutes to one hour on the single idea, two-plus hours on adjacent ideas, the rest on variants of the same ad. Order of work, as he does it himself: he wants the 70 done first, the 20 second, and the 10 only if he has time and energy.
applies_to: Accounts at 7+ ROAS that wonder whether to keep testing or push the current ad.
numbers: 70% proven, 20% adjacent, 10% wild.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#15 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:25:12
claim: A roofing firm at 7:1 ROAS from paid search should expand with global retargeting, then Meta, with set-and-close calls.
detail: Retarget site visitors on Google Display, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube (the shadow funnel; almost always positive). Meta leads are lower intent but older audiences and high tickets make it fine. Offer a free audit (in person or virtual), call every opt-in right away to confirm and qualify, set the call if not booked, and close on the booked appointment.

### YT-fSbqaTlWaYI#16 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:42
claim: Promises are the same across a market; proof and a believable mechanism are what differ.
detail: When ROAS and trust fall, make long-term content and show more proof, including a unique mechanism backed by many stories of people in the buyer's exact position.
