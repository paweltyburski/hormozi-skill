### MZ-4bX7JKeiPOk#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A video-testimonial agency for B2B software ($6M, aiming at $20M) grew on warm inbound but converts poorly on newly generated cold leads.
detail: Average order $15K; outbound and LinkedIn ads book about 100 calls a month, 85% show, close rate about 6% (unclear in audio). Hormozi: a warm sales process is being used on cold leads. Fix: VSL, set qualification and pre-frame for the close call, a training cadence for reps, and tests on the VSL; this shortens the cycle and raises close rate with fewer reps.
numbers: $6M revenue; ~100 booked calls a month; 85% show; ~6% close; AOV $15K (project size $4K-9K per testimonial, some contracts $100K).

### MZ-4bX7JKeiPOk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Build the sales process for cold leads and it works everywhere; built for warm, it works only on warm.
detail: Example from a book launch: emailing only a warm list with a heavy bonus pitch would have sold about a third of the total, and treating the whole audience that way would have under-converted by roughly two thirds. A lower close rate than warm introductions is not necessarily bad.
numbers: Warm-only approach about 1/3 of the sales; under-conversion about 2/3.

### MZ-4bX7JKeiPOk#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Split a project offer into quality (upfront) and quantity (ongoing) to create recurring revenue for testimonials.
detail: Phase one is a $15-25K package capturing testimonials from each ICP in their language. Ongoing package supplies regular short UGC-style five-star clips because stories older than 18 months lose relevance. Buyers only wanted three or four at a time, which blocked continuity.
numbers: Upfront $15K-25K; testimonials stale after 18 months.
