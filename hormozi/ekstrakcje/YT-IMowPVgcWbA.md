### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Triangle of non-performance: they were not told what you want, they do not know how, or they are not motivated.
detail: Intel's Andy Grove (High Output Management) named two causes, skills and motivation, making a boss's job to train and motivate. Hormozi adds the first, unclear expectations. Layla supplied much of the managing side of it.

### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:28
claim: Make an expectation stick by saying it, repeating it, writing it down in several places, and adding it to daily or weekly checklists and reports.
detail: What you ask people to report signals what matters; the faster the cadence (twice daily against one quarterly slide line), the more important they take it to be.

### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#03 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:28
claim: A new sales director did no call reviews for weeks because nobody had told him it was part of the job.
detail: Hormozi assumed it was obvious; the gap was his own communication.

### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:03
claim: Training step: explain what the task looks like, do two together, record them, and specify how to report the result.
detail: Training must also cover handling the workload, so priorities leave room for the task.

### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#05 | SCRIPT | topic: management-ops | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:25
claim: Open with "it's not like you to do X, what's going on?" and then offer the three boxes: did not know, did not know how, not motivated.
detail: The opener embeds a compliment and targets the behaviour and process, not the person (from Layla). Then isolate the box, fix it, and only if it still fails call it a motivation problem.

### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:31
claim: Leila Hormozi says that when someone admits they dislike the job, moving them to another role in the company is usually not the right call; you should probably just hire the person you would normally hire.
detail: Leila says that about nine times out of ten this is what you would do, and that a track record of failure does not make a likely strong candidate. She says that if you like the person, they fit the culture and they have other skills, you might consider another role for them.
numbers: Leila: "nine times out of 10".
### YT-IMowPVgcWbA#07 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:19
claim: Check the incentives: if pay rewards four other tasks and not this one, expect no motivation; also give the task a purpose.
detail: Explain the chain: CRM call notes help finance collect and customer success personalise onboarding; then tie to self-interest, e.g. notes screen out tire-kickers so reps close more of their time.
