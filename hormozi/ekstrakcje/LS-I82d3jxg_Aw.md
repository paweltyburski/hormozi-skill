### LS-I82d3jxg_Aw#09 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:18
claim: Discovery loop: alternate "tell me more about that" and "can you give me an example" until the pain is concrete, then recap a pain stack and get agreement before a short pitch.
detail: Open with why they came and what problem they want solved; then alternate the two prompts rather than asking "why" repeatedly. Recap: "you said X, so it sounds like you have an ads problem; you said Y, a funnel problem; is that right?" Once all problems are agreed, ask permission to describe the offer. Pitch: 60-90 seconds, three things you do, each with one striking example that makes disbelief unreasonable. On a second call, re-open the frame before closing.

### LS-I82d3jxg_Aw#11 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:31:21
claim: Train a rep with a themed week of role-play and correct mistakes the moment they happen.
detail: Monday: opening and frame, about ten repetitions. Tuesday: discovery. Wednesday: pitch. Thursday: close and objections. Friday: the weakest part (often discovery again). Tell the rep in advance you will interrupt. Feedback saved for the end of a run does not change behaviour.
