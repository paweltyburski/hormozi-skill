### POD-250819-03#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:02
claim: The refund condition can be tied to results, to actions, or to both, and each tests a different bet.
detail: Results: they win if they reach the outcome whatever they do. Actions: they win if they do what you ask (attend, log, do homework) whatever the outcome. Both: they bet on following directions and on your directions working, useful when customers lack the skill to succeed alone. Whatever is chosen must be simple to track.

detail: Choose actions the customer already tracks (phones count steps, word processors count words, cameras date photos). detail: Instead of three free months, spread the credit over 12 months: $600 / 12 = $50, so the customer pays $150 a month. Present this first; if they ask for the credit up front, explain that people fall off when they pay nothing.

claim: A fitness-certification business advertised a full-ride scholarship, then phoned everyone else with a partial scholarship.
detail: Applicants gave contact details and answered "why should we pick you". ### POD-250819-03#11 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: pricing | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:18
claim: Put a monetary value on the grand prize so it works as a price anchor for the discounted offer.
detail: Advertise the full value of the package, even if it sells for less. Create the promotional offer by adding a discount or bonus or by changing the offer slightly, which ethically justifies the lower price. A bigger stated value makes the discount more compelling.

### POD-250819-03#12 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: promotions-launches | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:19:36
claim: Worked discount maths: a 10% price cut presents as paying 36% of the stated value.
detail: Grand prize has a $5,000 value and a $2,000 retail price. Everyone else gets it at $1,800 (10% off retail) and is told it is $5,000 of value for $1,800, a 64% gap between value and price. Rule of thumb: the discount equals 10 to 30% of gross margin.
numbers: $5,000 value; $2,000 retail; $1,800 promotional price; 64% value-to-price gap.

### POD-250819-03#14 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:56
claim: Eligibility questions double as sales intelligence, and entry effort trades volume for quality.
detail: Ask questions like why this program, why now, what is your goal and why does it matter to you; the answers become ammunition on the call. The more work entry takes, the fewer but better qualified the entrants. Add qualifying actions such as attending an event, posting or joining a group to raise interest and spread the giveaway.

### POD-250819-03#15 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: objections-closing | fw: Urgency | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:47
claim: Add a deadline at three points: to enter, to claim and to use the prize.
detail: Show the entry window in ads, give winners a claim window and book their call the same or next day, then state how long they have to use it, in hours and up to five days. Limit entries to the number you can contact within seven days. Run a daily countdown with value and social proof, like a small launch, and a second seven-day countdown to claim the promotional offer.
numbers: Entry window 3 to 7 days; claim window 7 days; use window hours to 5 days.

### POD-250819-03#16 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: Free Giveaways | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:24:56
claim: On the claim call, frame two prizes, present the discount, and fall back to a same-percentage discount on another product.
detail: Open by telling them they qualified for two prizes and that you will help choose which fits. Present the promotional offer on the grand-prize product. If they decline, give the same percentage discount on any other product that suits them. For recurring revenue, spread the discount over the longest period they accept and bill normal rates afterward.
