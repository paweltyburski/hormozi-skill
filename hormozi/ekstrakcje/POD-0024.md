### POD-0024#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Selling is easy to learn; the harder edge is fulfilment, since every provider in a market makes the same big promise.
detail: He describes selling as asking where they want to go and painting it vividly. All local gyms promise weight loss, better sleep and energy, yet one charges 10 times more. The difference is how strongly the prospect believes this provider will deliver. Strong fulfilment then produces referrals and an inflection point where everyone starts talking about the business.
numbers: Price gap between providers making the same promise: about 10x.

### POD-0024#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:46
claim: Provider conviction rests on a track record: Gym Launch stated clients earn several times the fee within eight weeks.
detail: Hormozi says he is certain a signed gym will make more than they pay within eight weeks, usually by four or five times, because it has been done many times. Certainty comes from repeated evidence, not from saying it.
numbers: Claimed return: 4 to 5 times fee within 8 weeks, based on about 300 cases (2017).

### POD-0024#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:56
claim: Retain members by meeting six human needs through the gym experience, not through results alone.
detail: Needs as he lists them: status, connection, variety, routine, growth, contribution. Examples: he asks how likely you'd be to attend again if named MVP of the workout; people come back if plugged into a community, with friends, and extra status; in his example, you return for the dopamine of group status rather than for results between workouts. Before-and-after photos do not set a gym apart.
### POD-0024#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:24
claim: A lasting change happens when the activity becomes part of the client's identity.
detail: Engineer the community, status and role so training becomes who they are. Anyone can starve a person for 12 weeks and have them lift; the difference is a lifestyle change, which supports higher prices and referrals because the friends see happy results, not suffering.
