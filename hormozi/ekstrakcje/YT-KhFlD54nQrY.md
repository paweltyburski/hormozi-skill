claim: Premium first, volume later: Tesla started with a $250,000 roadster and moved down the price ladder, while Walmart and Amazon built for volume from day one.
detail: Selling cheaply requires infrastructure for huge demand and an efficiency culture (flying coach, paper cups). Selling to few wealthy buyers lets you tolerate inefficiency.
