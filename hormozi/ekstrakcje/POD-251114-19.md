### POD-251114-19#01 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: verticals | fw: Low Barrier Offer (LBO) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:32
claim: A COVID-era car wash was free but accepted donations for staff, and the idea was adapted to gyms.
detail: Hormozi paid $20, more than their top automatic wash normally cost, and felt good funding workers. Gyms used the script and closed people who normally would not buy.
numbers: Average ticket about $99, above the average low-barrier offer.

### POD-251114-19#02 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: pricing | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Advertise the product as free, then at checkout let the customer pick a price, with bonuses at small, medium and large levels.
detail: The free tier must stay available and carry low operating cost; costlier deliverables go to payers. State at the start that staff offer perks by level and nobody is obliged to pay. No maximum.

### POD-251114-19#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: promotions-launches | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Tier examples: a 21-day weight-loss programme and a coaching programme.
detail: Weight loss: most choose the middle tier, which adds a one-on-one call; higher tiers add a handbook and a result guarantee credited to services. Coaching: group access free; a course at a mid price; group calls at the top.
numbers: Weight loss $99 (extra call), $199 (supplement handbook), $499 (10 lb guarantee, else credit). Coaching $299 (course), $997 (six group calls).

### POD-251114-19#04 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:39
claim: Screen for commitment with hard questions during the pitch, then list the tiers, name payment methods and stay silent.
detail: Ask about willingness to change habits, to stop certain behaviour, and to keep showing up; weed out anyone who will not stay. The close is a choice of payment method, then silence.

### POD-251114-19#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:31
claim: A gallery gave each artwork a price range, and most buyers paid above halfway.
detail: Buyers did not want to seem cheap or unsupportive, giving a half-goodwill, half-capitalism version.
numbers: Example range $149 to $299.

### POD-251114-19#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: objections-closing | fw: Attraction Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:31
claim: Pick-your-price converts very often but at a lower average ticket and suits low-trust situations.
detail: It reads as a choice rather than a demand, builds goodwill and sets up later upsells.
