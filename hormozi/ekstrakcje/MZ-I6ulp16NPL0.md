### MZ-I6ulp16NPL0#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, money-models | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A door-to-door sales coaching company stuck at $7.5M serves reps, leaders and owners, but owners bring 80% of revenue.
detail: Three tracks with their own products. Advice: cut 80% of the work, lose about 20% revenue, margin rises, and sell only to owners. The sticky product is the per-user online training.
numbers: Revenue capped at $7.5M, goal $15M; rep and manager courses $197 and $1,500 with mastermind up to $10k; owner boot camp $2,500, mastermind $35,000, team training $6,900 setup plus $50 per user, pro package $15k.

claim: Organic-only sellers keep adding products to a fixed pool of fans; instead pick the highest-leverage, highest-retention product and widen the pool.
detail: Prefer a straight line (front door, door two, door three), then put more people through the front door.

### MZ-I6ulp16NPL0#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: money-models | fw: Ascension Path | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:47
claim: Staff ascension with the one or two best front-end sellers, since far fewer buyers than leads reach the back end.
detail: Front-end customers become the lead base for the next sale. High ticket sizes mean two or three people can ascend a company to $100M a year.
numbers: Two or three ascension sellers for up to $100M a year (his estimate).

### MZ-I6ulp16NPL0#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:34

detail: After fixing the model, ask what blocks selling 100 owners a week. Guess: per-user subscriptions are very sticky, reps and managers less so.
numbers: Target example: 100 owners a week.
