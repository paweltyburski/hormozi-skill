### MZ-pjgIBSAsL_o#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: outreach | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A patented-machinery maker sells to granite countertop fabrication shops, a conservative market of about 15,000 buyers.
detail: Its machine cuts the process from eight steps and three or four workers on two machines to three steps and one worker, with more output. The company hires door-knockers in every major city and closes via a virtual demo.
numbers: ~15,000 buyers in the US; 80-90% retention; 8 steps down to 3; 3-4 workers down to 1.
applies_to: Manufacturing or equipment sold to a narrow trade.

### MZ-pjgIBSAsL_o#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: In a tiny niche, low view counts do not matter, so post before-and-afters, demos and case studies that show rather than tell.
detail: Algorithms serve niche content to the right people, so a few hundred views can reach a large share of 15,000 prospects.
numbers: 15,000 people in the audience; 20 likes can be enough.

### MZ-pjgIBSAsL_o#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: outreach | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:41
claim: The competitor-name voicemail works well for equipment buyers who ignore cold approaches.
detail: Leave "calling regarding [competitor]"; on the callback, tell them the competitor is beating them with the machine they lack. Use a nearby competitor the buyer knows.
