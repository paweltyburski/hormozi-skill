### MZ-DOYLPw8rZDY#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Keep a no-bias when judging investments to manage risk, but check it, since saying no to everything means no return.
detail: At his investment firm it is always easy to find a reason to decline. Saying no keeps you right in the short run and wrong in the one way that matters. Treat it as a continuum to manage, not a problem to solve.

### MZ-DOYLPw8rZDY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: Many-Sided Die | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:19
claim: Doubters are right "99% of the time" but wrong where it counts, because one yes changes your life; they measure in days while you measure in decades.
detail: Friends say each partner or business will not last, and are right until the one that does. He failed his first nine businesses. Cynicism is a substitute for intelligence (the "cynical genius illusion"); doomsayers are remembered for hits, not misses. Optimists accept looking wrong more often.
numbers: First nine businesses failed; 19 of 20 doubts correct (illustrative).
