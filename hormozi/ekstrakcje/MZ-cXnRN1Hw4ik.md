### MZ-cXnRN1Hw4ik#01 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: pricing, verticals | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A builder guarantees on time and on budget, refunds its profit if it misses, and raises price 20% to match.
detail: The refund equals the builder's roughly 20% margin. The price increase pays for the guarantee in the rare case it is invoked. It closes the deal because rivals will not match it.
numbers: Refund about 20% of the deal; price raised 20%.
applies_to: Custom home builders and other contractors with thin margins.

### MZ-cXnRN1Hw4ik#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Make the guarantee void if the client changes their mind about anything in the agreed scope.
detail: Clients nearly always change something, so the guarantee closes the deal but is rarely owed. Rate limiters like tile or customs delays remain risks. If a client asks for extras, accommodate them but say the guarantee no longer applies.

### MZ-cXnRN1Hw4ik#03 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing, verticals | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Pitch the premium by quantifying competitors' overruns and delays so the fee is already paid back.
detail: Steps: acknowledge they are comparing builders; state typical overrun; compute it on their budget; add delay and lost appreciation; add appraisal advice from a specialist; conclude they pay more elsewhere, just in other ways.
numbers: Custom homes typically run 10-20% over; 15% over on a $2M home is $300,000; typically 3-9 months late; builder fee in example $250,000.
