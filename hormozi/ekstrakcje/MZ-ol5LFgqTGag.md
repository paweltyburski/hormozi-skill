### MZ-ol5LFgqTGag#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A customer-experience SaaS moving upmarket to banks and governments has a capital constraint, not a sales-cycle problem.
detail: Started three years ago with a simple, short-cycle product; now higher value, longer cycles, higher CAC, capital-heavy delivery. Pricing is adequate, so it is a short-term cash squeeze.
numbers: $4M ARR now; target $12M ARR by end of 2026.

### MZ-ol5LFgqTGag#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:49
claim: Bridge the cash gap by selling smaller short-cycle deals or by pulling enterprise cash forward.
detail: Check pricing first; if margins are fine, one option is to keep small deals and accept slower enterprise growth, the other is to change payment terms.

### MZ-ol5LFgqTGag#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Continuity Discount Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:38
claim: Front-load enterprise cash with setup fees, prepaid months credited back, phased tranches and a small first scope.
detail: Examples: setup or onboarding fees, two free months at the end for two prepaid, first and last month. Phase the project, pay by tranche; a small initial 90-day scope is easier to approve and then upsold.
numbers: Initial scope of 90 days.

### MZ-ol5LFgqTGag#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:35
claim: Many business traits are features, not bugs: enterprise buyers are slow but stay for years.
detail: Trade-off: long buying time against multi-year contracts, higher gross margin and larger value per customer.

### MZ-ol5LFgqTGag#05 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:07
claim: Change marketing to the upmarket message early, because upmarket advertising also attracts downmarket buyers.
detail: Big customers avoid minnows, but minnows want to be with big ones, so the reverse does not work.
