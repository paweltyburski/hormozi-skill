### MZ-R8IrKnlMQCI#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Criticism is the gap between expected and actual; an insult attacks character, so give the former and drop the latter.
detail: Example: "you were five minutes late" versus "you are lazy". Comparison is a built-in brain function, useful for setting steps to improve.

### MZ-R8IrKnlMQCI#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:54
claim: Translate any hate into "he lives his life in a way I would not prefer".
detail: This lowers the fear of being insulted when promoting or selling. Criticism is a fixed cost, since acting or not acting both draw it. Comparison leads either to study or envy.
