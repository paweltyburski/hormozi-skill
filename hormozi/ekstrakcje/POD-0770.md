### POD-0770#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Run the sales script through a readability checker and bring the language below third-grade level.
detail: At a B2B company reps were stacking technical features and the owner did not care. Simplifying the script was among the changes that took the close rate from 14% to 35% in four months (2.4x). Plain words show mastery; newcomers use big words neither side understands.
numbers: Close rate 14% to 35% in four months; reading level under third grade.

### POD-0770#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: Sales to Fulfillment Continuum | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:00
claim: Done-for-you offers get a detailed step-by-step pitch; do-it-yourself and done-with-you offers get the light three-pillar pitch.
detail: For heavy implementation, walk through every intricate step ("27 steps") so they see how hard it is and default to you doing it. For do-it-yourself, done-with-you is just insurance in case they get stuck, so keep it to three points; a detailed pitch backfires there.

### POD-0770#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: Three Pillar Pitch | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:57
claim: A three-pillar pitch takes one of two shapes: a sequence (step one, two, three) or three requirements that must all be present.
detail: Fitness, nutrition and accountability are requirements; set up, traffic, inventory is a sequence. Each pillar is one short phrase. Aim to talk as little as possible so the prospect speaks again quickly. To find three parts, use the before-during-after pattern, which fits most processes because it follows time.

### POD-0770#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: Three Pillar Pitch | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:47
claim: Give each pillar one to three analogies the audience already understands, move on once they say they get it, then recap and ask.
detail: Examples: SEO as an investment account versus ads as a paycheck; accountability as parents making a child brush teeth before habit takes over. Match the shared experience to the audience. After the recap ask for the sale, then handle money, decision-maker, past-experience and stall concerns by asking again.

### POD-0770#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:34
claim: Write scripts mostly as questions and keep words few, since a question cannot be wrong and keeps the prospect talking.
detail: Most people do not argue with a premise stated inside a question.
