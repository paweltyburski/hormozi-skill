### POD-0011#01 | CASE | topic: objections-closing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:52
claim: A gym owner said his market was too poor to buy, yet Gym Launch's own rep ran the same ads to the same audience and sold $95,000 in five weeks.
detail: This happened when the company moved from done-for-you launches (send a salesperson, run the ads, sell for the gym) to done-with-you, which reaches more gyms faster and lets gyms keep more profit. The test shows the problem was the seller's belief about the market, not the audience.
numbers: $95,000 in new-signup sales in 5 weeks, same ads, same demographic.
applies_to: Local fitness businesses

### POD-0011#02 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:44
claim: Sellers who judge a prospect as broke give a half-hearted pitch and phrase the ask as an easy out.
detail: Tone and wording signal the answer you expect. "Do you want to sign up?" invites a no; "Ready to take the last spot?" shows you assume a yes. Many lost sales are self-sabotage from the seller's judgement, not anything the prospect said.

### POD-0011#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:34
claim: A door-to-door smoke alarm salesman skipped the pitch because the house looked poor, and the husband chased him down offended.
detail: The husband asked whether the salesman thought he was too poor to protect his family. Lesson: give every prospect an equal pitch and equal enthusiasm; the seller who prejudges takes away their chance to buy.

### POD-0011#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: operator-mindset | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:40
claim: His belief: people who are broke from poor money habits are easier to sell, since they spend their last dollar readily.
detail: He says he told himself that if someone is poor because they spend everything, there is no reason their last dollar should not go to a product that can help them. Combined with conviction that you can help them, this removes the disadvantage. He admits the view is raw.

### POD-0011#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:15
claim: An unemployed client put the programme on a credit card, lost 30 pounds in six weeks, and later took a job paying three times as much.
detail: He cites it as proof that self-discipline from fitness spreads into other parts of life, which supports pitching the prospect who says they have no money.
numbers: 30 lb in 6 weeks; new job paid 3x.
