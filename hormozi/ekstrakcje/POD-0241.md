### POD-0241#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:53
claim: When two people criticise a third, call the third person over and say it to them directly.
detail: The company has a no-gossip policy, but he treats the content of gossip as feedback that the target needs. Examples: a manager hoarding tasks, a colleague seen as negative. Typically the person explains the cause (a newborn, wanting to be a good teammate) and the issue is solved on the spot. He says 90% of the time they have no idea.

### POD-0241#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:32
claim: Gossip spreads because people are starved of honest communication; that desire is a resource.
detail: Notice when people drop their voices or walk off to the edges to talk. Leaders build the environment where the same candour happens openly, and fostering it by repetition first in small, then larger groups makes it company-wide.

### POD-0241#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:00
claim: To a head whose division had not grown for two quarters, he asked what she was doing that outranked the rolled-over actions and what to take off her plate.
detail: He asked it in front of the team. He told her she was misprioritising her objectives. A similar line to a person seen as negative: many see you as negative, you would gain influence without that perception, what can we change.
numbers: Two quarters of no growth.
### POD-0241#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:36
claim: Make "don't sugarcoat it" a core value and model it by giving each other feedback in front of the team.
detail: Candid feedback is what lets speed work, as people cannot improve without knowing how others see them. Those who cannot take it are exposed early. He and his wife exchange blunt feedback publicly so staff see that it is meant to help. The company is moving into Q4 meetings, a natural time to apply it.
