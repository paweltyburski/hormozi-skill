claim: A design studio serving national brands at $3.5M a year is stuck at a $3-4M ceiling because all work is word of mouth and clients want the owner.
detail: Clients stay 10-11 years but budgets are shrinking; marketing is cut first in a downturn. The owner also runs a distillery, a solar battery company and real estate.
numbers: Revenue $3.5M; ceiling $3-4M; client tenure 10-11 years.

detail: A call or text about a side business interrupts hours of thinking even without work. Hard problems need many coats of paint over time.
