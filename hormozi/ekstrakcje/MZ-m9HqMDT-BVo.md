### MZ-m9HqMDT-BVo#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: In your 20s, trade your edge of long hours and fewer obligations for sheer volume in one narrow space.
detail: The weakness is lack of experience and skill. The one action item is to accumulate volume until good, after which options open.

### MZ-m9HqMDT-BVo#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Three sources of a path: an existing profession, a problem you suffered and solved, or an interest.
detail: Interests are risky unless you already have evidence you are good, as with a singer nobody wants to hear; as an example, many pop stars are already successful at that age, so if you were good enough you might already have been discovered.
### MZ-m9HqMDT-BVo#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:22
claim: Pick the path to fit your goal and risk tolerance; a job is acceptable.
detail: Trillionaire has about one route, millionaire many. Disliking risk is not a defect.
