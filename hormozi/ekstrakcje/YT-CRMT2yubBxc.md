### YT-CRMT2yubBxc#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Take dividends out as the business grows, as protection against the downside rather than a bet on upside.
detail: He cites that 90-95% of businesses fail within five years and only about 1% ever sell, so taking chips off the table regularly beats betting on a future sale. He has started 13 companies and owns eight more, and says the one you start is rarely the one you finish with.
numbers: 90-95% of businesses fail within 5 years; about 1% ever sell (his figures).

### YT-CRMT2yubBxc#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:48
claim: Hormozi's own view: when your wealth is small relative to your income, the opportunity cost of actively investing is your attention, so he put nearly all his time into growing income and kept money in passive vehicles.
detail: Example: $1M passive at about 10% earns $100,000; active investing might add another 5-10% ($50,000-$100,000). But spending a quarter to half of your time on it may cost more than it adds when you could probably lift a $500,000 business income to $750,000-$1M. Speculative stuff like crypto is probably taking more attention than it should and can make your net worth grow more slowly.
numbers: $1M at 10% = $100,000/yr; extra 5-10% = $50,000-$100,000; business income $500,000 could probably reach $750,000-$1M.
### YT-CRMT2yubBxc#03 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:22
claim: Keep six months to two years of living costs in cash, put the rest in passive index holdings, and borrow against them when you need capital.
detail: Banks may lend 50-70% of an index portfolio's value at low interest (about 1-2%, he says), so the money stays liquid for a new venture or property. This is his personal view, not individual advice.
numbers: cash reserve 6 months to 2 years of living; loan-to-value 50-70%; interest about 1-2%.

### YT-CRMT2yubBxc#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-team, retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:29
claim: First invest in your own earning power; for employees that means tying yourself to revenue streams and showing the revenue you create.
detail: In service roles show customers retained and kept; in front-end roles show more closed deals, a higher close rate and self-generated leads. Learning to sell could take someone from $60,000 to $250,000-$500,000 in about two years. If income is capped as an employee, keep living costs low and save into liquid assets. Sequence: skills, then truly passive assets, then wealth management once 10% of assets exceeds income.
numbers: $60,000 to $250,000-$500,000 within about 2 years after learning sales (his estimate).
