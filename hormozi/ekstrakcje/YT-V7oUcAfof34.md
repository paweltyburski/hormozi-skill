### YT-V7oUcAfof34#01 | EVOLUTION | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: Prep for Sale | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: He once said he would never sell and dismissed "a business that can't run without you is not a real business"; now he values structuring for sale as freedom and optionality.
detail: He reframes: you are CEO, not owner, if it cannot run without you. A dependent business sells for less or not at all. Structure it to be sellable whether or not you plan to sell.

### YT-V7oUcAfof34#02 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Being CEO of two companies at once was a costly mistake in time and stress, because CEO is a full-time role.
detail: He started the second business while the first still needed him, assuming he would not be involved in the second.

### YT-V7oUcAfof34#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:59
claim: "Nose in, hands out": meet each portfolio company's leadership quarterly as an advisor, bounce ideas, and leave with no homework.
detail: He treats companies he fully owns and those with minority stakes the same way. Taking to-dos from every company would stop him from doing anything. It only works if the company was built with that intention.
numbers: Quarterly meetings; 3 owned outright, 5 minority stakes.

### YT-V7oUcAfof34#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:59
claim: Ownership test: put someone else in charge for six months; the company must grow in revenue and profit, not just maintain.
detail: A capital investment or similar reason can be an exception, but he says to use common sense.
numbers: 6 months.
### YT-V7oUcAfof34#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:59
claim: Leave the company's central tools (Slack, Asana) and keep a separate channel where only portfolio executives can reach you.
detail: Their teams talk to those executives directly, not to you.

### YT-V7oUcAfof34#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:57
claim: A personality-based brand makes the owner a dependency; a faceless brand, with a mascot for example, lets the owner step out.
detail: He says his earlier companies built dependence on himself, partly through his own interpersonal needs.
