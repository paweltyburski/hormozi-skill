### MZ-Lm_PEN6PH-M#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:33
claim: Structure a VSL as promise, pain, proof, plan, then the objections one by one.
detail: Open with the promise and the pain the viewer feels, show proof, then a short walk through the named process with a visual roadmap, then each big objection.
numbers: Process section roughly 90 seconds at most.

### MZ-Lm_PEN6PH-M#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: verticals | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:01
claim: Each objection gets what they think, why it is wrong, what is right, proof; dead grass is the sample.
detail: For backyard work, dead grass can be replanted, resodded or returfed faster than expected and cheaper than irrigation; show examples. Same for mud.
