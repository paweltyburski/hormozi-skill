### MZ-0oBIpbuVKg0#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Chicago landscaper at $1.3M runs four service lines and loses money in the eight landscaping months; snow removal makes the profit.
detail: Landscaping did about $700K and lost $30K; the year finished at 30% net with snow. Winter needs about five times the crew, then lays them off each spring.
numbers: $1.3M revenue; about $700K landscaping at -$30K; 30% net for the year; goal $10M.

### MZ-0oBIpbuVKg0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Seasonal hiring is a feature of the business, not a bug; accept it and do not label the seasons "on" and "off".
detail: Call it two things done each year so crews keep work, and pay strong incentives for friends to join in the heavy season. Every business has a hard part that is why it pays.

### MZ-0oBIpbuVKg0#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:54
claim: When labor, not demand, is the limit, fix the sales motion first and then raise prices 20-30%.
detail: Thin gross margins and weak sales usually go together ("we can't charge more because sales is weak"). Higher pay lets the firm afford more full-time staff and stabilises the business.
numbers: Price increase 20-30%.

### MZ-0oBIpbuVKg0#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:50
claim: A common mistake is selling expensive services on calls with poor prospects and blaming the business; attract higher-income customers with the lead magnet.
detail: Then flow into a video sales letter with proof and fast response. To choose between design-build and maintenance, compare LTV:CAC; the owner's day will look the same.
