### MZ-qiloFrBdbq4#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Depth-then-Width | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: AI does not change business principles; start with one product, one avatar and one channel, and if one channel cannot yield about $100K/month the issue is skill.
detail: Ask "why a second channel?" first, since most channels have limitless scale next to a $1M run rate. The exception is a local business with a tiny market, such as a lawn service among 10,000 people, which may use every channel to saturate it.
numbers: $100,000/month per channel; 10,000-person local market example.
