### MZ-1PkRTV9sZgQ#01 | DEFINITION | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Branding is the deliberate pairing of things customers already know and like with your unknown business, until the liking transfers to you.
detail: Every customer already gives you a small brand; only reach differs. Big brands run non-direct-response ads purely to build associations. He says brand matters at all stages.

### MZ-1PkRTV9sZgQ#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:08
claim: Brand has three vectors: direction (toward or away), influence (strength of effect) and reach (how many people).
detail: Parents have strong influence but tiny reach; a polarising public figure has strong influence and wide reach. Brand is the multiplier, reducing to whether people like and believe you.

### MZ-1PkRTV9sZgQ#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:34
claim: A small business must supply overwhelming proof repeatedly; as promises are kept, your word becomes enough and conversion rises without proof.
detail: Example: a famous director's fans see his new film unprompted. Small businesses should deliver well repeatedly and give away valuable material to the core audience.
