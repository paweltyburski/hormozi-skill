### MZ-z2vLUuKOLyA#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A real-estate agent and coach closed $21M of own sales volume (office near $100M, about $500K gross commission income, 41 transactions) with no ads, no CRM and no real website, and wants $2M commission income.
detail: Marketing is her strength but her personal brand does not bring higher-priced units; screen time shows 10 hours a week on it. Marketing spend is about 11%. A half-million-dollar post drew eight offers in two days; a $17M listing brings buyers back down-market.
numbers: Volume $21M; GCI about $500K; target $2M; 41 deals; spend about 11%.

### MZ-z2vLUuKOLyA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: $100M Scaling Roadmap | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:26
claim: At the $1-3M crossroads (where he says it typically happens) pick your avatar: if you want to be known as a luxury agent, publicly show only the customer you want, and accept other deals privately without advertising them.
detail: Big attracts small, but small does not attract big. He personally would not hand a $25M house to someone who only sells $500K homes. He thinks luxury is still mostly word of mouth and personal brand, plus a deliberate curation of the portfolio. Post big deals, resurface old ones, and show the same home many ways (front, back, night, day) for posting cadence and visual variety.
numbers: Example mix: $17M listing versus $500K homes; $25M house in his own example.
