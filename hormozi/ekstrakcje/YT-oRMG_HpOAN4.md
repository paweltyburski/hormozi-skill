### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:50
claim: A generic online weight-loss business added a version priced six times higher, and customers bought it more often than the core offer.
detail: The idea came from a friend: list something 10 to 100 times pricier than the core, something you would be happy to deliver if anyone bought it. The weight-loss owner added the 6x version, it outsold the core product, and profit tripled overnight. Hormozi says roughly 10% of customers simply want the most expensive option. The anchor also makes the core price look small and frees a new seller from fear of raising prices.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: retention-ltv | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:17
claim: Until revenue is around $100,000 a month, focus almost entirely on advertising; past that point, switch to fixing the product and retention before pushing ads further.
detail: Below that level too few customers pass through to generate feedback on whether the product is good. Once about $100,000 a month is reached, he says to put attention on plugging holes: recurring and repeat purchase, happiness, referrals. The same ad effort will keep revenue growing while the holes are fixed, and afterwards you return to the front end to try for 10x. Growth then moves in stair steps even if revenue looks flat while the fixing is done.
numbers: Threshold roughly $100,000/month, also described as heading for $1M (annual).
applies_to: Early-stage owner-led businesses.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:15

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:47
claim: Add a second acquisition channel only when the first runs well without you, because the new channel starts weak and the old one drops if you leave it.
detail: Hormozi's rule is to delay the second channel as long as possible. First get the original channel working at equal or better levels with other people running it, with training that lets them match what you did. Then the second channel can be funded from the first one's cash flow. The switch of attention is where many owners wreck the business. The second channel needs months to be productive and costs money and time.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#16 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:24
claim: Inflation quietly erases margin if prices stay still: a product at $100 with 20% margin and no increase since 2017 now earns roughly zero.
detail: He says $79 in 2017 equals $100 today, so a 20% margin business that kept its price has had that margin consumed by cost growth. Compression of margins year after year is usually unadjusted pricing. Minimum discipline is a 3% to 6% raise at least once a year; he cites Warren Buffett's candy company, which raised prices 50 years in a row, sometimes by as much as 17% in a single year.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#17 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:19
claim: To raise prices for recurring customers, send a price-increase letter that lists what they will get, ties the change to keeping the promise of quality, and grandfathers them until a stated date.
detail: New customers just see the new price. The delay softens the pain and honours long-time customers. For transactional businesses, simply changing the price for new buyers is enough.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#29 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:44:06
claim: The look-back window: customers judge whether the product is worth paying for by the most recent purchase, not by the total received.
detail: Example agency: month one it charged $5,000 and the client made $40,000; month two the client made $5,000 and was less happy; by month three they cancel, though cumulatively they are far ahead. The longer the billing interval, the more good and bad months fall inside one judgement. Daily billing invites churn on any bad stretch; annual billing allows one churn decision a year, made against the full year's result.
numbers: Agency example: $5,000 fee against $40,000, then $5,000, client results.

detail: He is willing to cut price about 30% if three or six months paid in advance reduces churn threefold. He is not asking for contracts, but for payment up front. ### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#33 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:50:41
claim: Sawdust: build a new product or revenue line from by-products and assets you already hold, provided it adds no operational drag.
detail: Analogy from lumber mills, which glue sawdust into new boards. Inventory your people, digital assets and physical assets, including leftover know-how, and recombine them. The key condition is no added operational drag; in his own example he bought no building and hired no new team. Such added lines often bring big margin increases because the costs are already being incurred. The related idea is double or triple dipping, where one activity feeds several outputs.

### YT-oRMG_HpOAN4#37 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Eight Business Functions | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:59:33
claim: Every business needs three leaders: acquisition, delivery, and internal operations, and operations acts as a vendor to the other two without making the big decisions.
detail: These map to the only three ways to raise enterprise value: more customers, customers worth more, and less risk. Operations covers IT, legal, HR, contracts, payroll, CRM. Early on the founder holds all three hats, then picks the one nearest to their strength and hires complements. Rule of thumb: if interviews teach you nothing about what they do better than you, hire better. Taken to the extreme, every business ends with these three branches, so jumping between businesses to escape a function adds little.
