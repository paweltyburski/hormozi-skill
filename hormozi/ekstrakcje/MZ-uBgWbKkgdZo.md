### MZ-uBgWbKkgdZo#01 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: To stay compliant yet compelling, state facts about the past and make no claims about the present or future.
detail: Capture data on customer results systematically (he does this in almost every company he buys) so ads can say what happened, e.g. a share did well and a share did not, and let the buyer decide.
numbers: Illustrative split in the clip: 78% succeed, 22% do not.

### MZ-uBgWbKkgdZo#02 | CASE | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A coach helping Latinos buy a first multifamily property was told his result promise works better than the "join a community" promise of English-language leaders, and to require customers to have capital.
detail: Those leaders' promises are compliant but weaker. Qualifying by capital limits the audience but plenty of people have money.
numbers: He says 1 in 9 people in the United States is a millionaire.
