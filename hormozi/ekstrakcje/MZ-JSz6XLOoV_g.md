claim: Keep a free community as the lead-magnet group and a separate paid group as the ascension, instead of turning the free one into a $7 paid group.
detail: Across the platform, the people who seem to have the most success, almost everyone, use this two-group model; the free group's purpose is converting traffic into the paid group.
applies_to: Owners of free communities of about 1,000 members.
