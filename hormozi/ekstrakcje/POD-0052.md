### POD-0052#01 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: offers | fw: Unconditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Close with best case versus worst case: best you change your life, worst you get your money back and keep the workouts.
detail: Paint the best case vividly (shape, family, community), then the worst case (they dislike the community, refund, free workouts). Add confident zeal about how sure you must be to offer this. Also removes the "my spouse" worry, since they can say it was a great deal.

### POD-0052#02 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Scope the guarantee to service: refund if you did not get the service paid for, and let the window run to the final day.
detail: He ran 100% satisfaction on any product sold. For six-week challenges the claim stayed open up to five weeks and six days; for a membership, the refund is that month's fee. Over-delivering made the claim hard to justify.
numbers: only 2 refund takers across six locations in five years; example $200 monthly fee refunded.

### POD-0052#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:30
claim: Telling staff about the guarantee raises their standards and the strength of the sale, provided you honour it.
detail: Staff feel believed in and sit up; customers expect great service and, when it is delivered, trust more. He began using it after one hesitant buyer closed immediately, then others fell "like dominoes".
