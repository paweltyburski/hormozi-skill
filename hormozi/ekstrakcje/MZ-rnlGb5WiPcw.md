### MZ-rnlGb5WiPcw#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A real-estate brokerage capped at about six recruited agents a month should do more of what works: more recruiters, or more locations if the local market is saturated.
detail: One recruiter brings six agents a month by one-to-one outreach plus organic posts. Hormozi would ask how ten recruiters could bring sixty. Offices grow fast then cap by location, so opening more locations may be the answer in a local market.
numbers: 150 agents; about 6 recruits per month per recruiter; about 20% growth over two years.

claim: Raise agent productivity by studying what the top 10% do and the bottom 10% do not, then simplify those behaviours into one step at a time.
detail: With growth slow, he assumes about half of the agents are unproductive and treats them as affiliates. First look at onboarding. Create a culture ritual (the daily 100 reach-outs) and give brokerage-level tools as a value add.
numbers: About 50% of agents assumed unproductive; ritual of 100 daily reach-outs.
