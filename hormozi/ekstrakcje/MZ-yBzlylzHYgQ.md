### MZ-yBzlylzHYgQ#01 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:58
claim: Choose the first hire by auditing your schedule: list activities, put a dollar value on each, and hire for the role that absorbs the most of them.
detail: In this case it is an admin.

### MZ-yBzlylzHYgQ#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:00
claim: Hire someone you do not dislike, who does not dislike you, who you think you can teach; usually that means some degree of intelligence, and IQ with humility is probably the two things he would check off.
detail: As he has aged he hires more for raw IQ than EQ, and controls for work ethic through rewards. He distrusts the claim that you know in minutes whether to hire.
### MZ-yBzlylzHYgQ#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:44
claim: Punishment works only in one-off interactions; in lasting relationships praise works better because punishment fades and must keep escalating.
detail: Wait until the person does the desired thing and applaud it, then add a regular check-in cadence. People remember positives, so reward sticks while punishment fades.
