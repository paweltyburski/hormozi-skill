### MZ-A6uw_DcbNM8#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Win a negotiation by needing nothing from the other side while having something they want.
detail: Tactics like anchoring or log-rolling add incrementally; order-of-magnitude gains come from supply and demand, the two laws he puts at the centre of Acquisition.com.

### MZ-A6uw_DcbNM8#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:14
claim: In an employee contract, the party with scarcer demand holds leverage; when each needs the other, it becomes a game of chicken.
detail: A unique skill with many bidders gives the employee leverage. Who can live better without the other wins.

### MZ-A6uw_DcbNM8#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Negotiations are won before you sit down: do not attend without cards; improve your conditions first.
detail: If the deal would be bad, it is better not to sit down. Build the skill or asset that raises demand for you.
