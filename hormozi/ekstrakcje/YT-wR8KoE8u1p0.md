### YT-wR8KoE8u1p0#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Depth-then-Width | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Score each platform on five measures before investing: who is there, how many, ease of growth, depth of attention, and conversion, then check real results.
detail: Who is the share of high-income households present. How many is the platform's national market share. Growth is how easily a newcomer gets discovered. Depth is time spent per piece (average view duration and percent viewed). Conversion is how easily you move people to talk or buy. Finish with your own tracked traffic and on-site session length by source, kept in the CRM.
