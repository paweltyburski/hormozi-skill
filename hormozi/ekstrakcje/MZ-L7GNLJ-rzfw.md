### MZ-L7GNLJ-rzfw#01 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:26
claim: To write, reason up from statements you know are true and try to break each definition.
detail: Start as though you know nothing, list anecdotal findings, find unifying principles, then test them retroactively. Each book starts by defining its subject. Working definition of influence: the likelihood someone complies with a request, kept until something breaks it. The leads book took 19 drafts because media keeps changing.
numbers: 19 drafts for the leads book.

### MZ-L7GNLJ-rzfw#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:50
claim: Buyers are not emotional or logical types; the more information someone takes in, the likelier they buy, a continuum.
detail: Nobody, including the buyer, knows why they bought, so the binary cannot be observed.

### MZ-L7GNLJ-rzfw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:33
claim: Teach only what can be observed, as if explaining to a literal-minded reader or a court reporter.
detail: Everyone agrees on what they observe but not on feelings. Keep reading level at third to fifth grade or below, and define any harder word.

### MZ-L7GNLJ-rzfw#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:51
claim: Fast feedback loops breed superstition, so separate what you can observe from rituals.
detail: A salesperson who sneezes before an offer and sees a yes may repeat it; baseball and basketball routines come from the same loop.

### MZ-L7GNLJ-rzfw#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:36
claim: Tone has three constants and two variables.
detail: Constants: loud enough, articulate enough, slow enough; failing any loses comprehension and so persuasion. Variables: pitch (a higher pitch solicits a response) and pauses (solicit attention). So ask the question, then be silent. A script needs only marks for where to stop and where to raise the voice.
