detail: Dealers trust and rate it as their top client. Hormozi: either grow slower without debt or recalibrate how risk is judged.

### MZ-Oznnu_uAvtk#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:14
claim: Decide how much debt to take by modeling volatility, cash flow against covenants and the coverage you're comfortable with.
detail: Check seasonal volatility and the cash generated against debt payments and covenants. He says a good lender will already do most of that modeling when you ask for money. Example: a $100,000 truck paid over five years costs about $20,000 a year and yields $400,000 gross profit, so he says it is not super risky. The comfort level is individual.
numbers: Example truck: $100,000; $20,000 a year payment; $400,000 a year gross profit.
