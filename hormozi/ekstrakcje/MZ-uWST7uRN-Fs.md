### MZ-uWST7uRN-Fs#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:46
claim: Decide what a specialist hire is worth by the gross profit they add, then pay the recruiting cost without flinching.
detail: In an agency, a social director produces about $1.25M gross profit a year; the role costs $200K plus a pod of $350K, already netted. Headhunter fee is typically about 30% (about $60K on $200K). At an 8x multiple, one hire adds about $10M of enterprise value.
numbers: $1.25M gross profit per hire per year; role $200K; pod $350K; fee about $60K; 8x multiple gives about $10M.

### MZ-uWST7uRN-Fs#02 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:34
claim: A portfolio company built a 50-person sales team in 90 days by hiring 80 and keeping 50, using 15 headhunting firms plus internal sourcing.
numbers: 80 hired, 50 kept, 15 firms, 12 weeks.
detail: The outsourced sales team had blown up fast, forcing the rebuild.

### MZ-uWST7uRN-Fs#03 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:34
claim: A dentist earning $700K gross profit per added dentist faced $100K recruiting each; paying $300K to reach a three-year goal in six months was worth it.
detail: Three dentists were needed. The trade was $3.7M profit instead of $4M this year.
numbers: $700K gross profit per dentist; $100K recruiting each; $300K total.

### MZ-uWST7uRN-Fs#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:52
claim: Build recruiting infrastructure for roles you hire repeatedly; for a single specialised role just pay a headhunter.
detail: Treat each repeated role like a customer needing an acquisition process (sales team, support, editors, cleaners).
