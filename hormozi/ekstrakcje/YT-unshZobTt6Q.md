### YT-unshZobTt6Q#01 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: When cash is gone, trade your spare capacity for the services you would otherwise pay for.
detail: With almost no money he bartered classes and personal training for cleaning, IT help, a printer, video production for his first ads and meal-prep supply. It works because an extra class seat costs him nothing when his costs are fixed, and barter is tax efficient. Method: go through every expense line, find a person who does that thing, keep asking for less service until its value equals one membership, then swap.
applies_to: Solo founders and tiny local businesses with fixed costs and unused capacity.

### YT-unshZobTt6Q#03 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:11
claim: Pay to join a group of people already doing what you plan to do, and shadow them, so you skip their mistakes.
detail: He joined a gym-owner mastermind (30-40 owners) before owning a gym. In the first conversations he learned which markets, square footage, layout, parking and equipment brands to avoid, which repaid the entry fee on day one. ### YT-unshZobTt6Q#08 | CASE | topic: promotions-launches | also: offers, money-models | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:15:47
claim: The six-week challenge outspent competitors because it collected far more cash in the first transaction than their free or cheap trials.
detail: Goal was losing 20 lb in six weeks (about 10% of body weight for lighter people), roughly 80% hit it, and people gamed weigh-ins with water and weights, which he knew. Most who train six weeks want to continue, so the transition was moving them to a year to reach the larger goal. ### YT-unshZobTt6Q#09 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: objections-closing | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:48
claim: A guarantee gets its power from the stated consequence ("or what"), and a service-based guarantee (keep working until they hit it) carries weight when delivery has real cost.
detail: The words "we guarantee" mean little without the penalty you put on the line. His two most-used guarantees: a service guarantee (we continue serving you until you reach X) and an unconditional one. The service version suits businesses where extra delivery is costly (live training); extending access to a digital course is weak because access costs little. The unconditional one let any member claim a refund if they did not feel $500 of value within six weeks, and the team worked harder knowing it existed.
numbers: Two refund requests on the unconditional guarantee over the years.

### YT-unshZobTt6Q#10 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Win Your Money Back | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:47
claim: The best-case/worst-case close: lay out the best outcome and the worst outcome of trying and show that staying as is is the only real risk.
detail: Steps: zoom out; best case = life changes, community, sticks for life; worst case = six weeks of training for free, tells you you are bad, you refund; then point out the only sure outcome is not reaching the goal if they walk away; ask what they want to do. ### YT-unshZobTt6Q#12 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:22
claim: When customers must act to get a result, ask specific countable questions to find out what they actually did.
detail: "Did you follow the plan?" gets "100%". Follow with a count: on how many of the last seven days were all three meals exactly on plan with nothing extra? ### YT-unshZobTt6Q#14 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: Bonus Checklist | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:36
