### MZ-uwJLalV897Y#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: verticals, content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A doctor now splits time between patients and social media, with a second clinician being trained, and asks how to shift fully to promotion.
detail: His patients still bring a large share of revenue. Hormozi says to train the replacement and give a path to ownership, otherwise they will be loyal to themselves.

### MZ-uwJLalV897Y#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: Five S's of Advanced Compensation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Offer a profit interest or equity share of 10% to 20% of upside, vested over years.
detail: Law, consulting and accounting firms have partner tracks. It can be profit share paid in a sale rather than equity, for example 2% a year over five years, and a name or picture on the door so they feel ownership.
numbers: 10%-20% of upside; vest 2% a year over five years.
