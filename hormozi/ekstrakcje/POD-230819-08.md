detail: A warm outreach message reaches one person at a time, whereas a post is said once and reaches all. ### POD-230819-08#12 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: Integrated vs Intermittent Asks | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:27:39
claim: A one-hour podcast with three 30-second ads gives 58.5 minutes of giving to 1.5 of asking, but a friend who asked too often saw his podcast shrink.
detail: The friend's show blew up quickly, he tried to monetise at once, and it stopped growing and then declined.
