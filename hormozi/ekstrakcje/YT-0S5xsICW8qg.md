detail: Her account had 5,800 followers, about 19 likes and one comment per post, and 30-day reach of 2,800. Sales: 10-plus low-ticket sales a day (code cheat sheets) and about three higher-ticket sales a week. Her first sale came with eight people on her email list; the list is now ~2,900.
