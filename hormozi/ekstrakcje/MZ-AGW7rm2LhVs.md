claim: Place the overview video at the start or the end, because people remember the emotional peak and the ending (peak-end bias).
detail: The video walks through the workouts, nutrition, meal plan, community and network.

claim: Use only one to three meaningful testimonials, with the key lines highlighted, since viewers skip generic praise.
detail: Screen recordings show people skim fast and move past "nice things said".
