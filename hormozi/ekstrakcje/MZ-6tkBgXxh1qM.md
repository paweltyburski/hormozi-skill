### MZ-6tkBgXxh1qM#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A caterer with a side restaurant, all word of mouth, wants to grow from $1.2M to $10M in three years.
detail: About 80% of revenue is catering, the rest a part-week restaurant. Owner feels underpriced and kitchen-capped. Advice: focus on catering, add kitchen capacity, then paid leads.
numbers: $1.2M revenue last year; target $10M in 3 years; one commercial kitchen feeds 1,000+ people via up to 10 teams out.

### MZ-6tkBgXxh1qM#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:00
claim: Choose the arm of a food business that is not limited by its own four walls.
detail: A restaurant hits its walls quickly; catering is made centrally and delivered locally, so it scales further.

### MZ-6tkBgXxh1qM#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:34
claim: Seasonality is not by itself a defect; earn heavily in peak months to carry the off-season.
detail: Harry and David loses money outside December; See's Candies earns most profit in Q4. A caterer peaking in spring, fall and Q4 likely has a lead shortage, not volatility.
numbers: ~80% of Harry and David revenue falls in December.

### MZ-6tkBgXxh1qM#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:20
claim: Ask whether your worst month would become your best if you captured all local demand.
detail: With this caterer, the answer was yes, so Hormozi concluded the dip was not real volatility but an artificial, self-fulfilling constraint that better marketing would smooth out.
### MZ-6tkBgXxh1qM#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:14
claim: For a caterer, start paid acquisition with pay-per-click search, then add Meta ads.
detail: Search leads are high intent and warmest; expect to pay more than free word of mouth and to need a real lead process.
