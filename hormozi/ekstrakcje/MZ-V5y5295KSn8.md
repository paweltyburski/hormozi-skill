### MZ-V5y5295KSn8#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing, money-models | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A free community of 300 developers should be monetized around job placement, chosen after surveying members on what they want.
detail: Developers have high earning and spending power. Job placement could be priced around $9.99 per month. Sub-niches (languages, skill upgrades for higher pay) matter, so ask why members are there: love of coding, skill upgrade, higher pay or a job, then build the follow-on community on the main answer.
numbers: 300 free members; suggested about $9.99 per month for job-placement community.

### MZ-V5y5295KSn8#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:59
claim: An engineer YouTuber with about 100,000 subscribers made $130K in five days with a "speed run to promotion" offer.
detail: The audience was all engineers, who have money, and the offer was framed as a path to promotion.
numbers: About 100,000 subscribers; $130K in 5 days.

### MZ-V5y5295KSn8#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:09
claim: Sell to analytical audiences with a logical sale: facts, the income gap, and graduation rates.
detail: State the percentage of people with a given skill who earn a given income, the delta, and the percentage of your students who graduate; if the return on investment is obvious, they buy and are less likely to refund.
