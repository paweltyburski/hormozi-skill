### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Selling is a transfer of conviction across a bridge of trust, and how deeply the rep believes predicts closing more than script quality.
detail: A rep with no baseline belief has nothing to transfer. Conviction is a matter of degree, not belief versus disbelief. Skills and frameworks exist to communicate clearly and build trust so the rep's belief can reach the prospect, who must end up trusting that belief more than their own doubt.
quote: sales is a transference of conviction

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#02 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A nine-person team selling mortgage leads had a rep who did not believe in the product, which no script could fix.
detail: Hormozi spent the morning rewriting the script and reframing the offer, then trained the team in the afternoon. He asked the rep flagged as a problem how good the leads were and got a hesitant answer. His point: with real belief the rep would talk about the leads enthusiastically and barely need a script. He then modelled a believer's answer about the leads.
numbers: Team of 9 reps.
applies_to: Teams with a doubting rep

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: When a rep who started strong declines, suspect lost conviction rather than missing skill, and do not reach for training first.
detail: The rep already closed before, so skills are not the gap. Something changed, and oftentimes it is a negative remark heard from a prospect they sold. The fix is a deliberate process that rebuilds belief, beginning with a good product.
### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Hold a weekly long-form meeting where customer success shares client wins with the sales team.
detail: Both departments attend. Success reports what clients achieved; sales hears it and gains proof to believe in. Sales also learns which statements customers misunderstand, so they stop saying them, and success sees what is being promised.

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: lead-getters | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Build a standing pipeline of testimonials and have a client join the sales team to tell their story directly.
detail: Collect reviews and testimonials as an ongoing process, and where possible bring a customer onto a sales-team meeting to recount their journey in person.

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:12
claim: Have sales leaders read customer testimonials to the team every morning to set the day's tone.
detail: The daily reading reminds reps why they do their calls and outreach, linking the grind to client transformation. A sales director must keep refilling this belief because prospects pour doubt into reps all day.

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#07 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Gym consults held on programme graduation days closed almost everyone and took about half as long.
detail: His gyms graduated clients from six- or twelve-week programmes on the same Saturday, with emotional results on display and the staff present. Walk-ins that day closed at close to 100%, because staff conviction overwhelmed the prospect's doubts.
numbers: About 100% close rate on graduation Saturdays; consult length about half of normal.
applies_to: Gyms and programme-based services

### YT-bHnVItVGJPA#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:30
claim: Salespeople need reminding more than teaching; belief is what makes them fight longer for a sale.
detail: When reps believe the product will change the buyer's life, they focus on the prospect rather than their commission and persist further. The "mojo" of a good rep fades as prospects spread doubt, so the leader's job is to refill it regularly. This is the item he sees most missing from sales training.
