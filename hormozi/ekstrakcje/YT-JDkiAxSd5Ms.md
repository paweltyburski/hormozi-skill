### YT-JDkiAxSd5Ms#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:16
claim: To get call notes entered, show who is hurt without them (finance staff working overtime), appeal to the rep's honesty, then to money.
detail: Order of appeals: other people they care about; values (you would not promise what we cannot deliver); self-interest (notes help screening so reps close more at higher prices).
