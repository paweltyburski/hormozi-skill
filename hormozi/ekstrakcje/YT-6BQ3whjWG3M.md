### YT-6BQ3whjWG3M#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Inflation makes typical savings goals too small: $1 in 1975 buys what $6.02 buys today.
detail: Saving $100 a month from 18 to 67 at 9% gives $1M, worth only about $170,000 in today's money. A $1M nest egg paying $50,000 a year buys about $8,000 of today's goods. He says a $4M goal probably means needing about $24M in future dollars. He calls the 50-year measure conservative.
numbers: 6.02x purchasing power change in 50 years; $4M today probably needs about $24M.
### YT-6BQ3whjWG3M#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:43
claim: Increase income before anything else; an extra $1,000 a month invested from age 18 reaches about $10M by 67.
detail: A dollar saved at 18 becomes about $80 in 50 years, but that is worth about $13 in today's money, so each early dollar counts roughly thirteen-fold. A $200 gig is therefore like a $2,600 investment.
numbers: $1,000 grows to $80,000 in 50 years, equal to $13,000 today; $200 gig is worth about $2,600.

### YT-6BQ3whjWG3M#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:48
