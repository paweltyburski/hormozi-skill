### MZ-92ReEZwYBQQ#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:16
claim: Plan growth by workflows, not roles: split a role into its few activities (his editor example: four or five) and give each workflow to a separate agent.
detail: Instead of "hire an editor", ask what the editor does, group actions into workflows, and assign an agent per action set. He says every business can be connected action by action, possibly around 120 actions from inbound lead to delivery, and asks whether you can draw your whole business as one linear workflow; if you can't draw it, he asks how you have a business.
numbers: Editor role = four or five activities (his example); possibly about 120 actions for a business flow.
### MZ-92ReEZwYBQQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:56
claim: Map the big steps first, then break each into the actual actions until nothing can be reduced; that is what you automate.
detail: Idea, script, shoot, export, edit, post; under "post" there may be six or seven moves, such as checking the last posted time and adding three hours.

### MZ-92ReEZwYBQQ#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:28
claim: A call-recording workflow turns a transcript into finished clips through separate prompts in sequence.
detail: Steps: detect speaker changes, split into individual conversations, find the highest-tension moment and start the clip there, remove filler words, keep only the data points needed for the insight, export. Each prompt is updated like training for a human editor.

### MZ-92ReEZwYBQQ#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:09
claim: Instruct AI the way you train a toddler, in observable behaviours; vague terms like "more charismatic" or "off-brand" are useless to machines and to people.
detail: Charismatic would become raise your voice this way, talk faster, nod when they speak. Brand rules must be describable. The machine, unlike a person, never forgets.

detail: Worth it if cash flow allows; the price scales with skill.

### MZ-92ReEZwYBQQ#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:50
claim: For home services and brick-and-mortar, automate back office first (invoicing, billables, receivables, lead nurture); in-person delivery is safe for now.
detail: He expects general-purpose humanoid robots, not special-purpose lawn machines, to arrive eventually. Also automatable now: expectation setting, CAC expectations and brand controllables, as pattern detection.
