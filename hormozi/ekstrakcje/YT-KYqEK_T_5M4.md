### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:15:22
claim: Adopt the stance that AI is there to supercharge you, not replace you; the opposite stance leaves nothing to do.
detail: Technology keeps moving and he sees adapting as part of being human. He gives two levels of practical use: a general user level and an expert level, covered in the next atoms.

### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:16:12
claim: Use AI as a filter on both input and output: have it condense what you read and draft what you would say, and you validate.
detail: Examples: distill a book, summarise all email and chat messages and predict your replies, then approve them, turning a day of work into minutes. He treats it as a home screen through which everything is read. Consuming 10 times the information and producing 10 times the output is his claimed 100x.
numbers: 10x input times 10x output = 100x (his estimate).

### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#03 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:00
claim: Experts should move their time from generating work (formerly about 90% of it) to using the machine to generate and spending their time on quality checks and tweaks.
detail: That makes the expert more valuable to employers and clients rather than redundant.
numbers: Generation used to take about 90% of an expert's time.

### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:24
claim: He demonstrated iterating a draft with plain-language follow-up prompts, and says the limit is the quality of the questions you ask.
detail: He asked for a cold email to a plumbing company selling SEO services, then asked for it funnier and shorter, then as a text message, then as a tweet, each time getting a new version.

### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:29
claim: He predicts sales AI trained on millions of calls that holds calls around the clock and iterates on what works.
detail: Based on a 2018 demo of an assistant booking a haircut by phone, he imagines an AI that has learned from 10 million sales calls and handles any prospect in real time. Callers might be relieved to hear it is AI.
numbers: Example training set of 10 million sales calls (hypothetical).

### YT-KYqEK_T_5M4#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:19
claim: He argues the order of jobs exposed to AI is reverse to popular belief: copywriting, logo design, customer service and similar knowledge work go first, ahead of physical labour.
detail: Most people guessed low-skill physical work first and creative work last. He lists copywriters, logo designers, 24-hour customer service, physicians and finance staff as exposed.
