claim: Model a paid community's page on promise, plan, proof, and list the skills the audience wants (for a climber: grip, back, leg strength, recovery, places to climb).
detail: Setup cost is only hosting, so price can be high; the work is a strong offer.

### MZ-ARHRINniw1M#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: money-models | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Two-community model: a free community as a lead magnet (about five videos plus a weekly call) that soft-pitches the paid community.
detail: Organic stories and posts drive people to the free one. To raise conversion further, stack three mechanisms: a free onboarding call for every joiner, the weekly one-to-many call that sells, and an automated selling sequence for people who watched all videos.
numbers: ~5 videos; 1 weekly call; 3 conversion mechanisms.
