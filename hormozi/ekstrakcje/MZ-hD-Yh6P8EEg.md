### MZ-hD-Yh6P8EEg#01 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: He records once a week in a 3-4 hour live, and a team of platform owners clips from it.
detail: Roles: shorts, long-form YouTube, long-form strategist and editor, Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook, each sourcing from his recordings. Start with one or two multi-platform people, then specialise. Platforms fragmented, so shorts that win on YouTube do not win on Instagram or TikTok.
numbers: 3-4 hours per weekly live; at least 7 content staff.

### MZ-hD-Yh6P8EEg#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:56
claim: Split production into pre, peri and post; most leverage sits in pre-production and post can be cheaper and decentralised.
detail: Pre covers idea, packaging, research, key points, stories and visuals. Peri is capture, done in the state that energises the talent. Post is cheaper if pre was good ("an ounce of pre is worth a pound of post").

### MZ-hD-Yh6P8EEg#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:25
claim: To hit volume you need planned structure, and thinking before recording makes content shorter and better.
detail: Waiting for inspiration is fine for some pieces, but most content resembles a rambling voice memo.

### MZ-hD-Yh6P8EEg#04 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:49
claim: Give each content person a weekly quota to cut from a large pool of recordings, and personalise in post.
detail: One long recording can yield many 30-second clips. Capture is common; platform tailoring happens in post.
numbers: About 30 hours of launch footage available for clipping.
