### YT-qLM5G7N3l3I#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Entrepreneurs follow one of three paths: artist (loves doing the craft), entrepreneur (the business itself is the art), or a hybrid; none is right and one can switch later.
detail: Hormozi began as a fitness artist and found he preferred business once he opened a gym. In the hybrid version you keep the part you love (product, marketing or finance) and hire others to build the company around you. Past ventures listed by age 33: online training, gym, gym chain, gym turnaround, agencies, licensing, e-commerce, software, private equity.

### YT-qLM5G7N3l3I#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:40
claim: An artist who stays small scales by price, moving from commodity to premium to luxury as word of mouth grows.
detail: When demand exceeds the craftsperson's time, raise prices instead of units. A plumber doing standard work becomes premium by trust and speed, then luxury on large or ornate jobs. Income and profit rise while the person keeps doing the work they like.

### YT-qLM5G7N3l3I#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:43
claim: If you love the craft, start as an artist; when demand outruns your time, choose hybrid or full entrepreneur.
detail: Someone who loves hustle and doesn't care what they sell is probably more on the entrepreneur path: find needs, connect them to buyers, build the organisation and put an operator in charge. A hybrid needs the judgment to choose a leader, and outside advisers can help find trusted people.
### YT-qLM5G7N3l3I#04 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:32
claim: Rank advisers by experience: best is someone who has done it and taken others like you there; relatives with no business experience rank last.
detail: Order given: 1 done it themselves and guided others like you; 2 guided several like you without doing it; 3 did it only for themselves; 4 saw one or several others go all the way; 5 saw only one person; 6 family or neighbours who never ran a business. Use ranking to find people to build the company around you.

### YT-qLM5G7N3l3I#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:28
claim: Skill value order is product, then marketing, then sales: marketing scales anything only until the cost of each next customer rises, and only an exceptional product earns referrals.
detail: Selling takes weeks to learn, marketing (copy) longer, so most take the easy route. Good marketing of a weak product sells once; a product customers promote compounds and avoids a plateau.
