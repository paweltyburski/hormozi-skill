claim: A teen life-coaching firm has more demand than coaches, so the constraint is hiring.
detail: Sold to parents, $2.2M revenue, goal $5M next year; boys' side overflows, churn is low, margins strong. Bottleneck is male coaches aged 22-30. Hormozi says recruit, then build a talent funnel; do not add products.

### MZ-gI5qfmivSpc#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Define the ideal hire by micro-behaviours, then recruit from roles that already overlap most.
detail: Map behaviours the job needs rather than titles, so the skill gap to train is smallest. For mentors: camp counselors, recreation coaches, young underpaid teachers wanting remote work.
