### MZ-sBOxXgO-c2E#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Med spas combine rich aging demand with understaffed supply and a fragmented market, so owners do well without good marketing.
detail: Owners often just open and rely on word of mouth, in a supply-constrained market they do not recognise. Aesthetic treatments are short-term and cosmetic; a longevity angle is an unfilled slice.
numbers: One owner made about $1.2M free cash flow in a year on about $400,000 invested; about 10,000 to 15,000 med spas exist; target $6M revenue and 40% margins.

### MZ-sBOxXgO-c2E#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:46
claim: Roll-up makes most sense, though starting from scratch is cheap; with zero CAC and high margins the constraint is technician talent.
detail: Staff can easily leave and open their own shop, taking customers.

### MZ-sBOxXgO-c2E#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:46
claim: Win by talent strategy: pay people close to what they generate and give them a larger share so they never want to leave.
detail: Example from a wealth manager: give the other person 51 percent. High-touch services mean clients follow the person, so the model resembles a law firm with partners.
numbers: 51% share to the producer (example).
