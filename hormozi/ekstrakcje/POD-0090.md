### POD-0090#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:09
claim: Price, delay and decision maker each trace to an inadequate gap; understand each mechanism and you can create your own overcomes.
detail: Cook versus line cook: memorised lines versus understanding. Price is a value problem, delay is an unconfronted decision, decision maker needs past agreements.
### POD-0090#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:24
claim: A price objection means value is not clear; typically work on conviction, sometimes on perceived time and effort, rather than usually needing to increase outcome size.
detail: Most buyers already see the outcome; it is usually the belief it will work, then the time and effort. Show how easy you made it. Exaggerated example: offer a house for $500 and they would find the money.
numbers: example house for $500.
### POD-0090#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:22
claim: For delay, make them name the decision variables: do they believe the program works, want to do it with you, and believe they can.
detail: Ask what they need to think about, offer the three things most people weigh, and note no new information will come tomorrow. People fear being wrong, so paint best and worst case.

### POD-0090#04 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:50
claim: For decision maker, stack past agreements: partner knows, partner supports, so why not here.
detail: Ask whether the spouse knows they are unhappy with their body, is happy about that, and supports doing something. Then ask why they would not support this. Optional add-on: better to ask forgiveness than permission.
