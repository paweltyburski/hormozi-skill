### MZ-DOg1px0WYQ0#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:21
claim: Mental stability breaks into three behavioural measures: toughness, fortitude and resilience.
detail: Toughness is the fuse: how many bad events in a row occur before your behaviour changes. Fortitude is how steep the behaviour change is once past the fuse. Resilience is how quickly you return to baseline. All are behaviours, not moods, so you can still be upset while functioning.

### MZ-DOg1px0WYQ0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A worldview of cosmic irrelevance (being forgotten within about four generations) lowers day-to-day anxiety about status and results.
detail: Any overarching worldview, religious or not, helps; without one you become the centre of the universe and drift into a victim stance.
numbers: forgotten in about 4 generations.
