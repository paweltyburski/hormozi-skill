### POD-0125#01 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Keep recordings of each core routine (greeting, training session, sales call) as the baseline that staff compare themselves against.
detail: Without a recorded ideal there is no baseline. When someone slips, send them back to the footage until they can do it in their sleep. It standardises processes and spares repeated 30-minute explanations.
applies_to: Gyms and other service businesses with repeatable front-line routines.

### POD-0125#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:50
claim: Train phone sales by recording a real call with the script in front of you and highlighting the script lines as you say them.
detail: Phone on speaker, script in hand, mark which parts you hit while closing. Works for closing, appointment setting and high-ticket phone sales.

### POD-0125#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:50
claim: For in-person routines, show live footage next to the checklist in split screen and tick items off as they happen.
detail: Example: footage of a new member arriving, greeted and seated before the consultation, with the checklist beside it highlighting each step as it happens.

### POD-0125#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:06
claim: Capture footage with a camera and microphone in the facility, cut it into a short compilation, and handle consent by blurring faces or asking permission.
detail: Security cameras plus a microphone suffice. Blur faces or ask permission for training use. Cut whistle to whistle, like sports film, with basic editing software.
numbers: About 15 client arrivals viewable in one hour of edited footage.

### POD-0125#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: finance-exit | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:08
claim: Documented and recorded systems shorten onboarding, let you hold staff to a visible standard, and make the business a sellable asset.
detail: When someone misses a step, point to the exact missed parts and have them rewatch, instead of re-teaching. Trainings can be updated as processes improve. Hormozi regrets that he made over 4,000 fitness sales and recorded none, so his training effort is gone.
numbers: Over 4,000 fitness sales closed, none recorded.

### POD-0125#06 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:11
claim: A member planned to cancel an Orangetheory membership purely because the front desk person was unfriendly, despite loving workouts, trainers and vibe.
detail: The member, a neighbour who uses three gyms around $200 a month, said the product was great. Hormozi's conclusion: customers value consistency, and small front-desk details can make people leave. The best businesses repeatedly do the basics, and mastery is refining fundamentals.
numbers: Neighbour spends about $200/month per gym, uses three gyms.
