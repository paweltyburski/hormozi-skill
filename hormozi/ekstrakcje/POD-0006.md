### POD-0006#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Coaching works through tactics that are immediately actionable, not general theory like "serve customers better".
detail: Example: charge an initiation fee and waive it if the member gives the names of three friends. It multiplies the effect of paid advertising and produces fast turnarounds. Typical gurus achieve success with about 1 in 20 followers, because only the heaviest action-takers succeed when the customer is left to execute.
numbers: Initiation fee waived for 3 friends' names; traditional gurus: ~1 in 20 succeed.

### POD-0006#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:10
claim: A complete promotion kit does everything for the gym owner, beyond naming the promo and advising a few emails.
detail: The kit contained: five emails with autoresponder set-up and audience segmentation, each with a video; five Facebook posts with images; a text blast to nurture list and active clients; a landing page; a printed poster for bathrooms and the front door; a one-page script for selling it to trainers in their weekly meeting; and recommended workouts.
numbers: 5 emails; 5 Facebook posts.

### POD-0006#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:27
claim: For a fitness customer, a weekly grocery list with a store receipt and filmed recipes beats a meal plan template.
detail: He suggests three meal-plan versions (vegan, fish, chicken and beef), adjustable for gender and calorie level, with every breakfast, lunch and dinner, a shopping list priced at a local supermarket, and a filmed recipe each week. Clients notice that someone has gone above the standard "eat healthier" advice.
numbers: 3 plan versions; calorie levels such as 2,000, 2,400, 2,800.

### POD-0006#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:56
claim: Many gym owners probably would not have their parents work out at their gym; he thinks that if you are proud of your service you could sell it a lot harder.
detail: Many owners are ashamed of some sessions or support levels. He thinks that if you did the work down to the fine detail, you would probably be able to sell it a lot harder. Iterate three to ten times, asking clients if each step was clear and remaking whatever was fuzzy. Claims such as "we are like a family" do not differentiate, since every gym says it.
numbers: 3 to 10 iterations.
