### POD-0879#04 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:13

### POD-0879#07 | EXAMPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:31
claim: A Harvard-trained clinic owner should replace technical medical language with sad-face-to-happy-face plus "insurance covers it".
detail: Hormozi runs all his copy through a readability checker and aims below fifth grade, ideally third. A word that makes the reader pause means they are decoding instead of absorbing, and you lose them. He prefers clear over clever; the buyer cares about relief and about coverage, not about how the procedure works. With this change, he says, any channel (ads, referral sources) will work.
numbers: Reading level target below fifth grade, ideally below third.

### POD-0879#12 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:27
claim: Selling a one-time fix to someone in pain is easier than selling a recurring plan to someone who is not in pain; a membership works as a back end after a job, not as a front end.
detail: Hormozi has seen many venture-backed home-services membership companies and none that did it profitably. What works is a technician fixing something and then converting the customer into a membership. His test: keep taking emergency calls, then offer "let's make sure this does not happen to you again". If those customers cannot be closed, do not switch the model.

### POD-0879#19 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Eight Business Functions | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:46
claim: Rather than "three key roles", think of three functions every company needs: promote (including conversion), build (the product or service), and operate (day-to-day leadership, culture, recruiting, standards).
detail: One person can cover all three, but it is rare; usually you find three, or six who share them. Hormozi himself does product, advertising and some operations; his wife is strongest at operations and advertising. Hire where the deficiency ties to the current constraint, not just wherever a gap exists.

detail: Fix demand until supply is the limit, then fix supply and stop worrying about demand. He and his wife joke that one of them is always killing themselves, never both: one drives calendars full, then the other adds delivery capacity.
