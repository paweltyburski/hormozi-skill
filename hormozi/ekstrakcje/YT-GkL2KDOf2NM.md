### YT-GkL2KDOf2NM#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Cash in a bank is an unsecured loan to the bank, so he moved it into US Treasuries, treated as a bank account rather than an investment.
detail: Banks lend deposits on or buy government bonds and keep the spread; savers carry the risk beyond insured limits. Treasuries pay more than the account and carry lower risk, and you can borrow against them. He framed the question as who is likelier to fail and who pays more.
numbers: Bank interest 0.1-1% a year versus about 4% on government debt (2022); loans of up to 80% of the value of Treasuries; bank interest 40x lower than the government's.

### YT-GkL2KDOf2NM#02 | EVOLUTION | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: His investing went through three phases: skills and access first, then the S&P 500, then a barbell that he did not follow and that failed to produce investments.
detail: Phase one put excess cash into courses, coaching and workshops, still his highest return. Phase two, after money exceeded what he could reinvest in learning, was index investing. Phase three, post-sale, was a friend's barbell of stocks and real estate with little in between; in two years he maybe made five investments, perhaps 10% of his cash, and he was unable to write checks despite the theoretical strategy.
numbers: Maybe five investments in two years, maybe 10% of his cash.
### YT-GkL2KDOf2NM#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:04:28
claim: Allocate capital in proportion to what you actually know; concentrate on one or two games and play them many times.
detail: Following Dave Ramsey's reply to a real-estate-heavy investor, a portfolio should mirror the slice of knowledge you hold. Billionaires pick things they understand and do many of them. The sequence he gives: invest in yourself, then in indexes with the excess, then write large checks in your main game once you are good enough. He now invests in service businesses, where he can name the risks.
applies_to: owners with surplus capital
