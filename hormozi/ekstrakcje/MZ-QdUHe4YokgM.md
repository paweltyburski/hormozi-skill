### MZ-QdUHe4YokgM#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A three-store pizza owner ($4.2M revenue, about 30% margin) built an operating system that others now want, and asks whether to sell it.
detail: Options given: (1) use it only internally, buy cheap failing stores and build a big chain with a rare edge; (2) free for all stores and take a percentage of revenue of larger ones, a light model with high enterprise value; (3) go fully software. Option 3 means no longer being a pizza person.
numbers: $4.2M revenue; 30% margin; 3 stores.

### MZ-QdUHe4YokgM#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:19
claim: At scale both paths look alike: you end up in the people business with the same five to seven direct reports.
detail: Marketing, sales, IT, legal and staff problems are the same in pizza or software, so choose by what you want. Winners win either way.

### MZ-QdUHe4YokgM#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:19
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: if he pursues the software path, he would sell the stores, because competitors will be focused and he shouldn't expect to win with one arm tied behind his back.
detail: He says the caller needs all his focus on it, since competitors will surely be focused on it; he notes the stores seem outsourced already.
