### YT-42SOlVhDwWE#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Close rate is roughly skill times belief, and belief is a continuum that can range far higher than skill.
detail: Sales is a transfer of belief, so an empty or one-third-full seller cannot fill the buyer. He pictures skill on a 1-10 scale and conviction on 1-100: a seller with skill two and full conviction will close because buyers sense it. Managers over-train skill and under-build conviction.
numbers: Skill scale 1-10, conviction scale 1-100 (illustrative).

### YT-42SOlVhDwWE#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:36
claim: Total deals depend on skill, conviction and work ethic, and conviction counts twice because belief drives faster, more persistent follow-up.
detail: High activity (more follow-ups, quicker response, outreach in spare time) can offset a lower close rate with more at-bats. A seller who believes the product helps will follow up as if helping someone, as in a thought experiment of telling your past self to buy a stock that boomed.

### YT-42SOlVhDwWE#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:52
claim: Build conviction by training reps on the prospect's problem rather than on the product.
detail: Most sales training teaches product facts; most reps do not listen and just deliver the pitch, so they cannot believe it solves the prospect's problem. Teach thoughts, needs and pains of the avatar, especially when the team is not the avatar. Founders who suffered the pain have this conviction naturally.
