### MZ-LtSWOAuKjG4#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A bar-programme consultancy sells a $20,000 volume package and bespoke white-glove work for big brands and cannot decide which to pursue.
detail: Revenue is about half from each, but the white-glove share is only about 20% of clients; the founder serves them and two staff handle the admin of basic clients. Early clients came from referrals, affiliates and networking; paid ads were erratic, blamed on the agency.
numbers: Volume offer $20,000 (12-cocktail menu, four-day implementation); white-glove 20% of clients; only four white-glove clients in four years; contracts two to three years.

### MZ-LtSWOAuKjG4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Prefer scaling what is unscalable: the best customers are easier to find and retain and harder to fulfil.
detail: Going down-market often only feels easier. Key-person risk is handled later by building systems. White-glove clients also have higher margins and lifetime value.

### MZ-LtSWOAuKjG4#03 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:05
claim: Find the one activity that produces customers and do 10 to 100 times more of it.
detail: Volatility is a sign of low volume. At events, 10 contacts gave 2 closes; roughly 100 events could yield 15 white-glove clients a year. A friend in real estate runs 66 events in a quarter.
numbers: 10 contacts to 2 closes; 100 events to ~15 clients a year; 66 events in one quarter.
