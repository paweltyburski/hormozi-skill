claim: A photography business in Hormozi's portfolio raised its average ticket 5x and profit rose 38x.

### POD-0581#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: content-brand | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:01

### POD-0581#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:20

detail: To justify a higher price than same-outcome rivals, work on those three drivers.

### POD-0581#16 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:18:09
claim: Listing every cost of fitness beside surgery shows why effort and sacrifice drives price (about $25,000 surgery versus about $100 a month bootcamp).
detail: Fitness: wake 1-2 hours earlier, 5-10 hours weekly, diet, soreness, embarrassment, injury risk, meal prep, pricier groceries, fear of regaining. Surgery: sleep, wake up thin, 2-4 weeks sore. Surgeons' ads name exactly these burdens; copy that by naming the burden your offer removes.
numbers: Liposuction plus tummy tuck about $25,000 one-off; bootcamp about $100 per month.

### POD-0581#18 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:20:22

### POD-0581#19 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:21:12
