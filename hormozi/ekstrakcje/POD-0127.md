### POD-0127#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A three-hour talk to online trainers went well while teaching, then flopped when he tacked on a quick supplement pitch.
detail: The room was impressed for two hours; the late rushed pitch drew questions on sweeteners and sugar content that exposed beliefs he had not addressed, and he ran another hour in a bad mood. Mistakes: a half measure, and tactics given before beliefs were broken.
numbers: 3-hour speaking block; pitch in the last stretch.

### POD-0127#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:39
claim: When you speak, go all in on either teaching or pitching, never a mixture.
detail: Middle ground fails; he says he should have asked the organiser which one they wanted. He spent the next one-hour slot entirely on pitching and received more thanks than for teaching.

### POD-0127#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:56
claim: Break beliefs about what is possible first; tactics given to unconvinced people are not acted on.
detail: He argues that selling is the service, since it is how people take action. Peers in similar situations making several times more money is what breaks belief in his communities. Compares to large seminar events that give mostly mindset, and to converts who change everything after a belief shifts.

### POD-0127#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:09:05
claim: A belief-breaking pitch for selling supplements ran four stages: money, customer benefit, team buy-in, then how it works.
detail: 1) Show there is money in it. 2) Show it helps customers, with cases of people paying more and getting better results. 3) Show how to get staff invested by paying them more. 4) Offer to show how it works, which introduces the offer. No tactics in the hour.
numbers: Result: 30 to 40 gyms signed up from one 60-minute presentation (slide count of 437 unclear in audio).

### POD-0127#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:13
claim: He answers failure with anger directed at himself and a demand to learn, because sadness leads to inaction.
detail: He treats anger as a driver that makes people obsessive about fixing things, and gets excited because it precedes growth. If nothing is learned, the failure was in vain.
