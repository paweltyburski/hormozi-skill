### YT-3oD41B66NsM#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Give each role paired metrics, a speed or volume measure against a quality measure, instead of one key metric, so you manage a balance and not an extreme.
detail: Idea taken from Andy Grove's High Output Management. Push hard on the volume side while the paired metric guards against damage. Define both metrics for the person.
### YT-3oD41B66NsM#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery, finance-exit | fw: NPS | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Pairs by role: sales volume or close rate against backouts; support tickets or resolution speed against customer satisfaction or NPS; marketing applications against qualified applications; physical products inventory against shortages.
detail: Others he gave: tax filings against audits or IRS issues, and image editing volume against corrections for a photography business. Too much inventory ties up cash, too little causes shortages.

### YT-3oD41B66NsM#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:47
claim: To scale a service business you need training to get people going, culture to keep them going, and the right paired metrics to monitor them.
detail: Applying the exercise to each role also clarifies your own thinking.
