### POD-0144#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:02
claim: The hybrid decision tree has one root question (do you want an in-gym training community?) and two paths from it.
detail: If yes, add the remote one-on-one tier as a bolt-on service level on top of the gym. If no, build the hybrid business: as a side business if you own a gym, or directly as the main business if you do not.
applies_to: Gym and fitness studio owners.

### POD-0144#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Menu Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:51
claim: Keep promotion, landing pages and pitch unchanged and add an A-or-B close at the end: group sessions at set times in the gym, or one-on-one at the buyer's convenience.
detail: It is an assumed-sale close, prescriptive and cheap to apply. Existing members are ascended into the added tier, and new buyers choose, so both businesses are fed at once. The bolt-on can also be sold on top of in-person sessions.

### POD-0144#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:57
claim: A bolt-on remote tier can lift monthly EFT revenue per client to about $400 with no added fixed cost, and the coach costs about 20% as a variable.
detail: He compares it to semi-private training: same revenue and profit per customer, but hybrid avoids equipment outlay, floor space (which shrinks class size) and scheduling around trainers.
numbers: Revenue about $400/month per client; coach cost about 20% variable.

### POD-0144#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals, finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:37
claim: He suggests, as his view, shooting for 50 to 75 hybrid clients at about $400 a month before making the full switch away from the physical gym.
detail: Run the hybrid as a side business, keep marketing, and stop feeding the in-person gym; hybrid profit covers its decline and eventually exceeds it and its fixed costs. From experience, 75 clients covers the switch and 50 is a minimum, and the rest is mostly profit once old costs are gone. Owners without a gym skip the side-business step and avoid divided attention.
numbers: Threshold 50 minimum, 75 comfortable; price $400/month.
