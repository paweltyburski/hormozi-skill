claim: To leave a 50-50 partner, open by asking if it is working, then propose a joint exit plan with numbers ready.
detail: Bring run rate, profit and costs; propose winding down over about 12 months. A heat-pump installer in Germany is the case. Stopping growth spend turns revenue almost into profit, so the cash funds the next move.
numbers: ~12-month wind-down.
