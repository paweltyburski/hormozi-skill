### MZ-i4OQ3gBgQK0#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Eternal questions (push through or pivot, invest or consume) are dichotomies to manage, not problems to solve, and they are personal.
detail: A person who never consumes dies having enjoyed nothing; one who always consumes has no reserve. Define success for yourself in business before comparing with others.

### MZ-i4OQ3gBgQK0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:05
claim: Several games run at once and points won in one are traded away in another because time is fixed; business is only one game.
detail: Most dissatisfaction comes from wanting the spoils of all and the cost of none. Min-maxing money at 20 is fine, but the target can change at 30 or 40 without it meaning you lost.
