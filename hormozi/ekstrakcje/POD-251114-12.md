### POD-251114-12#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Payback Period | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:25

detail: Monthly doubling reaches 8x in a quarter; the slower cycle reaches only 2x in the same time, so payback speed multiplies growth.

### POD-251114-12#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: finance-exit | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:37
claim: Walk-through of 30-day financing: borrow CAC on a card, repay from the first month's gross profit, reborrow.
detail: Borrow $40 for customer one; in 30 days it yields $50 revenue less $10 cost, so $40 repays the card. Reborrow for customer two; customer one's second-month profit is kept. After 60 days two customers pay $80 gross profit monthly with no debt.
numbers: $40 CAC, $40 gross profit in 30 days, zero debt by day 30.

### POD-251114-12#07 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:48
claim: With $1 CAC and seven-day payback, only the first dollar is ever out of pocket.
detail: Day seven the first customer returns $8 gross profit, covering the dollar and leaving $7, which buys seven customers, and so on weekly. The recurring renewals add further profit. By day 60 the account holds well over $200 of gross profit, before fixed costs.
numbers: $1 CAC, 7-day payback, about $225 by day 60 from a $1 start.
