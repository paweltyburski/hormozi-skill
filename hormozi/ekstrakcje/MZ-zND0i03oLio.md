### MZ-zND0i03oLio#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals, sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A functional medicine telehealth practice at $1.6M relies on word of mouth and lacks a sales process for cold leads.
detail: The owner sells and delivers; a new practitioner's clients resist moving. Sales motion is one video consult. Plan: Google ads and a webinar for menopausal women. Margin is high because of no acquisition cost.
numbers: Revenue $1.6M, goal 10x; margin 70%.

### MZ-zND0i03oLio#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:59
claim: Never use a warm process on cold leads; a cold process works on warm leads too.
detail: A warm process sells only the easy buyers. At his book launch, the webinar was built for strangers and nobody minded. An email offer would have produced about a third of the sales. Add friction: local ads, a video sales letter, then a call.
numbers: Email alone about one third of the sales a full selling process produced.

### MZ-zND0i03oLio#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:47
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: rather than handing everything off, he would consider staying in charge, cutting contact to a five-times-less-frequent cadence and using soft touches.
detail: As a hypothetical, if the new practitioner lost a third of clients and marketing costs appeared, margin could fall from 70% to near zero. Clients keep the same proximity; the new practitioner shares notes internally.
numbers: Contact cadence five times less frequent; margin 70% could drop to near zero (hypothetical).
### MZ-zND0i03oLio#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Ask whether you are confident you can acquire customers to refill the practice without being the practitioner.
detail: Losing revenue short term is the price of escaping the permanent bottleneck.
