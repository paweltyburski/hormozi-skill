### MZ-6Ptk3cITn30#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, lead-getters | fw: Four Market Variables | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A real estate seller niched to high-income earners offsetting taxes grew to about $55-60M, but turnkey property setup and management distract him.
detail: After earlier advice to quit a lead-gen agency he sold real estate, then targeted rich buyers with tax needs. He earns 2.5-3% commission. Add-ons: Airbnb setup, done-for-you management. About 60 properties are live, the manager quit, and an arbitrage partner takes two-thirds of revenue. Most buyers want a deductible purchase, not management.
numbers: Revenue doubling yearly: about $10-12M, $24M, $36M, then $55M; $125M sold overall; 70-some deals; Q4 about $35-40M; closing rate 90-95%.

### MZ-6Ptk3cITn30#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:05
claim: Cut the low-value operations back end and keep the core sale; refer management to partners with expectations set.
detail: Management adds about $100k for a year of work while two house sales pay as much. He sold $24M a year before offering it. Pitch the core economics (tax benefit from buying right) and say management is partner-run and hit or miss.
numbers: Management profit about $100k a year; tax benefit pitch quoted as an instant 58% (unclear in audio).

### MZ-6Ptk3cITn30#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:10
claim: A 90-95% close rate means the price is too low: raise it.
detail: Done-for-you setup is $10,000 and should be about $20,000; management at 15% is 5-10 points below where it should be, though low fees bring repeat purchases. Dropping the turnkey offer may lose about 25% of deals, a home run offer instead of a grand slam.
numbers: Setup $10,000 vs $20,000 target; management 15%; possible 25% fewer deals.
