claim: Among small businesses he sees (about $1M-$50M), roughly 70% are demand constrained and 30% supply constrained.
detail: Demand constrained means the business could use more customers; supply constrained means calls come in but there are too few reps or technicians. These are rough estimates, not a study.
numbers: About 70% demand constrained, 30% supply constrained; businesses of roughly $1M-$50M.

### MZ-j13GOE87iLo#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-process | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Run each hiring stage like its sales equivalent: a video for applicants, a scripted and role-played interview, a 30-60-90 onboarding plan.
detail: Acquisition.com has applicants watch a short company video showing the business and real staff. Interviews use pre-set questions and role-play, since one top hire is worth more than one customer. Onboarding is planned for 30, 60 and 90 days with required learning, then a career path.
numbers: Applicant video about 13 minutes.

claim: Ask a valued employee, or a favourite customer, what it would take for them to stay forever, and be open to hearing it.
detail: For a customer he suggests going exclusive: a multi-year contract, extras for free, and agreeing not to work with competitors.

### MZ-j13GOE87iLo#04 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: hiring-talent | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:06
claim: A brokerage owner raised his agent-referral bonus from $500 to $25,000 and grew a roughly $10M company to about $400M.
detail: A mentor asked what a productive agent earns in gross profit per year. Since the answer was $250,000, a $500 incentive was too small. Owner said he would spend maybe $50,000, so they set $25,000 to start.
numbers: Referral bonus $500 to $25,000; productive agent gross profit $250,000 per year; company $10M to $400M (as told second-hand).
