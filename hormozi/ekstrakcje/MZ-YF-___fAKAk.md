### MZ-YF-___fAKAk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:13
claim: Early on, maximise the money from each hard-won customer and accept unscalable work for cash flow, since the fastest route to $100K is not the fastest to $1M.
detail: When customers are hard to get, sell expensive. Early scalability matters less than cash.

### MZ-YF-___fAKAk#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:13
claim: At his first gym he lived on one personal-training client who trained 90 minutes a day, five days a week, worth about $4,000-$5,000 a month.
detail: The gym's other income went back into equipment and ads.
numbers: One client; 90 minutes a day, 5 days a week; $4K-$5K a month.

### MZ-YF-___fAKAk#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:30
claim: Sell another business's packages on commission: ask for its packages and cheapest delivery price, then find and close customers yourself, keep an agreed percentage, and let them deliver.
detail: After losing everything he did this with gyms and made $100K in the first month. He picked the emptiest facilities because he could sell until full in about three weeks. If a campaign returns 10 to 1, spend $100 for $1,000 and reinvest.
numbers: $100K in 30 days; about 3 weeks to fill a facility; 10:1 return example.
applies_to: Solo operator with a proven local offer and no capital.

claim: His sequence for the commission model: offer, phone-recorded ads, landing page to a spreadsheet, phone the leads to schedule, present the advertised offer in person, collect cash, partner delivers.
detail: At the time the landing page simply fed a Google Sheet. It is functionally a one-person affiliate business.

### MZ-YF-___fAKAk#05 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: Implied Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:42

numbers: Savings target at least $100,000; fee 20% to a third of the savings.

### MZ-YF-___fAKAk#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:23
claim: Business reduces to a few money-making acts: let people know you exist, make a specific offer, get agreement, exchange money, deliver; everything else is noise.
