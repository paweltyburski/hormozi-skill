### YT-DpbXWP8fLbc#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: unit-economics | also: money-models | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition (CFA) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:25
claim: With $100 CAC and $100 fulfilment cost, you need over $400 of cash within 30 days; collecting $400 leaves $200, exactly the cost of the next customer, so you can repeat.
detail: He defines 30-day cash as net free cash collected in a customer's first 30 days. If it covers acquisition and fulfilment with money left, acquisition cost is negative. He puts costs on a credit card, interest-free for 30 days. Aim for far more than 2x (10x ideal) to keep cash for other investments.
numbers: CAC $100 + fulfilment $100 = $200; threshold 2x = $400.
