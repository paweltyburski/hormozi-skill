### YT-GO6VCcLlnLs#01 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: sales-team | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:07
claim: To raise a price from $2,000 to $3,000, quote $4,000, let them split it 2+2, or pay $3,000 same day.
detail: Steps: 1) keep the old price as a payment so the rep and buyer have a home base; 2) skip past the target and quote a higher anchor; 3) make the prepay discount price your real target; 4) the gap between comfort and prepay price is small, so prepaying is easy.
numbers: $2,000 to $3,000 via $4,000 anchor.

### YT-GO6VCcLlnLs#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Payment Plan Downsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:10
claim: To move $4,000 to $5,000, quote $6,000: $4,000 now and $2,000 in 30 days, or pay $5,000 today.
detail: The buyer sees only $1,000 extra to settle everything now. It turns an upsell into a downsell against an anchor.
numbers: $4,000 to $5,000 with a $6,000 anchor.

### YT-GO6VCcLlnLs#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:51
claim: Prefer uneven instalments that put more cash up front, such as 6+3+3 rather than 3 x $4,000 for a $12,000 price.
detail: Onboarding costs front-load, and a front-heavy split makes a small prepay discount more tempting.
numbers: $10,000 to $12,000: 6+3+3 instead of 4+4+4.

### YT-GO6VCcLlnLs#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:58
claim: Sales reps are comfortable with a familiar price, so give them an anchor they can say with conviction.
detail: Telling reps the resistance is in their heads rarely works. Meeting them at their comfort price preserves their conviction.

### YT-GO6VCcLlnLs#05 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:56
claim: Announce a price rise in advance to clear the pipeline, creating urgency and cash before the change.
detail: Say it happens next month or in two weeks, citing added features and results. It also answers later "I heard another price" claims, and funds any dip in the adjustment period.
