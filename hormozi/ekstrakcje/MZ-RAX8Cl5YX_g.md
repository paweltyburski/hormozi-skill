### MZ-RAX8Cl5YX_g#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: pricing, verticals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A white-label article provider flat at $1.5M for five years sells to PR firms who resell, but those agencies churn.
detail: Price $1,000 a month for about 10 articles; once did unlimited for $2,500 and earned more. Leads are organic; ads failed. Agencies have no customer journey.
numbers: Revenue $1.5M for five years, goal $5M in three; ticket about $1,000 a month.

### MZ-RAX8Cl5YX_g#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:26
claim: Add a low tier with a usage component, such as $199 a month plus about $100 per article.
detail: Cheap enough that small customers' bad months don't turn into your volatility; check whether churn comes from not using the product or from bad quality.
numbers: Example tier: $199 a month + $100 per article.

### MZ-RAX8Cl5YX_g#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: retention-ltv | fw: Customer Journey | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Marketing services to SMBs don't stack; stack through the distribution layer instead, keeping about 80% of agencies referring.
detail: SMBs are bad customers: price low enough that they won't cancel, or high enough that they can't afford you. If 80% of distributors remain active, revenue climbs year by year (his illustration: $3M, $4.5M, $6M). Build distribution in layers (a launch first for a quick win).
numbers: 80% of agencies retained; stated path from $1.5M to $3M, $4.5M, $6M.
