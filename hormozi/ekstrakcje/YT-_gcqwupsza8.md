### YT-_gcqwupsza8#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: Prep for Sale | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:17
claim: In March 2019 the gym-licensing business had a $4.4M revenue month and $1.7M of owner dividends, and his wife suggested shutting it down; instead he asked a fitness-sector banker what it was worth.
detail: The banker said that growing from zero to $17M profit in two years, and on pace to grow 50% again, it would sell for about $150M easily by year end. Founders usually expect to beg for buyers; this showed what a growth company fetches.
numbers: $4.4M revenue and $1.7M dividends in one month; $17M profit after 2 years; banker's estimate about $150M (about 9x profit).

detail: The same two points (a second channel, less branding on him) came back as feedback from the first sale round, and he then acted on them.
