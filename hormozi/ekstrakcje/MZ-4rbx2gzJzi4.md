### MZ-4rbx2gzJzi4#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: paid-ads | also: offers | fw: CRO | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A short Amazon-style page converts above typical e-commerce, and one-click checkout lifts it further.
detail: His school community platform page converts roughly 4% (he says '4-ish'); on average about 30% of people coming in come from the community/school, and one-click purchase raises conversion on that group.
numbers: About 4-ish% versus probably 1-2% typical e-commerce.
### MZ-4rbx2gzJzi4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Simplify the offer and test repeatedly instead of building elaborate landing pages.
detail: A simple platform gains more trust than any single seller on it.

### MZ-4rbx2gzJzi4#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:36
claim: Three physical books for a shipping-only fee converted 32% of visitors, so offer strength still matters.
detail: Result held across many purchases.
numbers: $15 for three books; 32% click-to-purchase; about 20,000 purchases.

### MZ-4rbx2gzJzi4#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:36
claim: Include a bullet only if it is worth two to three times the price to the right buyer.
detail: Each bullet costs attention; Hormozi believes buyers are single-issue.
numbers: Each bullet worth 2-3x price.
