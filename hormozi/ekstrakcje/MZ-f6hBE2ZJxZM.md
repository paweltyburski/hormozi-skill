### MZ-f6hBE2ZJxZM#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: content-brand, lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A doctor running a $51M genetic-report and supplement company, growing on word of mouth, wanted one clear message and funnel.
detail: Channel mix about 22% retail, 40% wholesale, remainder Amazon. Systems are in place except marketing and funnels. Google and Meta ads cost $50-60K per month and convert but are not the engine. He believes he is the bottleneck because of the breadth of conditions he treats. The panel found his message already clear.
numbers: Revenue $51M, growth about 22%, EBITDA about 22%; ads $50-60K per month; retail 22%, wholesale 40%.

### MZ-f6hBE2ZJxZM#02 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:07:04
claim: A quiz funnel can route each visitor to a video on their issue, then to a product recommendation, and takes only an afternoon to build.
detail: The form asks quiz questions, then a decision tree sends the visitor to one of about ten videos matching the most common problems. He says the owner needs one marketing person from outside his world, not a large project.
numbers: About 10 videos for the 10 most common issues.

### MZ-f6hBE2ZJxZM#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:46
claim: Pay science-minded influencers 100% of the profit on a low-barrier front-end test so they push traffic, and make your money on segmented follow-up.
detail: Sequence: build the quiz funnel, recruit influencers, make the genetic test the front-end offer, give the influencers all its profit (owner earns zero there), then run segmentation sequences to sell on the back end. Ancillary purchases also arrive from people who visit the site.
numbers: White-label whole-genome test costing about $400-500.
