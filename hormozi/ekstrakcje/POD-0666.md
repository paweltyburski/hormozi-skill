### POD-0666#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:11:37
claim: Private equity produces many billionaires because several arbitrages stack: buy at a discount, borrow against the asset, and raise partner capital.
detail: Illustration: a manager with $1M brings in partner money to reach about $100M equity, puts a third down on three $100M businesses with bank debt for the rest, grows them, pays partners their hurdle, then keeps a large carry. He says few other paths multiply small capital this fast in five to seven years. Real estate is more efficient, so it rarely gives 100x.
numbers: Assumed 20% growth, stated as roughly doubling business value in five years (arithmetic not exact); carry left over $100M-$200M.

### POD-0666#07 | EVOLUTION | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:27
claim: Hormozi reversed from "stop branding" (episode 7 of his podcast) to building a personal brand after seeing billion-dollar celebrity-brand outcomes cluster within about a year.
detail: His earlier view was cold outreach, ads, referrals and affiliates, and it suited local businesses: a very large personal brand does not monetise well through a local business. Triggers: Kylie Jenner becoming a billionaire at 20, then beauty, whiskey, tequila and drink brands reaching large valuations. A famous friend's remark about paying any price for impact made him start making content that week.
numbers: Beauty-brand owner sold a chunk at a $600M valuation (he thinks the company is now worth over $1B); the shift took about 12 months of such outcomes.
