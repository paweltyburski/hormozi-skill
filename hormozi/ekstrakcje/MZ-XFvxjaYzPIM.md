### MZ-XFvxjaYzPIM#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: pricing, content-brand | fw: Sales to Fulfillment Continuum | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A health and wellness creator stuck selling a $4.5K offer to an exhausted audience can add a low-ticket product built from the premium one.
detail: Creator had 400,000 organic followers, $10-20K per month, and lead-generation trouble. Hormozi says launching low ticket is easy if high ticket already exists, because the positioning is set.
numbers: 400,000 followers; $10-20K per month; high-ticket price $4.5K.

### MZ-XFvxjaYzPIM#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Sales to Fulfillment Continuum | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Enter a market from the top down: use the premium price as an anchor and sell a large share of its value at a tenth or hundredth of the price.
detail: Explain that you can only take a limited roster of premium clients (he cites about 40), then offer the resources given to them to everyone else at low ticket. The anchor makes the cheap offer a no-brainer.
numbers: Low ticket priced at 1/10 to 1/100 of the premium price.
