### MZ-GiVLL6F6tx0#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:19
claim: When information is free and unlimited, the value sits in selection and curation, and in compressing the buyer's time.
detail: Information has been free since the internet; consulting means handing over a cup from a river. Buyers pay for faster, easier, friction-free routes, so a personal way of doing something still sells even when AI can make the pretty ebook or graphics.

### MZ-GiVLL6F6tx0#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:53
claim: As AI content spreads, real people with real struggles become more valuable, so community stays a lasting bet.
detail: Answer to what humans can do that AI cannot: realness and authenticity, with the number of such things shrinking. Community is consumed month after month, so people keep wanting it.

### MZ-GiVLL6F6tx0#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing, product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:41
claim: Separate the consumable part of your value from the one-time teaching, and price the recurring fee on the consumable part only.
detail: Ask what inputs your method needs that change each period. Examples: tested ad winners (gym launch), weekly trending products (3D printing), vetted property deals (wholesaling). Courses and webinars are one-time value priced at their relative value; the recurring fee should be smaller and match what is consumed.
