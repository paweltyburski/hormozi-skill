### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A mastermind and group-coaching seller at about $3M, down from $4M, blamed Brazil and wanted to expand to English; Hormozi called expansion a distraction.
detail: Offers: 100 members at $20K mastermind and 200 at $10K mentorship, plus low-ticket feeders; 600K Instagram and 200K YouTube followers. He ran 4-5 launches a year and added evergreen, and ROI fell as spend rose. Hormozi: no supply constraint, sales process fine, so it is an advertising problem.
numbers: Revenue $3M (peak $4M in the pandemic year); 100 at $20K; 200 at $10K.

### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Trying many tactics without metrics means you cannot tell what works; usually one thing works a lot and the rest barely.
detail: "Everything works a bit" is poor attribution. Install tracking software for directional data, then put resources behind the one winner.

### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:14
claim: The coach's DM funnel moves boosted-reel followers to an application page with the price shown, then to an audio message and a closer.
detail: Steps: boost reels, DM new followers, send to an application that pitches $20K for seven-figure businesses or $10K for smaller; price is reviewed in the application; qualified applicants get a personal audio message; ready ones close in the DM, others go to team call.
numbers: $20K for seven-figure-plus businesses; $10K for below.

### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#05 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: paid-ads | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:04
claim: Test whether ads really reach cold people or only retarget your existing audience when ROI falls as spend rises.
detail: His theory: boosted posts to followers act as retargeting. Related checks: units sold per month, what stopped working, what stops more spend, and whether launch viewers convert over several launches.
numbers: One launch: 50,000 reais of spend returned 500,000 reais (10x).

### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#06 | BENCHMARK | topic: paid-ads | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:11:23
claim: Higher-cost leads can be more valuable: $17 leads worth $189 beat $5 leads worth $20.
detail: In his launch, lead-gen campaigns paid $5 per lead for about 4:1 return, while ROAS-optimised campaigns paid $17 per lead for over 11:1. Do not cut the more expensive source on cost per lead alone.
numbers: $5 lead worth $20 (4:1); $17 lead worth $189 (about 11:1).

### MZ-rLVyErufDSc#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:34
