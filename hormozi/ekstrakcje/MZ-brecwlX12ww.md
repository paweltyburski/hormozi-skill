### MZ-brecwlX12ww#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A direct-to-consumer wellness company (supplements plus dietary protocols, $16M this year, 22% net, goal $100M+) is capped because the founder personally makes the ads and buys media.
detail: Sales are pure direct response with no influencer strategy. Hormozi asks which tasks are least valuable; the founder names ad making, but Hormozi says media buying is increasingly commoditised and should be handed off first, and mentions an AI plugin managing Meta ads in real time.
numbers: Revenue $16M this year; net profit 22%; goal $100M+.

### MZ-brecwlX12ww#02 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: lead-getters | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:57
claim: Build a self-feeding creative machine in which ordinary customer activity produces the ad material.
detail: Three moves: run a testimonial contest with a year of supplements as the prize; add a small attractive digital bonus (such as a 30-day cleanse) unlocked in the first seven days for sending a video; and in a customer community run a monthly or quarterly referral push whose real aim is capturing posts. Each is a creative cadence.

### MZ-brecwlX12ww#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:29
claim: For a serious $100M e-commerce move, Hormozi says you will probably need a brand: bet on roughly one to three creators or authorities who embody its values, whitelist their ads and use their leverage to bring in micro and nano promoters underneath.
detail: Almost no e-commerce company reaches $100M a year without a brand; smash-and-grab sellers vanish. Choose between health and beauty influencers or a doctor figure (a doctor in a white coat reading scripts often scales fast but is less durable). Build a promoter community, not only a customer base.
numbers: One to three creators.
### MZ-brecwlX12ww#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:18
claim: Meta's new ad system rewards massive creative volume: instead of broad national campaigns, run hundreds of micro-ads.
detail: The platform now understands the content of ads and videos, so each piece of creative finds a narrow audience. Cost per result drops at first but each creative burns out faster, so cost rises sharply later; keep feeding the machine.
