### POD-0994#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His favorite question at the start of the year: which one thing, if it happened, would cause the rest of his goals to be accomplished by consequence.
detail: The answer becomes his priority for the year. Beyond the regular blocking and tackling of business, that is his full focus.
### POD-0994#02 | DEFINITION | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Priority is singular and comes first; strategy is how resources are allocated to it, and most owners fail by refusing trade-offs.
detail: Having several priorities means the biggest trade-off is never doing the most important thing.

### POD-0994#03 | DEFINITION | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:27
claim: Strategy is the curation and prioritisation of opportunities.
detail: Curation means listing the things you could do; prioritisation means choosing which you will actually do. Start from the long-term vision, then find the steps, even if unclear.

### POD-0994#04 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:23
claim: Hormozi's early career shows one stated goal per year, each leading to the next.
detail: 2010 consulting job; 2011 ace the GMAT; 2012 quit and start a fitness business; 2013 make the gym profitable; 2014 open second and third locations; 2015 reached 6 of a planned 10 gyms and pivoted to launching other people's gyms; 2016 launch others' gyms and sell his own; 2017 fix the Gym Launch money model.
numbers: 6 gyms reached against a goal of 10 (2015).

### POD-0994#05 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:58
claim: His later yearly goals moved from scaling Gym Launch to selling it, investing, brand and advisory.
detail: 2018 scale Gym Launch to $2M a month; 2020-21 sell Gym Launch and Prestige Labs (done 24 December 2021); 2022 design the investing model; 2023 build the social brand; 2024 launch the advisory practice; 2025 record book launch; 2026 credibility in legacy media.
numbers: Gym Launch target $2M/month (2018); advisory practice ~175 staff.

### POD-0994#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:11
claim: Two actions are one priority when one enables the other, as selling his own gyms financed launching others'.
detail: Link each year's goal to the next. The single focus is what lets you say no to everything else.

### POD-0994#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:55
claim: Big goals take about the same effort as small ones, so small goals are a poor use of limited resources.
detail: Quoted views from a private-equity founder and a startup thinker: a restaurant takes as many hours as a venture. A venture investor adds that people overestimate downside and underestimate upside, since a missed seed investment is lost many times over. Setting big goals is therefore a strategic bet.

### POD-0994#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:59
claim: Plan as far ahead as you can see, and save aggressively so you can swing at the rare big opportunity.
detail: First cover near-term needs such as rent, then plan six months, then a year, then business needs. He has always lived well below his income to keep the capacity for big bets. Confidence in "future me" comes from placing progressively larger bets on yourself.
