### MZ-LrsUgepHc3Y#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: paid-ads, strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A 20-year radio business, five years online, earns 80% from hobbyists and 20% from commercial buyers and asks when to pivot to B2B.
detail: Commercial buyers are first responders, county governments, hotels and warehouses, mostly via referral so far. Ads run on every platform. The owner hesitates because the commercial market is a very different product.
numbers: Revenue $3.5M; ~80% (about $3M) from ads; AOV ~$275; ~1,200 orders a month; ~800,000 US hobbyists; ~2,000 new licences a month.

### MZ-LrsUgepHc3Y#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: E-commerce often shows a cap near $1M a month, and wholesale distribution can then be layered on top.
detail: He hedges this from one e-commerce business that reached about $20M. The wholesaler pitch becomes: I send people in the door who buy other things in your shop.
numbers: Cap around $1M a month.

### MZ-LrsUgepHc3Y#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: paid-ads | fw: CRO | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:44
claim: Before pivoting, push $3M toward $10M with better creative, CRO and upsells that lift average order value.
detail: He cites another company where doubling AOV let it advertise five times harder.
numbers: Doubling AOV gave 5x ad capacity (other company).

### MZ-LrsUgepHc3Y#04 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:05
claim: Make UGC easy and incentivised enough to receive about 50 ads a month.
detail: The firm already runs a monthly giveaway where entries include user videos.
numbers: Goal ~50 inbound ads a month.

### MZ-LrsUgepHc3Y#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:25
claim: Bridge to commercial buyers by emailing the hobbyist list for introductions to first responders and giving them free radios.
detail: About 1 in 100 consumers may have a tie to such an organisation. Cost of a radio is about $50, trivial against the value of a department.
numbers: 1,200 orders a month gives ~12 qualified leads; radio cost ~$50.
