### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Taking the wrong lesson from a failure is more dangerous than the failure, such as concluding that employees do not work after one bad hire.
detail: A false story you tell yourself then guides later decisions, and experience does not guarantee the right takeaway.

### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#02 | EVOLUTION | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:46
claim: He suspects his early inbound success taught him that success should be big and fast, and he now steers toward sustainable growth.
detail: Inbound ads let him make money almost immediately, even tomorrow. Several things he has done well over a long time (fitness, marriage) came from slow, steady, sustainable choices. He once dismissed sustainable growth as nonsense and now tries to shift toward an endless horizon.
### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:08
claim: Hormozi says that as he has become more experienced, he has had to increase the time between his thoughts and his actions; in his experience, when you see a competitor run an ad or an employee leave with clients, most times no reaction is the best decision.
detail: Early on you need to shorten the gap because you're paralyzed and can't act, but at a certain point you get so good at taking action that you end up breaking things.
### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:08
claim: If he can sustain his current growth rate for 10 years, he says the business would be at $100 million plus or $200 million plus.
detail: He stresses consistency and low volatility, since the richest people look for reliability. Separately, he tells listeners to ask whether they can grow at 10 every quarter; the unit is garbled in audio, probably percent.
numbers: 10 years, to $100M-$200M+; separate example to listeners: growth of '10' per quarter (unit unclear).
### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:46
claim: He is now building businesses that truly grow without him, with his role limited to direction and being a sounding board for leaders.
detail: Not just stepping out of daily operations: the enduring parts must not require his skill.

### YT-FTgJ0mQi0uU#06 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:11
