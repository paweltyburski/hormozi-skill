claim: A solar sales company with 80 people and $2.5M revenue, three offices of 15-20 reps each, is limited by organic-only recruiting after eight years.
detail: Hormozi: the largest solar sellers are really recruiting businesses; more people selling means more money. The owner's own stated goal is to sell and fund a wellness facility, needing about $10M.
numbers: 80 staff; $2.5M revenue; goal $10M; 8 years to reach 80 people.

### MZ-4IWj8d32fPY#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: hiring-talent | also: retention-ltv | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Compare cost to acquire talent with the lifetime gross profit per rep, treating reps as recurring customers with activation checkpoints.
detail: Biggest drop-off is in weeks one to two, then day 30; after day 90 reps tend to stay. For this caller he said they probably need way more people on the front end ("kiss more toads").
numbers: Checkpoints at week 1-2, day 30, day 90.
