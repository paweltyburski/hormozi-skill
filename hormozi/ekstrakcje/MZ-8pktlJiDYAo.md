### MZ-8pktlJiDYAo#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A dance school with 800 students and $2M revenue (about $500K profit) wants to reach $4M in one location.
detail: County market of 100,000; space is available. With 72% retention students stay about four years; half re-enrol after the May show, 22% by September. Hormozi: growth is purely advertising.
numbers: 800 students; 72% annual retention; $2M revenue, $500K profit (stated as "top 500").

### MZ-8pktlJiDYAo#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: paid-ads | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:07
claim: The school spends about $20,000 a year to get 200 new students, a CAC near $100 against $1,600 yearly revenue per student.
detail: That is 16:1 in year one and about 64:1 over four years. Hormozi guesses ads lose efficiency at about $7,500-$10,000 a month on Meta alone, and even a fall to 2-3:1 would be fine since payment comes up front.
numbers: CAC about $100; revenue per student $1,600 per year; ratios 16:1 first year, 64:1 over four years; ad ceiling $7,500-$10,000 per month on Meta (his guess).

### MZ-8pktlJiDYAo#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:04
claim: When ad capacity is not the limit but people to work leads are, hire for it: a $60,000-a-year role is small against $500K profit.
detail: Too few staff to follow up leads limits spend; profit would dip from $500K to $440K.
numbers: $60,000 per year for one full-time lead handler.
