### MZ-BiaG-lHU0BQ#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals, hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A travel-food YouTuber should hire a high-leverage strategist rather than chase a 20% sponsorship lift.
detail: He makes two videos a week with a 21-person team in Vietnam, yet stays in research, shoot, voiceover and three to four review rounds. Advice: headhunt a proven channel builder, accept slightly lower profit, and use freed time to sell sponsors.
numbers: Revenue $4.3M; profit about $3.3M (over 70%); maxed sponsorships add about $800k (about 20%); strategist $200k+ a year; profit about $3.15M after.
