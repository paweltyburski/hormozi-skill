### MZ-wOjKcCodC8w#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A sales agency selling electricity plans and backup power at $300K a month had fulfilment partners with a 120-day wait and about 40% cancellations.
detail: Hormozi reads cancellations as a sale problem: reps promise "next month" and keep pushing it. Alternative seen elsewhere: collect commitment and bank details first, canvass until a local threshold is reached, then send installers. His script for the actual offer: lower bill, takes four months, nothing to do, sign and be first in line.
numbers: $300K a month, goal $1M; 120-day wait; ~40% cancel.
