### YT-iwskrByMIqo#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:47
claim: The seller who cares most about the buyer's interest wins the sale, and buyers can sense whose agenda it is.
detail: He calls the smell of self-interest "commission breath". Come to the call believing you care more about the buyer's outcome than they do, so that you can get them past their own obstacles. Once the buyer is real with you, you have won; even in the rare case they cannot afford it you are on the same side.

### YT-iwskrByMIqo#02 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A struggling gym owner went from zero for about 25 to five for nine on sales by changing only his intent, with the same script.
detail: On the daily coaching call he was told to remember why he cares about the customer. He realised at his 25th attempt that he had been selling on his own agenda; nothing in the script changed.
numbers: ~0 for 25, then 5 for 9 (approximate, as stated).

### YT-iwskrByMIqo#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:19
claim: There are two winning types of salesperson: the process-driven one who follows the script, and the personality-driven one whose evident care and conviction make up for lower skill.
detail: The second type are warm, genuine people who love the product. Their conviction in the product is what lets them close despite lack of technique.

### YT-iwskrByMIqo#04 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:04
claim: Confront the buyer's limiting beliefs from a stance of care, including who is responsible for past failures.
detail: Example for fitness: ask whose responsibility it was that earlier attempts failed (schedule, partner, themselves), say you ask because you want to help, and state that this time will differ. Done with care, they raise their baggage before the close instead of at it. He describes sales as coaching toward a defined outcome.

### YT-iwskrByMIqo#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:23
claim: Advanced people are defined by outcomes, not different tactics: they never skip the basics.
detail: Advanced lifters use the same methods as novices but lift more because they are more consistent. Best salespeople execute the same tactics more reliably.

### YT-iwskrByMIqo#06 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:29
claim: Entrepreneurs hit three kinds of breaking point, in turn: skills, character traits to execute them, and beliefs.
detail: Skills are many (copy, creative, landing pages, traffic, nurture, conversion mechanism, scripting). Character means patience, endurance and discipline. Beliefs cover what is possible and the standards you tolerate for speed and quality. The cycle repeats at each level, and each level adds to what was needed below.
