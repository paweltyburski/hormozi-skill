### YT-JbxxxVXzHEg#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: offers | fw: Starving Crowd | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Choose a market with a tailwind of more demand than supply, then niche down one or two steps inside that growing bucket.
detail: Quoting Buffett: in a good entrepreneur and bad market pairing the market wins, and picking the right boat matters more than how hard you row. Being in a rising market eases funding and demand. Check growth, ease of targeting, pain and purchasing power, then zero in.

### YT-JbxxxVXzHEg#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: Four Market Variables | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His 2021-22 list of growth markets: crypto-related products, cannabis, e-learning, remote living, and warehousing and logistics.
detail: Reasons given: crypto (NFTs, DeFi, Web 3.0) is booming; cannabis is relatively recession-resistant and not yet fully legal; e-learning keeps fragmenting into niche specific skills taught by top teachers; remote living drives home studios, home improvement and decoration with demand outrunning marketing; logistics benefits from people wanting delivery with least effort, which is the value equation applied.
applies_to: Founders choosing a new market (as of 2021)
