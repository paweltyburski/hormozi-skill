### POD-0675#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:33
claim: Four nurture pillars from paid ad to booked call: speed, volume of follow-up, personalisation and availability.
detail: Basics done at scale: contact leads immediately, follow up several times a day, personalise, keep an open loop between first and second call (offer real, valued service such as finishing the second half of something on the next call, giving reciprocity and a reason to show). Speed covers first contact and reply speed (answer a late text at once). Personalisation means using the channel they prefer: message on all channels, then stay on the one they respond to. Volume means number of touches per day and number of days.
applies_to: ad-driven appointment funnels

### POD-0675#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:07:25
claim: Show rate is driven first by number of available time slots per day; do not display a partly filled calendar.
detail: From data on 4,000+ appointments per day in his lead nurture software. More hours, days and slots means people book their ideal time and show more; a worse slot lowers show likelihood. Booking windows up to five days out were fine, which surprised him. Trade-off: more slots lowers show percentage but raises total booked and total shows, so more closers may be needed. The interviewer, not Hormozi, said he used to use the partial calendar for scarcity; Hormozi calls the idea horseshit.
numbers: 4,000+ appointments a day (data base); booking window up to 5 days acceptable.
### POD-0675#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:40
claim: Advanced operators do the same basics, just standardised and executed by good people at scale; the constraint becomes operations.
detail: Servicing clients one-on-one is easy; the hard part is getting 100 people to do it the same way. Said after taking one company to $10 million a month.
numbers: One company at $10M/month.

### POD-0675#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:23:32
claim: Three routes to create leaders beyond the founder face: events, productised delivery in a niche, and department heads paid on their P&L.
detail: One, nine-figure education firms nearly all use events since coaching teams do not scale. Two, hyper-niche so nearly all service is productised and non-founders can deliver equal value; the more general, the less standardisable. Three, exceptional leaders run a department as a micro company (Gym Launch agency; Amazon-style CEOs with own P&Ls) and are paid from its profit, aiming for ownership-like compensation.
applies_to: founder-led coaching/education company around $1M/month
