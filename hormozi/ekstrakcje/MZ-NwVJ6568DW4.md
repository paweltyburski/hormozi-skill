### MZ-NwVJ6568DW4#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: He thinks the first step in a big decision is answering what he wants to have happen, and he says people are terrible at knowing what they want.
detail: He calls decision-making the highest-leverage skill in life. At about 19 or 20 he wrote a diary list of goals (a luxury car, a million dollars, a fit partner by 25) and later realised, within three or four years, that these were not the right wants. Short time horizons let him correct cheaply; those who aim for a billionaire status can lose family and health before finding out.
numbers: Goal list aimed at before age 25, including $1 million.
### MZ-NwVJ6568DW4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:30
claim: People mostly want what they lack, and memory keeps rewards while punishment fades, which distorts decisions.
detail: Those without children want them, parents recall the childless period; the hungover swear off alcohol and return within a week. Because the cost fades and the reward sticks, people repeat choices that hurt them. Knowing this, treat strong wants with suspicion until tested.
