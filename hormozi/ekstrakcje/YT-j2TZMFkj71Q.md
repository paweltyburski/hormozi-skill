claim: For a craft membership he tied the activation step to ad creation: members unlock a secret bonus sticker set by filming themselves making their first sticker in the first seven days.
detail: The owner had failed to get members to post videos. His fix makes the first sticker a quick win in onboarding and turns every new customer into a video ad; at ~200 customers per launch, that is ~200 ads. Members already post photos of their stickers, which he expects to work as static ads.

### YT-j2TZMFkj71Q#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:05
claim: To keep ads working as spend rises, add hooks for pain- or problem-aware audiences; if acquisition cost jumps (e.g. triples) with spend, it is typically because the ads reach only product- or offer-aware people.
detail: Uses Eugene Schwartz's five levels of awareness: the warmest, smallest group is offer-aware, the coldest, largest is unaware. Some rise in cost with spend is normal; a sharp rise usually means the ads speak only to the bottom. Offer and bootcamp ads stay, but pain-driven hooks (e.g. bored, not sure what to do) open the targeting up.
