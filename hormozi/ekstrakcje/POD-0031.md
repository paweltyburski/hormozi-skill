### POD-0031#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A gym owner wanting to launch online training was told to first get the gym to $50,000 a month.
detail: He asked whether they would care about online coaching if the gym did $50,000 monthly; they said probably not. Other callers wanted to add a food-prep or online business while their gym struggled. Every business has problems, and a second one just doubles them.
numbers: $50,000 per month target for the gym.

### POD-0031#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Side projects often protect the ego: being too busy is easier to admit than a failed all-in effort.
detail: If you commit fully and it fails, it is on you; spreading thin offers an excuse. He tried five ventures at once and made no money, and income rose only when he focused on one.
numbers: 5 ventures at once: no income.

### POD-0031#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:51
claim: His mentor said he went from $3 million to $30 million a year by focusing on one business.
detail: Reported second-hand from a mentor's talk to a group of marketers.
numbers: $3M to $30M annual (mentor's claim, revenue implied).
