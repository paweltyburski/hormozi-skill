### YT-AN2KpRBsmRY#09 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:17:25

### YT-AN2KpRBsmRY#15 | DEFINITION | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:33:01
claim: Six concern types: no time, no money, someone else decides, not the right fit, other people's past experiences, and stalling.
detail: They sit under three groups: blaming circumstances, blaming other people, or avoiding the decision (blaming oneself). "Fit" means a preference that seems not to match the product (for example not wanting a meal plan). Past experience with others' products is overcome with "burn you twice". A stall is a request for time, not a time shortage.
