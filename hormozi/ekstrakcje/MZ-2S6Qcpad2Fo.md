### MZ-2S6Qcpad2Fo#01 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals, leads-fundamentals | fw: Referral Rate minus Churn | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A seller of listing software for Amazon sellers, helped by a partner's large YouTube audience, thinks lead generation is its limit but actually has a churn problem.
detail: Free information, weekly coaching and webinars feed a $50-a-month product. The leverage comes from free YouTube impressions.

claim: Typically, software should first reach a resting growth rate from word of mouth, and then you can add gas to it.
detail: He became a co-owner of School because he saw its metrics kept growing. Cheap software needs lots of traffic. One of two ways to grow software is to have more referrals than churn, so growth compounds even with high churn.
### MZ-2S6Qcpad2Fo#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:01
