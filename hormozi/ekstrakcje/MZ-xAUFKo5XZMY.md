### MZ-xAUFKo5XZMY#01 | CASE | topic: product-delivery | also: scaling-constraints, pricing | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A fitness, nutrition and sleep studio that keeps raising prices cannot grow because the founder does about 95% delivery.
detail: Revenue is about $700,000, mostly in-person, with a dietitian and a trainer under contract. Entry price is $6,000-$20,000 for 12 weeks. Hormozi concludes the bottleneck is delivery weight, not talent, and that demand exists but cannot be served.
numbers: Revenue about $700,000; 90% in person; price $6,000-$20,000 per 12 weeks; 95% of time on delivery.

### MZ-xAUFKo5XZMY#02 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Delivery Cube | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:06
claim: Sell expertise, not labour: be the architect who sets the plan each cycle, not the person counting reps.
detail: Like a concierge doctor, give access and tweaks, not three sessions a week. Customers who want hands-on training can be referred to a trainer. Move online later as a bridge.
