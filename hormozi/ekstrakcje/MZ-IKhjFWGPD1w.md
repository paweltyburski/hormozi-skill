### MZ-IKhjFWGPD1w#01 | CASE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: verticals | fw: Warm Outreach Steps | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A family office selling estate (legacy) planning, insurance and tax strategy sells about three plans a month from its personal sphere and aims for 21 trusts a month.
detail: Revenue about $210K so far this year, pacing a little over $1M (period unclear in audio). Life insurance has a 60-day close cycle and is not recurring.
numbers: 3 plans per month now; goal 21 per month.

### MZ-IKhjFWGPD1w#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:18
claim: For wealth advisers, three repeatable paths exist: ads to a webinar, local ads to an in-person event, or attorneys and accountants as referral partners; he told this caller in-person was the easiest for them.
detail: Local ads on Meta (older audiences) to a free dinner or session, then optionally a paid meeting of $50-100 to filter. Local trust can compensate for lack of brand. Those who scale fastest choose in-person.
numbers: Paid meeting $50-$100.
### MZ-IKhjFWGPD1w#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: promotions-launches | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:08
claim: Expect about 25% of confirmed registrants to show, half of those to book a consult, and half of those to close.
detail: 100 confirms means about 25 attend. His advice to this family-office caller: make the in-person event the main offer, give the lead magnet as a bonus for registering, print it out in person, then nurture hard.
numbers: 25% show; half book; half close.
### MZ-IKhjFWGPD1w#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: paid-ads | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:30
claim: When an ad draws views and clicks but no conversions, ask what the offer is, why someone clicks, and the landing page, optimisation and call-out.
detail: Here about $100 a week bought roughly 20,000 views and 600 clicks with no conversion; the call to action centred on legacy and his story, and Alex said that was not the offer, suggesting an assessment or free call.
numbers: $100 per week; 20,000 views; 600 clicks.
