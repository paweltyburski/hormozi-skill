### MZ-jtDzaRZTT5w#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Higher-priced offers show higher churn because of weak value matching and poor qualification, not price itself.
detail: Cutting price with everything else fixed will lower churn, but the question is whether revenue rises.

### MZ-jtDzaRZTT5w#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: unit-economics | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:55
claim: Judge price moves by lifetime value and earnings per click, not churn alone.
detail: A 50% price cut that reduces churn by 20% is a bad call by his example; he then asks what if page conversion doubles, which shows the answer depends on earnings per click.
numbers: Price cut 50%; churn improves 20%.
### MZ-jtDzaRZTT5w#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: The price-response curve is flatter than expected; a 50% price rise can cut conversion by 40%, so net effect may be only about plus or minus 10%.
detail: For a $500K per month business, a small price change adds maybe 2% revenue.
numbers: $6K versus $9K launch example; $1,250 to $1,350 gives about 2% more.
