### MZ-gt1Abe2hjmU#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A luxury spec home builder near $30M revenue is limited by cash for land and lenders, with a goal of $50M or more in three years.
detail: Homes sell at $1-2M, lots cost $200-400K, about 26 starts this year. Margins: about 25% gross, 12-15% net (about $4M). A warm investor network is exhausted and pays 9-15%.
numbers: Revenue about $30M; gross margin 25%; net 12-15%; lots $200-400K; builds take about six months.

### MZ-gt1Abe2hjmU#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:42
claim: There is no shortage of capital, only of good deals; a small-business borrower should expect to pay about 13-17%, not 9%.
detail: Offering the same return as safer assets is a bad deal. Find one backer for a rolling facility, senior-secured on the homes. Better terms beat searching for many investors.
numbers: Market return for this risk about 13-17% (he says maybe 13-15%).

### MZ-gt1Abe2hjmU#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:02
claim: Check what extra financing costs really are before changing the model: 12% a year on a $400K lot for six months is about $24K on a $1-1.5M house.
numbers: $400K x 1% a month x 6 months = $24K.
detail: A couple of points of capital cost barely moves net margin.

### MZ-gt1Abe2hjmU#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:58
claim: To do more, do more of what works rather than switching to something you do not know.
detail: He argues against a shift to custom-home sales with the customer's land: finding landowners with no builder is a small group, and custom clients are demanding. Restart direct mail, which worked years ago, for land.
