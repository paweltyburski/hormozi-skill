### YT-HGZOxBfnF-E#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:56
claim: Turn the custom path into a funnel: custom click, one-step form plus calendar, VSL, SMS qualification, then a sales call that takes the card.
detail: Present a DIY-and-custom brand with cool custom looks; opt-in books a call. Calendar sits with the form. After opt-in a video sales letter gives price ranges. SMS confirms budget, decision-maker and timing. The call closes the sale; do not run multiple calls.

### YT-HGZOxBfnF-E#07 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: BANT | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:26
claim: Qualify by text and in the VSL: ask who else must be on the call and frame it as a buying call, not a long sales process.
detail: Texts ask if a spouse should join, framed as respect for everyone's time. The VSL itself covers budget (come knowing it), authority (both partners, one has taste, one the money), need (assumed from intent), and timing (how fast do you need it, which also pushes urgency).

### YT-HGZOxBfnF-E#08 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:34

### YT-HGZOxBfnF-E#10 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:30
