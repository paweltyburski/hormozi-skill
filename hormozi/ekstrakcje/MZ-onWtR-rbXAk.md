### MZ-onWtR-rbXAk#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals, finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Brazilian accountants-only college with big distribution wants to sell and asks whether to build accounting software.
detail: Niche of over a million accountants; he wants a sale, not just growth.
numbers: Over 30,000 active and 100,000 total students in six years; 1.2M followers; revenue about 7M US dollars (12M reais, unclear in audio).

### MZ-onWtR-rbXAk#02 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: finance-exit, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:39
claim: Sell an annual continued-education membership to professionals: monthly expert webinars and a forum on new rules.
detail: Common in professional services and creates recurring revenue worth more to an acquirer without building software.
numbers: Typical price $1,000-$3,000 a year per member.

### MZ-onWtR-rbXAk#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:53
claim: Adding software does not earn a software multiple; value comes from viral growth, high gross margin and revenue retention above 100%.
detail: Net negative churn means even if 80 of 100 stay, upgrades make revenue exceed 100%. Only exceptional software helps. Build only with a technical co-founder given meaningful equity, keep equity for key engineers. He has not seen it done well.
numbers: Example: 80 of 100 customers retained, revenue retention above 100%.

### MZ-onWtR-rbXAk#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:28
claim: Hormozi's possible second idea for this caller, which might be better than building software: find the best accounting software in Brazil and strike a deal that is very profitable for you, collecting its sticky revenue.
detail: You can tie that revenue into your continued education offer without building another company.
