### YT-hHkdbr6_JJs#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: content-brand | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Acquisition.com's flow is attention, then a contact-capture conversion event, then a higher-value service, then reinvestment of the revenue.
detail: Media (content, courses, podcast, videos) is paid for and produces raw attention. Minor conversions collect contact data: the email newsletter, a School community trial, books. Those people are routed to the one higher-value service. Revenue from media, books, School and services is then redeployed.

### YT-hHkdbr6_JJs#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: money-models | also: pricing | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:23

detail: After these minor conversions, people can be funneled to higher value-added services; currently the only one is the advisory practice. The newsletter is in some ways a product in itself.

### YT-hHkdbr6_JJs#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Ascension Path | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:10
claim: The advisory practice has three levels about $5,000, $35,000 and $135,000, each a step up in hands-on help.
detail: The in-person workshops run at the Las Vegas headquarters with associates, analysts and directors. A share of level one buyers move to level two; a share of those want more detailed help from Hormozi himself and go to level three. Names are plain (L1, L2, L3) and prices may change.
numbers: L1 about $5,000, L2 about $35,000, L3 about $135,000.
