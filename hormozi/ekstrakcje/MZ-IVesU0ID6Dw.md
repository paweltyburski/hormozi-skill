### MZ-IVesU0ID6Dw#01 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A buy-and-hold group of four software firms wants to grow from about $12M to $90M in five years.
detail: Three firms serve property managers, one event management. It buys 100%, keeps founders with cash growth bonuses, has capital, and needs deal flow while also helping weaker acquisitions.
numbers: ~$12M revenue; $90M target in 5 years.

### MZ-IVesU0ID6Dw#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Spend most time on the one thing that delivers all other goals, here generating demand and brand.
detail: Brand brings talent and deal flow; hire others to run everything else, such as his venture portfolio.

### MZ-IVesU0ID6Dw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:17
claim: In small acquired companies the founder is usually the limit.
detail: Good founders ask what single thing would 10x the business; mediocre ones get sucked into day-to-day. It is a who problem.
