claim: Do not persuade, arrange the conditions so the desired action is the likeliest and easiest option.
detail: Grandpa-pill example: crush the pill, put it in cold lemonade with snacks, and offer the backgammon game after he drinks. Treat people like trainable subjects to predict whether they will buy.
