### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#01 | TACTIC | topic: outreach | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Warm Outreach Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Build the starting list from three sources: email contacts, social followers you can DM, and phone contacts.
detail: List one is everyone you ever emailed, list two every social profile where you can message people, list three the phone contacts exported. Pick one platform to start with, the one holding the most people. Personalise each first message with a detail seen in a 30-second look at their profile (new baby, new job, a move).

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: outreach | also: sales-process | fw: Warm Outreach Steps | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:38
claim: Illustration of ACA: acknowledge a detail, compliment it, and ask a question tied to the compliment that leads to your service.
detail: Weight-loss illustration: notice they have two kids, praise them as a super mom juggling a job, then ask how they fit in healthy eating and exercise. It reads as human conversation, not pitching.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: outreach | fw: Warm Outreach Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:26
claim: While talking, ask whether they know anyone who wants the result, framed as opening a few slots.
detail: It is a favour request to a friend, not a solicitation, so it removes the awkwardness of messaging. A contact who says "tell me more" has become an engaged lead.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#04 | SCRIPT | topic: offers | also: leads-fundamentals, outreach | fw: Perceived Hidden Costs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:12
claim: The free first-customer deal is a trade with three terms: use the service, give feedback, leave a review if deserved.
detail: Script structure: state that you do it free; list the three conditions; say why (reps, feedback, learning). The prospect is paying with time and effort, so they are real customers. Hormozi warns not to take more than about five, which also frames the offer as a limited case-study spot.
numbers: Cap at 5 free case-study customers.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#05 | SCRIPT | topic: outreach | also: lead-getters | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:17
claim: Referral-ask script: describe the struggle and dream outcome, say you take five free case studies, cite a similar past client, ask who comes to mind.
detail: Structure: name the person you seek (struggle, outcome, time frame); say you take five free case studies only; mention someone who recently got the result; ask for testimonials across different situations. If they answer no, joking "does anyone you hate come to mind?" sometimes yields the real answer. One-breath version: I help [type] get [outcome] in [time] without [effort], and guarantee [X].

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:16
claim: Buffett offered to work free for Graham and was told he was overpriced, because free still costs the teacher training time.
detail: Hormozi's point: free is not free for the buyer; time and effort are the payment, and beginners who are weak get better by doing more reps.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Perceived Hidden Costs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:14
claim: If people refuse even a free offer, ask why; the answer reveals hidden costs you can remove and later charge for.
detail: Example: a freelancer offered to cut short videos from Hormozi's existing material and post them himself, so the only action required was a yes, which Hormozi gave and later paid for.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#08 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:54
claim: Once referrals begin, switch to paid with a ladder: free, 80% off, 60%, 40%, 20% off, then full price, then above.
detail: Each step covers about five clients and reuses the same script; later he goes 20% or 40% above retail as feedback improves the product. When slots fill, scarcity is real. Tell free users that a paying client was accepted and ask whether they will pay to match; if not, ask for the review.
numbers: Steps of 5 clients: free, 80% off, 60%, 40%, 20% off, full price, then 20-40% over.

### YT-w7g08dVTwaE#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:46
claim: Free users bring business three ways: reviews, referrals, and converting to paying.
detail: Go through the full list a second time after the first pass, then keep contacts warm with regular value; testimonials and results posted over time make people start asking you.
