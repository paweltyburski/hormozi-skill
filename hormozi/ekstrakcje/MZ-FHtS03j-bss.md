### MZ-FHtS03j-bss#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: sales-team | fw: CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A self-generating roofing or home-services firm saw CAC rise with LTV because of percentage commissions.
detail: Hormozi said the link only holds with percentage commissions; with flat or scaled commissions CAC is team plus media plus software divided by customers.

### MZ-FHtS03j-bss#02 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:07
claim: Most home-services and real estate firms raise LTV by vertical integration, and the front-end rep need not earn on later pieces of the value ladder.
detail: Scale commissions down on later sales if they are paid.

### MZ-FHtS03j-bss#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:02
claim: Set commission by the skill the role needs, which depends on how much training and process exists.
detail: If a stranger can be trained to close near the top reps, pay little; strong process lets you pay less and improves margin.
numbers: Example: off-street hire within 20% of top reps in 30 hours.
