### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:19
claim: Hormozi asks how leads are worked — reminder sequence, speed, personalisation, number of attempts — because poor lead-working is among the largest revenue holes in small businesses.
detail: Most fail to contact leads fast enough, often enough, personally enough or at the right time. A documented sequence (here three texts, a call the night before and one two hours before) plus an ~80% show rate led him to say there are probably no immediate holes or low-hanging fruit there.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#08 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:21
claim: When a prospect questions a step such as giving a card for a free trial or an insurance card for a $29 visit, answer that it is simply how it has always been done.
detail: Hormozi found that appealing to policy ended the objection when he sold free gym trials; people accept that staff follow procedures and stop asking. He suggests it for any small process change introduced into a sales flow.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#09 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:29:47
claim: Hormozi: make in-person sales "clicks and checkboxes" — turn the top performer's steps into a presentation or checklist so every rep says the same thing.
detail: Two formats he has used: (1) a laptop turned to the customer, reading slides as a visual aid; (2) a clipboard script the customer cannot see, boxes ticked as you go, then stapled to the contract to prove the process was followed. This removes variation between reps.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#10 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:33:45
claim: Hormozi: to manage an ad agency, give it a target cost per acquisition or per lead and speak its language; keep a stated limit below your true limit.
detail: Agencies tend to overshoot, so pad the gap between the number you tell them and what you will really pay; as trust grows, narrow it — a higher allowed cost lets them buy more traffic. Agencies may set budgets by average clients (here per-visit chiropractors) and underspend for high-value models.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:35:41
claim: Hormozi: expect efficiency to fall as you scale spend; less return per dollar on a larger budget often yields far more absolute profit.
detail: $1,000 returning $10,000 nets $9,000; $10,000 returning $50,000 nets $40,000 at half the ratio. Raising the allowed acquisition cost 2-3x can multiply lead flow far more than proportionally. For this chain he would keep raising Google spend until returns fall to ~5:1.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#12 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:32:12
claim: Hormozi's advice to this chain: build local SEO on long-tail terms (town plus condition); he says local rankings are easy to win and give the best returns after about a year.
detail: National head terms are hopeless; local combinations (town plus chiropractor, sciatica, lumbar and so on) are not. Free articles on the site convert searchers through its calls to action; review volume helps. Rank each market and start before opening the next location.

### YT-sBJppqCeFGI#13 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:38:02
claim: Hormozi's way to find proven ad copy: identify agencies serving high-ticket operators in your niche, list their clients, and study those clients' ad libraries for the copy they all share.
