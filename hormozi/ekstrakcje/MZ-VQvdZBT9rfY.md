### MZ-VQvdZBT9rfY#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Pick one business; over-expansion usually means being under-talented, because the best team moves to the new market and the core suffers.
detail: Shore up the castle before invading. Expansion with improper talent leaves revenue up and profit near zero, and then you must cut one.

### MZ-VQvdZBT9rfY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:56
claim: Pay structured as a percentage of revenue blocks economies of scale, so a structural change is needed even at the risk of losing staff.
detail: Change it or lose the business: short-term pain versus long-term pain.

### MZ-VQvdZBT9rfY#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:28
claim: At full capacity without profit, raise prices even if some customers leave.
detail: Underpriced businesses cannot handle more customers and do not make money.

### MZ-VQvdZBT9rfY#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:28
claim: A single-product firm becomes break-even as CAC rises; it needs a second product and back end, not a new business.
detail: You built a product rather than an ecosystem.

### MZ-VQvdZBT9rfY#05 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:23
claim: A wedding-business coach at a $1M run rate blamed sales conversion, but Hormozi suspected leads were the real constraint.
