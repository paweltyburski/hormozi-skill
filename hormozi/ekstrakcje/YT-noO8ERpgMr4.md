### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Alex Mosey (not Hormozi) says three recurring founder mistakes stem from expectations, emotions and expenses, with expectations the most dangerous.
detail: Mosey says arbitrary expectations create scarcity feelings, which shorten decision loops and lead to poor decisions and unneeded expenses, which in turn endanger the original expectations.
### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#02 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: An entrepreneur at about $100,000 a month asked for a guarantee of $1,000,000 a month within six months; such arbitrary timelines lead to shortcuts and high-risk decisions.
detail: Forcing a set outcome by a set date causes hasty decisions that often set the business back by ten steps.
numbers: $100,000 per month to $1,000,000 per month in 6 months (asked).

### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Keep high standards but attach expectations to process, not outcomes, since good process drives good results.
detail: Those who earn more than he does tend to hold expectations that are reasonable given the process.

### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:13
claim: Flip-flopping on the same data signals emotional decision making; when you feel urgency, slow down and sleep on it for 24 hours.
detail: People usually speed up when emotional. Felt urgency to decide today is usually made up, so he extends the time between the feeling and the action.
numbers: 24-hour delay.

### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:47
claim: In his opinion, the best decisions come from contentment and gratitude, and he usually does not decide when he is not in that state.
detail: He reserves complex multi-variable decisions for rested, content moments, then sticks with the choice through later difficulties.
### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:49
claim: Do not hire in anticipation of growth; let growth pay for people.
detail: Expenses are liabilities that lower the odds of meeting expectations. The exception is commission-only sales reps. Problems are easier to solve with cash in the bank and signed contracts.

### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#07 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:25
claim: At his supplement company he hired 25 support reps ahead of growth, and two years later four people handled more business.
detail: Overstaffing lowered morale and efficiency, taught the team to throw bodies at problems rather than fix process, and gave customers a worse experience.
numbers: 25 support reps before; 4 later handling more volume.

### YT-noO8ERpgMr4#08 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:51
claim: Call your beliefs assumptions, which makes them easier to revise than expectations.
detail: A coach gave him this reframe: "this is my assumption" invites feedback, while "expectation" is a must or should.
