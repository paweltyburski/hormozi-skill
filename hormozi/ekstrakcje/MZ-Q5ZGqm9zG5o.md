### MZ-Q5ZGqm9zG5o#01 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: content-brand, paid-ads | fw: User-Generated Content Flywheel | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Host a community of your customers where enthusiasts show off their use of your product; it raises repurchases and supplies content.
detail: Members buy from the brand they share a group with, especially when the owner interacts. Their posts become organic content, paid creative and the backbone of an email cadence of customers using the products.

### MZ-Q5ZGqm9zG5o#02 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:22
claim: Email the best community posts (name, place, accessory used, links), with permission, so you do not have to write copy.
detail: Featured customers are usually pleased, and others see how products combine, which drives more sales.
