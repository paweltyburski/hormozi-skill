### MZ-a1XWGGCkU_g#01 | CASE | topic: unit-economics | also: verticals | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A dentist converting a general office into an implant centre ($4.67M a year for two years, wanting $100M) has high no-show rates for consultations and too few consultations.
detail: Average ticket is about $35,000, a consultation costs about $1,000, close rate is 15-20% overall (about 45% for patients with money), so roughly six consultations per sale, about $6K CAC. Profit swings from +$150-200K to -$100K a month; breakeven is about $350-400K a month. Leads: 75% Meta, 10-15% Google, through an agency reporting about 7x ROI.
numbers: Ticket $35K; $1,000 per consult; close 15-20%; CAC about $6K; breakeven $350K-$400K a month; agency ROI about 7x.

### MZ-a1XWGGCkU_g#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:28
claim: A dentist in the high-ticket market is really in the direct response and high-ticket sales business, so the key function (acquisition) should not be outsourced.
detail: The big competitor presumably controls its own acquisition. The plan to reach $100M cannot rely on one agency. He had tried in-house marketing for eight months with a $15-20K budget and a $5-6K/month hire and lost money, but Hormozi said that was a hire, not himself learning.
numbers: In-house trial: $15K-$20K budget, $5K-$6K a month pay, 8 months.

### MZ-a1XWGGCkU_g#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:59

detail: The agency has about 150 clients at $3,000-$4,000 each; an account rep is onboarded in one or two weeks. He recommends vertical stack ("clouds to dirt") entrepreneurship: run ads, write copy, build pages and make strategy.

### MZ-a1XWGGCkU_g#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:45
claim: Marketing is the constraint, so cut chair time, hire another dentist and learn marketing.
detail: Ask which current task is least valuable; you will not reach $100M from the chair. At a wall of time or ignorance you must make a bet, and losing is a feature: a mentor said a billion needs $100M of losses and $100M needs $10M, as tuition.
numbers: Mentor rule of thumb: lose about 10% of the target to make it.
