### MZ-tYd-tis3dO8#01 | CASE | topic: offers | also: money-models | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A seller of custom GPTs built from authors' frameworks ($100K, goal $1M in 18 months) has too many offers: $497-$997 one-time builds, a $3,800-a-year platform and a $997 four-week bootcamp.
detail: Hormozi: pick the bootcamp and the platform, turn the $497 product into a free bootcamp deliverable, and upsell the platform at $4K or more (likely $5-10K a year for businesses). All ads go to the bootcamp.
numbers: Prices $497-$997; platform $3,800 per year; bootcamp $997.
