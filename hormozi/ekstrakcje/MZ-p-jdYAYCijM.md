### MZ-p-jdYAYCijM#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A single 45,000 sq ft tennis and pickleball facility doing about $3M a year at 22% margin is capacity-constrained and wants to reach $10M.
detail: Revenue is mostly lessons and clinics; pickleball only fills dead slots and has weaker margins. Two paths: raise price and improve sales (roughly $600K to $1M-$1.2M profit) or build a national network; he suggests price first, since demand exceeds supply.
numbers: $3M revenue; 22% margin; 45,000 sq ft; $600K to $1M+ profit.
