### YT-pmxzhyF0NrE#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: The base unit in which you count money shapes which activities you will take on, so spend time around people with larger units.
detail: He once measured things in burritos; hedge funds call a million dollars a "stick". If your unit is $100,000, a $1,000 activity feels not worth doing. Rich parents teach children to chase opportunities with larger units.

### YT-pmxzhyF0NrE#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:36
claim: Shorten your default response time; default to end of day or four hours instead of end of week.
detail: Five exchanges at one day each take ten days, but the same exchange between fast people takes 30 minutes, so they can do in a day what others do in a month. Rapid turnaround is also a sign of someone active.
numbers: 10 days versus 30 minutes for a five-exchange project.

### YT-pmxzhyF0NrE#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:21
claim: Wealth potential shows in time horizon: people who talk in decades, not days, usually build more.
detail: Extended marshmallow test: the longer the reward delay a person accepts (hours, a week, a year), the more successful they are likely to be. Impulse control and patience drive long builds.
