### MZ-YJZov8GtqKw#01 | CASE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A financial-planning firm whose advisors cannot get past 20% close rate after the top advisor left is told the leader is the problem, not talent.
detail: The founder used to close 42%, the previous top advisor 36%. A fractional sales manager added nothing in seven to eight months at roughly $5,000 a month, since close rate was 20% before and after. Hormozi says to replace the part-timer with a full-time manager.
numbers: Close rates 42% (founder), 36% (former top advisor), now about 20% (from 18%); team of about 5; fractional manager about $5K/month.

### MZ-YJZov8GtqKw#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:27
claim: A full-time sales manager runs daily script training, weekly one-on-ones, and call review.
detail: Daily: the manager models one of four script parts (intro, discovery, pitch, close) and the team role-plays. Weekly: a one-on-one covering personal issues first, then short goals such as rapport, tone and pace. In between: listen to calls and give feedback, and meet below-KPI reps once or twice a day, effectively a performance plan.
numbers: Four script parts; below-KPI reps meet the manager once or twice daily.

### MZ-YJZov8GtqKw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:48
claim: The sales manager's job is culture, and winning should be tied to following the process so that winners want to join.
detail: Culture is the rules of engagement: what is rewarded and punished. Have reps reinforce each other, credit wins to the process rather than individual closers, raise the bar each year, and cut out losers.

### MZ-YJZov8GtqKw#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:52
claim: Junior reps cannot sell like an expert founder, so write a script that lets them assess the problem and hand off the solution.
detail: A 23-year-old cannot hold the same frame. He suggests hunters who manage relationships and a specialist behind them who manages the money, used as a selling point: you do not want the quants handling your conversation.
