### MZ-AaUxdkGSB5Q#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: retention-ltv | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:24
claim: Who is in a community is the most underrated part of its offer, so gate entry and remove misfits, even paying ones.
detail: Holding the line early lowers later work, raises self-policing, retention, margins and referrals. Say no to money for about six months; he removed members paying $3,000 a month.
numbers: Six months of saying no; $3,000 per month member removed.

### MZ-AaUxdkGSB5Q#02 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:56
claim: Set objective entry standards, and learn the profile from your most active members.
detail: B2B: revenue, headcount, assets. B2C: demographic such as women over 45 with two kids. Oscillate between quantity and quality at first. Once traffic is enough, the community itself becomes the magnet and a network effect.
