### MZ-lQFt6VZwvTQ#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A digital family-reconnection business (~$1.5M, 60-70% margin, five staff) has the founder doing ads, copy and management.
detail: Paid ads are only 20% of leads and profitable but under-managed. Fix: hire a full-time marketing director to own paid acquisition. At $150-250K cost, about $700K profit stays.
numbers: ~$1.5M revenue; 60-70% margin; hire cost $150K-$250K; ~$700K profit after.

### MZ-lQFt6VZwvTQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:40
claim: Recruit marketing leads from small agency owners: skilled, scrappy and used to working without a brand.
detail: Many take home about $100K with stress and lowish margins, so a salaried role is attractive.
numbers: ~$100K typical agency owner take-home.
