### POD-0884#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: money-models | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A poker coaching business with two overlapping front-end offers should fix delivery capacity first, then buy paid traffic, then decide which offer to cut.
detail: Business: $2M this year, goal $10M. Offers: $7.5K for 28 days (about 12 a month) and $2.8K for 7 days (16-32 a month). The 7-day one is supply constrained by coaches; demand is only warm inbound. His advice: cutting either now just loses money, so re-engineer delivery for 3-5x capacity, turn on paid ads, and then pick the split.
numbers: 12 sales a month at $7.5K (about $90-100K); 16-32 a month at $2.8K (about $50-70K).
