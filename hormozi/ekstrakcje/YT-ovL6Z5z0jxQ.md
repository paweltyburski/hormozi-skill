### YT-ovL6Z5z0jxQ#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: All profit is arbitrage, the gap between what something costs you and what someone pays, and you can build a personal gap by earning in a rich market while living in a cheap one.
detail: Examples he gives: selling English-language services from Thailand, Malaysia or Vietnam to US or European buyers; or moving geography to cut costs. Thailand's median income is about $2,700 a year against a US median of roughly $50,000 to $60,000, so selling at US levels while living locally yields more than 10x between earning and spending. Even a skill or information edge is an arbitrage.
numbers: Thailand median income about $2,700/year; US median about $50,000-$60,000; gap above 10x.

### YT-ovL6Z5z0jxQ#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Wealth is a ratio of what you spend to what you make, and you can raise it by making more or needing less; the capital you can reinvest is the gap.
detail: Someone on $5,000 a month spending $500 feels rich, while someone on $500,000 spending more feels poor. In business, the aggression you can afford depends on that differential. Hormozi's own start: living in a $400 spare bedroom, then on the gym floor, sharing a bedroom, eating Costco food, with $50,000 saved and about $4,000 a month from online training before opening his gym.
numbers: $50,000 saved; about $4,000/month online income; $400/month room.

### YT-ovL6Z5z0jxQ#07 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:09
claim: A software company went from zero to $1.7M per month in six months by giving agency owners a free full coaching and training course for using the software.
detail: Competitors charged agency owners for training on selling, packaging offers and running ads. ### YT-ovL6Z5z0jxQ#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:10
claim: A giveaway must carry a visible real cost and lead to a defined back-end product; a 10x needs an order-of-magnitude difference, not an incremental tweak.
detail: Value that people can see others charge thousands for creates reciprocity. Long-term giveaways need a monetisation system: the challenge led to memberships, the book to licensing, the training to software, the sales system to products. His favourite model is to see what everyone buys, make it better, make it free, and monetise another way. Acquisition.com did this with free books and a free 14-hour scaling roadmap with about 30 pages per stage. He says 95% will not do this because it requires waiting.
numbers: Roadmap 14 hours, about 30 pages per stage; 95% will not follow through (his guess).

### YT-ovL6Z5z0jxQ#15 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:22:32
claim: The profit maximiser is the item with the best margin, which is often not the one you sell most of; the core product can draw customers while a hands-on top tier makes the profit.
detail: McDonald's sells burgers but earns more on fries and drinks; franchisees live on those margins. In service businesses the maximiser is the white-glove, done-for-you or concierge version; in products, new designs or better materials. A free giveaway builds demand and the profit maximiser captures it.
