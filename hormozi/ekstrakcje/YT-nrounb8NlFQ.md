Affiliates: six matchmakers and private clubs bring a third of revenue from ~40 cold emails; send 400 more, 20 a day, to four partner types (#12). claim: Hormozi: if choosing between a more valuable but slower lead magnet and a less valuable immediate one, prospects prefer the immediate one; make it risk-free, easy and fast.
detail: The business does the work up front ("pre-digest the food") so the prospect just clicks and gets value. Her idea of preparation steps (measurements, mood board) failed this test; a colour-palette result from skin, hair and eye colour, or how to use the existing closet, passes. Peel off the first step you normally charge for and template it to limit your time.

### YT-nrounb8NlFQ#09 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: BANT | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:24:29
claim: Hormozi's rebuilt funnel for this business: headline promising the free assessment to stated qualifiers, a deprivation image, short BANT questions, then a scheduler only for the qualified.
detail: Steps: (1) landing page with headline and sub-image showing a common style mistake; (2) opt-in asking budget, authority, need, timing plus skin, hair and eye colour (personalisation); (3) qualified go to the scheduler, others get a free automated palette guide; (4) VSL before booking and a second video between booking and call carrying the repeated pitch (Sales Aids); (5) on the call use one of ~10 prepared palette templates, widen the gap, then pitch. Photo upload moves to follow-up — too much friction.

### YT-nrounb8NlFQ#10 | PRINCIPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:32:21
claim: Hormozi: good friction removes only bad prospects and raises lead quality; bad friction, like a slow page, removes everyone. Ask only the fewest questions needed to decide.
detail: Applied to his qualification form: keep the questions that sort buyers (budget, timing, openness to change); move effortful tasks such as photo uploads to after booking, because a decision about which photo to pick loses people.
