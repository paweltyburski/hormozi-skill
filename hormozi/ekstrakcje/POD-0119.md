### POD-0119#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:58
claim: Eliminate small daily decisions so more of your capacity goes to big ones.
detail: He wears the same outfit and eats the same three meals daily, which also makes body composition a question of quantity only and simplifies groceries. At home, switches and drawers are labelled, groceries come from a delivery service and reorder buttons restock supplies. The aim is to run daily life on autopilot and keep capacity for important work.

### POD-0119#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:39
claim: Sleep is the highest-return habit: better decisions, less reactivity, and fewer stimulants.
detail: Most people undersleep. Problems look obvious after a good night. He cites, via his wife, a Harvard Business Review claim that about 80% of terrible hiring decisions are made while tired (secondhand). Stimulants mask fatigue instead of improving performance. He quotes a study that 90 more minutes of sleep raises wellbeing more than an extra $50,000 a year.
numbers: 80% of bad hiring decisions made when tired (as he relays it); 90 minutes of extra sleep versus $50,000 of income.

### POD-0119#03 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:39
claim: His daily routine: finish work at 4 pm, lift from 4:30 to 6:30, eat, one episode of TV, asleep by 9, wake without an alarm.
detail: Sleep comes in cycles of about 80 to 90 minutes, so he aims for 7.5 or 9 hours. He starts the day early because it is quiet. He drinks pre-workout when he does not feel like training so that he follows through. Evening stress tasks are avoided.
numbers: ~7.5 hours of sleep; asleep by 9 pm; sleep cycles 80-90 minutes.

### POD-0119#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:05
claim: Hormozi and Layla make zero exceptions to their routine, and they skip speaking events if they feel the travel will be tiresome.
detail: The reasoning is that stress potential grows with scale, so everything else must be made rigid, as a lifter braces everything to transmit power in one direction. They do not drink, even with visiting friends, and skip speaking events when the travel is tiring.
### POD-0119#05 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:00
claim: Remove from your phone any app or channel that makes you anxious.
detail: He does not keep Facebook on his phone because it stresses him, while Instagram does not. Anything that detracts from mental clarity makes you more reactive, and the team feels it.
