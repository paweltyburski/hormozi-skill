### POD-0150#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:53
claim: Every sale drives to one aha: the prospect saying why they failed before, which has only two real answers: they lacked the right plan, or they lacked accountability.
detail: The early steps (clarify, label the goal) only confirm you are talking to the right person with a shared goal. Asking about past attempts raises pain, but the crux is the gap question: what was missing. "Wrong plan" means it did not work or was not sustainable. After they say it, move to the pitch; a missed aha explains later obstacles you cannot beat.

### POD-0150#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:34
claim: Probe with childlike curiosity, as if you had never had this conversation, then guide them to their own conclusion.
detail: It relaxes the prospect; they do not feel pushed. Layer questions: how often did anyone check in on you; did you enjoy eating chicken and broccoli every day; could you keep that for life. Let them name the gap, then add pain points of what should have happened.

### POD-0150#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:45
claim: In B2B he usually leans on the knowledge side ("wrong plan") and would never question a business owner's work ethic; in weight loss, accountability is a common angle (the plan can also be wrong), which removes blame.
detail: Business owners believe they already tried everything, so for the most part sell on knowledge. Many weight-loss prospects accept "of course you did not follow through, no one checked in, the best people have coaches", which lifts their self-blame and builds rapport.
applies_to: B2B coaching versus consumer weight loss
### POD-0150#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:38
claim: Open the diagnosis in this order: why today, what is the goal, why it matters, what have you tried, then the gap.
detail: Each step sets up the "elbow drop" pivot: "you did not have accountability; if you had, you would have stuck with it?" Then "since I know what was missing, you will like this programme" and go to the pitch. Sometimes both plan and accountability were wrong.

### POD-0150#05 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:38
claim: Ask "why today and not last month or last year?"
detail: Part of the setup questions that set the stage for the pivot point about why they failed (the gap).
