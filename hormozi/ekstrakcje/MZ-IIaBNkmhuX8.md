### MZ-IIaBNkmhuX8#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A community has four levers: traffic, conversion, pricing and churn.
detail: Pricing is a pick, conversion is fixed once. The ongoing inputs are churn work inside and traffic outside, mostly advertising.

### MZ-IIaBNkmhuX8#02 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A $300-$400/month newsletter had lowest churn with only two deliverables.
detail: Monthly call plus newsletter; adding weekly call, Facebook Fridays and Monday Q&As raised churn. Roughly $500,000/month; churn ~15%.
numbers: ~$500,000/month; $300-$400/month; ~15% churn.
