claim: For himself, he won't quit if no new information has come to light; if the original assumptions still hold, it's a push, and if new data changes them, he would quit/pivot.
detail: He reframes quitting as pivoting: everything except stopping is activity, and you stumble on what works then do more of it. Invest-versus-consume is a continuum to manage, not an either-or.
### MZ-AnYpgeJxeSY#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:33
