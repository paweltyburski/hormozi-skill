### MZ-m5EnjKd-mEY#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: If staff do not do what SOPs say, either they are all idiots or the process does not work; you control only the second.
detail: Having SOPs differs from having a system in which people actually do what you want. Training the VAs, who are both the growth engine and the product, may be the biggest lever.

### MZ-m5EnjKd-mEY#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Choosing the one most important constraint is the entrepreneur's job; do not spend time ranking them all, because the order often reshuffles once the first is solved.
detail: Delegating prioritisation hands decisions to people with less context. Tell the team they may let other things fail meanwhile.
### MZ-m5EnjKd-mEY#03 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: paid-ads, management-ops | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:21
claim: A marketing team with seven funnels, a faulty pixel and no attribution took three months; fixed funnel by funnel with twice-daily check-ins, it took three days.
detail: Of three problems (CRO, attribution, pixel), the pixel was first because bad data raised cost each cycle. Each funnel got a four-hour fix with a check-in at a set time. Attribution next: custom links from 28 traffic sources.
numbers: 7 funnels; 3 months to 3 days; 4-hour fix windows; 28 traffic sources.
