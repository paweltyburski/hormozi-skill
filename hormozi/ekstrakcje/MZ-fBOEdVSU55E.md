### MZ-fBOEdVSU55E#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: content-brand | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:17
claim: In his view, structure a page as promise, plan, proof: the promise first, tiles that each relate to a key part of the plan, then proof whose outcome ideally matches the promise.
detail: Reading a page that way gives the visitor the hero's journey in advance: promise, path, then someone else getting the result. Vicarious fulfilment makes them willing to risk their money to live it.
### MZ-fBOEdVSU55E#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:31
claim: Given the choice, six visual tiles with no words beat all words with no tiles.
detail: Visitors look at tiles first, then copy. Test the page as a stranger would: explain it so a toddler understands, and cut insider jargon and fluffy language that blocks outsiders.

### MZ-fBOEdVSU55E#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:09
claim: Add a postscript stating who it is for and who it is not for, even on a free offer.
detail: It attracts the right people and words away the wrong ones. Drop the calendar link on free offers; keep the headline-features-plan-proof order.
