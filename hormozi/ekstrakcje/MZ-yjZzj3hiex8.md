### MZ-yjZzj3hiex8#01 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A festival bleacher-rental owner could not get leaders to delegate fully because processes were missing.
detail: He asked how to train leaders; a key logistics role will be hired from outside because the COO is skilled but struggles to delegate it.
numbers: Revenue about $4.5M; profit about $1M.

### MZ-yjZzj3hiex8#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:50
claim: Hire the person with the harder-to-teach trait and train the easier skill.
detail: If sales takes two days to teach and kindness two months, take the kind person. A strong person with a small fixable flaw can work if you state it up front. Recruiting matters more than process ("lost the championship in the draft").
numbers: Training sales about 2 days versus kindness about 2 months (illustration).

### MZ-yjZzj3hiex8#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:44
claim: Checklists broken into behaviours train better than loose SOPs, and most firms hire for skill then fire the rest instead of training.
detail: Example: train "kind" as smiling, nodding, repeating back, raising voice entering a room.

### MZ-yjZzj3hiex8#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:20
claim: Give feedback as stop, start, keep with specific behaviours.
detail: Failure to change a leader is usually vagueness from above.

### MZ-yjZzj3hiex8#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:33
claim: A leader called a jerk changed after being told the three behaviours behind it: interrupting, snapping back without context, forcing agreement.
detail: The framing was that the issue is how others describe him, not his inner nature. He had never received this feedback.
