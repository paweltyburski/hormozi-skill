### MZ-aB42IXc3-Rg#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: verticals, scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A physical therapy and training clinic was supply-constrained by a shortage of practitioners and responded by raising prices.
detail: Two clinics for athletes aged 35 to 45; he asked whether to go to a concierge membership model.
numbers: Revenue just over $3M, goal $10M; two sites of 3,000 to 4,000 sq ft; about 16,000 open positions nationally.

### MZ-aB42IXc3-Rg#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: lead-getters | fw: Cost to Acquire Talent | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:34
claim: Size a staff referral bonus to the value of a hire and pay it at month six.
detail: A new practitioner adds about $350,000 gross profit a year, so a $500 bonus motivates nobody but $20,000 would. Paying at month six makes the referrer help onboarding and refer good people.
numbers: Hire adds about $350,000 gross profit per year; bonus $20,000 versus $500; return about 12 to 13:1 in year one.
