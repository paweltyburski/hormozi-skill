### MZ-9QVUrfltdm8#01 | CASE | topic: paid-ads | also: verticals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A cleaning and restoration firm with two locations ($1.9M revenue last year, aiming for a $100M exit) gets about 92% of leads from Google Local Services Ads and keeps raising that budget without moving the needle.
detail: The owner suspects a lead-gen and avatar problem; the 92% figure is unreliable because the CRM is poor. The firm bought an existing business four years ago, debt is paid off, and revenue is one third cleaning, two thirds restoration. LSA spend is about $10,000 a week, up toward 12 in busy season (whether it covers both locations was not answered).
numbers: $1.9M last year; about $1M so far this year; LSA about $10K/week; 92% of leads from LSA (inaccurate data).

### MZ-9QVUrfltdm8#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:25
claim: Local search can be very profitable yet capped by how many people search; past that you must move from intent to interruption marketing.
detail: Interruption (for example Meta ads) opens the ocean because few people wake up wanting a cleaner. His three bets: content, interruption ads, or a channel-partner programme. Choose the lowest-risk one for the owner's strengths.

### MZ-9QVUrfltdm8#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:58
claim: With two businesses to feed, if the foot in the door is easier with cleaning, he would most likely put his front-end lead-gen focus on cleaning and run a cross-sell between cleaning and restoration.
detail: Restoration has far more inbound intent, since flooded owners already know their need and no ad creates it. He says to get a first dollar across the line, with the restoration work coming later.
### MZ-9QVUrfltdm8#07 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: scaling-constraints | fw: More Better New | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:49
claim: Before any new channel, expand into keyword PPC next to LSA; it is a more-of move with a lot of headroom.
detail: He doubts a market cap near $300K for cleaning search when each location does about $900K split across both lines. Keyword search can absorb about $100K a month, and the inbound call set-up already exists. Stay on Google first, then try net-new.
numbers: Doubted cap $300K; each location about $900K; keyword search capacity about $100K/month (his estimate).

### MZ-9QVUrfltdm8#08 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:39
claim: The net-new option for this owner is centers of influence such as property managers and plumbers, a slower route but with recurring value.
