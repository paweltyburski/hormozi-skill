### MZ-NW-Nq73whoE#01 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Learn enough to judge candidates by interviewing many experts, giving each a case study on your own business.
detail: Reach out to experts to ask how they think and what they would want in the role, then ask for introductions. Present your real problem, ask what they would do, and push for specifics. Most people hold three interviews and pick the least annoying candidate, a bad strategy.
numbers: 10-20 interviews.
applies_to: Founder strong in product, weak in marketing or sales.
