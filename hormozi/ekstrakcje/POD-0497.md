### POD-0497#07 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: $100M Scaling Roadmap | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:29
claim: Six figures needs one avatar, one product and one channel; seven figures needs the same, done reliably and consistently, with predictable input-to-output numbers.
detail: Predictability means e.g. ad spend leads to booked calls leads to sales, or emails sent lead to replies, bookings, shows and closes, or posts and visitors to opt-ins. Hormozi says that once you can predict how many inputs give an output, you get to a million; at roughly one to three million you build the core team.

### POD-0497#08 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: paid-ads | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:48
claim: A channel is the combination of method, platform and media type: a single pathway by which a stranger becomes a customer.
detail: Pick one of the six ways to get customers (own list, cold outbound, content, paid ads, affiliates, referrals), then a platform (for cold outbound: call, text, DM or email), then a media format (voice memo, text, handwritten card). Focus on that one pathway.

### POD-0497#11 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: retention-ltv | fw: $100M Scaling Roadmap | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:59
claim: At about $3M the objective switches from proving fit and a profitable channel to raising lifetime value per customer; scaling marketing first is the common trap.
detail: Early on, more marketing equals more sales, so owners keep doing it ("getting cute" with the same lever). From 3 to 10 the work is customer experience, data tracking and an ascension offer so later channel expansion is more profitable. Otherwise margins compress by 10 million and free cash flow disappears.
numbers: Zero to 3M: fit plus a profitable channel; 3M to 10M: raise LTV.

### POD-0497#12 | TACTIC | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:18:24
claim: If a company grew to 10M with compressed margins, step back to fix product, customer experience, service, data and infrastructure, and replace people promoted too early; growth to 10M then follows on its own.
detail: Only after that does he push acquisition hard using more, better, new: max out what works, then improve (CRO, headline, lead magnet, video sales letters, follow-up emails), then add a channel or a new platform inside a current channel.
numbers: Aggressive acquisition from about $10M.

### POD-0497#17 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:31:02
claim: Scaling is control and leverage: relinquish control step by step, accept "good enough" until you can afford better, and treat hiring labour as the lowest form of leverage.
detail: You cannot review every email, post or call. Leverage is the gap between input and output; better cash flow lets you attract better people and fix the culture.

### POD-0497#18 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Buy vs Build | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:31:56
