### YT-rX5zKeQu7ow#01 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A gym operator partnering with Hormozi set a punctuality standard by locking the door on a late-staff meeting and announcing that latecomers were fired.
detail: The partner ran a gym earning about $4.2M a year and had a second location with a lax new staff. He called a mandatory meeting at the first site, had the door locked at the start time, and told his assistant to fire those knocking before the meeting began. The room understood the new standard: show up on time, clean, follow up with leads.
numbers: Gym revenue about $4.2M a year; second site about 45 minutes away.

### YT-rX5zKeQu7ow#02 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:40
claim: On his first shift at Smoothie King, a manager told him to skip the cleaning and restocking the owner had trained him to do, and that became the norm.
detail: He calls this the "Vince" problem: a manager who quietly undoes the owner's standard. Ask whether you are the Vince in your business or whether you have one. Culture is set by the lowest tolerated behaviour.

### YT-rX5zKeQu7ow#03 | EVOLUTION | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:04:14
claim: He says a business needs both smarts and hearts, but most people, himself included, overestimate smarts: strategy is not that complex and gets lost in execution, so fixing people and behaviour can serve better than tinkering.
detail: Earlier he leaned toward strategy, pricing and acquisition mechanics. He now sees the main constraint as people not doing what is asked because they don't see why, or dislike each other. Resolve interpersonal friction before inventing a new strategy, and drop the belief that employees cannot do what the owner does.
### YT-rX5zKeQu7ow#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:30
claim: Hire for character traits, interpersonal dynamics and self-awareness at least as much as for skill, and be intolerant of anything below the standard.
detail: Two companies with the same strategy differ by execution: the one that sets the tone and holds the bar gets better advertising returns, retention and lower staff turnover. Advanced people never skip the basics (example of basics: follow up on leads within five minutes, ask why they came, label the problem, onboard well).
numbers: Lead follow-up within 5 minutes (example of a basic).
