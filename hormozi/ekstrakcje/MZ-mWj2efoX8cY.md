claim: A law firm with excess demand hired only about two attorneys a quarter and tracked no outbound recruiting metric.
detail: HOA-litigation niche, top-caliber lawyers only; recruiters, job boards, LinkedIn, poaching. Room for 20 more lawyers.
numbers: 31 lawyers; about 8 hires per year; revenue about $15M; profit about $4M.

### MZ-mWj2efoX8cY#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:57
claim: Justify the hiring gap by laying out hires times cost, added profit and the sale multiple.
detail: 20 hires would cost about $1.2M and add roughly $4M to the bottom line; with multiples around 8 in the space, that implies roughly $32M (calculated from his figures). He then argues the firm needs to push recruiting volume much harder.
numbers: $1.2M cost; about $4M profit; around 8x multiple.
### MZ-mWj2efoX8cY#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:24
claim: Ask hires per period, value per hire, cost per hire, headcount needed and outreach volume.
detail: He concluded the firm likely needs to roughly 3x its recruiting volume.
### MZ-mWj2efoX8cY#05 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:12
claim: When the talent funnel is the only limit, multiply recruiters and outreach 3x-10x.
detail: Two recruiters become ten; outreach rises 10-20x.
