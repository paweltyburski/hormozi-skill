### MZ-HUsN0HMfOoA#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Florida dental group with 10 de novo offices (going to 12) and about $25M revenue wants $50M; it feels limited by dentist recruiting and bank funding.
detail: Offices are second-generation spaces: lease an old dental office, add equipment. Dentists came from the owner's podcast and partner's network, which stops working as the pace grows. Bank paused lending and asked for taxes and a stronger plan. Hormozi: business is fine, the owner just needs to cut distributions.
numbers: $25M revenue; EBITDA ~21% (about $5M); 10 offices; opening cost ~$250-300K each.

### MZ-HUsN0HMfOoA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:48
claim: A repeatable unit that returns its investment fast should be funded from cash flow; judge it by return on invested capital and payback.
detail: Hormozi's math: about $250K build plus ~$100K to recruit the team = ~$350K per office, against roughly $500K annual profit per mature office. First-year offices do about $1.5M revenue at lower margin. Even at 18 months to full profit, payback is 12-18 months, so spend 20-25% of profits. Using bank money only feels less risky.
numbers: ~$350K invested per office; first-year revenue ~$1.5M; payback 12-18 months; use 20-25% of profits.

### MZ-HUsN0HMfOoA#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:25
claim: Count how many hires a year the growth plan needs to decide whether recruiting needs its own person.
detail: Six new offices means six dentists (one per two months) plus four or five staff per office, plus leadership hires, roughly one hire every two weeks. At that rate a dedicated talent-acquisition hire is justified.
numbers: 6 offices/year = ~1 hire every 2 weeks.

### MZ-HUsN0HMfOoA#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:25
claim: Use headhunters for one-time specialised roles; build an in-house process for repeated roles that are core to the economic engine.
detail: Core roles here are dentists and treatment coordinators. A media company would own editor hiring, a sales company its salesperson hiring. For the in-house recruiter, find a recruiting firm on LinkedIn, go one level down to the person doing the work and offer better pay and treatment.
