### MZ-gk4SUbHRn6w#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Barbell: sell very expensive things to qualified buyers and keep them, or sell cheaply to everyone once you have capital; the middle is where businesses die.
detail: His low-price platform at $9 a month burns capital for years toward a multi-billion outcome. Undercapitalised marketers lack product skills because marketers think about what sells, not what they deliver.
numbers: $9/month platform price.

### MZ-gk4SUbHRn6w#03 | DEFINITION | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:50
claim: Software is valuable only if it has high gross margins, high revenue retention and a growth loop; any business with those three is valuable.
detail: A product that must be constantly refilled to hold revenue is not good; stickiness is required.
numbers: three tests: margin, retention, growth loop.
