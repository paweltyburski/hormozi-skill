### MZ-OyIYsKzPBHw#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: outreach, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An accounting firm for real estate entrepreneurs, 11 months old, thinks leads are the limit but spends too much time on fulfilment.
detail: Leads come from cold DMs and email (mostly LinkedIn) plus podcasts; an offshore team does 95% of heavy work while the owner reviews files. LinkedIn caps him at 150 connection messages, but Hormozi says workarounds and ads exist.
numbers: $250K in 11 months; goal $100K a month; LinkedIn cap 150 connection messages.

### MZ-OyIYsKzPBHw#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent, finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:51
claim: Every business incurs debt: talent debt, technology debt or financial debt; choose the one easiest to repay.
detail: Financial debt can buy systems and people but must be repaid. This founder chose talent and tech debt, paid in personal hours and stress, which changes but does not vanish.

### MZ-OyIYsKzPBHw#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:07
claim: Map the day minute by minute, hand off the lowest-leverage tasks, and reach about six hours a day of promotion.
detail: Keep this up until revenue is about $1M-$3M; then cash flow pays for a senior operator.
numbers: Six hours a day of promotion; $1M-$3M a year.

### MZ-OyIYsKzPBHw#04 | BENCHMARK | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:53
claim: At $1M revenue and 25% margin, a $180K-$200K operator removes the profit, so either the founder works 18 hours or the business is on thin ice.
detail: Illustrates why the first senior hire is painful.
numbers: $1M revenue; 25% margin = $250K; operator cost ~$180K-$200K.
