### MZ-lgT0K9AMtnU#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-team | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Puerto Rico software consultancy at $4M, mostly selling to government, can deliver about $6M with current staff but has only two people who can close.
detail: He wants $20-50M to raise his exit multiple. No sales training or scripting exists; he was the original closer. Ad spend is under $5,000 a month and works. Hormozi calls it ignorance, not a focus problem.
numbers: Revenue $4M; capacity about $6M; ad spend under $5K per month; two closers.

### MZ-lgT0K9AMtnU#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: sales-process | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:53
claim: If closers are booked out, better ads or landing pages add nothing; build sales capacity first, then raise marketing.
detail: Without a supply constraint, any front-end gain raises throughput; but the choke point is sales, so build a setter/closer system first. Posting more is fine, since it rarely lowers income.
