### MZ-gcOiI7rWipk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The person you share life with raises or lowers the odds of reaching your goals, so decide whether that matters to you.
detail: He calls marrying his wife Leila his best financial decision: she believed in him when he was a 26-year-old gym owner with a few gyms, and kept encouraging him as stakes rose. She also acted as a pure operator and leader whom staff were loyal to, so he could operate a more complex business.

### MZ-gcOiI7rWipk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:52
claim: Bigger problems feel the same in pressure, but later you face them with more resources; early stages build the grit.
detail: Starting out is like fighting a bear bare-handed; later it is fighting a dragon with an army. In some ways later is easier, but you only earn the right to face those problems by going through the scrappy stage first. He admits he has doubts about whether he can solve complex problems.

### MZ-gcOiI7rWipk#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00
claim: His risk is apathy, not anxiety: at some point "enough" appears and the drive to keep pushing fades.
detail: He compares it to reaching a very lean body weight and deciding it is enough. His wife cared about building a workplace people love, which kept him going through low-motivation stretches.
numbers: Body weight cited: almost 250 pounds and lean.

### MZ-gcOiI7rWipk#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:46
claim: Optimising for business outcomes and for wellbeing are different; decide what trades you will make because no partner is perfect.
