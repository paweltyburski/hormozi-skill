### YT-EdUAAVKl_io#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:09
claim: Jump two steps instead of one: delegate what you do now and find someone who already has the skill for the role you would take next, then step back.
detail: The usual path is to hand off a task and then learn the next skill yourself. Asking who already has the skill lets the business keep growing while you move to owner. He frames it as doing the wrong things (poor), the right things (rich), and owning the right things (wealthy), where owners assemble businesses and the self-employed build them.
### YT-EdUAAVKl_io#02 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:51
claim: Two fitness businesses made $17M profit combined in 2018 when he did everything and about $8.1M when he did nothing, yet he argues they are worth more now.
detail: He says the earlier figure paid him for his skill; the latter earns a return on what he owns. A 20% return would need about $9.6M next year, which he calls easy now that he sees all the pieces. Part of the fall was COVID for half the year.
numbers: 2018 profit $17M; current pace $8.1M; target $9.6M (20% growth).

### YT-EdUAAVKl_io#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:26
claim: If you interview about 20 candidates for a role and learn nothing from them, hire none; the person you hire should know more than you.
detail: Interviews should reveal what you are missing. You still need baseline knowledge of every function to judge talent and set the bar.
numbers: Example of ~20 interviews.

### YT-EdUAAVKl_io#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:54
claim: He suspects a business grows only as far as the point where the founder stops running it, so delegating at around $50,000 a month may slow growth (flagged as a belief, not proven).
detail: Skills across the business are what let you recognise the talent that will surpass you.
numbers: Example of a business at $50,000/month.
