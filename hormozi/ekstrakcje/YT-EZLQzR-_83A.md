### YT-EZLQzR-_83A#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Sell one product, one service and one avatar through one channel from zero to $1M, then add a second product line when moving from about $1-3M toward $10M.
detail: The pair is a front-end and a back-end offer. Accommodate different budgets through payment terms rather than additional products.
numbers: $0-$1M: one of each; $1M-$3M onward to $10M: two products.
applies_to: Owners with weak operations, below about $3M a year.
### YT-EZLQzR-_83A#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Large product catalogues built to serve every budget add operational complexity, and if staff cannot explain them, customers certainly cannot.
detail: A broad menu works only in theory with perfect training and infrastructure. In portfolio companies he simplified offers and reached each prospect's spending ceiling through payment terms.

### YT-EZLQzR-_83A#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:03
claim: Do not give reps a low, medium and high product to choose from; give them one solution and vary only how it is paid.
detail: Reps cannot decide what to push and prospects get confused. Instead of asking which product, ask how they would like to pay. Example on a six-month implementation: pay in full, two payments, three payments (priced higher, exact figure unclear in audio), or six.
numbers: Payment options 1, 2, 3 or 6 instalments over a 6-month implementation.

### YT-EZLQzR-_83A#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:51
claim: More payment options raise conversion, but cash flow may not rise because reps may default to the lower-paying options.
detail: Prospects focus on the monthly expense rather than total contract value. Because the offer should already carry very high gross margin, whether a client pays $1,000 or $100 a month leaves most of it as margin, while wealthier clients prepay and lift LTV.
numbers: Illustrative range $100 to $1,000 per month.

### YT-EZLQzR-_83A#05 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:39
claim: Customers who prepay the first offer ascend to the second offer faster, and the second is sold in a separate conversation.
detail: Selling several things in one conversation is harder, so solve the next natural problem in another call.
