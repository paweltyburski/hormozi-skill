### YT-rc7cxL7ql7Y#30 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:47:51
claim: The pace of hiring is a risk question of consumption versus investment, and reinvesting everything is probably only worth it where network effects make it a race.
detail: Your living draw is what you cannot reinvest; a rival without family costs may out-reinvest you.
