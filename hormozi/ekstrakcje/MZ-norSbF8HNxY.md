### MZ-norSbF8HNxY#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:21
claim: Compare the equity needed to bridge the cash gap for hires with the equity sold to investors to fund full cash pay.
detail: Take the gap between full-cash and cash-plus-equity pay. If giving staff equity costs less than investors would take for the cash, choose equity.
numbers: Example: selling 5% at a $100M valuation raises $5M.

### MZ-norSbF8HNxY#03 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:05
claim: Optimise anything by high volume, then study what the top 10% of the last 100 or 1,000 units had.
detail: Isolate that difference, do it in the next hundred, and repeat for ads, calls or content.
numbers: Look-back window of 100 to 1,000 units; compare top 10% to the other 90%.
