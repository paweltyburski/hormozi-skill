### MZ-FeU2l8sp3lo#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: At the start accept money from anyone; between roughly $1M and $3M, choose the customer type that pays most and is easiest to serve.
detail: Early on a pulse and a credit card are the qualifications. Later, with resources and skill, narrowing to one type of customer lets you templatise and systematise delivery. About 78% of US businesses are service based.
numbers: Niche decision point about $1M-$3M revenue; 78% of US businesses are services.
applies_to: Service businesses at $1M-$3M.

### MZ-FeU2l8sp3lo#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: Riches in the Niches | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:12
claim: Reword a generic podcast agency as "we help doctors generate patients using podcasts" so the right customer self-identifies and one product serves all.
detail: The goal is to make one thing for ten customers rather than ten things for ten customers: cut once, sell twice.
