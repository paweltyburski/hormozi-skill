### LS-0RjIw9M1j28#22 | FRAMEWORK | topic: finance-exit | also: money-models, retention-ltv | fw: Win-Back Campaign, Rollover Upsell, Attraction Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 01:23:21
claim: When a business needs cash fast (payroll, a tax bill, broken equipment), look in three places in this order: current customers, past customers, then the leads list.
detail: 1) Ask current customers to prepay, ideally for extra services at no cost rather than a discount (a discount only if needed). 2) Past customers: an "I owe you money" email or text crediting what they spent toward a much bigger package (e.g. $200 toward $2,000), framed as making good on past promises. 3) Leads who never bought: a compelling attraction offer from Money Models (e.g. buy one get one, win your money back).
