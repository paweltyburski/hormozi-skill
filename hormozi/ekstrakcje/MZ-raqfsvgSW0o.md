claim: A travel-gear DTC brand (over $5M a year, grown only on paid ads, 100K followers) aims for $10M a month, with only the founder and an assistant plus contractors.
detail: Founder was the avatar, found a backpack factory while studying in China and refined one design, then added two products. Profit was described as over 70% (unclear whether gross). Hormozi separates "double" advice (spend more on ads and content) from 20x advice.
numbers: Revenue over $5M; target $10M a month; ~100K followers (from 10K).

### MZ-raqfsvgSW0o#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: unit-economics, verticals | fw: CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:23
claim: As ad spend grows, margin compresses; offset it with product-driven word of mouth and brand reputation that lower CAC at scale.
detail: Word of mouth: each buyer sends about 0.2 more buyers. Reputation: bring in respected people as cornerstone associations. Without brand and distribution, a physical-product business cannot scale.
numbers: ~0.2 referrals per buyer.

### MZ-raqfsvgSW0o#03 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: lead-getters | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:41
claim: Aim for five to seven cornerstone products plus profit-maximising accessories, and use both micro-influencers and a few big affiliates.
detail: Activating micro-influencers might lift referrals per person from about 1.2 to 1.6. Big names add brand lift hard to measure but needed to scale. He says he builds businesses both ways.
numbers: 5-7 core SKUs; referrals per person 1.2 to 1.6 (example).
