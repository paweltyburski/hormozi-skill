### POD-0952#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A caterer busy September to February (large 60-100 person events) asks about a year-round corporate delivery arm.
detail: Winter corporate work has better LTV but lower spend. Acquisition is 40% SEO, 40% Google Ads, 20% referral. Search volume drops in winter. LTV is still above 12:1, so Hormozi's question was why not spend more on ads. The owner feared distraction.
numbers: LTV above 12 to 1; leads 40/40/20 (SEO/ads/referral).

### POD-0952#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Before accepting seasonality, ask why people do not buy in the slow season and whether the business is seasonal at all.
detail: He asked why people are not celebrating from now until September, whether the off-season loses money or breaks even (it stayed profitable), and what stops more ad spend.
