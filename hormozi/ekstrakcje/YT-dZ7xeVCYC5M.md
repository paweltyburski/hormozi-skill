### YT-dZ7xeVCYC5M#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Early on, the best return comes from investing surplus income in skills that raise earning capacity, not in index funds.
detail: Live cheaply and put all surplus into courses, coaching, mentorships and workshops. His arithmetic: a two-day, $500 certification that triples an hourly wage is worth more than any financial asset. A person earning about $50,000 and living on $20,000 could invest roughly $2,000 a month in learning, and raise that as income grows.
numbers: $7.50 to $25 an hour after a $500, two-day certification; $25/hour x 2,000 hours = $50,000; $2,000 a month invested in learning; at $80,000 income, $4,000 a month.
applies_to: people in their 20s starting from low income

### YT-dZ7xeVCYC5M#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:23
claim: Choose teachers by their free content, follow several for about seven days, and buy from the ones you like.
detail: Free value lowers purchase risk, since you already know you like them and learn from them. Treat each paid course as one class in an entrepreneurial degree, not a single bet that must make you rich; no one person teaches everything. He even suggests buying from all of them to learn what everyone knows.
numbers: Seven-day evaluation window.

### YT-dZ7xeVCYC5M#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:18
claim: Replicate before you iterate: be the number one student and copy exactly until you can duplicate the results, then change.
detail: Adding your own style comes only after you have earned the right by matching the original. His bridge image: 30 bricks stand between you and the first dollar; people quit at brick 3 and start another bridge, ending with many half-built bridges. Speed depends on finding which brick is missing.
numbers: Example bridge of 30 bricks; at 28 bricks you are about 90% of the way.
