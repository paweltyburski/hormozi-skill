### POD-0868#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:35
claim: Shut a client-based business down gently: announce, stop new clients, phase out over about six months, and sell the book of clients to trusted providers for kickbacks.
detail: Steps: thank loyal clients; say you no longer accept new customers; commit to the same service level during a six-month wind-down; recommend three trusted providers. Negotiate referral fees with those providers, which turns the revenue passive.
numbers: Six-month notice; expected kickbacks about $100,000 per year.

### POD-0868#05 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:06
claim: When a future supply constraint looms (facility lease) but sales and marketing limit growth today, fix demand first to generate the cash for the move.
detail: Equipment maker with two P&Ls had half its production already relocated to Texas; the rest sits in a dilapidated building with a lease ending in 24-36 months. The move would take about half a year of free cash. Hormozi first leaned toward doing both in parallel (different leaders, about a 12-week project), then, after learning the cost, advised sales and marketing first, then a project manager for the move.
numbers: Move cost about 45% of yearly free cash flow; deadline 24-36 months; move project about 12 weeks.

### POD-0868#12 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: product-delivery | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:20:21
claim: Quality means the absence of hard: list everything that makes existing providers painful and remove each item.
detail: Easy cannot be created, only the hard parts removed. Competitors may be impersonal, incomplete, unwilling to return, or carry risk for the restaurant. Operationalise value by removing those frictions, then sell the contrast.

### POD-0868#13 | SCRIPT | topic: offers | also: sales-process | fw: Grand Slam Offer | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:40
claim: Pitch to a price-focused buyer in one simple contrast line.
detail: Structure: here are the five things that go wrong with typical providers; we have none of them; in exchange you pay this instead of that; this is why it is worth it. Hormozi works on this difference before thinking about price, and puts it into every email, text and cold outreach.

### POD-0868#14 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:09

### POD-0868#18 | TACTIC | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: pricing | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:27:09
claim: Make the local lead magnet a banger by giving away a small slice of the job, then add friction through qualification.
detail: Examples: light the walkway or driveway free, or install free and charge for the lights only; markup can sit on materials with zero on labour.
