### POD-0012#01 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Run a small invitation-only event, such as a spa day, for top clients and top referrers so that referring becomes the entry ticket.
detail: Pick about 10 to 15 best clients. Those not invited ask why; the answer is that the event is for referrers, and the next one is open to anyone who refers. This ties scarcity and status to a free action. For larger events, tier the guest list: top spenders and referrers first, good customers second, the rest of the list last.
numbers: 10 to 15 top clients per small event.
applies_to: Gyms and other local service businesses.

### POD-0012#02 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:04
claim: Make the event visible: photograph it, give it a VIP name, and let non-attendees find out afterwards.
detail: Do not market it beforehand. People who ask what it was are told it was for top referrers, which makes them lean in. Send invitations by physical mail or courier, handwritten or signed, to make them feel special.

### POD-0012#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:12
claim: Ask each attendee to bring a plus-one, which turns a small event into new customers whose return can be calculated.
detail: High spenders and top referrers have networks of similar people who sign up and give the best word of mouth. Ten guests each bringing one friend yields about ten new clients. A cadence of every other month is possible.
numbers: 10 guests, 10 plus-ones, roughly every other month.

### POD-0012#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:13
claim: Referring raises lifetime value on both sides: referrers stay longer and refer again, and referred customers are less price-resistant.
detail: The sooner a client refers, the more likely they stay. Referred clients accept premium prices, move through the value ladder faster and also refer more, so the chain compounds.
