### MM-17#01 | DEFINITION | topic: money-models | also: - | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An upsell is whatever you offer next — step two of the money model, used after the attraction offer to maximise 30-day profit.
detail: Timing ranges from immediately to a month later. Every solved problem reveals another; upsell the solution to the problem your first offer exposes. Goal: more profit per customer and moving clients into back ends (00:03:05).

### MM-17#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Upsells often produce most of the profit even though the front end sells in far greater volume.
detail: Better to break even on ten times the customers and profit on the second sale than to profit on a few. Upsells make or break a money model.

### MM-17#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:01:32
claim: A burger shop earning $0.25 on a $2 burger reaches about $1.75 gross profit per sale with fries, soda and supersizing.
detail: Find your "fries and a Coke" — if you don't, a competitor will.
numbers: $2 price, $1.75 cost; $0.25 → $1.75 per sale (called 8×; figures give 7×).

### MM-17#04 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: money-models | also: sales-process | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: canon | ts: 00:02:20
claim: When an upsell fails, check whether it's the wrong thing, the wrong time, the wrong way — or a mix.
detail: Wrong thing: unwanted, too different, or not solving their problem. Wrong time: before they feel the problem, or after it's solved ("another steak?"). Wrong way: they don't believe you.

### MM-17#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: More Better New | novelty: canon | ts: 00:02:20
claim: Upsell more (quantity), better (quality) or new (complementary).
detail: Two burgers; sirloin or organic instead of mystery meat; fries and a soda.
