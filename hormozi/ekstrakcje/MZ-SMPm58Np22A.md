### MZ-SMPm58Np22A#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: When the structure of daily life stops working, call a recalibration conversation with your partner and change it.
detail: In December 2019 he told his wife he disliked how they lived; they agreed and changed priorities from business-first to marriage-first. He credits staying aligned to shared exposure to the same stimuli.

### MZ-SMPm58Np22A#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:32
claim: Couples who work and live together must manage closeness and make space to be missed.
detail: Remote work turns partners into roommates and removes mystery. They keep chemistry through physical distance during the day, and announce alone time ("three hours with a book") up front so it is not read as rejection. Distinguish wanting to be alone from not wanting to be with them.
numbers: Average couple has ~4-5 quality hours a week (his claim); they were apart only two weekends since meeting, excluding a three-week launch trip.

### MZ-SMPm58Np22A#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: other | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:50
claim: The person you esteem most holds the most reinforcers in your life, so your spouse shapes your behavior; choosing one is choosing which behaviors you take on.
detail: He says he became more patient, less angry and kinder through his wife, while she became more direct; they deliberately reinforce each other. Picking a spouse is partly buying the traits you want to acquire.
