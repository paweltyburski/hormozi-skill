### POD-0809#06 | CASE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:12:47
claim: TEDx-booking agency afraid to guarantee bookings since wait times doubled is told a guarantee is a math problem: compare extra closes against expected payouts.
business: agency — writes and books TEDx talks for entrepreneurs, $1.3M last year, goal $10M, average time to book rose from 10 to 20 months during COVID
detail: Owner fears another pandemic. Hormozi: she already survived one. If a guarantee doubles closes and costs one refund in ten, you earn far more. His story: a remodeler closing 80% but earning nothing raised prices 20% and promised on-time, on-budget delivery or forfeit of his entire profit (he hit both 8 times in 10; misses came from scope changes or late materials); he closed more and earned far more.
questions: TED or TEDx?
numbers: $1.3M revenue; goal $10M; time to book 10 → 20 months; remodeler: 80% close, on time 8/10, price +20%, guarantee = whole profit (~20%)
speakers: owner (Ashley): business facts; Hormozi: answer and story
applies_to: services with long or uncertain delivery times considering guarantees
