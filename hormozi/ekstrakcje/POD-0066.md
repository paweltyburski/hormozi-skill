### POD-0066#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Without a reason to earn beyond your own needs, income stalls at comfortable.
detail: Hormozi says he stayed below his earning potential for years because the only game was his own business. People with children earn more because they must, but that also caps out. To reach very high earnings the pull has to be bigger than the self, such as serving clients, and he describes it as the game outside the game.
numbers: Most people feel satisfied at about $100,000 a year (his view).

### POD-0066#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:13
claim: "Weapons-grade compassion": take full responsibility for the client's result and look at the customer, not the competitor.
detail: Most fitness owners entered through their own transformation and see the business as a way to pay bills. Competitors never send money and are never loyal, while customers are. His growth came from loving gym owners, which also rekindled his regard for weight loss clients.

### POD-0066#03 | TACTIC | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:53
claim: Reverse engineer the client experience instead of blaming clients for quitting or ignoring meal plans.
detail: Ask how to make it easier. Every industry can be disrupted because customers are served cheese and crackers by habit. Practical step: ask every customer what they struggle with most, then list ten ways to fix that.

### POD-0066#04 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:13
claim: SoulCycle built about $2.4M a year per studio with no recurring membership, by designing a memorable experience.
detail: The test: if a stranger tasted your product, would they return and tell friends the same day? Hormozi says anyone who answers "already" has lost, since someone will always raise the standard.
numbers: Average studio about $2.4M a year (as stated), no recurring billing.

### POD-0066#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:40
claim: More than half of Gym Launch's business came from word of mouth, unlike his own gyms.
detail: His gyms relied on marketing and sales. He pushed gym clients toward remarkable service as the next focus after acquisition was solved. The average client gym added a quarter million dollars of revenue.
numbers: Word of mouth over 50% of business; average added revenue per client gym about $250,000.
