### MZ-t7e4_ytUtsE#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: lead-getters | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:35
claim: Ask what would happen with 100 of your best lead-getters and what single thing would 10x the company in a year.
detail: Focus on getting many more big creators, since they are the biggest growth lever. Trust now sits with creators; pure direct response is declining and brand is needed.
