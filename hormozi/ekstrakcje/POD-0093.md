### POD-0093#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Once personal needs are met, people stop pushing; drive must come from the customer's unmet needs.
detail: A gym owner who hit all membership and income goals asked how to stay motivated. Salespeople plateau when they reach an income that satisfies their needs; he cites a single salesperson on $150,000 a year as typically satisfied. Parents earn more because their needs are larger.
numbers: ~$150,000/year as a typical satisfaction level for a single salesperson.

### POD-0093#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:49
claim: Solving a problem sustains effort better than chasing a goal, which gets a burst then a crash.
detail: Doubling sales one month to hit a target can be followed by a halving the next as the team burns out. A problem frame asks for the solution to stay solved. Gym Launch builds software and retention systems because the gaps in the industry are large needs.

### POD-0093#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:19
claim: Ask customers what they struggle with, list the problems, rank them and solve the next one.
detail: The answers will be a longer list than you can handle; feeling overwhelmed means you are on track. Customers' expectations only rise, as with delivery speed, so over-delivery earns only temporary satisfaction and keeps forcing improvement.

### POD-0093#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:18
claim: If you do not feel your customers' pain, either find customers you do love or commit to excellence at business itself.
detail: Many owners are fit people serving women in their late thirties losing weight after a second child, and cannot feel that pain. A friend with a cookie store is not passionate about cookies but about being excellent at business.
