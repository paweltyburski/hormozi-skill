claim: A founder bought a small niche listing site, raised venture money on a "marketplace" story it did not fit, and lost the company and his team.
detail: CustomMade.com listed woodworkers who paid about $35 a year. It was bought for $140,000 in 2008 (deposit first, then a 90-day diligence window used to raise money). applies_to: Founders weighing outside capital against a small profitable model.
