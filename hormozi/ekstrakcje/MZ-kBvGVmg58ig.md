### MZ-kBvGVmg58ig#01 | CASE | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A premium financial newsletter for 65+ do-it-yourself investors has 11% monthly churn after it began advertising to cold traffic.
detail: Price ladder is $97 front end, $1,200 and $2,300 back ends and a $5,000 bundle. About 65-70% of subscribers are annual. Onboarding is just 30/60/90-day emails of free content. Cohorts from the last 12 months stick less than 20-year subscribers, since ads only began 18 months ago.
numbers: Churn 11% per month; 65-70% on annual plans.

### MZ-kBvGVmg58ig#02 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Call about 20 customers from the last 18 months who stayed and 20 who left; study monthly-plan survivors since annual buyers could not churn.
detail: Ask why they bought and what they feared; the key question is the moment they knew it would be great. Then point all onboarding at that one action.

### MZ-kBvGVmg58ig#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:27
claim: In his School community, members who join several communities and get at least three people into one see roughly 11x lifetime value.
detail: Onboarding therefore pushes the first three members with a one-click text to contacts.
numbers: 3 members in one community; about 11x lifetime value.
