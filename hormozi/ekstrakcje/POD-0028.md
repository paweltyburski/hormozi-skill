### POD-0028#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:39
claim: Mental bandwidth spent resenting competitors is bandwidth taken from serving clients, so you lose to them with every thought.
detail: The same time can go to calling or messaging clients, personal videos, handwritten cards, events to which members bring friends, announcements before and after sessions, and a poster with a clipboard. These basics get neglected while owners watch competitors.
numbers: He imagines 20% of attention going to complaining about competition.

### POD-0028#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:08
claim: Two gyms in one big market can both use the same system and do fine, since the market is large and most people who want to lose weight are untouched.
detail: A gym owner objected to a second gym in the same city getting the system; Hormozi's answer was a shift from scarcity to abundance, pointing to Miami's roughly 3 million people. Ten gyms in one market running the system should all do well, like restaurants.
numbers: Miami about 3 million people.

### POD-0028#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:59
claim: Gym Launch reported that 50% or more of its new customers each month came from referrals, which he watches as a sign of word of mouth.
detail: A rising referral share means results are acceptable, the ask is compelling, and customers have a way to bring friends.
numbers: 50% or more of monthly new customers from referral (stated as 'now', no year given).
