### POD-0850#21 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Cohort Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 01:06:40

### POD-0850#27 | TACTIC | topic: unit-economics | also: finance-exit | fw: LTGP:CAC | novelty: new-detail | ts: 01:19:09
claim: Calculate conservatively: overstate CAC, understate LTV, using total lifetime revenue divided by all customers acquired as the floor.
detail: Use fully loaded CAC. For the floor LTV take all revenue since the business began over all customers ever acquired, even though young customers drag it down. If metrics pass on that ugly number you are safe.
