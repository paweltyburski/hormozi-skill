### MZ-DnjGWhXe85I#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: pricing, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Diagnostic sale for weight loss: compute the full timeline from current to goal weight, convert it to a total price, then ask and wait.
detail: Steps: current and goal weight; weekly loss rate; split into loss and maintenance phases; sum total weeks; multiply by the weekly price; offer a prepay discount; if no, half down with a smaller discount and payments; if no, seesaw downsell asking how low payments should be and raising the amount put down.
numbers: 200 lb to 140 lb at 2 lb/week gives 30 loss weeks + 16 maintenance = 46 weeks; at $200/week about $9,000; prepay discount 20%; half-down discount 10%.

### MZ-DnjGWhXe85I#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: objections-closing | fw: Pre-Paid Post Guarantee | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: For weight loss, sell prepayment only and make the guarantee contingent on prepaying.
detail: Seesaw downsell reduces monthly payments by maximising the amount paid up front; the guarantee is the sweetener for full prepayment.
