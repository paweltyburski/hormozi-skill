### MZ-Z_hOgtlFmss#01 | TACTIC | topic: other | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: To go from $50K to $500K inside a job, work at a company large enough, stay close to revenue, take risk and ask for a share of value created.
detail: Aim for a firm with at least $10M revenue, ideally $20M+, unless you are CEO. Example ask: if you bring in $5M extra, receive 10%. Think of yourself as a vendor.
numbers: $10M minimum revenue (ideally $20M+); 10% of $5M = $500K.
