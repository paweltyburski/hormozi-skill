### MZ-4fCY-znvWyM#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: verticals | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Restaurant software seller gets 30% close but about 1% of buyers show for onboarding or demo calls.
detail: Retention is 86%. Front end is a $99 training-manual offer via Meta ads, with upsells and a post-purchase scheduler. A hired person only chases show-ups. Funnel: 1,000 clicks, 10% opt in, 20% of those buy, about 25% schedule.
numbers: 86% retention; 30% close; ~1% show; $99 offer; 1,000 clicks -> 100 opt-ins -> 20 buys -> 5 schedule.

### MZ-4fCY-znvWyM#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:01
claim: Advice to this caller: a show rate near 1% among paying customers usually means something is disjointed, such as wrong targeting, an offer mismatch, incorrect tracking or a missing integration; he doesn't buy that restaurateurs are too busy.
detail: Check whether the buyers are the decision makers and whether they see the scheduler and get reminders. Match what the scheduling page promises to what they bought. He says everybody is busy, so he rejects 'too busy' as the explanation.
numbers: He has never seen anyone close to a 1% show rate among customers.
### MZ-4fCY-znvWyM#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:35
claim: Make the scheduled call promise match what they just bought, then introduce the software on the call.
detail: A mismatch between a training-book purchase and a software demo invite lowers attendance. Frame the call as showing how to get the most from the purchase.
