### POD-0050#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Money goes to whoever pays it the most attention, so an owner who never tracks it will not be paid.
detail: Many gym owners cover costs, pay no salary to themselves, live out of the business account and do not even know what the business earns. Money passes from the owner to trainers, suppliers and contractors who spend it on, and only the landlord, who watches money closely, tends to keep it.

### POD-0050#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:35
claim: Put yourself on a salary, budget, and know your per-session revenue, trainer pay and margins.
detail: The arithmetic to know at all times is revenue per session, what each trainer is paid and what margin is left. If these are unknown, he says the owner will not get paid; he says to be on salary at least.
applies_to: Owner-operators of gyms and similar small service businesses.
### POD-0050#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Many owners who stay busy but cannot hold onto money do not know what the business makes and do not pay themselves.
detail: The tell is revenue that looks healthy but nothing left at month end, with stress before payroll and a sale or two to get past it. Distraction from personal and staff issues is given as the reason attention never reaches the numbers.
