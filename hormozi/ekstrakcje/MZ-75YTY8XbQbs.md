### MZ-75YTY8XbQbs#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: When an employee asks for equity, unbundle it into four parts and ask which they want: risk, control, profit share, sale value.
detail: Ask how much risk they would take if the company lost money; if none, no control follows. Then offer to solve for profit participation and a share of a sale. Giving real equity is unattractive because the company must be valued and they pay tax on something they did not get cash for, or they must buy it.

### MZ-75YTY8XbQbs#02 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: For an LLC, a profits interest structured as a sale bonus gives employees 0.25% per year up to 1%, with the interest returning to the pool if they leave.
detail: It can be paired with a profit share equal to the same percentage. The employee does not pay up front. Downsides: ordinary income tax at sale rather than capital gains (he cites $37 against $20 per $100), and loss if let go; a smart employee will negotiate a tail so they are not fired before the deal. Sales staff value having equity. US-specific.
numbers: 0.25% per year up to 1%; tax about 37% versus about 20% (as quoted).
applies_to: US LLC with key employees, especially sales
