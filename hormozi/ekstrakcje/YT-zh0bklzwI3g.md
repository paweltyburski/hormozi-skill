### YT-zh0bklzwI3g#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Judge any advisor on two traits: integrity (their intentions and your best interest) and efficacy (skill); with only one, take trust.
detail: Mom has intention but not skill; a brilliant specialist knows how to exploit you. Most cases in practice are skilled but misaligned. He would take someone he trusts at 8 out of 10 skill over someone at 10 out of 10 whose intentions are uncertain.
numbers: 8/10 skill with trusted intent preferred to 10/10 with unsure intent.

### YT-zh0bklzwI3g#02 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Interview as many advisors as possible from reputable sources before deciding, because the spread of opinions gives you the perspective to judge.
detail: Example: talking to ten trainers gives keto, low-fat, balanced and calorie advice; one alone makes the first answer look like truth. Use your network and posts for referrals. Compare by asking one expert what another said and watching the reply. This is qualitative research, and the lazy step most skip, though one decision (a fund, a manager) can shape net worth the most.
numbers: 10-20 interviews suggested; people spend more time choosing a vacation than their advisor.

### YT-zh0bklzwI3g#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:16
claim: A realtor's commission rewards speed, not price: selling at $450,000 instead of $500,000 costs them $2,500, while for you it costs $50,000.
detail: At 5% seller-side commission (7% total, 2% buyer-side) a $500,000 sale pays $25,000, $22,500 at $450,000 and $27,500 at $525,000. A $5,000 swing for them, one possibly taking three months and the other a day, so they maximise money per unit time.
numbers: 7% total commission, 5% seller side; $500,000 = $25,000; $450,000 = $22,500; $525,000 = $27,500.

### YT-zh0bklzwI3g#04 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:33
claim: Align an agent's incentive with a tiered commission that rises steeply above a target price.
detail: His illustration: 0% below $400,000, 3% from $400,000 to $450,000, and 15-20% above $450,000, so each extra dollar to you is worth almost as much to them.
numbers: 0% / 3% / 15-20% tiers around a $500,000 house.

### YT-zh0bklzwI3g#05 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:26
claim: To test a specialist's skill when you lack expertise, read two or three books first, bring in a trusted person, and compare many experts.
detail: Arriving with some knowledge keeps you from looking easy to take advantage of and lets you follow what they say.
numbers: 2-3 books.
