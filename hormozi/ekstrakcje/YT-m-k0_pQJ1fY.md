### YT-m-k0_pQJ1fY#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Resenting wealth blocks it; money is a neutral tool of voluntary exchange where both sides say thank you.
detail: Poor parents often teach that money is scarce and rich people are greedy, while wealthy parents teach that creating value earns a reward. To grow your own economy, create as many positive-sum exchanges as possible. A tool multiplies the character of whoever holds it.

### YT-m-k0_pQJ1fY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Passive income is a continuum, and the richest people have far more active than passive income.
detail: Passive for you is active for someone else. Most wealthy people first built a large active income, then turned part of it passive. Every high earner he knows tried the beach lifestyle and came back. His advice: maximise active earning hours before chasing passive income, until passive covers your lifestyle.
