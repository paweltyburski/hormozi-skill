### MZ-xeDmJRUK-bA#01 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:30
claim: Take calls with prospects even at low prices to learn their language, concerns and benefits, then rewrite the page with their words.
detail: A $15/month community that added a calendar link probably converted better. Also show proof of transformations, what it looks like inside and results next to each feature, using a screenshot of a training and its feedback if testimonials are missing.
numbers: $15/month price in example.

### MZ-xeDmJRUK-bA#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:24
claim: Ask members two questions: which one feature they would keep if all others were removed, and which one they would not miss if all others stayed.
detail: After about 100 answers the votes show what members value. Repeat regularly to tighten to a stack of three or four things people cannot live without, which also lowers delivery effort.
numbers: ~100 answers per round.

### MZ-xeDmJRUK-bA#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:35
claim: The top reason communities lose members is overwhelm; in value-equation terms, perceived likelihood falls when buyers know they will not consume it all.
detail: At a price point of $99-$1,500 a month, a single consumed nugget buys another two or three months. Aim to give the fewest things that deliver the result.
numbers: $99/month to $1,500/month range discussed; one nugget buys 2-3 extra months.

### MZ-xeDmJRUK-bA#04 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:14
claim: Do not post recordings of daily calls as content; use the call as live support, for example greeting every new member each morning.
detail: Nobody will watch hundreds of recordings and newcomers feel swamped. His own community runs one call a week plus a monthly one-day mastermind drop.
numbers: 1 call/week; 1 mega drop/month.
