### POD-0942#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: paid-ads | also: unit-economics | fw: Track Lose Print | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A campaign test needs a budget of about twice the target CAC, otherwise it teaches nothing.
detail: Feedback is too slow below that level, and the first attempt rarely hits target, so you must be willing to overspend CAC while you make the channel efficient.
numbers: Target CAC $2,000 means spend at least $4,000; at $2,000 you get roughly one inefficient sale every two months.

### POD-0942#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:23
claim: A CRM and automation vendor to online fitness coaches fixed retention first and now needs a scalable channel and messaging.
detail: Customers come from partner mentors, organic and, last, Meta ads. The pain solved: turning followers into customers without DMing. The owner's worry is messaging, since they are not a mentorship brand.
numbers: Revenue about $2M, goal $1M a month; monthly churn 7% (as stated); LTV about $9,000; LTV:CAC about 4.6:1, CAC around $1,500-$2,000.

### POD-0942#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:49
claim: To answer "will it work for my niche", show five case studies from five different avatars, then VSL, call and cash up front.
detail: Pick distinct customer types (body type, diet, background, life stage) so each prospect sees someone like themselves.

### POD-0942#04 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:00
claim: If selling straight to small businesses is hard and you do not want to be a guru, sell your platform to agencies that serve them.
detail: In an earlier identical business, direct marketing to small businesses failed; agencies handed him the work they disliked and stayed. The cost is a two-sided operation: customer success for the agencies and for their end clients.

### POD-0942#05 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:36
claim: A $200M new-home builder limited by developed land should vertically integrate by buying a land developer.
detail: Raw land is easy to find, but turning it into finished lots takes years and idle cash is unwanted, so it buys from developers. First step: list developers it bought finished land from over the past 10 years and visit them; some are older with good teams. Feasibility depends on how relationship-dependent zoning is.
numbers: Revenue $200M, target $300M; raw land to finished lot takes 2-10 years.

### POD-0942#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:16
claim: At large scale a company becomes a business of businesses, and the constraint is the use of resources with the highest return.
detail: Like Amazon's separate units, a plateaued company should own its limiting input if that is the highest-return place for capital.
