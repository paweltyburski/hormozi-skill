### POD-0764#01 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Credit-community brothers earned 90% of revenue from a 200,000-follower Instagram, not their 800,000-follower TikTok: about 36 times the return per platform.
detail: Followers differ by 4x and revenue split is 90:10, which multiplies to 36 to 1 return on effort. Hence pick the platform by return, not audience size.
numbers: TikTok 800,000 followers; Instagram 200,000; 90% of revenue from Instagram; 4 x 9 = 36:1.

detail: One platform makes it easier to learn and consume it. Newer members are encouraged to comment first.

### POD-0764#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: outreach | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Kirby (not Hormozi) describes an organic adaptation of the Rule of 100: 100 minutes creating content, 100 minutes of outreach to people who engaged, and 100 comments.
detail: Kirby says it comes from patterns among people making $10,000 to $1M a month in Skool. Outreach goes to those who commented, liked or followed. Called the rule of 300 when paid ads are not part.
numbers: Per Kirby: 100 minutes content/day; 100 minutes outreach/day; 100 comments/day.
### POD-0764#04 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:47
claim: Comments beat posts for reach because they often rise to the top of the feed and the bar for the best comment is lower than for the best post.
detail: A strong comment mixes organic content and outreach, piggybacking on someone else's good post. Prefer comments inside communities with your avatar; there is less ranking competition. Outreach to engagers is warmer, converts better and risks account shutdown less.

### POD-0764#05 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:37
claim: Publish the 100 minutes of content every day, then engage with everyone who responded.
detail: The content time is for scripting and research, not editing: "an ounce of pre is worth a pound of post". It means roughly 90 minutes on one good piece, not 100 minutes of finished material.

### POD-0764#06 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:37
claim: In large communities, provide value first, become a top contributor, then approach the owner to build a partnership.
detail: Owners value helpful contributors; those owners sometimes have more lead flow than they can handle, which opens work for growth operators. Do not pitch up front.

### POD-0764#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: outreach | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Engagement compounds so outbound messages can come entirely from likes and comments within 7 to 30 days.
detail: Content keeps running for a week or more and people who visit a profile like several posts. Each day there are new engagers plus those from earlier days. Check notifications for every liker.
