### MZ-8zCv0qHqm0g#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:44
claim: Have assistants book meetings back to front from the end of the day so the longest uninterrupted block stays free.
detail: Blocking mornings alone left meetings starting at 1pm and finishing late. Filling backwards from 4-5pm leaves a large block. It took about ten years to find.
numbers: Meetings slotted 4-5, then 3:30-4, then 3-3:30.
