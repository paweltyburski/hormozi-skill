### MZ-XPjUSHYjUOc#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:48
claim: A Dutch medical practice that can take only 10-20% more leads is capacity constrained because it cannot hire podiatrists, so the AI question is really a strategy question.
detail: Rules of prioritisation still apply: find what is constraining the business. After years, recruiters paid a few thousand dollars each failed; offer was 30-40% higher pay, no relocation bonus, no interviews obtained. Hormozi did not accept that no one exists.
numbers: Capacity headroom 10-20%; pay offered 30-40% above market; recruiter fees "a couple of thousand" each.

### MZ-XPjUSHYjUOc#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:21
claim: If one thing limits the business, hit it with overwhelming effort on many fronts, not 10%.
detail: Fronts: LinkedIn and Indeed ads, direct outreach, relocation bonus, graduate and internship pipelines, foreign-trained professionals' relocation boards. He asks "are you trying or moving mountains", and notes bigger competitors exist, so it is possible.

### MZ-XPjUSHYjUOc#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:57
claim: After asking himself why capacity was short, he and two colleagues spent eight weeks of free time to fill six C-suite roles, almost none of whom knew his brand.
detail: He applied the same chain of questions to his own firm: why can't we do more, no operational capacity, which functions, who needed.
numbers: 6 C-suite roles in 8 weeks.

### MZ-XPjUSHYjUOc#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:23

numbers: 4-8 hours per block; webpage copy took ~3 hours.
