claim: A UAE business with about $4M tax-free profit is stuck because staff need an office and visa, forcing a large lease.
detail: Owner says revenue is about $6M. To hire, he must take an office for 40 people on a four-year contract, around $350,000 a year, and government approval is pending. Hormozi walks the checks first (market, funnel metrics, margins) and finds manpower is the only constraint.

### MZ-UmBWqjwfgtA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:07
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: a cost difference worth only a couple of days of income is not worth worrying about in negotiation.
detail: With about $500,000 earned per month, he says that if office rent is negotiated to $300,000 a year and the saving is about 50 (he says "pounds"), that is about two and a half days of work, so even a month of extra negotiating doesn't matter. He also told this caller to take 10% of profit and reinvest it in growth.
numbers: About $500,000 per month; saving equals about 2.5 days (his figure).
