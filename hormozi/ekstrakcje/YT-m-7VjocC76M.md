### YT-m-7VjocC76M#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Everything sold is a product, a service or access, each physical or digital, and mixing categories avoids commodity status.
detail: Physical product: supplements by mail. Digital product: a subscription like Netflix or media. Physical service: a massage. Digital service: a marketing agency. Physical access: concert tickets. Digital access: a virtual event or direct contact with an expert on Slack. Ask how to add a component from another category, for example a service to a product, so the business stands alone and can hold premium prices.

### YT-m-7VjocC76M#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:50
claim: Digital access and digital products run at near 100% margin; services are hard to scale and less attractive to buyers.
detail: Scaling services means scaling people, culture, training and onboarding, and anything with hard costs scales slowly. To raise enterprise value, add more than one of the six types to the business.
numbers: Digital access and digital products: about 100% gross margin.

### YT-m-7VjocC76M#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: offers | fw: Conditional Guarantee | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:05:00
claim: To raise cash fast, sell a higher-touch version of your own solution as limited access, paid in full up front.
detail: Offer a few seats (one-to-one or small group) for a year with an "I keep working free until you get the outcome" promise. Because the buyer gets access plus expertise, no discount is needed for prepaying the whole period. He says it can bring in $5,000 to $50,000 depending on business size.
numbers: Example: 5 seats, 12 months; cash raised $5,000-$50,000.
applies_to: Service businesses with an existing client base

### YT-m-7VjocC76M#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: money-models | fw: Scarcity | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:22
claim: Sell only a slice of the demand you could fill, keeping about 80% pent up for the next round.
detail: If you could sell 20, sell 5; if 100, sell 20. Selling out everything burns goodwill and makes the next round bomb. Growing the base from 100 to 200 lets you raise the next round from 20 to 40 slots, a controlled burn of goodwill.
numbers: Leave ~80% of demand unsold; 20 to 40 slots as base doubles.

### YT-m-7VjocC76M#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:03
claim: Very high-priced access offers attract the least needy customers and give strong testimonials and outcomes.
detail: The few who take the premium slots are often your best clients, so the offer also feeds proof for later sales.
