### MZ-DZbEt6BBZYQ#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals, lead-getters | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A UK optometry consultancy relies on lens manufacturers who pay its fee, and Hormozi says it is wildly undercharging.
detail: Clients buy practices and run out of money. Manufacturers send customers because the firm raises their sales. Clients later pay themselves. Firm gives a diagnostic tool and a guide, clients don't comply; charging helps. Demand also comes from the US and Canada.
numbers: Revenue just under $1M; EBITDA about 58%; average spend 7,000 pounds a year; lens suppliers' sales up 24%; about $50,000 extra bottom line per optometrist in year one.

### MZ-DZbEt6BBZYQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: money-models | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:27
claim: Charge $35-45k up front to fix the whole business, then $10-15k a year for ongoing supplier discounts.
detail: Optometrists get credit easily and are used to spending. Start by charging about $15,000 to the manufacturer and a client fee too (both sides pay), since it adds about $50k.
numbers: Upfront $35,000-$45,000; continuity $10,000-$15,000 a year; interim suggestion about $15,000.

### MZ-DZbEt6BBZYQ#03 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: verticals, finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:12
claim: Make continuity a buying association: bulk-negotiated discounts on lenses, frames and displays that offset the fee.
detail: If savings match the fee, the services feel free. Long term, roll all members under one flag and sell the group.
