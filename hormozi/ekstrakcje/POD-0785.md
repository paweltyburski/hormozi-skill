### POD-0785#20 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:29:38

detail: Steps: ask if they would do better doing this than not, better with you than alone, and whether they can access the money; three yeses mean it was a logical choice. Then find the fear. ### POD-0785#22 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:32:03

detail: If the sale truly needs the other party, get them on the call. In a one-shot situation: ask "what if they say no?" If the prospect says they would go ahead anyway, start. ### POD-0785#36 | FRAMEWORK | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 01:06:53
claim: Common factors analysis: after about 100 attempts, compare the best against the rest, find what the best share, and replicate it in the next 100.
detail: Steps: do 100 reps (reels, calls, dates); record them in as much detail as possible; separate top from bottom; ask what all the top ones have in common that the others lack; discard coincidences (a cough on one call); run the next 100 with those traits. numbers: 100 reps per cycle; ~30% better total views in his example; roughly 60% of Adele's songs never reached the top 10 (his loose claim, 'or something').
