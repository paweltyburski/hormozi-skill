### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:58
claim: Attention, not time, is the owner's scarcest resource, and the higher you rise the more you must remove drains on it.
detail: Time can be allocated, but without attention nothing gets done in it. The wealthiest people he has observed run quiet, uncluttered spaces and have low tolerance for distraction. What got you from zero to one (doing the work, outworking others) does not get you to two, so identity and traits must change.

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#02 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Owners stuck at $1M-$3M often spread attention over several businesses, which can leave them earning less than with one.
detail: A gym owner with four gyms made less than with one because attention per site fell and fixed costs ate the margin. Typical high-priced coaching clients are at $1M-$3M a year in gross revenue with nothing taken home. Growth requires doing less: delegating and choosing what not to do.
numbers: Typical client revenue $1M-$3M a year; coaching fee mentioned $100,000.
applies_to: Owners with multiple side businesses

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#03 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:16:28
claim: Two owners who closed one to three side businesses roughly tripled revenue within 30 days.
detail: After he told them to consolidate, two of them reached about three times prior total revenue in a month (stated by him). Related: a real-estate coach said there were not enough agents, though she made about $200,000 a month; he replied the agent market outnumbered his by 100 to 1 while his revenue was 20 times hers. Belief in small markets limits owners. Choose one business, e.g. one fat paycheck beats two.
numbers: 3x revenue in 30 days for two owners; coach at about $200,000/month; agents outnumber his market 100:1.

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:35
claim: Put layers of access between yourself and the outside world so no one can split your attention.
detail: His billionaire acquaintance's phone only rings for whitelisted contacts. Hormozi's layers are his wife, the COO, then a team of four executive assistants; no employee has direct access, and he does not answer social-media messages because doing so would harm the clients he serves.
numbers: Four executive assistants.

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#05 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:14
claim: Close open loops, in work and personal relationships, to recover attention.
detail: He paid a coach for nine months to listen to him talk through what was on his mind for 90 minutes each day. It exposed toxic relationships and unresolved matters draining attention (his "marbles" analogy). Quitting evening drinking also surfaced unresolved anxiety; resolving it took about six months, after which the urge faded.
numbers: 90 minutes a day for nine months with a coach.

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:35
claim: Ask an owner: where is your attention? Scattered answers predict stalled growth.
detail: He asks this at the start of every mentoring conversation. A companion filter: define the single number you need (his gym example: members on recurring billing for the lifestyle you want) and ask of each opportunity whether it grows that number; if not, ignore it.

### YT-6ZEZWuVC8-8#07 | EVOLUTION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:18
claim: He used to identify as someone who outworks everyone, and now says that identity must be fluid.
detail: Working harder is what gets a founder from zero to one. After that the needed skills (delegation, leadership) and traits differ, and the better he got, the better the opportunities he had to refuse.
