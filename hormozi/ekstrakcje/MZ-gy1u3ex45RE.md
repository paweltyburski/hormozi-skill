### MZ-gy1u3ex45RE#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A cloud consultant making $1.5M builds custom systems in customers' own clouds, then a managed service.
detail: Builds take 1.5 years of time and material with him as the advisor; no talent pipeline; he wants $5M and to sell managed service on systems he did not build.
numbers: $1.5M revenue; 1.5-year builds.

### MZ-gy1u3ex45RE#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: hiring-talent | fw: Big Head Long Tail | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:32
claim: Ask whether the initial build is constrained because nobody can do it or because you cannot attract them.
detail: Build then managed service looks to him very much like a front-end, back-end business, and the managed service side probably has very high gross margins.
### MZ-gy1u3ex45RE#03 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: Replace the long build with a short upfront patch of an existing cloud.
detail: A paid fix giving roughly a 20% improvement doubles as the assessment needed for the managed service.
numbers: ~20% improvement.
