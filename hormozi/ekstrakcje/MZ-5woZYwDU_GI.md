### MZ-5woZYwDU_GI#01 | CASE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops, verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A TikTok Shop agency owner who flips between marketing and service delivery was told to hire someone who leads people.
detail: He is better at marketing, with an 8-person team and a "people problem". Hormozi asked which he is better at, then prescribed a leader for the other half.
numbers: Revenue about $360K in six months (profit about 75% is unclear in audio).

### MZ-5woZYwDU_GI#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Hire a leader who likes people, not a systems operator; a sign you have the right person is that staff naturally go to that person rather than the owner.
detail: Typically in small businesses, roughly below $3M, maybe $10M, he says people who consider themselves operators and put in systems, technology and process just make everyone's lives harder. The owner then markets while the leader leads.
numbers: Applies to businesses below about $3M, maybe $10M (typically).
### MZ-5woZYwDU_GI#03 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: Internal Core Four (Recruiting Mirror) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:00
claim: List the activities of the role you want to offload and write the ad from that list.
detail: A clear activity list gives clarity, and the right candidate will name things on top that you should be doing.

### MZ-5woZYwDU_GI#04 | TACTIC | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:47
claim: Interview 10 to 20 people for a role you do not know, treating it as free education, and recontact the early ones.
detail: After 20 you have a filter for the 21st and 22nd; the best may have already found jobs, and some will return if you were classy.
numbers: 10 to 20 interviews per hire.

### MZ-5woZYwDU_GI#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Without capital, pay for early talent with upside (shares or profit share), upskill internally, or accept more interviews for lower pay.
detail: Early hires take risk, so cash buys you more upside retained. Options: grind on yourself, upskill someone from the team, or hire a skilled person who accepts less (rarer).
