### POD-0231#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: At least 80% of Gym Launch clients had consumed multiple pieces of content before buying, and only about 20% bought straight from an ad.
detail: From a recent data review of sign-ups. His reading: advertising mostly collects the effect of content, because skeptical buyers check several sources. He made videos and podcasts for about three years without real ROI tracking, but the ROI seems to be definitely there.
numbers: 80%+ multi-content buyers; about 20% direct from ad; 3 years untracked content.
### POD-0231#02 | DEFINITION | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Assets are one-time builds that keep giving value, while inputs are what you must keep feeding the machine.
detail: Car analogy: the car is the asset, driver and gasoline are the inputs. Acquisition planning starts from the beliefs a person must hold to become a customer.

### POD-0231#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: Epiphany Bridge | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Keep a story bank: for each key belief a prospect must hold, store a story that broke that belief for you.
detail: Idea from Dan Kennedy. Technical explanations (macros, workouts) lose people because that is not how they were sold. If a prospect does not resonate with a point, return to the state before your own epiphany and walk through the story. Stories stick and are repeated by trainers and coaches, unlike programme details.

### POD-0231#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:25
claim: Two stories for the belief "accountability works": brushing your teeth as a child, and an Amazing Race couple using local guides.
detail: Teeth: parents enforced the habit until it became internal, which is the aim of coaching. Race: a pair won so often the rules changed because they hired a local in the back seat for shortcuts, so coaching shortens the path to success.

### POD-0231#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:41
claim: Inventory every story on paper with the belief each is meant to break, and practise telling them.
detail: He listed about 300 stories he uses (assume closes, one-time versus recurring pricing, client financing) about two and a half years earlier, and reread Expert Secrets repeatedly to get better at storytelling. Each story follows 14 steps.
numbers: about 300 stories; 14 steps per story; Expert Secrets read roughly 8 times in 3-4 months.
