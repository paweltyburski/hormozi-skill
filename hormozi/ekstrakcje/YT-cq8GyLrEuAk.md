### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Assume you are the least informed person in the room, talk less, and you learn more and decide better.
detail: Early on he bragged at introductions and later found the person was far ahead of him. Listening reveals who someone is, their stage, and whether to follow up. Better still when a third party praises you after you leave.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:28
claim: Earn your own respect first; reputation follows changed behavior, not narrative control.
detail: His father's answer to "how do I change my reputation" was to stop behaving badly. Nothing changed externally at first, then reputation slowly followed.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:25
claim: To control what people say about your business, hand them simple words to describe it.
detail: Otherwise customers must invent a description in half a second while telling a friend. He rephrased his mission from documenting best practices of building world-class companies to making real business education available to everyone.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:22
claim: Read few books deeply: re-read the great ones until you can teach them, and judge a book by whether your behavior changed.
detail: He skims most books and drops them. For keepers he reads up to five times, builds a presentation and his own frameworks to remember it. Older books often beat new ones because the author wrote to pass on knowledge, not to sell.
numbers: Re-read 2-5 times until able to teach it.

detail: With equal hours, those ahead cut more: they say no to distractions, and even tolerate fewer people below their bar. Saying yes to everything earlier left him spread thin.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:02

detail: Goodwill is positive sentiment plus influence over behavior; shared from person to person it can grow several times over in about 12 weeks, which money rarely does. It can be turned into money later, at a time you choose.
numbers: 2x to 10x goodwill in about 12 weeks (his claim); 10x money in 12 weeks is very unlikely.

detail: Test: what problem does it solve and is it worth the time needed to do it right? He earns more now by doing fewer, different things. A book takes him a year because it can sell for decades.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#11 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:10

detail: Negotiation is not zero-sum. Know your own wants and what you will give up, draw out theirs. This concerns deals and vendors, not discounting your own price to customers.

### YT-cq8GyLrEuAk#12 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:13

detail: For fair deals he balances four points: his needs today and in ten years, theirs today and in ten years, and explains this to the other side.
numbers: About 10% extra paid to keep a vendor relationship was cheaper than the lost loyalty.
