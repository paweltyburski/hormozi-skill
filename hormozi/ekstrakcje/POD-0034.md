### POD-0034#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:51
claim: If staff do not care about customers, check first how you talk about and treat them.
detail: He once preached service while hiding in the back office when customers came in and being curt with them. The team copied what he did, not what he said. His own support staff became devoted because he changed it. Scaling service rests on training, which can be automated, and culture, which starts at the top.

### POD-0034#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:47
claim: Tell your origin story in team meetings so staff remember why the work matters.
detail: He did it in a company culture meeting and got emotional about hardships: sleeping on a floor for nine months, living in his car, a partner emptying his bank account. In weekly workshop meetings, spend a moment on why you started and why customers matter. Conviction in how you speak sells staff on their importance.
numbers: Nine straight months sleeping on a floor.

### POD-0034#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:34
claim: Don't talk about customers' ignorance as if they were stupid in front of staff; when a trainer laughs at it, address it.
detail: Jokes such as a member asking whether beans are carbs teach staff to look down on customers. He applied the same to his own support team about gym owners who cannot set up a tracking pixel or landing page: they pay because they do not know. Own it as the reason for team performance.
### POD-0034#04 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:45
claim: Lead by small visible acts; set your own standard at two to five times what you want from staff.
detail: A pastor of a large church picks up pebbles on the sidewalk and volunteers start doing the same unasked. An owner mopping the floor now and then shows what matters. Staff will follow at roughly half your level. He claims highly effective teams out-produce mediocre ones several fold (ratio unclear in audio, 3 to 10 times).
numbers: Productivity gap about 3 to 10 times (unclear in audio).
