### MZ-xKA-sxrumEA#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A beauty supplier to stylists and barbers plans a third pillar of its own service locations to funnel customers to its products.
detail: The business has distribution, education workshops and now services. Hormozi says it would be a different business and advises against it.
numbers: $400K revenue, goal about $1M next year; planned locations ~$500,000 each versus ~$50 products.

### MZ-xKA-sxrumEA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Do not open expensive brick-and-mortar sites to sell cheap products; plug into existing salons and teach them.
detail: Sell education that leads stylists to sell your products. A single venue is fine as a media location for cameras and in-person experiences, not as a running salon.
numbers: ~150,000 existing salons.

### MZ-xKA-sxrumEA#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Keep the main thing the main thing: make every detail perfect, but keep very few details to execute.
detail: He attributes the idea to Jack Dorsey.
