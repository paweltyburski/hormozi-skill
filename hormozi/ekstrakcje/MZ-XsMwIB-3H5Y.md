### MZ-XsMwIB-3H5Y#01 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: offers | fw: 80/20 Customer Analysis | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Find the fewest common traits among the most valuable customers, such as revenue over $1M, at least three employees, 20 or more customers and organic-driven.
detail: The other half of customers churn, create reputation problems and burn out the team.
numbers: example traits: >$1M/year, 3+ employees, 20+ customers.

### MZ-XsMwIB-3H5Y#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Price to Value Discrepancy | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: A blended customer base forces a price pitched at the weakest buyers; the right avatar gets two to five times more value, supporting a higher price despite fewer customers.
detail: With half the buyers unhappy, price sits low; selling only to the best allows up to an amount equal to 10x value.
numbers: 2-5x more value to the right avatar.
