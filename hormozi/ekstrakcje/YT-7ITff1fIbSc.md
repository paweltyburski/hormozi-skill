### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His podcast took about six years of weekly episodes to reach the top 10 in entrepreneurship, and the first 150 episodes were just about running a gym better.
detail: Started in 2017, one to three episodes every week without missing; the first year is for finding your voice. Around episode 150 the topics broadened to marketing, then sales, then business in general and M&A, with his delivery improving in parallel.
numbers: About 6 years; 1-3 episodes/week; 400 episodes by the time of this video.

### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: Many-Sided Die | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Make the action the goal, not the outcome: if you made the post and did the reach-outs, you succeeded that day.
detail: Time passes anyway, so think in decades. Divorcing activity from result keeps self-esteem steady while income moves.

### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#03 | CASE | topic: operator-mindset | also: content-brand | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:01:33
claim: A friend with a gym chain posted short videos daily for six months, then videos took off and he reached $100,000 a month from content, but kept stopping once sales arrived.
detail: Each time money came in he slowed down, then panicked at the empty pipeline and restarted. Hormozi's diagnosis: he tracked income instead of process, so he lacked consistency; the fix was to keep doing the activity whether results were good or not yet there.
numbers: 6 months of daily posts; then about $100,000/month; he had already run businesses for 9 years.

### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:16
claim: Winners and losers share the same goals; the winner commits to the activities, the loser to the goal.
detail: Cited from Atomic Habits. He quotes a survey in which about half of high-school seniors expect to be millionaires by 25, as an illustration that goals do not differentiate. Focus on what produces the million.
numbers: 52% of 18-year-old seniors expect to be millionaires by 25 (as stated).

### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: content-brand | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:01
claim: He told his YouTube vendor on the first call he was in for a decade and would review progress at five to ten years; the vendor said no client had ever said that.
detail: Others asked for leads within 90 days. Committing to the process leaves him feeling as good at year one as at year eight and lowers emotional swings.
numbers: Commitment horizon: 5-10 years.

### YT-7ITff1fIbSc#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:27

detail: Weekly output of 400 pieces equals about eight years; he was at roughly year six.
numbers: 400 episodes = about 8 years at 1/week.
