### MZ-Z7xWshH-WkE#01 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Burnout is the reward cycle failing: the reward becomes too delayed, too weak, habituated or stops, so behaviour goes through extinction.
detail: What once felt good no longer does after a hundred repetitions. It is harder to leave when competing contingencies exist, such as family reliance, where pay still reinforces while other rewards vanish.

### MZ-Z7xWshH-WkE#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Punishment and reward both habituate, so vary type and intensity to keep them effective.
detail: Reinforcement means anything that makes a behaviour occur more.

### MZ-Z7xWshH-WkE#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:51
claim: To change someone's behaviour, give them a label they can live up to and align actions with it, because the identity itself becomes reinforcing.
detail: Example: seeing yourself as a good provider is rewarding and keeps you working even when the job is dull.
