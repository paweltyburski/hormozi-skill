### MM-09#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Building money models is a complex skill because it rests on pattern recognition, which needs a large sample of cases — the reason people apprentice with experts.
detail: Like evaluating deals or surgical cases, you only learn what to look for after seeing many examples; the better you get, the better a student you become. See also MM-01 (00:02:20) on apprenticeship at scale.

### MM-09#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: Document Demonstrate Duplicate | novelty: canon | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Teaching has three steps: the teacher documents and demonstrates, and the learner duplicates.
detail: In one-to-many training the teacher can only do the first two, so duplication is on the learner. Hormozi tells the stories of how he learned each model to compress over a decade of experience into about a day.

### MM-09#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: offers, leads-fundamentals | fw: Money Model | novelty: canon | ts: 00:01:39
claim: From viewer messages he concluded that people grasp offers and leads but not how to sequence offers into profit that funds more advertising — the gap this course fills.
detail: A tactical video of three money models drew messages from viewers who said it made them $50K, $100K and even $1M, revealing that this connection was the missing piece.
numbers: Viewer-reported results $50K, $100K, $1M from one video.

### MM-09#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Money Model | novelty: canon | ts: 00:02:26
claim: Keep a journal of money models you spot in the wild, to pull out later like proven plays.
detail: Hormozi's book consolidates years of scattered notes, each a "scratch-off winner" already proven to work. Each model in the course comes with a visual, description, examples, key points and a summary. When an objection says "this won't work for my business", ask instead "how could I make this work?" — he claims every business can use any of the models.

### MM-09#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: money-models | also: - | fw: - | novelty: canon | ts: 00:03:58
claim: Advertising and pricing law varies by city, state, country and time, so check compliance for every model, and when in doubt give the money back.
detail: Regulators exist solely to punish deceptive practices. He calls the models hypothetical ways of thinking, and says that to his knowledge they were allowed in his state when he recorded. Refunding is less effort than repairing a reputation — getting another customer is easier.
