### YT-s9fSDCRPQNA#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:37
claim: With no money you have no leverage, so you wear every hat and must repeat the first-dollar action until you have $100,000.
detail: After that you can pay for outbound help, advertising and video, and rent stops being a worry.

### YT-s9fSDCRPQNA#03 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:48
claim: Hand off tasks in reverse order of their market value, and replace the time you buy back with higher-value work.
detail: Example: admin such as scheduling and phones can be bought for about $10 an hour, recovering four hours a day. Outsourcing then stopping work keeps income flat; to grow, invest the freed time in higher-leverage work.
numbers: Admin at $10 per hour; 4 hours per day recovered.

### YT-s9fSDCRPQNA#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:06
