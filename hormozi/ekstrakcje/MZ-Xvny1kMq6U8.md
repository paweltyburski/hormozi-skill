### MZ-Xvny1kMq6U8#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: sales-team, verticals | fw: Rollover Upsell | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A trainer at $60K revenue and $20K profit loses clients at the end; fix: one central salesperson handling ascensions halfway through.
detail: People do not re-sign when finished. At the midpoint offer a rollover upsell: credit the original payment toward either six months for another $2,000 or twelve months for another $4,000, as an either-or choice today. If they refuse, downsell to continuity.
numbers: $60K revenue; $20K profit; original $2,000; +$2,000 for 6 months or +$4,000 for 12 months.

### MZ-Xvny1kMq6U8#02 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: retention-ltv | fw: Five Upsell Moments | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:43
claim: Midpoint call positioning is "let's keep the party going": the owner checks how it's going, then notes goals take a lifestyle change and credits everything paid.
detail: Order: owner call for experience, confirm progress, ask whether reaching the goal means finished forever, then offer two options with full credit. If staff hate selling, coach them with a commission on the contract or bring in a salesperson; short term the owner may take the calls.
