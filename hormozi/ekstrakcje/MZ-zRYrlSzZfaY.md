### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#01 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Ask "is this a missed opportunity or a problem to be solved?" because only the problem exists in reality.
detail: A missed opportunity only exists in your mind, so you do not have to think about it now; the problem exists in reality and is what you need to solve. Chasing a new opportunity while something is weak leaves you more problems and less bandwidth. His wife asks him this question.
### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:09
claim: Feeling that everything is urgent is a prioritisation failure; triage and let some fires burn.
detail: Pilot landing on one engine does not fix a flickering cabin light while the other engine burns; an emergency room sends the broken ankle to wait and the heart attack straight in. Accept the business will never be perfect.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:54
claim: Strategy is curation then prioritisation: see all possible options first, then rank them.
detail: Most founders look only at what is in the refrigerator (six options) instead of deciding what they want and surveying everything available. He thinks this is where the 100x differences between entrepreneurs come from, because of unconstrained thinking.
### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#04 | TACTIC | topic: operator-mindset | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:40
claim: Keep a plan B that levels up with each skill you learn, so your worst case stays acceptable and big bets become safe.
detail: His first plan B after spending savings on a gym was to drive Uber or strip. After learning sales it was selling cars; after marketing, he could earn more by generating and selling leads himself. He advises against quitting a stable job with no savings, skills or business experience.
numbers: Plan B incomes: about $150,000 a year (Uber and stripping); $200,000-$400,000 a year selling cars; about $1M a year after learning marketing.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#05 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:06:19
claim: Red flag test: does this person offer solutions to my problems, or problems to my solutions?
detail: Someone with a new problem for every proposal makes each interaction worse. Rule: if the idea is the "best bad idea" we have, we go with it unless someone brings a better one. Show your own idea in the arena.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:10
claim: A brand does not need to go viral; educators who show they solve a specific problem sell, while entertainers only sell attention.
detail: Most businesses need educational content that leads to products. One-hit clips often bring few followers. Happy customers talking about you is a slower route to virality.
numbers: His top video has about 7-8 million views; he has made about 100,000 pieces of content.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#07 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:08:20
claim: Get quality volume by capturing work you already do in a structured way, not by recording everything.
detail: Internal name "qual-ium". Choreograph the routine into a repeatable before, during, after sequence. Example: a dentist seeing about 50 patients a day films greeting, the work and the walk-out to show the change.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#08 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:37
claim: Reach content volume by decentralising to a commissioned affiliate and UGC army, or by AI-enhancing internal production.
detail: Pay creators commission to post organic reviews; many creators posting a few times a week add up. Downside is less control. Brand should lean on live, interactive, demonstration content that competitors and AI cannot easily clone.
numbers: Example: 1,000 creators x 5 posts a week = 5,000 posts a week.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:11:47
claim: AI is least able to replace content that has real stakes; non-stakes, non-IRL content is most at risk.
detail: Chess grew despite engines because people care about real drama; a cloned video of 10 fake people on an island with fake money would draw no interest. Live streams, concerts and stand-up remain safe.

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#10 | PRINCIPLE | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:12:55

### MZ-zRYrlSzZfaY#13 | BENCHMARK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:16:15
