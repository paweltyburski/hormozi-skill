### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: A free virtual book-launch event was promoted mainly by affiliates with no cash payouts, and two emails to his list produced about 9,000 registrations.
detail: Method: an affiliate opt-in (name, email, phone) that returns a unique tracked link. Prizes only: the top 10 affiliates get a live Q&A with their own audience, and anyone who brings at least 10 attendees receives two bonus chapters held back from the book. Most registrations came from his own list becoming affiliates, not his social posts.
numbers: About 9,000 registrations from 2 emails; top affiliates each sent about 1,000 people.

claim: With affiliates you serve two customers, the affiliate and the end buyer, and you make two offers: yours to the affiliate and theirs to their customers.
detail: This is the main added complexity, and the affiliate's standards for how you treat their audience tend to be higher than your own. Having affiliates forced him to tighten his messaging.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#05 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:59
claim: Use two affiliate tiers: scarce, status-based prizes for competitive super affiliates and a tiny hurdle prize everyone can win.
detail: Super affiliates (big audiences) want status, so the top-10 prize was a live session with him, which is scarce because he rarely speaks to other audiences. For small-list people, an easy threshold of 10 attendees earns bonus material, so even someone with 100 contacts feels the effort is worthwhile.
numbers: Top 10 win the live Q&A; threshold 10 attendees for the bonus chapters.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:56
claim: Align your incentive, the affiliate's and the audience's: frame the prize as a benefit to the affiliate's own audience.
detail: Asking an audience to help the affiliate win a contest takes goodwill from them. Offering the audience a Q&A costs the affiliate nothing and builds goodwill. Paying the affiliate aligns only two parties.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:27
claim: Affiliates will promote for free only if you have a record of over-delivering; he emailed his list just three or four times in a year while publishing about 1,000 pieces of content a month.
detail: Because each ask was rare and worth the audience's time, the promise to deliver carries. A friend recommending shoes is a micro-affiliate paid in relational capital.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#08 | TACTIC | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:49
claim: Whisper starts six weeks or more out, tease runs from six weeks to seven days out, and shout is only the last seven days.
detail: Whisper: build anticipation without describing contents (like a teaser that only shows a date). Tease: show a page, read a paragraph, give a glimpse of value. Shout: reminders and direct asks. Common mistake: shouting for six weeks so people tire of it. The frequency curve is intermittent, then more frequent, then a crescendo.
numbers: Whisper 6+ weeks out; tease 6 weeks to 7 days; shout last 7 days.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#09 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:02
claim: Showing how long you have worked on something raises perceived quality, and effectiveness of the shout depends on value delivered before it.
detail: Two years on a 10-minute video seems better than two hours. Those who skip the value phase try to fake it with extra output and look disjointed, since they have no goodwill to draw from.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: content-brand | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:11
claim: During a promotion keep regular content volume unchanged and add the promotion on top, and make the promotion itself valuable.
detail: His brand director's discussion concluded not to reduce normal value while asking for more. New viewers arrive from promoters with no goodwill, so quality must rise, not fall. Trailers are the model: valuable enough that people share them.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#11 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:24
claim: Add sign-up friction (tax forms, qualification) when selling B2B services and strip it when selling a cheap mass-market product.
detail: Paid affiliates fill forms and are more committed; for services you want only the right referrers. A book delivered without his time can accept volume over quality. Rule: easy sign-up for volume, harder for quality.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#12 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:23
claim: Small page details change affiliate participation: show the custom link on the thank-you page and also email it, and test the banner.
detail: Sign-up asked only first name, last name, email and phone and took about 30 seconds, applying the value equation to every step (short time delay, low effort). Emailing the link and showing it on the page lifted promoters by about 25%; a new banner on the form lifted sign-ups by 60%.
numbers: Link on page plus email about +25% promoters; banner change +60% sign-ups; sign-up time 30 seconds.

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#13 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:18:43

### YT-60_7PU9JDIw#14 | DECISION_RULE | topic: promotions-launches | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Give:Ask Ratio | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:19:30
