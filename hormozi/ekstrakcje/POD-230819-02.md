### POD-230819-02#01 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:41
claim: In March 2017 two gym launches were hit by $150,000 in refunds after gym owners told new members to refund; the company had to find $150,000 of profit in 30 days.
detail: One owner told members to refund and go home to avoid the workload; another offered members half price if they refunded with Hormozi's company and paid him. The agreement did not allow Hormozi to contact them, and they ignored calls. The refunds wiped profit and the team could not sell its way out because more sales meant more refunds.
numbers: Refunds about $150,000 after two gym launches in one month.

### POD-230819-02#02 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:21
claim: The first version of the gym-launch business was a free fill-your-gym offer paid by keeping the first six weeks of new members' fees.
detail: He sold leads into whatever gym he was camped at that month, working it with his own lead machine. Referrals would have booked him for over two years without hiring help, so he sold his own gyms. Problem he saw: gym owners kept all long-term profit; he tried part-ownership (50-50) but his first partner took his money.
numbers: Payment model: first six weeks of membership fees; demand backlog of more than two years.

### POD-230819-02#03 | CASE | topic: offers | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:13:58
claim: To cover the refunds, Hormozi first tested selling online fitness coaching by phone, with leads from ads, before an unplanned sale of the gym launch system.
detail: His partner Layla already coached online clients at $300 a month with a few hours per week and no overhead. He built an offer in 38 hours; the first call closed a $499 sale; within days it reached $1,000 per day, cash upfront and few refunds. His plan: eight reps selling the same would make $8,000 a day, about $240,000 in 30 days.
numbers: Existing coaching $3,600/month at $300 each; $499 first sale; $1,000/day; plan of $8,000/day with eight reps for $240,000 in 30 days.

### POD-230819-02#04 | CASE | topic: verticals | also: pricing | fw: Money Model | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:16:36
claim: A desperate gym owner asked to be shown how to get leads; Hormozi quoted $6,000 to scare him off, he agreed, and the licence offer was born.
detail: He was cancelling launches and telling gyms to look elsewhere. Boise gym owner said he could close but needed leads. Hormozi packaged his entire lead and sales system in 48 hours for Monday. numbers: Prices $6,000, $8,000, $10,000; eight gyms; about $60,000 collected in one day, a third of the $150,000 hole.
