### MZ-dBljR5Iiqno#01 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:13
claim: Guilt means breaking your own rules; shame means breaking other people's rules.
detail: Shame is a signal of whose rulebook you follow. It has a social purpose: group enforcement of pro-social behaviour, rooted in the old risk of being cast out. Today the stakes are lower, but it feels the same.

### MZ-dBljR5Iiqno#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:10
claim: Outsized outcomes need willingness to do what most people disagree with, so shame does not pay in innovation.
detail: Successful founders often have weaker social feedback loops and think from first principles. Asking ten people for agreement leads to staying normal and doing nothing. A quoted rule from Larry Ellison: if people disagree, either they are right (most of the time) or you are right and a business built on it pays hugely.

### MZ-dBljR5Iiqno#03 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:42
claim: Find opportunities by comparing demand with supply: look where many more people want something than suppliers serve.
detail: Analogy: betting on a 16th seed you judge far better than its seeding. Example: an aging population holds the wealth, yet new businesses target the young, so insurance and care for older people look underserved; peptides and med spas still have more demand than supply.

### MZ-dBljR5Iiqno#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:54
claim: Ninety-nine percent of businesses are existing business types, so you do not need an invention; truly net-new ideas usually need large up-front capital.
detail: In a college entrepreneurship class almost every plan was an invention, while the real economy is lawn mowing, laundry, cars and insurance. Doing the same thing better can pay a lot.
numbers: Roughly 99% of businesses are not inventions (his estimate).

### MZ-dBljR5Iiqno#05 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:21
claim: Pick a starting business by scanning your own credit card statement as a customer, and start small with cheap equipment.
detail: The first business need not be the one for life. Marketing, selling, delivering, hiring, firing and training learned in a small local business transfer to better opportunities later.
