### POD-0859#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:07:31
claim: A creator has five ways to monetise, arranged on an axis of control: sponsorship, affiliate or performance deal, equity partnership, white label, and a product built from scratch.
detail: Control rises towards the right, and he says the further right, the more enterprise value. Partnership means contributing work, brand and capital to an existing company; white label means putting your name on another firm's product; the last is creating the product or service yourself. There is a right path for each person; the skill is knowing which.
### POD-0859#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:52
claim: A seller of attention should take performance deals because the seller knows better than any buyer what the attention is worth.
detail: Asking for a larger share of the upside shifts risk from the sponsor to the creator; the more risk taken, the bigger the share. The seller always holds the information advantage, as in selling a business. Only if the audience will not convert does a flat sponsorship fee make more sense.

### POD-0859#10 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:26:41
claim: The consultant's method for learning an unfamiliar field fast is a chain of expert referrals.
detail: Ask the client for the five smartest people on the problem, interview them, ask each for five more, and repeat until the same names return. ### POD-0859#12 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:28:34
claim: Three ways to build a business: copy what already works; hold an advantage in either acquiring customers cheaper or delivering better; or hold one advantage so strong it offsets weakness in the other.
detail: He thinks copying is a wildly underestimated path: to "what business do I start" his answer is "look around, pick one". With an advantage in either customer acquisition or delivery you need little in the other. A true product-driven business could be poor at acquisition.

### POD-0859#15 | CASE | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:33:55

### POD-0859#19 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:43:02
claim: Influence has four sources: likeness, credibility, power and status.
detail: Likeness: the person looks like me. Credibility: they show proof. Power: following their directive produced a good result (a hiccup tip that worked). Status: relative control over reinforcers (a bartender controlling scarce alcohol). The goal is influence over behaviour, best earned by directives that improve lives.
