### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#01 | EXAMPLE | topic: pricing | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Implied Guarantee | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: You can earn $1M by selling a result to a large company for a small share of the value created.
detail: Example: a $100M-a-year e-commerce store; if conversion work, rewritten emails and new landing pages lift sales 5%, that is $5M extra and 1% of the store's revenue is $1M for you. It works because of who the client is, not your size. If you lack the skill, learn it first.
numbers: $100M store, 5% lift = $5M; fee 1% of that revenue = $1M (as quoted).

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: verticals | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Real estate is borrowing to buy an asset that tenants pay off; a friend flipped a 14-unit building for a $500,000 gain in four months.
detail: He used other people's money, then moved to commercial property (buy at $10M, flip at $13M) and did 30-40 flips a year with a couple of virtual assistants. Higher-priced assets meant higher gains.
numbers: $500,000 gain in 4 months; $10M to $13M flips; 30-40 per year.

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#03 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:42
claim: Get a contract or option on an asset below market value and either resell the contract or refinance for the difference.
detail: Call many owners offering about 20% under value; someone who bought long ago will accept. Illustration: a $1M house under contract at $800,000, sold on at $900,000, leaves $100,000 with no money down. Another: a $100,000 house contracted at $80,000, with partial seller financing and bank funding.
numbers: Offer about 20% below value; $800,000 contract resold at $900,000 = $100,000.

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Commission on very large transactions pays a million without owning the asset.
detail: A skyscraper sale yields 3-4%, and a realtor on a $20M residence earns about 5%.
numbers: 3-4% of $100M = $3-4M; 5% of $20M = $1M.

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#05 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:38
claim: A million a year is about $20,000 a week, achievable as 20 sales of $1,000, two of $10,000 or one $80,000 sale every four weeks.
detail: Example: a $100/month membership kept 10 months gives $1,000 per member; selling 20 a week stacks up and caps at $1M, after which you need more units or more value per unit.
numbers: $20,000/week x 52 = $1.04M/year.

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#06 | CASE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:34
claim: A friend merged four small information businesses into one by paying their owners the income they would have earned for two years.
detail: Each earned about $1M a year; combined, $4M revenue and $1.5M profit at no upfront cost. He could sell the whole for about $5M against roughly $2M of payments to the owners, clearing over $1M.
numbers: 4 businesses x ~$1M revenue = $4M revenue, $1.5M profit; sale about $5M against about $2M owed.

### YT-7sLXhCDRaV8#07 | DEFINITION | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:40
claim: Arbitrage is selling the same thing in two markets with different prices and keeping the difference.
detail: Examples: Bitcoin at $19,000 in one market and $19,100 in another; borrowing at 1% and lending at 6% for a 5-point spread. Also affiliate an honest, tested favourite product.
numbers: $100 spread on $19,000; 1% borrowed vs 6% lent = 5%.
