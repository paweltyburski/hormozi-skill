### MZ-X6vwJUrOquI#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:00
claim: Support means lowering the other person's likelihood of failure, regardless of intentions.
detail: Even people who criticise you can promote you if a share of their audience finds and likes you. Relationships come down to whether you help the other reach their goals.

### MZ-X6vwJUrOquI#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:34
claim: A reason to end a business partnership, in his view, is that the partners want different things; with aligned outcomes and agreed behaviours, conflict is much less likely.
detail: It is very hard to optimise one machine around two different outcomes. Aligned goals plus agreed behaviours act like two guardrails, making conflict much less likely and making it easier to support the other person when they deviate.
