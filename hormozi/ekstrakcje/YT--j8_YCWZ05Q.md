### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: Activation Points | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:28
claim: Aim to get members past day 90 and month six, each with a different driver, rather than at a vague lower churn number.
detail: Month one: activation, onboarding, honest expectations, right promise, daily presence. Day 90: a real outcome or behaviour change. Month six: connections with other members.

detail: MRR grows only when joins exceed cancels: five in and four out grows; five and five is flat.

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#06 | EVOLUTION | topic: retention-ltv | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: updates | ts: 00:05:17
claim: Hormozi once dismissed "too expensive" cancel reasons; he now collects and tags them.
detail: Message every cancelling member, never act on one answer, log reasons (price, overwhelm, not using, missing feature, not as expected) and review the top one or two after about every 20 cancels. Also ask the longest-tenured members what keeps them; what repeats is the retention engine, everything else optional.

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:49

detail: Remove every annoyance between the customer and the promised result so the product just works.

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#08 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:07:40

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#09 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:51

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#10 | CASE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: Value vs Willingness-to-Pay Matrix | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:09:55
claim: Planet Fitness found members used only 10-15% of a full gym and sold just that part cheaply.
detail: Using one of five paid things feels like 20% of the value; the same price for the one used thing feels like 100%. Extra features can raise churn.
numbers: $10 per month vs about $59 per month clubs; 10-15% of facility used.

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#12 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:57
claim: Survey customers: what one thing would you fight for, and which one thing would you not miss?
detail: Chart the answers; the top one or two are usually far ahead, so do those and forget the rest. Think value per second, not seconds of value; one post, call recording or action a week can be enough.

### YT--j8_YCWZ05Q#13 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: GROWTH Onboarding | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:12:05
claim: Offer a busy-person path and a power-user depth path, and put the stickiest element plus a fast win in onboarding.
detail: Busy members need a start-here and weekly flow; heavy users get optional side quests. Remove friction ("snakes") and move the long ladder to the front, so new members hit value within 24 hours.
numbers: First win within 24 hours.
