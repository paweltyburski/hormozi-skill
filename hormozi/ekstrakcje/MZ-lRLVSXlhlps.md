### MZ-lRLVSXlhlps#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Most stuck businesses are in one of four traps: unclear what to sell, underpriced, wrongly paying the team, or serving the wrong or too many avatars.
detail: Each has a feared loss: customers if you raise prices, the team if you change pay, money if you narrow your avatar. In most of these rock-and-hard-place scenarios, he says you have to lean into the hard part. Underpriced firms are at capacity but not making any money.
### MZ-lRLVSXlhlps#02 | CASE | topic: offers | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A solo video producer at $250K should pair video skill with ads, social or selling and sell the result.
detail: B2B, so find the content type driving most client value (e.g. sales-letter videos). The video is the vehicle, not the product, so price can rise sharply.
numbers: $250K revenue.
