### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Write the call framework as questions only, never statements or paragraphs.
detail: Reps read paragraphs aloud and stop thinking. Questions let them pause and ask the next one without needing skill, which makes the script scalable.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: - | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:10
claim: The only thing a rep must truly know is three short stories matching the three missing ingredients of the solution.
detail: Pick three elements customers usually lack (weight loss: training, nutrition, accountability; lead sales: exclusive, timely, local). For each, tell a 30-second story, such as parents forcing you to brush teeth to illustrate accountability.
numbers: 30 seconds per story.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: - | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:56
claim: Handle the three concerns by cause: price is a value gap, stall needs a decision walk-through, and decision maker relies on prior implied agreement.
detail: For a partner objection, ask why the partner would oppose solving a problem both already know about. Drill the answers with flashcards.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#04 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:41
claim: Teach the decision as a series of yes-or-no checks, then ask for the card.
detail: Do you like me, like the product, believe we can get the outcome, have the money or access to it? After yeses, ask which card; then ask for their main remaining concern, answer it and close.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#05 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:27
claim: Reinforce the decision immediately, since buyers back out: a personal founder video, handwritten card or gift.
detail: Example: a 30-second named video from the founder moments after a software signup.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: Game Tape Review (Five A's) | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:01
claim: Record every call, review them, and run daily huddles plus weekly one-on-ones where reps tag a best, worst and average call.
detail: Recording drives script adherence and compliance. Huddles share testimonials so reps recall why they sell despite daily rejection. In one-on-ones check where each rep clarified the problem, covered the pain, asked for the sale, and overcame objections. Tool named: Gong.
numbers: Daily huddles, weekly one-on-ones, 3 tagged calls per review.

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: sales-team | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:50
claim: Cutting the bottom 10% of reps regularly lifts team productivity about 30% each time.
detail: Do it on a schedule even when the team is doing fine.
numbers: Bottom 10% cut regularly; about 30% productivity jump per cut (his claim).

### YT-bgmh7p1s7zs#08 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:38
claim: Keep a daily public leaderboard with a sale notification (virtual bell) and run six-week contests with three-person teams.
detail: Team contests let reps share practices and root for each other instead of cutthroat individual competition. Prize: a short weekend trip.
numbers: Contest length six weeks; prize budget about 25% of one monthly paycheque per person (example: monthly pay of about $7,000 gives a trip of about $1,500 per rep; pay figure unclear in audio).
