### LS-_ooj0V4hpgw#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:39
claim: People skip what they know they should do because a short-term payoff reinforces them more; the fix is to act first, then label the pattern as an identity.
detail: Answer to a chat question. A competing behaviour with an immediate reward beats the long-term one. Long term you break this by taking the action, then naming the pattern ("I am organised") as an identity you value; behaving consistently with that label then becomes the more rewarding option. Children get the label from others after a behaviour; adults have to assign it to themselves, otherwise they stay in short-term behaviour with short-term results.

### LS-_ooj0V4hpgw#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:00
claim: Cold traffic pushed through a sales motion built for a warm audience will not convert; a cold motion has to warm prospects up first.
detail: A warm funnel (e.g. short VSL to book a call) relies on trust the audience already has. Cold prospects need a step that builds that trust before the ask. He adds that a cold motion also helps warm traffic: warm people going through it arrive more sold and more likely to take a higher-priced offer, while cold people become more likely to buy at all. He describes a direct-response ad to a warm audience as harvesting only the bottom slice, in effect retargeting.
