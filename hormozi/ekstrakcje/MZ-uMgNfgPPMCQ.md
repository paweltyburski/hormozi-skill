claim: A buyer of a stake in a ketamine clinic with two partners has only word of mouth, cannot advertise the product, and faces partners wanting several new services.
detail: A new service line would raise staff from six to 22 per location.
numbers: Revenue $1.3M last year, $1.7M this year; EBITDA $700K; growth ~30% a year; staff 6 versus 22 per site.

### MZ-uMgNfgPPMCQ#02 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: When the product cannot be advertised, find referral nodes and recruit them on purpose.
detail: In word of mouth, about one customer in ten brings ten and the other nine bring none. Advertise the referral or affiliate opportunity without naming the drug.
numbers: 1 in 10 customers refer ~10.
