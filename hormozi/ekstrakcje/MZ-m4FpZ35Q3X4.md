### MZ-m4FpZ35Q3X4#01 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Anxiety appears when there are many choices and unclear priorities; commitment, which removes alternatives, is the cure.
detail: A decision is the cutting off of options. Someone who already knows how to beat the first levels of a game feels little anxiety because the next step is obvious.

### MZ-m4FpZ35Q3X4#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:21
claim: Treat anxiety or anger as fuel you channel, not as a flaw, and ask what it gets you.
detail: His wife leans anxious, he leans angry; both channel it into getting things done. Nervousness before a race or speech that drives extra preparation is a payoff, so the emotion was not purely negative.
