### YT-vKnAWYqMJ10#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:38
claim: A full "flex" lifestyle costs roughly $300,000 a year after tax.
detail: Itemised: private flights billed at about $4,000 an hour (six round trips of four flying hours each is about $100,000, against $6,000-$10,000 for first class); an exotic or luxury leased car $1,000-$4,500 a month (about $50,000 a year); a penthouse $5,000-$10,000 a month outside the top cities (up to $120,000); designer clothes about $3,000; weekly $400 dinners about $20,000. Pre-tax this needs about $500,000-$550,000. Dropping the jet and choosing a cheaper flat halves it to about $150,000.
numbers: total about $300,000/yr after tax; about $150,000/yr without jets and with a cheaper flat.

### YT-vKnAWYqMJ10#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:35
claim: Having tried it all, he rates most luxury as not worth it: private jets are nice but guilt-inducing, exotic cars fade within weeks, and mass-market products are often better tested.
detail: His luxury car interface was harder than a rented family SUV; mid-price restaurants gave him as much enjoyment as a $500 steak. He suggests treating a primary residence as an investment, such as a duplex where the other unit pays the rent.

### YT-vKnAWYqMJ10#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:11
claim: Do not take money advice from people whose lifestyle uses all of their income; spend on luxury only when it is under 10% of income.
detail: A business making $80,000 a month with $40,000 taken home already pays for that lifestyle, so it is not proof of wisdom. At $30M or more of investable assets, 10% returns give about $3M a year, ten times the cost of the lifestyle.
numbers: luxury spend under 10% of income; $30M investable assets earning 10% = $3M a year.

### YT-vKnAWYqMJ10#04 | DEFINITION | topic: finance-exit | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:49
claim: Wealth is the ratio of income to expenses, and happiness is what you have divided by what you want.
detail: A person earning $300,000 and spending $30,000 (10:1) is very wealthy, with no stress; keeping spending under 10% of income can free you within two to five years. Cutting wants raises abundance by lowering the denominator.
numbers: income-to-expense ratio of 10:1 as the bar for "very wealthy"; spend below 10% of income.
