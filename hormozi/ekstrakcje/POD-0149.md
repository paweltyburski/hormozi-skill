claim: Coach a salesperson by checking each CLOSER step: where did they clarify, label the pain and overview past experiences.
detail: His sales manager Cole turned around many fitness sales teams (the count is unclear in audio) only by following the framework in the book. Scaling is doing the one-on-one steps more, with systems.

### POD-0149#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: Proof Over Promise | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:05
claim: Make the first thing a visitor sees floor-to-ceiling testimonials, framed five-star reviews and before-and-afters.
detail: Print screenshots of Yelp, Google and Facebook reviews. The sale starts before any words, in the parking lot and when the appointment is set.

### POD-0149#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:00
claim: Greet every arrival within 10 seconds, ideally running over, then sit them with a pre-sale questionnaire.
detail: Anyone on staff can do it, reading questions aloud and squatting to eye level. Questions: why did you come, main goal, what have you tried, what was missing. It reminds them of the pain and extends their time in it.
numbers: greet within 10 seconds.

### POD-0149#04 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:34
claim: Include on the questionnaire "what bad habits do you have and what do you spend on them per week".
detail: The answer defuses "I cannot afford it", since they have already said they spend about $600 a month on habits they could cut.
numbers: example: $600/month.

### POD-0149#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:38
claim: A systematic pre-sale makes closes consistent, helping weaker reps; he and Layla once sold $600 from a card table in an empty warehouse.
detail: The owner is always the best seller, so the process should carry the ball most of the way. Doing so can close an extra 10% to 20% of walk-ins, at the cost of an afternoon or two of training and printing.
numbers: +10% to +20% closes; $600 warehouse sale.
