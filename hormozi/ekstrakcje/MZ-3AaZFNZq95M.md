### MZ-3AaZFNZq95M#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Managers need chunked days of short contacts; makers pretty much have two long chunks a day, maybe three.
detail: Deep work might take maybe 30-45 minutes to get into flow, and a looming meeting is an open loop that makes it hard to get in. A ten-minute meeting can cost a maker half a day.
numbers: Maybe 30-45 minutes to get into flow; about 2 chunks a day, maybe 3 if working 18 hours.
### MZ-3AaZFNZq95M#02 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Dedicate whole days to maker work, recording and meetings, and have the team stack meetings on the meeting day.
detail: His week has a meetings day, recording days and a day for portfolio and external companies.

### MZ-3AaZFNZq95M#03 | EVOLUTION | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:25
claim: His schedule moved from 4-9 a.m. maker blocks, to making until noon or 1 p.m. with meetings after, to near-empty days.
detail: As leverage grew the maker window stretched; meetings went late in the day because they need less energy.
numbers: 4-9 a.m. early; about 5 a.m.-12/1 p.m. for roughly five years.

### MZ-3AaZFNZq95M#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:11
