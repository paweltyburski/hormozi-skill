### POD-0077#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: retention-ltv | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:55
claim: Gym Launch's top programme charged at least $3,200 a month (maybe more) and had under 1% churn across 500+ gyms.
detail: The programme does not run ads for them; it is not an agency. Hormozi attributes the retention to a reverse of the usual "stay and pay" approach.
numbers: Price at least $3,200 a month; churn below 1%; more than 500 gyms (as stated).
### POD-0077#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: retention-ltv | also: money-models | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:36
claim: Run a recurring revenue model like a launch model: customers must re-sell themselves every month.
detail: Each cycle gives content and goodwill, builds excitement for something new, then delivers it, so effort recurs as well as payment. A portal alone is not enough. Auto-debit is not a guarantee. The gap between 5% and 1% monthly churn is five times customer value, which lets you pay far more for acquisition.
numbers: 5% churn versus 1% churn: 5x customer value.

### POD-0077#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:20
claim: Netflix keeps making new content despite an unwatchable back catalogue because subscribers must keep seeing the service is alive.
detail: Without new output, customers assume the owner is disengaged and the business stagnant, and eventually conclude they have got everything and leave, rightly so.

### POD-0077#04 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:48
claim: Mine customer problems, build a fix for those who struggle most, test it on three, then roll it out.
detail: Steps: (1) ask members what blocks them (nutrition, getting to workouts, motivation); (2) consolidate and choose the ones you can solve best; (3) give a solution built on how earlier members overcame it (for example emotional eating) to a small group of strugglers; (4) fix what failed and retest; (5) release it, and happy testers hype it for you. The task can be delegated to a passionate trainer.

### POD-0077#05 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:30
claim: Show members the pipeline of what is coming so they stay for the next release.
detail: Legacy members saw a list of upcoming ads (new Instagram story ads in beta, a revamped Facebook set, Snapchat ads) tested at around $30,000-$40,000 across about 20 markets, with delivery about six weeks out. Someone waiting for a feature will pay another month to get it.
numbers: Testing spend $30,000-$40,000 in about 20 markets; release about 6 weeks out.

### POD-0077#06 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: retention-ltv | fw: Client-Financed Acquisition | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:16:19
claim: Acquisition cost is rarely recouped when customer lifetime value is low: he says most gyms that come to him have customers lasting no more than four or five months.
detail: If the average customer pays only about three times, the acquisition cost can never be recouped. If you could never lose a customer, he says, you could pay whatever you want for acquisition.
numbers: Gyms that come to him: customer lifetime no more than 4 to 5 months; average customer paying about 3 times.
