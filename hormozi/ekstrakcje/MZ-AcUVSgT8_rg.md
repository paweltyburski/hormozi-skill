### MZ-AcUVSgT8_rg#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: The two real shelters are real-estate professional depreciation and charitable giving; location matters most otherwise.
detail: A real-estate professional can use 100% depreciation against ordinary income; charitable donations up to 30% of income save about 9% effective. Moving to a low-tax state beats 50% versus 37% taxes by roughly a third of take-home (his estimate).
numbers: Charitable up to 30% of income, ~9% saving; 50% vs 37% tax.
