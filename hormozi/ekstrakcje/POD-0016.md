### POD-0016#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: Wrap what customers need (the garlic) in what they want (the ham): sell the desired framing, deliver the needed substance.
detail: From a mentor's story about hiding garlic in ham to rid a dog of ticks. It justifies commercial front-end names even when you know the real answer is different.

### POD-0016#02 | CASE | topic: offers | also: promotions-launches | fw: MAGIC Naming Formula | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:16
claim: A six-week squat and deadlift seminar sold as a "big booty bootcamp" drew 40% of his customers, while the plain name would not sell.
detail: The training content was unchanged: every squat and deadlift variation. Only the commercial name changed.
numbers: 40% of his customers took the booty programme.
applies_to: Gyms.

detail: His highest coaching programme costs over six figures, a buyer he created by ascending. Owners who want ten $1,000-a-month clients should create them rather than look for them.

### POD-0016#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: money-models | fw: Problem-Solution Cycle | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:29
claim: Beginners are the widest base, intermediates far fewer and advanced fewest, so start at beginner awareness and educate upward.
detail: A customer taken through the full path values your service more because they have seen the journey from start to your level.

### POD-0016#05 | TACTIC | topic: offers | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:10
claim: Drop the purist pride about popular front-end promises, meet prospects where they are and educate them over time.
detail: Prospects want "weight loss" framing; there was a time you did not know the difference either. Systematically raise their awareness until they can laugh at old beliefs.
