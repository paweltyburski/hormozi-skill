claim: A lawyer for Latin musicians had $300,000 trailing-12-month revenue, lost about $100,000 and had about six months of cash because of an unfocused three-unit business.
detail: Services made $131,000 revenue (about $10,000 profit); education $131,000 (roughly break-even alone); software about $40,000 revenue. ### YT-6Fg4VXjRphQ#08 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: sales-process | fw: Anchor Upsell | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:14:42
claim: Sell a fixed-outcome package instead of hourly legal time, explaining that hourly billing rewards slowness.
detail: Pitch: hourly lawyers are incentivised to take longer, so we charge for the result. For commission-type deals charge $5,800 plus a percentage (5% of deals above $500,000, recent commissions in the $40,000s); without the percentage the price is $25,000, which acts as an anchor and makes the lower option feel cheap. He pushed the owner from $3,000 toward $5,000-$6,000 and they settled at $5,800. Pitching one outcome stops three outcomes confusing buyers.
numbers: $250/hour to $5,800 plus 5%; alternative $25,000 flat; owner had been selling three months for $4,000.
