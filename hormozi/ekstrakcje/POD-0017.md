### POD-0017#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:56
claim: Contact every new lead within 30 minutes, ideally within 5, because interest peaks at opt-in and drops sharply in that first half hour.
detail: After the click, interest declines quickly for 30 minutes and then trickles down over weeks. He says that if Facebook leads are not working, late first contact is the likely cause, and that fast contact can triple sales from the same leads.
numbers: within 30 min (better 5 min); about 3x sales.

### POD-0017#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:58
claim: Have the opt-in page text the lead's number automatically, then click the number on your buzzing phone and call at once.
detail: Calling while they are at the form means they have time to talk. Then you only chase the ones who did not answer, which saves hours versus batching calls later.
numbers: he says this converts about 50% of leads (stage unclear in audio); half to three quarters of the work done upfront.

### POD-0017#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:50
claim: In a two-step sales process, the set is part of the close: the setter must build up the closer before the handoff.
detail: Presenting the closer as a high-status expert raises the prospect's respect for that person's time and the likelihood they buy. Alongside it he repeats "pain is the pitch" and "time kills all deals".

### POD-0017#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:50
claim: Students he coached took online personal-training sales from zero to $30,000 a month as their close rate rose from 0 of 10 to 7 or 8 of 10.
detail: They kept practising: 3 of 10, then 5 of 10, then 7 or 8, then moved to group pitches.
numbers: $0 to $30,000/month; close rate 0/10 to 3/10 to 5/10 to 7-8/10.

### POD-0017#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-process | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:02
claim: For a low-ticket phone offer, contact within five minutes can compress interest to payment into about ten minutes.
detail: He compares it to Amazon one-click: when the call arrives right after the form, the sale happens in the same moment.
numbers: about 10 minutes from enquiry to payment.
