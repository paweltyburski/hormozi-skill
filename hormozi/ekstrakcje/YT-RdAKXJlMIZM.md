### YT-RdAKXJlMIZM#01 | DEFINITION | topic: scaling-constraints | also: operator-mindset | fw: Leverage | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:41
claim: Work is output, and output is volume (times you act) multiplied by leverage (what each act yields); work rate is output per unit of time.
detail: He rejects force times distance, hours and effort as definitions. Doing more is capped by time and ability, while leverage is not capped. So outworking competitors means out-leveraging them.
