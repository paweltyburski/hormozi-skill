### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Downsell by exchange, not by begging: after a "no", the party who sets the terms holds the power, and small favours make a bigger ask hard to refuse.
detail: Cutting price with nothing in return sounds desperate ("commission breath") and unethical. Cards to trade: commitment length, a testimonial, a review, bringing a friend.

### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: pricing | fw: Downsell Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:11
claim: Step-down ladder for a $300 three-week program: $49 a week for 12 weeks with commitment, drop the commitment, $39 with a testimonial, $29 a week with a friend in 30 days.
detail: Each step is small, delivered lightly and acknowledged as getting ridiculous. Ladder: half price with 12-week commitment; remove commitment; add testimonial for a lower rate; final pay-as-you-go with a friend referral to roughly cover costs.
numbers: $300 (3 weeks, $100/week) down to $49/week, $39/week, $29/week.

### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#03 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:57
claim: Add asks instead of removing bonuses, so a different price can be explained by extra terms the other customer did not agree to.
detail: He prefers asking for more over cutting the package, since he wants buyers to have the items.

### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#04 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:17
claim: Close each step with a genuinely asked "fair enough?" and then small micro-commitments, such as morning or afternoon for the nutrition appointment, before the card.
detail: Tone carries the message; on the phone only a small part of communication is the words (his figure: 9%). Stay on the same side of the table, e.g. "we don't have to get married, just a first date".
numbers: 9% verbal (as he states).

### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:38
claim: In Hormozi's opinion, if a prospect still says no after about six step-downs, it's not a money thing: you lost them earlier in the sale and they became disengaged.
detail: Fix this earlier in the call: when answers are surface-level, ask permission to be real rather than nice, then state the pain and soften it.
numbers: ~6 step-downs.
### YT-gza5RtQCVsA#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: money-models | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:32
claim: The point of a low entry downsell is to get started so they trust you within 7-14 days and buy the full-price program later.
detail: Everyone has some money and some problem; being able to downsell can raise close rate considerably.
numbers: 7-14 days to trust.
