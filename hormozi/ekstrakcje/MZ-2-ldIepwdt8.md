### MZ-2-ldIepwdt8#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals | fw: Trial with Penalty | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A community owner testing a seven-day free trial gets 40 customers a day but sees MRR fall, and is told to wait for the data.
detail: Build a sheet: clicks, percent starting a trial, percent turning paid, LTV at the new churn, compared with the old flow. Post-trial churn is probably lower. Free also raises clicks.
numbers: 40 trial customers per day.

### MZ-2-ldIepwdt8#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: money-models | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:00
claim: A free trial helps if the lift in conversion outweighs trial cancellations.
detail: With a 4x lift: if half cancel you still double sales; if three-quarters cancel you break even. He also said two-thirds cancelling is about +50%, though the arithmetic gives about +33% (unclear in audio).
numbers: 4x conversion lift; 50% cancel = 2x; 75% cancel = even.
