### POD-0046#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:49
claim: Physical retail will be displaced by online sellers with more variety and better prices, so what remains local is food, services and experiences.
detail: Things that need the body present (restaurant, hair, dentist, chiropractor, cinema) survive. Low-price, high-volume gym chains consolidate into a race on facilities and price, so a local service business should avoid that and sell service at a high ticket.
applies_to: Brick-and-mortar service businesses.

### POD-0046#02 | TACTIC | topic: strategy-positioning | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Do not spend extra profit on equipment; compete on service.
detail: Equipment is irrelevant to the bottom line when customers come for service. A cheap high-volume model cannot win against chains charging $30 a month.
numbers: Chains with $10M gyms sell at about $30 per month.

### POD-0046#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: pricing | also: offers | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:53
claim: To sell a $3,000 package, design it to deliver $30,000 of value.
detail: Thinking in 10x value changes the design and puts you in a different mindset from owners selling a $99 bootcamp. Higher lifetime value lets you acquire customers more easily and they stay more easily. His own programmes cost 3-10x competitors but are claimed to return about 10x their price.
numbers: $3,000 price against $30,000 value; $20,000 programme claimed to produce $200,000.
### POD-0046#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:42
claim: Online, narrow niches can win globally with one winner per category; a local business typically serves the general population and wins through superior experience and results.
detail: The local reward is customers who stay longer and bring friends, which funds reinvestment.

### POD-0046#05 | EXAMPLE | topic: product-delivery | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:33
claim: His margin funded a service competitors could not afford: 11 full-time US tech-support staff and a five-minute response promise.
detail: He credits this for 90% of customers succeeding against the roughly 10% he claims is normal in the industry.
numbers: 11 full-time support staff; reply within 5 minutes; 90% vs 10% success claimed.

### POD-0046#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: verticals | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:29
claim: People will keep paying a human to hold them accountable because status drives attendance, which a robot trainer would not supply.
detail: Relationships bring people in and keep them; a low-margin, high-volume gym will eventually lose.
