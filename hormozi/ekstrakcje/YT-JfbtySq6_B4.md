### YT-JfbtySq6_B4#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: objections-closing | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Damaging admission: state a true, costly negative first and follow with "but" and the positive, because the word "but" amplifies what comes after and diminishes what comes before.
detail: Example: you must go through hundreds of hours of video and work about five hours a day, but you will make more than ever. The more true and damaging the negatives, the more believable the claim that follows. Also controls where attention goes.

### YT-JfbtySq6_B4#02 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:28
claim: Weave damaging admissions into copy, especially near the call to action, and leave negative reviews visible.
detail: Gym Launch has many one-star reviews beside thousands of five-star ones; a 4.7 rating over 3,000 reviews is more credible than 5.0 over 19.
numbers: 4.7 stars x 3,000 reviews vs 5.0 x 19.

### YT-JfbtySq6_B4#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:28
claim: The Viagra ad's warning about erections lasting over four hours made the product's power believable, an admitted risk that amplified the claim.
detail: His parallel: warn that working with Gym Launch will greatly raise your tax bill.
numbers: 4-hour warning.

### YT-JfbtySq6_B4#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: lead-getters | fw: Three Core Beliefs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45

detail: Even a "10 back for every dollar" claim fails if not believed.
