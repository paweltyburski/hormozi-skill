### MZ-0f15GANrLCw#01 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An owner-broker of RV parks and campgrounds ($1.3M) grew to a team of 12 and is drowning in management.
detail: Net just over $500K. Six agents, a COO and three VAs. Plans an advisory offer: $10,000 upfront, credited against commission if they list. Advice: rank tasks, keep promoting and closing, hand the rest to a coordinator, and give a junior a structured path.
numbers: $1.3M revenue; ~$500K net; $10,000 advisory fee credited at close.

### MZ-0f15GANrLCw#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:30
claim: Advice to this caller, not stated as a general rule: she probably has one person on her team who drains her time, and he said to get rid of that person even if they perform, since she is the highest revenue generator.
detail: He told her to treat herself as the top producer. He also says that the longer he has been in business, the more he indexes on intelligence; smart generalists can usually figure things out quickly, and lower-intelligence staff tend to create undue process.
