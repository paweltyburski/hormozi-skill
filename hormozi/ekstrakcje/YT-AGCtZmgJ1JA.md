### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:29
claim: Conviction is the top predictor of good selling, trust transfers it, and conviction is a matter of degree.
detail: One person already believes, the other does not yet. Trust comes from expertise and some rapport. Reps rarely have full conviction, which matters more than drills.

### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:29
claim: He keeps a very short tryout for new sales reps because strong ones do well in the first week.
detail: He has never had a killer rep who failed the first week, so he turns over weak hires fast; the remaining team is strong and knows it.
numbers: Tryout judged within about the first week.

### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:24
claim: Reps sell better after hearing last week's customers, so he stacked sales appointments on the day participants finished their challenge.
detail: At the in-person finish day people were emotional and excited, and reps who saw results closed close to every call. Believing in the product changes tone more than training does.
numbers: Close rate on those days: about 100%.

### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#04 | SCRIPT | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:22
claim: A typical call runs: why are they here, what is the problem, what have they tried, why it failed, how this differs, ask permission to explain, explain by experiences with analogies, then a no-based close.
detail: Explain the product through experiences they will have, not features. The close is phrased so that "no" means yes, such as "would you mind", as natural persuaders do.

### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#05 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:14
claim: Handle an objection like "my husband won't approve" with childlike curiosity rather than argument.
detail: Steps: ask why he would not, note that he wants what is best, ask whether he knows they struggle, then ask whether he would prefer they keep struggling, and finally whether they are opposed to moving forward today.

### YT-AGCtZmgJ1JA#06 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:12
claim: Train reps to slow their breathing under pressure, as fighters do, so curiosity replaces fight-or-flight.
detail: Adrenaline makes people breathe faster and get defensive. A calm "tell me more" makes the prospect feel helped rather than combated.
