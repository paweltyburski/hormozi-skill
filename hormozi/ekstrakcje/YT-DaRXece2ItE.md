### YT-DaRXece2ItE#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His last book launch drew about 500,000 registrations, nearly a third from people who did not know him and came through referrals.
detail: He describes affiliates as independent businesses with their own advertising who offer your product to their engaged leads in exchange for money, free goods, or both, which differs from simple referrals. About 30-something thousand affiliates promoted the launch the year before, and he uses the same machine for the next launch.
numbers: ~500,000 registrations; roughly one third not previously his audience; ~30,000+ affiliates in prior launch.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#02 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: pricing | fw: Three-Tier Affiliate Commissions | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:03
claim: He pays affiliates in non-cash value, because tax paperwork for thousands of payees is a burden and experiences can be worth more than a percentage.
detail: Paying many people means collecting forms and reporting; free goods and access avoid that. The prize should also benefit the affiliate's audience, so that promoting does not spend the affiliate's goodwill, and ideally adds to it: a win for you, the affiliate and their people.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:03
claim: Build a two-tier affiliate prize structure so that both large-audience partners and people who bring only a few friends have something to win.
detail: Top tier for the big hitters, a floor for the long tail, and approximations in between so the ladder rises toward performance. His layout: top 10 get an in-person fireside Q&A (audience questions submitted through the affiliate's link) plus a dinner; top 50 attend a three-hour in-person session at his headquarters; everyone above the entry threshold gets a bonus audiobook (70+ one-minute tactics recorded only for affiliates), a raffle chance at 50 further seats, and a recording of the fireside.
numbers: Top 10 in person plus dinner; top 50 three-hour session; 50 raffled seats; entry threshold 10 opt-ins.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:20
claim: Set the entry threshold by how hard the action is: for example, it might be two for in-person visits, four or five for text introductions, and he made it ten plus opt-ins when friction is low.
detail: The lower the friction of the action, the higher he makes the threshold. He used 10 plus opt-ins for the free book launch because it worked well the year before. The minimum activation point should still let people with small groups (ten friends, a Slack or WhatsApp group) earn something.
numbers: Threshold examples: might be ~2 for in-person visits; might be 4-5 for text intros; 10+ opt-ins for his low-friction launch.
### YT-DaRXece2ItE#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: promotions-launches | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:01
claim: Make the top affiliate prize big and unscalable, then create descending approximations, such as a recording, for everyone else.
detail: A chat with a thousand people would not work for anyone, so scarcity at the top is real. He spent two days designing the permutations for clarity and benefit to all parties; it looks obvious afterwards. He calls the affiliate offer the highest leverage thing and says a strong enough one can move mountains.
numbers: Two days spent designing the offer.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#06 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:04
claim: Sell the programme on a dedicated affiliate page that lists the prizes, what to do (opt in for a tracked link) and how you will help.
detail: Opt-in gives a personal link that ranks them on a leaderboard. The name varies by industry: partner, influencer, centre of influence or JV page. He also uses a thank-you-page button for people who just registered, so every traffic source lands on the same page.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#07 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:29
claim: Hand affiliates ready-made posts, ads and emails that already worked, plus test messages for chats, so promoting needs only copy and paste.
detail: Expecting them to build a campaign is too big an ask. Collateral also keeps messaging consistent with what you can deliver, with a simple compliance rule: do not lie or exaggerate. Split collateral in two: the starting set, then updated winners as data arrives so affiliates double down on what converts. Tailor versions for Instagram, email-list and text-based writers, and ready-to-run ads for pure ad buyers (several of his previous top affiliates only ran provided ads).

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#08 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:29
claim: Share behind-the-scenes launch data with affiliates as an exclusive benefit.
detail: He shows how each email and ad is performing during the launch, so affiliates learn the process from outside; marketers like seeing winners and statistics he normally cannot share. It is one of the most underrated parts of the offer.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#09 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: promotions-launches | fw: Whisper Tease Shout | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:16
claim: Promote the affiliate programme through limited channels: two emails to the list, re-invite last year's affiliates, a thank-you-page button, and one explainer video.
detail: Two emails only (an announcement and a reminder), since the focus is the launch itself. Past affiliates, already opted in, get one or two extra messages. Everyone who registers sees an offer to earn prizes. He is not running ads to recruit affiliates; ads go to the launch itself. Warm and cold outreach, email and free content cover the Core Four for recruiting, and the affiliates then run the same methods.
numbers: 2 list emails; 1-2 extra emails to previous affiliates.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#10 | FRAMEWORK | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:11:49
claim: Activate affiliates with fast rewards and feedback: onboarding, an immediate custom link, a live leaderboard, collateral and regular communication.
detail: A custom link plus visible attribution on a leaderboard shows tracking is trustworthy; if they doubt attribution they will not send traffic. Send the leaderboard as often as possible because competitors check daily. His marketing director contacts rising top performers to help them do more. If any of the steps (offer, tell people, make it easy) is missing, the programme will not work.

### YT-DaRXece2ItE#11 | DEFINITION | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: Lead Getters | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:19
claim: Promoters have many industry names for the same role: influencers, UGC creators, affiliates, referrals, centres of influence, joint ventures.
detail: They are people who tell others about your product for money, free goods or love of it. Speak each type's language so none faces friction; as soon as there is friction, they stop.
