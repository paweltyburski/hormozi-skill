### YT-7wN8n3qrqnQ#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:14

detail: From a persuasion book he read. It explains why helped clients later say they would have managed anyway, including owners he took from broke to seven-figure gyms who now speak against him. Treat it as a human bias, not an insult.

### YT-7wN8n3qrqnQ#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: objections-closing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:50
claim: Ask for a return favor (referral, review) immediately after delivering a win, when perceived value peaks.
detail: Further favors over time create repeated peaks of goodwill, like poles holding up a wire; without them goodwill sags.

### YT-7wN8n3qrqnQ#04 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: retention-ltv | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:15
claim: Record the testimonial at the moment the result lands, not months later.
detail: Example: a client who just lost 25 pounds, filmed right after weighing in, describes the change accurately and emotionally; six months on they credit themselves.
numbers: 25 pounds lost (example).

### YT-7wN8n3qrqnQ#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: retention-ltv | also: product-delivery | fw: Problem-Solution Cycle | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:03
claim: Information loses perceived value as soon as it is consumed, so keep teaching new things on a regular schedule.
detail: Customers judge you by what you did lately. Each solved problem exposes the next gap, so you must stay ahead to build the next bridge; the value you delivered expires.
