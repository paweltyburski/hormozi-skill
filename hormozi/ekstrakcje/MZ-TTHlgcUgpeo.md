### MZ-TTHlgcUgpeo#01 | CASE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Owners of a cash-based pelvic-floor coaching business ($2.1M) also run a clinic and a conference and are overloaded.
detail: Coaching and education earn about $1.6M and feed a six-month programme plus continuity. The brick-and-mortar practice makes $1.7M revenue and $350K profit at 25-30% margin but eats time and emotion. The conference earns about $500K profit.
numbers: $2.1M coaching; clinic $1.7M revenue, $350K profit, 25-30% margin.

### MZ-TTHlgcUgpeo#02 | TACTIC | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:47
claim: Sell an unwanted practice to a customer in your network, with free coaching membership as part of the price.
detail: Walking away from a transfer is faster than shutting down. It gives a clean exit story, continuity for staff and a success story for clients; the buyer works inside your model.

### MZ-TTHlgcUgpeo#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:05:25
claim: Hormozi sold six gyms in 90 days for the price of one to focus on Gym Launch.
detail: Advisers said to spend 18 months polishing for a sale at maybe $2-3M. Within six months Gym Launch made $1M a month, so opportunity cost beat holding on.
numbers: 6 gyms to 0 in 90 days; ~$2-3M possible later; $1M/month in 6 months.

### MZ-TTHlgcUgpeo#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:19
claim: A single location rarely has margin to pay a manager; management works at around 20 sites.
detail: One manager over about five locations makes economics work; one site means the owner is the manager.
numbers: ~20 sites for layered management; one manager per ~5 locations.
