### MZ-IjF5-Z0hzhs#01 | CASE | topic: sales-process | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A Spanish fitness and nutrition coach (3-month plan about $800, 6-month about $1,400, four trainers, 150,000 organic Instagram followers) sees only 20% of qualified leads show up to the call.
detail: Funnel: about 600 form fills, 300 qualified, about 60 calls. After the form only one WhatsApp message is sent and 80% never answer. Hormozi: this is lead nurture, not show rate; fix it before anything else because inbound organic should convert far better.
numbers: 600 forms, 300 qualified, 60 calls (20%); 150K followers; $800 and $1,400 plans.

### MZ-IjF5-Z0hzhs#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:18
claim: Lead nurture has four vectors: how many times you reach out, how fast, how personalised, and how available you make scheduling; rate yourself 1-10 on each.
detail: Hormozi rated this caller about 1 on volume and a 5 on personalisation (voice notes). He would probably crank up volume, follow-ups and speed first; that will then make the caller start booking up and need more people.
### MZ-IjF5-Z0hzhs#03 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: outreach | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:44
claim: Personalise by medium: send text, voice and video variants, since many people never open voice memos; a meme in between can pull strong responses.
detail: Also call outbound; adding operational complexity in the funnel raises LTV and lets you outcompete rivals.

### MZ-IjF5-Z0hzhs#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:45
claim: Use the ratio of local to US median income as a pricing indicator, then scale price by brand strength.
detail: Spain average income is about $30,000. If US peers charge $3,000, adjust by that fraction; more pricing power comes from brand, and some sell at $10K.
numbers: Average income Spain about $30,000; US peers $3,000 to $10,000.

### MZ-IjF5-Z0hzhs#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: management-ops | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:47
claim: Every funnel step should carry a conversion percentage that you check daily, before the bank account or social media.
detail: From DM response through form fill to automation, ask what can raise each step.
