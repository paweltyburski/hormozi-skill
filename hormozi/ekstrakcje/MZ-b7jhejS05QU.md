### MZ-b7jhejS05QU#01 | CASE | topic: pricing | also: verticals, strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A diagramming tool for theory-of-constraints work, used by consultants and defence contractors, is underpriced and wants enterprise deals.
detail: It moved from perpetual licences to subscriptions, reaching $10K MRR, and has three staff. It started as software built for a large contractor and licensed back. Users there spread it to other firms, but it cannot name customers. Enterprise buyers send purchase orders for 25 seats with long contracts and no lawyer to review them.
numbers: Revenue $200K; $0 to $10K MRR in ~15 months; proposed enterprise site licence $250,000.

### MZ-b7jhejS05QU#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: pricing, leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Three things to settle: who you sell to, how much you charge, and how you get them.
detail: Once the "who" is clear, price and channel follow.

### MZ-b7jhejS05QU#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: When there is a clear enterprise use case, he would probably go whale hunting rather than minnow hunting.
detail: At such firms he doesn't think budgets even round down to $10,000, and he thinks they wouldn't believe a price under a million.
numbers: Hormozi: they might doubt anything under a million.
### MZ-b7jhejS05QU#04 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:07
claim: Name the biggest number you can think of in the conversation and watch the reaction.
detail: Use the response to calibrate enterprise pricing.
