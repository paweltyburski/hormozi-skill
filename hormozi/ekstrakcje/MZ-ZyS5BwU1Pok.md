### MZ-ZyS5BwU1Pok#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: His goal is to do significant things and be useful, not to be happy, because what he later enjoys most is what he sacrificed for over a long time.
detail: He frames it as short-term cost against long-term memory payoff. He says an obsession with happiness in college, including positive psychology, left him sadder than before.

### MZ-ZyS5BwU1Pok#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:56
claim: Wanting to be happier creates a gap between where you are and where you want to be, and that gap is itself the source of unhappiness.
detail: Once he dropped happiness as a requirement for his activities, the activities became pleasant surprises, and he gave himself permission to be unhappy for an extended period while pursuing what he wanted. A few years later he noticed he was not miserable.

### MZ-ZyS5BwU1Pok#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:18
claim: Unused potential will, in his view, eat a person alive until acted on, so he aims to extract all of what he has.
detail: He says his own early career looked successful on paper (finished school in three years, good job) but felt empty.
