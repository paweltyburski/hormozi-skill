### MZ-X03kc381UEM#01 | CASE | topic: money-models | also: verticals, finance-exit | fw: Upsell Offers | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A soccer-training coach selling a $397 digital plan at $1M a year was told his problem is leads, but the offer lacks a back end.
detail: Goal is $12M revenue and an eventual exit; everything is organic and the founder-face cannot make endless content. Hormozi: fix the offer first. Add a roughly $4,000 higher-touch product for a share of $400 buyers (maybe 1 in 20, maybe 1 in 4) simply by calling past buyers; this triples the business, then fund ads for a second triple.
numbers: $1M revenue with near-full profit becomes ~$3-4M profit on ~$10M revenue (his estimate); front end $397, back end ~$4,000.

### MZ-X03kc381UEM#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Prep for Sale | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: For this soccer-program caller, Hormozi said a digital product definitely won't sell; to exit, build a brand and a different, better product, or add recurring revenue.
detail: If the product gets any success, expect China to make dupes, so people must buy because of who you are and what it is. Alternative: sell education to teams on subscription. Building just to sell is super rare outside boring roll-up businesses; provide consistent value over a long time and it becomes valuable enough to buy.
### MZ-X03kc381UEM#03 | TACTIC | topic: retention-ltv | also: pricing | fw: Continuity Offers | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:46
claim: Turn a one-time purchase into recurrence with a six-month or annual term, or an association that gives members aggregate discounts.
detail: If a member pays $400 a year and saves about 10x that, it sticks. Unlike services, a network gets better as it grows because buying power rises.
numbers: $397 renewed every six months; 10x saving ($400 fee, $4,000 saved).

### MZ-X03kc381UEM#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:47
claim: Sellable companies are worth more because they are slower and harder to build; choose cash now or an exit.
detail: Adding a back end plus ads makes more money but is not sellable; the recurring-revenue route is slower. Which path depends on what the owners want.
