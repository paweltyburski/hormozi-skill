### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#01 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:37
claim: "Organic plus CTA": take your best-performing organic posts, add a call to action at the end, and pay to promote them.
detail: Organic content gives free test shots; the algorithm shows which clips hold attention and get shares and clicks. A long call to action would hurt the organic version, so add it only to the paid copy. Platforms are glad to show a post that already performs in exchange for money. He calls it the easiest option and says it is working very well for his companies. Paid ads are also the fastest channel to scale, since one skilled person with good creative can do it.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#02 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: Ad Assembly Process | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:27
claim: Source hooks from the best organic content and ads in all industries, not only yours, since hooks transfer when they do not name the industry.
detail: A hook that works in finance can work for hotels. The hook is the large share of the ad (about 80%) even though it is short. Do the research before recording and prepare 50+ hooks; he says you can never have too many ads at any spend level, because more options let the algorithm find winners.
numbers: 50+ hooks; hook is about 80% of the ad's value (his estimate).

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#03 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:59
claim: Four-step persuasion builds the body of an ad or pitch from four angles on the desired action.
detail: If you act: more good things. If you act: fewer bad things. If you do not act: more bad things. If you do not act: fewer good things. Gym example: act and you gain energy and sleep; skip it and brain fog persists and the weight keeps going (50 pounds heavier now may become 70 in a few years) with fewer happy memories with your kids. Add the "who and when" layer: status relative to spouse, kids and rivals, in the present and future.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#04 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:33
claim: Make copy specific by thinking in moments: the concrete scene where the customer realises the result happened.
detail: Break vague terms like "feel better" into moments, such as an older person lifting a child, or tying shoes without holding in a stomach and losing breath. Find them by reading positive reviews, for example on Amazon, of similar products even if you sell services, in the buyer's own words.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:59
claim: Make the call to action visible in the ad and make the next page look as the ad described, for congruence.
detail: Show the action, such as the next page, on screen. When expectations are met, fewer people bounce, and the person making the recommendation gains influence, because someone whose predictions come true is believed on the next one, which seeds the later sale.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#06 | EXAMPLE | topic: leads-fundamentals | also: verticals | fw: Lead Magnet Steps | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:10:39
claim: He calls the best lead magnet he has seen one from dating: how to know if a woman wants you to kiss her.
detail: The answer is a simple test (brush hair behind her ear and see whether she moves away or towards the hand). It solves one tiny problem completely, so the reader believes you can solve the next ones. It is a one-step-of-a-multi-step-process magnet, a tiny loop in the problem-solution cycle (dates, replies, talk, proposal). Most first-time lead magnets, ads and sales calls are poor, which is why most beginners lose money at first.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:31
claim: Replace "emotional versus logical buyer" with "high versus low information": giving everyone more information closes more.
detail: A minority buys straight from an ad or video. The others, about 97%, need more time and information to decide, and the process should supply it. If you give more information to everyone, more people close overall.
numbers: About 97% of buyers need more information than an ad gives.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#09 | FRAMEWORK | topic: sales-process | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Sales Aids | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:18:32
claim: VSL structure one is the five Ps: promise, pain, proof, plan (visual), picture, followed by an FAQ block and a proof stack with calls to action.
detail: The FAQ pre-handles every question prospects ask on calls (how long have you done this, is it legit). Finish with customer stories and a testimonial reel plus another CTA; people do watch them. Structure two, for a service people already understand (electrician, HVAC), uses five Ws (who, what, where, when, why), still with FAQs, proof stack and CTAs. There is no magic in a VSL: it answers the questions someone reasonably has before buying.

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#11 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: paid-ads | fw: Create Flow Monetize Add Friction | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:21:43

### YT-Ds_Qp2U5I8U#16 | EXAMPLE | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:25:42

detail: A follower watching five 30-second shorts a week for a year gets about two hours of information. The funnel gives about 30-45 minutes of material and an hour on the call, over a couple of days.
