### MZ-EBnTfYfaaLI#01 | DEFINITION | topic: offers | also: - | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Effort is what you must start doing because of a decision; sacrifice is what you must stop doing.
detail: In fitness, effort includes waking earlier, eating food you dislike and being sore; sacrifice includes giving up foods you like and sleeping in. Both sit in the denominator of the value equation.

### MZ-EBnTfYfaaLI#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: offers | also: pricing | fw: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Likelihood explains price gaps for the same outcome, such as a PDF programme versus a personal trainer.
detail: A $19 fitness PDF and a $3,000 in-person trainer share an outcome; the difference is perceived likelihood, since effort and time are roughly fixed. The same logic applies to consuming content: a four-hour video is watched less than a three-minute one.
numbers: $19 PDF versus $3,000 trainer, three sessions per week.

### MZ-EBnTfYfaaLI#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:22
claim: Speed is the most underused lever: copy what competitors do and deliver it in half the time.
detail: He says this works in nearly any business because someone always pays a premium for faster results. He ties it to a Bezos idea of betting on what will not change: customers prefer lower prices, more selection and faster delivery (two hours rather than two days). Reviews and one-click buying raise likelihood and cut effort.
numbers: Delivery example: 2 hours versus 2 days.

### MZ-EBnTfYfaaLI#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: offers | also: - | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:56
claim: Buyers discount distant future outcomes to near zero, even guaranteed ones.
detail: A guaranteed return from investing weekly over decades loses to a latte today because the delay cuts perceived value. Offers should bring benefits forward.
