### MZ-d_ybS3iBDX4#01 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent, operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Keep or drop people by asking whether having them around raises or lowers the likelihood you reach your goals, then weigh the opportunity cost of their time.
detail: Everyone may raise the odds, but someone absent may raise them more. The raise need not be business-related: a spouse who keeps life enjoyable raises the odds you keep going.
