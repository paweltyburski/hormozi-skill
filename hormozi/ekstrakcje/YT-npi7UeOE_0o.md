detail: On the Gemini ad, the reviewer said creating urgency would not be on brand for them, but he still marked them down for it. On the Booking.com ad, he said he personally had a hard time feeling like the avatar.
### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:40
claim: The best ad is the one that drove the most sales, and objectives differ: build sentiment, reinforce a position, or pre-direct response.
detail: Data beats opinion, and the objective must be known before judging.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#03 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:08:45
claim: Crowdsource ads from customers: a fan contest cost the brand about a prize and yielded a creative already upvoted by the audience.
detail: Customers know the product intimately, while an agency would cost more than the prize without proof of success. Community votes act like a pre-test. Judge only if you are in the target.
numbers: Prize $1M, against agency production costing more.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#04 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:12
claim: Short ad structure: open with a question about the pain your viewer has, show life without the product, then demonstrate the product solving it at once.
detail: Make the hook contextual to the audience. He also puts the call to action in text, not only audio, because TV is often watched from afar without subtitles.
numbers: A 15-second ad guessed at $4M-$5M against $8M for 30 seconds.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:11:37
claim: Hormozi breaks the Jeep brand ad into an association chain: Jeep equals freedom, freedom is stuff the demo likes, then many such things are stacked; the ad did not mention price or financing.
detail: He bets the associations come from customer research and says the ad is specific about its avatar. He calls it a good, A-tier pure brand ad.
numbers: He loosely guesses a two to two-and-a-half minute ad cost 'eight times five' (in millions).
### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#06 | DECISION_RULE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:31:24
claim: A sentiment ad should make the sponsor clear: Hormozi says a very short ending tag (about one second) left him unsure whose ad it was.
detail: A mortgage ad built on shared American associations lost points because the logo appeared in the last second. Tie the emotion to the product through a link word such as "own". Compared to a story ad that shows the product being used throughout, it scored lower.
### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: offers | fw: Value Equation | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:14:26
claim: Show the dream outcome the audience wants, not only what they hate; a fear-based ad with no dream outcome and a contradictory message scored worst.
detail: He disliked an ad that criticised the profit motive then asked viewers to buy, did not show the target person's desired result, and repositioned the brand as healthcare.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:17:02
claim: Do not make the ad about the company: a celebrity discussing the brand's name speaks about "us", not the customer.
detail: Rule he cites: nobody cares about you, they care about themselves. A callout aimed at actors failed with small-business owners.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#09 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: Call-Outs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:19:01
claim: Better structure for a software or services ad: show several emotive things the owner cannot do, insert the product, revisit each one now solved, then recap with a call to action.
detail: Use real owners with real pains (payroll, phone, email). The ad he reviewed had to be watched twice to understand, and a viewer rarely re-watches.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#10 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: promotions-launches | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:22:14
claim: Show the problem and the immediate fix near the call to action, and consider co-op funding from suppliers whose products you distribute.
detail: In a grocery-delivery ad the final five seconds (forgotten milk, quick order) were the clearest use case, so the first 70-80% could have been trimmed. Brands sold through a distributor may share ad costs.

### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#11 | FRAMEWORK | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:25:02
claim: Awareness-ad template: grab attention, state an uncomfortable fact, give a testimonial of someone helped by the service, then a call to action.
detail: Advertising makes things known; persuasion changes behaviour, so ask how many people act, not whether it is liked. He thinks a version showing all the moments you would miss out on would probably have been more compelling.
### YT-npi7UeOE_0o#12 | PRINCIPLE | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:56
