### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:02:34
claim: Values are the non-negotiables that are truly innate to the team, not aspirations, and are used to hire and fire.
detail: Derive values from what you would not tolerate. A person who breaks one cannot work there (Southwest's "have fun" example). Because they are non-negotiable, hiring and firing decisions run through them.

### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#02 | DECISION_RULE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:22
claim: A value must draw a line in the sand and repel some people, otherwise it is only a platitude.
detail: Test: can you say "we lean toward X, not Y" (for example justice over mercy)? If the value repels nobody, it is not a value. Phrase it in the words your team already uses (Gym Launch: "Speed is King", "do the boring work", "don't sugarcoat it") rather than generic labels.

### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#03 | BENCHMARK | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:12
claim: He says they have found three is the sweet spot for core values, and you can have too many values, so be selective.
detail: Three is what the human brain can comprehend as lenses to make a decision. A lot of times, if torn between two, you chunk up into a broader value. The number has continued to distill down over time.
numbers: 3 values (sweet spot in their experience).
### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:02
claim: Scale decision-making by requiring a candidate to meet all values, not most of them.
detail: Values let someone hire without the founder present. Accept only 3 of 3; a person with 2 of 3 or 1 of 3 is not accepted. Stricter values make it easier to give black-and-white examples.
numbers: 3 of 3 required; 2 of 3 or 1 of 3 rejected.
### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:02
claim: If you would not work with a client or employee for a decade, do not work with them for a day.
detail: Apply the values to clients as well as staff. Removing people or clients who break the non-negotiables is short-term pain that lets the organisation grow, like pruning a tree. Attributed to a Naval saying.

### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#06 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: hiring-talent | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:07:39
claim: Character test: would I trust this person with my wife, and with my money held for a month, a year or a decade?
detail: Two questions borrowed from a SEAL Team 6 story: the story says every SEAL would lay down their life for a teammate, so Team 6 asks more: can you leave your wife with this person for a month and expect nothing to happen, and can you leave a large sum with them for a month, a year or a decade and expect it untouched? Watch how candidates answer, not just what. He used to rationalise a slightly "off" feeling because of other strengths; now he does not negotiate on it.
### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#07 | PRINCIPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:39
claim: A high performer with an ego is a cancer on the team and is screened out.
detail: Ego shows as not taking feedback, pointing fingers, and assuming one's own idea is best. The cultural damage lowers total company performance, even if the person's own output is high.

### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#08 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:09:45
claim: "Sincere candor" means both giving feedback accurately and receiving it without taking it personally.
detail: Progress needs feedback; unspoken resentments fill the space between people and degrade communication, and running a company is clarity of communication. Check on interview whether the person can take feedback and give it usefully.

### YT-kW2vDMAmlPI#09 | DEFINITION | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:28
claim: "Competitive greatness" (from John Wooden): being at your best when your best is needed, enjoying a hard challenge.
detail: It is the third Acquisition.com value, with unimpeachable character and sincere candor. Together the values should convey the "vibe" of the place so a reader can tell who would not fit. Character is the foundation, because things will get hard.
