### POD-0014#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:45
claim: See the business as a five-stage pipeline: lead generation, lead work, the sales conversation, fulfilment, and ascension plus resale.
detail: Lead gen is traffic and offers. Lead work gets an inquiry (name, phone, email, application) into the door. The sales stage is the script and the choreographed experience. Fulfilment should consistently over-deliver. The fifth adds referrals, buying more expensive offers, and buying again. He credits Alex Sharfman with the chart.
numbers: 5 functions.

### POD-0014#02 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: unit-economics | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:23
claim: Lead generation is the most expensive stage, yet owners fixate on it while the other four are cheaper to improve.
detail: If you never lose customers and keep upselling them, you earn a lot; you need all five stages for a growing business.

### POD-0014#03 | EXAMPLE | topic: money-models | also: retention-ltv | fw: Ascension Path | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:23
claim: A bootcamp member can ascend into a 12-week abs-and-guns challenge for about $800 on top of a $150 to $200 monthly membership.
detail: Retention is also frequency: buying another month, another month.
numbers: $800 add-on; $150-$200 per month membership.
