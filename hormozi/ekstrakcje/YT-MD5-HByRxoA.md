### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#01 | FRAMEWORK | topic: content-brand | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Five phases of content: post once; post on a steady cadence; post on all platforms; max out each platform; then create and capture (for example a call-in show repurposed everywhere).
detail: Phases 1-2 serve nurture and hook testing; phases 3-5 aim to produce new customers. Most people never complete phase 1, and few reach 4. He was only starting phase 5 at the time.

### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#02 | BENCHMARK | topic: content-brand | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:37
claim: Short-form platforms can sometimes take 5-10 posts a day; newsfeed-style platforms tend to fatigue the audience faster, so you usually get capped at one or two a day.
detail: Phase 4 means finding each platform's maximum and raising volume on all at once. Rules change all the time.
numbers: Twitter and TikTok sometimes 5-10 posts/day; feed platforms usually 1-2/day.
### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#03 | TACTIC | topic: content-brand | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Phase 2: choose one platform you already use and set a fixed interval, for example every seven days; he keeps one day a week for marketing work.
detail: What he does that day varies but is always marketing.

### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Core Four | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:29
claim: If your customers mainly come from outbound, affiliates or paid ads, treat organic content as lead nurture, not lead generation, and do not take your eye off the paying channels.
detail: New leads check your profile and website; the test is a recent post that is not bad. They will probably view two to four pieces to judge whether you are legitimate and still in business.
### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#05 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: content-brand | fw: Greatest Hits | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:15
claim: Keep a master sheet of your best-converting content plus the questions asked on sales calls, and send matching pieces between call one and call two or before the call.
detail: The same list guides future content topics. Some content convinces buyers more than the rest, whether or not you realised it.

### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#06 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:15
claim: Use organic posts that get disproportionate saves and shares as low-risk tests of hooks and headlines, then weave winners into paid ads and cold emails and split test against the control.
detail: Content is a cheap way to find what makes people buy.

### YT-MD5-HByRxoA#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: content-brand | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:46
claim: A small owner can do just phases 1-2; making content your lead machine means investing like in paid marketing: you'll probably need vendors first, probably bring employees in-house over time, then scale volume.
detail: Vendors who are specialists on each platform get you up to speed about five times faster, and by phase 3 it is pretty much beyond your capacity to do the work alone.
numbers: Vendors: about 5x faster ramp.
