### MZ-Aui7yNifFTg#01 | CASE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An aviation-safety trainer earning about $500,000 a year wanted to "close the knowledge gap" for all pilots and was supply constrained by one-to-one coaching.
detail: Former helicopter pilot sells coaching and courses; charges helicopter owners $10,000 for initial training, about 50 sales a year, mostly via an aircraft-broker and insurance-broker referral loop. His insurer-backed training cuts owners' premiums from about $180,000 to $90,000 a year. Hormozi's diagnosis: model is fine, only 10x more customers needed, so change delivery ratio then advertise more.
numbers: ~$500,000 revenue/year; $10,000 per owner, ~50/year; premium $180,000 to $90,000; one-to-one price $1,750.

### MZ-Aui7yNifFTg#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: strategy-positioning | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:10
claim: He keeps coming back to asking how much money the owner wants to make.
detail: Reaching about $2M a year means doing the same thing better; a much bigger goal needs a different model. The answer here was $65M over ten years, which he stated as roughly $5M a year, 10x what the owner currently makes.
numbers: He stated $65M over 10 years as about $5M/year, i.e. 10x current.
### MZ-Aui7yNifFTg#03 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:02:35
claim: To scale an impact idea sold to insurers, give one insurance company the service free and use the savings as the case study.
detail: If underwriters spend about $100M per crash, preventing one proves the value; one demonstration then supports pitches to others. He suggests borrowing a deal to get into one carrier, then using that proof.
numbers: ~$100M per crash cost to underwriters; 10 crashes a year saved = $1B.

### MZ-Aui7yNifFTg#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: scaling-constraints | also: leads-fundamentals | fw: Rule of 100 | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:59
claim: When a business is supply constrained and the model works, ignore other ideas and ask only how to get 10x the leads from the current method.
detail: First check whether the current advertising method is maxed out. Back-calculate customers needed: $65M at $10,000 each needs 6,000 buyers, about 1% of 600,000 pilots entering the market in a ten-year retirement wave.
numbers: 6,000 buyers x $10,000 = $60M (about the $65M goal); 1% of 600,000 pilots.
