### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#01 | BENCHMARK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: The mastermind room held eight founders doing over $500M a year combined, and Hormozi was near the bottom of it.
detail: Hormozi expected to finish the year around $32M, second smallest. One member was near $7M and one near $35M, most were above $100M, and one above $250M. His comparison group is the source of the lessons below.
numbers: Combined revenue over $500M per year across 8 founders; Hormozi about $32M that year; largest over $250M.

### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:00:51
claim: "Scale zero": to grow without being the bottleneck, drive the owner's required involvement down to zero.
detail: Outsourcing roughly goes: administration, then back-end customer service, then sales, then maybe marketing, then maybe the messaging behind market positioning. What remains should be only the biggest decisions. The biggest founders in the room did strikingly little by design. Expect to fail repeatedly, hire people who make mistakes and absorb those costs.
### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#03 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:51
claim: Ask: if the business doubled, would I have to double what I personally do?
detail: If the answer is yes, you are the bottleneck. Example given: 20 hours a week of work at current size becomes 40 hours when the company doubles.
numbers: 20 hours/week doubling to 40 hours/week at 2x business.

### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: Puddles-Ponds-Lakes-Oceans | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:03:25
claim: At his current stage Hormozi concluded he sat in a lake while the other founders sold into oceans, though niching early was right.
detail: Peers were impressed he reached $100M in a single niche (gyms) but urged a bigger market. He notes that for a founder starting out, a lake is a sound choice; his gym niche was correct then, and the skill has since been applied to agencies in 100+ niches.
numbers: $100M generated in one niche; agencies served in 100+ niches.

### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#05 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:32
claim: Running three businesses cost him roughly two years of development; the others had one business and one customer.
detail: Hormozi was the only one in the room with several businesses and had to work himself hard to get all three to eight and multiple eight figures. Others who own several firms hold them passively: a hired CEO and executive team, often with equity, and the owner advises strategically. Being CEO of all of them is feasible for almost nobody.
numbers: About 2 years of development lost (his estimate).
applies_to: Founders tempted to run several companies at once.

### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#06 | BENCHMARK | topic: unit-economics | also: strategy-positioning | fw: Gross Profit | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:30
claim: Every founder in the room but one sold a very high gross-margin product or service, and the two lowest-margin sellers were the smaller businesses.
detail: Hormozi's takeaway is to look for something high in margin and high in value together, which is hard to find, with less hard cost and risk for the owner.
### YT-sKCrYIWPHLc#07 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: scaling-constraints | fw: A Players | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:16
claim: Hormozi says he personally wants high-level leaders who have already done the job rather than teaching someone to do it.
detail: The progression runs doers, managers, leaders, decision makers. For the senior layer pick people who have run and won before, arriving "batteries included" with the solutions preloaded, so the founder stops being the bottleneck.
