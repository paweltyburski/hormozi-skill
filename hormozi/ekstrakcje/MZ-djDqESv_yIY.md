### MZ-djDqESv_yIY#01 | DEFINITION | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:46
claim: Culture is the set of spoken and unspoken "if this, then that" rules, and the first three to five sales hires set them.
detail: Examples: show up on Saturday; every lead gets the full nurture steps. Team members then police each other.
numbers: First 3-5 sales hires set the culture.

### MZ-djDqESv_yIY#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: An 18-year-old won a sales job by emailing everyone in the company a personalised cover letter.
detail: He applied the day he turned 18 and works six to six, seven days a week, which sets the standard for the others.

### MZ-djDqESv_yIY#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:34
claim: There is no advanced sales, only never skipping basics; "simple scales, fancy fails".
detail: Delete the system down to what a manager can watch: followed the script, followed up, worked the lead, made the offer when qualified.

### MZ-djDqESv_yIY#04 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:20
claim: After a ramp of about two weeks, a rep failing on a proven system is not working; the standard is 80-90% of the team performing.
detail: He estimates 95% of organisations cannot train, so hiring is a dice roll.
numbers: Ramp about 2 weeks; 95% of organisations cannot train (his estimate).

### MZ-djDqESv_yIY#05 | DECISION_RULE | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:52
claim: Replicate before you iterate: a rep proposing a script change must first meet the standard, then the change is tested on that rep alone.
detail: The rest of the team is the control. If the close rate is higher, roll it out; usually nothing changes and the rep returns to the script.
