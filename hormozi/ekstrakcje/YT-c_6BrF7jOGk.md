### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#01 | DEFINITION | topic: sales-process | also: unit-economics | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:45
claim: Offer rate (share of people shown up to whom an offer is made) is an indicator of lead quality, since unqualified people should get no offer.
detail: Example: for a gym-owner-only business, a personal trainer who shows up is not offered anything. A 100% offer rate means everyone who shows is qualified.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#02 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Track close rate both as a share of those who show and of those offered, per rep and as a team, to catch a rep who inflates close rate by offering only sure buyers.
detail: Example: team offers 70% of attendees and closes 30%, one rep offers 30% and closes 100%. The data separates bad lead quality from weak selling.
numbers: Team offer rate 70% and close 30% versus one rep at 30% offer and 100% close (illustrative).

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-process | also: unit-economics | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:31
claim: Read close rate together with the share of cash collected up front: low close with high cash share is a hard-line seller; high close with low cash means anyone is told yes with a token deposit.
detail: Goal is the best joint combination of yeses and up-front cash. Units sold is the product of show, offer, close.
numbers: Cash share example: $1,000 widget with $500 average collected = 50%.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#06 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: sales-process | fw: Lead Scoring | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:04:33
claim: Optimise ads around cost per sale, not lowest cost per lead or appointment; the media buyer was targeting 18-24 year olds when buyers were 25-35 year olds with a job they like.
detail: Wrong targeting causes bookings that do not show or do not fit. Reps spent most of their time chasing cancellations; the first step toward 70% show was fixing targeting and replacing the media buyer who had drifted (a side business).

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#07 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process, outreach | fw: Basic Sales KPIs | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:40
claim: Do not let setters both cold-call for new appointments and nurture existing ones; split the roles, and fix three habits: double dialing, speed to contact, and morning-of reminders.
detail: Calling and reminding are two different mental states. Leads were left 30 minutes to 3 hours without contact. Dialing twice gets through call-screening on the first ring. Reminder cadence: same day, next day, and the morning of the appointment.
numbers: Lead response delays of 30 minutes to 3 hours seen.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#09 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: sales-process | also: objections-closing | fw: CLOSER | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:02
claim: In one company's case, surface-level discovery ("how much money do you want to make?") was a problem behind a low close rate; Hormozi says to always dig for intention: what changes, how life looks different, who else is affected, why it matters.
detail: Different intentions (replace income, quit job, side money) call for different talking points. If the salesperson talks more than the prospect, he says these are likely issues coming up.
### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#10 | PRINCIPLE | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:08:58
claim: Obstacles come up before the price and objections after it; an obstacle like "just want information" must be addressed early or it blows up at the close.
detail: Shallow discovery produces a flood of stock objections (too much, need to think, ask spouse, send a brochure) and smoke screens that are not the real reason.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#11 | TACTIC | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:46
claim: Delivery changes the message: ask "what are the main things you are considering?" with childlike curiosity, head tilted, voice rising at the end; emphasis on different words changes meaning.
detail: Trained reps say the script but the prospect hears something confrontational. The team's tone was off because it skipped the first five minutes of discovery and framing.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#12 | PRINCIPLE | topic: sales-team | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:00
claim: Companies over-educate reps on the product and under-educate them on the prospect; knowing the person deeply sells better than knowing the product.
detail: The "sell me this pen" exercise really tests asking questions about the person. Also, the best closer was the CEO acting as sales manager, a common error since closing and managing are different skills; rep churn was very high.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#13 | DECISION_RULE | topic: hiring-talent | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:03
claim: Fix the people problem first: they hired an experienced sales director before touching anything else, since a problem recurring for quarters under the same person might be a "who" issue, not a "what".
detail: The director had trained similar consumer sales. Interview approach: founders judge culture fit; subject experts drill tactics, asking what data the candidate would collect and how they would fix issues; vague answers signal shallow thinking. Good sign: wants to build people and names metrics.
### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#14 | TACTIC | topic: sales-team | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:17:35
claim: Cut sales headcount by utilisation: if reps can take about 10 sales calls a day and take 4, remove the lowest closers; the rest get a rhythm and close more.
detail: Fewer, better reps lifted sales; too few calls makes reps desperate and talk more than listen. A low performer on the team demotivates high performers. Setters' daily minimum was raised from two to four (also described as two to three, 50% more).
numbers: Capacity about 10 per rep per day versus 4 actual; setter minimum 2 to 4 (unclear in audio).

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#15 | TACTIC | topic: sales-process | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:18:22
claim: Promote one setter to lead-nurture specialist as bridge between setters and closers, with a checklist: three-way intro by phone between setter and closer, and a personalised voice or video message from the closer the morning of the appointment.
detail: The known person introduces the unknown one, so a no-show is less likely.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#16 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-process | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:19:58
claim: Move objections to the front ("kill zombies when they are far away"): before the appointment ask whether anyone else must be involved in the decision and, if so, reschedule so everyone attends.
detail: Also re-scripted discovery with deeper questions. Small 1% improvements stack into 50% gains.

### YT-c_6BrF7jOGk#17 | SCRIPT | topic: objections-closing | also: sales-team | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:20:44
claim: Looping: after each objection, resolve the concern, then ask again, repeating; the number of sales is proportional to how many times you ask.
detail: Sequence: "need to think" leads to "what are your main concerns", then the specific piece (complicated), solve it (a vendor handles the tech), then move straight to the ask (ID, card). On "this feels fast", ask how long they have wanted to solve this; a long time means the decision was already made.
