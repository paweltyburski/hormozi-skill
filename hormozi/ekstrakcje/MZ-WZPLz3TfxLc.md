### MZ-WZPLz3TfxLc#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: scaling-constraints | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: Most wealth comes from doing one thing very well to the extreme; ecosystems appear only after one channel is built.
detail: Almost no business starts as an ecosystem. Founders who add side lines to sell customers more usually create operational complexity they cannot staff.

### MZ-WZPLz3TfxLc#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: verticals | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:47
claim: A high-school acquaintance ran roofing, general contracting and build-and-flip, estimated at $5-6M a year; the largest US roofing firm exceeds $1 billion.
detail: His point: the owner does not need three businesses to grow; he is limited by getting customers and ends up under-talented, since the core does not earn enough to attract good people. He calls these mutant businesses that feed each other only in the founder's head.
numbers: Estimated $5-6M a year; biggest roofer over $1 billion.

### MZ-WZPLz3TfxLc#03 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:42
claim: Feed the winner and starve the losers; compare a new venture's year one with your current business's year five, not year three.
detail: A new idea seems to match your current business by year two, but with the same focus the existing business would be at year five. Time in the market beats timing the market, like time under the bar in lifting. Diverting resources slows the main plant; if you accept that trade, know it.

### MZ-WZPLz3TfxLc#04 | EXAMPLE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: management-ops | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:06:11
claim: When he met Layla he ran four or five gyms, a dental agency, a chiropractic agency and Gym Launch, and she noticed the launch business took about 20% of his time yet made nearly all the money.
detail: He cut the rest after three dreaded conversations, all held the same day. He calls distractions the woman in the red dress.
numbers: 4-5 gyms plus 2 agencies; Gym Launch about 20% of his time.

### MZ-WZPLz3TfxLc#05 | TACTIC | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:07:37

detail: After the first, you want to do the rest.
