### MZ-cfuWZqU8Hbo#01 | CASE | topic: lead-getters | also: verticals | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:00
claim: A lead-gen agency finds accounting firms undervalue its leads and drop out after about 30 days.
detail: Hormozi's diagnosis: most accounting firms are capacity-constrained, not demand-constrained, since the work is sticky and they lack accountants. They quit when overloaded or when they see no results.
applies_to: Lead sellers to professional-service firms.

### MZ-cfuWZqU8Hbo#02 | DIAGNOSTIC_Q | topic: scaling-constraints | also: verticals | fw: Theory of Constraints | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:47
claim: Hormozi says most accounting firms are supply constrained more than demand constrained, which is one reason they may not see value in being sold leads.
detail: A lot of the time, supply-constrained firms are not starving for customers, so they may not value more leads.
### MZ-cfuWZqU8Hbo#03 | TACTIC | topic: pricing | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:01:33
claim: Ask a partner the lowest amount they would pay for a customer that costs them nothing, then charge above it.
detail: Do not tie price to your delivery cost.

### MZ-cfuWZqU8Hbo#04 | TACTIC | topic: money-models | also: lead-getters, pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:08
claim: Sell a one-time paid onboarding service that the partner delivers free in exchange for the customer, to speed cash flow.
detail: Example: a tax assessment sold for about $10,000, delivered by the firm at no charge to the agency, which collects from the customer.
numbers: $10,000 price versus about $200 delivery cost.

### MZ-cfuWZqU8Hbo#05 | FRAMEWORK | topic: lead-getters | also: offers | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:03:15
claim: Reverse affiliate deal: build a Grand Slam offer for the affiliate by doing a service they can sell, in return for a free customer.
detail: A flat 20% commission is ignored. Instead you deliver the service under their pricing; their cost per qualified lead equals your delivery cost, and a paying buyer is more qualified.
numbers: 20% standard commission that fails.
