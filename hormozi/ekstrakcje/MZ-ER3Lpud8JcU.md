claim: Four levers drive lead-to-conversation rates: speed, personalization, volume and availability.
detail: Speed means both speed to contact and speed of each reply in a back-and-forth. Convenience (same-day slots) is part of availability. Volume and personalization raise reach and replies, while speed and convenience raise bookings.
