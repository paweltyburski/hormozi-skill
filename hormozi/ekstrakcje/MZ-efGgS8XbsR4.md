### MZ-efGgS8XbsR4#01 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: operator-mindset | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:00:00
claim: People act because they were rewarded for it before; explain behaviour that way, not by labels.
detail: "He stole because he is dishonest" is circular. This improves predictions.

### MZ-efGgS8XbsR4#02 | EXAMPLE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:00:54
claim: An executive threatened to quit over a title change, but past changes never made him leave.
detail: Use history of behaviour to judge the threat; he states it to lower the chance of change.

### MZ-efGgS8XbsR4#03 | DEFINITION | topic: operator-mindset | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:02:19
claim: Trauma is a permanent behaviour change from an aversive event; judge it by effect on goal odds.
detail: Fix harmful cases by reconditioning, not by digging into the past.

### MZ-efGgS8XbsR4#04 | PRINCIPLE | topic: management-ops | also: - | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:30
claim: Feedback works when immediate and specific.
detail: "That sucked" lowers behaviour; "raise your tone at the end of the intro", given at once, changes it.

### MZ-efGgS8XbsR4#05 | CASE | topic: management-ops | also: hiring-talent | fw: - | novelty: new-example | ts: 00:04:49
claim: A top-performing director described as abrasive was fixed in a week by naming specific behaviours.
detail: Four earlier one-on-ones failed. Hormozi aimed to lower the chance others describe him that way, without judging. Behaviours: interrupting calls, telling others how to do their jobs. Replacement: say nothing or ask permission to share a tip, framed as behaviour.
numbers: Fixed within one week.
