### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#02 | FRAMEWORK | topic: strategy-positioning | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-framework | ts: 00:01:07
claim: Four ways to monetize an audience, on a continuum of difficulty, risk and value.
detail: 1. Affiliate: paid after sales. 2. Sponsorship: paid first to advertise or endorse. 3. Partnership: equity in a business with a product and infrastructure. 4. Own brand. Behaviour for 1-3 is similar; only compensation differs. Deals can be combined.
applies_to: Creators and the businesses that rent their audiences.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#03 | PRINCIPLE | topic: finance-exit | also: lead-getters | fw: Affiliate Army | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:03:14
claim: Affiliate income is fastest and lowest risk but has low exit value.
detail: Sign-up is usually automatic on standard terms, with no payment before a sale and no cap. Its sale value is a multiple of the cash flow, discounted by how reliable the paying business is (a government recruiting programme versus an arbitrage info product that may vanish).

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#04 | DEFINITION | topic: lead-getters | also: paid-ads | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: A sponsorship pays you as a traffic source up front, usually priced per thousand impressions, rather than for sales.
detail: Creator posts are the basic form.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#05 | TACTIC | topic: paid-ads | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:05:00
claim: Whitelisting scales a sponsorship: the business runs the creator's post as a paid ad.
detail: A creator avoids over-posting to followers, while the brand reaches beyond the audience. The business pays to grow the creator's brand and gets higher conversion than from its own ads because the face is known.
numbers: Example: 1M audience, a post reaches ~200,000.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#06 | PRINCIPLE | topic: pricing | also: lead-getters | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:06:42
claim: Sponsorship terms follow relative leverage; they are harder to get than affiliate deals.
detail: A big influencer with a tiny firm gets a strong deal; a small one with a large firm may get none. Exit value exists only for a media company that bundles many influencers under umbrella deals.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#07 | BENCHMARK | topic: finance-exit | also: strategy-positioning | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:00
claim: Partnership equity ranges widely and can be performance-linked with a cap.
numbers: Range 0.01% to 99%; example 1% per 1,000 customers delivered, capped at 10%.
detail: Either side can be the majority holder depending on relative size.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#08 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:09:47
claim: A creator doing an equity deal should get part of it up front, since the association is permanent.
detail: Endorsement transfers goodwill at once. A business wanting long promotion can vest the rest, forfeited on breakup.
numbers: Example: 25% up front, 25% on performance, 50% over time (four years).

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#09 | TACTIC | topic: lead-getters | also: content-brand | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:10:51
claim: Specify deliverables up front using who, what, where, when, why, how.
detail: Example: link in bio one week a month, one long piece, five short posts or stories, and monthly recorded ads for whitelisting. Integrate tastefully so the brand does not look like a shill.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#10 | DECISION_RULE | topic: finance-exit | also: pricing | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:11:55
claim: The more cash you already have, the more you should take equity over sponsorship cash, if you believe in the product and exit.
detail: Cash taken now reduces equity. Taking more risk should earn a disproportionate stake.

### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#11 | DECISION_RULE | topic: verticals | also: product-delivery | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:13:11
claim: For an own brand, he personally prefers custom products over white label, because he doesn't want others selling something identical and wants pricing power, though custom costs more and white label is faster.
detail: Slapping a logo on a generic product is usually faster and tends to cost less, but others can sell the same product; a custom formulation or materials means no one else has it. He says there is nothing wrong with white label. Example fields: supplements, T-shirts.
### YT-oRqnTOT9ZG8#12 | DECISION_RULE | topic: strategy-positioning | also: finance-exit | fw: - | novelty: new-detail | ts: 00:15:41
